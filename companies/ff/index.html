    What FutureFuel does

    FutureFuel makes chemicals and biofuels at one integrated plant in Batesville, Arkansas, where it is also headquartered. It operates mainly through its subsidiary FutureFuel Chemical Company. It reports two segments, Chemicals and Biofuels.

    The Chemicals segment makes custom products for other companies, along with inorganic chemicals and bio-based specialty chemicals. FutureFuel says it is making more of the key raw materials it uses, which it can also sell to outside buyers. The Biofuels segment makes biodiesel, a fuel made from fats and oils. The plant has the capacity to make about 59 million gallons a year, but in 2025 it made little biodiesel because of the lack of regulatory guidance on certain government support for sustainable fuels.

    The company says its biofuels business benefits from government subsidies and mandates, and it has historically sold a large part of its biofuels in one state. Its shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange. FutureFuel paid quarterly cash dividends in 2025 and declared one for the first quarter of 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Biofuel demand that depends on government programs, subsidies and mandates that could change.
    • Relying on sales of its biofuels in one state for a significant part of its revenue.
    • Competition in each of its industries, and U.S. biodiesel capacity above the federal mandate.
    • Swings in commodity prices, and reliance on certain strategic raw materials.
    • Slow growth of the infrastructure needed to distribute biodiesel.
    From FutureFuel's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
