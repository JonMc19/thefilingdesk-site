    What PrimeEnergy Resources does

    PrimeEnergy Resources is an independent oil and natural gas company founded in 1973. It buys, develops and produces oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids. Its properties are in the United States, mainly in Texas and Oklahoma.

    It makes money by selling the oil and gas from the wells it owns. Through two subsidiaries it also acts as operator, running the day-to-day work on many onshore wells, and provides well-servicing support to its own wells and to third parties. It also buys producing properties through joint ventures with industry partners. Its main method of developing reserves is horizontal drilling.

    It owns a 12.5% overriding royalty interest (a share of a well's revenue without running it) on land in West Virginia, but it receives no revenue from this because development has not begun. Through an offshore subsidiary it also owns a pipeline off the Texas coast that is currently idle. The company says competition in its industry is intense and that many competitors have far more money and technology.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Sharp, lasting drops in oil, natural gas liquids and gas prices would hurt its business.
    • It has large capital needs and may not be able to get financing on acceptable terms.
    • Purchasers or other operators that run into financial trouble could cut its cash flow.
    • It lacks business interruption insurance, and pollution risks are not fully insurable.
    • Competitors with more money and technology could weaken its position.
    From PrimeEnergy Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
