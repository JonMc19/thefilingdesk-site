    What Ramaco Resources does

    Ramaco Resources mines metallurgical coal, the kind used to make steel, in southern West Virginia and southwestern Virginia. Its customers are North American blast furnace steel mills and coke plants, and steelmakers abroad. It also has a developing rare earth and critical minerals project near Sheridan, Wyoming, called the Brook Mine.

    The company reports two segments: Metallurgical Coal, and Rare Earths and Critical Minerals. Its coal comes from the Elk Creek, Berwind, Knox Creek and Maben mining complexes. It says its coal is among the lowest delivered-cost U.S. metallurgical coal. It says it holds 85 million tons of coal reserves.

    The Brook Mine has so far produced ore for testing, with the aim of showing that rare earths can be processed at full commercial scale. The company says it has no rare earth reserves yet. It also runs a carbon research facility next to the mine, which works on products made from coal.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Coal prices that swing widely, with no long-term sales contracts to protect it.
    • Customers that depend on the steel industry, which is hurt when the economy weakens.
    • The Brook Mine may not become a commercial-scale mine, and the company has no rare earth reserves yet.
    • Mining hazards and accidents that insurance may not fully cover.
    • All of its coal operations sit in one region, and it relies on transport and preparation plants.
    From Ramaco Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
