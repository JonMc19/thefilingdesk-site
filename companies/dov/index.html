    What Dover does

    Dover is an industrial company that makes equipment, components, software and services for many industries. It is based in Downers Grove, Illinois, became public in 1955 and employs approximately 24,000 people worldwide. It has five segments.

    Engineered Products serves the vehicle aftermarket, aerospace and defense, winches and hoists, and precision soldering and fluid dispensing. Clean Energy & Fueling makes equipment to store, move, dispense and monitor fuels, including liquefied natural gas, hydrogen and electric vehicle charging, and it equips convenience stores, fueling stations and car washes. Imaging & Identification sells marking and coding equipment, product traceability tools and digital textile printers, with their supplies and software. Pumps & Process Solutions makes specialty pumps, flow meters, connectors and precision components for biopharmaceutical, chemical, oil and gas, food and beverage and semiconductor customers, among others. Climate & Sustainability Technologies makes equipment for commercial refrigeration, heating and cooling, and beverage can-making.

    In October 2024 Dover sold its Environmental Solutions Group business, which is reported as discontinued operations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Recessions or downturns in the markets its segments serve.
    • Higher labor costs, labor disputes or work stoppages, and trouble hiring skilled workers.
    • Harm to its reputation and business from improper conduct by employees, agents or partners.
    • Sales and operations in countries such as Brazil, India and China that carry currency, political and legal risks.
    • Competition and the need to keep developing products across its five segments.
