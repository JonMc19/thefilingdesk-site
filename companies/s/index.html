    What SentinelOne does

    SentinelOne sells cybersecurity software. Its Singularity Platform uses artificial intelligence to spot and stop attacks on computers, cloud workloads, user identities and other connected devices, with little human effort. When it finds a threat, the software can act on its own, and it can roll back unauthorized changes on a device. A generative AI tool called Purple AI helps security teams search for and investigate threats.

    The company sells to organizations of all sizes, from large enterprises to small and medium-sized businesses around the world. It sells through its own team and through channel partners, such as distributors, resellers and managed service providers, which run security for other companies. It says it grows revenue from existing customers as they add licenses and adopt more of its products.

    The platform runs on Windows, macOS, Linux and Kubernetes, and customers can deploy it in public, private or hybrid clouds. Its fiscal year ends in January, so this profile comes from its report for the year that ended January 31, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a history of losses and may not reach or keep profitability.
    • Intense competition could cost it market share.
    • Its AI efforts may not succeed, which could hurt its business and reputation.
    • A security breach at the company or its vendors, or flaws in its software, would damage its reputation.
    • It depends on channel and alliance partners to reach customers.
