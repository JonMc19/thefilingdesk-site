    Arch Capital Group's revenue rose 14.3% to $19.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Its net margin was 22.1%, down from 24.7% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $6.1 billion, down 7.5%. It paid out $1.9 billion in dividends and buybacks, 31% of free cash flow. The diluted share count fell 1.6%.
