    What CoStar Group does

    CoStar Group runs online real estate marketplaces and sells real estate data, analytics and 3D digital twin technology, which are 3D scans of buildings. Its customers are real estate professionals, property owners, renters, buyers and the businesses that serve them, in the U.S., Australia, Europe, Canada and Asia-Pacific.

    It has spent more than 35 years building a database of commercial real estate, with details such as location, ownership, sales and lease comparisons, tenants, and listings, and it sends researchers to inspect properties. It sells this information mostly through subscription license agreements that renew automatically, and most have a term of at least one year. Its marketplaces include Apartments.com, LoopNet for commercial listings, Homes.com for home listings, and a commercial real estate auction platform. Advertisers and listers pay to reach renters and buyers.

    The company also owns Matterport, which sells 3D digital twins and capture cameras. It is adding artificial intelligence to its products and internal work. Because it has bought many businesses, it carried approximately $6.7 billion of goodwill and intangibles at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep its data accurate, complete and reliable, which would cut demand.
    • Trouble attracting and keeping advertisers on its Apartments.com and LoopNet sites.
    • Results that vary from period to period.
    • Possible write-downs of the goodwill and intangibles from its acquisitions.
    • Rules on data privacy and artificial intelligence that add cost and risk.
    From CoStar Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
