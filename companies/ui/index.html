    What Ubiquiti does

    Ubiquiti designs and sells networking equipment and the software that runs it. Its products include wireless links for internet providers, switches, Wi-Fi equipment, cameras and access-control devices for businesses, service providers and smart homes. Its UISP and UniFi OS software platforms manage this gear. Founder Robert Pera is its chairman and chief executive.

    It reports one segment. It sells through over 100 distributors, online retailers and its own web stores, and has no traditional direct sales force. Instead it relies on online reviews and its user community. Its devices are used in over 200 countries and territories. Revenue was $3.3 billion in the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, up from $2.6 billion the year before.

    Ubiquiti names Cisco, Fortinet, Hewlett Packard Enterprise and Ruckus among its rivals in enterprise Wi-Fi and switching, and Axis, Hikvision, Hanwha Vision and Verkada in video surveillance. At June 30, 2026, it employed or contracted with 1,818 full-time equivalent people, most of them in research and development.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Limited visibility into future sales because it relies on distributors, and on a small number of them.
    • Relying on a few contract manufacturers and suppliers for its products and parts.
    • Competitors with longer histories, bigger brands and larger customer bases.
    • Keeping its products up to date with technology, and defects or security flaws in complex products.
    • Its dependence on its founder and chief executive, and on the strength of its brand.
    From Ubiquiti's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 21, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
