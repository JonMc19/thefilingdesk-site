    What Archer Aviation does

    Archer is building an electric aircraft called Midnight, which takes off and lands vertically like a helicopter. It is designed to work as an air taxi, carrying four passengers plus a pilot on short city trips. Archer is also developing a hybrid-electric, pilotless aircraft for defense and cargo customers with Anduril, a defense technology company.

    Archer has not yet started commercial air taxi service. It says it plans two lines of business: commercial, which would include selling aircraft and running air taxi service itself, and defense, which would include selling aircraft and technology to military customers. It is working with aviation regulators, including the Federal Aviation Administration and the regulator in the United Arab Emirates, on approval to fly passengers.

    Archer builds early aircraft at sites in Silicon Valley and Georgia. It also took control of Hawthorne Municipal Airport near Los Angeles. At the end of 2025 it had a workforce of 1,660 people, including 1,160 full-time employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its aircraft may not win the regulatory approvals needed to carry passengers, and the market for air taxis may not develop as it expects.
    • A history of losses and a business plan that needs a large amount of new capital.
    • Reliance on outside partners to supply and store the parts and components for its aircraft.
    • Its defense aircraft is at an early stage and may not meet military requirements.
    • An accident involving its aircraft, or any eVTOL aircraft, could hurt its reputation.
