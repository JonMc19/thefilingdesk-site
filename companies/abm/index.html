    What ABM Industries does

    ABM is a facility solutions company: it provides janitorial, facility maintenance, engineering and infrastructure services to clients in specific industries, including aviation and education. It began in 1909 as a window washing company in San Francisco and grew through acquisitions to revenue of more than $8.5 billion.

    Its services include janitorial work, lighting, mechanical and electrical technical solutions, and energy offerings such as EV charging, microgrids and power systems. It operates in the United States, the United Kingdom and Ireland.

    Notable deals include Air Serv, which started its Aviation group; GCA Services in 2017, its largest acquisition, which strengthened it in education; Able in 2021; and RavenVolt, a microgrid provider, and Momentum Support in Ireland in 2022. In 2024 it bought Quality Uptime Services, which maintains backup power systems for data centers and other critical facilities. It also sold its Security and Government Services businesses, as part of a plan to focus on industry groups.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competitive pressure that makes it hard to win profitable business.
    • Labor shortages, turnover and rising labor costs.
    • Losing long-term client relationships.
    • Liability and financial risk from using subcontractors or joint venture partners.
    • Fewer people using offices, because of hybrid work, which could hurt demand for services.
    From ABM Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 19, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
