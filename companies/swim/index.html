    What Latham Group does

    Latham designs, makes and sells in-ground residential swimming pools, along with pool covers and pool liners. Its main market is North America, with sales in Australia and New Zealand too. It says it is the largest company of its kind in those regions. Its pools are mostly fiberglass, which it argues installs faster than concrete.

    Latham sells through independent dealers rather than directly to homeowners, but it markets to homeowners online and passes the leads it creates to its dealers. It reports a single business segment. Demand depends on things such as interest rates, credit, housing affordability, consumer confidence and tariffs.

    Its operations include about 1,850 employees on average, spread across about 30 locations, which it says lets it deliver pools to dealers on short lead times.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak consumer spending, housing and credit conditions that cut demand for pools.
    • Bad weather and seasonal swings that make sales uneven from one period to the next.
    • Tariffs, inflation and trade policy that raise its costs.
    • Heavy debt at variable interest rates that could strain its cash flow.
    • A production stoppage at one of its factories, and the loss of large dealers as the dealer base consolidates.
    From Latham Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
