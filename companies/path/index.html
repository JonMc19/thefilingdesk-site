    What UiPath does

    UiPath sells a software platform that lets companies automate work. It began with robotic process automation, software that copies the clicks and keystrokes people use in computer programs, and has added artificial intelligence so that software agents can handle tasks that need judgment. The company started in Bucharest, Romania, in 2005 and was incorporated in Delaware in 2015.

    Its UiPath Platform lets customers build, run, manage and test automations and AI agents in one place. UiPath sells it through a direct sales team, backed by technical and professional services staff, and through channel partners. Customers often start with one use and then add more automations. UiPath says substantially all of its revenue comes from the platform.

    The company says the automation software market is increasingly competitive. Many of the large software vendors it works with also build their own automation features. UiPath has bought several smaller companies to add industry-specific tools; the most recent it names is WorkFusion, in February 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of net losses and no assurance that it will sustain profitability.
    • Dependence on one platform for substantially all revenue.
    • Customers not renewing or buying more, and a small number of large customers making up much of revenue.
    • Reliance on channel partners to generate a large share of sales.
    • Strong competition, including from vendors that build their own automation features.
    From UiPath's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
