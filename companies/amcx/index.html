    What AMC Global Media does

    AMC Networks makes and distributes TV shows and films. It owns streaming services (AMC+, Acorn TV, Shudder, Sundance Now, HIDIVE and others) and cable networks (AMC, BBC America, IFC, SundanceTV and We TV). It also distributes independent films and runs an art-house venue, the IFC Center in New York.

    It earns money from subscription fees paid by cable and satellite distributors and by streaming subscribers, from advertising, and from licensing its content to other platforms. Its AMC Studios unit produces much of its scripted series, including The Walking Dead Universe and the Anne Rice Immortal Universe, which it owns and can license to others.

    Its international division delivers programming to subscribers in more than 100 countries and territories. The company has worked in entertainment for more than 40 years and was spun off from Cablevision Systems in 2011.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Viewer appeal of its programming is unpredictable, and it may not secure enough good programming.
    • Falling cable and satellite subscriber numbers, and dependence on a few large distributors.
    • Rising programming costs and difficulty attracting and keeping streaming subscribers.
    • Advertising market conditions that can sharply reduce its revenue in a given period.
    • Intense competition, and theft of its content.
    From AMC Global Media's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
