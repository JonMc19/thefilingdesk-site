    What Suncor Energy does

    Suncor is a Canadian energy company that covers the chain from digging oil out of the ground to selling fuel at the pump. It mines and drills for oil sands bitumen, a thick form of crude oil, in northern Alberta and upgrades part of it into lighter synthetic crude. It also produces oil offshore, refines oil in Canada and the United States, trades oil and fuels, and runs the Petro-Canada gas station and wholesale network across Canada.

    Its work is split into Oil Sands, Exploration and Production, and Refining and Marketing. Oil Sands is by far the biggest by staff. It includes its own mines, wells that use steam to free deep bitumen, and a 58.74% interest in the Syncrude mine, which Suncor operates. Refining and Marketing turns crude into fuels and sells them, including through Petro-Canada. Its partnership with Canadian Tire made Suncor the main fuel supplier to Canadian Tire's gas stations.

    Suncor had 15,424 full- and part-time employees at the end of 2025. About 26% of them are covered by collective agreements. Its shares trade in Toronto and New York. Canadian law limits how much of its voting stock any one holder can own.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A drop in its credit ratings could raise its borrowing costs and limit how much money it can raise or what hedging it can do.
    • A law that caps any one holder at a fifth of its votes and requires its head office to stay in Calgary.
    • Limits on selling all or most of its assets in one deal, or to non-residents of Canada.
