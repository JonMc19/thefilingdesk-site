    What Hackett Group does

    The Hackett Group is a consulting firm that helps large companies change how they operate, with a focus on generative AI. It advises on finance, procurement, technology and other back-office and front-office work. It sells advisory services and implementation of business software from Oracle, SAP and OneStream, and an eProcurement offering. In 2024 it launched an AI assessment platform, AI XPLR, to help clients find and design uses of generative AI.

    It also sells benchmarking: studies that compare a client's performance with others. It says it has completed over 28,400 such studies. It reports three segments: Global S&BT (strategy, benchmarking, advisory, generative AI work, OneStream and eProcurement), Oracle Solutions and SAP Solutions. Total revenue for fiscal 2025 was $305.6 million, and one customer made up 6% of it.

    The company operates mainly in the United States and Western Europe, and is based in Miami, Florida. On December 26, 2025 it had 1,503 associates, not counting subcontractors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending heavily on a limited number of clients, and possibly losing large ones or follow-on work.
    • Possibly losing money on its contracts.
    • Highly competitive markets for consulting services.
    • Quarterly results that swing because projects vary in size, timing and cancellation.
    • Difficulty hiring, training and keeping enough skilled professionals.
