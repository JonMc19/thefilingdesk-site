    What Hilltop does

    Hilltop Holdings is a Texas-based financial holding company in Dallas. Its main business is banking for businesses and consumers through its bank, PlainsCapital Bank, with offices across Texas. It also has a securities broker-dealer and a mortgage lending business.

    It reports three segments: banking, broker-dealer and mortgage origination. The bank offers traditional banking plus wealth, investment and treasury management services, and makes home loans across the country. Its securities arm, Securities Holdings, does investment banking, municipal advisory work, trading and underwriting of bonds, clearing, securities lending, structured finance and retail brokerage. Its income comes from interest on loans and investments, from fees, and from trading and mortgage activity.

    At the end of 2025 it had total assets of $15.8 billion, total deposits of $10.9 billion and total loans, including loans held for sale, of $9.2 billion. It says it may grow through acquisitions as well as organic growth.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Borrowers not repaying loans, with allowances for credit losses turning out too low.
    • Swings in mortgage lending, which move with interest rates and the housing market.
    • Results in its securities and broker-dealer business that depend on financial markets.
    • Strict banking and securities regulation.
    • Concentration of its banking in Texas.
    From Hilltop's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
