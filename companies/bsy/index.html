    What Bentley Systems does

    Bentley Systems sells software used to design, build and run infrastructure such as roads, rail, bridges, airports, water and power networks, mines and pipelines. Its customers are engineering firms, contractors, government agencies, utilities and other owners of infrastructure assets. Its software covers planning, design and construction of new projects and the operation and maintenance of assets already built.

    Bentley sells mostly subscriptions and tracks them as annualized recurring revenue (ARR). By sector, Public Works and Utilities makes up about 59% of ARR that can be tied to a sector, Resources about 27%, Industrial about 9% and Commercial and Facilities about 5%.

    Its products include modeling and simulation applications, which run on desktops and connect to the cloud, and the Bentley Infrastructure Cloud, which helps teams share work and manage engineering information. It also sells through channel partners. It became a public company in September 2020 and grows partly by buying other companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand that swings with the infrastructure projects of its customers.
    • A large share of revenue comes from outside the U.S., so currency moves and weaker investment in Asia-Pacific, including China, can hurt it.
    • Failing to add new subscribers or keep existing ones renewing.
    • Software defects or security flaws that harm its reputation.
    • Acquisitions that may not be bought or combined with its business well.
    From Bentley Systems's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
