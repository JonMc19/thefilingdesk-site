    What Diebold Nixdorf does

    Diebold Nixdorf makes and services the machines and software that banks and retailers use to serve customers. It has two customer segments, Banking and Retail, and a presence in more than 100 countries with approximately 20,000 employees. Its main factories are in North Canton, Ohio, Manaus, Brazil and Paderborn, Germany.

    In Banking, it sells ATMs, cash recyclers, teller automation and kiosks, plus software and services for bank branches. Services, which cover installation, maintenance and managed services such as running an ATM fleet for a bank, are its largest revenue-generating area. In Retail, it sells checkout and self-service systems, software and services such as maintenance, store rollouts and remote monitoring. It says its Retail clients include 21 of the top 25 European retailers, and that seven of the top ten petroleum companies in the Global Fortune 500 are its customers.

    The company says it faces strong competition on price, delivery, service and product quality, including from local providers in some countries and from newer payment methods. It says it expects to compete with less traditional rivals offering alternative payments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • New services and products may be unsuccessful, or may not earn back their development costs.
    • It may not generate enough cash to fund operations, make capital investments and return capital to stockholders.
    • Cost savings from its improvement programs and restructuring may not be fully realized.
    • Competition worldwide, including from local providers and newer payment methods, could hurt its sales.
    • Failing to hire or losing key employees could have a significant impact on it.
