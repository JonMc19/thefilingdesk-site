    What Haleon does

    Haleon is a consumer health company based in the United Kingdom. It makes and sells everyday health products that people buy without a prescription, such as toothpaste, pain relievers, vitamins and supplements, cough and cold remedies, antacids and skin treatments. Its brands include Sensodyne, Advil, Centrum and Panadol.

    It groups its products into six market categories: Oral Health; Vitamins, Minerals & Supplements; Pain Relief; Respiratory Health; Digestive Health; and Therapeutic Skin Health & Other. Haleon says it is number 3 in Oral Health worldwide and number 5 in Therapeutic Skin Health & Other. It sells through retailers and other customers, and it also works with health professionals who recommend its products.

    Its shares are listed on the London Stock Exchange, and its American Depositary Shares are listed on the New York Stock Exchange. As at 31 December 2025 it had 24,535 employees. It expects to grow by launching new products, such as the Clinical range of Sensodyne, and by reaching more consumers in lower-middle income groups.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Missing its medium-term organic growth goals if its plans, innovation or execution fall short.
    • Losing customer confidence if it does not keep up with ingredient science, regulation and consumer views.
    • Disruption or limits in its global sourcing and supply network that stop it meeting demand.
    • A cyber attack that steals or changes data or disrupts its systems or those of its suppliers.
    • Competition from new global and local rivals, which pressures prices, margins and market share.
