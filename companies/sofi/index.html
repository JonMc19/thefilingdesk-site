    What SoFi Technologies does

    SoFi is a digital bank for consumers, mostly in the United States. Through one mobile app, its members can take out personal, student and home loans, keep checking and savings accounts, use a credit card, buy and sell stocks and crypto, and get tools for tracking their money. SoFi says it has served about 13.6 million members since it started. It also sells banking software to other companies.

    SoFi reports three segments. Lending earns interest and fees on the loans it makes; it can keep loans on its books or sell them to large financial institutions and through securitizations, which bundle loans into bonds. Financial Services covers the checking and savings accounts, investing, credit cards and services for employers and partner lenders. Technology Platform, built on its Galileo and Technisys businesses, rents out payment and core banking systems to banks, government bodies and other firms, mainly in North and Latin America.

    SoFi is a bank holding company, and its national bank, SoFi Bank, now makes all its new loans. Deposits there give it a cheaper way to fund loans than borrowing, and accounts can get federal deposit insurance of up to $3 million through a network of banks. At the end of 2025 it had about 6,100 employees, 82% of them in the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Making poor credit and pricing decisions, or underestimating how many borrowers will fail to repay their loans.
    • Its reliance on a small number of buyers for the loans it sells and of partners in its loan platform business.
    • Changes in interest rates that cut demand for its loans and its checking and savings accounts.
    • Galileo and Technisys depend on a few large clients, and losing any of them could hurt those businesses.
    • Its gain-on-sale model ties its results to the cost and availability of funding in the capital markets.
    From SoFi Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
