    What Martin Marietta Materials does

    Martin Marietta is a building materials company. It supplies aggregates, meaning crushed stone, sand and gravel, from approximately 400 quarries, mines and distribution yards in 28 states, Canada and The Bahamas. Aggregates are used in roads and other infrastructure, in commercial and home construction, and in agriculture, utilities and environmental work. The company also sells cement, ready mixed concrete, asphalt and paving services in places where it has a strong aggregates position.

    The 10-K reports the main business through two geographic segments, the East Group and the West Group. A separate Specialties segment makes magnesia-based chemical products and dolomitic lime, which customers use in steel production and soil stabilization. In 2025, aggregates generated 88% of the company's total reportable segment gross profit.

    The 10-K says that no material part of the business depends on one customer or a few customers. It describes its demand as tied to construction, which is cyclical, and its Building Materials business as seasonal and sensitive to weather.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand depends on construction activity, which rises and falls with the economy and with funding.
    • Widespread declines in aggregates prices could hurt its results.
    • The Building Materials business is seasonal and affected by weather and climate conditions.
    • Its growth depends on finding, permitting and developing aggregates reserves.
    • Acquisitions may be harder, costlier or slower to integrate than expected.
