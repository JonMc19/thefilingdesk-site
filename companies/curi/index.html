    What CuriosityStream does

    CuriosityStream is a media company that offers video and audio programming in factual categories such as science, history, society, nature, lifestyle and technology. John Hendricks, who founded the Discovery Channel and was chairman of Discovery Communications, founded it. Its library has about 14,000 programs, with new titles launched each week, and large parts of it are translated from English into eleven languages.

    It reaches viewers through subscription video on demand (SVOD), content licensing, bundled licenses for SVOD and linear channels, talks and courses, and partner bulk sales. It sells subscriptions in the U.S. since March 2015 and abroad since September 2015.

    It has four main sources of revenue: subscription and license fees from its direct-to-consumer business and partner direct subscribers, license fees from content licensing, bundled license fees from distribution affiliates, and other revenue such as advertising and sponsorships. It runs as a single operating segment. It became public in October 2020 through a reverse merger with a blank check company.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Attracting and keeping users, and the risk of high user churn (subscribers canceling).
    • A history of net losses that it expects to continue for the foreseeable future.
    • Cost-savings plans that may not deliver the benefits it expects.
    • Difficult-to-predict operating results and the challenge of managing growth.
    • Costs and challenges of acquisitions and joint ventures, and growth strategies that are untested.
    From CuriosityStream's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
