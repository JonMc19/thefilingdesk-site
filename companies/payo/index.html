    What Payoneer Global does

    Payoneer is a financial technology company that helps small and medium-sized businesses sell, pay and get paid across borders. Its multi-currency account lets a business receive money from and pay customers, suppliers and contractors in other countries as if it were local. Services include funds management, expense management, workforce management and working capital. It serves customers in more than 190 countries and territories and has nearly 2 million active customers.

    Its customers include goods exporters, service companies, independent professionals, creators, contractors, vacation rental hosts and businesses that work with overseas suppliers. They sell through online marketplaces, directly to other businesses, or through their own web stores. Its revenue includes interest income. In 2025 volume was $87.5 billion and revenue was $1,052.8 million.

    Payoneer was founded in 2005 and relies on close to 100 banking and payment providers worldwide. Its revenue usually rises in the fourth quarter, when holiday e-commerce picks up.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Needing to keep up with fast-changing markets and products.
    • Banking partners or payment processors ending or changing their relationships with it.
    • A decline in e-commerce or cross-border trade.
    • Failing to manage customer funds well or to keep customer fund balances growing.
    • Changes in interest rates that affect its interest income.
    From Payoneer Global's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
