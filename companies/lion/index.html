    What Lionsgate Studios does

    Lionsgate Studios produces and distributes films and television shows. It licenses them to movie theaters, streaming services, broadcast and cable channels and other partners around the world, and sells or rents films on discs and through digital stores. Its 10-K says it has a library of more than 20,000 film and television titles, and it also owns the talent management and production company 3 Arts Entertainment.

    Lionsgate has two reportable segments. Motion Picture made up 60.8% of revenue in the year ended March 31, 2026, and Television Production made up 39.2%. In Motion Picture, home entertainment, meaning sales and rentals of films, was the biggest source, with theatrical releases, television licensing, international licensing and other sources making up the rest. In Television Production, the biggest source is licensing series to domestic television and streaming platforms.

    Lionsgate says it faces substantial competition in all parts of its business. It does not have long-term arrangements with many of its production or co-financing partners.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Large capital needs and financial risks from the cost of making films and shows.
    • Results that swing widely and depend on a small number of releases or library titles in a given period.
    • Write-offs if films or shows do not earn enough to cover their costs.
    • Changes in how people watch, including new technology such as AI and shifts in distribution.
    • Reliance on a few major retailers and distributors, and the risk of labor disputes and strikes.
    From Lionsgate Studios's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
