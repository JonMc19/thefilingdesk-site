    What MercadoLibre does

    MercadoLibre runs an online shopping marketplace and a payments and financial services business in Latin America. It says it is the largest e-commerce platform in the region by the value of goods sold, and that its payments app, Mercado Pago, has the most monthly active users among fintech companies in Argentina, Chile and Mexico. The marketplace is in 18 countries and Mercado Pago is in 8.

    Outside sellers account for most of the goods sold on the Marketplace. MercadoLibre also sells directly to consumers in some categories, which accounts for less than 10% of goods sold. Other services include Mercado Envios, a logistics service with fulfillment centers and delivery partners; Mercado Ads, which sells advertising to sellers and brands; and a classifieds service for vehicles, property and services. Mercado Pago handles payments and offers day-to-day financial services, including lending.

    It points out that online shopping makes up a small share of total retail in Latin America compared with places such as the United States, the United Kingdom and China, and that it competes in an environment it describes as highly competitive.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Slower growth in online shopping and digital financial services in the region.
    • Credit risk from the loans it makes to merchants and consumers.
    • Fraud by users, and complaints or bad publicity that follow.
    • Relying on banks, investment funds and payment processors to fund transactions.
    • Problems in its logistics and fulfillment network, and its reliance on the Apple and Google app stores.
    From MercadoLibre's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
