    What Iovance Biotherapeutics does

    Iovance is a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company, meaning it has approved products on sale. It makes cell therapies for solid-tumor cancers called tumor infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) therapies. Each one is made from a patient's own immune cells, which are grown into very large numbers in a centralized manufacturing process and given back to the patient.

    It has two commercial products. Amtagvi (lifileucel) is a one-time, individualized T cell therapy for adults with advanced melanoma, a skin cancer, who have already been treated. It is given with a short course of chemotherapy-type conditioning and Proleukin (aldesleukin), an interleukin-2 drug that Iovance also sells and that is used in other applications as well.

    Amtagvi is approved in the United States and Canada. Approvals are pending in the United Kingdom, Australia and Switzerland. In the European Union, Iovance withdrew its first marketing application in July 2025. The company has restructured, including cutting its workforce.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a history of operating losses and may never be profitable, even with Amtagvi approved.
    • Its therapies are novel approaches, so the path to approval and acceptance is uncertain.
    • Its newer manufacturing process may not meet FDA rules or lower the cost of making the therapy.
    • It may not gain the expected benefits from its restructuring and workforce reductions.
    • It faces competition from other drug companies and from non-profit institutions.
