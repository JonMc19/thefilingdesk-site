    Net margin is net income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Vivmark Residential's filings tag its rental income as its revenue from 2020. Debt is the sum of Vivmark's unsecured notes, mortgage notes, and credit line and commercial paper, as its balance sheet shows them; the long-term debt figure in its filings already includes the commercial paper, which would count it twice. Vivmark's filings from 2019 label its spending on its apartment buildings in their own way, so capital spending and free cash flow are left blank.

Net debt to EBITDA is debt minus cash, divided by operating income before depreciation and amortization: roughly how many years of those earnings the net debt equals. Debt to equity is debt as a multiple of shareholders' equity. Interest coverage is operating income divided by interest expense. The current ratio is current assets over current liabilities; the quick ratio leaves out inventory.
