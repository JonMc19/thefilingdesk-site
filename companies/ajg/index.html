    What Arthur J. Gallagher does

    Arthur J. Gallagher & Co. (Gallagher) is an insurance and reinsurance broker. It helps businesses, nonprofits, public sector bodies, insurance companies and, to a lesser extent, individuals buy insurance and manage risk. It also advises on employer-sponsored benefit programs and handles claims for others, around the world.

    It reports three segments. Brokerage places insurance and reinsurance and provides consulting. Risk management settles and administers claims, provides loss control services and risk management consulting for organizations that insure themselves or prefer an outside claims manager. Corporate is the third. Brokerage was approximately 87% of 2025 revenue and risk management approximately 13%.

    Gallagher says it does not take on underwriting risk on a net basis, apart from small amounts needed to set up captives and similar vehicles. The insurance companies, reinsurers and other capital providers it calls underwriting enterprises supply the capital that pays for losses. It has grown by buying many insurance brokers and consulting firms.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions or geopolitical events that hurt the regions and industries it serves.
    • Financial trouble at insurers, or less risk-taking capital, that could hurt its results.
    • Acquisitions of brokers that may not work out as planned.
    • Falling behind in using technology and data analytics effectively.
    • Relying on third-party providers and keeping qualified talent.
    From Arthur J. Gallagher's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
