    What Sturm Ruger & Co does

    Sturm, Ruger designs, makes and sells firearms in the United States. Its products are rifles, pistols, shotguns and revolvers, sold under the Ruger name and also the Marlin and Glenfield names. Its design and manufacturing is based in the United States and almost all product content is domestic. Exports are a small part of firearms sales.

    Almost all its sales come from the firearms segment. A castings segment, which makes steel investment castings and metal injection molded parts, is mostly used inside the company and brings in only a tiny share of sales. Rifles are its largest category, at $338.2 million of net sales in 2025.

    The company sells through independent wholesale distributors, mainly to the commercial sporting market, and to a smaller extent to law enforcement. Three distributors account for most firearms sales, and the largest, Lipsey's, represented 27% in 2025. Ruger says that because demand really comes from end users, losing a distributor would not do lasting harm. It says competition in firearms is intense and comes from foreign and domestic makers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in firearms laws or government policy could cut demand or raise costs.
    • State laws that try to get around the federal law protecting gun makers from lawsuits could expose it to liability.
    • A few distributors account for a large part of its sales.
    • Intense competition from domestic and foreign firearms makers.
    • Its sales depend on consumer demand for firearms, which can change quickly.
