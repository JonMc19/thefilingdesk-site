    What Amplitude does

    Amplitude sells subscription software that helps companies understand how people use their websites and apps. Its AI Analytics Platform analyzes behavior data and turns it into insights that teams can act on. The platform combines analytics, session replay, experiments, audience targeting, in-product guides and surveys, and AI agents in one place.

    Amplitude says it earns revenue mainly from subscriptions, with plans that scale with each customer's use. It reaches customers through direct sales, solution partners and product-led growth, where customers start using the product on their own and then expand. It describes a land-and-expand model, in which usage grows across teams and uses within a customer.

    Amplitude was founded in 2012. As of December 31, 2025, it had 4,797 paying customers, including 27 of the Fortune 100. It competes in a crowded software market and says the AI features in its platform are central to its plans.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses, with no assurance that revenue will grow enough to reach and keep profitability.
    • Results that are hard to forecast and may swing from period to period.
    • Growing dependence on large customers, which can make sales less predictable.
    • Intense competition, including rivals adding AI features faster or better.
    • Mistakes in AI features and the reputational harm they could cause.
    From Amplitude's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
