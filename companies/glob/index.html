    Globant's revenue rose 1.6% to $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 7.0%, down from 9.3% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $282 million, up 27.6%. It paid out $56 million in dividends and buybacks, 20% of free cash flow. The diluted share count rose 0.9%.

    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash.

    About these figures. Globant is a foreign company: it files an annual report on Form 20-F, prepared under international accounting standards (IFRS), instead of a 10-K, and no quarterly reports, so this page has fiscal years only. Its shares trade in the US as American depositary shares (ADSs), and one ADS can stand for several ordinary shares, so per-share figures are per ordinary share and the page has no market value, P/E or valuation model.
