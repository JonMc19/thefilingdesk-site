    What Planet Fitness does

    Planet Fitness franchises and runs gyms. Its clubs are typically about 20,000 square feet and are filled with cardio and strength equipment, with staff trainers who give free group instruction. It skips extras such as group classes, pools and day care. It markets a relaxed, non-intimidating atmosphere it calls the Judgement Free Zone. A standard membership starts at $15 per month for new members.

    Most of its clubs are owned by franchisees, who pay the company royalties. The company also owns and runs some clubs itself. Its revenue was $1.3 billion in 2025. At the end of the year it had approximately 20.8 million members and 2,896 clubs: 2,604 owned by franchisees and 292 by the company. They are in all 50 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, Canada, Panama, Mexico, Australia and Spain.

    The company says it competes with the wide range of businesses in the health, fitness and wellness industry.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Harm to its brand, which its success depends on heavily.
    • Competition in the health, fitness and wellness industry.
    • Clubs, its own and franchisees', may not attract and keep enough members.
    • Reliance on high-profile partnerships whose reputations it does not control.
    • Failures of its information systems, or a breach of member payment and personal data.
    From Planet Fitness's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
