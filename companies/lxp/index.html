    What Lexington Realty Trust does

    Lexington Realty Trust (LXP) is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns warehouse and distribution buildings. It focuses on high-quality buildings in target markets in the Sunbelt and lower Midwest. Its tenants are companies that lease space for storage and distribution.

    It earns money mainly as rent. A majority of its properties are under net or similar leases, in which the tenant pays all or most of the costs such as property taxes, utilities, insurance and repairs. It buys buildings, and it develops new ones, often with merchant builders who handle land approvals and most cost overruns for build-to-suit and speculative projects. It also owns interests in some loans and in joint ventures.

    At the end of 2025 it had interests in about 108 properties in 14 states, with about 52.7 million square feet of space, of which 97.1% was leased. It had 58 full-time employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tenants defaulting on leases, or ending them early, or leases expiring.
    • Development projects bring extra risks, and its growth plans may not work out.
    • A substantial amount of debt could limit its financial flexibility.
    • Long-term leases without market rent adjustments, and real estate that is hard to sell quickly.
    • Rising tariffs and trade disputes could reduce demand for its properties.
