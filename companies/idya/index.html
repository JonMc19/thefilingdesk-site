    What IDEAYA Biosciences does

    IDEAYA Biosciences is a precision medicine company that develops cancer drugs. It has no product approved for sale. Its work centers on drugs matched to the genetic drivers of a tumor, including a type called synthetic lethality, and on antibody-drug conjugates, which carry a cancer-killing drug to tumor cells.

    Its most advanced drug, darovasertib, is an oral pill that blocks an enzyme called protein kinase C. It is tested for uveal melanoma, a rare eye cancer, in combination with crizotinib, a drug from Pfizer. The company is running trials in cancer that has spread and in earlier-stage disease where doctors might try to preserve the eye. Its pipeline has nine candidates in all.

    IDEAYA has deals with Pfizer, Servier, Hengrui Pharma and Gilead for licensing or clinical studies. It has no product sales, and says it expects to keep losing money for the foreseeable future.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has no approved products, a short operating history and expects losses.
    • It depends on third parties, including partners and companies that run its trials.
    • Clinical trials may be delayed or fail, and early results may not carry over.
    • Its drugs may not gain regulatory approval or sell well if they do.
    • It may not be able to protect its patents.
