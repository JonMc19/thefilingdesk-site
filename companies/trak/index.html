    What ReposiTrak does

    ReposiTrak sells cloud-based software (software-as-a-service) that helps food retailers, wholesalers, distributors and their suppliers manage supply chains and meet safety rules. Its tools are grouped in three suites. Compliance helps customers check that suppliers meet food safety rules. Traceability helps them track products from farm to shelf, as federal law now requires. Supply Chain helps them manage orders, out-of-stock products and supplier relationships.

    It uses a hub-and-spoke model. Large retailers and wholesalers, the hubs, sign up for the service, and they in turn require their suppliers, the spokes, to use it. Most customers are in the U.S. consumer retail sector: food, convenience stores and general merchandise. The company says no single customer was more than 10% of its revenue in the year ended June 30, 2026. It also offers consulting through two professional services groups.

    ReposiTrak runs its service from a third-party data center. At June 30, 2026 it had 67 employees, 22 of them overseas. In 2026 it also began taking shares of its customer SPAR Group in payment for services and agreed to buy more of that company's shares.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Revenue growth and profits may not continue, and results can swing from quarter to quarter.
    • Its dependence on its founder and chief executive officer.
    • Competition from larger rivals and from new technologies.
    • Customers may not renew or upgrade, and its software could fail or contain errors.
    • Reliance on a third-party data center, and legal claims if a customer sues over a recalled product.
    From ReposiTrak's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 28, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
