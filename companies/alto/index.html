    What Alto Ingredients does

    Alto Ingredients makes and sells specialty alcohols, fuel ethanol and food and feed ingredients in the United States. It runs five alcohol plants: three in Illinois, one in Oregon and one in Idaho. Its alcohols go into mouthwash, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, cleaners, alcoholic drinks, paints and fuel. Its ingredient products include dried yeast, corn protein, distillers grains and carbon dioxide, used in animal feed, pet food and food.

    The company reports three segments. Pekin production covers the three Illinois plants. Western production covers the Oregon and Idaho plants, including a carbon dioxide plant. Marketing and distribution sells the company's own alcohols and ethanol that other producers make. Its results depend heavily on the prices of corn and natural gas, which it buys, compared with the prices of the alcohols and ingredients it sells. Specialty alcohols for industry and agriculture, food and beverage, and health, home and beauty made up about 11%, 6% and 2% of 2025 sales to customers in those three markets.

    The company has about 390 full-time employees. It says it can make 330 million gallons of alcohol a year.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the prices of corn and natural gas versus the prices of its alcohols and ingredients, which can push sales below the cost of production.
    • Having to idle or stop plants when market conditions are poor, as it did at its Magic Valley plant.
    • Customers that may pay late, not pay, or seek new prices even under longer-term contracts.
    From Alto Ingredients's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
