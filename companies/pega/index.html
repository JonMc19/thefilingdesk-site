    What Pegasystems does

    Pegasystems develops enterprise software that helps large organizations make decisions and run business processes automatically. Its clients include big companies and government agencies. They use its Pega Platform to personalize offers to customers, automate customer service, and handle work such as claims and case management across departments.

    The software combines artificial intelligence (AI) decision-making with workflow automation and low-code tools, which let people build applications with little hand-written code. A key product is Customer Decision Hub, which predicts what a customer is likely to do and recommends the next action in real time. Pega Blueprint is a tool for sketching and building applications quickly.

    Pegasystems makes money by licensing its software, by hosting it for clients as Pega Cloud subscriptions, and by consulting and support work. It sells directly and through partners such as systems integrators and cloud providers, and says it wants to deepen its relationships with existing clients and add new ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling short in its investments in AI, including generative AI.
    • Revenue and bookings from licenses and Pega Cloud that are hard to time, so results can vary a lot.
    • Not keeping its subscription clients or winning follow-on business from them.
    • Reliance on third-party hosting providers, and outages or security breaches.
    • A trade secret lawsuit with Appian, and dependence on its founder and CEO, who is its largest stockholder.
    From Pegasystems's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
