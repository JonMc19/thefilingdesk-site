    What American Resources does

    American Resources, based in Fishers, Indiana, is a holding company in coal and critical minerals. Through subsidiaries it has mined and processed coal in eastern Kentucky, western West Virginia and southwest Indiana. The coal is mainly metallurgical coal, which is used to make steel, and bituminous coal sold to industrial customers and for specialty products.

    It has also set up businesses to diversify. Electrified Materials collects, recovers and sells recycled metal and steel. ReElement Technologies works on purifying rare earth elements, the metals used in magnets and batteries, from deposits and from old magnets and batteries.

    At the end of December 2025 the company decided it was no longer the main beneficiary of its coal-mining parent subsidiary, American Infrastructure, or of ReElement, and stopped including their results in its own. Electrified Materials is still fully consolidated. The company says it competes on mineral quality, delivered cost and reliability of supply, and that many rivals have more money and larger coal deposits. It employs very few people directly and also uses contract labor.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in coal, critical minerals and rare earths, where many rivals have more money and larger deposits.
    • Competition from coal producers in other countries, including China, Australia and Indonesia.
    • Relying on contract labor and a very small direct staff to run its operations.
    • Its report gives no separate list of risk factors, because smaller reporting companies need not provide one.
