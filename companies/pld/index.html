    What Prologis does

    Prologis is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns, manages and develops logistics buildings such as warehouses and distribution centers, in 20 countries on four continents. It is based in San Francisco. Its buildings are mostly near big commercial hubs and end consumers, and it also offers energy and sustainability services to customers, including solar generation and storage. It is developing some data centers as well.

    It reports two segments. Real Estate covers rental operations and development: it leases buildings to customers and builds new ones, including build-to-suit and speculative projects. Strategic Capital covers the properties it manages for co-investment ventures, which are partnerships with large institutional investors that broaden its access to capital. It often sells newly built properties into these ventures and reuses the proceeds.

    At December 31, 2025, it owned or had investments in properties and development projects expected to total about 1.3 billion square feet, on its own or through co-investment ventures. Amazon is its largest customer by share of net effective rent.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Social, geopolitical and economic risks of operating in many countries.
    • Local economic or real estate downturns where its properties are concentrated, especially California.
    • Real estate that is hard to sell quickly, while costs stay the same when income falls.
    • Needing outside capital to meet REIT distribution requirements.
    • Oversupply of, or lower demand for, logistics space.
    From Prologis's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
