    What Goosehead Insurance does

    Goosehead Insurance is an independent insurance agency that sells personal insurance policies, such as home and auto coverage, across the United States. As an independent agency, it works with many insurance companies and uses technology to find a policy that fits a client's needs and price.

    It says it partners with over 200 carriers, the insurers whose policies it sells. It earns money mostly through commissions from those carriers on the policies it sells and renews, and it also earns contingent commissions, which depend on results and are less predictable. It sells through its own agents and through franchise and referral partners.

    It competes with other insurance agencies on reputation, client service, product choice and its ability to tailor products to a client.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling insurance premiums or commission rates set by carriers, or carriers asking for commissions back.
    • Contingent commissions that are less predictable and could shrink.
    • Strong competition from other agencies that could cost it clients.
    • Errors and omissions claims from clients over its advice or service.
    • Security breaches or failures in the information systems it relies on.
    From Goosehead Insurance's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
