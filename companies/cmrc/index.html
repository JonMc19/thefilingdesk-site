    What Commerce.com does

    Commerce.com sells cloud software that lets businesses run online stores and sell across many channels. It was named BigCommerce Holdings until July 2025 and is the parent of three products. BigCommerce is its ecommerce platform for building and running online storefronts, catalogs and orders, used by large brands, business-to-business sellers and small businesses. Feedonomics manages product data and sends it to marketplaces, ad channels, search engines and social platforms. Makeswift is a visual editor for building storefront and content pages.

    It earns money mainly from subscription fees for its platform and its product data service, along with some one-time fees, a share of payments revenue and professional services. It says it is built around partners: its platform connects with payment, tax, shipping, order management and marketing tools from other companies, rather than controlling the whole technology stack.

    Commerce.com is based in Austin, Texas, and was founded in Sydney, Australia. At the end of 2025 it had 1,079 full-time employees, most of them in the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of operating losses, with no promise of reaching profit on its expected timeline.
    • Intense competition that may limit its ability to add and keep customers.
    • Advances in AI that may reduce demand for ecommerce software if it cannot keep up.
    • Its partner-centered strategy, and its rebranding, may not work as planned.
    • A data breach, or outages at its data center providers, could cost it customers.
    From Commerce.com's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
