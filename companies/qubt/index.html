    What Quantum Computing does

    Quantum Computing Inc. is a New Jersey company that builds machines based on photonics, which uses particles of light to process information. Its main product is the Entropy Quantum Computer, designed to solve complex optimization problems. It runs at room temperature and low power, unlike quantum machines that need extreme cooling.

    The company says customers can use its computers through the cloud or have one installed on their own premises. It also offers photonic technology for sensing and imaging (including lidar, which measures distance with laser light), machine learning and secure communication. Several of these are early in commercialization, and some are available through its research and development offerings.

    It makes chips from thin film lithium niobate, a crystal material, at a research and small-batch facility in Tempe, Arizona, and plans a larger plant. In February 2026 it bought Luminar Semiconductor, which designs laser diodes, optical amplifiers and other photonic chips for aerospace and defense, sensing and optical communications customers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing money for years and possibly never reaching profit, since quantum computing has not been sold at large scale.
    • Needing more money to fund research and factories, which could dilute current shareholders.
    • Relying on parts from several countries, including China, which trade tensions could cut off.
    • Not scaling production or building reliable products as quickly as it hopes.
    From Quantum Computing's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
