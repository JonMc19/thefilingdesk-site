    What Twist Bioscience does

    Twist Bioscience makes synthetic DNA. Its core technology "writes" DNA on a silicon chip, which lets it build more than a million short pieces of DNA on each chip. The company says this cuts the chemicals needed per gene compared with older plate-based methods. It combines the chips with its own software and an online ordering platform.

    It sells synthetic genes, tools for next-generation sequencing (machines and methods that read DNA), sample preparation products, and products and services for drug discovery, such as antibody discovery. Its customers are researchers and companies in health care, chemicals and materials, food and agriculture, and academic research, in many countries. Nearly all of its revenue so far has come from its synthetic DNA products, and it says it needs to grow its synthetic biology and sequencing product groups and spread into other areas.

    Twist says it competes in a crowded field and that losing a few large customers could hurt its revenue. Its fiscal year ends on September 30.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has lost money in every period so far and may never become profitable.
    • Nearly all its revenue comes from synthetic DNA products, so it depends on their continued success.
    • It could lose a few large customers, which would cut its revenue.
    • It competes in a crowded industry, and rivals may develop technology that replaces its products.
    • It may be unable to keep revenue growing or to manage that growth.
