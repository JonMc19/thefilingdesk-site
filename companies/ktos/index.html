    What Kratos Defense & Security Solutions does

    Kratos Defense & Security Solutions builds systems and technology mainly for the US government and its allies. Its business areas include ground systems and software for controlling satellites, jet-powered unmanned aerial drones, hypersonic vehicles and rocket systems, propulsion systems for drones and missiles, microwave electronics for missile, radar, air defense and space systems, and virtual and augmented reality training systems.

    Kratos says it aims to design products so they can be built in large quantities at low cost. It bids as the prime (lead) contractor when it thinks its chance of winning is high and the investment it must make is manageable, and otherwise teams up with a large traditional systems integrator. Its customers include the Department of War, large prime contractors and commercial customers.

    The company says it has record backlog (contracted future work) and is spending its own money on facilities and products ahead of expected awards. Examples include the Valkyrie unmanned jet aircraft, a hypersonic fabrication facility in Indiana, more small jet engine capacity in Michigan and a planned facility in Oklahoma, and more microwave electronics space in Israel.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition that could stop it from winning contracts and cut revenue.
    • Relying on the US government for a large part of its revenue, and delays or cuts in government funding.
    • Losing or delaying one of its largest customers, programs or applications.
    • Subcontractors or suppliers that fail to do what their contracts require.
    • Keeping pace with changes in technology.
