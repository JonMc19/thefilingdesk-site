    What OpenText does

    OpenText, a Canadian software company, sells products that manage and govern a company's data so it can be used with AI. Clients can run the software on their own servers or in public, private or sovereign clouds. Its customers include global enterprises, small and medium-sized businesses and regulated industries such as financial services, government, healthcare and manufacturing.

    Its portfolio has seven product categories: Content, Business Network, IT Operations Management, Cybersecurity for enterprises, Cybersecurity for small businesses and consumers, Application Delivery Management, and Analytics. Business Network, for example, supports digital supply chains and electronic invoicing. Cybersecurity helps large organizations detect and respond to cyber threats.

    OpenText sells to existing clients through a "One OpenText" model that gives each client a single point of contact, and it works with system integrators and technology partners to co-sell its products. In the Analytics category, the Vertica and eDOCS businesses made up approximately 34% and 8% in fiscal 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Developing new products is costly and uncertain, and the products may not win market acceptance.
    • Relying on third-party software, cloud infrastructure and platforms that may not be stable.
    • Existing clients may cancel or fail to renew contracts, and new clients may be hard to win.
    • Competitors, including those using AI, and consolidation among large rivals in its industry.
    • Sales to government clients that face budget cycles, early termination and audits.
    From OpenText's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
