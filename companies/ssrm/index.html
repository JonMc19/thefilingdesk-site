    What SSR Mining does

    SSR Mining is a precious metals mining company with assets in the United States, Türkiye, Canada and Argentina. It operates, buys, explores and develops mining properties, mainly in Türkiye and the Americas. Its mines produce gold doré, which is a rough, unrefined gold-silver alloy, and copper, silver, lead and zinc concentrates, a semi-processed form of ore that is sold to smelters.

    It makes money by selling those metals, so its results follow metal prices. Its shares trade on the Toronto Stock Exchange and Nasdaq. It voluntarily delisted from the Australian Securities Exchange in April 2025.

    Its report centers on the Çöpler Incident at its Çöpler mine in Türkiye. It says responding to the incident, including remediation and restart work, takes significant management attention. It also describes the Hod Maden project, whose development the incident could affect.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losses, liability and investigations from the Çöpler Incident, and possible environmental contamination around the mine.
    • Not achieving its production, development plans and cost estimates for Çöpler.
    • Insurance that may not cover all losses from the incident.
    • Debt covenants that could limit borrowing, and access to capital that the incident may hurt.
    • Gold, silver and other metal prices that have swung widely.
    From SSR Mining's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

Accruals ratio: net income minus cash from operations, over average total assets; lower means profit is backed by cash.
