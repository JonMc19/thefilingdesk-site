    What Northwest Bancshares does

    Northwest Bancshares is the holding company for Northwest Bank, a Pennsylvania-chartered savings bank based in Warren, Pennsylvania. The bank offers personal and commercial banking, investment management and trust services. At the end of 2025 it operated 161 community-banking locations in Pennsylvania, western New York, northeastern Ohio and Indiana. The company's executive offices are in Columbus, Ohio.

    It earns most of its income as interest on loans and securities. Its main lending is loans secured by first mortgages on owner-occupied one-to-four-family homes, shorter-term consumer loans, and commercial business and commercial real estate loans. It funds these mainly with personal and business deposits, borrowed money, and payments on loans and securities. Its biggest expenses are employee pay and benefits and interest paid on deposits and borrowings.

    On July 25, 2025 it completed its acquisition of Penns Woods Bancorp. The bank's predecessor was founded in 1896, and the company was created in 2009 when a mutual holding company converted to stock ownership. At the end of 2025 it had 2,169 full-time equivalent employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Growth in commercial loans, which carry a higher risk of loss and could mean more loan losses.
    • Its commercial real estate loans could draw extra regulatory scrutiny.
    • An allowance for credit losses that proves too small to cover actual losses.
    • Higher costs and tougher rules from changes in laws, including anti-money-laundering and consumer protection rules.
    • Having to put more capital into the bank if the Federal Reserve requires it.
