    What Entravision does

    Entravision is a media and advertising technology company. Its media business owns and runs Spanish-language television and radio stations in the United States, aimed at Latino audiences, and sells advertising on them. It also provides digital marketing services to local businesses that want to reach Latino consumers.

    It reports two segments. Media made up about 39% of revenue and advertising technology and services, which helps advertisers (mostly mobile app developers) buy digital ads, made up about 61%. Total net revenue for 2025 was $447.6 million. The technology business runs two brands: Smadex, an automated ad-buying platform, and Adwake, a performance marketing agency.

    Its stations include 47 television and 44 radio stations. It is the largest affiliate group of the Univision and UniMás networks, which TelevisaUnivision owns. It earns advertising revenue, fees that cable and internet distributors pay to carry its stations, and political advertising in even-numbered election years. Its agreements with TelevisaUnivision expire at the end of 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its network agreements with TelevisaUnivision expire at the end of 2026 and may not be renewed on current terms.
    • Retransmission agreements with distributors may be ended or not extended.
    • Advertisers do not make long-term commitments, so it may lose or fail to attract them.
    • It may not be able to renew its FCC broadcast licenses.
    • Its digital advertising business could suffer if it fails to respond to industry changes or loses access to third-party platforms.
