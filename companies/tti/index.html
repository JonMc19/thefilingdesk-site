    What TETRA Technologies does

    TETRA Technologies is an energy services and chemicals company that operates on six continents. It sells products and services to oil and gas companies, and calcium chloride for other uses. It says it is also moving into low-carbon energy, using its chemistry skills, mineral acreage and global infrastructure.

    It reports two segments. Completion Fluids & Products makes and sells clear brine fluids, additives and related services that oil and gas companies use when drilling, finishing and repairing wells, in the United States and in parts of Latin America, Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa. The segment also sells liquid and dry calcium chloride. Water & Flowback Services gives onshore oil and gas operators water management, and handles frac flowback and well testing, in major U.S. producing regions and in some countries abroad.

    It also holds mineral leases in Arkansas and has plans to extract lithium, bromine and other minerals from them. Its stated growth plan includes expanding its core businesses, adding new technology and making acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand and prices for its products and services follow oil and natural gas supply, demand and prices.
    • It faces intense competition in the sale of its products and services.
    • It may not be able to extract lithium, bromine or other minerals from its Arkansas leases at an acceptable cost.
    • Its minority investments in other companies can swing in value and make its results volatile.
    • Its work carries operating risks, and some of its operations are seasonal and depend on weather.
    From TETRA Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
