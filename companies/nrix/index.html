    What Nurix Therapeutics does

    Nurix Therapeutics is a clinical-stage drug company that develops medicines based on targeted protein degradation. This approach is meant to mark harmful proteins for the cell's own disposal system rather than just block them. It targets cancer and inflammatory diseases and has not sold a product. It says its discovery engine uses machine learning.

    Its own pipeline has three candidates in human trials: bexobrutideg, a degrader of a protein called BTK; zelebrudomide, which degrades BTK and two other proteins; and NX-1607, which blocks a protein called CBL-B that restrains immune cells. It also has earlier degraders in preclinical work.

    Nurix also works with partners. Under collaborations with Gilead Sciences, Sanofi and Pfizer it has a partnered pipeline that includes a degrader of IRAK4, and for some candidates it keeps options to share in development, sales and profits in the United States. It has never earned revenue from product sales.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has lost money since it began and may never be profitable.
    • It will need a lot more funding, which could dilute shareholders.
    • Its protein degradation approach is unproven.
    • Drug development is long and costly, and early trial results may not predict later ones.
    • Side effects found in trials could slow or stop development.
