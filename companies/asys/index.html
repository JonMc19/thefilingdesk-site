    What Amtech Systems does

    Amtech Systems makes equipment, supplies and services for making and packaging semiconductor chips. Its customers are chip packaging, electronics assembly and chip fabrication companies around the world. Its equipment is used for chips such as the graphics processors used in AI, and for silicon carbide and silicon power chips.

    It reports two segments. Thermal Processing Solutions, the larger one, sells reflow ovens that bond chips to their packages and circuit boards, high-temperature belt furnaces, and diffusion furnaces used in wafer production. Semiconductor Fabrication Solutions sells polishing supplies, cleaning chemicals, wafer-cleaning equipment, parts and services used to make flat, smooth wafers. Selling supplies and services helps it offset the swings in equipment orders.

    Amtech builds its ovens and furnaces in its own plant in Shanghai and through contract manufacturers in China, Canada and Singapore. It sells worldwide: in 2025, 71% of its net revenue came from customers outside North America. The company says it is relatively small and competes with firms that have more financial and other resources.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the semiconductor equipment industry, where customers slow their equipment purchases when they have spare capacity.
    • Competing against larger firms with more financial and other resources.
    • Relying on a few major customers, often selling to them on credit terms.
    • Its heavy dependence on customers outside North America, including in China.
    • U.S. export controls and tariffs that could hurt its business in China.
    From Amtech Systems's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 10, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2017 to 2022, earnings per share grew 12.4% a year and net income 13.8% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 5% and diluted each share's earnings. Amtech Systems then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
