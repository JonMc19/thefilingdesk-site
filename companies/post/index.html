    What Post does

    Post Holdings is a consumer packaged goods holding company with brands in cereal, refrigerated retail, foodservice and food ingredient categories. It became a public company in 2012 through a spin-off from its former parent and trades on the New York Stock Exchange.

    It reports four segments. Post Consumer Brands makes ready-to-eat cereal and granola, Peter Pan peanut butter, other nut butters, pasta, dried fruit and nut products, and pet food, including private label. Weetabix makes cereals and related foods mainly outside North America, and distributes UFIT protein shakes in the United Kingdom. Foodservice supplies mainly egg and potato products to foodservice and food ingredient customers. Refrigerated Retail sells side dishes, eggs, sausage, cheese and other dairy products, under brands including Bob Evans and Egg Beaters.

    Post has grown mostly through acquisitions. They include Michael Foods in 2014, Weetabix in 2017, Bob Evans in 2018, pet food businesses in 2023 and Potato Products of Idaho in March 2025. On July 1, 2025, it bought the rest of 8th Avenue, its private brand food business, in which it had held 60.5% of the common equity.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher costs or limited supply of ingredients, packaging, energy and freight.
    • Agricultural diseases or pests that could harm its businesses.
    • Disruption of its supply chain.
    • Trouble recruiting and keeping a qualified workforce.
    • Damage to its reputation.
    From Post's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 21, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
