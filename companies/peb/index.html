    What Pebblebrook Hotel Trust does

    Pebblebrook Hotel Trust is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns hotels, mainly in major U.S. cities and nearby resort areas, with an emphasis on coastal markets. As of December 31, 2025, it owned interests in 44 hotels with 11,052 guest rooms. Its hotels are in places such as Boston, Chicago, Key West, Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco and Washington, D.C.

    It makes money from its hotels. Because a REIT cannot operate hotels itself, it leases them to a taxable subsidiary, which hires independent third-party companies to manage them. It invests mainly in upper-upscale hotels, a competitive part of the lodging market. It runs everything through an operating partnership, in which it is the sole general partner and owned 99.0% of the common units at the end of 2025.

    It is internally managed, meaning its own executives run the company rather than an outside manager. It was formed as a Maryland REIT in October 2009.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on its executive officers, whose loss would hurt it.
    • Outside companies that manage its hotels may not run them well.
    • Limited growth if it cannot get financing.
    • Distributions to shareholders that depend on swings in its results and spending needs.
    • Upper-upscale hotels being a highly competitive and more volatile part of the lodging industry.
    From Pebblebrook Hotel Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2019, earnings per share fell 0.5% a year and net income grew 16.1% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 81% and diluted each share's earnings. Pebblebrook Hotel Trust then lost money in fiscal 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
