    What Transocean does

    Transocean is an offshore drilling contractor, based in Switzerland. It rents out mobile offshore drilling rigs, together with equipment and crews, to oil and gas companies that drill wells under the sea. It reports one operating segment, contract drilling.

    As of February 17, 2026 it owned, or partly owned, and operated 27 mobile offshore drilling units: 20 ultra-deepwater drillships and seven harsh environment semisubmersibles. It focuses on technically demanding regions, mainly very deep water and harsh environments. Its customers are oil and gas companies, and it relies on a fairly small number of them.

    Its revenue comes from contracts to hire out its rigs. On February 9, 2026 it agreed to buy Valaris, another offshore driller, in an all-share deal. The 10-K says its business depends on offshore oil and gas activity, which is cyclical, and that it has a substantial amount of debt.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling activity in offshore oil and gas drilling, a cyclical industry with intense price competition.
    • Backlog of contract revenue that may not be fully realized.
    • Not renewing expiring contracts, or having contracts terminated.
    • Relying on a small number of customers.
    • The pending business combination with Valaris may be delayed or not happen.
    From Transocean's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
