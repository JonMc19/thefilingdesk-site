    What Viant Technology does

    Viant Technology runs a cloud-based advertising platform, called a demand side platform, that lets marketers and their ad agencies buy digital ads automatically instead of through salespeople. Customers use it to plan, buy and measure ads in one place across connected TV, streaming audio, digital billboards, mobile and desktop. It is a self-service tool: a marketer sets campaign goals and can control each detail, or let the platform run, adjust and measure the campaign on its own.

    Viant earns money from advertisers using its platform to buy ads. It supports several ways of buying, including real-time bidding, private marketplaces and programmatic guaranteed deals, and gives customers forecasts and reports on available ad space for a chosen audience. Its Household ID system connects devices in a household, and it works with more than 70 data partners in fields such as automotive, retail, travel and healthcare.

    It receives a significant amount of revenue from a select number of advertising agency holding companies. At the end of 2025 it had approximately 380 employees in 10 offices across North America.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a handful of ad agency holding companies for a significant share of revenue.
    • The industry move away from cookie-based tracking may not bring the benefits it expects.
    • Programmatic advertising may develop more slowly or differently than expected.
    • Long sales cycles and the cost of training self-service customers to use its platform.
    • Losing access to advertising space or to the data that makes its platform effective.
    From Viant Technology's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
