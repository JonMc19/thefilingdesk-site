    What Principal Financial Group does

    Principal Financial Group is a financial services company. It sells retirement plans, asset management, and employee benefits and insurance to businesses, individuals and institutions. At the end of 2025 it had $1,814.6 billion in assets under administration, including $781.0 billion in assets it manages itself.

    It has three reportable segments. Retirement and Income Solutions runs 401(k) and 403(b) plans, pension and executive benefit plans, stock plans for employees and pension risk transfer services, and sells annuities, mutual funds and trust services. Principal Asset Management manages money for institutional, retirement, wealthy and retail investors around the world, in stocks, bonds, real estate and other assets. Benefits and Protection sells specialty benefits and insurance to business owners and their employees.

    The company says small and medium-sized businesses are an underserved market. It also expects aging populations around the world to raise demand for retirement savings and income products, which its international businesses target. A fourth, Corporate segment holds assets and activities not assigned elsewhere. The company earns fees for administering plans and managing assets, and takes in premiums and investment income on its insurance and annuity products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak capital and credit markets that could hurt its results and its access to funding.
    • Falling stock, bond or real estate values that reduce fees and investment income.
    • Changes in interest rates, or a long period of low rates.
    • Defaults on bonds it holds or on its commercial mortgage loans, which are concentrated in California.
    • Currency swings that affect its international businesses.
    From Principal Financial Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
