    What Otis Worldwide does

    Otis makes, installs, services and modernizes elevators, escalators and moving walkways. It serves customers in over 200 countries and territories and has a direct presence in more than 70 countries, with more than 1,400 branches and offices.

    It has two segments. New Equipment designs, makes, sells and installs elevators and escalators for homes, offices, shops and infrastructure such as airports and metros. Its customers are mainly property developers and general contractors, as well as government agencies. Service maintains, repairs and modernizes elevators and escalators already in use. Otis reports: 35% of net sales came from New Equipment and 65% from Service, while Service produced 91% of segment operating profit and New Equipment 9%.

    About 71% of 2025 net sales came from outside the United States. Otis says it is not dependent on any single customer. Customers usually make an advance payment on new equipment orders, followed by progress payments, and Otis recognizes that revenue as the work is completed. Its main elevator models are Gen2, Gen3, Gen360 and SkyRise.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weakness in construction and infrastructure markets, which drives demand for new equipment.
    • International risks, including trade policy, emerging markets and geopolitical conflicts.
    • Reliance on suppliers, and on estimating costs and timing correctly on projects.
    • Cost-cutting efforts that may not deliver the benefits expected.
    • Product safety claims, lawsuits and anti-corruption compliance.
    From Otis Worldwide's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
