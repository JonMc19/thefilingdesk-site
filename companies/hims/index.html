    What Hims & Hers Health does

    Hims & Hers runs an online health and wellness platform. Customers use its websites and mobile apps to get treatments for conditions such as sexual health, hair loss, hormone health, weight loss, dermatology and mental health. The platform connects them with licensed healthcare professionals, who can prescribe medicine when appropriate. It also sells over-the-counter products, cosmetics and supplements.

    The company makes money when customers buy these products and services, many of which involve prescription medicine taken on a recurring basis. Most prescriptions are filled by pharmacies that the company owns. It does not own the medical groups whose providers do the consultations. It says it has facilitated over fifty million online consultations in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union, including Germany, Ireland, France and Spain.

    It says it competes with large healthcare providers, traditional retailers, pharmaceutical providers and technology companies. The company launched in 2017 and has expanded its range of treatments and countries since then.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its dependence on affiliated medical groups, which it does not own, to supply the providers who give consultations.
    • Competition from large healthcare providers, retailers, drug companies and technology companies.
    • Rules on healthcare, prescriptions and privacy that could limit its marketing or how it serves customers.
    • Reliance on social media and paid influencers, which could harm its reputation or bring fines.
    • Its growth may slow, and expanding into new countries and offerings may be harder than it expects.
    From Hims & Hers Health's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
