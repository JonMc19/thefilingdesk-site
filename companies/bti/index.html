    What British American Tobacco does

    British American Tobacco (BAT), based in the United Kingdom, sells cigarettes and other tobacco and nicotine products to adult consumers around the world. Its brands cover value-for-money to premium cigarettes. It also sells smokeless products: vapour products such as Vuse, heated tobacco products and modern oral nicotine products, which it groups as New Categories, plus traditional oral products such as moist snuff and snus.

    In 2025, combustible products (mainly cigarettes) made up 78.9% of its revenue, and New Categories made up 14.1%. The rest came from traditional oral products and other sources. It is organized into three regions: the U.S.; Americas and Europe; and Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Africa. Its products reach retail outlets through a globally integrated supply chain.

    It had 47,797 employees worldwide at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tighter rules on tobacco and nicotine products, including taxes and limits on flavors.
    • Substantial legal liability and ongoing payments tied to tobacco litigation, such as the U.S. state settlement agreements.
    • Illegal trade in cheaper products, which limits its ability to raise prices.
    • Failing to predict what consumers want across categories and to deliver its strategy.
    • Regulatory approvals and recalls for its newer vapour and oral products.
    From British American Tobacco's annual report on Form 20-F, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
