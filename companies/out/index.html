    What Outfront Media does

    OUTFRONT Media is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that sells advertising space on outdoor structures and sites in the United States. Its inventory is mostly billboards along busy highways and roads in large metropolitan areas, plus transit advertising displays in big cities under exclusive multi-year contracts with municipalities. It has displays in about 120 markets, including the 25 largest, with many sites in and around New York City, Los Angeles and San Francisco.

    It earns money by selling space on those displays to advertisers who want to reach audiences in public places. It has two reportable segments: Billboard and Transit. It says out-of-home ads are always viewable and cannot be turned off, skipped or blocked.

    OUTFRONT is investing in digital displays and in tools for campaign processing, measurement and location-based targeting. It also says it is one of the largest providers of outdoor advertising space in the country. It competes with other outdoor advertisers and with other forms of advertising.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Spending on advertising falling with the economy or other outside events.
    • Government rules on outdoor advertising, including limits on what can be advertised.
    • Winning and renewing key municipal transit contracts on good terms.
    • Its digital display platform proving harder or costlier than planned.
    • Its substantial debt.
    From Outfront Media's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
