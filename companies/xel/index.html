    What Xcel Energy does

    Xcel Energy is a US utility holding company. Through four regulated utility subsidiaries, NSP-Minnesota, NSP-Wisconsin, Public Service Company of Colorado and Southwestern Public Service, it generates, transmits and delivers electricity and distributes natural gas. It serves customers in eight states: parts of Colorado, Michigan, Minnesota, New Mexico, North Dakota, South Dakota, Texas and Wisconsin.

    It serves about 3.9 million electric customers and 2.2 million natural gas customers. Its utilities earn money from rates that state and federal regulators approve, and costs for fuel and purchased energy are generally passed on to customers through rate adjustments. It also has a transmission-only subsidiary, an interstate natural gas pipeline company and a joint venture that develops natural gas pipelines and storage.

    The filing names operational safety, including at its nuclear generation facilities, as a key factor in its results.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Accidents and other problems that can come with running electric and natural gas systems.
    • Wildfires linked to its power lines and equipment.
    • Long-term planning and project risks around meeting demand and keeping the system reliable.
    • Dependence on suppliers to deliver equipment on schedule.
    • Weather, climate change and natural disasters that can damage its facilities.
    From Xcel Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
