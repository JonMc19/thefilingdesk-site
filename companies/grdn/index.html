    What Guardian Pharmacy Services does

    Guardian Pharmacy Services supplies medicines and related services to residents of long-term care facilities. It focuses on assisted living facilities, behavioral health facilities and group homes, which tend to be lower-acuity settings. It also serves residents of skilled nursing facilities. It provides prescription filling and delivery plus technology-enabled services meant to help residents stay on their drug regimens.

    At the end of 2025 it had 61 pharmacies, 54 of them full-service, serving about 205,000 residents in about 8,400 facilities across 38 states. Its revenue comes from dispensing drugs to those residents, paid through health plans, government programs such as Medicare Part D and the residents themselves. More than two-thirds of its revenue in each of the past three years came from residents of assisted living and behavioral health facilities.

    Guardian says these facilities need extra coordination because residents are on many different benefit plans, often have no on-site medical staff, and can choose their own pharmacy, so one facility may use several pharmacies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition could cut its profit margins.
    • Losing its relationships with drug wholesalers or manufacturers.
    • Failing to keep its contracts and relationships with the facilities it serves.
    • Changes to Medicare Part D, and any failure to follow Medicare and Medicaid rules.
    • Inflation and higher labor costs could reduce profit.
