    What Amylyx Pharmaceuticals does

    Amylyx Pharmaceuticals is a clinical-stage drug company, which means it has no approved product on the market and is still testing its medicines in people. Its programs target endocrine conditions and neurodegenerative diseases.

    Its lead candidate is avexitide, which blocks the receptor for a hormone called GLP-1. It is being studied for post-bariatric hypoglycemia (dangerously low blood sugar after weight-loss surgery) and for congenital hyperinsulinism, a condition in which the body makes too much insulin. The company says there are no approved therapies for the first condition. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has given avexitide special designations for these uses, and a Phase 3 trial in post-bariatric hypoglycemia is under way at sites in the U.S.

    Amylyx stopped selling its earlier product in the U.S. and Canada, so it no longer earns revenue from it. It expects to need substantial new funding to pursue its plans.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It depends heavily on the success of avexitide and its other main candidate, AMX0035.
    • It has stopped selling its earlier product and will not return to profit unless a new product succeeds.
    • It expects to need substantial new funding, and raising it may dilute shareholders.
    • Its reliance on third parties to run trials and make its medicines.
