    What Tucows does

    Tucows provides Internet services in three segments: Ting, Wavelo and Tucows Domains. Its stated aim is to make using the Internet simpler for its customers.

    Ting sells high-speed Internet access, mainly fiber and fixed wireless, to home and business customers in select towns in the United States. It bills monthly and mostly has no fixed contract terms. At the end of 2025 it had access to 126,000 owned serviceable addresses, which are homes and businesses it can connect, and managed 54,000 active accounts. Wavelo sells platform and professional services, and billing software, to Internet service providers. Tucows Domains sells domain name registration, wholesale and retail, along with related services such as email, under brands including OpenSRS, Enom, Ascio, EPAG and Hover.

    During 2025 Ting began a review of strategic alternatives, including asset sales and partnerships, because its need for capital keeps growing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Ting's heavy need for capital, and its ability to continue as a going concern depends on changes or new sources of cash.
    • A wind-down or big cut in Ting could bring costs such as severance and contract terminations.
    • Highly competitive, consolidating industries that could limit growth and profits.
    • Keeping existing customer relationships and winning new ones.
    • Needing to keep pace with fast technology change in its services.
    From Tucows's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2021, earnings per share fell 27.0% a year and net income 26.7% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 1% and diluted each share's earnings. Tucows then lost money in fiscal 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
