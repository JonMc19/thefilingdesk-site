    What Baidu does

    Baidu is a Chinese internet company built around its search engine, and it has been investing in artificial intelligence (AI) since 2010. It has its own family of AI models, called ERNIE, and says it offers a full AI stack of cloud infrastructure, a deep learning framework, foundation models and applications.

    Its main business, which it calls Baidu General Business, covers three areas. The Mobile Ecosystem includes the Baidu App, ERNIE Bot and other apps, and earns money mainly from online marketing services for advertisers. AI Cloud sells infrastructure and platform services to businesses and public bodies, plus AI software products such as Baidu Wenku and Baidu Drive. Intelligent Driving and Other Growth Initiatives includes Apollo Go, its driverless ride-hailing service, and the DuerOS smart assistant.

    Baidu also owns the video streaming company iQIYI. It names other internet companies, online marketing platforms in China, other search engines, cloud providers and robotaxi providers as its main competitors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing existing advertisers or failing to attract new ones for its online marketing services.
    • Weak advertising demand in a challenging economy.
    • Uncertain returns from large investments in foundation models and generative AI, and changing AI laws.
    • Strong competition that could cost it users and customers.
    • Harm to its brand from negative publicity, or from content shown on its apps.
    From Baidu's annual report on Form 20-F, filed March 17, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
