    Leggett & Platt

    LEG · Consumer Discretionary · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Leggett & Platt's revenue fell 7.5% to $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Its net margin was 5.8%, up from −11.7% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $281 million, up 25.4%. It paid out $29 million in dividends and buybacks, 10% of free cash flow. The diluted share count rose 1.8%.
