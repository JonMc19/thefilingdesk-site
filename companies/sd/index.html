    What SandRidge Energy does

    SandRidge Energy is an independent oil and natural gas company, organized in 2006. It buys, develops and produces oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids, mainly in the U.S. Mid-Continent. It earns money by selling what it produces.

    At the end of 2025 it had an interest in 1,446 gross producing wells, about 930 of which it operates, and one active drilling rig. Its estimated proved reserves were 69.1 MMBoe, meaning million barrels of oil equivalent. It plans development in the Cherokee play and says it will look at acquisitions and business combinations.

    Its strategy is to grow the value of its assets and to use its net operating loss carryforwards, which can reduce future income taxes, to maximize cash flow. It also has a planned return of capital program. Its headquarters are in Oklahoma City.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Oil, natural gas and NGL prices swing widely because of factors it does not control.
    • Drilling and producing are high risk activities with many uncertainties.
    • Market conditions or operational obstacles that could limit its access to markets or delay production.
    • Not finding, completing or integrating acquisitions, which could slow or end its growth as its wells decline.
    • Dividends and share repurchases are at the Board's discretion, with no guarantee of them.
    From SandRidge Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
