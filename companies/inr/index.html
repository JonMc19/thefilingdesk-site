    What Infinity Natural Resources does

    Infinity Natural Resources finds, develops and produces oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids (NGLs) in the Appalachian Basin. It works in the Utica Shale in eastern Ohio and in dry gas areas of the Marcellus and Utica Shales in southwestern Pennsylvania. It is based in Morgantown, West Virginia, and its Class A shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange.

    It earns revenue from selling the oil and gas it produces, and says its mix of oil and gas lets it shift its plans as prices change. On February 23, 2026, after the year covered by the report, it completed the Antero Acquisition of Ohio oil and gas properties for a cash price of about $800 million, and related pipelines, water and other facilities for about $400 million.

    It owns an undivided 60% interest in those Ohio assets, and Northern Oil and Gas owns the other part. Unless it says otherwise, the figures in the report do not include the Antero assets or a related preferred investment.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in oil, natural gas and NGL prices, and costs that rise with prices.
    • Failing to integrate the Antero Ohio assets or earn the expected benefits.
    • Co-owning the Antero assets with Northern under a joint ownership structure.
    • Reserve estimates that prove wrong, and the need to replace the reserves it produces.
    • Undeveloped acreage that must be drilled before leases expire.
    From Infinity Natural Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
