    What Robert Half does

    Robert Half places workers with businesses and also sells consulting. Its customers are employers that need accounting and finance staff, technology workers, marketing and creative talent, legal staff, and administrative and customer support workers. It has three reportable segments: contract talent solutions, permanent placement talent solutions and Protiviti.

    In contract talent solutions, Robert Half employs the workers and pays them, and the client pays a fixed rate for the hours worked. Clients use contract workers for peak workloads, leaves and special projects, and they can convert a contract position to a permanent one for a one-time fee. In permanent placement, it finds full-time employees for clients. Protiviti is a wholly owned consulting firm that helps companies with regulatory compliance, finance, technology, risk and internal audit.

    Robert Half has bought up the franchised offices it once used and now owns its offices directly. Since 2022 its talent businesses all use the Robert Half brand. It says it had about 14,500 full-time internal staff at the end of 2025. It competes with many other staffing firms, and Protiviti competes with the large accounting firms.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower demand for its staffing services when the economy weakens.
    • Damage to its reputation from the performance or conduct of the workers and consultants it supplies.
    • Not finding enough job candidates for its talent business.
    • Competition with low barriers to entry, including clients using AI tools in place of contract workers.
    • Liability from placing temporary employees in clients' workplaces and from consulting errors at Protiviti.
    From Robert Half's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
