    What Bunge Global does

    Bunge buys crops from farmers and turns them into food, animal feed and fuel. It buys, stores, ships, processes and sells grains, oilseeds and related products, and it also offers financial, risk management and logistics services to customers. It says it operates in more than 50 countries, with facilities in North and South America, Europe and Asia-Pacific.

    It reports four segments. Soybean Processing and Refining turns soybeans into protein meal and vegetable oils for food, animal feed and biofuel customers. Softseed Processing and Refining and Other Oilseeds Processing and Refining do the same for other oilseeds. Grain Merchandising and Milling trades grain and makes flour. Bunge says it ranks among the largest oilseed processors and grain merchandisers by volume, and sells packaged plant-based oils worldwide.

    Its acquisition of Viterra affects its soybean, softseed and grain segments. Its results depend on weather, harvest cycles and commodity prices, which it does not control.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Bad weather, including from climate change, that affects the supply, quality and price of crops.
    • Swings in the prices of crops, raw materials and energy that it does not control.
    • Intense competition in each of its businesses.
    • Government policies and regulations affecting agriculture, and the risks of operating in emerging markets.
    • Not realizing the expected benefits of acquisitions such as Viterra.
    From Bunge Global's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
