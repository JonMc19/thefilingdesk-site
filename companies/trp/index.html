    About these figures. TC Energy is a foreign company: it files an annual report on Form 40-F, prepared under US accounting standards (US GAAP), instead of a 10-K, and no quarterly reports, so this page has fiscal years only. Its shares trade in the US as American depositary shares (ADSs), and one ADS can stand for several ordinary shares, so per-share figures are per ordinary share and the page has no market value, P/E or valuation model. Its figures are in CAD, as it reports them: they aren't converted to US dollars. Free cash flow margin for fiscal 2025 is blank: the 10-K's structured data doesn't include every figure it needs.
