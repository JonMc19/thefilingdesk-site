    What Kenvue does

    Kenvue sells consumer health products. Its brands include Tylenol and Zyrtec, Listerine, Neutrogena, Aveeno, Johnson's, Band-Aid and Nicorette. Its portfolio has three product groups: Self Care, Skin Health and Beauty, and Essential Health. Its products are marketed in more than 165 countries across North America, Asia Pacific, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Latin America.

    Kenvue earns its revenue by selling these products. Net sales were $15.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Kenvue says it is the world's largest pure-play consumer health company by revenue, and that it focuses only on consumer health. It says it competes in highly competitive markets, and that it depends increasingly on key retailers, e-commerce and other new retail channels.

    Kenvue was part of Johnson & Johnson (J&J). J&J transferred its consumer health business to Kenvue in April 2023, and Kenvue sold shares to the public in an initial public offering in May 2023. J&J completed its exit as owner in August 2023 through an exchange offer. On November 2, 2025, Kenvue agreed to combine with Kimberly-Clark. Kenvue's shareholders approved it on January 29, 2026, and the deal still needed other closing conditions, including foreign regulatory approvals.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The Kimberly-Clark deal may not close, may be delayed, or may not deliver the benefits expected.
    • Damage to its brands or reputation, and strong competition in its product markets.
    • Not keeping up with changing consumer preferences, or marketing that fails to work.
    • Reliance on third parties, including manufacturers, and disruptions at its own or suppliers' plants.
    • Growing dependence on a few key retail customers and changes in the retail landscape.
    From Kenvue's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
