    What Motorcar Parts of America does

    Motorcar Parts of America makes and sells replacement parts for cars and light trucks. Its main products are alternators and starters, brake parts such as calipers, rotors and pads, test equipment for parts makers and the aerospace industry, and heavy-duty electrical parts. Most are sold under its customers' own private-label brand names, and the rest under its own brands such as Quality-Built.

    Its customers are large auto parts chains, including Advance Auto Parts, AutoZone, Genuine Parts (NAPA) and O'Reilly Auto Parts, plus warranty replacement programs, professional installers and distributors. A few customers matter a great deal: its three largest accounted for 85% of net sales in its latest fiscal year, and the largest alone for 42%.

    Most of its products are remanufactured: it takes back worn parts, takes them apart, cleans them, replaces worn components and reassembles them. This work is done mostly in Mexico, plus Canada and Malaysia. It had about 5,600 employees on March 31, 2026, most of them in Mexico.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying on a few large customers for most of its sales.
    • Competition, including from low-cost manufacturers in China and elsewhere.
    • Delays in getting component parts, and higher prices for the raw materials in its products.
    • Tariffs, and higher costs or disruptions in the flow of imported products.
    • Debt that limits its operations, and lenders that may not waive future defaults.
    From Motorcar Parts of America's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 8, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
