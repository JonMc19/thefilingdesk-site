    What EverQuote does

    EverQuote runs an online marketplace for insurance shopping. Consumers use its websites and call center, and its partners' sites, to look for insurance and get quotes. The marketplace connects them with insurance providers, meaning both carriers and independent agents, with a focus on property and casualty insurance such as auto insurance.

    Its services are free for consumers. EverQuote earns its revenue mainly by selling consumer inquiries to insurance providers as referrals. Providers use a web portal to see data on consumer type, volume and referral pricing, to target the shoppers they want, and to track how well their marketing spend performs.

    It gets consumers from its own websites and from third-party publishers, including verified partners that pass along quote requests, and from calls placed through its own or partners' call centers. EverQuote says it competes with other companies and technology firms that find customers for insurers online. It also says its revenue depends on insurance providers that make no long-term minimum spending commitments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its business moves with the insurance industry's cycles, especially auto insurance.
    • Insurance provider customers have no long-term minimum spending commitments, and a small number account for a significant part of its revenue.
    • It relies on third-party publishers for a significant part of its visitors, and their prices could rise.
    • Limits on cookies and on collecting and using consumer data could reduce the value of its services.
    • Changes in insurance and data privacy rules.
    From EverQuote's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
