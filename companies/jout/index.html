    What Johnson Outdoors does

    Johnson Outdoors makes and markets branded seasonal outdoor recreation products, used mostly for fishing from a boat, diving, paddling, hiking and camping. It is controlled by its chairman and chief executive, Helen P. Johnson-Leipold, members of her family and related entities.

    Its Fishing segment includes Minn Kota electric trolling motors, marine battery chargers and shallow water anchors, Humminbird sonar and GPS equipment, and Cannon downriggers. Most sales are in North America, through outdoor specialty retailers such as Bass Pro Shops and Scheels, large store chains, distributors and boat makers such as Tracker, Skeeter and Ranger, and its websites. Its Camping & Watercraft segment includes Jetboil portable cooking systems, Old Town canoes and kayaks and Carlisle paddles. Its Diving segment sells SCUBAPRO diving equipment to dive training centers, resorts, internet retailers and public safety units.

    It makes the Minn Kota and Cannon products mainly in Mankato, Minnesota, and the Humminbird products in Eufaula, Alabama and Alpharetta, Georgia. It reaches markets outside North America through independent international distributors, and it hires temporary employees to handle the peaks of its seasonal manufacturing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to design and market new products that appeal to consumers.
    • Uncertainty over global tariffs and their financial impact.
    • Competition, consolidation and financial distress among its retail customers and rivals.
    • Product recalls and other claims, and intellectual property disputes.
    • Raw material price swings and shortages of components, plus supply chain trouble among its suppliers.
    From Johnson Outdoors's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 12, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
