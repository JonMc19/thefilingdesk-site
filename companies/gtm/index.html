    What ZoomInfo does

    ZoomInfo sells cloud-based software and business data to sales, marketing, operations and recruiting teams. Its platform gathers data on companies and the people who work at them, such as contact details, job titles, locations, technologies in use and signals of buying interest. Customers use it to find and reach the businesses and people they want to sell to or hire.

    The platform has three layers. An intelligence layer holds the data. An orchestration layer routes and enriches that data and connects it to customers' sales software. An engagement layer lets users send messages, record and transcribe meetings, run marketing campaigns and manage hiring. ZoomInfo says an engine driven by artificial intelligence and machine learning, backed by a team of research analysts, collects and cleans the data from millions of sources.

    ZoomInfo earns its revenue from subscriptions to the platform and its data. It competes with other firms that gather sales, marketing and recruiting data, and says it may also face large AI model providers. At the end of 2025 it had 3,180 employees, 72% of them in North America.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Customers cutting spending on sales, marketing and recruiting tools when the economy weakens.
    • Failing to win new customers or to keep and grow existing subscriptions.
    • Not keeping its data accurate, complete and reliable.
    • Competition from data providers, and possibly from large AI model companies.
    • Privacy and data laws that could limit how it collects and provides data.
    From ZoomInfo's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
