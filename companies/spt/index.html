    What Sprout Social does

    Sprout Social sells cloud software that helps organizations manage their social media. Its platform lets teams publish posts, respond to customers, track what people say about a brand and measure results across social networks. It adds artificial intelligence features, including an AI agent it calls Trellis, to analyze messages.

    It earns money from subscriptions, and recognizes the revenue evenly over the term of each customer contract. It sells to small and medium businesses, mid-market companies and large enterprises, and it expects its growth to come mostly from mid-market and enterprise customers. It also works through channel partners.

    Its platform connects to social networks through programming interfaces (APIs) that those networks own, and it sells in many countries.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to attract new customers and keep or grow what existing customers spend.
    • Depending on APIs owned by social media networks, which it may not be able to renew on acceptable terms.
    • Its history of losses, and the chance it does not become profitable.
    • AI advances that could disrupt social media and cut demand for its services.
    • Failing to adapt to new social platforms and changing technology.
    From Sprout Social's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
