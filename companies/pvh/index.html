    What PVH does

    PVH designs and sells clothing and related products, mostly under two brands it owns: Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein. It has approximately 26,000 associates and says it is one of the largest global apparel companies. It sells to department stores and other wholesale customers, through its own stores, and online. It also licenses its brands to other companies, and licenses Van Heusen and Nike for some product categories.

    The two main brands together generated over 95% of its revenue in both 2025 and 2024. Since the start of its 2025 year, PVH reports four segments: EMEA (Europe, the Middle East and Africa), Americas, APAC (Asia Pacific) and Licensing. The first three cover wholesale and retail sales of both brands in their regions.

    PVH was incorporated in Delaware in 1976 as the successor to a business begun in 1881, and its offices are in New York.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on the value of the Tommy Hilfiger and Calvin Klein brands.
    • A small number of wholesale customers account for a meaningful part of its revenue.
    • China's Ministry of Commerce placed PVH on a list of unreliable entities after an investigation.
    • Reliance on third parties to make its products and on a limited number of distribution facilities.
    • Cost-saving plans that may not deliver the savings it expects.
    From PVH's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 31, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
