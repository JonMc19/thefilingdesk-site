    What Inspired Entertainment does

    Inspired Entertainment supplies games, software and gaming machines to licensed lottery, betting, casino, bingo and online gaming operators, selling to other businesses rather than directly to players. Its customers include sports bookmakers, casinos, online operators, adult gaming centers, pubs and lotteries.

    It has four segments: Gaming, Virtual Sports, Interactive and Leisure. Gaming supplies terminals, software and game content for betting offices, casinos and gaming halls. Virtual Sports makes simulated sports and racing products. By location, 69% of 2025 revenue came from the United Kingdom, and the rest came from Greece, North America and other markets. It holds licenses from gaming regulators in many places, including the UK, Greece, Italy, several U.S. states and Canadian provinces.

    The company is headquartered in the U.S. and says it works within gaming, virtual sports and lottery rules in each jurisdiction.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on a small number of customers for much of its Virtuals sales.
    • Strict gaming regulation that varies by country and can limit operations or growth.
    • Reliance on key suppliers for equipment and supplies.
    • Competition from many worldwide gaming businesses.
    • Material weaknesses in its financial reporting controls, which it says it expects to fix in 2026.
    From Inspired Entertainment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
