    What LegalZoom does

    LegalZoom sells online legal and business services to individuals and small businesses in the United States. Its main service is forming a business, such as an LLC or corporation. It also offers compliance help, trademark filings, virtual mail, legal forms, bookkeeping and estate planning. Customers can do things themselves with its online tools, or get help from its independent attorney network or its own law firm.

    The company says a customer's first purchase is typically a business formation. After that it tries to sell ongoing services, such as a legal advice subscription or a compliance service that manages a business's filings. In 2025, approximately 65% of its revenue came from subscriptions and the rest from one-time transactions.

    In February 2025 LegalZoom bought Formation Nation, a small business services company whose brands include Inc Authority. LegalZoom says it has formed over 5.0 million businesses since it started. It relies on its brand name and on marketing channels to find customers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its business depends mainly on new business formations.
    • Getting transaction customers to become subscribers and keeping existing subscribers.
    • Marketing that may fail to bring in and keep customers.
    • Adding artificial intelligence to its services, which brings legal and reputation risks.
    • Reliance on third-party providers and partners.
    From LegalZoom's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
