    Astrana Health's revenue rose 56.4% to $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 2.5%, down from 4.4% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $104 million, up 136.6%. It paid out $33 million in dividends and buybacks, 32% of free cash flow. The diluted share count rose 2.9%.

    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Astrana's balance sheet shows a negative 'mezzanine' amount for the minority owners of Allied Physicians of California (US$235 million at the end of 2025). Total liabilities plus shareholders' equity therefore exceed total assets by that amount, as filed. In fiscal 2025 the SEC's structured data shows assets that differ from liabilities plus equity by more than 3%, so total liabilities may be incomplete.
