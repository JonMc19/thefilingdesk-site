    What Toast does

    Toast sells a cloud-based platform for restaurants and food and beverage retailers. It combines software, payment processing, its own hardware and partner apps, and calls itself a restaurant operating system that links front-of-house and back-of-house work across dine-in, takeout, delivery and catering.

    The platform covers point-of-sale terminals, handheld devices and kiosks, kitchen display screens, digital ordering and delivery, labor and workforce tools, inventory and supply chain tools, marketing and loyalty, and financial management. Toast's software runs on its Android-based system. It makes money from software subscriptions, from payment processing and other financial technology, and from hardware.

    Its customers range from small and mid-sized operators to larger multi-location and enterprise businesses, though most are small and mid-sized. Toast says it wants to sell more to new and current customers and to expand outside the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to attract new customers, keep current ones and get them to use more of its platform.
    • Not sustaining recent revenue growth, or managing growth well.
    • A limited operating history at its current scale in a changing industry.
    • Dependence on revenue and profit from its payment processing services.
    • A customer base that is mostly small and mid-sized businesses, which are costly to retain and hit hard by economic swings.
    From Toast's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
