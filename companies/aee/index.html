    What Ameren does

    Ameren is a holding company for regulated electric and natural gas utilities in Missouri and Illinois. Its customers are homes, businesses and industries that buy electricity and natural gas delivered over its lines and pipes. Ameren Missouri generates, transmits and delivers electricity and also delivers natural gas in Missouri. Ameren Illinois delivers electricity and natural gas in Illinois. A third business, ATXI, owns high-voltage transmission lines.

    State and federal regulators set the rates the utilities may charge, so Ameren earns money by recovering its costs and a set return on the money it invests in its systems. It reports four segments: Ameren Missouri, Ameren Illinois Electric Distribution, Ameren Illinois Natural Gas and Ameren Transmission. Ameren Missouri brings in about half of the company's operating revenues.

    Ameren, based in St. Louis, Missouri, passes some costs, such as purchased power and natural gas bought for resale, on to customers. Regulators, not the company, decide how much of its spending it can recover and how fast.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Regulators may not let it recover its costs in a timely way or earn an adequate return on its investments.
    • Heavy regulation, with possible penalties and higher costs if it falls short of the rules.
    • Building and upgrading power and gas systems, including its Smart Energy Plan, carries large risks.
    • Getting enough coal to run its coal-fired energy centers in Missouri.
    • Energy demand that turns out different from forecasts, or falls as customers use less.
    From Ameren's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
