    What Arhaus does

    Arhaus sells premium furniture and home decor, including outdoor furniture, lighting, bath products and textiles. It designs products and sources them from a network of nearly 400 vendors and artisans around the world, and makes upholstered furniture at its own factory in North Carolina. It calls its customers clients.

    It sells through showrooms, interior design services, its online store and a catalog, selling both directly to consumers and to design professionals. As of December 31, 2025, it operated 107 showrooms in 31 states: 90 traditional showrooms, 9 design studios and 8 lofts. Design consultants and interior designers help clients in showrooms and online.

    Arhaus was founded in 1986 by John Reed, its chief executive, and his father, and became a public company in November 2021. It says the premium home furnishings market is split among many independent retailers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losses in the future, and not reaching or keeping profitability.
    • Problems with its new distribution and manufacturing centers, and with deliveries to clients.
    • Dependence on foreign manufacturers and vendors for much of its merchandise, with import risks.
    • A weaker high-end housing market or lower consumer spending.
    • Higher commodity, freight and transportation costs, and reliance on outside carriers.
    From Arhaus's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
