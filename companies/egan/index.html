    What eGain does

    eGain sells subscription software, over the internet, that helps companies manage the knowledge their customer service teams and customers need. The software gathers a company's answers and guidance in one place and serves them to contact center agents, to employees, to customers through self-service, and to AI agents. eGain says this gives AI tools trusted content to work from.

    Its customers are large companies and government organizations, mainly in North America and Europe. It is based in Sunnyvale, California, and also operates in the United Kingdom and India. It earns subscription revenue, which it recognizes over the length of the contract, so changes in sales show up in revenue only gradually.

    eGain sells directly and through strategic and third-party distribution partners. It competes in the market for customer engagement software, including generative AI products. It also offers a limited free version of its service, called Innovation in 30 Days, to get new customers started.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Revenue and operating results can swing, and subscription revenue builds up over time, so downturns show up late.
    • It cannot accurately predict whether customers will renew their subscriptions.
    • It depends on a relatively small number of customers for a substantial part of its revenue.
    • Its sales cycles are long and hard to predict.
    • Fast changes in AI and strong competition in customer engagement software.
    From eGain's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
