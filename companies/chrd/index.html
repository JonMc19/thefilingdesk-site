    Chord Energy's revenue fell 7.1% to $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 4.0%, down from 20.9% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $693 million, down 24.6%. It paid out $683 million in dividends and buybacks, 99% of free cash flow. The diluted share count rose 9.7%.

    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Chord doesn't report a gross profit; the only cost of sales its filings tag is a line for oil and gas it buys and resells, so gross profit and gross margin are left blank. Capital spending is Chord's (until 2022 Oasis Petroleum's) spending on drilling and developing its oil and gas properties, its main capital spending line in every year; acquisitions are a separate line. Oasis Petroleum emerged from bankruptcy in November 2020, so 2020 is split into two periods with no full-year figures and is not shown. Its old shares were cancelled, so earnings per share before and after 2020 aren't comparable.
