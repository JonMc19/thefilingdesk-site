    What USANA Health Sciences does

    USANA develops and makes nutritional supplements, functional foods and personal care products and sells them around the world. It was founded in 1992 and operates in 25 geographic markets. In 2025 it had $925 million in net sales and about 387,000 active customers in its core nutritional business.

    It has historically sold through direct selling, in which independent Brand Partners sell its products and earn pay under a Compensation Plan. Mainland China is its largest market, at about 41.3% of net sales. It also sells in other ways: directly to consumers, through marketplaces such as Amazon, and through retailers.

    In December 2024 it bought a majority interest in Hiya Health Products, which sells children's health and wellness products directly to consumers. In 2022 it bought Rise, a nutrition brand it has expanded.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Direct selling is closely watched and regulated, and changes in law or its enforcement could hurt its business.
    • Brand Partners breaking marketing or advertising laws, or promoting its Compensation Plan improperly.
    • Changes it must make to its Compensation Plan in some markets, or Brand Partners reacting badly to them.
    • Depending on Greater China for much of its business and expected growth, and the strict rules there.
    • A decline in sales or customers in China, or the government not approving its BabyCare business model.
    From USANA Health Sciences's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
