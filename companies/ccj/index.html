    What Cameco does

    Cameco is a Canadian company that supplies fuel for nuclear power plants. It mines uranium, refines it, converts it into the forms reactors use, and makes fuel for CANDU heavy water reactors. Its customers are the utilities that run nuclear plants. It is based in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan and is listed in Toronto and New York.

    Its business segments include uranium, fuel services, and its investment in Westinghouse Electric Company, which supplies fuel fabrication, design and engineering for light water reactors. It also has an investment in GLE, a company developing uranium enrichment technology. Cameco says that if this succeeds, it would let Cameco take part in the whole nuclear fuel chain.

    Cameco says its uranium production capacity is among the world's largest, and that its tier-one operations, its highest-grade mines, accounted for 15% of world production in 2025. It makes most of its money from selling nuclear fuel products and services, so its results vary with that market.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Public opinion on nuclear energy shifting, or another major nuclear accident, which could hurt demand.
    • Its revenue comes mainly from nuclear fuel, so its results rise and fall with that market.
    • Competition and international trade limits, including sanctions on Russia-linked suppliers of enrichment services.
    • Hazards and strict environmental rules that come with uranium mining and processing.
