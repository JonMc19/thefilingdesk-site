    What Dave & Buster's does

    Dave & Buster's owns and runs 243 entertainment and dining venues in North America under two brands. As of February 3, 2026 it had 179 Dave & Buster's stores in 43 states, Puerto Rico and Canada, which combine a full food and drink menu, arcade games and sports viewing. It also had 64 Main Event stores in 22 states, which offer food and drinks along with bowling, laser tag, arcade games and virtual reality. Outside North America it is a franchisor, with four franchised Dave & Buster's locations open.

    Game play is a core part of the business. Customers buy game cards or credits at kiosks, through the mobile app or from staff. Entertainment revenue made up about 62.9% of total revenue in fiscal 2025, with the rest from other sources. Dave & Buster's stores average about 125 games and Main Event stores about 112.

    It leases space for its stores under long-term leases, and the company has substantial debt. In fiscal 2024 it moved its year-end from Sunday to Tuesday to avoid ending periods on its busier weekends.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to carry out its strategy, including growing sales at existing stores.
    • Changes in consumer tastes and spending, and competition from other entertainment and restaurant options.
    • Its substantial debt and loan terms that limit what it can do.
    • Long-term, non-cancelable store leases.
    • The cost of opening new stores and the chance of writing down the value of its assets.
    From Dave & Buster's's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 31, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2024, earnings per share fell 4.4% a year and net income 5.4% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 8%. Dave & Buster's then lost money in fiscal 2025.
