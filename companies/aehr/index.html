    What Aehr Test Systems does

    Aehr Test Systems, based in Fremont, California, makes equipment that tests semiconductor chips for early failures. Its machines run a process called burn-in, which operates chips at high voltage and heat to weed out weak ones before they reach customers. The company says it has installed thousands of systems worldwide.

    Aehr sells these test systems, along with the consumables that go with them, such as the carriers and contactors that hold wafers and individual chips during testing. Its FOX family tests whole wafers or single chips, and its Sonoma systems test chips after they are packaged. The chips it tests go into artificial intelligence data centers, electric vehicles and chargers, solar and wind power, data and telecommunications networks, and memory storage.

    Aehr says it competes with established equipment makers, many with more money, engineering and manufacturing capacity than it has. Its customers are semiconductor makers, and it sells in large, high-value orders.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a small number of large customers, so losing one could hurt results sharply.
    • Sales that come in a few large, high-value orders, which makes results uneven.
    • Intense competition from larger equipment makers with more resources.
    • Needing its FOX and Sonoma systems to win acceptance from new and existing customers.
    • Supply chain problems and tariff changes that could delay shipments or raise costs.
