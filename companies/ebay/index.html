    What eBay does

    eBay runs an online marketplace where people and businesses sell new and used goods to buyers in more than 190 markets. eBay does not sell the goods itself: it connects buyers and sellers through its website and apps. It focuses on categories that draw enthusiasts, such as car parts, collectibles and trading cards, refurbished goods, luxury items, clothing and sneakers. At the end of 2025 it had 135 million active buyers and 2.5 billion live listings.

    eBay reports a single business segment. It earns most of its money from fees on completed sales, so its revenue follows the value of goods sold, which was nearly $80 billion in 2025, 51 percent of it outside the United States. It also charges sellers to promote their listings, and earns money from shipping and financial services for sellers. It says it competes with a wide range of online and offline sellers, including retailers, distributors, auctioneers, search engines and other online marketplaces.

    eBay employed about 12,300 people at the end of 2025, about 7,200 of them in the United States. In February 2026 it agreed to buy Depop, an online marketplace for secondhand clothing, for about $1.2 billion in cash. It offers buyers refunds when items don't arrive or don't match their listings, and checks that some luxury and collectible items are genuine.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Stiff competition from other online and offline sellers, including for its advertising business.
    • Its dependence on consumer spending, which makes its sales swing with shoppers' confidence.
    • New tariffs and the end of duty-free treatment for low-value imports, which could hurt the cross-border trade it relies on.
    • Fraud by users, and liability for counterfeit, unsafe or illegal items listed by sellers.
    • Falling behind on technology such as AI, or on what buyers and sellers expect.
    From eBay's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
