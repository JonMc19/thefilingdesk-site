    What Nabors Industries does

    Nabors owns and runs one of the world's largest fleets of land drilling rigs, and also supplies offshore platform rigs in the United States and many other countries. Oil and gas producers hire its rigs and crews to drill their wells. It also sells drilling software, services that run steel pipe into wells, and equipment for drilling more precisely.

    The company reports four segments: U.S. Drilling, International Drilling, Drilling Solutions and Rig Technologies. International Drilling works mostly in the Middle East and Latin America, including Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Argentina, Colombia and Mexico. Rig Technologies makes drilling equipment and instruments. Nabors says its customers include major international, national and independent oil and gas companies, and that Saudi Aramco alone accounted for approximately 30% of its revenue in 2025, mostly through a joint venture with Aramco.

    Nabors competes on price, service quality, safety record, equipment and the availability of skilled workers. At the end of 2025 it employed approximately 13,900 people worldwide, most of them outside the United States. It is a Bermuda company that operates in more than 20 countries.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower oil and gas prices could cut drilling activity, rig use and the daily rates customers pay.
    • Too many rigs for the work available keeps competition and price pressure high.
    • Customers can cancel or renegotiate drilling contracts, and losing a large customer would hurt.
    • Its dependence on state-owned energy companies and on work in politically unstable countries.
    • Its debt and other long-term commitments may limit its financial flexibility.
    From Nabors Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
