    What First Citizens BancShares does

    First Citizens BancShares is a bank holding company for First-Citizens Bank & Trust, which opened in 1898 in Smithfield, North Carolina. It runs branches mostly in the Southeast, Mid-Atlantic, Midwest and Western United States, with total assets of $229.70 billion at the end of 2025.

    For consumers it offers deposits, loans, wealth management and private banking, and it has a nationwide online Direct Bank that gathers deposits. For small and mid-sized businesses it offers lending, leasing, capital markets and advisory services. It also serves private equity and venture capital firms and companies in technology, life sciences and healthcare, as well as homeowner associations and property managers. It owns a fleet of railcars and locomotives that it leases to railroads and shippers.

    Subsidiaries sell investment products, brokerage and annuities and give investment advice. The company has grown by opening branches and by acquisitions. Descendants of Robert P. Holding, who came to control the bank in the 1920s, own a large share of its stock and hold top roles.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Risks from past and future acquisitions, which may face more regulatory scrutiny.
    • Competition that may cut its market share and profits.
    • Customers choosing not to use banks for their financial transactions.
    • Its dependence on dividends from the bank to pay shareholders and cover its own debts.
    • Control by a stockholder group tied to the founding family, and charter provisions that may deter takeovers.
