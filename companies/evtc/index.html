    What EVERTEC does

    EVERTEC processes electronic payments and provides financial technology in Puerto Rico, the Caribbean and Latin America. Its customers are banks, merchants, corporations and government agencies. It runs payment networks, including the ATH debit network, and offers services such as core banking software, cash processing and fraud monitoring.

    It reports four segments: Payment Services in Puerto Rico and the Caribbean, Latin America Payments and Solutions, Merchant Acquiring, and Business Solutions. Merchant acquiring means handling card payments for stores. Most revenue in the first three segments, and in much of Business Solutions, recurs, because customers pay for ongoing processing. A smaller part comes from one-time projects and license sales.

    EVERTEC says it is one of the largest merchant acquirers in Latin America and the largest in the Caribbean. Its headquarters is in Puerto Rico.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing merchant relationships or its alliance with Popular.
    • Not renewing client contracts on good terms.
    • Failures in its systems, or in the suppliers and staff it depends on.
    • Fraud by merchants, and merchants who cannot pay obligations it may also be liable for.
    • Rapid technology change that could make its products less competitive.
    From EVERTEC's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
