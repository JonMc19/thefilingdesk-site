    KeyCorp

    KEY · NYSE · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    KeyCorp's revenue rose 62.6% to $7.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $1.8 billion. Its return on equity was 9.5%, up from −1.0% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 62.6%, down from 98.4% a year earlier. It paid out $1.1 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count rose 16.7%.
