    What Vertiv does

    Vertiv makes the power and cooling equipment that keeps data centers, communication networks, and commercial and industrial sites running. It also sells software, monitoring and a global service network to install, maintain and repair that equipment. The company traces its roots to 1946 and was spun off from Emerson.

    It sells mainly through direct sales, with approximately 3,000 salespeople worldwide, and also through independent sales representatives, channel partners, distributors, IT resellers and equipment makers. It reports three segments by region: the Americas, Asia Pacific, and Europe, Middle East and Africa. Of its 2025 net sales, 62% came from the Americas, 20% from Asia Pacific and 18% from Europe, Middle East and Africa. It says it competes with niche players and with larger companies.

    As of December 31, 2025, Vertiv employed approximately 34,000 full-time and part-time employees. About 41% of them work in manufacturing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its reliance on continued growth in data center and communication network spending by its customers.
    • Large orders that arrive or get canceled unpredictably, so results can swing from period to period.
    • Orders in its backlog that may not turn into the sales it expects.
    • Larger customers that demand better terms and push down its prices.
    • Production costs and supply chain problems it can't fully control.
    From Vertiv's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
