    What Skyworks Solutions does

    Skyworks designs and sells semiconductors that handle radio signals, manage power and process analog and mixed signals. Its chips go into smartphones and other connected devices, and it also sells to makers of defense and aerospace equipment, data center and networking gear, and cars. It sells directly and through distributors, and works with customers on their products from the start of each design.

    Apple, through sales to distributors and contract manufacturers, was more than ten percent of net revenue in each of the last three fiscal years. Skyworks says it competes with companies such as Analog Devices, Broadcom, Qualcomm and Texas Instruments, on time to market, performance, price, supply reliability and other factors. Customers can cancel orders with limited notice, so Skyworks says its backlog may not reliably show future revenue.

    On October 5, 2026, after the fiscal year ended, Skyworks completed its combination with Qorvo, a maker of similar chips. The fiscal 2026 results in the report generally do not include Qorvo. After the combination Skyworks says it has about 7,000 customers. It spent $837.0 million on research and development in fiscal 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying on a small number of customers for a large portion of its sales.
    • Joining Qorvo may not bring the expected benefits, and could disrupt relationships with employees and partners.
    • Depending on outside companies to make, assemble and test its products and to supply materials.
    • Doing business in China and other countries, for itself, its customers and its suppliers.
    • Swings in the semiconductor industry, and strong competition in wireless chips.
    From Skyworks Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
