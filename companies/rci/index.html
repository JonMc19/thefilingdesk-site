    What Rogers Communications does

    Rogers is a Canadian company with three businesses: wireless phone service, cable and internet service, and media. It sells mobile plans, home internet, TV, home phone and home monitoring to consumers, and network and cloud services to businesses and public bodies. Its wireless brands are Rogers, Fido and chatr.

    It reports three segments. Wireless covers mobile service for consumers, businesses, the public sector and wholesale providers. Cable covers internet, TV, satellite, home phone and home monitoring, plus business network services. Media covers sports and entertainment, TV and radio broadcasting, specialty channels and digital media.

    At the end of 2025 Rogers had about 12.2 million mobile phone subscribers and about 4.5 million retail internet subscribers. Its network passes about 10.5 million homes. In July 2025 it bought BCE's stake in Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment and now owns 75% of it. That company owns the Toronto Maple Leafs, Raptors and other teams. Rogers also owns the Toronto Blue Jays and the Rogers Centre venue.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Canadian rules limit foreign ownership of its licensed businesses, and its board can restrict share transfers and voting to stay within them.
    • A cut to its credit ratings could raise its borrowing costs and limit its access to financing.
    • Its licences to operate telecom and broadcast businesses depend on staying in compliance with Canadian communications laws.
    From Rogers Communications's annual report on Form 40-F and the annual information form filed with it, filed March 6, 2026, in our words; the 40-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
