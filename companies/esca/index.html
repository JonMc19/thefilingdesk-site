    What Escalade does

    Escalade, based in Evansville, Indiana, makes, imports and distributes sporting goods and recreation products. Its brands cover archery (Bear Archery), table tennis (STIGA), basketball goals (Goalrilla, Goalsetter), pickleball (Onix), play systems, fitness gear, game tables, billiards, darts and outdoor games. It reports one segment, Sporting Goods.

    It sells through major sporting goods retailers, specialty dealers, online retailers, its own e-commerce sites, department stores and mass merchants. It makes some products in the United States and imports others from South America and Asia, using contract manufacturers. It had 444 employees at December 31, 2025.

    Sales depend heavily on a few customers: one customer accounted for approximately 19% of its revenues in 2025 and 2024, and another for approximately 11% in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Highly competitive markets that could limit its growth and cut profits.
    • Misjudging consumer demand, which could lose customers and lower sales.
    • Excess inventory, which has hurt its results before.
    • Strategic deals that may not produce the results it expects.
    • Dependence on a few large customers, and on contract manufacturers in other countries.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
