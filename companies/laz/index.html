    What Lazard does

    Lazard, founded in 1848, is a global financial advisory and asset management firm with operations in North and South America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Australia. It advises companies and governments on mergers and acquisitions, capital markets and capital solutions, restructuring and liability management, geopolitics and other strategic matters. It also manages money for institutions and individuals.

    It has two main segments. Financial Advisory earns fees for advice, and these fees are not long-term contracted revenue. The ten largest fee-paying clients made up approximately 17% of the segment's net revenue in 2025, and no client was more than 10%. Asset Management offers equity, fixed income, asset allocation and alternative investment strategies, plus private equity funds and wealth management, to corporations, public funds, endowments, labor unions and others. Its top ten clients held 24% of assets under management (AUM), the money it manages for clients.

    About 82% of AUM at the end of 2025 was managed for institutional and intermediary clients. Lazard says that having several lines of business, regions and industries helps even out its revenue. It also has a Corporate segment. Financial Advisory had 216 managing directors and Asset Management had 124.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Difficult market conditions that reduce deal volumes and the value of the assets it manages.
    • Conflicts of interest that it handles badly, which could damage its reputation.
    • Financial results that can differ widely from period to period, making steady growth hard.
    • Hiring and keeping productive people, in a very competitive industry.
    • Reliance on advisory fees that are not long-term contracts, and on restructuring work that depends on defaults and bankruptcies.
    From Lazard's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
