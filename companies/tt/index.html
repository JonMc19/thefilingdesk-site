    What Trane Technologies does

    Trane Technologies, an Irish company, designs, makes, sells and services heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) equipment, transport refrigeration and custom refrigeration. Its two main brands are Trane, for buildings and homes, and Thermo King, for refrigerated trucks and trailers. Its products include air conditioners, heat pumps, air handlers, building controls and refrigeration systems.

    It reports three segments by region. Americas, covering North America and Latin America, had 2025 net revenues of $17,168.8 million. EMEA, covering Europe, the Middle East and Africa, had $2,802.1 million. Asia Pacific had $1,351.0 million. Americas includes commercial systems, residential heating and cooling, energy services and transport refrigeration.

    Trane says it expects to grow by adding recurring revenue from services and rental options on top of equipment sales. It sets sustainability goals for 2030, including helping customers cut greenhouse gas emissions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Economic risks from operating around the world, including trade policy changes and currency swings.
    • Shortages and price increases for commodities and raw materials, and supply chain problems.
    • Significant competition in the markets it serves.
    • Needing to develop and sell new and improved products on time for growth.
    • Risks and uncertainties from the Aldrich and Murray Chapter 11 cases.
    From Trane Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
