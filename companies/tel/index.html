    What TE Connectivity does

    TE Connectivity makes connectors and sensors, which are parts that carry power, signals and data between pieces of equipment or measure things such as pressure and temperature. They are used in cars, factories, data centers, aircraft, medical devices, homes and energy networks. Its customers are mostly manufacturers that build these parts into their own products.

    It has two reportable segments. Transportation Solutions sells terminals, connector systems, sensors, relays and heat shrink tubing, with automotive its largest end market. Industrial Solutions sells connectors, interventional medical components, relays, wire and cable and filters for markets such as digital data networks, factory automation, appliances, aerospace and defense, energy and medical devices. In fiscal 2025 Transportation Solutions was the larger of the two.

    TE says it competes with thousands of companies, from large multinational firms to local manufacturers, and that it faces steady downward pressure on prices. Competitors named for Transportation Solutions include Yazaki, Aptiv, Sumitomo, Sensata, Honeywell, Molex and Amphenol. It employed about 93,000 people worldwide, including 13,000 contract workers. Its fiscal year ends on the last Friday of September.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A slowdown in the automotive and other industries it depends on.
    • Whether new products win acceptance and whether it keeps up with fast technology change.
    • Pressure to lower its prices from customers and competitors.
    • Currency swings, since much of its business is outside the United States.
    • Business interruptions from severe weather, natural disasters and similar events.
    From TE Connectivity's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 10, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
