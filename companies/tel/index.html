    What TE Connectivity does

    TE Connectivity makes the connectors, sensors, relays, wire and cable that carry power, signals and data inside cars, trucks, factories, power grids, data centers, aircraft and medical devices. Its customers are mostly manufacturers.

    It has two segments. Transportation Solutions sells mainly to automakers, with commercial vehicles and sensors making up the rest. Industrial Solutions sells to data networks (including AI data centers), factory and building automation, energy utilities, aerospace, defense and marine, and medical customers. Each segment was about half of fiscal 2026 net sales. TE says its two segments served a combined market of approximately $135 billion. It names Amphenol, Molex, Yazaki and Aptiv among its competitors.

    TE sells into approximately 130 countries, mainly directly to manufacturers: direct sales were approximately 70% of total net sales in fiscal 2026, and the rest goes through distributors. At fiscal year end it employed approximately 104,000 people, including contract employees, and about 65,000 of them worked in manufacturing. It is incorporated in Ireland.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Heavy dependence on the automotive and other industries, where downturns have hurt its results.
    • Competition and pressure to lower prices in electronic components, which can cut its margins.
    • Its new products and innovations may not win market acceptance in time.
    • Shortages, quality problems or price increases in raw materials such as copper and precious metals.
    • Product liability, warranty and recall claims, and acquisitions that may not work out.
    From TE Connectivity's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
