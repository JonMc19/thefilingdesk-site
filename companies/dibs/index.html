    What 1stdibs.com does

    1stDibs runs an online marketplace where sellers list vintage, antique and contemporary furniture, home decor, jewelry, watches, art and fashion for design-minded buyers. Its sellers are vetted by in-house experts, and the company says most of them are small businesses. It started as a listings site for antique furniture dealers and moved to a full e-commerce marketplace in 2016.

    It does not take physical possession of the items sold. It provides the platform, helps arrange shipping, and offers buyers a purchase protection program it calls the 1stDibs Promise. In 2024 it shifted to fewer, more engaged sellers and dropped its option of no monthly subscription fee with higher commission rates.

    At December 31, 2025 it had 7.8 million users, approximately 1.9 million listings and approximately 5,700 unique sellers. It had approximately 60,700 active buyers in 2025, down from approximately 64,300 in 2024, and an average order value of approximately $2,600.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of operating losses; it may not become or stay profitable.
    • Results that swing from period to period, and with the seasons.
    • Needing to attract and keep an active community of buyers and sellers.
    • Proving the authenticity of listed items; counterfeit or illegal listings could damage its reputation.
    • Acquisitions that may need significant resources and may not work out.
