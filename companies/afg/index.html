    What American Financial Group does

    American Financial Group is an insurance holding company based in Cincinnati. Through its Great American Insurance Group, it sells property and casualty insurance, focused on specialized coverage for businesses. The group has been in business for over 150 years, and the company has its own team that manages its investment portfolio.

    It makes money in two ways: it collects premiums for the insurance it writes, and it earns income on the investments it holds with those premiums. Its property and casualty business runs through 36 insurance businesses, each handling a niche such as a particular type of coverage or customer group. Each makes its own choices on pricing, claims and policy servicing, with some central controls. Policies are sold through independent agents.

    Great American Insurance is its largest insurance subsidiary by premiums written. AFG says that holding strong financial strength ratings from rating agencies is important to compete in most of its lines. It also has some international operations and crop insurance.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Catastrophes such as severe weather and other natural or man-made events that cause large insurance losses.
    • Reserves set aside for claims that turn out to be too low.
    • Swings in crop prices or yields that affect its crop insurance.
    • Intense competition, and losing the independent agents who sell its policies.
    • Mass tort claims, and risks from its own and its partners' use of artificial intelligence.
    From American Financial Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
