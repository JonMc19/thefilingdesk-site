    What NiSource does

    NiSource is an energy holding company. Its main subsidiaries are regulated natural gas and electric utilities that serve about 3.8 million customers in six states. Customers are homes, businesses and industrial users who buy gas and electricity for heating, cooling and operations.

    It has two reportable segments. Columbia Operations delivers natural gas to about 2.4 million customers in Ohio, Pennsylvania, Virginia, Kentucky and Maryland. NIPSCO Operations delivers natural gas in northern Indiana and also generates, transmits and sells electricity there. Nearly all of NiSource's revenue and earnings come from these rate-regulated businesses, meaning state regulators set the prices it can charge. Gas sales are higher in the heating season, from October through May.

    NiSource competes with other electric utilities, gas pipelines and other fuels such as propane and fuel oil. In 2025 it signed a contract to supply electricity to a data center customer, and it plans to build power generation through a subsidiary to serve that demand. NIPSCO's power plants include coal, natural gas, hydro, wind and solar facilities, and two coal plants are scheduled to retire.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Regulators set most of its prices, and rate decisions may not let it recover its costs or earn its allowed return.
    • Delays, cost overruns or financing problems in building the power plants for its data center contract.
    • Its large amount of debt, and a drop in its credit ratings.
    • Dangers in running gas and electric systems, including aging equipment that can fail.
    • Weather, energy prices and the economy changing how much gas and electricity customers use.
    From NiSource's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
