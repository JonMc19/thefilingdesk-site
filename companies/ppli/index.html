    What People Inc. does

    IAC is a holding company that owns and runs internet businesses and holds stakes in other companies. Its main businesses include People Inc., a large digital and print publisher, and Care.com, an online marketplace for families looking for caregivers. It also holds minority stakes in MGM Resorts International and Turo, a car sharing marketplace, and a controlling interest in Vivian Health, a platform that connects healthcare professionals with job openings.

    People Inc. owns over 40 brands in subjects such as entertainment, food, home, beauty, health, travel and finance, including PEOPLE, Allrecipes, Better Homes & Gardens, Investopedia and Travel + Leisure. It says more than 175 million people use it each month. It publishes through websites, magazines and licensing deals, and the 10-K says advertising is a significant part of IAC's revenue. Care.com earns money from paid memberships, in which families pay to contact caregivers, and from services sold to employers who offer care benefits.

    IAC has repeatedly bought, spun off and sold businesses over the years. In March 2025 it spun off Angi Inc., the home services marketplace, to its shareholders. Barry Diller, its Chairman and Senior Executive, and his family together hold approximately 46% of the company's voting power.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on search engines, and on arrangements with Google, to bring visitors to its sites.
    • AI and other changes in how people find information that could reduce traffic to its sites.
    • Advertising makes up a significant part of revenue, which makes it sensitive to the economy.
    • Marketing to attract visitors that may not work or may cost too much.
    • Needing access to personal data about its users and subscribers.
    From People Inc.'s annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
