    What BorgWarner does

    BorgWarner makes parts for cars and trucks that run on combustion engines, hybrid systems and electric power. Its products affect vehicle performance, propulsion efficiency, stability and air quality. It sells mainly to makers of light vehicles (cars, SUVs, vans and light trucks), and also to makers of commercial and off-highway vehicles, to vehicle systems suppliers and to the aftermarket. It has plants serving customers in Europe, the Americas and Asia.

    It reports four segments: Turbos & Thermal Technologies, Drivetrain & Morse Systems, PowerDrive Systems, and Battery & Charging Systems. In 2025, products used in or for electric vehicles brought in about $2.6 billion, or 18% of revenue. Products for combustion engines and hybrids brought in about $11.7 billion, or 82%.

    The company says its strategy is profitable growth across products for electric, hybrid and combustion vehicles, through its own investment and acquisitions. It says electric vehicle adoption has varied across regions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A portfolio strategy that may not succeed, and acquisitions or sales of businesses that may not go as planned.
    • Conditions in the auto industry and changes in how the industry evolves.
    • Pressure from automakers to cut the prices of its products.
    • Volatile commodity costs and high inflation.
    • Possible government investigations about vehicle emissions standards.
    From BorgWarner's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
