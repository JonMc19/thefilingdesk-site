    What C3.ai does

    C3.ai sells enterprise artificial intelligence software to large organizations. Its core product is a platform that customers use to build, run and manage AI applications without writing large amounts of code. It also sells ready-made applications for specific industries and uses, and tools that let AI agents retrieve data, analyze it and carry out workflows.

    The company has five product areas: the C3 Agentic AI Platform, C3 AI Studio (a workspace for building applications), C3 AI Applications, C3 Generative AI and C3 Code, which generates applications from plain-language instructions. Customers can run the software on major public clouds, private clouds or their own servers.

    It earns substantially all of its revenue from subscriptions to this software and from related support services. Historically, a small number of customers have accounted for a large part of its revenue. It also sells to government entities and works through partners. C3.ai says it faces intense competition in enterprise software.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses, with operating expenses expected to keep rising.
    • Reliance on a limited number of customers for a large share of revenue, and on their renewals.
    • Intense competition and fast technology change in enterprise AI.
    • Long, unpredictable sales cycles and the challenges of selling to government and regulated customers.
    • Whether its restructuring plan and its partner relationships deliver what it expects.
    From C3.ai's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
