    What Optimum Communications does

    Optimum Communications, known until November 2025 as Altice USA, provides broadband internet, video, telephone and mobile service to home and business customers in 21 states, under the Optimum brand. Its area is mainly the New York metropolitan region and parts of the south-central United States. It also offers news programming and advertising services.

    It earns money from monthly fees from its roughly 4.3 million residential and business customers, and from advertising. It delivers service over a network that mixes fiber and coaxial cable, and over a fiber-to-the-home network that it is still building. The company says it offers 1 Gbps speeds in most of its cable area and up to 8 Gbps in its fiber area. It competes with other broadband and video providers, some of which own or have deals with content providers.

    It is controlled by Patrick Drahi through Next Alt. It is a holding company that does not run business operations itself, and it was formed through its 2016 acquisitions of Cequel and Cablevision.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from other broadband and video providers.
    • Rapid changes in technology and in how customers watch video.
    • Rising programming and retransmission costs that it may not be able to pass on to customers.
    • Past losses, and debt that could make borrowing harder and costlier.
    • Limits on how subsidiaries can send funds to repay debt.
    From Optimum Communications's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
