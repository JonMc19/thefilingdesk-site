    What First Interstate BancSystem does

    First Interstate BancSystem is a bank holding company focused on community banking. Through its bank, First Interstate Bank, it takes deposits, makes loans, originates and services mortgages, and offers wealth management to individuals, businesses, government entities and others. It operated 290 banking offices in 12 states, mostly in the Mountain West and Upper Midwest.

    It reports one operating segment, community banking. Like other banks, it earns money mainly from the interest on loans and securities and from fees for its services. At the end of 2025 it had $26.6 billion in assets, $22.1 billion in deposits and $15.2 billion in loans held for investment. Commercial loans, including commercial real estate, are about 69.1% of that loan portfolio.

    The company is reshaping itself. It sold its banking operations in Arizona and Kansas in October 2025, plans to sell branches in Nebraska, exited indirect lending, outsourced its credit card portfolio and is moving to a flatter management structure.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak credit quality, with loans concentrated in commercial real estate and business borrowers.
    • Changes in interest rates that cut demand for loans or the value of its securities.
    • Losing deposits, or being unable to raise funding when it needs cash.
    • New or stricter bank rules, higher capital requirements and higher deposit insurance fees.
    • Problems from its plan to sell branches, exit markets and restructure management.
