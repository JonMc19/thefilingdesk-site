    What Lovesac does

    Lovesac designs and sells modular furniture. Its main product is the Sactional, a couch built from standardized pieces called seats and sides, with removable, washable covers that customers can swap and rearrange. It also sells oversized foam beanbag chairs called Sacs, the StealthTech surround sound system built into its couches, a recliner seat, and accessories.

    It sells directly to consumers through its own showrooms and its website, and it says delivering large upholstered items by express courier is unusual in the furniture industry. Sactionals made up 91.1% of net sales in fiscal 2026. The company holds patents on its core products and has the sound system made with Harman Kardon speakers.

    It relies on suppliers abroad for much of its manufacturing, and has started investing in some U.S. production.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to manage its growth effectively.
    • Failing to maintain its brand or to win new customers cost-effectively.
    • Dependence on foreign manufacturers and ocean shipping, which tariffs and port disruptions can hurt.
    • Rising raw material, sourcing and distribution costs.
    • Difficulty managing inventory across showrooms and online sales.
    From Lovesac's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
