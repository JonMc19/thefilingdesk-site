    What Bloomin' Brands does

    Bloomin' Brands owns and franchises restaurants. Its chains are Outback Steakhouse, Carrabba's Italian Grill, Bonefish Grill and Fleming's Prime Steakhouse & Wine Bar. They range from casual dining to fine dining.

    As of December 28, 2025, it owned and operated 967 restaurants and franchised 493 across 46 states, Guam and 12 countries. It treats each U.S. restaurant concept and each international franchise market as an operating segment and reports two segments: the U.S., which includes all U.S. restaurants, and international franchise, which covers franchised restaurants outside the U.S. In the U.S. segment, it owned and operated 957 restaurants and franchised 138.

    Outback is a casual steakhouse with Australian decor and a menu built around steaks, ribs, chicken and seafood, and it is known for its Bloomin' Onion appetizer. On December 30, 2024, the company sold the majority of its Brazil operations, and all restaurants there now run as unconsolidated franchisees. It says it competes with many other restaurants and prepared-food options.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Food safety and food-borne illness problems in its restaurants or the wider supply chain.
    • Strong competition in restaurants, and a growing range of other prepared food options.
    • Hiring, training and keeping restaurant managers and hourly team members.
    • Changing consumer preferences, and the fast effects of social media on its brands.
    • Suppliers or distributors that cannot meet their contracts.
    From Bloomin' Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
