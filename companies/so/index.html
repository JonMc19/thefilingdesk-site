    What Southern does

    Southern Company is a holding company for electric and natural gas businesses in the Southeast. Three of its subsidiaries, Alabama Power, Georgia Power and Mississippi Power, are regulated electric utilities. They own power plants and power lines and sell electricity to homes and businesses in their states and to wholesale customers. Southern Company Gas distributes natural gas in Illinois, Georgia, Virginia and Tennessee.

    Southern Power develops, builds and runs power plants, including battery storage, and sells electricity to wholesale buyers at market-based rates. Other subsidiaries run the system's nuclear plants, provide wireless communications and fiber services, and build microgrids and other local energy systems for businesses, governments and utilities. A service company provides shared services, such as engineering, legal and accounting, to the other subsidiaries.

    Since 2023 its electric companies have signed contracts with new data centers and other large customers covering about nine gigawatts of electric load. The 10-K lists new power plants and battery storage that its Georgia utility is building.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The high cost of complying with environmental laws, including limits on greenhouse gas emissions.
    • Risks of running nuclear plants, with costs that could exceed insurance or decommissioning funds.
    • Accidents and operating problems in producing and delivering electricity and natural gas.
    • Physical attacks and cyber attacks on its facilities and systems.
    • More competition from other energy suppliers and changes in how much energy customers want.
    From Southern's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
