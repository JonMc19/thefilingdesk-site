    What Hinge Health does

    Hinge Health sells a digital program for muscle and joint (MSK) care, covering injuries, chronic pain and recovery after surgery. Members use motion-tracking software and a wearable device that gives electrical nerve stimulation, and a care team of licensed physical therapists, physicians and health coaches, supported by AI, monitors the program.

    Its customers are mainly self-insured employers, including public-sector employers such as city governments and labor unions. It also serves health plans' fully insured and Medicare Advantage members, and federal insurance plans. It sells through its own sales force and through partners such as health plans and benefit managers.

    Hinge Health is usually the only digital MSK provider offered to a client's covered people, and its average contract term was three years at the end of 2025. It earns recurring revenue for the life of each contract, and it aims to reduce its clients' costs. It has a limited operating history.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It may not carry out its growth plans and strategies.
    • Clients may not renew their contracts, or may renew on worse terms.
    • It may fail to keep existing members or add new ones.
    • It pays upfront costs for each client and partner and may not recover them if relationships don't grow.
    • Competitors and other advances in treating joint and muscle conditions could limit its acceptance.
    From Hinge Health's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
