    What Sprouts Farmers Market does

    Sprouts Farmers Market is a grocery chain that focuses on fresh, natural and organic food. It is based in Phoenix and had 477 stores in 24 states at the end of December 2025. Shoppers buy produce, meat and seafood, deli and bakery items, dairy, packaged groceries, vitamins and supplements, bulk foods, frozen foods, beer and wine, and health and body care products. Sprouts also offers delivery and pickup through its website and app.

    All of its revenue comes from selling food and related goods to shoppers at its stores and online, and it reports one segment. Its stores put produce in the center, and it typically dedicates approximately 20% of selling space to it. Its own Sprouts Brand products made up just over 25% of revenue in fiscal 2025. Stores average about 28,000 square feet, and newer, smaller stores are between 21,000 and 25,000 square feet. Sprouts says it plans to keep opening stores.

    Sprouts buys from hundreds of vendors and runs six fresh distribution centers that handle nearly all of its produce. In 2025 it began moving meat and seafood distribution in-house too, and it says supply disruptions during that change led to availability problems. It names grocery chains and specialty natural food stores as competitors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from other grocery and natural food retailers that may cut into its sales and profit.
    • Changes in product and commodity prices and availability, including from tariffs.
    • Interruptions at its distribution centers or with its distributors, including its shift to self-distribution of meat and seafood.
    • Food safety concerns and product recalls that could lead to lawsuits and hurt its reputation.
    • Heavy concentration of its stores in certain regions, which exposes it to local downturns and disasters.
    From Sprouts Farmers Market's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
