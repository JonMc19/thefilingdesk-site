    What Church & Dwight does

    Church & Dwight makes household and personal care products. Its best-known brand is ARM & HAMMER, which began with baking soda in 1846 and now covers laundry detergent, cat litter, deodorizers and toothpaste. Other brands include OXICLEAN, BATISTE, HERO, THERABREATH, TROJAN, NAIR, WATERPIK and ZICAM. It sells through supermarkets, mass merchandisers, wholesale clubs, drugstores, discount stores, pet stores and online.

    It has three segments. Consumer Domestic made up 77% of its sales in 2025, Consumer International 18%, and the Specialty Products Division 5%. The Specialty Products Division sells to businesses and covers animal nutrition, specialty chemicals and commercial products. Its laundry detergents are its largest consumer business.

    The company makes baking soda at its own plants in Wyoming and Ohio, and it uses outside contract manufacturers for some products. It has also been exiting some businesses, including Spinbrush and its VITAFUSION and L'IL CRITTERS vitamins.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing a principal customer, since a few retailers account for a large part of its sales; Walmart is the largest.
    • Rising costs of raw materials and packaging that it may not be able to pass on in higher prices.
    • Shoppers turning to lower-priced private label products and stronger competitors.
    • Higher prices from inflation or tariffs reducing demand for its products.
    • Acquisitions and sales of businesses that may not work out as planned.
    From Church & Dwight's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
