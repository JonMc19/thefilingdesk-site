    What RideNow Group does

    RideNow Group is a retailer of powersports vehicles in the United States. It sells new and pre-owned motorcycles, all-terrain vehicles, side-by-side vehicles and personal watercraft, along with parts, apparel and accessories. It also sells finance and insurance products and does repair and maintenance work.

    It buys new vehicles from manufacturers such as BRP (Can-Am and Sea-Doo), Polaris, Harley-Davidson, Yamaha and Kawasaki under standard dealer agreements. It gets pre-owned vehicles from consumers, trade-ins and auctions, including through its online RideNow Cash Offer tool. The company says it competes with franchised dealers, independent pre-owned dealers and private sellers.

    At the end of 2025 it operated 48 dealerships in 12 states and was based in Chandler, Arizona. Until August 2025 it was called RumbleOn. It also ran a vehicle transportation brokerage business, which it stopped in December 2025, so it now operates only as a powersports dealer group.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not being able to buy enough new and pre-owned vehicles to meet demand.
    • Depending on manufacturers whose vehicles it sells and on the rules they set.
    • Relying on third-party lenders to finance many customers' purchases.
    • Heavy debt that needs a lot of cash to service.
    • Leases with companies controlled by its directors.
    From RideNow Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
