    What Global Payments does

    Global Payments is a payments technology company. It provides the services and software that let businesses accept card and other digital payments, and it runs business management software for them. It operates in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and Latin America and has approximately 26,000 employees, which it calls team members, and is based in Georgia.

    Through 2025 its main reporting segment was Merchant Solutions. The 10-K says most of its revenue comes from services priced as a percentage of the value of each transaction or as a set fee per transaction. It sells directly to customers and through other distribution channels. It competes with banks, merchant acquirers, financial technology companies and other payment providers, and it says some of them have more financial, sales and marketing resources.

    The company is changing shape. In January 2026 it acquired 100% of Worldpay Holdco, LLC (Worldpay) from Fidelity National Information Services. It also sold its Issuer Solutions business, now shown as discontinued operations, and its payroll business, which it sold for approximately $1.1 billion, including contingent payments. It is also running a transformation program to merge its technology, operations and brands.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from banks, payment providers and fintech companies, some with more resources.
    • The Worldpay acquisition and its transformation program may not deliver the results it expects.
    • Merging its technology, operations and brands could cost more or take longer than planned.
    • Security breaches or failures in the systems that process payments for its customers.
    • Changes in payment methods and technology could reduce demand for its services.
