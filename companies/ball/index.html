    What Ball does

    Ball makes aluminum packaging for drinks, personal care products and household goods. Its biggest product line is aluminum beverage cans. It also makes extruded aluminum aerosol containers, resealable aluminum bottles and aluminum slugs, which are small aluminum discs that are formed into containers. It produces them in plants around the world and sells them to large multinational beverage, personal care and household products companies.

    Ball earns its revenue by selling these containers, often under long-term supply contracts with customers it has served for years. It sells a significant part of its output to major companies and brands, as well as to many regional customers. In 2025 its total consolidated net sales were $13.16 billion. The company says aluminum packaging is its focus and that it competes with other packaging made from different materials.

    Ball is based in Westminster, Colorado, and had about 16,000 employees at the end of 2025, about 5,000 of them in the U.S. It reports a significant level of debt, with $7.01 billion of debt at December 31, 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing a key customer, or an adverse change in a key customer's requirements, since it sells to a limited number of large companies.
    • A significant level of debt that could limit its flexibility and cash available for the business.
    • Competition from other types of packaging that could lower its profit and cash flow.
    • A narrow product range, so weaker demand for aluminum packaging or the drinks in it would hurt it.
    • Risks from operating in many regions, including conflicts, tariffs and other trade measures.
