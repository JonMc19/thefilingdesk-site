    What Angi does

    Angi runs an online marketplace that connects consumers with home professionals, called Pros, in more than 500 categories, from repairs and remodeling to cleaning and landscaping. Its brands include Angi, Angie's List, HomeAdvisor and Handy. It says it had about 111,000 average monthly active Pros in the fourth quarter of 2025, and that consumers used its businesses to find a Pro for about 16 million projects over the year.

    Consumers use the matching service, booking of pre-priced services and directories free of charge once registered. Pros get tools to reach customers, quote and invoice. Pros are independent businesses, and the services are fulfilled by them.

    It reports two segments: the U.S., and International, which covers Europe and Canada. Angi became an independent public company on March 31, 2025, when IAC spun it off by distributing its Angi shares to IAC's holders. It was formed in 2017 by combining IAC's HomeAdvisor and Angie's List.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on internet search engines to send traffic, including search engines that offer competing services.
    • Marketing that may not bring in customers in a cost-effective way.
    • Keeping enough trustworthy Pros on the platform, and a legal challenge to Pros' status as independent contractors.
    • Its ability to access and use consumer data and to reach consumers and Pros by email.
    • A competitive, fast-changing industry, and the pace at which home services move online.
