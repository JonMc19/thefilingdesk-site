    What Flotek Industries does

    Flotek is a chemistry and data company that serves the oil and gas industry. It has two segments.

    The Chemistry Technologies segment designs, makes, packages and sells specialty chemicals to energy producers, for use in drilling and well completion. The company says these products help customers lower costs and reduce their environmental impact. The Data Analytics segment sells measuring equipment and digital services that give near real-time readings for controlling processes in oil and gas, and in newer uses such as power generation.

    In April 2025 Flotek bought mobile power generation equipment from ProFrac GDM, a subsidiary of ProFrac, a company that is also a customer. The deal covered twenty-two operating units and eight more under construction, which were all in service by December 31, 2025. Flotek then leased the equipment back to ProFrac GDM. Total consideration for these transactions was $107.5 million, which included offsetting part of a contract shortfall fee that ProFrac owed it.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in commodity prices that change how much its energy customers spend.
    • Reliance on its agreements with ProFrac affiliates, which could hurt its finances and cash flows.
    • Not being able to develop new products or set existing ones apart, which could cost it customers.
    • Losing key customers, or key suppliers and raw materials.
    • Increased competition that pushes down prices, and not having enough capital.
