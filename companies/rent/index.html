    What Rent the Runway does

    Rent the Runway rents out designer clothing and accessories online. Customers can join a subscription to its "unlimited closet," rent items one at a time through its Reserve option, or buy items through Resale. The range covers evening wear, workwear, denim, outerwear, jewelry, handbags and more.

    Subscribers drive most of the business. In fiscal year 2025, 90% of total revenue came from subscribers. It has served about 3.5 million customers since its founding in 2009. It gets virtually all of its products directly from, or with, designer brand partners.

    It says it competes in a highly competitive fashion industry. The company recently completed recapitalization transactions that included a new credit agreement with covenants, and it is going through a change in its board.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses and no certainty it will reach or keep profit.
    • Debt terms from a new credit agreement that limit its choices, and uncertain benefits of its recent recapitalization.
    • Customer spending on fashion falling when the economy weakens.
    • Misjudging fashion trends or buying the wrong products.
    • Shipping and logistics problems that hold up its deliveries.
    From Rent the Runway's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
