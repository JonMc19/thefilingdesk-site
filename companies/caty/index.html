    What Cathay General Bancorp does

    Cathay General Bancorp is the holding company for Cathay Bank, a California state-chartered commercial bank. The company was organized in 1990. Its main office is in Los Angeles. It is regulated as a bank holding company by the Federal Reserve, and the bank is regulated by California's financial regulator and the FDIC.

    Like other banks, Cathay takes deposits and makes loans and offers other banking services, and it earns the difference between what it charges on loans and pays on deposits, plus fees. Its loan portfolio is largely secured by real estate. At December 31, 2025 it had $24.23 billion in total consolidated assets.

    The company competes for loans and deposits with savings banks, credit unions, brokerage firms and other financial companies. In California, it says, one larger Chinese-American bank and at least two super-regional banks compete with it. It has limited partnerships investing in affordable housing and trusts that issued capital securities.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions in California and the other markets it serves.
    • A real estate downturn, because most of its loans are secured by real estate.
    • Conditions in Asia and elsewhere that could hurt its customers and business.
    • Having to set aside more for loans that are not repaid.
    • Interest rate changes that shrink what it earns from loans after paying for deposits.
