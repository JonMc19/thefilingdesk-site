    What Coherent does

    Coherent, based in Saxonburg, Pennsylvania, makes lasers, optical transceivers and other optical and optoelectronic devices, modules and systems, plus engineered materials. Transceivers are the parts that send data as light through fiber in data centers and networks. It describes itself as vertically integrated, meaning it makes much of what goes into its products, including specialty materials.

    Since July 1, 2025 it reports two segments: Datacenter & Communications, and Industrial. Industrial customers use its products in precision manufacturing, energy, semiconductor and display equipment, and instruments. Before that it reported three segments: Networking, Materials and Lasers. It earns nearly all of its revenue by developing, making and selling these products and related services.

    It sells to large service providers and product companies. Many products include software it writes itself. The 10-K says part of the company's business is cyclical and that it uses contract manufacturers for some production.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Having to keep developing new products and processes, which may take large investment.
    • A cyclical business whose market size and growth it may judge wrongly.
    • Large customers with strong bargaining power that can push on prices and terms.
    • Its reliance on contract manufacturers and on a few sources of key materials and parts.
    • Not getting the expected returns from investments such as added capacity.
    From Coherent's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
