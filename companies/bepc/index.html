    About these figures. Brookfield Renewable's shareholders' equity is negative at the end of 2025, so return on equity isn't meaningful and is left blank for that year. Brookfield Renewable Corporation is a foreign company: it files an annual report on Form 20-F, prepared under international accounting standards (IFRS), instead of a 10-K, and no quarterly reports, so this page has fiscal years only. Its shares trade in the US as American depositary shares (ADSs), and one ADS can stand for several ordinary shares, so per-share figures are per ordinary share and the page has no market value, P/E or valuation model.
