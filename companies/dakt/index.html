    What Daktronics does

    Daktronics designs, makes and sells electronic display systems for sports, commercial and transportation uses. Its products range from small scoreboards to large video displays in stadiums and arenas, and many include control, timing and audio systems and software that show live data, graphics and video.

    It sells both standard products and custom-designed systems. The company handles marketing and sales, engineering, manufacturing, installation and ongoing customer support itself. Besides equipment and installation, it earns money from technical support, professional services and software that customers use to manage their displays.

    Daktronics was founded in 1968 in Brookings, South Dakota, where it is still based, and became publicly traded in 1994. It employs 2,693 people worldwide, with manufacturing, sales and service in several regions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tariffs and trade policy changes that have affected, and may keep affecting, its results.
    • Disruptions or shortages in the supply of raw materials and components, and rising costs.
    • Weak economic conditions, which can lower demand for its display systems.
    • Reliance on third parties to complete some of its contracts.
    • Failing to protect its intellectual property, or infringing someone else's.
