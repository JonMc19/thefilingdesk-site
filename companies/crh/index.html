    What CRH does

    CRH supplies building materials and products for construction in North America, Europe and Australia. Its core materials are aggregates (crushed stone, sand and gravel) and cementitious materials such as cement. It also sells road construction materials and services, including paving, and other building products and services. Customers range from governments to contractors and other construction companies.

    The company says it generated $37.4 billion of total revenues in 2025. By end market, 40% of that came from infrastructure, 32% from residential construction and 28% from non-residential construction. Projects it serves include highways, bridges, rail, water, energy, telecom, factories and data centers.

    CRH grows by buying other companies. It says it has completed over 1,250 deals, including 38 acquisitions in 2025 for total consideration of $4.1 billion. The largest was Eco Material Technologies, a supplier of supplementary cementitious materials in North America. Its shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange and the London Stock Exchange.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower or delayed government spending on infrastructure.
    • Swings in construction activity and the economy.
    • Shortages of frontline workers in a labor-intensive business.
    • Losing access to mineral reserves, or failing to get or keep permits for its quarries.
    • Competition in commodity products, where it may be unable to hold prices.
    From CRH's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
