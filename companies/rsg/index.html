    What Republic Services does

    Republic Services collects, recycles and disposes of waste, and provides other environmental services, across the United States and Canada. It runs 377 collection operations, 255 transfer stations, 79 recycling centers and 207 active landfills, plus treatment and disposal sites for hazardous and industrial waste, 84 landfill gas-to-energy and other renewable energy projects, and two polymer centers. It is also responsible for 124 closed landfills.

    Collecting and recycling or disposing of material is largely a local business, so Republic combines local operating managers with standard business practices. It aims for a top market position in each market it serves, and may sell assets where it cannot, or where returns are poor. It grows by adding customers and services, through capital investment, and through acquisitions.

    Republic estimates the U.S. and Canadian environmental services market at about $163 billion a year. It also says it is investing in sustainability businesses tied to recycling and lower carbon use.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from rivals that may have more financial and operating resources.
    • Higher fuel and petrochemical costs that it may not be able to pass on to customers.
    • Swings in prices and demand for the recycled materials it sells.
    • Needing more capital than expected, and trouble getting or keeping permits and landfill capacity.
    • Mistakes in estimating landfill closure, post-closure and cleanup costs.
    From Republic Services's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
