    What BigBear.ai does

    BigBear.ai provides artificial intelligence software and services, mainly to U.S. defense and national security customers and to a few commercial partners. It combines AI with computer vision, predictive analytics and biometrics. Its core industries are national security, travel and trade, and it says most of its employees hold security clearances and often work alongside customers.

    The company says it completed the acquisition of Ask Sage on December 31, 2025. Ask Sage is a generative AI platform built for defense and intelligence users and authorized under FedRAMP, the government's cloud security program. BigBear.ai works with commercial partners on supply chain visibility and on streamlining processes and technical workflows.

    Its revenue comes from contracts, a mix of fixed-price and time-and-materials work. Most of its contracts allow the customer to terminate for convenience, and sales cycles are long.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Operating losses in the past, with no assurance of reaching or keeping profitability.
    • A limited operating history that makes its prospects hard to evaluate.
    • Customer contracts that can be ended early, and U.S. government options that may not be exercised.
    • Fixed-price contracts where misjudged costs could cut profit.
    • Large customers cutting spending, and long, unpredictable sales cycles.
    From BigBear.ai's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
