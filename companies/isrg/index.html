    What Intuitive Surgical does

    Intuitive Surgical makes robotic-assisted systems for minimally invasive surgery and diagnosis. Its main product is the da Vinci surgical system, first launched in 1999. A surgeon sits at a console and views a three-dimensional, high definition image, and the system translates hand movements into smaller, precise movements of instruments inside the patient. Models in use include the fifth-generation da Vinci 5, with force feedback technology, and the da Vinci X, Xi and SP.

    It also sells the Ion endoluminal system, which extends its offerings beyond surgery into diagnostic procedures such as lung biopsies. Beyond the systems themselves, it sells instruments and accessories, customer learning, and customer support services, all linked by a digital portfolio.

    Intuitive offers usage-based arrangements as well as sales of systems. Its customers are hospitals, which depend on coverage and reimbursement for procedures that use its products. It says the commercial landscape is highly competitive and the company is more than 30 years old.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition, and customers choosing rival products or not accepting robotic-assisted procedures.
    • Hospitals being unable to get coverage and reimbursement for procedures with its products.
    • Its reliance on sole- and single-source suppliers for key materials.
    • Usage-based arrangements that expose it to a higher risk of lost revenue.
    • Legal proceedings and investigations over its products, customers and competitors.
    From Intuitive Surgical's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
