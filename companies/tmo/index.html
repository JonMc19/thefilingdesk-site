    What Thermo Fisher Scientific does

    Thermo Fisher Scientific sells instruments, supplies and services to scientists and laboratories. Its customers include drug and biotech companies, hospitals and clinical labs, universities, research institutes, government agencies, and industrial and environmental testing operations. Its brands include Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, Patheon and PPD.

    It has four segments. Life Sciences Solutions sells reagents, instruments and consumables for biological research, drug and vaccine development and the production of biologic drugs. Analytical Instruments sells instruments such as mass spectrometers and electron microscopes, with the supplies, software and services that go with them. Specialty Diagnostics sells diagnostic test kits, reagents, culture media and instruments. Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services sells lab supplies, equipment and chemicals, and offers outsourced services to drug makers, including clinical trials and commercial drug manufacturing.

    The company says it grows through investment in research, capacity and acquisitions, and through its global sales channels.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Slow or falling growth in the research and drug markets it sells into, which can be cyclical.
    • Currency swings, tariffs and political risk in its many international markets.
    • Failing to complete or successfully absorb acquisitions.
    • Reliance on single or limited suppliers for some materials and parts.
    • Its debt could limit what it can invest in and affect its credit ratings.
