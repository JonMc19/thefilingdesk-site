    What Polaris does

    Polaris designs, builds and sells powersports vehicles: off-road vehicles, including all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) and side-by-side vehicles, plus military and commercial versions, snowmobiles, three-wheeled moto-roadsters, quadricycles and boats. It also makes or sources parts, garments and accessories. Customers use the vehicles for recreation such as trail riding, hunting and fishing, and for work on farms, ranches and construction sites.

    It reports three segments: Off Road, On Road and Marine. Polaris sells online and through independent dealers and distributors, mainly in the United States, Canada, Western Europe, Australia and Mexico. In North America it has about 2,400 independent dealers. Outside North America it sells through 25 subsidiaries to more than 1,500 independent dealers and 70 distributors. Many of them also carry competitors' products.

    Its off-road lineup includes the RZR, RANGER, GENERAL and Sportsman brands. It says its competition in off-road vehicles comes mainly from North American and Asian manufacturers and is based on price, quality, reliability, styling, features and warranties. It has been in business since 1954.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker demand for its products when the economy or customer spending is weak.
    • Tariffs and other trade matters, and higher costs or shortages of raw materials and parts.
    • Intense competition in all its product lines, and the need to keep launching products customers want.
    • Disruption at its suppliers or at the few North American plants where it makes its products.
    • Product recalls and warranty claims that could bring large repair and replacement costs.
    From Polaris's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
