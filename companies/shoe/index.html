    What Shoe Station Group does

    Shoe Carnival sells shoes and accessories for the whole family through stores and websites. It says it is one of the nation's largest omnichannel retailers of footwear, meaning it sells through stores and online together. It carries name brands such as Nike, Skechers, Crocs, adidas, Puma, HOKA and Birkenstock. At the end of its 2025 fiscal year it ran 426 stores in 35 states and Puerto Rico.

    It has two store banners. Shoe Carnival has 282 stores and serves moderate- to low-income families with value-oriented brands, in a lively store with in-store contests and specials. Shoe Station has 144 stores and aims at a more affluent shopper, with a wider range of higher-priced athletic and non-athletic shoes. It bought its first Shoe Station stores in 2021 and Rogan's, a work and family shoe retailer, in fiscal 2024. It is converting some Shoe Carnival stores into Shoe Station stores, a plan it calls rebannering.

    It earns money by selling shoes. Online sales were about a tenth of merchandise sales. Online orders are mostly filled from store stock, and a distribution center in Evansville, Indiana, helps at busy times.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its plan to convert Shoe Carnival stores to Shoe Station may not deliver the expected sales or savings.
    • Tariffs and trade changes affecting China and other countries where most footwear is made.
    • Dependence on a few major brands and on suppliers delivering on time.
    • Heavy competition and pressure to keep up the promotions that draw shoppers.
    • Problems with its computer systems or its Evansville distribution center.
