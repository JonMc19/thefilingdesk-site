    What Flagstar Bank does

    Flagstar is a national bank headquartered in Hicksville, New York. At the end of 2025 it had $87.5 billion of assets, $61.0 billion of loans and $66.0 billion of deposits. It operates about 340 locations in nine states, with a strong base in the New York and New Jersey area and the upper Midwest, plus a presence in Florida and on the West Coast. About 20 private bank branches serve wealthy individuals and their businesses.

    The bank takes deposits and makes loans, and it reports one business segment. Its loans include multi-family loans, most of them secured by rental apartment buildings in New York City, commercial real estate loans in the Northeast and Midwest, and commercial and industrial loans to mid-sized companies across the country. It also offers mortgages, investment products and insurance. It competes with other banks, credit unions, online banks and fintech companies.

    In October 2025 the bank became the reporting company itself, after its former holding company, Flagstar Financial, was merged into it.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates that could cut the income it earns from loans and lower the value of its assets.
    • Heavy lending concentrated in multi-family and commercial real estate loans.
    • Possible changes to New York State rules for multi-family loans, and weakness in the New York City economy.
    • An allowance for credit losses that may not cover its actual loan losses.
    • Relying on models and internal controls that could fail or prove wrong.
    From Flagstar Bank's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2022, earnings per share grew 3.7% a year and net income 4.6% a year, with the share count about the same. Flagstar Bank then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
