    What Designer Brands does

    Designer Brands, originally DSW Inc., designs, produces and sells footwear and accessories. It reports two segments: Retail and Brand Portfolio.

    The Retail segment sells dress, casual and athletic footwear and accessories for women, men and kids in stores and online. Its banners are DSW Designer Shoe Warehouse in the U.S. and Canada, The Shoe Co. in Canada, and Rubino in Quebec. It bought Rubino on April 8, 2024. Online orders can be filled from stores, directly by suppliers, or from a New Jersey distribution center it shares with the Brand Portfolio segment.

    The Brand Portfolio segment mostly earns revenue by selling its exclusive and licensed brands wholesale to retailers, to its own Retail segment and to international distributors. It also sells its Vince Camuto, Keds and Topo brands directly to consumers on e-commerce sites. Beginning with this annual report it combined its U.S. and Canada retail segments into one, and it recast earlier periods to match.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker economic conditions or consumer confidence that cut discretionary spending.
    • Changes to U.S. tariff policy.
    • Strong competition in footwear, and changing consumer preferences and fashion trends.
    • Relying on suppliers to provide enough merchandise at good prices.
    • Losses or disruptions at its distribution centers and stores, or in IT services.
    From Designer Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 30, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2023, earnings per share fell 15.6% a year and net income 18.7% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 23%. Designer Brands then lost money in fiscal 2024 and 2025.
