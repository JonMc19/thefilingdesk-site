    What Lumentum does

    Lumentum makes optical and photonic products: lasers and the parts and equipment that send data as light. Its customers include cloud data center operators, AI infrastructure providers, telecommunications network operators and equipment makers, and manufacturers that use lasers to cut or shape materials. It also makes laser emitters for 3D sensing in consumer electronics.

    It reports one business segment and splits revenue into two product types. Components are building blocks such as laser chips and subsystems that customers build into their own equipment. Systems are finished products such as optical modules, optical circuit switches and industrial lasers. Its products carry data in networks that range from local links to undersea cables, and in data centers and corporate networks.

    Lumentum was spun off from JDS Uniphase in 2015 and is based in San Jose, California, with factories in North America, Asia-Pacific and Europe. It has grown through purchases, including Oclaro in 2018 and Cloud Light in 2023. It says demand from AI and cloud customers is outpacing its current supply, so it is adding manufacturing capacity, both its own and through contract manufacturers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on a few customers for much of its sales, most of whom have no contractual purchase commitments.
    • Depending on a limited number of suppliers for raw materials, packages and components.
    • Intense competition that may push down prices, margins and market share.
    • Limits on selling to a previously significant customer.
    • Tariffs, trade restrictions and other risks of working in many countries.
    From Lumentum's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
