    What MasTec does

    MasTec is a North American infrastructure engineering and construction company. It designs, builds, installs, maintains and upgrades communications networks, energy and utility systems and other infrastructure. That includes wireless and fiber networks, power lines, renewable power plants, data center and industrial projects, and pipelines. At the end of 2025 it had approximately 36,000 employees and 810 locations.

    MasTec earns its money by doing this work for customers under contracts, and it has five reportable segments: Communications; Clean Energy and Infrastructure; Power Delivery; Pipeline Infrastructure; and Other. The Other segment holds certain equity investees and small business units with activities in some international markets. In 2025 it moved a utility operations component from Communications to Power Delivery, which changed the segment figures but not its total results.

    The company says demand should come from federal infrastructure programs, including funding for broadband, clean energy and grid upgrades, and from rising electricity demand and aging power grids. It also says it expects growth in natural gas and water pipelines. These are the company's own expectations, not facts about the future.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tariffs and trade actions that could raise costs or disrupt work.
    • Misjudging the cost of work done under fixed price contracts, which can cut profit.
    • Backlog that may be cancelled or adjusted and so may not turn into revenue or profit.
    • Subcontractors and suppliers that may fail to meet their obligations.
    • Obligations tied to its unionized workforce, plus the risks of acquisitions and joint ventures.
    From MasTec's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
