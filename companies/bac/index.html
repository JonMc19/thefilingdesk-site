    Bank of America's revenue rose 6.8% to $113.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $30.5 billion, up 13.1%. Its return on equity was 10.2%, up from 9.2% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 61.6%, down from 63.1% a year earlier. It paid out $31.0 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 3.2%.
