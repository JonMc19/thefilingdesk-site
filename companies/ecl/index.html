    What Ecolab does

    Ecolab sells water treatment, cleaning, sanitizing and infection prevention products and services to businesses. Its customers include food and beverage processors, hotels and restaurants, hospitals, factories, power plants, refineries, data centers and drugmakers. It says it has $16 billion in annual sales and customers in more than 170 countries.

    It earns money by selling chemicals, equipment and programs, and by providing on-site service. It reports four segments. Global Water treats water for industrial customers and cleans and sanitizes plants. Global Institutional & Specialty sells cleaners and sanitizers to restaurants, hotels and healthcare. Global Pest Elimination sells services to find and remove pests such as rodents and insects. Global Life Sciences serves drug and personal care makers, including through its Purolite purification products. About 47% of its 2025 net sales came from outside the United States.

    Ecolab follows a "One Ecolab" strategy, in which one customer may buy from several of its segments. It says it also aims to help customers use less water and energy.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economies or downturns in markets it serves, such as foodservice, hospitality, travel and healthcare, can reduce customers' purchases.
    • Its large non-U.S. business exposes it to currency swings, trade rules and political and legal risks.
    • Tariffs, trade restrictions and supply chain problems can raise its costs.
    • Changing prices and supply of its raw materials, which it may not be able to pass on in prices.
    • Trouble integrating the companies it buys could harm its business.
    From Ecolab's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
