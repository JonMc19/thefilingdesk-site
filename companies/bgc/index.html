    What BGC Group does

    BGC Group is a global brokerage and financial technology company. It helps large financial firms trade with each other and takes a fee for the trades it arranges. It specializes in energy, commodities and shipping (which it calls ECS), such as listed derivatives, oil and refined products, environmental markets and ship chartering. It also brokers government and corporate bonds, interest rate and credit derivatives, foreign exchange, equities, and futures and options.

    Its clients include banks, broker-dealers, trading firms, hedge funds, governments, corporations and commodity traders. Trades happen by voice, by hybrid mixes of voice and screens, or fully electronically. The company also sells market data, network and connectivity services, and post-trade services. Its technology arm is called Fenics, which includes FMX, a U.S. interest rate futures exchange, a cash U.S. Treasuries platform and a spot foreign exchange platform. Several large banks and trading firms hold minority stakes in FMX.

    BGC grew out of the brokerage business of Cantor, which began its wholesale brokerage operations in 1972. It has offices in dozens of cities worldwide, with 2,510 brokers, salespeople, managers and other front-office staff at the end of 2025. It has grown partly through acquisitions, including GFI, Poten & Partners and OTC Global.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower trading volumes in financial markets, which have cut its revenue and profit before.
    • Central bank actions on interest rates and other market conditions that affect trading.
    • Failing to find and use new products, markets and hires, and the risks of acquisitions.
    • Technology and regulatory risks of its offerings linked to cryptocurrencies.
    • Variable-rate borrowings, and credit rating downgrades that raise its borrowing costs.
    From BGC Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
