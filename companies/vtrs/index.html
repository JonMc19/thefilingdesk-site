    What Viatris does

    Viatris makes and sells medicines. Its portfolio includes generic drugs (including complex products), well-known brands and a smaller group of newer medicines. Customers include retail and pharmacy chains, wholesalers, governments, institutions and doctors. The company says its medicines reach about 1 billion patients each year, in more than 165 countries and territories.

    It makes money by selling these medicines. It reports four segments by region: Developed Markets, Emerging Markets, JANZ (Japan, Australia and New Zealand) and Greater China. Its revenue was $14.30 billion in 2025. Over the last few years it has sold several businesses, including its women's healthcare business, its India-based active ingredients business and its over-the-counter business, to simplify the company and pay down debt.

    Viatris has 27 manufacturing, packaging and distribution sites and more than 30,000 employees. It is headquartered in the United States, with global centers in Pittsburgh, Shanghai and Hyderabad.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strategic plans and business sales that may not deliver the benefits it expects.
    • Pressure on drug prices and reimbursement from governments, insurers and large buyers.
    • Health care reform laws that could reduce what it is paid or change how its medicines are covered.
    • Counterfeit and infringing medicines entering the drug supply.
    • Failing to launch new products on time, and the heavy regulation of the drug industry.
    From Viatris's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
