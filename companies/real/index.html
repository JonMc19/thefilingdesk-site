    What TheRealReal does

    The RealReal runs an online marketplace for authenticated, mostly pre-owned luxury goods. It sells women's and men's fashion, jewelry and watches from brands such as Cartier, Chanel, Gucci, Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Prada and Rolex. Sellers consign their items, and buyers pay for authenticated goods.

    It earns money from the sale of items, and pays consignors a share of the proceeds. The company says more consignors bring more supply, which draws more buyers.

    It also runs neighborhood stores of roughly 1,800 to 3,500 square feet, where consignors can drop off items, and a few larger flagship stores in San Francisco, Los Angeles and New York City. It says the resale market is fragmented and has many counterfeits, which is why it authenticates items.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses and no certainty it will reach or keep profit.
    • Not getting enough new supply of pre-owned luxury goods, or losing brand partnerships.
    • Spending on luxury goods falling when the economy weakens.
    • Failing to win new buyers and keep repeat ones.
    • Returns higher than the amount it sets aside for them.
