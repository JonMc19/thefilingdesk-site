    What Torrid does

    Torrid sells clothing, intimates and accessories designed for women who wear sizes 10 to 30. Its range includes tops, bottoms, denim, dresses, intimates, activewear and footwear. The products are sold under its own brand, through its website and its stores. Torrid says each item is fitted on a real woman rather than scaled up from non-plus-size designs.

    It sells through two channels, e-commerce and stores. As of January 31, 2026 it operated 483 stores in the U.S., Puerto Rico and Canada, mainly in malls, shopping plazas, lifestyle centers and outlet locations. It describes its approach as a unified commerce platform in which customers can shop across both channels. It also runs a customer loyalty program that lets it tie sales to individual customers.

    Torrid splits its range into Basics, which are year-round styles that it keeps restocking, Core items, which update the Basics, and trend items that it buys in small amounts and reorders only if demand is there. It focuses on bottoms and intimates.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak consumer spending, inflation or a slowdown could hurt sales.
    • Misjudging customer preferences, or failing to keep a strong brand among its target customers.
    • Competition from other brands and retailers.
    • Reliance on third parties, such as search and social sites, to bring traffic to its website.
    • Store sales depend on the shopping centers where its stores are located.
    From Torrid's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 31, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
