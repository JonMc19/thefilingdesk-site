    What Novo Nordisk does

    Novo Nordisk is a Danish healthcare company that makes and sells medicines for obesity, diabetes and rare diseases. Its portfolio includes glucagon-like-peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists for obesity and diabetes, and modern and human insulins for diabetes. It employs more than 69,500 people in around 80 countries and markets its products in approximately 170 countries.

    For obesity it markets three drugs: Saxenda and Wegovy, which comes in injectable formats, and a Wegovy pill that launched in the first quarter of 2026. Its biggest product by sales is Ozempic, for type 2 diabetes. In 2025, Wegovy revenue grew 36%, to DKK 79 billion.

    Novo Nordisk says that, because of increased competition in the US and other markets, it lost volume market share in the GLP-1 markets for both obesity and diabetes in 2025. It also had periodic supply constraints for some products, including Ozempic, although supply improved through the year as it expanded manufacturing. More than 10,000 employees work in research and development.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing market share in GLP-1 medicines to competing products in the US and other markets.
    • Pricing pressure in obesity and diabetes care, which it expects to continue in 2026.
    • The expiry of certain patents, which could affect sales starting in 2026.
    • Supply constraints for key products such as Ozempic.
    • Cybersecurity breaches that could disrupt its systems or expose its intellectual property.
    From Novo Nordisk's annual report on Form 20-F, filed February 4, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
