    What Tripadvisor does

    Tripadvisor runs online travel platforms under three main brands: Viator, Tripadvisor and TheFork. People use them to search for, book and review experiences such as tours, as well as hotels and restaurants. The Tripadvisor site also carries traveler-written reviews and travel planning tools.

    It reports three segments and earns money differently in each. Experiences, which covers Viator and Tripadvisor's experience listings, earns mainly commissions on completed bookings. Hotels and Other, mostly Tripadvisor's hotel and restaurant guidance and hotel metasearch, earns mainly from click-based advertising, plus some impression-based ads. TheFork, a restaurant booking marketplace in Europe, earns fees per seated diner and subscription fees from restaurants for its reservation software.

    The company says its strategy is to grow Experiences and TheFork while managing Hotels and Other for profit. It is exploring strategic alternatives for TheFork as part of a broader portfolio review. At the end of 2025 it had approximately 2,590 employees, and most of them were in Europe.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strategic plans, including restructuring and a review of TheFork's future, may not deliver the expected benefits.
    • Drawing enough visitors and turning them into paying customers, partly because it relies on search engines.
    • Competition from large travel companies and search engines that offer rival products.
    • Heavy dependence on search engine traffic, where providers may change the rules or compete with it.
    • Legal and regulatory demands on its platforms, and risks to its information security.
    From Tripadvisor's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
