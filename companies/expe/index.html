    What Expedia Group does

    Expedia Group runs online travel booking sites and apps. Travelers use them to book hotels, vacation rentals, flights, rental cars, cruises and activities. At the end of 2025 it offered approximately 3.6 million lodging properties, including a large number of vacation rentals on Vrbo. Its main consumer brands are Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo.

    It reports three segments. B2C sells to travelers through its consumer brands. B2B sells its travel supply and technology to other companies, such as airlines, travel agents and banks, which offer travel to their own customers. trivago, which it majority owns, is a hotel search site that earns advertising revenue by sending visitors to booking sites.

    In 2025 total revenue was $14.7 billion. Merchant sales, where Expedia collects payment and is the seller of record, were 70% of revenue. Agency sales, where it earns a commission or fee from the supplier or traveler, were 22%, and advertising, media and other was 8%. Expedia says its competitors include other online travel agencies, vacation rental sites, airlines and hotels selling directly, and large online companies in search, social media and generative AI.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Declines or disruptions in travel, caused by the economy, conflicts, health crises or weather.
    • Strong competition from other online travel companies, travel suppliers selling directly and large online platforms.
    • Its dependence on travel suppliers and on search engines and other sources of traffic.
    • Losses from fraudulent bookings, a risk that AI-driven booking tools could increase.
    • Problems integrating acquisitions or the added risks of its international operations.
    From Expedia Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
