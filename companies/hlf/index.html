    What Herbalife does

    Herbalife sells nutrition products: weight management items such as meal replacement shakes and teas, targeted nutrition supplements, energy and sports products, and skin and hair care. At December 31, 2025 it sold 144 product types in 95 markets. It does not sell mainly through stores. It uses direct selling: a network of independent Members, some who buy for their own use and some who resell or build their own sales organizations.

    Weight management is its largest category, followed by targeted nutrition. Its best-selling line, Formula 1 Nutritional Shake Mix, approximated 25% of net sales.

    The company gives Members coaching and support, and says its Members provide personalized support to their customers. It is subject to a Consent Order with the FTC and to extensive rules covering its products and network marketing program.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep its Members and sales leaders, who sell its products and are not employees.
    • Members' conduct and bad publicity about the company or direct selling.
    • One product line, Formula 1, makes up a significant part of net sales.
    • Rules on network marketing and its Consent Order with the FTC.
    • A large part of its business is outside the U.S., including in China, with its own legal and economic risks.
    From Herbalife's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
