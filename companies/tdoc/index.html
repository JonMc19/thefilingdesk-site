    What Teladoc Health does

    Teladoc Health provides virtual care: doctor and therapy visits by video, phone, app and text. It sells to employers, health insurers, hospitals and health systems, and also directly to consumers. It says it completed 17.1 million telehealth visits in 2025 across its business and consumer channels.

    It reports two segments. Integrated Care sells mostly to employers and insurers, and gives members access to services such as primary care, urgent care, mental health, chronic condition care and expert second opinions. For hospitals and health systems it also sells hardware, software and services for telehealth. BetterHelp is its online counseling and therapy service, which uses a network of nearly 35,000 licensed clinicians.

    The company says it uses data, AI and human expertise to guide members to the care they need, and refers them to in-person care when appropriate.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a history of cumulative losses and may never reach lasting profitability.
    • A large part of its revenue comes from a few clients, and losing one could hurt it badly.
    • It may have to write down the value of goodwill and other intangible assets, which would reduce its reported results.
    • The virtual care market may grow more slowly than expected or its services may not draw enough member use.
    • It pays large upfront costs to win clients and may not earn them back if clients leave.
    From Teladoc Health's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
