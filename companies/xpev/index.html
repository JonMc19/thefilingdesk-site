    What XPeng does

    XPeng is a Chinese company that designs, develops, makes and sells electric vehicles, which it calls Smart EVs and NEVs (new energy vehicles). It aims its cars mainly at middle-class buyers in China and is expanding in other countries, focusing on mid- to high-end passenger vehicles.

    It sells several models, including the sedans MONA M03 and P7+, as well as the G6, the G7 and the G9, in battery-electric versions and, for some models, versions with a gasoline range extender (EREV). It writes its own driver-assistance software, called XNGP, and builds much of its key technology in-house.

    The deliveries of its vehicles rose from 141,601 units in 2023 to 190,068 units in 2024, and further to 429,445 units in 2025. Volkswagen invested in the company under a 2023 share purchase agreement, and the two companies also have a technical framework agreement for working together on technology.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its limited operating history as a newer maker of electric vehicles and the strain of managing fast growth.
    • Intense competition in China's passenger vehicle market, where demand for electric vehicles may be cyclical and volatile.
    • Dependence on a limited number of vehicle models for its revenue.
    • Risks tied to driver-assistance software, and research that may not produce the expected results.
    • Shortages of semiconductors, which could disrupt its operations.
