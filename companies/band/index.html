    What Bandwidth does

    Bandwidth sells cloud communications services to businesses and software companies. Customers use its application programming interfaces (APIs), which are software connections, to build voice calling, text messaging, emergency calling and AI voice features into their own apps and services. It owns and runs its own network, which spans more than 65 countries.

    It reports one segment and sells through three offerings. Global Voice Plans supplies calling for providers of cloud phone and contact center software, and the company names Microsoft, Google, Zoom, Cisco, RingCentral, Genesys and Five9 among them. It also sells messaging services to platforms and direct voice and messaging services to large enterprises. It is also pushing into AI voice, with a platform called Maestro that helps businesses run AI voice agents.

    Bandwidth says it is the only provider of this kind of platform service that also owns and operates its own global cloud communications network. It competes in a crowded market with other communications platforms and carriers. It has about 1,100 employees, mostly in the United States, Europe and Asia Pacific.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition in a crowded market, and the cost of winning new customers.
    • A large share of its revenue comes from a limited number of large customers.
    • Its newer services, such as AI voice, may not gain acceptance or may bring legal and reputation problems.
    • Litigation over taxes and charges tied to its 911 emergency calling services.
    • Changing communications regulation, including rules on emergency calling.
    From Bandwidth's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2019, earnings per share fell 59.5% a year and net income 51.9% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 85% and diluted each share's earnings. Bandwidth then lost money in fiscal 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
