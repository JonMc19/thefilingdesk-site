    What Trupanion does

    Trupanion sells medical insurance for cats and dogs in the United States, Canada and some countries in Continental Europe. Pet owners pay a monthly premium, which the company calls a subscription payment, and the insurance helps cover veterinary bills when a pet becomes sick or injured. It prices each policy using the pet's own characteristics and the level of coverage chosen.

    It reports two segments. The subscription business, its core, earns revenue mainly from premiums paid by pet owners, including some "Powered by Trupanion" products sold by other companies, and cheaper products in Canada and Germany and Switzerland. The other business mostly underwrites policies for third parties, with its biggest relationship being Pets Best Insurance Services; it expects that enrollment from Pets Best will keep falling as that company uses other insurers. This segment earns lower margins and is not part of its core strategy.

    Trupanion finds customers through Territory Partners, who are independent contractors, and through veterinarians and other partners. It also offers veterinary hospitals software that lets them be paid directly by the company.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has lost money since it began and may not become or stay profitable.
    • It spends heavily to win new pets, and may not earn that money back if its estimates are wrong.
    • Its prices rest on assumptions about vet costs, and actual claims could be higher than it expected.
    • It depends on Territory Partners, veterinarians and other third parties to find customers.
    • It must keep enough capital to meet insurance regulators' minimum requirements.
