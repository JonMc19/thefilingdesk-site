    What Centerspace Trust does

    Centerspace is a real estate investment trust (REIT) organized in North Dakota. It owns, manages, buys, develops and redevelops apartment communities. It focuses on the Midwest and Mountain West, with an emphasis on markets such as Minneapolis/St. Paul, Denver, Boulder/Fort Collins and Salt Lake City. At the end of 2025 it owned 61 apartment communities.

    It earns money mainly from the rent that residents pay for its apartment homes. It says it is working to improve residents' experience, redevelop some existing communities to match current demand, and buy new communities in large markets. Its headquarters is in Minot, North Dakota, with a corporate office in Minneapolis.

    The company says its Board of Trustees is conducting a review of strategic alternatives, which could include a sale, a merger or other business combination, or continuing as an independent company. It says it has no commitments to any transaction.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The strategic alternatives review may not lead to a transaction, or to one on acceptable terms.
    • Having all its holdings in one kind of property, apartments, in a few regions.
    • Struggling to buy, develop or lease up new projects on schedule and on budget.
    • Difficulty borrowing or meeting debt covenants when credit markets are tight.
    • Needing to cut the distributions it pays on its common shares.
    From Centerspace Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
