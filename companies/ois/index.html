    What Oil States International does

    Oil States International, based in Houston, makes equipment and provides services for customers in the energy, military and industrial sectors. Its customers include national, major and independent oil and gas companies, offshore drilling companies, other oilfield service companies, and defense and industrial buyers.

    It has three segments. Offshore Manufactured Products makes highly engineered capital equipment and consumable products for offshore projects and also serves defense and industrial uses. Completion and Production Services provides well completion and production services, mainly on land in the United States. Downhole Technologies makes tools and products used inside wells, and contributed 18% of revenue in 2025. In each segment it competes with other companies, from large oilfield service firms to many smaller ones.

    At the end of 2025 it had 2,172 full-time employees, most of them in the offshore products segment, and collective bargaining agreements covered about 450 employees outside the United States. The company says it has restructured its U.S. land-based businesses because those service lines are highly cyclical, and that it looks for growth offshore and abroad.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to compete with other companies in its industry, including as customers and rivals consolidate.
    • Losing one of the significant customers each segment depends on, or a customer that cannot pay.
    • Backlog in Offshore Manufactured Products can be adjusted or canceled unexpectedly.
    • Supply chain disruptions and tariffs that raise the cost of materials and imports.
    • Failing to develop new competitive technologies and products.
