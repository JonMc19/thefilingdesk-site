    What Eastman Chemical does

    Eastman Chemical makes specialty materials used in everyday products, such as plastics, films, coatings, textiles and personal and home care items. It is based in Kingsport, Tennessee, and has 36 manufacturing sites plus stakes in four manufacturing joint ventures, in 12 countries.

    It reports four segments: Advanced Materials, Additives & Functional Products, Chemical Intermediates and Fibers. In 2025 it reported sales revenue of $8.8 billion, and about 55 percent of it came from outside the United States and Canada.

    Eastman says it has moved from commodity chemicals toward specialty products since 2004. It has invested in molecular recycling, which breaks down plastic waste into building blocks for new products, and began running what it calls the world's largest polyester molecular recycling facility in 2024.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the cost of raw materials and energy, or disruptions in supplying and shipping them.
    • Operating risks common to chemical plants that could disrupt production.
    • Growth projects that may not reach their goals or may cost more than planned.
    • Risks from running large operations in many countries.
    • Climate-change and environmental rules that could raise its compliance costs.
    From Eastman Chemical's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
