    What Cardinal Health does

    Cardinal Health is a healthcare services and products company based in Dublin, Ohio. It supplies hospitals, health systems, pharmacies, surgery centers, clinical laboratories, physician offices and patients at home with drugs and medical products, along with services to run the healthcare supply chain more efficiently.

    It has two reportable segments. Pharmaceutical and Specialty Solutions distributes branded and generic drugs, specialty drugs, and over-the-counter products in the United States. It also provides services to drug makers and healthcare providers, runs a few pharmacies, and supports physician-office management platforms. Global Medical Products and Distribution makes, sources and distributes Cardinal Health brand medical, surgical and laboratory products, and distributes other brands to hospitals and surgery centers in the United States and Canada.

    Other businesses include Nuclear and Precision Health Solutions, which prepares radiopharmaceuticals for imaging and treatment; at-Home Solutions, which supplies medical products for people with chronic conditions at home; and OptiFreight Logistics, which handles shipping for healthcare providers. Revenue for fiscal 2026 was $254.2 billion, led by growth in branded and specialty drug sales.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strict licensing and regulatory requirements, with the risk of lost licenses, recalls or penalties.
    • Government investigations into healthcare fraud and abuse laws, including a Department of Justice inquiry into a 2022 deal.
    • Uncertain results from its generic drug program, which have hurt profit in some past years.
    • Changes in healthcare policy, such as the 340B drug pricing program.
    • Disruption to its operations or systems from weather, cyber incidents or third-party failures.
    From Cardinal Health's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
