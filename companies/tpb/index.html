    What Turning Point Brands does

    Turning Point Brands makes, markets and distributes tobacco and alternative smoking products to adult consumers. It has two reportable segments. Zig-Zag sells rolling papers, tubes, finished cigars, make-your-own cigar wraps and other accessories, and it holds a majority stake in a Canadian marketing and distribution firm. Stoker's makes and sells moist snuff tobacco, markets modern oral nicotine products under the FRE and ALP names, and markets loose-leaf chewing tobacco.

    The company says Zig-Zag is the number one rolling paper in the U.S. by market share, at approximately 33% according to a third-party data firm. It sells to about 900 distributors, who supply about 600 secondary wholesalers. At the end of 2025 its products were in approximately 220,000 retail locations in North America, including convenience stores and other traditional retailers, and it also sells online.

    It makes its Stoker's snuff in Tennessee. It relies on suppliers for some products, including rolling papers sourced through a relationship with Republic Technology International and loose-leaf chewing tobacco made by Swedish Match. It sold off its former Creative Distribution Solutions segment on January 2, 2025, in exchange for a minority stake in the buyer. At the end of 2025 it had 484 full-time and part-time employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Sales of tobacco products are generally expected to keep declining.
    • Its reliance on a small number of outside suppliers and producers for its products.
    • Strict and growing regulation, including the FDA's broad powers over tobacco and nicotine products.
    • Competition from much larger tobacco companies, and from illicit or unauthorized products.
    • The risk that licences to use certain brands and trademarks are ended or not renewed.
    From Turning Point Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
