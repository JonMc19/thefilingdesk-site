    What Eaton does

    Eaton is a power management company. It makes electrical equipment and systems for data centers, utilities, industrial and commercial buildings, machine builders, homes, aircraft and vehicles. Founded in 1911, it serves customers in 180 countries and had revenues of $27.4 billion in 2025.

    Its business has several segments. Electrical Americas and Electrical Global help customers manage power from the grid to homes, buildings, data centers and industrial sites. Aerospace supplies technology for aircraft. Vehicle and eMobility supply the transport market, and in early 2026 Eaton combined them into one segment called Mobility. In 2025, 22% of the Electrical segments' sales went to six large customers.

    Eaton has grown by buying companies. In 2025 it bought Fibrebond and Resilient Power Systems, and it agreed to buy Boyd Thermal. In January 2026 it completed the purchase of Ultra PCS. Sales in its electrical segments are usually lower in the first quarter and higher in the third and fourth quarters. Eaton competes on product performance, technology, service and price.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Problems with buying and joining other companies, including integrating the ones it has bought.
    • Production disruptions at its factories around the world.
    • Inflation or shortages of raw materials, energy, parts or labor.
    • Swings in the end markets it serves.
    • Weather disruptions and uncertain economic and political conditions in the countries it works in.
    From Eaton's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
