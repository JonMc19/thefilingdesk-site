    What Motorola Solutions does

    Motorola Solutions sells safety and security technology to public safety agencies, governments including defense, and businesses such as schools, hospitals and stadiums. It serves customers in over 100 countries. Its technology covers three product lines: mission-critical networks, video security and access control, and command center software.

    Mission-critical networks include two-way radios, broadband devices and the infrastructure behind them, plus installation and support services. Video covers cameras, including body-worn and in-vehicle ones, access control, sensors and analytics software. Command center software helps emergency teams handle calls and respond.

    The company has two segments: Products and Systems Integration, and Software and Services. The first one sells devices and builds systems, and it accounted for 62% of net sales in 2025. Software and Services covers software, cybersecurity subscriptions, and managed and support services. The company also works to combine its products into one safety and security system.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Large, multi-year system and services contracts that could go wrong or cost more than planned.
    • Rules on radio frequencies that could slow growth or let new competitors in.
    • Losing senior managers and key engineers.
    • Currency swings on sales made outside the United States.
    • Catastrophic events that interrupt its business or its customers' and suppliers' operations.
    From Motorola Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
