    M&T Bank

    MTB · NYSE · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    M&T Bank's revenue rose 4.4% to $9.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $2.9 billion, up 10.2%. Its return on equity was 9.8%, up from 9.2% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 56.7%, down from 57.8% a year earlier. It paid out $3.5 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 5.1%.
