    What M&T Bank does

    M&T Bank Corporation is a bank holding company based in Buffalo, New York. It has two bank subsidiaries, M&T Bank and Wilmington Trust, N.A. They offer retail and commercial banking, wealth management, trust and institutional services to consumers, businesses, professional clients, governments and financial institutions.

    M&T Bank has branches and ATMs across New York, Maryland, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Connecticut, Massachusetts, Maine, Vermont, New Hampshire, Virginia, West Virginia and Washington, D.C. Its lending focuses on consumers in those areas and on small and medium-size businesses, and it also makes commercial mortgage loans. Subsidiaries provide multifamily real estate lending, equipment leasing and financing, and institutional brokerage.

    At the end of 2025 M&T had total assets of $213.5 billion, deposits of $166.9 billion and shareholders' equity of $29.2 billion. M&T Bank accounts for over 99% of the company's assets. Its bank traces its history to 1856.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling credit quality on its loans.
    • Heavy concentration in the regions where its retail branches are, which leaves it exposed to a weak local economy.
    • Swings in debt and equity markets, and interest rate changes on its floating-rate funding and products.
    • Extensive government regulation and capital and liquidity requirements that limit what it can do.
    • Needing enough funding and liquidity, and relying on dividends from its subsidiaries.
    From M&T Bank's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
