    What H&R Block does

    H&R Block helps people prepare their income taxes. It offers assisted preparation, in which a tax professional prepares the return, and do-it-yourself preparation through its website, mobile app and desktop software. Clients are mainly in the United States, Canada and Australia. The assisted service is provided through company-owned and franchised offices, or virtually.

    It also sells financial products and small business services. Spruce is its mobile banking app. Block Advisors and Wave offer small business owners bookkeeping, payroll, advisory and payment processing. The company reports a single segment that includes all its continuing operations.

    H&R Block makes most of its money from tax preparation fees and its business is highly seasonal, because most clients file between February and April. In the U.S., its franchisees pay the company approximately 30% of gross tax return preparation and related service revenues as a franchise royalty. It was organized in 1955 in Missouri.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in tax laws that reduce demand for, or the price of, its services.
    • Competition for clients, and free tax products offered by others.
    • Fraud, and failures of its systems or the internet during tax season.
    • Dependence on a single vendor or a few vendors for key services.
    • Franchisees' relationships with the company, and the heavy seasonality of its business.
    From H&R Block's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
