    What Four Corners Property Trust does

    Four Corners Property Trust is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns, buys and leases properties used by restaurants and other retailers. Most of its revenue comes from leasing properties to tenants under net leases, in which the tenant pays most of the costs of running the property, such as utilities, property taxes, insurance and repairs. It also runs seven LongHorn Steakhouse restaurants near San Antonio, Texas, under franchise agreements with Darden.

    The company began as a subsidiary of Darden Restaurants and became independent in November 2015. At December 31, 2025, it leased 1,303 properties in 48 states, with 99.6% occupancy. In 2025 it bought 105 properties and ground leaseholds across 35 brands, including Chuy's, Crash Champions, Hawaiian Bros, Little Caesar's, Mission Pet Health and United Rentals.

    Four Corners says it plans to keep buying restaurant and retail properties to diversify, which it expects will reduce its reliance on Darden over time.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tenants failing to pay rent because their finances worsen.
    • Reliance on Darden, which also affects its ability to do business with other restaurant operators.
    • Some geographic concentration that leaves it open to local events.
    • New property purchases that do not work out as expected.
    • Uninsured or underinsured losses at its properties.
    From Four Corners Property Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
