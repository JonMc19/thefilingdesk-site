    What Blend Labs does

    Blend sells cloud software that banks, credit unions, mortgage lenders and mortgage servicers use to take loan applications and open accounts online. Its products cover mortgages, home equity loans, refinancing, vehicle loans, personal loans, credit cards and deposit accounts. A workflow engine applies pre-set rules for collecting data, verifying it, choosing products, pricing and sending disclosures. A tool called Blend Builder lets customers assemble their own products from modular parts.

    Blend has a single business segment. It says its model is largely success-based, so it often relies on customers reporting completed transactions to it, which makes revenue harder to estimate. In 2025, 75.0% of its revenue came from 25 of its largest customers. In 2025 it decided to exit its title business, and reports those results as discontinued operations.

    It also connects data, technology and service providers to the lenders using its platform, and says that more of these partners make it more useful to lenders and consumers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on financial services firms, so mortgage industry swings and higher interest rates hurt it.
    • A large share of revenue comes from a small number of key customers.
    • A history of net losses, with profit not assured.
    • Intense competition from other software providers.
    • Success-based pricing that relies on customers' own reporting, making revenue harder to forecast.
