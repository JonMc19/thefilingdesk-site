    What HeartFlow does

    Heartflow sells software and artificial intelligence (AI) that help doctors diagnose and manage coronary artery disease (CAD), in which plaque builds up in the heart's arteries and restricts blood flow. Its platform takes a heart scan known as a coronary CT angiogram and builds a personalized 3D model of the patient's heart. From the model, it shows how blood flows, how narrow the arteries are, and how much plaque there is and what it is made of.

    Heartflow says its platform has been used to assess CAD in more than 600,000 patients. Its revenue comes almost entirely from one product, Heartflow FFR CT Analysis. A newer product, Heartflow Plaque Analysis, is still at an early stage of sale.

    The company competes with traditional tests for CAD, such as stress tests, and says its approach is more accurate. Its sales depend on hospitals and other providers changing how they evaluate CAD and on insurers paying for the analysis. It has a concentrated customer base.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Almost all its revenue comes from one product, Heartflow FFR CT Analysis.
    • Healthcare providers may be unwilling to change how they evaluate coronary artery disease.
    • Insurers and government payors may not cover or pay enough for the platform.
    • It has a concentrated customer base and has lost money since it began.
    • Its AI models may have errors or create legal and reputational problems.
    From HeartFlow's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
