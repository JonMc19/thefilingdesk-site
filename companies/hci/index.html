    What HCI Group does

    HCI Group mainly sells homeowners' property and casualty insurance. Florida is its main market, and it also sells in some other states in the northeast and southeast. Its insurance subsidiaries are Homeowners Choice Property & Casualty Insurance Company and TypTap Insurance Company.

    It reports five segments: Insurance Operations, Exzeo, Reciprocal Exchange Operations, Real Estate, and Corporate and Other. Exzeo, a majority-owned subsidiary that went public in November 2025, sells insurance software and operations services, and HCI uses that software to find underwriting opportunities and handle claims. HCI also provides management services to reciprocal insurance exchanges that are owned by their policyholders, and a real estate group develops and runs commercial properties.

    All of its revenue comes from the United States, mostly in Florida. It has a Bermuda captive reinsurance subsidiary that lets it keep some risk and lower the cost of outside reinsurance.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Actual claim losses exceeding the reserves it has set aside.
    • Failing to price the risks it insures accurately.
    • Its heavy concentration in Florida homeowners insurance.
    • Risk reduction and loss limiting methods that may not work.
    • Conflicts of interest between HCI and Exzeo and its other stockholders.
