    What Dynatrace does

    Dynatrace sells software that watches over a company's computer systems and applications and shows what is going wrong and why. This is called observability. Its platform gathers logs, traces, metrics and other data from cloud, on-premises and mainframe systems, including those on Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. Companies use it to keep software running, deliver it securely and, increasingly, to monitor their own AI applications.

    The platform stores its data in a system it calls Grail and maps how applications, networks and services depend on each other. It also offers Dynatrace Intelligence, an AI system the company says can help find problems and act on them. Its customers include many large enterprises.

    Dynatrace sells directly and through channel partners. In each of the last two fiscal years, one channel partner accounted for 10% of revenue, and no end customer was above that level. As of March 31, 2026, it had approximately 5,600 employees in about 40 countries. Its fiscal year ends March 31.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand for observability software may not grow as it expects.
    • Competition in a crowded market, which could cost it new and existing customers.
    • Failing to keep up with customers' needs through new products, including in AI.
    • Long and unpredictable sales cycles that make quarterly results hard to predict.
    • Dependence on partners to market, sell and deliver its software.
    From Dynatrace's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
