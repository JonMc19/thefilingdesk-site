    What DiamondRock Hospitality does

    DiamondRock Hospitality is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns premium hotels and resorts in the United States. As of December 31, 2025 it owned 35 hotels with 9,595 rooms in 26 markets. It targets destination markets where new supply is limited, markets that add geographic variety, and markets it expects to see strong demand growth.

    It is an owner, not an operator. Third parties run all of its hotels, and it receives the operating profit or loss after paying management fees, plus brand fees in some cases. It operates nearly 40% of its portfolio as independent hotels, and the rest operate under brands from Marriott International, Hilton Worldwide or IHG Hotels & Resorts.

    It began operating in July 2004 and became a public reporting company in May 2005. Its strategy is active asset management and disciplined use of capital. Depending on its cost of capital, it plans to invest in improvements, buy hotels, or take other steps.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A business model focused on premium full-service hotels that can swing sharply.
    • Economic conditions and other factors that hurt the lodging industry.
    • Seasonal swings in hotel results.
    • Online travel intermediaries and alternative lodging, and business travel falling as technology replaces trips.
    • Long-term hotel management and franchise agreements, and ground leases that limit sales of hotels.
    From DiamondRock Hospitality's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
