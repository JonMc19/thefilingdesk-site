    What CarMax does

    CarMax sells used vehicles and says it is the nation's largest retailer of them. It operated 256 used car stores in 110 U.S. television markets as of February 28, 2026. Customers can buy online, in a store or through a mix of both, at no-haggle prices, and it also buys cars from people who want to sell.

    It reports two segments. CarMax Sales Operations covers selling and buying used vehicles, related products and services, and service work. CarMax Auto Finance is its own lending arm for customers buying retail vehicles from CarMax; it services a portfolio of auto loans of $16.37 billion. CarMax also runs wholesale vehicle auctions, and sold 780,684 used vehicles at retail in the fiscal year ended February 28, 2026.

    It bought Edmunds, an online automotive information site, in 2021. CarMax began in 1993 under Circuit City and became independent in 2002.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in the used-vehicle market, including rivals copying parts of its offer.
    • Sensitivity of car sales to economic conditions and to vehicle prices.
    • Its need for capital to run the business and fund its auto loans, and debt covenants that limit it.
    • Less access to vehicle inventory or reconditioning parts.
    • Harm to its reputation for integrity.
    From CarMax's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 15, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
