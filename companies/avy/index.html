    What Avery Dennison does

    Avery Dennison makes labeling and packaging materials, RFID tags and related software. RFID, short for radio-frequency identification, uses tiny chips that let companies track items. Its customers span home and personal care, apparel, retail, e-commerce, logistics, food and grocery, pharmaceuticals and automotive.

    The company has two segments. The Materials Group, which made approximately 69% of 2025 net sales, sells pressure-sensitive label materials, graphics and reflective films, and tapes and fasteners. Label materials are sold to converters, which turn them into finished labels. The Solutions Group, approximately 31% of net sales, sells RFID inlays and tags, branding and information products and software that connect physical items to digital data.

    International operations made up approximately 69% of net sales in 2025. At the end of 2025 the company operated over 200 manufacturing and distribution facilities, with locations in more than 50 countries. It names competitors by product, including UPM Adhesive Materials, Fedrigoni, Lintec, 3M and Checkpoint Systems.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its sales and costs depend on the availability and price of raw materials and energy.
    • Currency swings and its operations outside the US add risks, including in less stable emerging markets.
    • Consolidation among its customers could hurt its business.
    • Part of its sales go through third parties, so it depends on their financial health.
    • Acquisitions it has made or may make carry risks and may not work out.
