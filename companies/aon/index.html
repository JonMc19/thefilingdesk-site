    What Aon does

    Aon is a professional services firm that helps companies manage risk and workforce costs. Its main business is insurance and reinsurance brokerage: it advises clients on what coverage to buy and places it with insurers. It also sells consulting on health benefits, pensions, retirement plans and investments. It serves clients in more than 120 countries.

    Aon has two segments. Risk Capital covers commercial insurance brokerage, reinsurance and related advice, and Human Capital covers health, benefits, talent and retirement services. In 2025 total revenue was $17,181 million, including $11,290 million from Risk Capital and $5,907 million from Human Capital before certain eliminations between its own units.

    It earns money mainly from commissions, fees from clients, and fees from insurers for services it provides to them. Aon names Marsh McLennan, Willis Towers Watson, Arthur J Gallagher and Lockton among its competitors. At the end of 2025 it had about 60,000 employees. Its largest single client was about 1% of revenue.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Commission income that rises and falls with insurance and reinsurance market cycles it does not control.
    • Competition from other brokers, consultants, and insurers that sell directly to clients.
    • Claims from clients over errors and omissions in its advice and services.
    • Handling large amounts of client money held in transit between clients and insurers.
    • Failing to gain the expected benefits from its restructuring plan and other operating changes.
    From Aon's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
