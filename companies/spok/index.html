    What Spok does

    Spok, based in Plano, Texas, sells communication software and services to hospitals and other organizations. Its products send clinical information to care teams, and include secure texting, paging services, contact center tools and public safety response. It sells mainly in the United States, and on a limited basis in Europe, Canada, Australia, Asia and the Middle East.

    It serves three markets: healthcare, government and large enterprises, with the most emphasis on healthcare. It earns money from software that it develops, sells and supports, and from wireless services, meaning paging subscribers who pay to receive messages on its pagers. Its customers include hospitals, government agencies, universities, hotels and manufacturers.

    The report says the healthcare industry is shifting toward paying for value rather than for each service, and that it keeps digitizing information.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Wireless paging customers and revenue shrinking faster than Spok can cut costs.
    • Wireless service being hurt when network operators rationalize their networks.
    • Depending on the US healthcare industry for most of its revenue.
    • Long sales cycles for its software, and reliance on its sales team's productivity.
    • Product defects or security flaws that could harm its reputation.
    From Spok's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
