    What Capitol Federal Savings Bank does

    Capitol Federal Financial is the holding company for Capitol Federal Savings Bank, a federally chartered savings bank based in Topeka, Kansas. The bank takes deposits from the public and from businesses and lends the money out. It offers savings accounts, money market accounts, checking accounts and certificates of deposit, plus treasury management services for business customers.

    Its main source of income is net interest income, which is the interest it earns on loans, securities and cash minus the interest it pays on deposits and borrowings. The bank is shifting from a retail focus to commercial customers. It lends mainly through commercial loans, secured by real estate or made for business purposes, and through home loans on one- to four-family houses. It stopped buying home loans from other lenders in June 2024. It also holds investment and mortgage-backed securities and borrows from the Federal Home Loan Bank of Topeka.

    At the end of September 2025 it had a network of 46 branches in Kansas and Missouri. It is also building up small business, private banking, trust and wealth management services.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates that could squeeze the gap between what it earns and pays.
    • A downturn in real estate values in the areas where it lends or holds loans.
    • Credit risk from growing commercial lending, which is riskier than its older home loans.
    • Competition and heavy regulation that limit its growth and activities.
    • Dependence on technology and outside vendors, with the risk of cyberattacks.
