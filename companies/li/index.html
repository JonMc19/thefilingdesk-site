    What Li Auto does

    Li Auto is a Chinese company that designs, develops, makes and sells smart electric vehicles for families. Its vehicles include extended-range electric vehicles (EREVs), which carry an engine that charges the battery, and battery electric vehicles (BEVs). It started volume production of EREVs in November 2019 with its first model, the Li ONE, a six-seat electric SUV.

    Its current lineup includes a family MPV (a large people carrier), four Li L series extended-range electric SUVs and two Li i series battery electric SUVs. It says it focuses on new energy vehicles priced over RMB200,000. It launched the Li MEGA in March 2024 and began deliveries in the same month.

    Li Auto reports that cumulative deliveries of its vehicles reached 1,540,215 as of December 31, 2025. It says it hit the one million mark on October 18, 2024, 58 months after delivering its first vehicle in December 2019.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition, especially as more entrants move into extended-range electric vehicles.
    • Technical defects or performance problems in its EREV powertrain, super charging or autonomous driving system.
    • Managing its growth, which could strain its business if it does not do so well.
    • Mishandling of battery cells, which may disrupt its facilities or cause accidents.
    • Lawsuits seeking significant damages, and negative publicity.
