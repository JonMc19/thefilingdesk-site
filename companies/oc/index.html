    What Owens Corning does

    Owens Corning makes building materials, mainly for homes. It has three segments. Roofing makes asphalt roofing shingles and related products. Insulation makes fiberglass and other insulation for homes, commercial buildings and industrial uses. Doors, added with the 2024 Masonite purchase, makes interior and exterior doors of wood, glass, fiberglass and metal.

    The company sells through distributors, home centers, lumberyards and contractors, mainly in the United States, and also in Canada, Europe and Latin America. In 2025 it recorded net sales of $10.1 billion. Roofing, Insulation and Doors made up about 43%, 36% and 21% of segment net sales, respectively. Demand depends on new home building and on repair and remodeling work, and storm damage can lift roofing sales.

    Owens Corning has about 25,000 employees in 31 countries. In 2025 it agreed to sell its global glass reinforcements business, which it now reports as a discontinued operation.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Low levels of home and commercial construction activity.
    • Higher costs or shortages of raw materials, energy and transportation.
    • Sales that can drop quickly because of customer concentration and no long-term volume commitments.
    • Government trade actions that create uncertainty in global markets.
    • Strong competition, and difficulty integrating acquisitions or managing capacity expansions.
    From Owens Corning's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
