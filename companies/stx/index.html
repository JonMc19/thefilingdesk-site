    What Seagate Technology does

    Seagate designs and makes hard disk drives (HDDs), storage systems and related products. It serves two main markets. Data Center covers cloud and enterprise customers that need large amounts of storage, including for AI-related computing. Edge / Internet of Things covers industrial and consumer uses where data is stored closer to where it is created, such as video, network-attached storage, gaming and backup.

    Its Data Center products include HDDs of up to 44TB and storage systems of up to 3.5PB. They are sold mainly to cloud service providers, equipment makers (OEMs), distributors and enterprise customers, often under master purchase agreements. Its Edge products range from 1TB to 32TB and are sold mainly through OEMs, distributors, retailers and resellers, and some as branded products for casual users, gamers and creative professionals.

    Seagate says its competitive position rests on making its own drives and from raising how much data fits on a disk, including with a heat-assisted magnetic recording technology it calls Mozaic. It had about 30,000 full-time employees on July 3, 2026, about 25,000 of them in Asia.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to launch new products and win market share, with customers concentrated in a few large end markets.
    • Intense competition from other makers of hard drives and solid-state drives.
    • A small number of key customers account for a significant share of its revenue.
    • Weak or falling demand for drives in consumer and client-centric markets.
    • Reliance on sales through distributors and retailers, which can increase price erosion and sales swings.
    From Seagate Technology's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
