    What F5 does

    F5 sells software, hardware and services that help companies deliver and protect their applications, wherever those run: in company data centers, in public clouds or at the edge of a network. Its main product families are BIG-IP, for traditional applications, NGINX, for modern container-based applications and APIs, and Distributed Cloud Services, a software-as-a-service (SaaS) offering.

    F5 sells packaged software under perpetual, subscription and usage-based models, SaaS and managed services on subscription and usage-based terms, and high-performance hardware systems. It also sells services such as maintenance, consulting, training and technical support. Customers include large businesses, public sector bodies, governments and service providers.

    The company manages its business by region: the Americas; Europe, the Middle East and Africa; and Asia Pacific. It was incorporated in 1996 and is based in Seattle. On October 15, 2025, it disclosed a security incident in which an attacker kept long-term access to F5 systems and took certain files.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Harm to its reputation and customer relationships from the October 2025 security incident.
    • Security flaws in its own systems and products, which protect other companies' applications.
    • The shift to cloud and SaaS computing, which brings competitive and execution risks.
    • Reliance on contract manufacturers and outside suppliers for its hardware.
    • A sales cycle that is hard to predict and a rush of orders late in each quarter.
    From F5's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
