    What indie Semiconductor does

    indie Semiconductor, based in Aliso Viejo, California, designs chips and software for cars. It focuses on advanced driver assistance systems, the features that help a car sense its surroundings and avoid hazards, plus some industrial uses. Its products include mixed-signal chips that combine analog and digital circuits, sensors using radar, LiDAR, ultrasound and computer vision, and chips for power management and system control. It says it has shipped over 550 million devices since it began.

    It sells to car makers and works with tier 1 suppliers, the companies that supply parts to car makers, developing products together with key customers. Aptiv, a tier 1 supplier, was approximately 6% of revenue in 2025. It spent approximately 71% of its revenue on research and development in 2025.

    indie holds a 34.38% stake in a China-based company, Wuxi. In October 2025 it agreed to sell that stake to United Faith, a listed Chinese company, for approximately $135 million, subject to approvals. Wuxi accounted for approximately 43% of indie's consolidated revenue in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not completing the Wuxi sale, or completing it on worse terms, and big changes to its reported figures if Wuxi is deconsolidated.
    • A history of losses that may continue.
    • The cyclical semiconductor industry, falling selling prices and strong competition.
    • Depending on outside makers and a limited number of suppliers for its chips and materials.
    • Tariffs, export controls and trade restrictions that could hurt revenue.
    From indie Semiconductor's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
