    What Thor Industries does

    THOR Industries makes recreational vehicles (RVs). Its products include travel trailers, fifth wheels, and motorhomes in several classes. It says it is the largest RV maker in the world and in North America, and one of the largest in Europe. It builds RVs in the United States and Europe and sells them, along with parts and accessories, mainly to independent dealers in the United States, Canada and Europe.

    THOR reports three segments: North American Towable RVs, North American Motorized RVs and European RVs. In North America its brands include Airstream, Jayco, Keystone, KZ, Thor Motor Coach and Tiffin Motorhomes. Its European business has eight main RV production locations and brands such as Hymer, Dethleffs, Elddis, Buerstner and Laika. Its supply businesses include Airxcel and Postle.

    The company's fiscal year ends July 31. It says RV sales are both cyclical and seasonal, so production and results can swing from year to year and within a year. It competes with other RV makers in North America and Europe.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • RV sales volumes swing with the economy and the seasons, making production hard to plan.
    • Macroeconomic, geopolitical and trade developments across its global operations.
    • Higher costs of raw materials and parts, and reliance on a small number of suppliers for key items such as chassis.
    • Product recalls and product liability claims.
    • The loss of its largest independent dealer, or dealers consolidating.
    From Thor Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
