    What Soluna does

    Soluna Holdings builds and runs data centers next to renewable energy power plants. It calls this model Renewable Computing. The idea is to use electricity that renewable plants would otherwise waste when the grid cannot take it, and to turn it into computing power for jobs that use a lot of energy, including Bitcoin mining, artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance computing (HPC). Its data centers sit behind the meter, so they can draw power directly from the plant or from the grid through the plant's connection.

    It earns or plans to earn money from four lines. It mines Bitcoin itself and through joint ventures. It hosts Bitcoin mining machines for outside customers. It is developing data centers for AI and HPC, starting with a project called Kati 2 that it is designing with Metrobloks. It also sells demand response services, which help the grid by cutting power use when asked. Its own software, MaestroOS, adjusts operations based on power prices, weather and grid demand.

    The company has lost money since it began and has funded itself mostly by selling new shares and borrowing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has lost money since it began and may not reach profit.
    • It relies on selling new shares and on debt to pay for growth, which could dilute owners.
    • It may be unable to refinance its debt, or to complete growth projects on time and on budget.
    • Its plan depends on acquisitions, joint ventures and partners that may not work out.
    • It may have trouble getting banking services for its cryptocurrency activities.
