    What Forum Energy Technologies does

    Forum Energy Technologies makes equipment for the oil and natural gas, defense and renewable energy industries. Its customers include oil and gas operators, oilfield service companies, pipeline and refinery operators, defense contractors and renewable energy companies. Its products include capital equipment, such as drilling rig equipment, subsea and pressure pumping equipment, and consumable products used in drilling, well construction and completion and at refineries.

    It reports two segments. Drilling and Completions makes products for drilling, subsea, coiled tubing, well stimulation and intervention markets. Artificial Lift and Downhole makes products for artificial lift (equipment that helps bring oil up from a well), well construction, production and infrastructure markets.

    In 2025, about 80% of its revenue came from consumable products and activity-based equipment. Most of the rest came from capital products, with a small amount from rental and other services. FET is based in Houston, Texas, and its shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its business depends on activity levels in the oil and natural gas industry, which change a lot.
    • Strong competition, including from companies with substantial market share.
    • Holding excess inventory if customers order less than expected, and not realizing revenue on its backlog.
    • Relying on key suppliers for raw materials and specialized equipment.
    • Customer consolidation and tighter spending discipline by customers.
