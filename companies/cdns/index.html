    What Cadence Design Systems does

    Cadence Design Systems makes the software, hardware and ready-made circuit designs that engineers use to design electronic products. Its customers are semiconductor companies that design and make integrated circuits (chips), and systems companies that build electronic and electromechanical products that contain chips.

    Cadence sells design and verification software, accelerated computing hardware used to test designs, and silicon intellectual property (IP), which is pre-built circuit designs that customers license and drop into their own chips. It also sells tools to analyze whole systems, such as circuit boards. It adds artificial intelligence (AI) features to its products. Many of its software licenses bring revenue over time rather than all at once.

    Cadence says its growth depends mainly on the semiconductor and electronics industries. It sells to customers around the world, so export and import controls and trade rules affect it.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its growth depending mainly on the semiconductor and electronics industries.
    • Operating results that vary from period to period, with licenses recognized over time.
    • Export and import controls that could limit what it can sell and to whom.
    • Not getting the expected benefits from companies it acquires.
    • Protecting its proprietary technology and IP, and claims that it infringes others.
    From Cadence Design Systems's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
