    What Fidelity National Information Services does

    FIS is a financial technology company based in Jacksonville, Florida. It sells software and services that banks and other financial institutions use to run their operations, including core processing systems for deposits and lending, digital banking apps, and wealth and retirement tools.

    It reports two operating segments plus a corporate group. Banking Solutions serves financial institutions with core and transaction processing software and related applications. Capital Market Solutions serves asset managers, private equity firms, brokerages and trading firms, insurers, lenders and other companies with trading, treasury, risk management and lending software. Corporate and Other holds overhead costs and some non-strategic businesses.

    In January 2024 FIS sold a majority of its Worldpay merchant payments business to funds managed by GTCR. FIS kept a non-controlling 45% equity interest in the new joint venture. In April 2025 it agreed to buy the Issuer Solutions business from Global Payments and to sell its remaining Worldpay stake to Global Payments. FIS says it is moving to a platform model and building AI into its products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to innovate or adapt its solutions as technology and the market change.
    • Competition from other providers that could hurt its business and results.
    • Mergers and failures among banks that eliminate some of its existing and potential clients.
    • Losing clients or failing to renew contracts on good terms.
    • Adding AI to its products and operations brings new risks, including harm to its reputation.
