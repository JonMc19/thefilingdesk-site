    What Savers Value Village does

    Savers Value Village is a for-profit thrift retailer. It says it is the largest in the United States and Canada by number of stores. It sells used clothing, shoes, accessories, housewares, books and other goods at low prices in stores called Savers, Value Village, Value Village Boutique, Village des Valeurs, Unique and 2nd Ave.

    At the start of January 2026 it operated 367 stores: 179 in the United States, 170 in Canada and 18 in Australia. It has nearly 24,000 team members. It buys the goods it sells from non-profit partners, who collect donations, and that gives the partners revenue for their causes. It then sorts, prices and sells the items in its stores. Items that do not sell in stores go to wholesale buyers who reuse or repurpose them.

    Its income comes from retail sales and from selling to wholesale customers. It says its loyalty program members, who shopped with it in 2025, accounted for most of its retail sales. It describes its approach as local sourcing, and says it competes with other secondhand and value-based retailers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It may fail to get enough good secondhand items at attractive prices.
    • Both its supply of goods and demand for them depend on economic conditions and consumer spending.
    • Its business depends on attracting and keeping workers for its stores and processing facilities.
    • It leases nearly all its locations and must find and keep suitable sites.
    • It may not be able to keep up its recent sales growth, and its foreign operations, mainly in Canada, add risks.
