    What United Natural Foods does

    United Natural Foods, known as UNFI, is a grocery wholesaler. It buys food and other goods from suppliers and sells them to retailers in the United States and Canada, from some of the largest grocers in North America to small independent stores. It also provides support services to retailers. The company is based in Providence, Rhode Island.

    It offers over 200,000 products, both national and regional brands and its own private labels. They are grouped into center store and general merchandise, fresh and perishables, frozen, wellness and personal care, and bulk and foodservice. It reports three segments: Natural, Conventional and Retail. It has a long history in natural, organic and specialty foods and says it wants to be the partner of choice for grocers focused on those and other differentiated offerings.

    UNFI serves customers in all 50 states and all ten Canadian provinces, from 46 distribution centers and warehouses with about 26 million square feet of space. Its customers include over 30,000 locations. It says its business runs on low margins and faces intense competition.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A significant share of its revenue comes from a few principal customers.
    • Low margins that are sensitive to inflation, deflation and intense competition.
    • Many customers are not required to keep buying from it.
    • Strategic initiatives that may not deliver the benefits it expects.
    • Debt and loan terms that limit its operating flexibility.
    From United Natural Foods's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
