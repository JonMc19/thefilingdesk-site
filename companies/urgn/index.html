    What UroGen Pharma does

    UroGen is a biotechnology company that develops and sells treatments for urothelial and specialty cancers, such as cancers of the lining of the bladder and upper urinary tract. Its technology is RTGel, a gel that is liquid when cold and turns to a gel at body temperature, so a drug stays in the urinary tract for several hours.

    It has two approved products, both made of the drug mitomycin in RTGel. Jelmyto treats low-grade upper tract urothelial cancer. Zusduri treats recurrent low-grade intermediate risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer. Both are designed to destroy tumors without surgery. It earns money by selling them.

    It is also working on UGN-501, an investigational virus designed to attack cancer cells. It has a loan from Pharmakon, and under a prepaid forward contract it owes tiered cash payments to RTW based on net sales of its products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Needing more financing, and diluting shareholders or giving up rights to its technology if it raises it.
    • Depending heavily on the success of its two approved products, Jelmyto and Zusduri.
    • Debt from its loan with Pharmakon and covenants under its RTW contract that limit extra borrowing.
    • Results for its drugs in larger, broader groups of patients that may differ from its clinical studies.
    • Raising money may force it to cut back operations or give up product rights.
