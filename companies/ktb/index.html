    What Kontoor Brands does

    Kontoor Brands designs, makes, buys, sells and licenses clothing, footwear and accessories under three main brands: Wrangler, Lee and Helly Hansen. Its products include denim, workwear and outdoor clothing. It sells them through wholesale (to retailers) and direct to consumers in the United States and abroad, mainly in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, Asia-Pacific and the rest of the Americas, and it licenses its brands in some regions. Its brands are sold or licensed in more than 90 countries.

    It makes money from selling products and from licensing fees. A small number of large customers account for a large share of its revenue and profit. In May 2025 it bought Helly Hansen and Musto, an outdoor, workwear and sailing business, for initial cash of about $957.5 million in U.S. dollars, paid with debt and cash on hand.

    The company has been changing how it runs: it closed some of its factories in 2025, is moving its sourcing to a wider set of suppliers, and has a business transformation program it calls Project Jeanius. It also says higher U.S. tariffs cut its profit margin in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker consumer spending on clothing, which depends on the economy.
    • Depending on a small number of large customers for much of its revenue and profit.
    • Trouble combining Helly Hansen with Kontoor or getting the benefits it expects.
    • Higher tariffs and changes in trade policy, plus supply chain and shipping disruptions.
    • Relying on outside suppliers and a few North American mills for its fabric and products.
    From Kontoor Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
