    What Provident Financial Services does

    Provident Financial Services is the holding company for Provident Bank, a New Jersey bank founded in 1839. The bank runs full-service branches across New Jersey, parts of eastern Pennsylvania (Bucks, Lehigh and Northampton counties) and parts of New York (Orange, Queens and Nassau counties). It serves individuals, families and businesses, and says it stresses personal service.

    It takes deposits from the public and businesses and, with those deposits and borrowings, makes commercial real estate, multi-family, commercial business, residential mortgage and consumer loans. It invests in mortgage-backed securities as well. The bank also earns fees: through subsidiaries it offers wealth management and fiduciary services (Beacon Trust Company) and insurance brokerage (Provident Protection Plus). Total non-interest income, which includes these fees, was $109.8 million in 2025.

    In May 2024 the company bought Lakeland Bancorp, which added $10.59 billion in total assets and 68 full-service banking offices in New Jersey and New York. The total consideration was $876.8 million. It then closed 13 of the acquired offices and nine of its own branches because they overlapped.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in what drives its net interest income, the gap between what it earns on loans and pays on deposits.
    • Commercial real estate, commercial and construction loans that carry more risk and earnings swings.
    • Heavy regulation, extra supervision as a larger bank, and conditions attached to the Lakeland merger approvals.
    • Costs of mergers and combining operations, and possible write-downs of goodwill.
    • An economic slowdown that cuts returns, wealth management fee income and access to capital.
    From Provident Financial Services's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
