    What Agios Pharmaceuticals does

    Agios is a drug company focused on rare diseases, starting with blood disorders. Its main medicine, mitapivat, activates an enzyme called pyruvate kinase in red blood cells to treat hemolytic anemias, in which red blood cells break down too fast.

    The drug is sold under the brand name PYRUKYND in the United States for anemia in adults with pyruvate kinase deficiency, and in the European Union and Great Britain for that condition. In the United States it is also approved, under the name AQVESME, for anemia in adults with thalassemia, an inherited blood disorder, and it has approval for thalassemia in Saudi Arabia. Agios earns revenue from selling these medicines.

    The company is also testing mitapivat in sickle cell disease and in children with pyruvate kinase deficiency, and says it plans to seek US approval for sickle cell disease after meeting with the FDA. It describes a growing pipeline of other rare disease medicines.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its medicines may not sell well enough if it does not successfully commercialize them.
    • Doctors and patients may not accept them, or it may not keep up the sales and marketing it needs.
    • Clinical trials may be slow to enroll patients, and early results may not predict later ones.
    • Trials held outside the United States may not be accepted by the FDA.
    • It may spend limited resources on the wrong drug candidate or condition.
