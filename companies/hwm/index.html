    What Howmet Aerospace does

    Howmet Aerospace makes engineered metal parts for aircraft and commercial trucks. Its main businesses are jet engine components, aerospace fastening systems (the bolts and fasteners that hold aircraft together), airframe structural components, and forged aluminum wheels for commercial trucks. It also serves defense, gas turbine and other markets.

    It has four reportable segments, organized by product: Engine Products, Fastening Systems, Engineered Structures and Forged Wheels. Howmet operates in 19 countries. By where products shipped from, North America and Europe made up 72% and 22% of 2025 sales.

    Howmet was formerly named Arconic Inc., and before that Alcoa Inc. It took its current form when Arconic split into two companies on April 1, 2020. On December 22, 2025 it agreed to buy Consolidated Aerospace Manufacturing from Stanley Black & Decker for a cash price of about $1.8 billion, expected to close in the first half of 2026. In February 2026 it bought Brunner Manufacturing, a maker of fasteners.
