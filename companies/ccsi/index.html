    What Consensus Cloud Solutions does

    Consensus Cloud Solutions provides secure ways to send and exchange information. Its best-known brand is eFax, an online fax service it has run for more than twenty-five years. It has added software that pulls out, reads and converts data in documents, and tools that help systems share information with each other. It sells these as subscription cloud software, and says they suit regulated fields such as healthcare, government, finance, law and education. Healthcare is its largest industry.

    It has about 703 thousand customers, from small businesses to large enterprises and the federal government. About 65 thousand are Corporate customers, who buy mainly through salespeople with custom pricing, extra phone lines, connections to their software and added security. Corporate revenue was approximately $223 million in 2025. About 638 thousand are small office and home office customers who sign up online. Its fax services make up substantially all of its revenue and operating income.

    It offers local phone numbers in 46 countries and territories, and had 520 employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on fax services for substantially all of its revenue and operating income.
    • Changes in the healthcare industry, its largest market, could hurt demand.
    • Falling behind rapid technology change, or markets for its newer products developing slowly.
    • Relying on a small number of telecommunications carriers and on getting local phone numbers.
    • Internet providers blocking or degrading its services, or higher costs for carriers and email.
    From Consensus Cloud Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
