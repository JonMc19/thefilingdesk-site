    What USA TODAY Co. does

    USA TODAY Co., called Gannett until November 2025, is a media and digital marketing company. It publishes the national newspaper and website USA TODAY and a network of local news properties in the United States. It also owns Newsquest, a news publisher in the United Kingdom. Its marketing brand, LocaliQ, sells digital marketing products to small and medium-sized businesses.

    It reports three segments: USA TODAY Media, Newsquest and LocaliQ. It earns money from advertising, from digital marketing services, and from digital and print subscriptions, and also from content licensing, syndication and artificial intelligence partnerships. Digital revenues grew to 46% of total revenues in 2025.

    It says its strategy is digital first, focused on growing audiences and adding new sources of revenue. It had about 1.5 million paid digital-only subscriptions at the end of 2025, more than its print subscriptions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition for audiences and advertisers, and the need to launch new products that work.
    • Heavy debt, with most of it held by one creditor and lender consent required for some actions.
    • Possibly being unable to repay its 2027 and 2031 notes if holders demand it after a change of control.
    • Its LocaliQ segment depends on online media bought from third parties that could change their terms.
    • Changes to data privacy practices that could cut advertising revenue.
    From USA TODAY Co.'s annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
