    What GigaCloud Technology does

    GigaCloud runs the GigaCloud Marketplace, an online business-to-business platform for large, bulky goods. It connects manufacturers, mostly in Asia, with resellers, mostly in the U.S., Europe and Japan, and covers everything from finding products to payment and shipping. It launched in January 2019 with furniture and has added home appliances and fitness equipment.

    It earns money in two ways. It sells its own products, which it calls 1P products, through the marketplace and on third-party sites such as Amazon, Walmart, Home Depot, Overstock and Wayfair in the U.S., Rakuten in Japan and OTTO in Germany. These sales were 66.8% of total revenues in 2025. The rest comes mainly from the marketplace, including services such as fulfillment and shipping. Goods sold on the marketplace reached over $1,576.8 million in 2025, measured as GMV, the total value of goods sold.

    It has bought other companies: Noble House, a home furnishings distributor, and Wonder, a digital signage software company, in 2023, and New Classic Home Furnishings on January 1, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions that cut demand for large bulky goods sold online.
    • Relying on third-party platforms, sellers and buyers to keep the marketplace growing.
    • Trade restrictions and international risks, since its products come mostly from Asia.
    • Depending on trucking and freight companies, and managing its own inventory and fulfillment network.
    • Not getting the expected benefits from its acquisitions.
    From GigaCloud Technology's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
