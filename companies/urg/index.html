    What Ur-Energy does

    Ur-Energy is a uranium mining company. It mines uranium, processes it, and explores for and develops uranium properties in the United States. Uranium is the fuel for nuclear power. It began operating its first in situ recovery (ISR) facility, at its Lost Creek Project in Wyoming, in 2013. ISR extracts uranium from the ground with solutions pumped through wells.

    Lost Creek in Sweetwater County, Wyoming is its main property. It runs it through a Wyoming subsidiary, Lost Creek ISR, LLC. A second subsidiary, Pathfinder Mines, holds the Shirley Basin and Lucky Mc properties in Wyoming. Ur-Energy is completing the build out and commissioning of production at Shirley Basin.

    It sells uranium under term sales contracts for part of its Lost Creek and Shirley Basin output. At December 31, 2025, Ur-Energy USA had 30 regular full-time employees, and Lost Creek ISR, LLC employed 83 people full time. Its shares trade on the NYSE American and the Toronto Stock Exchange.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The uranium price is volatile and the market has few customers.
    • Being unable to sign more term sales contracts on good terms.
    • Operating problems as it returns to steady output at Lost Creek and starts up Shirley Basin, which could delay deliveries.
    • Resource estimates that may be unreliable, and depleting its resources without enough new ones.
    • Strict environmental rules and permits, public opposition to nuclear energy, and the need for capital and debt.
    From Ur-Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
