    What Terreno Realty does

    Terreno Realty is a real estate investment trust (REIT), a company that owns property and passes most of its income to shareholders. It buys, owns and operates industrial real estate, such as warehouses and truck terminals, in six coastal U.S. markets: New York City and Northern New Jersey, Los Angeles, Miami, the San Francisco Bay Area, Seattle and Washington, D.C. It is managed internally, not by an outside adviser.

    It earns money as a landlord, collecting rent from tenants. By annualized base rent at the end of 2025, warehouse and distribution space was 80.5% of the total, and improved land was 10.1%. The rest came from transshipment sites and flex space, which combines office and warehouse use. The company says it targets functional buildings in infill locations, meaning areas already crowded with buildings, that can be shared by several tenants.

    At the end of 2025 it owned 309 buildings with about 19.8 million square feet, 46 parcels of improved land and six properties under development or redevelopment. It had 683 customers, and the largest accounted for approximately 4.9% of annualized base rent.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its growth depends on buying more properties, which can bring integration and other problems.
    • All its investments are in industrial real estate, so a downturn in that sector would hurt it.
    • Events in the six coastal markets where its properties are clustered could hurt its results.
    • It may be unable to renew leases or re-lease vacant space, and may need to fund tenant improvements.
    • Cash distributions to shareholders are uncertain and limited by Maryland law.
    From Terreno Realty's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
