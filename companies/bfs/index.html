    What Saul Centers does

    Saul Centers is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns and runs shopping centers and mixed-use properties, mostly in the Washington, DC and Baltimore area. It was incorporated in Maryland in 1993. Its tenants pay rent, which is its main source of income.

    At the end of 2025 it owned 50 shopping centers, nine mixed-use properties that combine office, retail and apartments, and three development properties that are not yet operating. Many of the shopping centers have a grocery store as the main tenant, called an anchor.

    As a REIT, it must pay out at least 90% of its taxable income, excluding net capital gains, to stockholders each year. Its strategy is to add transit-oriented, mixed-use projects with apartments and to expand its grocery-anchored centers, while adding pad sites and redeveloping its core centers. It manages its properties itself, and shares some services, such as IT, payroll and legal work, with companies controlled by the family of Chairman and CEO B. Francis Saul II.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak results at its retail tenants, and tenants that go bankrupt and do not pay.
    • Losing its grocery and other anchor tenants, or having trouble renewing leases and filling vacant space.
    • Development, redevelopment and acquisitions that carry risk and may fall short.
    • Its portfolio is concentrated in the Washington, DC and Baltimore area.
    • Adverse trends in the retail, office and residential real estate sectors.
    From Saul Centers's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
