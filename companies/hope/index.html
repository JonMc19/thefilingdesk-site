    What Hope Bancorp does

    Hope Bancorp is a bank holding company in Los Angeles. Its only bank, Bank of Hope, started as a Korean-American focused bank. It now calls itself one of the largest independent commercial banks headquartered in California and serves customers of many backgrounds across the United States.

    The bank earns money mainly from interest on loans and investment securities, which it funds with customer deposits and other borrowings. It also earns fees and gains on selling financial assets. Its products include commercial and commercial real estate loans, Small Business Administration loans, home mortgages and other consumer loans, treasury management, foreign currency and trade financing. Its online banking is offered in English and Korean.

    Branches and loan offices are in California, New York, Texas, Hawaii, Washington, Illinois, New Jersey, Georgia, Florida, Alabama, Colorado and Oregon, plus a representative office in Seoul, South Korea. On April 2, 2025, it acquired Territorial Bancorp, based in Honolulu, Hawaii.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Worse credit quality in its commercial and commercial real estate loans, which would force higher loan loss provisions.
    • A downturn in real estate, since many of its loans are secured by real estate.
    • Local economic slowdowns in the markets where it operates.
    • Fraud and cybersecurity incidents.
    • Interest rate swings that change what it earns on loans and pays on deposits.
    From Hope Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
