    What Assured Guaranty does

    Assured Guaranty is a holding company based in Bermuda. Through its subsidiaries it sells credit protection, mainly financial guaranty insurance, to the U.S. and non-U.S. public finance (including infrastructure) and structured finance markets. If the issuer of an insured bond or loan misses a scheduled payment, Assured Guaranty must pay the shortfall of that payment to the holder.

    It markets this insurance directly to issuers and underwriters of public finance and structured finance securities and to investors. Most guaranteed obligations are issued in the U.S. and the United Kingdom, with others in Western Europe, Australia and other places. It also writes specialty insurance and reinsurance on deals that look like its structured finance exposures. It earns money from premiums and from investing its assets.

    It also takes part in asset management through its ownership interest in Sound Point Capital Management and related affiliates. In January 2026 it bought Warwick Company (UK) Limited, the owner of Assured Life Reinsurance, a Bermuda life reinsurance company.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Large single or correlated insured exposures could cause big losses.
    • Losses on insured obligations could far exceed what it expects, or recoveries fall short.
    • Public finance issuers it insures may face big budget deficits.
    • Narrow credit spreads could lower demand for its financial guaranty insurance.
    • Credit losses and changes in interest rates could hurt its investments.
    From Assured Guaranty's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
