    What Southside Bancshares does

    Southside Bancshares is the holding company for Southside Bank, a Texas state bank headquartered in Tyler, Texas, formed in 1960. It describes itself as a community-focused financial institution serving individuals, businesses, municipal entities and nonprofit organizations. It operates through 53 branches, 12 of them in grocery stores, plus wealth management, trust, brokerage and loan offices.

    It earns money mainly from interest on loans and from fees for its services. Its consumer loans include home loans, home equity loans, home improvement loans and auto loans. Its commercial loans include working capital loans, equipment loans, commercial real estate loans, municipal loans and construction loans. It takes deposits in savings, money market, checking and CD accounts and offers investment management and trust services.

    At the end of 2025 its total assets were $8.51 billion and its total deposits were $6.87 billion. It says it has paid a cash dividend every year since 1970.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Earnings that depend on interest rates it does not control.
    • Credit risk: borrowers who do not repay, and credit policies that may not avoid losses.
    • A high concentration of loans secured by real estate, which a real estate decline would hurt.
    • Cyberattacks, security failures and fraud that could cause losses and hurt its reputation.
    • Weak economic conditions in the United States.
    From Southside Bancshares's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
