    What Olin does

    Olin makes chemicals and ammunition at capital-intensive plants. It has three segments: Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls, Epoxy, and Winchester.

    Chlor Alkali Products and Vinyls, which made up 54% of 2025 sales, makes chlorine, caustic soda, and vinyls ingredients such as EDC and VCM, which go into PVC for products like siding, pipe and auto parts. It also makes chlorinated organic chemicals. Chlorine and caustic soda are made together from salt, and are used in water treatment, paper, soaps and many other chemicals.

    Epoxy, 20% of sales, makes epoxy materials and their ingredients, including acetone, phenol and epoxy resins. Winchester, 26% of sales, makes sporting ammunition, reloading components, small caliber military ammunition, industrial cartridges and clay targets, and earns revenue on contracted U.S. military projects.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak results in economic and industry downturns.
    • Falling average selling prices because of the cyclical nature of its products.
    • Rising raw material, energy, utility and transportation costs.
    • Reliance on a limited number of third-party suppliers for key feedstocks.
    • Operating hazards at its plants, and climate-related physical risks to its facilities.
    From Olin's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
