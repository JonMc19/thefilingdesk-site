    What AdvanSix does

    AdvanSix is a U.S. chemical maker that Honeywell spun off in 2016. It runs five manufacturing plants in the United States and sells materials used in building and construction, farming, plastics, packaging, paints, adhesives and electronics.

    It has three product groups. Nylon solutions are Nylon 6 resin, which customers turn into fibers, plastics and films for carpets, car parts, sports clothing and food packaging, and caprolactam, the main ingredient of that resin. Plant nutrients are mostly ammonium sulfate, a fertilizer that supplies nitrogen and sulfur, sold mainly to distributors, farm cooperatives and retailers in North and South America. Chemical intermediates are products such as acetone, phenol and cyclohexanone, which go into solvents, paints, coatings, adhesives and herbicides.

    Most of this comes from its Hopewell, Virginia plant, where one process feeds the next. The company says the plant is the world's largest single-site producer of ammonium sulfate and one of the largest of caprolactam. At the end of 2025 it employed about 1,410 people.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the industries it sells to, which can sharply change its cash flows.
    • Higher costs for raw materials such as cumene, natural gas and sulfur that it cannot pass on to customers.
    • Downtime or accidents at its few production plants, including hazards of making and shipping chemicals.
    • Losing one or more of its significant customers.
    • Needing large amounts of capital for its plants and growth projects, which may not be available on good terms.
    From AdvanSix's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
