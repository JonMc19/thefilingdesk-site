    What Ring Energy does

    Ring Energy explores for, develops and produces oil and natural gas, with all of its properties in the Permian Basin of Texas. It is based in The Woodlands, Texas. Its drilling targets the Northwest Shelf and the Central Basin Platform, two producing areas of the basin.

    It earns money by selling the oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids it produces. Its reserves lean toward oil: approximately 59% of its proved reserves at the end of 2025 were oil. It sells to end users, marketers and other purchasers near pipelines, and the company says three customers accounted for 89% of its sales in 2025.

    In March 2025 it closed the Lime Rock acquisition, which added oil and gas assets. Its production reached a record that year, even as it cut capital spending in response to lower commodity prices and drilled fewer wells. The company says competition in the oil and gas industry is intense.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A long or deep drop in oil and gas prices could hurt its results and its ability to meet commitments.
    • Drilling is high risk, and wells may not find oil or gas in commercial amounts.
    • Much of its proved acreage is undeveloped, which raises the risk of its plans.
    • Lower prices could shrink its bank borrowing base and force earlier debt repayment.
    • Hedging can limit gains or cause losses.
    From Ring Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
