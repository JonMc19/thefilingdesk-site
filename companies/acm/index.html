    What AECOM does

    AECOM is a professional services firm that advises, plans, designs and manages infrastructure projects for governments, businesses and other organizations around the world. Its work includes advisory, planning, consulting, architectural and engineering design, construction and program management, and investment and development services. Its main markets are transportation, facilities, water, environmental and energy.

    The company reports three segments: Americas, International and AECOM Capital. Americas serves public and private clients in the United States, Canada and Latin America. International serves clients in other regions. AECOM Capital develops real estate and earns income from development sales and management fees. In its professional services work, AECOM mostly earns money by billing for its employees' time on client projects, so managing staff costs matters to its results.

    AECOM says it ranks first by design revenue among architectural and engineering design firms in an industry survey by Engineering News-Record. Its fiscal year ends on the Friday closest to September 30. It sold its Management Services business and its self-performed construction businesses in earlier years.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition that could reduce its revenue, profit and market share.
    • Losing government contracts, or governments changing or ending contracts before they are completed.
    • Failing to meet guaranteed contract schedules or performance requirements.
    • Variable-rate debt that raises its interest costs when rates rise.
    • Working in high-security-risk places abroad, which could harm its people or add costs.
    From AECOM's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 19, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
