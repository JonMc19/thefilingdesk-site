    What Generac does

    Generac, founded in 1959, makes backup and prime power equipment and energy technology for homes and businesses. It sells standby generators for homes, shops and factories, portable and mobile generators, energy storage systems, and devices and software to manage energy use. Its customers are in the residential, commercial, data center, telecom, rental and industrial markets.

    It sells through a mix of channels and relies on independent dealers and distribution partners. Demand for most of its products rises with power outages, which are unpredictable. It says it holds a top position in North American power equipment, and it has an International segment that gives it access to markets outside the United States, reached largely through acquisitions.

    Generac has added large diesel generators that it says let it sell to data centers. It is also building connected energy products and services for homes and businesses and uses natural gas generators in microgrid projects for commercial and industrial customers. Microgrids are small local power networks that can run on their own. It says demand from data centers is hard to project and may not last.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand that swings with unpredictable power outages.
    • Higher costs or short supply of raw materials, key parts and labor.
    • Depending on a small number of outside manufacturers and single-source suppliers.
    • Data center demand that is hard to forecast and may not last.
    • Reliance on independent dealers and distribution partners.
    From Generac's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
