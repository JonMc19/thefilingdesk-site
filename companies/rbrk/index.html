    What Rubrik does

    Rubrik sells software that protects companies' data from cyberattacks and helps them recover it if an attack succeeds. Its main product, Rubrik Security Cloud, runs as a cloud service and secures data in company data centers, cloud services, software-as-a-service applications and identity systems. It combines backup and recovery with data security in one platform. In February 2026 it began selling Rubrik Agent Cloud, a tool for monitoring and controlling AI agents that act inside a company's systems.

    Rubrik earns money mainly by selling subscriptions to Rubrik Security Cloud through its own sales team and a network of channel partners. It uses a "land and expand" approach: it first signs a customer to secure one type of data, then sells more subscriptions as the customer adds data and applications. The company says its business grows with its customers' business data.

    Rubrik says it derives substantially all of its revenue from this platform. It also relies on third-party cloud providers to host its products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Slower growth than it has had recently, which the company says is hard to predict.
    • A history of operating losses and no assurance that it will become profitable.
    • Customers not renewing or expanding their subscriptions, or the company failing to win new customers.
    • Its dependence on one platform for substantially all revenue, and on outside cloud providers to host it.
    • Flaws or failures in its products, or in its use of AI tools, that could harm its reputation.
    From Rubrik's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
