    What Macerich does

    Macerich buys, owns, develops, redevelops, manages and leases shopping centers across the United States, mainly regional malls. It is a real estate investment trust, or REIT. Its customers are the retailers and other businesses that rent space in its centers.

    It earns money mainly from rent and related charges paid by those tenants. It runs its centers with on-site property managers and says many owners, developers and managers of malls and other retail property compete with it, both for acquisitions and for tenants. In 2024 it began what it calls the Path Forward Plan, which aims to reduce debt, invest in its key properties and bring some joint venture properties under its control.

    As of December 31, 2025, it owned or had an interest in 37 regional retail centers, including office, hotel and residential space next to them, and one community center, with about 39 million square feet of leasable area. In 2025 it bought Crabtree Mall in Raleigh, North Carolina, for $290.0 million, and sold Wilton Mall in New York.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Investing mainly in shopping centers, which face risks outside its control.
    • Many centers are concentrated in certain regions, so local economic conditions matter more.
    • Being unable to renew leases or re-let space on good terms, leaving excess vacant space.
    • Its acquisition, development and redevelopment strategies, including the Path Forward Plan, may not succeed.
    • Debt that must be refinanced, and the effect of higher interest rates.
    From Macerich's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2021, earnings per share fell 54.3% a year and net income 51.9% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 35% and diluted each share's earnings. Macerich then lost money in fiscal 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
