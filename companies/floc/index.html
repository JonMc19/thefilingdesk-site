    What Flowco does

    Flowco sells equipment and services that help oil and natural gas wells keep producing after they are drilled. Its main technologies are high pressure gas lift, conventional gas lift and plunger lift, which are different ways to lift oil and gas to the surface as a well's natural pressure falls, along with vapor recovery units that capture methane and other gases that would otherwise escape. Its digital tools monitor and control the equipment remotely.

    It reports two segments: Production Solutions (the lift systems and digital tools) and Natural Gas Technologies (equipment that captures and uses emissions and other natural gas products). It earns revenue throughout a well's long production life, and says it had a fleet of over 4,600 active systems at the end of 2025. Its customers include large and independent oil and gas producers in every major U.S. onshore region.

    Flowco is based in Houston, Texas, and works only in the United States. It uses an "Up-C" structure, in which earlier owners keep their stake in a partnership under the listed company and can exchange it for shares, and a tax receivable agreement gives them a share of certain tax savings.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing one or more significant customers, and credit losses from customers.
    • Competition, and failing to develop new products and technologies.
    • Hazards in oil and gas operations where its products are used, which could bring liabilities.
    • Rising costs or shortages of raw materials, and inflation squeezing its margins.
    • Restrictions in its credit agreement that limit what it can do.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak. Accruals ratio: net income minus cash from operations, over average total assets; lower means profit is backed by cash.
