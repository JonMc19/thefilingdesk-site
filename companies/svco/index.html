    What Silvaco Group does

    Silvaco sells software and design building blocks to semiconductor companies. Engineers use its products to improve chip manufacturing processes and to bring chips to market more efficiently. Its customers include chip foundries, makers that design and manufacture their own chips, and companies that design chips but have others build them.

    It has three product groups. Technology computer-aided design (TCAD) software models and improves manufacturing processes and device performance, including newer tools that apply machine learning to process development. Electronic design automation (EDA) software is used to design, simulate and check chips, and includes circuit simulation and layout tools. Semiconductor intellectual property (SIP) is a set of ready-made chip design blocks, such as standard cells, memory compilers and interface technology. Its SIP line grew through acquisitions, most notably Mixel.

    It makes money from software licenses, from maintenance and services, and from SIP. The company is headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and has 12 offices worldwide. It had 406 employees at the end of 2025. It says demand for its tools depends on the semiconductor and electronics industries.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It operates in highly competitive industries and must keep meeting customers' demand for new technology.
    • Its growth depends mainly on the semiconductor and electronics systems industries.
    • It relies on sustaining or growing its software license, maintenance and service revenue.
    • Customers merging could leave it with fewer customers that have more bargaining power.
    • Its global operations bring compliance obligations and exposure to currency swings.
