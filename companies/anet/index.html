    Arista Networks financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Arista Networks, Inc. · NYSE: ANET Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 17, 2026 · 0001596532-26-000013 Updated October 7, 2026

    Arista Networks' revenue rose 28.6% to $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 42.8%, up from 42.0% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion, up 15.7%. It paid out $1.6 billion in dividends and buybacks, 38% of free cash flow. The diluted share count fell 0.4%.

    How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation, and this site does not show share prices.
