    What Lithia Motors does

    Lithia Motors, which also uses the Driveway name, sells and services cars and light trucks. It says it is the largest global automotive retailer. At the end of 2025 it operated 455 locations carrying 54 brands in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada.

    It sells new and used vehicles and also makes money from financing and insurance products, vehicle repair and service, and parts. It runs a captive finance arm, a business that lends to its own customers, and offers fleet management. Customers can shop in stores or online through its Driveway e-commerce site. The company says the finance and insurance business earns higher margins than vehicle sales.

    Its stores operate under franchise agreements with car makers, so its sales depend on what those makers supply. New vehicles from five makers, Honda, Toyota, Ford, BMW and Stellantis, account for 25% of its sales. It grows partly by buying other dealerships.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Sensitivity of car sales to economic conditions and the availability of affordable vehicle financing.
    • Dependence on car makers for supply, vehicle quality and franchise agreements.
    • Intense competition among dealers and from new brands and online sellers.
    • Changes to how manufacturers distribute vehicles, which could disrupt its model.
    • Natural disasters, bad weather and public health emergencies.
    From Lithia Motors's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
