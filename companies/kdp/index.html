    What Keurig Dr Pepper does

    Keurig Dr Pepper makes, markets, distributes and sells hot and cold drinks and Keurig single-serve coffee brewers in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean and other markets. Its brands include Dr Pepper, Canada Dry, Mott's, A&W, Peñafiel, GHOST, 7UP, Snapple and Green Mountain Coffee Roasters, and it offers more than 125 owned, licensed and partner brands. It was created on July 9, 2018, by combining Keurig with DPS, a group of beverage brands built through acquisitions.

    It has three segments. U.S. Refreshment Beverages makes concentrates, syrups and finished drinks and sells them to bottlers, distributors, retailers and fountain customers such as fast food restaurants. U.S. Coffee sells Keurig brewers, coffee and K-Cup pods, and also makes pods for partner brands such as Starbucks, Dunkin', Folgers and Peet's. International covers Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean and other markets. Its largest retailer, Walmart, made up approximately 16% of net sales in 2025. It names Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Starbucks, J.M. Smucker, Kraft Heinz and Nestlé as its main competitors, though it says many are also its partners or customers.

    In August 2025 it agreed to buy JDE Peet's, a coffee company whose brands include Jacobs, L'OR and Peet's. It has also announced plans to separate its beverage and coffee businesses into two independent public companies after that deal closes. It has approximately 30,600 employees, primarily in North America.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Disruptions to its manufacturing, distribution or supply chain, including higher costs for ingredients and materials.
    • Its dependence on third-party bottlers and distributors for a large part of its business.
    • Changes in how retailers operate, or lower sales to any of its key customers.
    • Losing its relationships with the outside brand owners whose drinks and pods it makes or distributes.
    • The JDE Peet's deal may not close on time or at all, or may be harder and costlier to integrate than expected.
    From Keurig Dr Pepper's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
