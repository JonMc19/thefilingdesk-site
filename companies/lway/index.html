    What Lifeway Foods does

    Lifeway Foods makes and sells kefir, a tart, cultured dairy drink, and other dairy products. It was founded in 1986 in the Chicago area and says it is the largest producer and marketer of kefir in the U.S. It sells under the Lifeway, Fresh Made and GlenOaks Farms brands and makes private label products for some customers.

    Drinkable kefir is its main product. It also sells European-style soft cheeses, cream, drinkable yogurt, ProBugs kefir for children, and butter and sour cream. It sells through three routes: directly to retailers, which was approximately 51% of 2025 net sales; through distributors, approximately 47%; and a small direct-store-delivery service in the Chicago area, approximately 2%.

    It makes products itself and through co-packers. Its competitors include Danone, General Mills, Chobani and Nestle, and it also competes with private label products and non-dairy items.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition from larger food companies and private label products, and fights for retailer shelf space.
    • Pressure from retailers for lower prices.
    • Its strategy of leaning on its brands and new products may not work.
    • Changing consumer tastes, and other makers mislabeling their products as kefir.
    • Supply chain interruptions, including reliance on single suppliers or co-packers for some items.
    From Lifeway Foods's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
