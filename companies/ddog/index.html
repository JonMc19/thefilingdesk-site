    What Datadog does

    Datadog sells cloud software that lets technology teams see what is happening inside their computer systems and applications. Its platform collects data such as metrics, logs and traces from a company's servers and software, and shows it in one place so teams can find and fix problems, watch for security threats and track how people use their apps. It has products for infrastructure monitoring, application performance, log management, cloud security and incident response, plus new AI tools.

    Datadog sells subscriptions to its software to organizations of all sizes. As of December 31, 2025 it had about 32,700 customers in over 160 countries. It says it follows a land-and-expand model: customers start with a few products that are easy to adopt, and often use more over time or add other products. It also offers a free tier and free trials to attract developers. It says its competitors include other makers of monitoring, security and software delivery tools.

    Datadog's products run on cloud infrastructure from outside providers, and it sells to large companies as well as smaller ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its growth depends on existing customers renewing and buying more, and on winning new customers.
    • It has a history of operating losses and may not stay profitable.
    • It relies on outside cloud providers to host its products, so a disruption there could affect its service.
    • Errors or bugs in its software, or a security breach, could harm its business and reputation.
    • The markets it sells into are competitive, and it must keep up with fast-changing technology.
    From Datadog's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
