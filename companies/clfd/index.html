    What Clearfield does

    Clearfield makes fiber management, fiber protection and fiber delivery products. These are the cabinets, panels, enclosures and cable assemblies that connect and protect fiber-optic lines between a provider's network and homes, businesses and mobile towers. It sells them to broadband providers mainly in North America: small and mid-sized telephone carriers, utilities, cities, cable companies, regional providers and national carriers, plus some customers in Europe, Canada, Mexico and the Caribbean.

    Clearfield says its products let providers connect homes faster and with less labor and material. It reports one segment. In November 2025 it sold its Nestor Cables business, now shown as discontinued operations. Much of its sales go through distributors. In its fiscal year ended September 30, 2025, one distributor made up approximately 18% of net sales, and a second distributor made up a smaller share.

    Clearfield had approximately 243 full-time employees at the end of fiscal 2025, all in the United States. Sales peak in its third and fourth fiscal quarters.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Shortages of materials, and reliance on single-source suppliers and outside manufacturers.
    • Changes in trade policy and inflation that raise costs.
    • Depending on a few distributor customers for a large share of sales.
    • Customers merging, which can reduce or delay sales.
    • Needing to keep launching new products that the market accepts.
    From Clearfield's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak. Rule of 40: revenue growth plus free cash flow margin; software investors treat 40% or more as the bar. Accruals ratio: net income minus cash from operations, over average total assets; lower means profit is backed by cash.
