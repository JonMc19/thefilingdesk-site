    What National Energy Services Reunited does

    National Energy Services Reunited (NESR) provides oilfield services in the Middle East and North Africa. Its customers are oil and gas companies, mainly national oil companies, large integrated oil companies and independent operators. It has major operations in Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, the United Arab Emirates, Iraq, Algeria, Egypt and Libya.

    It earns revenue from two groups of services. Production Services are used when a well is completed and producing, and include hydraulic fracturing, coiled tubing, cementing and artificial lift. Drilling and Evaluation Services include rigs, directional drilling, drilling fluids, well testing and wireline logging. In some long-term deals it manages production for a customer and is paid from the cash flow the field generates.

    Four customers each made up a large share of revenue in 2025, and the largest alone accounted for 49%. NESR says its competitors include large multinational firms and smaller local providers. It employed 7,352 employees and independent contractors at the end of 2025. The company grew by acquisition after it formed in 2017, most recently buying a minority stake in a reservoir testing and consulting business in 2022.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower oil and gas prices or reduced customer spending would cut demand for its services.
    • A few customers, and one in particular, account for much of its revenue.
    • Its operations are concentrated in the Middle East and North Africa, where conflict or instability could disrupt work.
    • Larger competitors with more resources could win work or push prices down.
    • Failing to integrate acquired businesses would limit its growth.
