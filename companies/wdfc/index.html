    What WD-40 does

    WD-40 makes and sells maintenance products that loosen, lubricate and protect metal and moving parts, used in workshops, factories and homes. Its best-known product is the WD-40 Multi-Use Product, a spray that acts as a lubricant, rust preventative, penetrant and moisture displacer. For decades it was the company's only product.

    The company sells in more than 176 countries and territories, mainly through hardware stores, auto parts shops, industrial distributors, mass retailers, home centers, grocery stores and online sellers. Its sales come from two product groups: maintenance products, which are sold worldwide, and homecare and cleaning products, which are sold mainly in North America and Australia. The company calls its homecare and cleaning brands "harvest brands" that it supports with less marketing over time. Its growth plan focuses on maintenance products, including the WD-40 Specialist line for professionals.

    WD-40 says its markets are highly competitive and that large retailers have strong negotiating power. It reports in three segments: the Americas, EIMEA (Europe, India, the Middle East and Africa) and Asia-Pacific. It has 753 employees in 19 countries.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition that could reduce its sales and profits.
    • Damage to the reputation of its main brands, above all WD-40.
    • Its reliance on a few outside contract manufacturers, logistics providers and suppliers.
    • Its dependence on a small number of key customers.
    • Changes in its relationships with the distributors that sell its products in some markets.
    From WD-40's annual report on Form 10-K, filed October 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
