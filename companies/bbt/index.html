    What Beacon Financial does

    Beacon Financial is a bank holding company based in Boston. Its main subsidiary, Beacon Bank & Trust, has 147 banking offices across New England and New York. It offers business and retail banking, including cash management, foreign exchange services, online and mobile banking, and consumer and residential loans.

    Its wealth management arm, Clarendon Private, is a registered investment advisor. Together with the bank's Trust and Investments Division, it serves individuals, families, endowments and foundations. The bank also runs a commercial payment processing business for payroll service companies, whose deposits can swing by hundreds of millions of dollars as payroll cycles pass.

    On September 1, 2025, the company completed a merger of equals with Brookline Bancorp, changing its name from Berkshire Hills Bancorp and its ticker from BHLB to BBT. The merger roughly doubled the business: loans and leases reached $18.0 billion at the end of 2025. For accounting purposes, Brookline was treated as the acquirer, so the filing's history is Brookline's.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates that could hurt its results.
    • Loan losses, especially from its commercial real estate and commercial loans and leases, which it calls riskier than other loans.
    • Losing deposits or relying on costlier wholesale funding to replace them.
    • Weak economic conditions in New England and New York, where it operates.
    • Competition from other financial firms and the cost of new technology.
