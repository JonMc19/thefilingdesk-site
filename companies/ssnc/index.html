    What SS&C Technologies does

    SS&C Technologies provides software and outsourced services to the financial services and healthcare industries. It says it is the world's largest hedge fund and private equity administrator, as well as a mutual fund transfer agent. Fund administration means keeping a fund's books, records and reports for its managers. Its other offerings cover securities accounting, back-office operations, performance and risk analytics, regulatory reporting and healthcare information processing.

    It sells in two forms: software-enabled services, in which clients hire it to run business processes using its software, and subscription-based cloud solutions that it hosts, and specialized software products that clients run in their own facilities. The company was founded in 1986, is based in Windsor, Connecticut, and has more than 28,000 employees and more than 100 offices in 35 countries. It says it has more than 23,000 clients.

    In 2025 it generated 67% of its revenues from clients in the United States and 33% from clients outside it. Its largest client accounted for less than 5% of revenues. It has grown through acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It may not get the expected benefits from acquisitions or may struggle to integrate them.
    • Its revenues may fall if activity in the securities markets declines.
    • Significant competition may force price cuts, lower margins or lost market share.
    • Cyber-attacks, security breaches or IT failures, at the company or its service providers.
    • It may be unable to keep up with rapid changes in technology, industry standards and regulation.
