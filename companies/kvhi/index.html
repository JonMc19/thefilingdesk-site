    What KVH Industries does

    KVH sells satellite and cellular connectivity to ships and other mobile users at sea and on land, mostly commercial and leisure customers in the maritime market. It provides high-speed internet and internet phone service over satellite, together with 5G and LTE cellular service. It also licenses movies, television, news and music to commercial ships, and sells related services such as cybersecurity, email and crew internet.

    Services are the core of the business. Airtime on low-earth-orbit and high-throughput satellite networks made up 82% of net sales in 2025, and it earns this revenue mainly by reselling satellite capacity it buys. Hardware is a smaller part. It mainly distributes products made by other companies that work with low-earth-orbit satellites, and it also makes satellite antennas for ships and receive-only satellite television antennas.

    KVH is winding down its own manufacturing in Middletown, Rhode Island, and plans to stop almost all manufacturing by the end of 2026. It has operations in Denmark, the United Kingdom, the Philippines and Singapore, and it sells in many countries around the world.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a history of losses and may take longer than expected to become profitable, or never do so.
    • It must buy satellite capacity and resell it at a sufficient profit.
    • Its planned switch to relying on third-party hardware may not work.
    • More sales of third-party airtime and products could lower its margins.
    • It depends on its executive officers and key employees and must manage changes in its business.
