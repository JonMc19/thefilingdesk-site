    What Dime Community Bancshares does

    Dime Community Bancshares is the holding company for Dime Community Bank, a commercial bank founded in 1910 and based in Hauppauge, New York. The holding company mainly owns the bank's stock. The bank also owns Dime Abstract, a broker of title insurance services.

    The bank takes deposits from businesses, consumers and local governments in its market area. It uses those deposits, plus money it earns and borrows, mainly to make loans and buy securities. Its loans include commercial real estate, multi-family mortgage, residential mortgage, commercial and consumer, home equity, and construction and land loans. It also invests in mortgage-backed securities, US Treasury securities, state and local government bonds and corporate bonds.

    It offers services beyond loans and deposits, including merchant card processing, cash and treasury management, escrow accounts, lockbox processing, online banking, remote deposit capture, safe deposit boxes and individual retirement accounts. It also offers programs that extend federal deposit insurance coverage on large deposits, and investment services.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Having much of its loan portfolio in real estate loans in one region.
    • Larger credit losses than it expects.
    • Limits that regulators could place on its commercial real estate lending.
    • Changes in interest rates that cut its profit.
    • Failing to comply with anti-money-laundering, fair lending and other banking rules.
