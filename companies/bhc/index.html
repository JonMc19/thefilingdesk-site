    What Bausch Health Companies does

    Bausch Health is a specialty drug and medical device company. It makes and sells branded and generic medicines, over-the-counter products and aesthetic medical devices, mainly in stomach and liver, nerve and skin care. Through its roughly 88% ownership of Bausch + Lomb, it also sells eye health products such as contact lenses, intraocular lenses and eye surgery equipment. Its products are sold directly or indirectly in about 90 countries.

    It reports five segments: Salix (stomach and liver products sold in the U.S.), International, Solta Medical (aesthetic devices), Diversified (neurology, dentistry and other U.S. products) and Bausch + Lomb. Its 2025 revenues were $10,266 million. Xifaxan, a Salix product, brought in $2,212 million of that.

    The company has said it plans to separate Bausch + Lomb from the rest of Bausch Health, leaving two companies. It reports that this separation has been challenged and may be delayed or not completed.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The planned Bausch + Lomb separation may be delayed, challenged or fail to bring the expected benefits.
    • Large debt that restricts how it runs the business.
    • Legal and government proceedings, and scrutiny of its marketing and sales practices.
    • Reliance on third parties to meet their obligations to the company.
    • Generic drug makers challenging the patents on key products, including Xifaxan.
    From Bausch Health Companies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
