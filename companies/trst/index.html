    What TrustCo Bank Corp NY does

    TrustCo Bank Corp NY is a savings and loan holding company based in Glenville, New York. Its main subsidiary, Trustco Bank, is a federal savings bank that serves individuals and businesses. At the end of 2025 TrustCo had $6.4 billion in total assets and $5.6 billion in deposits.

    The bank takes deposits and makes loans and investments, which is the largest part of its business, and it offers personal and business banking. Its trust department, Trustco Financial Services, acts as executor of estates and trustee of personal trusts, and provides wealth management, estate planning and custodial services. A subsidiary, Trustco Realty Corp., is a real estate investment trust that holds residential mortgage loans and mortgage-backed securities.

    At year-end 2025 the bank operated 134 banking offices and 154 automatic teller machines in New York, Florida, Vermont, Massachusetts and New Jersey. Its direct competitors for deposits have been commercial banks, savings associations and credit unions, from local banks to branches of the largest U.S. financial institutions. The company says the bank accounted for substantially all of its 2025 net income and assets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates could cut its net interest income, its main source of income.
    • Risks in its lending, including loans with fixed rates and longer terms than its deposits.
    • Strong competition for deposits and loans from banks, savings institutions and credit unions.
    • Heavy bank regulation, including capital rules and limits on the dividends it can receive from the bank.
    • Cybersecurity, third-party and technology risks.
