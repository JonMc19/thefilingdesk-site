    What NCR Atleos does

    NCR Atleos makes and runs ATMs and the services around them for banks, credit unions, fintech companies and retailers. It sells ATM and interactive teller machine hardware and software, installs and maintains the machines, and runs the whole ATM operation for banks that want to hand it off, a package it calls ATM as a Service. Interactive teller machines connect customers by video to remote bank staff for tasks like opening accounts.

    It reports three segments. Self-Service Banking sells and services ATM hardware, software and managed services. Network gives financial companies access to its ATM network, including its Allpoint network for fee-free cash withdrawals, and offers branding and management of ATMs for retailers. Its LibertyX service lets consumers buy and sell Bitcoin. Telecommunications & Technology provides network and infrastructure services to businesses through communications service providers and technology makers.

    The company became independent in October 2023, when it separated from NCR Corporation, now called NCR Voyix.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Growing its business and developing new products in a fast-changing, competitive field.
    • Holding large amounts of vault cash, which carries risk of loss and cost swings.
    • A large debt load, with variable interest rates and covenants that limit what the company can do.
    • Dependence on outside suppliers to make and deliver its products on time.
    • Tariffs and other trade measures, and risks of operating in many countries.
    From NCR Atleos's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
