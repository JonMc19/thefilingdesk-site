    What Fulton Financial does

    Fulton Financial is a Pennsylvania bank and financial holding company. Through its bank, Fulton Bank, it offers consumer banking, commercial banking and wealth management, mainly in a five-state area: Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland, New Jersey and Virginia. It stresses relationship banking and a community-oriented style.

    Consumers get checking and savings accounts, certificates of deposit, mortgages, home equity loans and lines of credit, and auto, student and personal loans. Businesses, generally small and mid-sized ones with annual gross revenue under $500 million, get commercial real estate, commercial and industrial and construction loans, equipment leasing and cash management. Wealth management covers investment management, trust, brokerage, insurance and advice. As a bank, it earns money mainly from interest on loans and securities and from fees on its services.

    A large part of its loan portfolio is commercial loans, commercial mortgages and construction loans, but it says it does not depend on one or a few customers. In November 2025 it agreed to merge with Blue Foundry, a deal it expects to close around April 1, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic and market conditions hurting the business.
    • Interest rate changes that affect loan demand, funding costs, borrowers' ability to repay and securities values.
    • Credit risk from a loan book with many commercial mortgage loans.
    • Needing to raise capital, or failing to meet capital standards.
    • Heavy regulation and the risks of the pending merger with Blue Foundry.
    From Fulton Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
