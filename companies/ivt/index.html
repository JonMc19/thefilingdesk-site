    What InvenTrust Properties does

    InvenTrust Properties is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns, leases, redevelops, buys and manages shopping centers. It focuses on grocery-anchored neighborhood and community centers, and on power centers, which are larger centers with big stores, in the Sun Belt region of the United States. Its shares have traded on the New York Stock Exchange since October 2021.

    It earns money from rent paid by the stores that lease space. At the end of 2025 it owned 73 retail properties with about 11.6 million square feet of leasable space. Its strategy is to buy retail properties in Sun Belt markets, sell some when the chance arises, and keep a flexible balance sheet.

    The company began in 2004 as Inland American Real Estate Trust and took its present name in 2015. It had 103 full-time employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A downturn that leads retail tenants to default on rent.
    • Shoppers moving from stores to online buying.
    • Geographic concentration, which ties its results to a few regional economies.
    • Its success depends on its anchor tenants staying and succeeding.
    • Struggling small shop tenants, and delays in re-leasing empty space.
