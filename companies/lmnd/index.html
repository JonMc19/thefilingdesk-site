    What Lemonade does

    Lemonade is an insurance company that sells policies online and through an app. It offers renters, homeowners, pet, car and life insurance. It owns insurance carriers in the United States and Europe, including the UK, and runs its own technology. A chatbot called AI Maya signs up customers, and a second bot, AI Jim, takes claims. Lemonade says Maya and its software links sell 98% of its policies.

    Lemonade earns premiums from its policyholders. To lessen the swings that weather and other events cause in claims, it buys reinsurance, which means other insurers carry a share of the premiums and the claims. It says it has a Giveback program: after buying a policy, customers pick a charity to support, and Lemonade gives remaining money to those causes instead of keeping it.

    Lemonade says roughly 55% of its claims were automated at the end of 2025, processed with no human involved. It competes with other insurers, including traditional companies that sell mostly through agents.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses, and it may not reach or keep profitability.
    • Denying claims or paying them wrongly or late.
    • Its artificial intelligence models may not work as planned, which could mean bad pricing or underwriting.
    • Intense competition among insurers.
    • Keeping enough capital to satisfy insurance regulators, and needing to raise more.
    From Lemonade's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
