    What Granite Ridge Resources does

    Granite Ridge Resources owns interests in oil and natural gas wells and gets its revenue from the oil and gas those wells produce. It says it aims to give shareholders exposure like energy private equity, through operated partnerships and traditional non-operated assets. It owns assets in six unconventional basins across the United States.

    Most of its work is as a non-operator: other companies drill and run the wells, and Granite Ridge pays its share of the costs and receives its share of the output. It says it invests alongside operators it describes as experienced, in a large number of opportunities, and aims to keep leverage low. It is managed through an external manager, and the loss of a key member of the manager's team is one of its risks.

    Granite Ridge is a Delaware corporation formed in May 2022. It began trading after a business combination with a special purpose acquisition company in October 2022, which also brought in the net assets of certain funds managed by Grey Rock Energy.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying on other companies to run the wells and meet their obligations to it.
    • Depending on transportation and processing facilities owned by others.
    • Losing a key member of its manager's team, whose knowledge and relationships it relies on.
    • Falls in oil and natural gas prices, which have hurt it in the past.
    • Reserve estimates that prove wrong, and lease acreage that may expire before it is drilled.
    From Granite Ridge Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
