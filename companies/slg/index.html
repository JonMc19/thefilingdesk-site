    What SL Green Realty does

    SL Green is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, a company that owns property and passes most of its income on to shareholders. It owns, manages, develops and finances commercial real estate, mostly office buildings, in the New York area and mainly in midtown Manhattan.

    It makes money mainly by leasing space to tenants. At the end of 2025 its core portfolio held 44 properties, including 26 Manhattan office buildings, 5 Manhattan retail properties, 6 suburban office buildings and 2 residential buildings, plus 5 development or redevelopment properties. It also held 5 properties in what it calls its Alternative Strategy Portfolio. It owns some of these properties directly and some through joint ventures with partners, and it manages two buildings owned by others.

    Five properties, One Vanderbilt Avenue, 11 Madison Avenue, 420 Lexington Avenue, 1515 Broadway and 245 Park Avenue, brought in 36.6% of its annualized cash rent. The company says it faces strong competition for tenants, based on rent, location, lease terms and the condition of the building. It was formed in 1997 and runs its operations through an operating partnership.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker demand for office space in New York, especially midtown Manhattan.
    • Being unable to renew leases or find new tenants as leases expire.
    • Heavy reliance on a few buildings for a large part of its rent.
    • Strong competition from other owners for tenants.
    • Cost and delays on development projects that are still under construction.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2024, earnings per share fell 34.4% a year and net income 26.2% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 37%. SL Green Realty then lost money in fiscal 2025.
