    What Gartner does

    Gartner sells research, advice and events to business and technology leaders. It says it serves over 13,000 enterprises in approximately 90 countries and territories, across every major function and industry. Clients are mostly executives and their teams who use its insights to make decisions.

    It reports three segments. Business and Technology Insights sells subscriptions to research, guidance and tools. Conferences runs events where executives learn and network. Consulting does project work for clients. Insights subscription contracts typically run twelve months or longer, and at December 31, 2025, 77% of its contracts were multi-year.

    Gartner used to count Digital Markets, a business that helps software buyers and sellers connect, within Insights. It now reports it under "Other", and said it completed the sale of that business on February 5, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Clients not renewing their subscriptions, which depends on many factors.
    • Consulting work that is not recurring, so a failure to win new engagements cuts revenue.
    • Delays or problems in launching new products and services.
    • Acquisitions, dispositions and investments that carry substantial risks.
    • Government sales that depend on budgets and can be ended early.
    From Gartner's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
