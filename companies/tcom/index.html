    What Trip.com Group does

    Trip.com Group runs travel booking and travel content platforms, mainly for travelers in Asia and increasingly worldwide. It was founded in 1999, listed on Nasdaq in 2003 and on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 2021. Its brands are Ctrip and Qunar, two online travel agencies in China; Trip.com, an online travel agency for global travelers; and Skyscanner, a global travel search company.

    Its platform sells hotel rooms, transportation tickets, packaged tours and in-destination activities. Its ecosystem partners, such as hotels and airlines, offer these through the platform, and Trip.com Group also shows reviews and other content that users share about their travels. It says it recognizes revenue mainly from commissions it earns from its partners rather than from the value of each transaction. For example, it earns substantially all of its accommodation reservation revenue as commissions from hotel reservation partners.

    Its products and services through Trip.com are available in 27 languages, and through Skyscanner in 43 languages and over 50 countries and regions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • General declines or disruptions in the travel industry.
    • Failing to keep its relationships with hotels, airlines and other ecosystem partners.
    • Competition from other travel agencies and consolidators that could cost it market share.
    • Actions or claims under anti-monopoly and competition laws, which may bring fines or limits on its business.
    • Negative publicity about it or the travel industry that harms its reputation.
