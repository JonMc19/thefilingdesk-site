    What CNH Industrial does

    CNH makes and sells farm equipment and construction equipment, and finances its customers' purchases. Its farm products include tractors, harvesters, hay and forage equipment, planting equipment and sprayers, sold mainly under the Case IH and New Holland brands. Its construction products include excavators, loaders, graders, dozers and compact machines, sold mainly under the CASE and New Holland Construction brands. Customers include farmers, contractors, rental companies and local governments.

    CNH has three segments: Agriculture, Construction and Financial Services. The financial arm lends to buyers of new and used equipment and finances dealers' stock of machines. Sales to farmers depend on things like farm income, government subsidy programs, weather and the price of used equipment. Sales of construction equipment follow building activity and government spending on roads and other projects.

    It has operations in 32 countries and sells in about 166. It competes with large full-line makers, makers that specialize in one product, regional makers, and low-cost local makers in emerging markets. CNH says it is investing in precision farming technology, such as satellite positioning, automation and machine vision, and it bought Raven, Hemisphere GNSS and Augmenta to build that. The company says the farm equipment industry is in a cyclical downturn, especially for large farms in the Americas.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Problems with acquisitions, joint ventures or partners that could hurt its product lines.
    • Falling behind rivals in technology, including AI-enabled farm and construction equipment.
    • Cyberattacks or computer system failures that disrupt operations and expose data.
    • Currency devaluations that reduce the value of money it holds in other countries.
    • Having to pay for underfunded pension plans out of its operating cash.
