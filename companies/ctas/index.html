    What Cintas does

    Cintas provides products and services that help businesses keep their workplaces and workers clean, safe and presentable. It says it serves more than one million businesses, mostly in the United States, plus Canada and Latin America. Its offerings include uniforms, floor mats, mops, restroom supplies, workplace water, first aid and safety products, safety training, and fire extinguisher, sprinkler and alarm testing.

    It reports two main segments. Uniform Rental and Facility Services rents and services uniforms, mats, mops and shop towels, and cleans restrooms; it is by far the largest by revenue in the year ended May 31, 2026. First Aid and Safety Services sells first aid and safety products and workplace water. Fire protection and direct sales of uniforms make up the rest.

    In March 2026 Cintas agreed to buy UniFirst, another uniform and workwear supplier, in a deal it values at about $5.5 billion. UniFirst shareholders approved it in June 2026. The deal still needs regulatory approvals and other conditions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A weak economy could reduce demand from its business customers.
    • Competition could cut into its sales and margins.
    • The pending UniFirst acquisition may not close, or may be hard to integrate and may not bring the expected benefits.
    • Higher costs from suppliers and tariffs on products it sources.
    • Difficulty attracting and keeping employees, and keeping good relations with them.
    From Cintas's annual report on Form 10-K, filed July 29, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
