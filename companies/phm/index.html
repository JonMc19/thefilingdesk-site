    What PulteGroup does

    PulteGroup is a homebuilder. It buys and develops land, mainly for homes, and builds houses on it. It was organized in Michigan in 1956 and dates its founding to 1950. It has delivered over 875,000 homes. Homebuilding generated 98% of its 2025 consolidated revenues of $17.3 billion. The rest comes from financial services, which include mortgage banking, title and insurance agency operations.

    As of December 31, 2025, it operated out of 1,014 active communities in 47 markets across 26 states. Its brands include Centex, Pulte Homes, Del Webb, DiVosta Homes, and John Wieland Homes and Neighborhoods. It sells to first-time buyers, move-up buyers and active adult buyers. In 2025 it closed sales on 29,572 homes. Single-family detached homes made up 83% of closings, and the rest were attached homes such as townhomes, condominiums and duplexes.

    The company says national publicly traded builders have advantages over local builders, such as cheaper and more reliable financing and better access to land and labor. In 2025 it said weaker demand, driven by affordability problems, led it to slow its house starts and raise sales incentives.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher interest rates, less mortgage availability or higher costs of owning a home that keep buyers from buying.
    • A cyclical industry, with weaker demand when the economy or housing market declines.
    • Inflation and shortages of skilled labor and building materials that raise costs and delay deliveries.
    • Trouble buying suitable land, and write-downs if land values fall.
    • Warranty and other claims, and challenges from putting in a new enterprise resource planning system.
    From PulteGroup's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
