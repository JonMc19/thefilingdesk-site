    What NetApp does

    NetApp sells data storage and data management products to businesses. Its main software is ONTAP, which manages data across a company's own data centers, private clouds and public clouds. It sells all-flash and hybrid-flash storage systems, cloud-based storage services and data protection and security tools, and says its products work with Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud. It was founded in 1992 and is based in San Jose, California.

    NetApp has two segments. Hybrid Cloud sells storage systems and management software to help customers run data centers. Public Cloud sells products delivered mainly as a service, along with related support. It also sells professional and support services through itself and certified partners.

    It sells through a direct sales force and an ecosystem of partners, including the large cloud providers. Its customers come from many industries, such as financial services, government, healthcare, manufacturing, media and telecommunications. NetApp says it is positioning its products for artificial intelligence workloads.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak global economic and geopolitical conditions that hurt demand and revenue growth.
    • Falling behind rapid changes in technology or in the market for data storage.
    • Intense competition and industry consolidation.
    • A revenue shift to consumption-based models that could hurt revenue and profit in other areas.
    • Reliance on a limited number of suppliers for key components, and supply chain disruptions.
    From NetApp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
