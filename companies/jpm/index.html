    JPMorgan Chase financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    JPMORGAN CHASE & CO · NYSE: JPM Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 13, 2026 · 0001628280-26-008131 Updated October 7, 2026

    JPMorgan Chase's revenue rose 2.8% to $182.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $57.0 billion, down 2.4%. Its return on equity was 16.1%, down from 17.4% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 52.4%, up from 51.7% a year earlier. It paid out $48.2 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 3.4%.
