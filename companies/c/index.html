    Citigroup's revenue rose 5.6% to $85.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $14.3 billion, up 12.8%. Its return on equity was 6.8%, up from 6.1% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 64.7%, down from 66.4% a year earlier. It paid out $18.6 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 3.5%.
