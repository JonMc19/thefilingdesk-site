    What Hamilton Beach Brands does

    Hamilton Beach Brands designs, markets and distributes brand-name small electric household appliances, such as air fryers, blenders, coffee makers, food processors, indoor grills, irons, juicers, mixers, slow cookers and toasters. It also sells commercial products for restaurants, fast-food chains, bars and hotels, and provides connected devices and software for home healthcare management.

    It reports two segments. Home and Commercial Products sells to brick-and-mortar and online retailers, distributors and directly to consumers, mainly in North America. Health earns lease revenue from connected devices that it leases to specialty pharmacy networks and drug companies, and licensing revenue from its healthcare management software.

    It depends on a few large customers. Walmart accounted for about 29% of 2025 revenue and Amazon.com about 19%, and its five largest customers about 62%. It relies on outside suppliers, mainly in the Asia-Pacific region, for all of its products. It had about 660 employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying on a few big retailers for most of its sales, so losing one would hurt.
    • Weak consumer spending in North America.
    • Depending on outside suppliers in Asia-Pacific for all its products, some from a single supplier.
    • Competition and shifts in how people shop, including online and AI-driven shopping.
    • Challenges from its expansion into the health and wellness market.
    From Hamilton Beach Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
