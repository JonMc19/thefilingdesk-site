    What F&G Annuities & Life does

    F&G sells life insurance and retirement products. Through its insurance subsidiaries, Fidelity & Guaranty Life Insurance Company and its New York affiliate, it markets fixed indexed annuities, registered index-linked annuities and multi-year guarantee annuities. It also sells pension risk transfer deals, in which a company hands the pension payments it owes retirees to F&G, as well as indexed universal life insurance and institutional funding agreements.

    It serves retail customers through three distribution channels and institutional customers in two markets, using national marketing organizations and independent agents. Fidelity National Financial bought F&G on June 1, 2020. F&G says its insurance financial strength ratings have improved since then.

    F&G earns money mainly from the spread between what it earns investing customers' money and what it credits to them, and from fees. In 2025 it launched a reinsurance sidecar, an outside-funded vehicle that takes on some of its business. It says it wants a larger share of fee-based earnings, which need less capital.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A drop in its financial strength ratings, which could raise its costs and limit its sales.
    • Actual claims and policyholder behavior differing from its reserving assumptions.
    • Its ability to keep attracting and holding the national marketing organizations and independent agents that sell its products.
    • Heavy competition in the annuity and life insurance industry.
    • Debt terms that restrict its operations, and its need for continued access to capital.
    From F&G Annuities & Life's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
