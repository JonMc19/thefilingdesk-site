    What JBG Smith does

    JBG SMITH is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns, runs and develops mixed-use properties in and around Washington, D.C., mostly near Metro stations. Its main area is National Landing in Northern Virginia. It also earns fees from a business that provides real estate services to other owners.

    At the end of 2025 its operating portfolio had 39 assets: 15 apartment buildings with 6,519 units, 22 commercial buildings with 7.3 million square feet, and two land parcels it leases out under ground leases. It also had a development pipeline of 4.9 million square feet of potential development. It earns money mainly from rent.

    It runs its business through an operating partnership, JBG SMITH LP, of which the REIT owned 82.0% at the end of 2025. Five of its assets together produced 29.9% of its share of annualized rent.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large share of its portfolio is office space, where demand has been lower.
    • Its properties are concentrated in the Washington, D.C. area, so local conditions matter.
    • A significant share of revenue comes from U.S. federal government tenants.
    • Rent control or rent limits on its apartments.
    • Development and redevelopment costs and delays.
    From JBG Smith's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
