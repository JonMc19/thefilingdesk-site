    What IQVIA does

    IQVIA provides clinical research services, data and technology to drug makers, medical device companies, researchers, government agencies and other health care groups. It runs clinical trials on behalf of its clients and sells software and information about prescriptions, sales and treatments. Its data comes from about 150,000 suppliers and covers records on patients who are not identified by name. It says its information covers around 90% of the world's pharmaceuticals, as measured by 2024 sales.

    It has three reportable segments. Technology and Analytics Solutions sells cloud-based software and data insights. Research and Development Solutions runs clinical trials and related research for clients. Contract Sales and Medical Solutions provides outsourced sales and medical staff to life sciences companies. It says the segments support one another when a client buys several.

    IQVIA has about 93,000 employees in over 100 countries, with roughly 51,000 in research and development, 31,000 in technology and analytics, and 7,000 in contract sales and medical solutions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on data bought from third-party suppliers for its information services.
    • Failing to meet contract, regulatory and ethical standards in trials, which could be costly.
    • Underpricing contracts or running over its cost estimates on fixed-price work.
    • Security breaches or failures of its information systems, or those of its vendors.
    • Protecting its intellectual property, and claims that it infringes on others'.
    From IQVIA's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
