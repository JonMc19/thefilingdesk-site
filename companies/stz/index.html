    What Constellation Brands does

    Constellation Brands makes and sells beer, wine and spirits, with operations in the U.S., Mexico, New Zealand and Italy. Its beer brands include Modelo Especial, Corona Extra, Pacifico and Victoria. Its wine and spirits brands include Kim Crawford, Ruffino, The Prisoner Wine Company, Robert Mondavi Winery, Mi CAMPO and High West.

    It reports three segments: Beer, Wine and Spirits, and Corporate Operations and Other. Beer is the core, and the company says it depends on sales of its beer brands. In the U.S. it holds an exclusive sub-license to use the trademarks for its beer brands, so it does not own all of those marks outright. Its Wine and Spirits segment sells higher-priced wine and spirits in the U.S.

    The company runs its own marketing, sales and customer service teams for each segment. At the end of February 2026 it had approximately 9,400 employees, including about 1,100 at a glass-making joint venture it owns equally with Owens-Illinois. That plant supplies approximately 60% of the glass bottles for its beer brands. During fiscal 2026 it spent more than $700 million on added brewing capacity and expects to keep adding it.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Drops in how much of its products people buy, and its dependence on sales of its beer brands.
    • Tariffs and other uncertainty from its international operations.
    • Supply of water, farm products and packaging materials, including some bought under supply contracts.
    • Protecting its trademarks and other rights, including the licenses for its beer brands.
    • The change of president and chief executive officer.
    From Constellation Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
