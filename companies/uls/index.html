    What UL Solutions does

    UL Solutions provides testing, inspection and certification services, plus related software and advisory work, to help customers show that their products are safe and meet standards. It owns the UL certification mark that appears on products worldwide, and says it is the largest provider of these services headquartered in North America, with a global network of laboratories.

    It reports three segments through 2025. Industrial covers energy, industrial automation, engineered materials and buildings. Consumer covers consumer electronics, medical devices, appliances, lighting and retail products. Software and Advisory sells software, data and advisory work to help customers handle regulations, supply chain transparency and sustainability. It reorganized its segments starting in the first quarter of 2026.

    UL Solutions says it served more than 80,000 customers in over 110 countries in 2025. A nonprofit's affiliate, UL Standards & Engagement, holds the majority of the voting power of its common stock.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Artificial intelligence advances that could reduce demand for its testing and certification services.
    • Risks from doing business outside the United States.
    • Tariffs, trade barriers and export or import controls that could hurt its business.
    • Economic and political instability in regions where it operates.
    From UL Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
