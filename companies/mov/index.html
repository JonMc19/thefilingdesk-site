    What Movado does

    Movado Group designs, sources, markets and distributes watches around the world, and also sells jewelry and other accessories under most of its brands. Its owned brands are Movado, Concord, EBEL, Olivia Burton and MVMT. It also makes and sells watches under licenses for Coach, Tommy Hilfiger, Hugo Boss, Lacoste, Calvin Klein and Kate Spade New York.

    It makes money by selling watches to retailers and directly to consumers. Its growth has come from buying watch brands and signing license agreements with other brands, for which it says it picks only a few long-term partners. In July 2025 it signed a license agreement with Coach Services for Coach watches. It faces competition across the watch industry, including from smart watches.

    The company was incorporated in New York in 1967 and took the Movado Group name in 1996. Its watch brands range from the Swiss luxury names EBEL and Concord to fashion brands such as Olivia Burton and MVMT, which sells mainly online.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions in key markets that reduce consumer spending.
    • U.S. tariffs and other trade restrictions, and retaliation by other countries.
    • Currency exchange rate swings.
    • Shoppers moving online and changes among the retailers that sell its watches.
    • Intense competition, including from smart watches.
