    What Illinois Tool Works does

    Illinois Tool Works, known as ITW, is a global manufacturer of a wide range of industrial products and equipment. It was founded in 1912. It runs 88 divisions in 49 countries and employed approximately 43,000 people at the end of 2025.

    Its operations are reported in seven segments. Automotive OEM makes plastic and metal components, fasteners and assemblies for carmakers and their suppliers. Food Equipment makes commercial warewashing, cooking, refrigeration and food processing equipment for restaurants, food retailers and institutions. Test & Measurement and Electronics makes equipment, consumables and software for testing materials and for assembling electronics. Welding makes arc welding equipment, consumables and accessories for fabrication, shipbuilding and other industrial uses.

    The other three segments are Polymers & Fluids, Construction Products and Specialty Products. The company says its divisions are organized and managed by similar product offerings and end markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its results depend on global economic conditions, and downturns in the markets it serves could hurt it.
    • Swings between the US dollar and other currencies could hurt its operating results.
    • Its enterprise strategy may not bring the benefits it expects.
    • It may be unable to introduce new products, which would hurt its growth.
    • Raw material price increases and supply shortages could hurt its results.
