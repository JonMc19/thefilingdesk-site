    What O'Reilly Automotive does

    O'Reilly Automotive sells replacement auto parts, tools, supplies, equipment and accessories in North America. It sells to do-it-yourself customers and to professional service providers, which it calls its dual market strategy. It was founded in 1957 and began with one store in Springfield, Missouri.

    At the end of 2025 it ran 6,447 stores in 48 U.S. states and Puerto Rico, 112 stores in Mexico and 26 in Canada. Its stores carry new and remanufactured hard parts and maintenance items, such as batteries, brake parts, filters, oil and wiper blades, plus accessories such as floor mats and seat covers.

    Stores also offer services such as battery diagnostic testing, battery, wiper and bulb replacement, check engine code reading, a loaner tool program, and recycling of used oil, oil filters and batteries. In June 2025 the company split its stock 15 for 1.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions that cut demand and customers' access to credit.
    • Heavy competition in the automotive aftermarket.
    • Changes in its relationships with key suppliers, or limited supply of key products.
    • Interruptions at its distribution centers and other facilities.
    • Growth objectives it may fail to achieve, and risks from its international operations.
