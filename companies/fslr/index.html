    What First Solar does

    First Solar makes solar panels, called photovoltaic (PV) modules, that turn sunlight into electricity. Its panels use a thin film of cadmium telluride (CdTe) on glass instead of the crystalline silicon used by most rivals. The company is based in the United States and develops the technology at research labs in California and Ohio. It says its process does not depend on Chinese crystalline silicon supply chains.

    It makes money by selling modules, mainly in large orders to developers and owners of solar power plants. It also warrants its modules to produce at least 98% of their labeled power in the first year, with the coverage declining each year after that for up to 30 years. It has factories in the United States, Malaysia, Vietnam and India.

    First Solar says it is the largest thin film PV module maker in the world and the largest PV module maker in the Western Hemisphere. It competes with makers of crystalline silicon modules. Because of competition, its costs and tariffs, it has cut production of its older Series 6 modules at international factories.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes or threatened changes in government policies on solar power and trade.
    • Losing large customers, or customers and partners not meeting their contracts.
    • Competition from makers of crystalline silicon modules, and global oversupply that lowers prices.
    • Product quality problems that lead to damages, warranty costs or harm to its reputation.
    • Limited sources for key materials such as tellurium and for custom factory equipment.
    From First Solar's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
