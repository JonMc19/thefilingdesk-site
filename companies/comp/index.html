    What Compass does

    Compass is a real estate services company based in New York City. Its agents help people buy and sell homes. They are independent contractors who attach their real estate licenses to Compass and use its platform and technology. On January 9, 2026, Compass completed its purchase of Anywhere Real Estate, which owns well-known brokerage and franchise brands, and it now describes itself as a global real estate services company.

    Compass earns money in two main ways. At its owned brokerages, it collects a share of the sales commissions its agents earn, usually a percentage of the home sale price, plus some fees. In its franchise business, independent brokerages pay royalties to use brands such as Century 21, Coldwell Banker, Better Homes and Gardens Real Estate and Sotheby's International Realty. It also offers title and escrow services, and mortgage services through a minority-owned joint venture.

    After the merger, it says its network includes more than 340,000 real estate professionals, and its franchise brands are present in about 120 countries and territories.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its results depend on the health of the US housing market, which swings with the economy and the seasons.
    • High mortgage rates, tight lending standards and low home inventory that reduce home sales.
    • Trouble combining Anywhere's business with its own and reaching the benefits it expects.
    • Debt taken on in the Anywhere merger that could reduce its flexibility and raise interest costs.
    • A possible need to raise more capital on terms it may not like.
    From Compass's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
