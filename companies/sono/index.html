    Sonos' revenue fell 4.9% to $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was −3.5%, down from −3.2% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $108 million, down 19.6%. It paid out $81 million in dividends and buybacks, 75% of free cash flow. The diluted share count fell 2.0%.
