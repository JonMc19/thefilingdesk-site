    What Ericsson does

    Ericsson is a Swedish company that supplies mobile network equipment, software and services. It sells mainly to communications service providers, which are the phone and data network operators, and also to enterprises and the public sector.

    Its portfolio spans three segments: Networks, Cloud Software and Services, and Enterprise. Enterprise includes the Global Communications Platform, wireless wide area networks and private 5G networks. Ericsson is also investing in network APIs, which let outside developers use features of a mobile network.

    Its value chain runs from the extraction of raw materials for electronic parts to the delivery of communication services to end users. In 2025 it had approximately 15,000 active direct suppliers, and about 200 of them provided the materials and components used in its hardware. It keeps supply and component hubs in Sweden, Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands, the United States, Mexico, the United Arab Emirates, India, Malaysia, Singapore and China. At the end of 2025 it had 88,826 employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition from existing competitors and new entrants, including vendor consolidation.
    • Geopolitical and trade uncertainty, including tariffs and sanctions.
    • Changes in its industry, such as the regulatory setting and the makeup of its customers.
    • Risks tied to intellectual property, key employees and disruptions from natural or man-made events.
    • Cybersecurity threats to its products and systems.
