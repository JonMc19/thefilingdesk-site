    Wells Fargo's revenue rose 1.7% to $83.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $21.3 billion, up 8.2%. Its return on equity was 11.8%, up from 10.8% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 65.5%, down from 66.3% a year earlier. It paid out $22.9 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 6.5%.
