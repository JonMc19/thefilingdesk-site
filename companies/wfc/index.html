    Wells Fargo financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    WELLS FARGO & COMPANY/MN · NYSE: WFC Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 24, 2026 · 0000072971-26-000133 Updated October 5, 2026

    Wells Fargo's revenue rose 1.7% to $83.7B in fiscal 2025. Net income was $21.3B, up 8.2%. Its return on equity was 11.8%, up from 10.8% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 65.5%, down from 66.3% a year earlier. It paid out $22.9B in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 6.5%.
