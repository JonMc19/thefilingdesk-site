    What OneSpan does

    OneSpan sells software and devices that protect online banking and similar digital transactions. It reports two segments: Cybersecurity and Digital Agreements. It says that more than 60% of the world's 100 largest banks are its customers.

    Cybersecurity includes authentication, mobile app protection and fraud prevention software, plus Digipass authenticator devices, which are small hardware tokens used to log in securely. Digital Agreements covers electronic signatures, online notary tools, identity verification and smart digital forms. The company says its main e-signature competitors are DocuSign and Adobe, which are much larger.

    OneSpan sells mostly under a subscription licensing model, in the cloud or on a customer's own systems. It sells through its own sales staff and through distributors, resellers, systems integrators and OEMs worldwide. A significant part of its sales goes to a limited number of customers, and its Digipass business depends on a limited number of component suppliers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing a large sale to one of a limited number of customers.
    • Competition, especially in e-signatures from DocuSign and Adobe, which are much larger.
    • Product updates that do not keep pace with customer needs or win acceptance.
    • Dependence on a few component suppliers for its Digipass devices.
    • Debt covenants in its credit agreement that limit what it can do.
