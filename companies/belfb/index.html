    What Bel Fuse does

    Bel Fuse designs, makes and sells electronic components that power, protect and connect circuits. Its products are used in defense, commercial aerospace, networking, telecommunications, computing, industrial equipment, high-speed data transmission, transportation and electric mobility, and also in automotive, medical, broadcasting and consumer electronics.

    It organizes its products in three groups. Power Solutions and Protection covers power supplies, modules and circuit protection. Connectivity Solutions covers fiber optic, copper, RF and RJ connectors and cable assemblies. Magnetic Solutions covers integrated connector modules, transformers, inductors and other discrete parts. Bel was incorporated in 1949 and has facilities in North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

    Acquisitions have been important to its growth. In November 2024 it bought an 80% stake in Enercon, which supplies customized power conversion and networking products to aerospace and defense markets and is part of the power group. Bel is planning to buy the remaining stake. Bel has substantial manufacturing operations in China.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition in its industry.
    • Substantial manufacturing operations in China, which expose it to significant risks.
    • Losing certain substantial customers.
    • Not gaining the expected benefits from its acquisitions, including Enercon, which has risks tied to Israel.
    • Cyclical demand in its end markets, which can lower orders for its products.
