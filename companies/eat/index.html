    What Brinker International does

    Brinker owns, runs and franchises two restaurant brands: Chili's Grill & Bar and Maggiano's Little Italy. Chili's is a casual dining chain with a Southwest-inspired American menu, built around burgers, fajitas, chicken and margaritas. It operates in the United States and in other countries, including through franchisees. Maggiano's is a full-service Italian American restaurant brand that serves everyday meals and also hosts banquets and large-party events in dedicated spaces.

    Brinker earns its money mainly from sales at the restaurants it owns, and also from franchise fees and other income from franchised locations. Most sales at both brands come from food and non-alcoholic drinks, with alcoholic drinks making up the rest. Brinker says Chili's competes with other casual dining restaurants on food quality, value, service and atmosphere.

    Most restaurant staff are hourly team members, and most of those work part-time. The company is based in Dallas, Texas.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to carry out its business strategy for its restaurant brands.
    • Shifts in what customers want to eat, which could reduce demand at its restaurants.
    • Food safety incidents or bad publicity at its restaurants or in its supply chain.
    • Problems recruiting, training and keeping restaurant managers and team members.
    • Many company-owned restaurants sit in a few states, so regional trends affect it more.
