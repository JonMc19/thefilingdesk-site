    What Flexsteel does

    Flexsteel designs, makes, imports and sells furniture for the home. Its products include stationary and motion sofas, loveseats, chairs and sectionals, plus bedroom furniture, dining tables and chairs, occasional and entertainment tables, and kitchen storage. It sells under the Flexsteel name and several sub-brands: Zecliner, Statements, Zen, Perfect Match and Pulse.

    The company reports one segment, furniture products. It sells through retail partners and online channels across the U.S. It makes some products at its own plants in Mexico and buys finished products from suppliers outside the U.S., which lets it offer both made-to-order and ready-to-deliver items at a range of prices. It has export sales, but says they are minimal.

    Flexsteel says the furniture industry is highly competitive, with many U.S. and foreign makers and distributors, none of which dominate it, and that some competitors have much higher sales. It points to its patented Blue Steel Spring, a coil design, as one of its strengths. At the end of fiscal 2026 it had about 1,200 employees, around 900 of them in Mexico.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tariffs and other changes in U.S. trade policy that raise the cost of imported products.
    • Inflation, shipping costs and currency swings that cut into profit.
    • Customers can put off furniture purchases when the economy is weak.
    • Managing a global supply chain of Mexican plants and offshore suppliers.
    • Failing to keep up with changing consumer and designer tastes.
    From Flexsteel's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
