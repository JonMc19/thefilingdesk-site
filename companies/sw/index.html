    What Smurfit Westrock does

    Smurfit Westrock makes paper-based packaging. Its main products are corrugated boxes, the cardboard used to ship, store and display goods, and the containerboard paper they are made from. It also makes folding cartons, paper sacks, bag-in-box packaging, kraft paper and market pulp. It was formed in July 2024 when Smurfit Kappa, a European corrugated packaging maker, combined with WestRock, a North American corrugated and consumer packaging company.

    The company is incorporated in Ireland and reports three segments: North America (the U.S., Canada and Mexico); Europe, the Middle East and Africa and Asia-Pacific; and Latin America. In each segment, a system of mills and plants makes containerboard, which the company turns into corrugated containers itself or sells to others. The North America segment also makes paperboard, kraft paper and pulp, and the Latin America segment includes forestry.

    It makes boxes close to its customers, and says its plant footprint helps it serve those near its corrugated box plants.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Economic downturns and industry cycles that can lead to overcapacity and lower prices for its products.
    • Swings in the price and supply of energy, transportation and raw materials.
    • Strong competition in the paper and packaging industry.
    • Spending on new capacity that may cost more than planned or not deliver the intended results.
    • Work stoppages and other labor problems, and the risks of operating in many countries.
