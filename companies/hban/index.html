    Huntington Bancshares

    HBAN · Nasdaq · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Huntington Bancshares' revenue rose 10.6% to $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $2.2 billion, up 14.0%. Its return on equity was 10.0%, up from 9.9% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 61.4%, down from 61.8% a year earlier. It paid out $908 million in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count rose 1.9%.
