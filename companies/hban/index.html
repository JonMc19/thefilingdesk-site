    What Huntington Bancshares does

    Huntington Bancshares is a regional bank holding company based in Columbus, Ohio, organized in 1966. Through its bank, Huntington National Bank, it has served customers since 1866. It provides commercial and consumer deposit and lending services, plus payments, mortgage banking, investment banking, capital markets, equipment financing, investment management, trust, brokerage and insurance.

    At the end of 2025 it ran more than 1,000 branches in 14 states. It bought Veritex Holdings, a Dallas bank holding company, in October 2025. On February 1, 2026 it completed its merger with Cadence Bank, a regional bank based in Houston and Tupelo, Mississippi, paying in stock. The 10-K values that deal at about $8.1 billion and says Huntington now runs nearly 1,400 branches in 21 states.

    Its business segments follow how management tracks results. The company says its aim is to offer the full set of products larger banks offer, delivered locally with local customer service.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Setting aside too little for credit losses, or credit risk that hurts results.
    • Weak economic conditions, and its emphasis on commercial lending.
    • Changes in interest rates that could reduce its net interest income.
    • Competition in the banking industry.
    • Depending on dividends from its subsidiaries, and needing access to capital markets.
