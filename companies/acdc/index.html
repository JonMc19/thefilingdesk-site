    What ProFrac does

    ProFrac provides services and products to oil and natural gas producers in the United States. Its main service is hydraulic fracturing, in which fluid and sand are pumped into a well under high pressure to free oil and gas from rock. It also produces frac sand, makes and repairs its own equipment, and offers distributed power generation.

    ProFrac reports four segments: Stimulation Services (the fracturing fleets), Proppant Production (frac sand), Manufacturing, and Flotek Industries, a chemicals business it owns. It had 22 active fleets at the end of 2025 and works in most major U.S. oil and gas basins. It names Halliburton, Liberty Energy, ProPetro and Patterson-UTI among its fracturing competitors.

    Its sand business has about 21.5 million tons of yearly capacity at eight mines in Texas, Louisiana, Arkansas and New Mexico, and sells to outside customers as well as to its own fleets. Making its own equipment and sand is meant to lower costs and give it control over supply.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on a few large customers, whose drilling spending depends on oil and gas activity.
    • Dependence on outside suppliers for equipment, parts and raw materials, with delays and higher prices.
    • Heavy debt and restrictions in its debt agreements that limit its flexibility.
    • A fast pace of acquisitions that may be hard to pay for, manage and integrate.
    • Operating hazards and limits on water supply that could interrupt its work.
    From ProFrac's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
