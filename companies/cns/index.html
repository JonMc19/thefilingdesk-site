    What Cohen & Steers does

    Cohen & Steers is an investment manager, founded in 1986, that specializes in real assets and alternative income. These include listed and private real estate, preferred securities, infrastructure, resource equities and commodities, as well as multi-strategy solutions. It is based in New York City, with offices in London, Dublin, Hong Kong, Tokyo and Singapore, and serves institutional and individual investors around the world.

    It manages money through three types of investment vehicles: open-end funds, institutional accounts and closed-end funds. Open-end funds issue and redeem shares every day at net asset value, and include a non-traded REIT it advises and, since 2025, active exchange traded funds (ETFs). Institutional accounts are portfolios it manages for clients such as pension plans and sovereign wealth funds. Like other asset managers, it earns fees for managing these assets, so its revenue depends on how much money it manages.

    It sells through two channels. The wealth channel covers intermediaries such as private banks, broker dealers and registered investment advisers. The institutional channel covers sovereign wealth funds, pension plans, insurers, endowments, foundations and investment consultants. Cohen & Steers, Inc. is the holding company for its operating subsidiaries and was organized in Delaware in 2004.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A fall in the performance or value of real estate securities, which would hurt its assets under management.
    • Limits on its growth from the size and number of real estate securities issuers and REIT ownership restrictions.
    • Losses on seed investments it makes to launch new strategies and products.
    • The loss of senior executives or investment professionals.
    • Substantial competition in all parts of its business.
