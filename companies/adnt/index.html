    What Adient does

    Adient designs and makes car seats for automakers. It supplies complete seating systems as well as parts such as frames, mechanisms, foam, head restraints, armrests and trim covers. Its customers are the large vehicle makers, and it sells for passenger cars, commercial vehicles and light trucks, including vans, pick-ups and crossover SUVs.

    Adient earns its revenue by selling seats and seat parts to automakers under long-term relationships. It works with them as new vehicle platforms are developed. Adient says it holds strong positions in the Americas, Europe and Asia. It also has partly owned affiliates in China, Asia and Europe, and it says its business in China faces aggressive competition.

    The company has 65,000 employees working in many manufacturing, assembly or sequencing plants across the major vehicle-producing regions. Adient says it uses standardized manufacturing processes and its own parts production to keep costs down.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in the condition of the global auto industry and of individual automakers.
    • Its joint venture partnerships, and aggressive competition in its China business.
    • Higher tariffs and changes in US trade policy.
    • Failing to meet customer specifications, or trouble launching new vehicle programs.
    • Being unable to win price terms or cost cuts that offset the price reductions automakers demand.
    From Adient's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
