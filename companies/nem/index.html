    What Newmont does

    Newmont is a gold mining company based in Denver, Colorado. It also produces copper, silver, lead and zinc, some as co-products (sold alongside gold) and some as by-products. Its mines and projects are in several countries, and it holds a large land position for exploration.

    Newmont earns money by selling the metals it mines. It has 13 reportable segments: each of its 12 mining operations that it manages, plus its 38.5% share of Nevada Gold Mines, a mine group it does not manage itself.

    In November 2023 Newmont bought Newcrest Mining. After that, its board approved a plan to sell six non-core mines and one development project. The company finished those sales between the fourth quarter of 2024 and the fourth quarter of 2025. In October 2025 it declared commercial production at its Ahafo North project in Ghana.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to replace the gold, copper and other reserves it mines as they run down.
    • Reserve and resource estimates that turn out to be wrong, and mine plans that cost more than expected.
    • Higher operating and capital costs that cut profit.
    • Mine closure and cleanup costs that exceed what it has set aside.
    • Currency swings and capital controls in the many countries where it operates.
    From Newmont's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
