    What HF Sinclair does

    HF Sinclair is an independent energy company that makes and sells gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, renewable diesel and specialty products. It owns seven refineries in Kansas, Oklahoma, Washington, New Mexico, Utah and Wyoming, which serve the Mid-Continent, Southwest, Rocky Mountains and Pacific Northwest regions. It also owns three renewable diesel units, which make diesel from vegetable oil and animal fats, and logistics assets such as pipelines and terminals. In December 2023 it merged with Holly Energy Partners, its pipeline and terminal partner.

    It reports five segments: Refining, Renewables, Marketing, Lubricants & Specialties and Midstream. Refining includes the refineries and an asphalt business. Marketing covers branded fuel sales. Lubricants & Specialties includes the Petro-Canada Lubricants, Sonneborn and Red Giant Oil businesses, which make base oils, white oils, waxes and engine oil. Midstream holds pipelines, terminals and tanks that mainly support its refineries.

    The company says its refineries can convert cheaper heavy or sour crude oil into higher-value products. Its seven refineries can process a combined 678,000 barrels per stream day of crude oil. It buys renewable fuel credits to meet federal blending rules, and says prices for those credits move.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Refinery accidents, fires and other hazards that may not be fully insured.
    • Strong competition in refining, marketing and lubricants.
    • Swings in crude oil and fuel prices and in prices of renewable fuel credits.
    • Reliance on distributors to sell many of its lubricants and specialty products.
    • Less water or higher water prices, and disruption by those who oppose its industry.
    From HF Sinclair's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
