    What Adtran does

    Adtran makes networking equipment, software and services that move data over fiber-optic and other broadband networks. Its customers are telephone and broadband providers, plus utilities, cities and other organizations that build fiber networks, cable companies, large enterprises, data center and content companies, and government agencies. It sells through its own sales staff and through distributors.

    Adtran reports two segments. Network Solutions covers hardware and software products. Services & Support covers network design and installation, support, and cloud-based software for running networks. Across both, it groups sales into three categories: Subscriber Solutions (equipment at the customer's home or business), Access & Aggregation Solutions, and Optical Networking Solutions. It competes in markets it describes as increasingly competitive.

    Adtran owns ADTRAN, Inc. and holds a majority of Adtran Networks, a German-based business. At the end of 2025 it had 3,338 employees worldwide.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Large debt and loan covenants that limit what it can do and could speed up repayment if broken.
    • Depending heavily on a few customers, so losing one or a major project would cut revenue.
    • Revenue that swings from period to period, partly because service providers take long to approve new products.
    • Relying on a few suppliers for key parts, with shortages delaying deliveries.
    • Costs and obligations tied to its agreement with the shareholders of Adtran Networks.
    From Adtran's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2020, earnings per share fell 48.7% a year and net income 48.9% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 1%. Adtran then lost money in fiscal 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
