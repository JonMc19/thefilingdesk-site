    What Signet Jewelers does

    Signet Jewelers is a jewelry retailer. In the United States its brands include Kay, Zales, Jared, Diamonds Direct, Banter by Piercing Pagoda and the online brands James Allen and Blue Nile. Its stores are in shopping malls, off-mall centers and outlet malls, and it also sells online. It sells bridal and gift jewelry, fashion jewelry and watches.

    It makes money by selling jewelry to shoppers. Polished diamonds and gold make up most of its merchandise costs, and lab-grown diamonds are now a meaningful part of its sales and inventory. It says Kay is the largest specialty jewelry brand in the U.S. by sales, Zales the third largest and Jared the fifth largest.

    In its fiscal year ended January 31, 2026 the company launched a strategy it calls Grow Brand Love, which treats its banners as distinct brands rather than store names. Part of the plan is to fold the James Allen and Rocksbox brands into its other brands, limit its exposure to declining malls, and cut costs. It says it competes with other jewelers and with other ways people spend, such as electronics, entertainment and travel.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A fall in consumer spending on a discretionary item like jewelry.
    • Tariffs and trade limits on jewelry and materials it imports, including from India.
    • Swings in the price and supply of polished diamonds and gold.
    • Lower costs for lab-grown diamonds changing the value of its products and inventory.
    • Fewer shoppers at malls where many of its stores are.
    From Signet Jewelers's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
