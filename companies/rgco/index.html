    What RGC Resources does

    RGC Resources is a holding company whose main business is Roanoke Gas, a natural gas utility serving the city of Roanoke, Virginia and nearby areas. Roanoke Gas buys gas from suppliers and delivers it through its pipelines to homes, businesses and factories. It also runs a liquefied natural gas storage facility.

    The utility is regulated, and its rates are set with a state commission. Almost all of the company's revenue comes from it: more than 99% of total revenue in fiscal 2025. Residential customers make up over 91% of its customers and bring in more than half of its revenue. Changes in the cost of gas are passed on to customers through a purchased gas adjustment, so revenue moves with gas prices, weather and how much gas customers use. Some industrial customers buy their gas elsewhere and pay Roanoke Gas only to deliver it.

    A second subsidiary, RGC Midstream, holds the company's investment in Mountain Valley Pipeline, a company that built and runs interstate gas pipelines. RGC Resources had 106 full-time employees on September 30, 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Operating a gas pipeline and liquefied natural gas storage facility carries safety and accident risks.
    • The price and supply of natural gas can swing, and it needs enough pipeline capacity to serve customers.
    • Regulators may not grant timely rate increases, and safety rules and fines may grow.
    • All its business is in one region, and other energy sources compete for its customers.
    • Its investment in Mountain Valley Pipeline carries risks it does not control.
    From RGC Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 4, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
