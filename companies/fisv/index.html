    What Fiserv does

    Fiserv provides technology that helps businesses and banks handle payments and money. Its clients include merchants, banks, credit unions, other financial institutions, and corporate and public sector clients around the world. Its products cover account processing and digital banking, card processing, payments, e-commerce, and merchant processing. It also runs Clover, a cloud-based point-of-sale and business management platform.

    Fiserv reports two segments. Merchant provides products that let businesses of all sizes accept payments and sell online. Financial serves banks and other financial institutions with loan, deposit, digital payment and card processing. In 2025 it had $21.2 billion in total revenue. Processing and services revenue, mostly account- and transaction-based fees under multi-year contracts, was 80% of the total.

    Most revenue comes from the United States and Canada. Fiserv says it competes in a crowded field and must keep up with technology, including AI.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling behind in technology, including artificial intelligence, or failing to compete well.
    • Losing clients when contracts are not renewed or are ended early.
    • Its One Fiserv action plan not delivering the benefits it expects.
    • Dependence on its merchant relationships and alliances.
    • Banking consolidation that removes clients, and changes in card network fees.
    From Fiserv's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
