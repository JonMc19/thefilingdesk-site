    What Peloton Interactive does

    Peloton sells connected fitness equipment and subscriptions that bring live and on-demand classes to people at home. Its products include bikes, treadmills and rowers, and it sells refurbished Original Bikes. Its Cross Training Series launched in October 2025. It also owns Precor, which makes commercial fitness equipment, and sells accessories, delivery and installation, warranties and apparel.

    It earns money from hardware sales and from monthly subscriptions, both for connected equipment and for its apps, which work without Peloton hardware. It also rents some bikes in select markets. Peloton says it had about 5.5 million Members as of June 30, 2026, in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, Australia and Austria, and commercial customers in over 60 countries.

    The company was founded in 2012 and is headquartered in New York City. It counts a person as a Member if they hold a paid subscription and work out at least once in a trailing 12-month period.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to attract and keep paying subscribers.
    • Misjudging demand for its products and services.
    • Restructuring and cost-saving plans that may not deliver the benefits it expects.
    • Operating losses in the past that could return.
    • Harm to the value and reputation of the Peloton brand.
