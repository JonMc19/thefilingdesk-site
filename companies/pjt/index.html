    What PJT Partners does

    PJT Partners is a global investment bank that gives advice rather than lending or trading. Its clients are corporations, financial sponsors such as private equity firms, boards, creditors and fund managers. It earns fees for advice and for helping clients raise money.

    It has three main businesses. Strategic Advisory advises on mergers and acquisitions, spin-offs, joint ventures, shareholder matters and capital raising in debt and equity markets, and also offers geopolitical and policy advice. Restructuring and Special Situations advises companies and creditors in financial distress, on debt restructurings, Chapter 11 bankruptcy cases and distressed deals. PJT Park Hill advises private funds and helps them raise money from investors.

    Its fees depend on the number of clients paying for advice and on deal activity, which rises and falls with market conditions. The company began trading on the New York Stock Exchange in October 2015. It says it competes with other advisory firms for clients and for senior talent.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker markets that cut the number of mergers and capital raises it advises on, with fixed costs it cannot quickly reduce.
    • The departure of partners or groups of professionals.
    • A drop in the number of fee-paying clients in a given period.
    • Dependence of its fund advisory business on the availability of private capital.
    • Failing to manage conflicts of interest, which could damage its reputation.
    From PJT Partners's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
