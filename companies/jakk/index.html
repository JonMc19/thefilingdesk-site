    What JAKKS Pacific does

    JAKKS Pacific designs, makes, markets and sells toys and other products for kids. These include kids' indoor and outdoor furniture, costumes, and some sporting goods and home furnishings products. It focuses on licensing or buying well-known characters and brand names that have long product histories, which it believes are less prone to fads, and it also makes products under its own brands.

    It reports two segments, Toys/Consumer Products and Costumes. Its products include action figures based on franchises such as Nintendo, Sonic the Hedgehog and The Simpsons, toy vehicles, dolls based on Disney licenses, ride-on products, role-play and dress-up items, and Halloween and everyday costumes. It also makes private label products for certain retailers.

    It sells to toy and mass-market retail chains, department stores, drug and grocery chains, club stores and others. Target and Walmart, its two largest customers, accounted for 26.6% and 26.1% of net sales in 2025. Sales outside the United States were 27.0% of net sales, and 57.9% of net sales came in the second and third quarters.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to redesign and extend its products as children's tastes and play habits change.
    • Losing licenses from the companies whose characters and brands it sells.
    • Losing or reducing orders from its largest customers.
    • Higher costs for raw materials and shipping, and delays in delivering goods.
    • Guessing wrong on demand and making too much of some items and too little of others.
