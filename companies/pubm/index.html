    What PubMatic does

    PubMatic is a technology company that helps websites, apps and streaming services sell their advertising space. Its platform connects publishers, which own the content and the ad space, with buyers: advertisers, agencies and the software tools they use to bid on ads. Publishers use it to get more from their ad space and audiences, and buyers use it to get control and transparency over where their ads run.

    The company says advertising money is shifting toward connected TV and premium video, retail and commerce media, and mobile apps, where results are easier to measure, and that more of the buying is moving onto fewer platforms. It also says it is using more automation and AI to process the large amount of data that ad auctions create.

    At the end of 2025 PubMatic had 1,030 employees: 322 in the United States, 573 in India and 135 in its other offices around the world.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue depends on overall demand for advertising, which can fall.
    • Failing to keep existing customers spending more or to win new ones.
    • Results that swing from quarter to quarter and may miss what analysts and investors expect.
    • Relying more on AI, which brings new operating and competitive risks and could change how the ad market competes.
    • Having to scale its platform for growing volumes of ad impressions without costs getting too high.
    From PubMatic's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
