    What Customers Bancorp does

    Customers Bancorp is a bank holding company. Its subsidiary, Customers Bank, takes deposits and makes loans to businesses and consumers. It describes a branch-light approach: it has a limited number of branches and offices, in Pennsylvania, New York, New Jersey, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, New Hampshire, California and Nevada, and serves many customers through technology and a single point of contact.

    It earns money mainly from interest on loans and leases. It lends to commercial businesses, including commercial and industrial loans, commercial real estate and multifamily loans, and nationally to corporations through specialized lending such as fund finance, mortgage finance, equipment financing, healthcare, and technology and venture banking. It also offers residential mortgages and personal loans, payments and treasury services, and banking-as-a-service to fintech companies.

    At the end of 2025 it had total assets of $24.9 billion, with total deposits of $20.8 billion.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Credit losses if its allowance for credit losses proves too small.
    • Its focus on commercial, commercial real estate and mortgage finance lending.
    • Conditions in the commercial real estate market and changes to New York multifamily rules.
    • Local economic downturns in the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, where it is concentrated.
    From Customers Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
