    What Moderna does

    Moderna develops medicines and vaccines built on messenger RNA (mRNA), the molecule that carries instructions from genes to a cell's protein-making machinery. It has three commercial products: Spikevax and mNEXSPIKE, its COVID vaccines, and mRESVIA, a vaccine against respiratory syncytial virus (RSV). It launched mNEXSPIKE in the third quarter of 2025 and says it is now its top product in the U.S. retail channel.

    Moderna earns its revenue from selling these vaccines. Total revenue in 2025 was $1.9 billion, largely from COVID vaccines. It also has experimental medicines in development for cancer, rare diseases and infectious diseases. With Merck, it is testing an individualized cancer therapy called intismeran in eight Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials. It has applications under review for a flu vaccine and a combined flu and COVID vaccine, and a norovirus vaccine in a Phase 3 study. In January 2026 it agreed with Recordati to carry a rare disease therapy through the final stages of development and, if approved, sell it worldwide.

    Moderna says it has built a global manufacturing network since 2022, including facilities in the UK, Canada and Australia. It says the vaccine and drug markets are intensely competitive.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Regulatory and market uncertainty that has affected, and may keep affecting, demand for its products.
    • Difficulty carrying out its near-term strategy and focused pipeline of medicines.
    • Intense competition in vaccines and drugs, and commercial challenges it has already faced.
    • Delays or failures in clinical trials and in getting regulatory approvals for new products.
    • Dependence on third-party reimbursement, and on single-source suppliers and manufacturing.
    From Moderna's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    From fiscal 2021 to 2022, earnings per share fell 28.9% a year and net income 31.5% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 3%. Moderna then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
