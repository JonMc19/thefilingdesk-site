    What Baxter International does

    Baxter makes medical products used in hospitals, nursing homes, rehabilitation centers, surgery centers, doctors' offices, dialysis centers and patients' homes. They include sterile IV solutions, infusion pumps, nutrition given through a vein, inhaled anesthetics, generic injectable drugs, surgical products, smart hospital beds, patient monitoring and diagnostic tools, and respiratory devices. Baxter makes products in over 20 countries and sells them in over 100 countries.

    It reports three segments. Medical Products & Therapies sells IV solutions, infusion systems, nutrition therapies and surgical hemostat and sealant products. Healthcare Systems & Technologies sells connected care products such as smart beds, patient monitors and respiratory devices, and surgical equipment. Pharmaceuticals sells specialty injectable drugs, inhaled anesthetics and drug compounding services.

    In January 2025 Baxter sold its Kidney Care business, now called Vantive, to Carlyle for an aggregate purchase price of $3.80 billion in cash, and it used the proceeds mostly to pay down debt. It had earlier sold its BioPharma Solutions business in 2023. It also owns Hillrom, which it bought earlier.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The sale of Kidney Care and other major deals may not bring the benefits it expected.
    • A large amount of debt that takes up cash and limits its choices.
    • Strong competition and pricing pressure from other medical products and drug companies.
    • Trouble launching or earning money from new products and keeping up with technology.
    • Hospital consolidation and government price controls that push for lower prices.
    From Baxter International's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2023, earnings per share fell 7.5% a year and net income 8.5% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 8%. Baxter International then lost money in fiscal 2024 and 2025.
