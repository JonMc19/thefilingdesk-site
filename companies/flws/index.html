    What 1 800 Flowers Com does

    1-800-FLOWERS.COM sells flowers, plants and gifts, mainly online and by phone. Its brands include 1-800-Flowers.com, Harry & David, Cheryl's Cookies, Shari's Berries, The Popcorn Factory, Wolferman's Bakery, Personalization Mall and Things Remembered. It also runs a loyalty program, Celebrations Passport, that gives members free standard shipping on eligible products.

    It reports three segments: Consumer Floral & Gifts, Gourmet Foods & Gift Baskets, and BloomNet. BloomNet serves independent florists, providing services such as order settlement, advertising and other products. A majority of the company's floral orders are fulfilled by BloomNet member florists, which lets it offer same-day or next-day delivery. The company says competition in floral, gift basket, gourmet food and specialty gift products is intense.

    The company began in 1976, when its founder bought a single florist shop in New York City. In April 2024 it bought Card Isle, an e-commerce greeting card business, and it also bought the Scharffen Berger chocolate business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on international growers for flowers, mostly in Colombia, Ecuador and Holland, which exposes it to tariffs, trade limits and price swings.
    • Higher shipping costs and supply chain problems, since many products are delivered from manufacturers or its sites.
    • Results that swing with the seasons, and consumers cutting spending when the economy is weak.
    • Local florists and other vendors that fill orders may let customers down, so they may not come back.
    • Intense competition in flowers, gifts and gourmet foods.
    From 1 800 Flowers Com's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
