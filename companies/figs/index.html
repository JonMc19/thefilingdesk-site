    What FIGS does

    FIGS sells scrubs and other apparel to healthcare professionals. It designs its products in-house, uses outside suppliers and manufacturers to make them, and sells mostly through its own website and mobile app. It also runs a business-to-business arm called TEAMS and a few physical stores it calls Community Hubs.

    Its range includes scrubwear and non-scrubwear items such as outerwear, underscrubs, footwear, compression socks, lab coats and loungewear. Core scrubwear styles brought in over 60% of net revenues in 2025. It says it tests new products with small first orders and decides what to reorder from sales data.

    FIGS says it had about 2.9 million active customers at the end of 2025. It also runs an Ambassador Program, in which healthcare workers from around the world help it connect with its community. Nearly all of its suppliers and manufacturers are outside the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its growth may not last, and it has not always been profitable.
    • Damage to its brand, or marketing that fails to attract and keep customers.
    • Competition from other healthcare apparel sellers.
    • Relying on a limited number of suppliers, nearly all outside the United States, and on shipping.
    • Trade policy that affects its ability to source and ship products at a profit.
    From FIGS's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
