    What Celsius does

    Celsius makes and sells energy drinks and wellness beverages in the United States and abroad. Its portfolio is mainly energy drinks under the Celsius, Alani Nu and Rockstar brands, with Celsius and Alani Nu also offering other wellness products. Many of its drinks contain caffeine and ingredients such as green tea extract, vitamins and electrolytes, and many have zero sugar. The products are sold in the U.S., Canada, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.

    It sells through grocery, natural-food and convenience stores, fitness centers, mass-market and vitamin retailers and e-commerce sites. It relies on distributors, and Pepsi is its primary distributor in the U.S. and Canada for Celsius, Alani Nu and Rockstar. Under a long-term arrangement it calls the Captaincy, Pepsi has agreed to use commercially reasonable efforts to sell and distribute Celsius products in the U.S. Pepsi holds preferred stock in Celsius and two seats on its Board. Celsius relies mostly on co-packers, which are outside companies that make its drinks.

    Celsius bought the Alani Nu brand for a total that included $1,275.0 million in cash, shares of its common stock, and up to $25.0 million more in cash. It also acquired the Rockstar business in the U.S. and Canada as part of its deals with Pepsi. It funded part of the Alani Nu cash price with a term loan, which it later refinanced with a new, smaller one. Celsius says it faces significant competition from other functional beverage companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying on distributors, and on Pepsi in particular, so a dispute or an end to those arrangements could hurt it.
    • Pepsi's larger ownership stake and Board seats may give it more influence over Celsius' strategy.
    • Trouble integrating Alani Nu, Rockstar or other acquired businesses, or not reaching the expected benefits.
    • Reliance on co-packers to make its drinks, and the cost or shortage of raw materials.
    • Its focus on the functional beverage category, and strong competition within it.
    From Celsius's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
