    What Chemours does

    Chemours makes industrial and specialty chemicals used as inputs in other products. Its main products are refrigerants, titanium dioxide pigment, which gives paints, coatings and plastics their whiteness and opacity, and fluoropolymer resins, which are plastics that resist heat, chemicals and friction. Its customers work in refrigeration and air conditioning, paints and coatings, plastics, transportation, semiconductors and consumer electronics, and oil and gas, among other fields.

    It reports three segments. Thermal & Specialized Solutions makes refrigerants, propellants, blowing agents and specialty solvents. Titanium Technologies makes titanium dioxide pigment. Advanced Performance Materials makes high-end polymers and advanced materials. Chemours says rules that phase down older refrigerants, such as the AIM Act in the U.S., support demand for its newer Opteon refrigerants.

    The company runs 28 major production facilities in eight countries and serves about 2,400 customers in about 110 countries. It says no single customer made up more than 10% of its consolidated net sales at the end of 2025. Chemours was spun off from its former parent, EID, which is now part of Corteva.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Litigation and environmental liabilities, including those tied to its current and past operations.
    • Costs and limits from extensive environmental and health and safety laws.
    • Swings in energy and raw material prices and in demand for its products.
    • Tariffs and other trade restrictions that could hurt its results.
    • Exposure to claims and costs from an audit committee internal review that began in 2024.
    From Chemours's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2024, earnings per share grew 35.7% a year and net income 33.1% a year, as buybacks cut the share count by 18%. Chemours then lost money in fiscal 2025.
