    What Merchants Bancorp does

    Merchants Bancorp is a bank holding company based in Carmel, Indiana. Its bank, Merchants Bank, lends to builders and owners of multi-family housing and healthcare facilities, finances mortgage lenders, and offers community banking, with seven branches in Indiana. It also makes SBA, agricultural and residential mortgage loans.

    It reports three segments. Multi-family Mortgage Banking originates and services loans for affordable rental housing and healthcare facilities, and syndicates low-income housing tax credit funds. Mortgage Warehousing lends to other mortgage lenders and takes their deposits. Banking holds portfolio loans and runs retail and correspondent mortgage banking, agricultural and SBA lending and traditional community banking.

    Its business model is mainly to originate loans that meet government program standards and sell them, keeping adjustable-rate loans on its books. It earns interest on loans, plus gains on loan sales, servicing fees and tax syndication fees. It competes with community banks, large banks, credit unions, online lenders and many other financial companies. At the end of 2025 it had $19.4 billion in assets, $13.0 billion of deposits and about 735 employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower mortgage origination volume, competitors' pricing and changes in interest rates could cut its mortgage banking profits.
    • Relying on programs of Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA and other agencies, and on a market for selling loans.
    • Violating HUD requirements could end its multi-family FHA lending and servicing business.
    • Construction loans rest on estimates of project costs and values that may prove wrong.
