    What Heritage Financial does

    Heritage Financial is a bank holding company whose only subsidiary is Heritage Bank. The bank is headquartered in Olympia, Washington, with branches across Washington State, the Portland and Eugene areas of Oregon, and Boise, Idaho. It also uses the Whidbey Island Bank name on Whidbey Island. Its deposits are insured by the FDIC.

    The bank's business is mostly commercial lending and deposit relationships with small and medium-sized businesses and their owners, and taking deposits from the public. It also makes real estate construction and land development loans and consumer loans. It earns most of its income from the interest on its loans.

    In January 2026 it bought Olympic Bancorp and its Kitsap Bank, which added branches in Washington, paying with Heritage shares.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Interest rate swings and changes in monetary policy.
    • Credit risk, including large loans to certain borrowers and many loans to commercial borrowers.
    • Falling real estate values that weaken the collateral behind its loans.
    • Losing low-cost deposits and funding, and relying on dividends from the Bank for liquidity.
    • Having to raise more capital or failing to keep enough capital.
    From Heritage Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
