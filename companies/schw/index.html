    Charles Schwab financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    SCHWAB CHARLES CORP · NYSE: SCHW Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 25, 2026 · 0000316709-26-000009 Updated October 7, 2026

    Charles Schwab's revenue rose 22.0% to $23.9B in fiscal 2025. Net income was $8.9B, up 49.0%. Its return on equity was 18.1%, up from 13.3% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 52.1%, down from 60.8% a year earlier. It paid out $9.7B in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 1.4%.
