    What Charles Schwab does

    Charles Schwab is a savings and loan holding company that runs a brokerage, a bank, and asset management, custody and advisory businesses. At the end of 2025 it had $11.90 trillion in client assets, 38.5 million active brokerage accounts, 5.7 million workplace plan participant accounts and 2.2 million banking accounts.

    It serves clients through two segments. Investor Services offers brokerage, investment advice, banking and trust services to individual investors, and retirement plan and stock plan services to businesses and their employees. Advisor Services provides custody, trading, banking and support services to independent registered investment advisors, independent retirement advisors and recordkeepers. Its products include brokerage accounts, mutual funds, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), managed portfolios, checking and savings accounts, mortgages, home equity lines of credit and trust services.

    Schwab says its major sources of revenue come from both segments, based on their levels of client assets and activity. Its risk factors say its income depends on how clients allocate their cash and on interest rate changes. It competes with brokerage, wealth management and asset management firms, banks, financial technology companies and others. In November 2025 it agreed to buy Forge Global Holdings, which runs a private market trading platform, in a deal valued at about $660 million.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A significant change in how clients allocate their cash could reduce its income.
    • Interest rate changes that could affect its profitability.
    • A significant fall in its liquidity, which could also reduce client confidence in it.
    • Credit losses, and problems at other financial institutions that could affect financial markets generally.
    • New and uncertain risks from offering clients direct access to select digital assets.
    From Charles Schwab's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
