    What Vulcan Materials does

    Vulcan Materials is a U.S. producer of construction aggregates: crushed stone, sand and gravel. These materials go into homes, offices, data centers, schools, hospitals, factories, highways, bridges, ports, water systems and airports. Vulcan also makes asphalt mix and ready-mixed concrete. It ships by truck, rail, barge and ship.

    It reports 425 active aggregates facilities in 2025, plus asphalt and concrete plants in Alabama, Arizona, California, Maryland, New Mexico, Tennessee, Texas, Virginia, the U.S. Virgin Islands and Washington, D.C. Its top ten states by revenue were 90% of 2025 revenues. It also has aggregates operations outside the U.S., in British Columbia, Honduras and Mexico. Production and sales are halted at its Calica operations in Mexico and its Puerto Cortés operations in Honduras.

    Vulcan says its strategy is built on aggregates, which it ties to construction activity, population growth, jobs and household formation. It buys other businesses and sells assets that no longer fit that plan, and it says the aggregates reserves it owns near growing areas are critical to its long-term success.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A downturn in construction activity, or less federal, state and local infrastructure funding.
    • Government action against its operations in Mexico, including shutdown orders at its Calica quarry.
    • Securing and permitting new aggregates reserves in the right places.
    • Competition in local markets, and substitutes for aggregates.
    • Weather, mining hazards and the high fixed costs of a capital-heavy business.
    From Vulcan Materials's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
