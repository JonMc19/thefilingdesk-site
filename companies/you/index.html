    What Clear Secure does

    Clear Secure, which uses the CLEAR name, runs an identity-verification service. Its best-known product is CLEAR+, a travel subscription that gives members dedicated lanes at airport security checkpoints across the United States. It also offers TSA PreCheck enrollment, a free app for planning trips, and a mobile ID.

    It makes money in two main ways. Consumers pay for CLEAR+, which the company says retails for $209 per year per member and is billed upfront. Businesses pay to use its identity-verification technology, called CLEAR1, which checks that a person is who they claim to be using biometrics, documents and device signals. People who sign up through those business partners can use CLEAR for free.

    The company says it had 38.0 million total members at the end of 2025. It says its primary competitors are offline alternatives, such as manual security checks and screening. It also keeps a large part-time and full-time team of about 3,708 employees in airports and other locations to sign up members.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to add and keep members, or to keep them using the platform.
    • Failing to add or keep business partners, or to earn money from those relationships.
    • Its business depends on concessionaire agreements and on a healthy travel industry.
    • Public acceptance of biometrics and identity platforms, and privacy and biometrics laws.
    • Competition, including new ways of verifying identity at the places where it operates.
    From Clear Secure's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
