    What Regeneron Pharmaceuticals does

    Regeneron is a biotechnology company that discovers, develops, manufactures and sells medicines for serious diseases. Its marketed medicines treat eye diseases (EYLEA HD and EYLEA), allergic and inflammatory conditions (Dupixent), cancer (Libtayo), high cholesterol (Praluent) and rheumatoid arthritis (Kevzara), among others. Its research also covers heart, metabolic, nerve, blood and infectious diseases.

    Regeneron records sales itself for some of its medicines and shares the work and the results with partners on others. It works with Bayer on EYLEA outside the United States and with Sanofi on Dupixent, Kevzara and Praluent outside the United States. On products its partners sell, Regeneron records its share of profits or royalties rather than the sales. Competitors include makers of rival and biosimilar (copy) medicines.

    Regeneron makes medicines at its own sites in Rensselaer, New York and Limerick, Ireland, and also uses outside manufacturers. It also runs clinical trials of new medicines and of new uses for current ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It depends heavily on the success of EYLEA HD, EYLEA and Dupixent.
    • Sales depend on insurers and government programs covering and paying for its medicines, and pricing rules could change.
    • It relies on its collaborations with Bayer and Sanofi to sell some products.
    • Studies of new medicines are costly and slow, and may fail or bring safety problems.
    • Patent challenges and biosimilar competition could shorten the time its products are protected from copies.
    From Regeneron Pharmaceuticals's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
