    What Highwoods Properties does

    Highwoods Properties is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns, develops, buys, leases and manages office buildings. They are mainly in the best business districts of Atlanta, Charlotte, Dallas, Nashville, Orlando, Raleigh, Richmond and Tampa. It is based in Raleigh and was incorporated in Maryland in 1994.

    It earns money from rent paid by the companies that lease its office space. It runs the business through an operating partnership, in which it holds nearly all the common units. Its stated strategy is to own and run high-quality workplaces in these districts and keep a strong balance sheet.

    Highwoods says it is in the "work-placemaking" business, and that it aims to create workplace environments and experiences for its customers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Work-from-home habits cutting long-term demand for office space.
    • Weak local economies and job losses reducing occupancy and rents.
    • Its operating and financing policies being set by its board without stockholder approval.
    • Risks from its investment activities, such as buying and developing office properties.
    • Risks from its debt and financing activities.
