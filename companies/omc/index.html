    What Omnicom Group does

    Omnicom is a holding company for advertising, marketing and communications agencies. It sells services that include media planning and buying, creative work, public relations, health-care marketing, branding and retail marketing, events, and customer data and technology consulting. Its clients are many of the largest companies in the world. Its agencies include BBDO, TBWA, McCann, OMD, FleishmanHillard and Ketchum.

    Omnicom earns its money by providing these services to clients. It groups its agencies into four global networks: Omnicom Advertising, Omnicom Media, the DAS Group of Companies and the Communications Consultancy Network. Its clients work in nearly every sector, and no one industry made up more than 15% of revenue in 2025. Its largest client was 2.4% of revenue, and its 100 largest clients were approximately 54%.

    On November 26, 2025, Omnicom completed its merger with Interpublic Group (IPG), which became a wholly owned subsidiary. After the merger, former Omnicom shareholders owned approximately 60.6% of the combined company and former IPG shareholders approximately 39.4%. Omnicom says it is building generative AI into its planning, creative, media and analytics work.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker client spending in a downturn, and the risk of loss on media bought on clients' behalf.
    • Losing several of its largest clients, or losing clients because of conflicts of interest between them.
    • Failing to join its business with IPG's as planned, and the costs of the merger.
    • Using generative AI and failing to keep up with new technology.
    • Cybersecurity incidents involving the systems and data it relies on.
    From Omnicom Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2024, earnings per share grew 5.7% a year and net income 3.2% a year, as buybacks cut the share count by 17%. Omnicom Group then lost money in fiscal 2025.
