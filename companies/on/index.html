    What ON Semiconductor does

    ON Semiconductor, which operates under the onsemi brand, designs and sells chips for power and for sensing. Its power chips help electric vehicles, solar systems and AI data centers use energy more efficiently. Its sensing chips, such as image sensors, support safety features in cars, factory automation and robots. Automotive and industrial customers are its main markets.

    It has three segments: the Power Solutions Group, the Analog and Mixed-Signal Group and the Intelligent Sensing Group. It sells through its own sales teams and through independent distributors. It says it competes in a semiconductor industry that is highly competitive and has seen a lot of consolidation.

    In 2025 it began a manufacturing realignment to match its factory capacity to long-term needs. It recorded severance costs of $67.1 million for about 2,400 employees, plus large charges for equipment at some plants. In January 2025 it bought a silicon carbide transistor business from Qorvo for $118.8 million in cash, aimed at power supplies for AI data centers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on automotive and industrial customers for a large share of revenue.
    • Manufacturing that relies on complex facilities and equipment, and on restructuring plans that may not work.
    • A highly competitive, consolidating industry that demands heavy research spending.
    • Reliance on independent distributors.
    • Tariffs, trade policy and geopolitical conditions that could hurt sales or costs.
    From ON Semiconductor's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
