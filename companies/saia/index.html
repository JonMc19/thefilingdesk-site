    What Saia does

    Saia is a trucking company based in Johns Creek, Georgia. It runs a national less-than-truckload (LTL) network, which means it carries shipments too small to fill a whole truck and combines several customers' freight on the same trucks. Its main carrier, Saia LTL Freight, was founded in 1924 and serves the 48 contiguous states directly. It reaches Canada and Mexico through partner carriers. Its typical shipment weighs between 100 and 10,000 pounds.

    About 97% of its revenue comes from carrying LTL shipments. It also sells brokered truckload and expedited shipping and other logistics services. In 2025 it earned $3.2 billion in revenue.

    At the end of 2025 it had 213 owned and leased terminals, about 7,700 tractors and 26,500 trailers. It has spent heavily on terminals, trucks and technology and is expanding into new and existing markets. Saia competes with other LTL and truckload carriers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A weak economy, which reduces how much freight customers ship.
    • Competition that could cut its revenue, profit margins or market share.
    • The cost and availability of drivers and other employees, and of diesel fuel.
    • Insurance and claims costs that can swing earnings, including claims insurance does not cover.
    • Risks in expanding its terminal network into new geographic areas.
    From Saia's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
