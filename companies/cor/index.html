    What Cencora does

    Cencora distributes drugs and healthcare products and sells services that support that supply chain. It buys brand-name, specialty and generic medicines, over-the-counter products, and home healthcare supplies from manufacturers and delivers them to hospitals and health systems, retail and mail order pharmacies, clinics, long-term care sites, physician practices and veterinarians. It operates in the United States and select global markets, in both human and animal health.

    It also sells services to providers and drugmakers, including data analytics, outcomes research, reimbursement and regulatory consulting, specialty logistics, inventory management, pharmacy automation and packaging. Its International Healthcare Solutions segment distributes pharmaceuticals and related services, mainly in Europe, and offers global commercialization services.

    On January 2, 2025, Cencora acquired an 85% interest in Retina Consultants of America. It says it faces competition and industry consolidation, and it has a close relationship with Walgreens and Boots.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing a key customer or group purchasing organization, or renewing on worse terms.
    • Its relationship with Walgreens and Boots may not bring the benefits it expects.
    • Acquisitions and investments that don't perform as it expects, or divestitures it can't complete.
    • Risks from international operations, including foreign currency changes.
    • Competition and industry consolidation that may erode its profit.
    From Cencora's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
