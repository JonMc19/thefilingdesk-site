    What DTE Energy does

    DTE Energy is a Michigan energy company. Its two regulated utilities sell and deliver power and gas. DTE Electric makes, buys and distributes electricity to about 2.3 million customers in southeastern Michigan. DTE Gas buys, stores, transports and distributes natural gas to about 1.4 million customers across Michigan. The Michigan Public Service Commission and the federal energy regulator set the rates these utilities may charge.

    It reports four groups of businesses: Electric, Gas, and two non-utility segments. DTE Vantage builds renewable energy projects, including renewable natural gas, and provides custom energy services to industrial, commercial and institutional customers. Energy Trading buys and sells power, natural gas and environmental products and manages gas pipeline and storage contracts. A Corporate and Other group holds the parent company's debt and certain investments.

    DTE says its utilities are investing to build a more reliable grid and cleaner energy, and that it plans to stop using coal-fired power plants in 2032. A Michigan law requires utilities to meet a clean energy standard by 2040.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Regulators setting lower rates than it wants or refusing to let it recover certain costs.
    • Changes to Michigan's electric retail access program, which lets some customers buy from other suppliers.
    • Changes to federal and California fuel standards that its renewable natural gas projects rely on.
    • Cyber incidents, physical attacks and terrorism that could disrupt its operations.
    From DTE Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
