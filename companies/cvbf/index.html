    What CVB Financial does

    CVB Financial is a bank holding company in California. Its main asset is its bank, Citizens Business Bank, National Association, which began operating in 1974 and changed from a California-chartered bank to a national bank on December 15, 2025. The holding company has no other significant activities, and its main source of funds is dividends from the bank.

    The bank earns money mainly from lending and from fees for banking and trust services. It serves small and medium-sized businesses and their owners, professionals and individuals through banking centers across California, along with three trust offices that sell wealth management, trust and investment products. It offers checking, savings, money market and certificate of deposit accounts, and loans that include commercial, agribusiness, consumer, SBA, real estate and construction loans, plus equipment and vehicle leasing.

    At the end of 2025 the company had $15.63 billion in total consolidated assets, $8.62 billion in net loans and $12.07 billion in deposits. It has agreed to a merger with Heritage Commerce Corp.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its allowance for credit losses may not cover actual losses.
    • A decline in real estate values could shrink the value of the collateral behind its loans.
    • The merger with Heritage may not be completed, or may be hard to integrate.
    • An inability to raise funds through deposits, borrowings and other sources could hurt its liquidity.
    • Its dependence on key personnel, whose loss could hurt its prospects.
