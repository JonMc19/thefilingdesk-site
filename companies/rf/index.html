    Regions Financial

    RF · NYSE · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Regions Financial's revenue rose 6.2% to $7.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $2.2 billion, up 13.9%. Its return on equity was 11.7%, up from 10.7% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 57.3%, down from 59.9% a year earlier. It paid out $2.0 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 2.4%.
