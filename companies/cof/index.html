    Capital One's revenue rose 36.6% to $53.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $2.5 billion, down 48.4%. Its return on equity was 2.8%, down from 8.0% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 57.1%, up from 54.9% a year earlier. It paid out $5.6 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count rose 41.1%.
