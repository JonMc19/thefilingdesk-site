    What El Pollo Loco does

    El Pollo Loco operates and franchises quick-service restaurants that specialize in citrus-marinated chicken grilled over fire. The menu serves it in Mexican-inspired burritos, tostadas, bowls and salads, and as bone-in chicken meals in sizes for one person or a group. Most restaurants are free-standing buildings with a drive-thru.

    It reports one segment and earns its revenue from food and beverage sales at company-run and franchised restaurants. At the end of 2025 the system had 503 restaurants in the United States, 175 run by the company and 328 by franchisees, mostly in California. It also had 8 licensed restaurants in the Philippines. It competes with other quick-service and fast-casual restaurants, and says its prices are typically lower than fast-casual and slightly above quick-service.

    The first restaurant opened on Alvarado Street in Los Angeles in 1980. Today restaurants also operate in Arizona, Nevada, Texas, Utah, Colorado, Washington and Louisiana.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to open new company-run or franchised restaurants or enter new markets as planned.
    • Competition from other quick-service and fast-casual restaurants.
    • Higher costs for food and supplies, especially chicken.
    • Its restaurants are concentrated in a few regions, mainly California.
    • Impairment charges on assets, particularly in new markets.
    From El Pollo Loco's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
