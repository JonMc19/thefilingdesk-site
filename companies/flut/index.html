    What Flutter Entertainment does

    Flutter Entertainment is an online betting and gaming company based in Ireland. It runs sportsbooks, online casino games (called iGaming), daily fantasy sports, horse racing wagering and, in the U.S., prediction markets. Its U.S. brands are FanDuel and TVG. It says it had 15.9 million average monthly players in fiscal 2025.

    It reports two segments. The U.S. segment offers its products online in many states, with some sports betting in a small number of retail outlets, and made up 43% of 2025 revenue. The International segment operates in approximately 100 markets and offers sports betting, casino, poker and lottery. It made up 57% of revenue.

    By product, 53% of fiscal 2025 revenue came from sportsbook and 44% from iGaming, and 88% came from online businesses. Flutter grows partly through purchases. In fiscal 2025 these included a 56% interest in Brazil's NSX Group, which runs the Betnacional brand, and the owner of Italy's Snai, one of that country's omni-channel betting operators, which means it takes bets both online and in shops.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker economies and consumer spending, and rising competition in online betting, iGaming and prediction markets.
    • Unclear or changing laws on online betting and iGaming, and public opinion about gambling.
    • Failing to keep customers or sign new ones, or to launch products that people use.
    • Losses from setting betting odds wrongly, which can cut profit sharply.
    • Losing key staff and failing to keep its workforce skilled.
    From Flutter Entertainment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    From fiscal 2016 to 2024, earnings per share fell 12.5% a year and net income 13.4% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 8%. Flutter Entertainment then lost money in fiscal 2025.
