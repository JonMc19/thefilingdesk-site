    What Boyd Gaming does

    Boyd Gaming is a casino company based in Las Vegas that has operated since 1975. It runs 27 casino properties in Nevada, Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Missouri, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Virginia. It also owns Boyd Interactive, an online casino gaming business in the United States and Canada, and manages the Sky River Casino in California for the Wilton Rancheria tribe.

    It reports four segments: Las Vegas Locals, Downtown Las Vegas, Midwest & South, and Online. Most revenue comes from gaming at its casinos, which made up approximately 64% of revenues in 2025. Online reimbursements, which are payments from partner operators for gaming taxes and other expenses Boyd pays under its market access agreements, were the next largest source at 14%. Food and beverage, rooms, management fees and other sources each made up a smaller share.

    In November 2025 it opened a transitional casino in Norfolk, Virginia, with a permanent resort expected in late 2027, and closed its Sam's Town Tunica property. In 2025 it agreed to sell its five percent stake in FanDuel.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker discretionary consumer spending from inflation or an economic downturn.
    • Intense competition, including from casinos on Native American tribal lands.
    • Extensive government regulation and taxes on gaming.
    • Failures of its technology systems, or a breach of customer information.
    • Debt it has taken on and may take on in the future.
    From Boyd Gaming's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
