    What American Public Education does

    American Public Education runs online and campus-based colleges through three subsidiary schools, serving about 108,600 students. Its students pay tuition, which is its main source of revenue.

    American Public University System is fully online and serves about 88,700 adult learners. It began as American Military University in 1991 for military officers and now also serves veterans, military families and public service workers such as first responders. Its two brands are AMU, aimed at the military and national security community, and APU, aimed at working adults. Most of its students are undergraduates, with some in certificate, graduate and doctoral programs.

    Rasmussen University and Hondros College of Nursing teach nursing, including practical nursing diplomas, associate degrees and, at Rasmussen, bachelor's degrees. The 10-K describes a planned combination of the three institutions. Its schools are accredited by agencies recognized by the U.S. Department of Education.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Attracting students and keeping them enrolled in its programs.
    • Dependence on its relationship with the military and on access to military bases and education centers.
    • Falling enrollment at Rasmussen University, which could hurt the whole company.
    • Accreditation and government regulation, including the planned combination of its three institutions.
    • Strong competition in postsecondary education that could cut its share and raise the cost of finding students.
    From American Public Education's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
