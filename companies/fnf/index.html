    What Fidelity National Financial does

    Fidelity National Financial is a title insurance company. Title insurance protects a buyer or lender against problems with who owns a property. The company says it is one of the nation's largest in this field, and that its underwriters, which include Fidelity National Title, Chicago Title, Commonwealth Land Title, Alamo Title and National Title of New York, issue more title insurance policies than any other title company in the United States. It also provides escrow and other title-related services.

    It reports segments for Title, for its annuities and life insurance business, and for a corporate and other group. The Title segment also covers transaction services for the mortgage industry through its subsidiary ServiceLink, which helps produce and manage mortgage loans, including loans in default. The second segment, F&G Annuities & Life, sells deferred and immediate annuities and indexed universal life insurance to retail customers, and funding agreements and pension risk transfers to institutional clients.

    After a 2022 distribution of part of F&G's stock to its shareholders, the company kept control of F&G through an approximate 85% ownership stake.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Less real estate activity would lower its revenues.
    • Interest rate changes could hurt its business, results and liquidity.
    • Falling economic and credit conditions could hurt the value of its investments.
    • F&G faces liquidity risk, meaning it may be unable to meet near-term obligations as they come due.
    • Actual losses may differ significantly from its reserve assumptions.
