    What Deckers Brands does

    Deckers Brands designs, markets and distributes footwear, apparel and accessories for everyday casual wear and for high-performance activities. It sells mainly under three brands it owns: HOKA, UGG and Teva. Independent contract manufacturers make all of its products.

    HOKA sells year-round performance footwear with extra cushioning, first designed for ultra-runners and now covering running, trail, hiking, fitness and lifestyle shoes plus apparel. UGG sells footwear, apparel and accessories, started in California, that is focused on comfort and warmth. Teva sells sandals and outdoor footwear.

    Deckers sells in two ways. In its wholesale channel, it sells to domestic and international retailers and to international distributors. In its direct-to-consumer channel, it sells to shoppers through its own e-commerce sites and retail stores. Its brands compete in the fashion, casual lifestyle, performance, running and outdoor markets, and it says its products appeal to a broad range of buyers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Rapid changes in consumer taste that could leave its brands out of favor and cut sales.
    • Relying on independent manufacturers, and their suppliers, to make all of its products.
    • Unusual weather that reduces demand for seasonal products, particularly UGG.
    • Weaker consumer spending, including from tariffs, inflation and high interest rates.
    • Disruptions from extreme weather, war or other events that hurt its supply chain.
    From Deckers Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
