    Morgan Stanley financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    MORGAN STANLEY · NYSE: MS Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 19, 2026 · 0000895421-26-000086 Updated October 7, 2026

    Morgan Stanley's revenue rose 14.4% to $70.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $16.9 billion, up 25.9%. Its return on equity was 15.6%, up from 13.2% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 68.4%, down from 71.1% a year earlier. It paid out $6.6 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 1.2%.
