    What NCR Voyix does

    NCR Voyix, based in Atlanta, sells software, hardware and services that retailers and restaurants use to take payments, run stores and kitchens, and manage their operations. It calls this "unified commerce" and builds it around its Voyix Commerce Platform. Its solutions handle in-store and online payments, including credit, debit, gift, loyalty and fuel cards. It has approximately 13,500 employees across nearly 30 countries.

    It reports two segments. Retail serves enterprise and mid-market retailers in convenience and fuel, grocery, drug and mass merchandise, and department and specialty retail. Restaurants serves quick-service, table-service and fast casual restaurants and other food service businesses of all sizes. The company says it is shifting toward recurring software and service revenue.

    The company has changed a lot recently. In October 2023 it spun off its ATM business as NCR Atleos and changed its name from NCR Corporation. In September 2024 it sold its Digital Banking business for $2.45 billion in cash, plus possible contingent payments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Customers may not adopt its commerce platform, or its platform investments may not pay off.
    • Acquisitions and sales of businesses that may not deliver the results it expects.
    • Competition from industry consolidation and new entrants.
    • Debt agreements whose terms could limit what the company can do.
    • Increases in card network fees or rule changes that affect its payments business.
    From NCR Voyix's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
