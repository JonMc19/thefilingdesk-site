    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on equity is net income as a share of shareholders' equity. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Matador doesn't report a gross profit; the only cost of sales its filings tag is a small line for its midstream and purchased-oil business, so gross profit and gross margin are left blank. Matador's filings to 2021 tag its senior notes, its credit line and its midstream subsidiary's credit line separately, with no total (2018 and 2019 show the notes alone), so debt and debt-based ratios are left blank in every year, to keep the years comparable.
