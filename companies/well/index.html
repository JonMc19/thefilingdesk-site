    What Welltower does

    Welltower is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, a company that owns property and passes much of its income to shareholders. It focuses on housing for older adults in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada. Its portfolio is made up mostly of more than 2,500 seniors and wellness housing communities, such as independent living, assisted living, memory care and care homes. It also owns some post-acute care communities and outpatient medical buildings.

    It reports three segments. In Seniors Housing Operating, it owns the properties and operators run them day to day, so the company receives the property's operating results. In Triple-net, it leases properties to operators under long-term leases in which the tenant pays operating costs, taxes, insurance and maintenance. In Outpatient Medical, it leases medical office buildings, mostly to healthcare providers.

    Welltower says it competes for investments based on relationships, certainty of execution and deal terms. Its operators compete with nearby properties for residents. The company is set up as an umbrella partnership REIT, where nearly all its business runs through Welltower OP LLC.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Operators and tenants may see lower revenue or higher costs, such as labor, and struggle to pay or run properties well.
    • The insolvency or bankruptcy of its tenants, operators, borrowers or managers.
    • Acquisitions of seniors housing and healthcare properties may not meet its expectations.
    • A bad cold and flu season, epidemics or other public health crises could lower occupancy.
    • Owning property outside the United States carries different or greater risks.
    From Welltower's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
