    What LTC Properties does

    LTC Properties is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns and finances seniors housing and health care properties in the United States. It does not run the properties itself. It buys them and leases them to operators, lends against them with mortgage loans, and invests through joint ventures and other structures.

    The properties include independent living communities, assisted living communities, memory care communities for people with dementia, and skilled nursing centers. Substantially all of its revenue comes from rent under triple net leases (where the tenant pays property costs), resident fees and services, and interest on loans and other financing. It depends on its operators to manage and market the properties day to day.

    In the second quarter of 2025 it began using a structure called RIDEA, which lets a REIT own a senior housing property and have an independent operator run it for a management fee. Under it, LTC takes on the property's operating results and risks. It says it seeks to spread its portfolio across operators and regions, and prefers newer properties with experienced operators.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on its operators for revenue and cash flow.
    • Taking on the operating and legal risks of properties under its new RIDEA structure.
    • Depending on third-party operators to run its RIDEA communities well.
    • Impairment of the real estate it owns.
    • Debt rules that could limit how it runs its business.
    From LTC Properties's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
