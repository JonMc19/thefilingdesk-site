    What Science Applications Intl does

    SAIC provides technology services to the US government. It designs, builds, modernizes, connects and maintains IT systems for the military and for civilian agencies. Its customers include the Army, Air Force, Navy, Marines, Coast Guard and Space Force, the Department of War (formerly the Department of Defense), NASA, and the State, Justice and Homeland Security departments, as well as the intelligence community.

    It has two reportable segments. The Defense and Intelligence segment serves the Department of War and intelligence agencies. The Civilian segment serves federal, state and local governments on border security, travel, trade and health. Its work includes IT modernization, cloud computing, cybersecurity, digital engineering, artificial intelligence, mission systems support, training and simulation, and ground vehicle support. It earns money by winning contracts and task orders from government agencies.

    SAIC says it has served customers for over 50 years and works on about 1,700 active contracts and task orders, with about 23,000 employees. It reorganized into five business groups at the start of fiscal 2025 and cut them to three on January 31, 2026. It says this does not change its reportable segments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on US government agencies as its main customers.
    • Cuts or delays in US defense spending and government funding.
    • Strong competition for contracts, which could hurt future revenue and profit.
    • Profit that moves with the mix of its contracts and with how well it estimates its costs.
    • Government audits, cost reviews and investigations that could lower its profit or cash.
    From Science Applications Intl's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
