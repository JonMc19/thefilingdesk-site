    What Blue Owl Capital does

    Blue Owl is an alternative asset manager. It invests private capital for institutional and private wealth clients, and it manages $307.4 billion in assets. Its work falls into three platforms: Credit, Real Assets and GP Strategic Capital. It has about 1,365 employees worldwide.

    Credit makes loans to companies. Real Assets owns and finances property, including net lease real estate and digital infrastructure. GP Strategic Capital takes stakes in other private capital managers. Blue Owl earns fees for managing client money. Much of its capital is "permanent capital," which it describes as funds that stay invested for long periods. It also holds minority stakes in professional sports teams.

    Blue Owl formed in May 2021 from the combination of Owl Rock and Dyal Capital. It has since bought Oak Street, Wellfleet, Atalaya and other firms to add real estate, loan and insurance-related products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak markets and geopolitics that cut the value or performance of the investments it manages.
    • Not raising new funds and products, which its growth depends on.
    • Intense competition in the investment management business.
    • Use of leverage in its products, and how its assets are valued.
    • Investors asking to redeem or sell shares in its products at once.
