    What Gold.com does

    Gold.com, which was called A-Mark Precious Metals until December 2025, buys and sells gold, silver, platinum, palladium and copper bullion, collectible coins and related products. Its customers include mints, refiners, coin and bullion dealers, banks, industrial users, investors, collectors and retail buyers on four continents.

    It has three segments. Wholesale Sales & Ancillary Services sells bullion and coins to businesses and also provides financing, secure storage and logistics. It owns two mints and distributes coins and bars from government and private mints. Direct-to-Consumer sells precious metals to retail customers through its consumer-facing subsidiaries. Secured Lending makes loans backed by bullion, collectible coins and graded sports cards.

    At June 30, 2026 it had about 4.7 million total retail customers, 0.8 million of them active. Its growth has come partly from acquisitions: JM Bullion in 2021, the Spectrum Group business with Stack's Bowers Galleries in February 2025, Monex Deposit Company in January 2026 and the rest of Sunshine Minting in April 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in what people want from owning precious metals, which drive demand for its products.
    • Heavy dependence on bank financing, and limits on liquidity that could slow its growth.
    • Interruptions to supply and to the computer systems it uses to trade and process transactions.
    • Tariffs and other trade policy changes, since it earns significant revenue outside the United States.
    • Loss of a government mint purchaser or distributor arrangement, or a concentrated customer base.
    From Gold.com's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
