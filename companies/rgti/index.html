    What Rigetti Computing does

    Rigetti builds and operates quantum computers. They use superconducting qubits, the basic units of quantum information, to process data in ways ordinary computers cannot. The company says its aim is to make machines that can solve problems in areas such as drug discovery, materials science, finance and AI.

    It owns and runs Fab-1, a factory where it prototypes and makes its quantum chips, and it handles the full path from chip design to cloud delivery. Its multi-chip design joins small chips, called chiplets, into larger processors. Customers use its machines over the cloud through its own platform and through public cloud providers, and since 2023 it has also sold quantum computers directly. In December 2023 it launched the Novera, its first commercially available quantum processing unit.

    Rigetti calls itself an early-stage company with a limited operating history.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A short operating history and a record of losses, which it expects to continue.
    • Commercial demand for quantum computing may never develop, or may grow more slowly than expected.
    • Trouble scaling its production and operations quickly enough to meet demand.
    • Managing rapid growth, including in its workforce and operations.
    • Limits on its ability to use past tax losses to reduce future taxes.
