    What Dole does

    Dole plc grows, buys, ships and sells fresh fruit and vegetables. It handles more than 300 products, sources them from over 100 countries and distributes them in more than 85 countries. Its customers include grocery stores, wholesalers, mass merchandisers, foodservice operators, club stores and convenience stores, and it says no one customer accounted for a major share of 2025 sales. Its best-known brand is DOLE.

    It reports three segments. Fresh Fruit covers bananas, pineapples and plantains, mostly grown in Latin America on company-owned and leased farms and by independent growers. Approximately one-third of the bananas it markets and 75% of its pineapples come from its own production. It also runs refrigerated ships and sells spare cargo space to other shippers. Diversified Fresh Produce, Europe, the Middle East and Africa sells imported and local produce across Europe. Diversified Fresh Produce, Americas and the Rest of the World markets items such as avocados, kiwis, apples, berries and cherries.

    On August 5, 2025, Dole sold its Fresh Vegetables division for approximately $140.0 million, and it reports that division as discontinued operations. It owns approximately 112,000 acres of farms and other land.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Bad weather, natural disasters, crop disease and pests, including a banana disease called Tropical Race 4.
    • Product contamination and product liability claims.
    • Swings in market prices and demand for its produce, and changes in the cost of the fruit it buys.
    • Strong competition, and reliance on a few key customers and key suppliers.
    • Debt, underfunded pension plans and trade or tariff policy changes that could hurt its results.
    From Dole's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
