    What Climb Global Solutions does

    Climb Global Solutions is a distributor of information technology products. Its larger Distribution segment, sold under the name Climb Channel Solutions, buys software, maintenance agreements and networking, storage and security products from vendors and sells them to resellers, consultants and systems integrators worldwide. It carries products from software makers such as Adobe, Microsoft, Fortinet and Sophos.

    The Distribution segment is the company's main business and accounts for most of its net sales. Its margins are low as a percentage of gross billings and it faces price competition. It says most of the products it sells are digital, or hardware that the vendor ships directly to the end customer. Its smaller Solutions segment, Grey Matter, sells software, hardware and services directly to end users.

    The company has grown partly through acquisitions in the United States, the United Kingdom and Ireland. It operates distribution facilities in Millersville, Maryland, and Dublin, Ireland, and was incorporated in Delaware in 1982.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on its vendor partners for products, marketing funds and incentives.
    • Lower demand for the IT products it sells from changes in the industry or the economy.
    • Sales on a purchase order basis, with no long-term or minimum purchase commitments from customers.
    • Competitors offering better or different products and services.
    • Selling shares or convertible securities that could dilute existing owners.
    From Climb Global Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak. Rule of 40: revenue growth plus free cash flow margin; software investors treat 40% or more as the bar. Accruals ratio: net income minus cash from operations, over average total assets; lower means profit is backed by cash.
