    What Digital Turbine does

    Digital Turbine runs a mobile advertising and app distribution business. It works with advertisers, app publishers, wireless carriers and phone makers, which the filing calls device original equipment manufacturers. Its technology helps advertisers find and acquire app users and gives carriers, phone makers and app developers a way to earn money from apps and content.

    It reports two segments. On Device Solutions delivers apps and content to phone users through partnerships with carriers and phone makers. Application Media places app installs, and Content Media shows news, weather and sports on the phone itself and earns from ads and sponsored stories. App Growth Platform sells advertising solutions to app developers, brands and agencies, and helps app owners earn from ads in their apps.

    Digital Turbine uses its own tools, including a demand-side platform, which is software advertisers use to bid for ad space, and a one-touch app install tool called SingleTap. It earns revenue largely from fees tied to installs, ad views and clicks.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of net losses, with no assurance it will reach or keep profitability.
    • Revenue concentrated in a limited number of wireless carriers and other customers.
    • Lower advertising spending, which it cannot control, would cut the revenue it earns.
    • A highly competitive and fragmented mobile app market.
    • A shift in technology platforms by carriers and phone makers that could raise its costs.
    From Digital Turbine's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
