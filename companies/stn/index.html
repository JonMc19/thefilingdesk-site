    What Stantec does

    Stantec is a Canadian firm that sells design and consulting services for infrastructure, buildings, water systems, energy projects and environmental work. Its clients include governments, utilities and private companies that need to plan, design, build or clean up projects. It works across the life of a project, from planning and design to construction administration, maintenance, decommissioning and remediation.

    It runs five business units: Infrastructure, Environmental Services, Water, Buildings, and Energy & Resources. It also reports three regions: Canada, the United States and Global. The United States is the largest region by gross revenue. Stantec focuses on the design phase of projects, which it says offers higher margins and more controllable risk than integrated engineering and construction firms. It competes with large multinational professional services firms, mixed contracting and consulting firms, and smaller local and specialist providers.

    As of December 31, 2025, Stantec had approximately 34,000 employees. Its first and fourth quarters usually have the lowest revenue and project activity because of holidays and winter weather in the northern hemisphere.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Cuts or disruptions in public spending on infrastructure and other projects.
    • Claims and lawsuits over its professional work, and workplace health and safety incidents.
    • Competing for skilled employees in a field where turnover is costly.
    • Changes in demand for design services, and competition in the regions and fields where it works.
