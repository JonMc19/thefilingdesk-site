    What Core Laboratories does

    Core Laboratories sells measurement services and products to oil and gas companies. Its labs study reservoir rock and fluids such as crude oil, natural gas and water, so clients can judge how a field performs and recover more oil and gas. It also helps clients evaluate underground sites for carbon capture and storage. Its clients are mostly major, national and independent oil companies.

    It reports two segments. Reservoir Description covers lab analysis of rocks and fluids, field services, studies and the lab equipment it makes. Production Enhancement covers products and services for well completions, perforating, stimulation and well abandonment, including perforating products it makes. Revenue comes from these services and product sales, mainly to the oil and gas industry.

    Core Lab was established in 1936. It has over 70 offices in more than 50 countries and approximately 3,300 employees. In 2023 it changed its home from the Netherlands to Delaware through a redomestication.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling spending by oil and gas companies in downturns, which lowers demand for its services.
    • Conflict in the Middle East, where it owns and runs several laboratories.
    • Risks of doing business in many countries, since it relies on its international operations.
    • Cost-cutting steps that could disrupt operations or delay new products.
    • Holding too much unneeded inventory if it misjudges what clients will order.
    From Core Laboratories's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
