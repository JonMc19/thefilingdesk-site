    What Toll Brothers does

    Toll Brothers designs, builds and sells luxury homes and arranges financing for buyers. It builds single-family and attached homes, master-planned communities and urban low-, mid- and high-rise buildings. Its buyers are luxury first-time, move-up, empty-nester, active-adult and second-home buyers in the United States. At the end of its 2025 fiscal year it operated in 24 states and Washington, D.C. In that year it delivered 11,292 homes.

    Revenue comes mostly from selling homes. Its backlog, homes under contract but not yet delivered, was $5.49 billion at October 31, 2025. It has been adding more ready-to-move-in homes alongside its traditional build-to-order homes. It also owns its own architecture, engineering, mortgage, title, insurance and landscaping subsidiaries, and some lumber and house-component operations.

    Through City Living it builds urban condominiums with partners. It also developed for-rent apartment and student housing, mostly through joint ventures. In September 2025 it announced plans to leave that business, starting with a sale of its interests in about half of its portfolio and its operating platform to Kennedy Wilson for approximately $380 million.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in housing demand, and weaker economic conditions where its buyers live.
    • Inflation and higher interest rates that raise costs and make homes harder to afford.
    • Land that is hard to get at fair prices, and the risks of its land and lot inventory.
    • Earning a large part of its revenue and income in California.
    • Its business strategies are uncertain and it may not reach its goals.
    From Toll Brothers's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 19, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
