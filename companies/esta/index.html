    What Establishment Labs Holdings does

    Establishment Labs is a medical technology company that makes breast implants and related products for cosmetic and reconstructive surgery. It was incorporated in Costa Rica in 2004. Its main product line is Motiva Implants, silicone gel-filled breast implants, which it launched in October 2010 and which have produced most of its revenue.

    Motiva Implants are registered for sale in over 100 countries, and its SmoothSilk round implants were approved in the United States. It sells through exclusive distributors and its own direct sales force. Its product families include round, oval and anatomical implants and a tissue expander. It says its implants carry safety features such as a smooth surface designed to reduce a hardening scar tissue problem and an electronically readable serial number.

    The company also develops Mia Femtech, another product it has studied in clinical research, and it says it competes with companies that have more resources than it does.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a short history in the United States and has lost money to date.
    • Its results depend on the commercial success of Motiva Implants.
    • FDA and other agency disruptions could slow approvals.
    • Competitors with more resources, and pricing pressure, could cut its sales.
    • Product liability claims could lead to big costs.
