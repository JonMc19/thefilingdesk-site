    What Otter Tail does

    Otter Tail is a holding company with an electric utility, manufacturing businesses and plastic pipe businesses. Its offices are in Fergus Falls, Minnesota, and Fargo, North Dakota. It reports three segments: Electric, Manufacturing and Plastics.

    The Electric segment is Otter Tail Power, which dates back to 1907. It generates, buys, transmits and sells electricity in western Minnesota, eastern North Dakota and northeastern South Dakota, serving customers in a mainly rural and farming area. The Manufacturing segment does metal fabrication and makes thermoformed plastic products in Georgia, Illinois and Minnesota. The Plastics segment makes PVC pipe, mostly used in municipal water systems, at plants in North Dakota and Arizona, and sells it in the western United States and Canada.

    Its stated strategy is to invest in the utility and to use cash from the manufacturing and plastics businesses to help pay for that investment. The company says this mix is meant to give steady earnings and dividends.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Large capital investments that carry their own risks.
    • Price and supply of PVC resin, and a limited number of resin vendors.
    • Loss or sharp drop in revenue from key customers.
    • Costs of environmental rules and of co-owned coal-fired plants that it may not fully recover.
    • Regulation of the utility by federal and state agencies and the rates they allow.
    From Otter Tail's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
