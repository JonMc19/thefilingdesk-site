    What Lumen Technologies does

    Lumen Technologies sells networking and communications services, mostly to businesses. It runs a large fiber-optic network across North America and Asia Pacific, and sells connectivity, edge computing, network-as-a-service and cybersecurity products to customers that range from small offices to global enterprises. Its Lumen brand serves enterprise and wholesale customers.

    At the end of 2025 Lumen had two segments. The Business segment serves domestic and global customers. The Mass Markets segment serves households and small businesses in the United States, mainly under the CenturyLink brand, which relies on older copper-based services that the company manages for cash flow.

    In February 2026 Lumen completed the sale of its Mass Markets fiber-to-the-home business in 11 states to AT&T, which narrows the company toward business customers. It sells through direct sales staff, call centers and channel partners. It also reports declining revenues, and it describes legal matters over lead-sheathed cables.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Declining revenues, and the uncertainty that follows from them.
    • Not realizing the benefits of its focus on its private connectivity fabric solutions for business customers.
    • Efforts to win business in artificial intelligence that may fall short.
    • Failures of its network, platforms or services.
    • Claims about lead-sheathed cables that could bring lawsuits, regulatory action and costs.
    From Lumen Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
