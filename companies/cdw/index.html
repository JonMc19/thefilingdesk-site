    What CDW does

    CDW is a reseller and service provider for information technology (IT). It sells hardware and software from many manufacturers, along with integrated IT solutions and services that cover cloud and on-premise systems, digital workplace tools and security. Its customers are businesses, government agencies, schools and universities, and health care organizations in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada.

    It acts as a sales channel for makers of equipment, software publishers and cloud providers, which it calls vendor partners, and for wholesale distributors. It says its approximately 10,500 customer-facing employees include sellers, specialists and engineers. CDW says it can provide integrated IT solutions in approximately 150 countries for customers based in those three countries.

    It reports three segments: Corporate, which serves private-sector US businesses with more than 250 employees; Small Business, for those with up to 250 employees; and Public, for government, education and health care in the US. CDW UK and CDW Canada are grouped in an "other" category. The US business is approximately 90% of net sales. From the first quarter of 2026 it will report Commercial, Government and Education segments instead.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its business depends on its relationships and agreements with vendor partners and wholesale distributors.
    • Its sales depend on technology advances by its vendor partners and on how competitive their products are.
    • Substantial competition could reduce its market share.
    • Failures in its own IT systems, or data breaches, could hurt its operations.
    • Accounts receivable and inventory risks, and rising or disrupted delivery services.
    From CDW's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
