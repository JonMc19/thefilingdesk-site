    What Stitch Fix does

    Stitch Fix is an online personal styling service in the United States. Clients fill out a quiz about their style, fit and budget, and then buy clothing, shoes and accessories in two main ways. In a "Fix," a stylist sends a box of items picked with the help of computer recommendations; clients try the items at home, keep what they want and send back the rest in a prepaid bag. In "Freestyle," clients buy directly from the website or app from personalized suggestions.

    Clients can set up regular shipments or order a Fix on demand. The assortment covers women's, men's, kids', petite, maternity and plus-size apparel, with items from partner brands and from its own private label brands. Some partner brands design and supply items exclusively for it. It earns its revenue from the items clients keep and buy.

    As of August 1, 2026, Stitch Fix had approximately 2,277,000 active clients, and it says it has delivered over 100 million Fixes.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep clients, or to keep them engaged and spending, and failing to attract new ones.
    • Relying on artificial intelligence and machine learning to pick and recommend items.
    • Higher merchandise costs and supply problems, including those from tariffs and trade policy.
    • Paid marketing that may not be effective or affordable.
    • Managing inventory, fulfillment centers and shipping, and its ability to carry out its business changes.
    From Stitch Fix's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2018 to 2019, earnings per share grew 5.9% a year and net income fell 17.8% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 28% and diluted each share's earnings. Stitch Fix then lost money in fiscal 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026.
