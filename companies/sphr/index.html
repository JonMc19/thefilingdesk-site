    What Sphere Entertainment does

    Sphere Entertainment owns Sphere, an entertainment venue in Las Vegas that opened on September 29, 2023, and MSG Networks, which runs regional sports and entertainment TV channels. It is a holding company, and it reports these two as its segments.

    Sphere is a venue with a huge LED display wrapped around the audience, a custom sound system and haptic seats that shake with the action. The outside of the building, called the Exosphere, is covered with nearly 580,000 square feet of LED lighting, which can also show advertising and artistic content. The company plans to build more Spheres and to make its own immersive productions. Sphere relies on licenses from third parties to perform music.

    MSG Networks runs two regional sports and entertainment networks and a direct-to-consumer streaming product. It depends on fees it receives from cable and satellite distributors. The company, formerly Madison Square Garden Entertainment, spun off the Madison Square Garden Entertainment business in April 2023 and no longer holds shares in it. It moved its fiscal year-end to December 31 at the end of 2024.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Building more Sphere venues is complex and costly, and the venture may not succeed.
    • Sphere uses new technology and needs a lot of capital, with no guarantee of lasting success.
    • Large investments in original immersive productions that may not pay off.
    • Depending on third-party licenses to perform music, which could be lost or renewed on worse terms.
    • MSG Networks relying on fees from distributors and possibly being unable to repay its term loan.
    From Sphere Entertainment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
