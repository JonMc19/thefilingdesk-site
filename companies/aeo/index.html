    What American Eagle Outfitters does

    American Eagle Outfitters is a clothing retailer. Its main brands are American Eagle, which sells casual clothing and is known for jeans, and Aerie, which sells intimates, apparel, activewear and swimwear, including the OFFLINE by Aerie line. It also owns Todd Snyder, a menswear brand, and Unsubscribed, a brand of what it calls slow fashion. It sells online and through stores in the U.S., Canada and Mexico.

    At the end of fiscal 2025 it had 1,168 company-owned stores and 357 licensed store locations. Third parties run American Eagle and Aerie stores and online marketplace businesses under license in Asia, Europe, Latin America and the Middle East. The company reports two segments, the American Eagle brand and the Aerie brand. Todd Snyder and Unsubscribed are small enough to be shown together as Other. Revenue comes from selling clothing to customers in its stores, online and in shops within other stores.

    As of January 31, 2026, it operated 805 American Eagle stores, 332 stand-alone Aerie stores, 23 Todd Snyder stores and eight Unsubscribed stores. In fiscal 2025 it decided to close Quiet Platforms, a business that ran its fulfillment centers and also served outside customers, as part of a supply chain project. AEO says it operates in a highly competitive industry and faces strong pricing pressure from existing and new competitors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker discretionary spending, and not keeping up with changing fashion trends and customer preferences.
    • Intense competition and pricing pressure from existing and new rivals.
    • Trade matters, including tariffs and quotas, that could disrupt its supply chain and raise product costs.
    • Its need to grow and manage its online and omni-channel business and the delivery networks behind it.
    • Heavy lease obligations and risks tied to shrinking shopping-center traffic and rising occupancy costs.
    From American Eagle Outfitters's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 30, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
