    Ameriprise Financial

    AMP · NYSE · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Ameriprise Financial's revenue rose 5.5% to $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $3.6 billion, up 4.8%. It paid out $3.5 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 4.6%.
