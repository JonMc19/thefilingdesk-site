    What Ameriprise Financial does

    Ameriprise Financial is a holding company whose subsidiaries give financial planning and advice and manage money for individuals and institutions. It runs two main businesses, Wealth Management and Asset Management.

    Wealth Management works through a network of more than 10,000 financial advisors who serve mostly retail clients. Advisors offer financial planning, cash management and banking products, and full-service brokerage. They sell Ameriprise's own products, including life and disability income insurance and annuities under the RiverSource brand, as well as other providers' products. Ameriprise targets households with investable assets in the middle of the market, and also serves wealthier ones.

    Asset Management, with Columbia Threadneedle Investments as a key brand, offers investment products to individual, institutional and high net worth investors. These products are sold mainly through third parties, and also through Ameriprise's advisors, direct to retail clients and its institutional sales force. The business has expanded from its roots in the United States and the United Kingdom to gather assets worldwide.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Market swings and economic conditions that can cut the value of the assets it manages and advises on.
    • Changes in interest rates that may affect its results.
    • Weaker investment performance than competitors, which could reduce its revenues.
    • Intense competition, including from new technologies and larger rivals.
    • Failing to keep third-party distribution channels, or sales of other firms' products reducing sales of its own.
    From Ameriprise Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
