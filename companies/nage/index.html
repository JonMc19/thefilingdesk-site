    What Niagen Bioscience does

    Niagen Bioscience sells products built around nicotinamide riboside (NR), a form of vitamin B3 that helps the body make NAD+, a coenzyme found in every cell. It was formerly named ChromaDex and changed its name in March 2025. It says NAD+ levels fall with age, by up to 65% between ages 30 and 70.

    It sells Niagen, its brand of NR, as a dietary and food ingredient, and it began selling it in 2013. Since 2017 it has sold Tru Niagen, a dietary supplement sold directly to consumers. In 2024 it launched Niagen Plus products, which contain pharmaceutical-grade Niagen. It also supplies pharmaceutical-grade Niagen to FDA-registered outsourcing facilities and compounding pharmacies abroad, which prepare and distribute injectable Niagen formulations, available only with a prescription.

    It says its future success depends largely on sales of Tru Niagen. The company lists a history of operating losses and says it may need more financing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying largely on sales of one product, Tru Niagen.
    • Competitors that are larger and have more resources, and pressure on its prices.
    • Operating losses in the past, and possibly needing more financing.
    • Depending on its own sales, marketing and distribution capabilities and on key personnel.
    • Not being able to earn money from its products for pharmaceutical uses through partnerships or licensing.
    From Niagen Bioscience's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
