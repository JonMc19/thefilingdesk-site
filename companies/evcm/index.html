    What EverCommerce does

    EverCommerce sells subscription software, over the internet, to small and medium-sized businesses that sell services. Its tools help them automate manual work, find new customers, take payments and keep customers coming back. At the end of 2025 it served more than 745,000 customers.

    It is organized into three verticals, each with software built for one type of business. EverPro serves home services, such as home improvement contractors and maintenance technicians. EverHealth serves health services, such as physician practices and therapists. EverWell serves wellness services, such as salon owners. Many of its customers are very small: it estimates that about 93% of them each brought in less than $2 thousand of revenue in 2025.

    The company has reshaped its portfolio. It sold its fitness software businesses in 2024 and its marketing technology business in October 2025, which is now shown as discontinued operations. In September 2025 it bought ZyraTalk, an AI-powered customer engagement product that it plans to extend across its verticals. EverCommerce says it faces strong competition in each of its markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has had net losses in the past and may not reach profitability.
    • Its growth rate in the past may not carry on, and quarterly results may swing.
    • Intense competition in each of its industries could cost it market share.
    • It may need more debt or equity to fund growth and acquisitions, which may not be available.
    • Errors, failures or security incidents in its software and those of its providers.
    From EverCommerce's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
