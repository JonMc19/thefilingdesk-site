    Akamai Technologies financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Akamai Technologies, Inc · Nasdaq: AKAM Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 20, 2026 · 0001086222-26-000022 Updated October 7, 2026

    Akamai Technologies' revenue rose 5.4% to $4.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 13.5%, up from 13.4% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion, down 10.4%. It paid out $800 million in dividends and buybacks, 79% of free cash flow. The diluted share count fell 4.7%.

    How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation.
