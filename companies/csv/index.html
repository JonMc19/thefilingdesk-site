    What Carriage Services does

    Carriage Services provides funeral and cemetery services and merchandise in the United States. It reports two segments. Funeral Home Operations account for about 65% of revenue and Cemetery Operations for about 35%. At December 31, 2025, it operated 155 funeral homes in 24 states and 28 cemeteries in 9 states.

    Funeral homes provide consultation, preparation of remains, caskets and related merchandise, use of their facilities and chapels for visitation and services, and transportation. Cemeteries sell interment rights such as grave sites, lawn crypts, mausoleum spaces and niches, plus markers, burial containers and monuments, and provide services such as interments. It sells both at-need (at time of death) and preneed (planned ahead).

    In 2025 it bought eight funeral homes, one cemetery and one cremation-focused business in Florida, and sold thirteen funeral homes and four cemeteries. It competes with other public and independent funeral and cemetery operators. In January 2026 it named Steven Metzger as President and Chief Operating Officer, effective February 2, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on one or a few key employees at each local business.
    • A bonus program, called Good to Great, that could mean large payments to its Managing Partners.
    • Whether it can run its Standards Operating Model and find acquisitions on good terms.
    • Competition, including price competition, that could cost it market share.
    • Preneed sales that depend on incentives and economic conditions.
