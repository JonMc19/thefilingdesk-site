    What Coupang does

    Coupang is an online retailer that also runs restaurant delivery, video streaming and financial services. It sells under brands that include Coupang, Eats, Play, Rocket Now and Farfetch, the last of which is an online marketplace for luxury goods. Customers can order everyday items, general merchandise, fresh groceries and niche products, with delivery that can arrive the next day or the same day.

    The company reports two segments: Product Commerce and Developing Offerings. It earns money by selling products, by letting small and medium-sized merchants sell through its apps and websites, and through its WOW membership program, which gives members delivery benefits and other perks. It also earns from advertising on its platforms.

    Coupang says it controls the whole experience from technology to warehouses and delivery, and it builds its own fulfillment and logistics network. It also ships goods for sellers in the United States from its own facilities. It says it competes in a crowded retail industry.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to manage its growth, including the expansion of its warehouse and delivery network.
    • Strong competition in online retail and delivery that could cost it customers or sales.
    • Expanding into new countries and new services that may prove harder or costlier than expected.
    • Inventory risks, and the strain that seasonal peaks put on its operations.
    • Rising food, energy, labor and other costs.
    From Coupang's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
