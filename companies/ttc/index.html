    What Toro does

    Toro makes equipment for caring for land. Its products include professional turf maintenance machines, turf and farm irrigation systems, landscape lighting, snow and ice management gear, underground construction equipment, and residential lawn mowers and snow throwers. Brands include Toro, Ditch Witch, Exmark, Spartan, BOSS, Ventrac, Hayter and Lawn-Boy.

    The company sells worldwide through distributors, dealers, mass retailers, hardware stores, rental centers and home centers, and also online and directly to users. It has two reportable segments, Professional and Residential. In fiscal 2025 the Professional segment made up 80.3 percent of consolidated net sales, Residential 19.0 percent and Other 0.7 percent.

    Toro says a large share of its net sales comes from new or improved products, which it defines as those introduced in the current and previous two fiscal years. It has grown partly by acquisition, most recently Tornado Infrastructure Equipment, and sometimes sells product lines it no longer considers core.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions in the places where it sells.
    • Failing to develop and market new and improved products that customers want.
    • Shortages or disruptions in the supply of commodities, components and parts.
    • Weather, which can strongly affect demand for its products.
    • Intense competition, and rising costs for materials and parts.
    From Toro's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 17, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
