    What Newmark Group does

    Newmark is a commercial real estate adviser and service provider. Its clients are large institutional investors, global corporations and other people who own or occupy commercial property. For owners and investors it sells investment sales and mortgage origination services, landlord leasing, loan servicing, valuation and advisory work, and property management. For corporate occupiers it offers tenant leasing, project and facilities management, and consulting.

    Newmark makes money mainly from commissions on leasing and capital markets deals, mortgage origination and loan servicing fees, property and facility management fees, and consulting and technology fees. For 2025 it generated revenue of about $3.3 billion. Its top 10 clients made up about 9.1% of total revenue. It says it competes with many firms, some with more financial and operating resources.

    The company was founded in New York City in 1929. BGC, a subsidiary of Cantor, bought it in 2011, and it was spun off from BGC in November 2018. It has expanded outside North America since late 2019.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weakness in the economy, the commercial real estate market or the banking sector.
    • Business concentrated in certain regions, and with certain large owners and corporate clients.
    • Intense competition from firms that may have more resources.
    • Liability claims over appraisals, valuations, sales, leasing and property management work.
    • Employment claims linked to the large numbers of building staff it employs in managed properties.
    From Newmark Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
