    What RF Industries does

    RF Industries makes connectors, cables and related products that link communications and network equipment. Its products include RF connectors and adapters, coaxial and data cables, fiber optic cables, wire harnesses, custom cabling, energy-efficient cooling systems and integrated small cell enclosures. It sells mainly to telecommunications and wireless carriers and equipment makers, and to manufacturers in several other markets.

    It has one reportable segment. It used to report two, but combined them after restructuring that included consolidating manufacturing, cutting staff and moving to a single sales organization built around customers.

    It relies on outside contract manufacturers for much of what it sells, including most of its coaxial connectors, and sells partly through independent distributors. Its fiscal year ends October 31.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending heavily on wireless and broadband communications providers.
    • Losing one of a few principal customers that account for a large part of its sales.
    • Relying on outside contract manufacturers for a significant share of its products.
    • Needing continued access to capital while carrying a credit facility.
    • Changes in technology reducing demand for some of its products.
    From RF Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed January 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
