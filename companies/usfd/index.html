    What US Foods does

    US Foods is a food distributor. It buys fresh, frozen and dry food and non-food items from thousands of suppliers and delivers them to restaurants and other foodservice operators across the United States. It supplies about 250,000 customer locations, including independent restaurants, restaurant chains, hospitals and nursing homes, hotels, schools and government sites. It also sells under its own Exclusive Brands and runs an online ordering platform called MOXē.

    The company manages its business as a single segment. It calls itself a broadline distributor, meaning it carries a wide range of products, and competes with local, regional and national distributors as well as cash-and-carry stores, club stores and grocers. In fiscal 2025 no single customer was more than 2% of its customer sales. Group purchasing organizations, which negotiate prices for their members, accounted for about 27% of net sales.

    It delivers through more than 70 distribution facilities and a fleet of more than 6,500 trucks, and it has more than 90 cash-and-carry stores. It completed two acquisitions in 2025, Jake's Finer Foods and Shetakis.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Fewer meals eaten away from home if consumers cut back or the economy weakens.
    • Thin profit margins that food price swings and inflation can squeeze.
    • Intense competition, including rivals that may offer lower prices.
    • Customers that buy by individual purchase order and may leave, including through purchasing groups.
    • Fuel and labor costs, and difficulty hiring and keeping workers.
    From US Foods's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
