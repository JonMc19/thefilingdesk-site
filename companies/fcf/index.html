    What First Commonwealth Financial does

    First Commonwealth Financial is a bank holding company in Indiana, Pennsylvania. Its main subsidiary is First Commonwealth Bank, a Pennsylvania bank and trust company that provides commercial and consumer banking. It also offers trust and wealth management services and offers insurance products through an insurance subsidiary.

    At December 31, 2025, the bank had 126 community banking offices in 30 counties across western and central Pennsylvania and Ohio. It also has commercial lending offices in Canfield, Canton, Hudson, Independence and Lewis Center, Ohio, and in Pittsburgh and Berwyn, Pennsylvania. It runs a network of 132 ATMs and belongs to ATM networks that let customers withdraw cash without surcharges.

    Like other banks, it takes deposits and makes loans, and it earns money on the difference between what it charges on loans and pays on deposits, along with fees for its services. First Commonwealth has grown over the years by buying smaller banks and thrifts, mostly in its home area, and more recently in Ohio.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • An allowance for credit losses that may turn out too small.
    • Exposure to the commercial real estate market.
    • Unrealized losses in its securities portfolio that could affect liquidity.
    • Reliance on dividends from its subsidiary bank for most of its revenue.
    • Competition from other financial institutions for loans and deposits.
