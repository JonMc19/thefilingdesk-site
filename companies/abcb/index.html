    What Ameris Bancorp does

    Ameris Bancorp is a financial holding company. Nearly all of its business runs through its bank subsidiary, Ameris Bank, which serves retail and commercial customers in select markets in Georgia, Alabama, Florida, North Carolina and South Carolina. It also serves some consumer and business customers nationwide through chosen lending channels.

    The bank offers a full range of everyday banking and lending products, treasury and cash management, insurance premium financing, and mortgage and refinancing services. It earns money mainly from the interest on loans and securities, less the interest it pays on deposits, plus fees from services such as mortgage banking. The bank says it competes with larger banks by letting local market leaders make decisions.

    At the end of 2025 it had approximately $27.52 billion in total assets and operated offices only in the United States. Besides growing its existing business, it says it expects to buy other banks when suitable chances come up.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition from other banks and financial firms.
    • Earnings that depend closely on market interest rates.
    • Losing deposits, which could mean liquidity problems and higher funding costs.
    • Credit losses larger than the amount it has set aside for them.
    • Acquisitions that may not bring the revenue or savings it expects.
    From Ameris Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
