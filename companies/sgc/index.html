    What Superior Group Of Companies does

    Superior Group of Companies sells branded merchandise, healthcare clothing and contact center services. It was organized in 1920. It has three reporting segments: Branded Products, Healthcare Apparel and Contact Centers.

    Branded Products, the largest, sells customized promotional products, merchandise and uniform programs to retail chains, food service, entertainment, technology and transportation companies, among others. It accounted for about 64% of net sales in 2025. Healthcare Apparel sells scrubs, lab coats, patient gowns and protective clothing, under brands such as Wink and Fashion Seal Healthcare, mostly in the United States. It accounted for about 20% of net sales. Customers include healthcare laundries, dealers, distributors, retailers and consumers.

    The Contact Centers segment runs call centers that handle customer support for North American companies from sites in Central America and the Caribbean. The company makes its products in its own plants or through third parties, and ships them from its US distribution centers or directly from suppliers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Shortages or interruptions at the suppliers and factories it depends on for goods and raw materials.
    • Customers canceling or cutting back their orders.
    • Failing to forecast customer demand and manage inventory correctly.
    • Problems with its distribution systems that could keep it from filling orders.
    • Acquisitions that could be hard to integrate or may not bring the expected benefits.
