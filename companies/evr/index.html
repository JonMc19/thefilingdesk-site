    What Evercore does

    Evercore is an independent investment bank, meaning it does not run a commercial bank or do significant trading for its own account. It gives strategic advice on mergers and acquisitions, capital raising, and debt and restructuring matters for companies, investors and other clients. It also has an equities business. It was founded on the idea that clients benefit from advice free of the conflicts that can arise inside large, multi-product financial firms.

    The company has two segments. Investment Banking & Equities made $3.69 billion, or 98% of 2025 revenues excluding Other Revenue, net. It earns advisory and underwriting fees from clients. Investment Management, which includes wealth management, made 2% of those revenues.

    Evercore says it grows by promoting, recruiting and acquiring senior professionals in investment banking. It depends heavily on those professionals and says its revenue and profits are highly volatile.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak market conditions that reduce the volume and value of the deals it advises on.
    • Losing its senior professionals, who it depends on heavily.
    • Volatile revenue and profit that make steady earnings growth hard.
    • Conflicts of interest, or employee misconduct, that could damage its reputation.
    • Clients being unable to pay for its services.
    From Evercore's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
