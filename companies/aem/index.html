    What Agnico Eagle does

    Agnico Eagle is a Canadian gold mining company. It mines and sells gold and other precious metals from operations in Canada, Australia, Finland and Mexico, and it explores for and develops new projects. It says it is Canada's largest mining company and the second largest gold producer in the world. It was founded in 1957.

    Its operating mines include LaRonde, Canadian Malartic, Goldex, Detour Lake and Macassa in Canada, Meadowbank and Meliadine in Nunavut, Fosterville in Australia, Kittila in Finland and Pinos Altos in Mexico. Its income depends on the price of gold and on how much gold its mines produce. It says it does not rely on any particular buyer or contract for its gold.

    Gold is its main focus. The company also watches for, and has invested in, projects for zinc, copper, nickel, phosphate and lithium. At the end of 2025 it counted 18,216 people at work for it, including permanent employees, contractors, temporary workers and students.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the price of gold, which can change its results widely.
    • Mining accidents or adverse conditions that cut the gold it produces.
    • Remote Nunavut operations that may face difficulties.
    • Rising costs per ounce from inflation and other outside factors.
    • Reserve estimates that may not match actual recovery, and environmental liabilities.
