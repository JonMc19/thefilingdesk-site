    What TriMas does

    TriMas, based in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan, designs and makes a mix of products mainly for consumer products, aerospace and defense, and industrial markets. It is organized into TriMas Packaging, TriMas Aerospace and Specialty Products groups. Packaging makes items such as caps, closures, pumps and dispensers. Specialty Products includes Norris Cylinder, a maker of high- and low-pressure cylinders. The company has approximately 3,700 employees at 37 manufacturing and support locations in 13 countries.

    On November 4, 2025, TriMas agreed to sell TriMas Aerospace to an affiliate of Tinicum and funds managed by Blackstone. The purchase price is approximately $1.45 billion in cash, and the sale is expected to close in the first quarter of 2026. Because of the sale, the company shows Aerospace as a discontinued operation. Its results for continuing operations cover the rest of the business: net sales were $645.7 million in 2025, and about 66% of them came from North America.

    TriMas says many of its products are customized for its customers, for example caps and closures with tailored colors, sizes and dispensing features, and that it has long relationships with many of them. It competes in markets it describes as highly competitive.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker sales and margins when the economy slows or customers in cyclical industries cut back.
    • Competition in many of its markets that could limit sales volumes and reduce margins.
    • A more concentrated customer base, and the loss of key customers or critical suppliers.
    • Higher raw material or energy costs, and shifts in U.S. trade policy.
    • Buying and selling businesses, including the sale of Aerospace, which can bring unexpected liabilities.
    From TriMas's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
