    What Broadridge Financial Solutions does

    Broadridge provides technology and communications services to banks, broker-dealers, mutual funds and public companies. Its biggest job is handling the proxy process: it delivers proxy materials to people who own shares through a broker or bank and collects their votes for the companies and funds that hold shareholder meetings. It also produces customer statements, fund reports and other regulated documents, in print and digitally.

    It has two reportable segments. Investor Communication Solutions is the larger, at about 74% of revenue in fiscal 2026. It covers proxy and regulatory services, fund data and communications, services for company issuers, and customer communications. Global Technology and Operations supplies trading and back-office software and outsourced processing to financial firms.

    Many banks and brokers have contracted the proxy work out to Broadridge. A large share of its revenue comes from a small number of financial services clients.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large share of its revenue comes from a small number of financial services clients.
    • Consolidation among financial firms, which could remove clients.
    • Falling activity in the securities markets, which would reduce its revenue.
    • Its reliance on postal services and other carriers to deliver printed communications.
    • Cybersecurity incidents or system failures that could disrupt services and expose client data.
    From Broadridge Financial Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
