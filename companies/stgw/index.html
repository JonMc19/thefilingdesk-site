    What Stagwell does

    Stagwell is a marketing and communications company. It owns a group of agencies, which it calls Brands, that sell creative work, digital marketing and technology services to companies. It had more than 4,500 clients at the end of 2025 and staff in over 34 countries.

    Its services fall into several groups: marketing services, digital transformation, media and commerce, and communications, which includes advocacy work. It also sells software and data tools to marketers under the name The Marketing Cloud. Those tools cover research and insights, communications technology and media, and some use artificial intelligence to create content and run quick market research. The company says this software is a key part of its future strategy.

    Stagwell makes money mainly from fees clients pay its agencies for their work. It says it competes in a crowded and fragmented industry, against older holding companies as well as smaller firms. It says its digital focus is an advantage and that it can bid for large multi-region contracts. It has grown through both its own expansion and acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue falls quickly when economic conditions weaken, because clients cut marketing budgets.
    • It depends on winning and keeping clients, and rivals may discount their prices to take work.
    • It must keep pace with changes in technology and offerings, including generative AI.
    • Its international operations are exposed to a range of risks.
    • Clients may fail to pay what they owe.
    From Stagwell's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
