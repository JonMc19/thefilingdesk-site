    What Energizer does

    Energizer makes and sells batteries, auto care products and portable lights. Its household and specialty batteries include alkaline, lithium, rechargeable and hearing aid types, sold under the Energizer, Eveready and Rayovac brands, and under Varta in Latin America and Asia Pacific. It also sells car appearance and fragrance products under brands such as Armor All, Nu Finish and California Scents, performance products such as STP fuel and oil additives, and air conditioning recharge products.

    It makes money by selling these products, mostly to retailers, and sells around the world. It says competition is based on brand perception, innovation, product performance, customer service and price. It says the loss of any of its principal customers could significantly lower its sales.

    The company has about 6,050 employees in 34 countries. It trades on the New York Stock Exchange as ENR. The 10-K says the company has made recent acquisitions, which it describes in its financial statements.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition in batteries and auto care that could hurt profits or its relationships with customers.
    • Losing a principal customer, which could significantly cut its sales.
    • Changes in the retail environment and in what consumers prefer.
    • Rising raw material, transport and tariff costs, and reliance on certain key suppliers.
    • Risks of international operations, including tariffs and currency swings.
    From Energizer's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
