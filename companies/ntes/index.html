    What NetEase does

    NetEase is a Chinese internet company whose main business is online games. It also runs a music streaming service (NetEase Cloud Music), an online learning business (Youdao) and internet content services. It develops and operates games for players in China and, as part of its growth plan, increasingly for players in other countries.

    Its games and related value-added services segment includes both the operation of online games and other services that go with the games. Online games bring in the large majority of that segment's net revenues. It earns this money from players and works with third-party platforms to distribute its games and collect payments.

    NetEase runs its primary businesses in mainland China, including games, music streaming, online learning and internet content, through contractual arrangements with companies known as VIEs. Foreign investment in these kinds of businesses is restricted by law. NetEase says it consolidates the VIEs because it receives their economic benefits and directs their key activities, and that any violation of these agreements could disrupt its operations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to develop and launch popular new games on time, or to keep existing games engaging.
    • Chinese rules limiting how long minors may play online games, and the need for government approval of new games.
    • Reliance on third-party platforms to distribute its games and collect payments, on terms that may change.
    • Risks of expanding its games to players outside China.
    • Its dependence on contractual arrangements with VIEs to run its businesses in China.
    From NetEase's annual report on Form 20-F, filed April 15, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
