    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Antero Midstream's figures start in 2019, when it combined with Antero Midstream Partners; its earlier filings are those of the partnership's general partner, a holding company with a small fraction of the business. Antero Midstream's cash flow statement splits its spending across gathering, water and other lines under its own labels, and the lines tagged cover only part of it in some years, so capital spending and free cash flow are left blank. Antero Midstream held no cash at the end of 2021, 2022 and 2024 (its cash flow statements show its cash falling to zero), so cash is counted as zero in those years.
