    What PPL does

    PPL is a utility holding company based in Allentown, Pennsylvania. Through regulated subsidiaries, it delivers electricity to customers in Pennsylvania, Kentucky, Virginia and Rhode Island, delivers natural gas in Kentucky and Rhode Island, and generates electricity at power plants in Kentucky.

    PPL reports three regulated segments by region: Pennsylvania, Kentucky and Rhode Island. In Pennsylvania, PPL Electric sends power over its transmission and distribution lines. In Kentucky, LG&E and KU generate, transmit and deliver electricity, and LG&E also sells natural gas. In Rhode Island, Rhode Island Energy handles electricity and gas distribution.

    Regulators set the rates these utilities charge, so PPL's earnings depend on recovering its costs of serving customers. PPL itself is a holding company, so it relies on cash from its subsidiaries to pay its own debts.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not recovering the costs of its utility services in the rates regulators allow.
    • Its capital plans depending in part on the continued growth of data centers.
    • Big capital projects that may run into unforeseen costs or delays.
    • Relying on its subsidiaries' cash to meet PPL's own debt obligations.
    • Costs tied to environmental cleanup and to natural gas supply infrastructure.
    From PPL's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
