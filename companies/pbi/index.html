    What Pitney Bowes does

    Pitney Bowes provides shipping and mailing technology and financial services to businesses and government agencies. Its customers range from small businesses to large enterprises. It sells equipment and software for sending letters and parcels, along with supplies, maintenance and shipping services that let customers buy postage, print labels and track packages from multiple carriers.

    It has two main segments. SendTech Solutions sells the shipping and mailing technology, and its bank subsidiary, The Pitney Bowes Bank, offers financing for equipment, a revolving credit product for meter rental payments and postage, and a deposit product for prepaid postage. Presort Services sorts mail for customers so they can qualify for postal discounts; the company says it is the largest workshare partner of the United States Postal Service. An Other category holds leftovers from its former Global Ecommerce business, which it wound down in August 2024.

    The company sells through its own sales force, partners and digital channels. It has approximately 6,600 employees, with 75% in the United States. It competes with other mail equipment makers and online shipping and mailing services.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The financial condition and rules of the USPS and other national postal services.
    • Falling volumes of physical mail.
    • Reliance on contracts with the USPS and other carriers for its shipping business.
    • Postal regulations that could hurt its results.
    • Competitive markets, and reliance on third-party suppliers.
    From Pitney Bowes's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
