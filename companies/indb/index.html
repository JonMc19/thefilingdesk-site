    What Independent Bank does

    Independent Bank Corp. is the holding company for Rockland Trust, a Massachusetts bank founded in 1907. The bank offers everyday banking, lending, investment and wealth services to people and businesses through more than 150 retail branches, plus lending centers and investment management offices, mainly in Eastern Massachusetts, Worcester County, southern New Hampshire and Rhode Island. It also offers online, mobile and telephone banking.

    Like most banks, it makes money mainly from the difference between the interest it earns on loans and securities and the interest it pays on deposits, plus fees for its services. Rockland Trust is its only reportable segment.

    At the end of 2025 it had total assets of $24.9 billion, total deposits of $20.1 billion and stockholders' equity of $3.6 billion. On July 1, 2025 it completed its purchase of Enterprise Bancorp, paying part in its own shares and part in cash.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates that squeeze the difference between what it earns and pays.
    • Loan losses if borrowers fall behind, especially in commercial real estate.
    • Failing to keep deposits and loan quality steady at banks it has bought, such as Enterprise.
    • Banking rules and policy changes that raise its costs or limit what it can do.
    • Cyberattacks or technology failures that disrupt service or expose customer data.
    From Independent Bank's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
