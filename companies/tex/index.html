    What Terex does

    Terex is a global maker of industrial equipment. It builds materials processing machinery, waste and recycling equipment, mobile elevating work platforms (MEWPs, the lifts workers use to reach heights) and equipment for electric utilities. Its products are used in maintenance, manufacturing, energy, waste and recycling, minerals, construction and the entertainment industry. It makes them in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific and sells them worldwide, and it also supplies parts, service and digital tools.

    It reports three segments. Environmental Solutions makes refuse truck bodies, recycling equipment, and utility products such as digger derricks and insulated aerial devices, under brands such as Heil and Marathon. Material Processing makes materials processing machinery. Aerials makes MEWPs. Some of its sales go through dealers.

    Terex has grown through acquisitions and sold off businesses it saw as non-core. It bought the Environmental Solutions Group from Dover in 2024 and completed a merger with REV on February 2, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Managing the expanded business after the REV merger.
    • Strong competition and pricing pressure in its industry.
    • Dependence on dealers, so problems in its dealer network could hurt sales.
    • New or higher tariffs on its materials and products.
    • A large amount of debt, with covenants it must meet.
    From Terex's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
