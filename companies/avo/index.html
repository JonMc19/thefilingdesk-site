    What Mission Produce does

    Mission Produce buys, grows, packs, ripens and distributes avocados, mostly the Hass variety, to food retailers, distributors, wholesalers and foodservice customers around the world. It sources fruit mainly from Mexico, Peru and California, from thousands of third-party growers and from its own orchards. It also sells a small amount of other fruit, such as mangos.

    It reports three segments. Marketing & Distribution sources fruit from growers and sells it through the company's distribution network, which has centers in North America, China, Europe and the U.K. International Farming owns and runs orchards, mainly in Peru and Guatemala, and most of its fruit is sold through the first segment. Blueberries grows blueberries, and substantially all of them are sold to a single distributor under an exclusive agreement. Mission also earns fees for ripening, bagging, custom packaging and logistics. It has no long-term supply contracts with customers. It says its competitors range from other distributors and packers to farming businesses of all sizes, and that competition is sharper because fresh fruit is perishable.

    At October 31, 2025 it had approximately 3,800 employees worldwide, about 2,100 of them in Peru.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance mainly on one product, avocados, so problems with supply or demand hit the whole business.
    • Market prices for its fruit, which it does not control, and the perishable nature of fresh produce.
    • Political and economic conditions in Mexico and Peru, where much of its fruit is grown.
    • Losing one or more of its largest customers.
    • Weather, climate and other farming risks, and food safety events or product recalls.
    From Mission Produce's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
