    What Veeva Systems does

    Veeva sells cloud software, data and business consulting to life sciences companies: drugmakers, biotechnology firms and makers of medical devices and diagnostics. Its products help them run clinical trials, handle regulatory and safety work, manage quality and manufacturing, and market and sell their products while following government rules.

    Its offerings are grouped into four product categories: Veeva Development Cloud, Veeva Quality Cloud, Veeva Commercial Cloud and Veeva Data Cloud. For financial reporting, it combines the last two as Commercial Solutions and the first two as R&D and Quality Solutions. Many of its applications run on its own Veeva Vault platform, which manages documents and data. It is also adding artificial intelligence agents, called Veeva AI, to its applications.

    Veeva is a Delaware public benefit corporation. That means its directors must weigh shareholders' financial interests against the interests of other groups and a stated public purpose. It is moving customers of its customer relationship management software to a version built on Vault. Its fiscal year ends January 31, and a small number of key customers account for a large share of its revenue.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition that could cost it customers.
    • Revenue that is concentrated in a small number of key customers.
    • Newer products that may not be adopted widely enough to sustain its growth.
    • Disruption from moving its CRM customers to the Vault version.
    • Security breaches or defects that could harm demand for its software.
    From Veeva Systems's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
