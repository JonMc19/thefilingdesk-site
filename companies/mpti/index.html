    What M-tron Industries does

    M-tron Industries, known as Mtron, designs and makes electronic components used where accuracy and reliability matter in harsh conditions. Its parts go into aerospace and defense systems, avionics, satellites, global positioning systems, down-hole drilling, medical systems, instruments and industrial devices.

    It reports one segment, Electronic Components, with three product groups. Frequency Control covers parts such as quartz crystal resonators and clock oscillators. Spectrum Control covers radio frequency and microwave filters and power amplifiers, and Integrated Microwave Assemblies combines components into larger products. It is moving from mainly crystal-based parts toward more integrated products. Mtron supports customers from product design and prototyping through production and upgrades.

    It has factories in Orlando, Florida; Yankton, South Dakota; and Noida, India, and a sales office in Hong Kong. Its U.S. plants hold defense export registration and aerospace quality certifications. Its strategy is to grow through its own research and through partnerships, joint ventures and acquisitions. Its shares trade on the NYSE American.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a single line of business.
    • A small number of customers making up much of its revenue, and losing any of them.
    • Reliance on the aerospace and defense industry, which is hit by changes in government spending.
    • An order backlog that may not predict future revenue, and results that swing from period to period.
    • Variable-rate debt that raises its interest costs when rates rise.
    From M-tron Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
