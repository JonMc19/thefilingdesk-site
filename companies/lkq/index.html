    What LKQ does

    LKQ distributes replacement parts and accessories for cars and trucks. It sells to repair shops and other businesses, mainly in North America and Europe. Its products include collision parts such as bumper covers, fenders, lights and paint, mechanical parts such as brake pads, filters, batteries, engines and transmissions, and accessories that change a vehicle's performance or look.

    Most of what it sells is "alternative" parts: aftermarket parts made by companies other than the carmakers, salvaged parts taken from vehicles written off after accidents, and reconditioned parts. LKQ has three segments: North America, Europe and Specialty. The Europe segment sells in Germany, the United Kingdom, the Benelux countries, Italy and other countries. The Specialty segment distributes aftermarket products and accessories in the U.S. and Canada.

    LKQ competes with local, national, international and internet-based parts sellers, as well as with carmakers' new parts. In September 2025 it sold its Self Service segment, and that business now shows as discontinued operations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on insurers and customers to choose alternative parts instead of new carmaker parts.
    • Fewer accidents and repairs, or a change in the mix of vehicles on the road, reducing demand.
    • Competition from local, national and internet-based parts sellers.
    • Problems with suppliers or inventory supply, including suppliers in China and Taiwan.
    • A large debt load and the restrictions that come with it.
    From LKQ's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
