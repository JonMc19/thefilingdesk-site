    What Masco does

    Masco designs, makes and sells home improvement and building products. Its best-known lines are BEHR paint, DELTA and HANSGROHE faucets and shower fixtures, LIBERTY cabinet and door hardware, and HOT SPRING spas. It sells mostly to home center retailers, online retailers, wholesalers and distributors, which in turn sell to contractors, remodelers and consumers.

    Masco reports two segments. Plumbing Products covers faucets, showers, tubs, shower enclosures, spas, saunas and plumbing components, sold mainly in North America and Europe. Decorative Architectural Products covers paint and other coatings, along with decorative hardware. Architectural coatings made up about 31 percent of consolidated net sales in 2025. Masco names competitors such as Kohler, Fortune Brands' Moen, and Sherwin-Williams, and says it also faces private label and online-only brands.

    The Home Depot is Masco's largest customer. It holds exclusive rights to sell the BEHR brand at retail in North America, and Masco says losing those sales would hurt its business badly. Masco had about 18,000 employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Making use of its brands, products and ideas across many channels and regions may not deliver the benefits Masco expects.
    • Rising or unstable costs for raw materials, and tariffs on imported materials and products.
    • Reliance on suppliers and on a few large retail customers such as The Home Depot.
    • Competition, including private label and online-only brands, and pressure on prices.
    • Risks that come with operating in many countries.
    From Masco's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
