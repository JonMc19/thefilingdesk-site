    What Papa John's does

    Papa John's operates and franchises pizza restaurants, mostly for delivery and carryout. At December 28, 2025, there were 6,083 Papa Johns restaurants in 50 countries and territories: 475 owned by the company and 5,608 run by franchisees. It says most of its sales occur through digital channels.

    Papa John's has four reportable segments: Domestic Company-owned restaurants, North America franchising, North America commissaries and International. Company-owned restaurants earn money from retail sales of pizza and other food and drinks. Franchising earns royalties and fees from franchisees in the United States and Canada. Commissaries supply ingredients and other goods to restaurants. Of the North American restaurants, approximately 13% of restaurants were company-owned. The company-owned restaurants in its comparable base averaged $1.3 million in annual sales.

    Papa John's says it competes with other quick-service restaurants, especially in pizza, for customers, workers, franchisees and restaurant sites. In December 2025 it began a new business transformation program, called the Enterprise Transformation Plan, after finishing a plan to reshape its international business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in the quick-service restaurant and pizza market for customers, workers and franchisees.
    • Risks from its franchise model, and from its international franchisees, who face their own difficulties.
    • Its turnaround efforts in the United Kingdom could stall and hurt its results.
    • Higher costs for food ingredients and labor, and its reliance on a sole or limited number of suppliers for some items.
    • Food safety and quality concerns that could damage its brand and sales.
    From Papa John's's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
