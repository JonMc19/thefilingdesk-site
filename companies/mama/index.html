    What Mama's Creations does

    Mama's Creations makes and sells fresh prepared foods for the deli sections of grocery stores. Its main products are meatballs, meat loaf, sausage products, and pasta and rice entrees under the MamaMancini's brand. Through two other businesses, T&L Creative Salads and Olive Branch, it also sells salads and olives. It sells to supermarkets, club chains, mass-market retailers and food distributors, and says its products are in over 12,000 stores.

    The company earns its revenue by selling these foods to retailers and distributors, mostly through a network of commission brokers. It makes almost all of its products in its own plants. It says the deli-prepared food industry is highly competitive, with local and national competitors.

    At the end of January 2026 it had 581 full-time employees. In September 2025 it bought the assets of Crown I Enterprises, a food manufacturer of value-added proteins and ready-to-heat meals, for about $17.3 million in cash.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Product recalls, or illness or harm that people blame on its foods.
    • A limited number of large customers account for the majority of its business.
    • Higher costs for raw materials and energy, and supply chain disruptions or reliance on single suppliers.
    • Competition from larger companies with more money and longer track records.
    • Trouble carrying out its growth plan, including buying and combining other companies.
    From Mama's Creations's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
