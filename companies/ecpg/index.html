    What Encore Capital Group does

    Encore Capital buys portfolios of defaulted consumer debt and collects on them. Defaulted receivables are unpaid consumer obligations owed to banks, credit unions, consumer finance companies and retailers, and the company buys them at deep discounts to face value. It then works with the people who owe the money as they repay and recover financially.

    It earns money from what it collects on the portfolios it buys, and in Europe also from servicing non-performing loans for the lenders that own them. In the United States it operates through Midland Credit Management (MCM). In Europe and the United Kingdom it operates through Cabot Credit Management (Cabot). Smaller operations in India and Mexico are not significant to its results.

    The company says it plans to keep investing in portfolio buying and recovery in the United States and United Kingdom and to build its business in France and Spain. It is based in San Diego, California.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions can make it harder for consumers to repay, hurting collections.
    • It may be unable to buy portfolios at good prices, or may lose supply if a main seller buys less.
    • Portfolios may turn out unprofitable, and its models of future cash flows may prove wrong.
    • A large part of its collections relies on lawsuits against consumers, which raises costs and legal exposure.
    • Rising consumer bankruptcies or changes in bankruptcy law could reduce what it collects.
    From Encore Capital Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
