    What StoneX Group does

    StoneX runs a global financial services network that connects companies, traders and investors to financial markets. It gives clients access to nearly all major markets worldwide and handles the whole life of a trade, from placing the order to clearing and settlement. It has more than 5,400 employees.

    It serves more than 80,000 commercial, institutional and payments clients, and over 400,000 self-directed retail accounts, in more than 180 countries. Clients include businesses, broker-dealers, asset managers, insurers, banks and individual traders. It reaches them through direct sales, mobile and web platforms such as FOREX.com, introducing brokers and white-label partners.

    It makes money from commissions and spreads when clients trade, from interest and fees on client balances, and from consulting fees for market intelligence and risk management. It also trades for its own account as a market maker, so it holds positions that can lose value. It buys small and mid-sized companies to grow.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Revenue and results that swing with markets and may not stay steady even as it adds new sources.
    • Losses on positions it holds as a market maker when prices move sharply.
    • Losses on over-the-counter derivative trades, including from weak internal systems and controls.
    • The high and rising cost of complying with financial regulation.
    • Competitors that use new technology faster, and acquisitions that may not work out.
    From StoneX Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 28, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
