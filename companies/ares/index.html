    What Ares Management does

    Ares Management is an investment manager that invests money for institutions and individuals. At the end of 2025 it managed $622.5 billion of client assets, up from $94.0 billion ten years earlier. It has over 4,250 employees in more than 25 countries and was founded in 1997. Ares calls itself a global alternative investment manager, meaning it manages investments other than ordinary stocks and bonds.

    Its investment groups are Credit, Real Assets, Secondaries and Private Equity. Its investors include more than 2,850 direct institutional relationships, plus individual investors who hold its publicly traded funds, sub-advised accounts and perpetual wealth vehicles (funds with no fixed end date).

    Ares earns management fees on the assets it runs for these funds, and part of its revenue, earnings and cash flow is variable. Its 10-K says the management fee from one fund, Ares Capital Corporation (ARCC), makes up a significant portion of its management fees. It describes its investment approach as credit-oriented and says it competes in an intensely competitive business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak markets can cut the value of the assets it manages and make it harder to raise money.
    • Its business depends on raising capital from investors, and revenue from fees can vary a lot.
    • Reliance on the management fee from Ares Capital Corporation for a large part of its fees.
    • Pressure from investors to lower fees.
    • Conflicts of interest among its funds that could damage its reputation if handled badly.
    From Ares Management's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
