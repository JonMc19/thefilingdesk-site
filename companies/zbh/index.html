    What Zimmer Biomet does

    Zimmer Biomet designs, makes and sells medical devices for bones, joints and soft tissue. Its products include knee and hip replacements and other orthopedic reconstructive products, sports medicine, biologics, extremities and trauma products, craniomaxillofacial and thoracic products, bone cement and surgical products. It also sells digital and robotic technologies that use data and artificial intelligence.

    Its customers include surgeons and other specialists, hospitals, stocking distributors, healthcare dealers and buying groups. It sells through two main channels: directly to healthcare institutions such as hospitals and ambulatory surgery centers, and through stocking distributors and healthcare dealers. Much of its inventory is held on consignment, meaning it stays in the company's ownership until it is used in surgery. The 10-K says consignment sales were approximately 85 percent of net sales in 2025, and that no single customer accounted for more than 2 percent.

    At the end of 2025 the company employed approximately 17,000 people worldwide, about 7,000 of them in the United States, and about 6,000 work in manufacturing. The 10-K says the orthopedics and wider musculoskeletal care industry is highly competitive.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its restructuring programs may not deliver the savings it expects.
    • Its product portfolio rationalization may not deliver the expected savings.
    • It may be unable to integrate acquired businesses or earn the expected profit from them.
    • Interruptions to manufacturing or distribution could hurt its business.
    • Problems putting in a new enterprise resource planning system have already hurt its business.
