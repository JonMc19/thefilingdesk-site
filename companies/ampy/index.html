    What Amplify Energy does

    Amplify Energy produces oil and natural gas. It buys, develops and runs oil and gas properties and sells what they produce. It reports one business segment. In 2025 it sold its Oklahoma, East Texas and North Louisiana and non-operated Eagle Ford assets, so its remaining properties are the Bairoil field in the Rockies and the Beta field in federal waters off Southern California.

    Its revenue comes from selling that oil, and the 10-K says it depends on a small number of customers for most of its production sales. Its estimated reserves are mostly oil, about 93% oil and the rest natural gas liquids (NGLs). It says it operates all of the properties that hold its reserves. At Bairoil it injects carbon dioxide (CO2) into the ground to recover more oil.

    Amplify says Beta held about 64% of its estimated proved reserves at the end of 2025. It cut its workforce in late 2025 and early 2026, and it used the money from its sales to reduce borrowings under its revolving credit facility, on which it owed nothing at year end. It competes with larger companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing one of the few customers that buy most of its production.
    • Its properties are concentrated in a few places, including an offshore field.
    • Reliance on a supply of carbon dioxide for its Bairoil oil recovery.
    • Failing to replace the oil reserves it produces, and reserve estimates that prove wrong.
    • Swings in oil prices, and lenders' limits on how much it can borrow.
    From Amplify Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
