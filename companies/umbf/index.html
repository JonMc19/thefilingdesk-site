    What UMB Financial does

    UMB Financial is a bank holding company based in Kansas City, Missouri. It provides banking services and asset servicing to customers in the United States and around the world. It owns one national bank, UMB Bank, and several other subsidiaries. The bank has its main office in Missouri and serves customers mainly in the Midwest, Southwest and West.

    The bank offers a full range of banking products to businesses, individuals, governments and other banks, including asset management, trust, bankcard and cash management services. The company reports three segments: Commercial Banking, Institutional Banking and Personal Banking. A subsidiary, UMB Fund Services, provides fund accounting, transfer agency and other services to mutual fund and alternative-investment groups.

    On January 31, 2025, UMB bought Heartland Financial USA, a bank holding company, in an all-stock deal. UMB says it faces intense competition in all of its segments from banks, asset managers, brokerage firms and other financial companies, both traditional and new ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates, which affect the income it earns on loans and the cost of its deposits.
    • Weaker economic conditions that raise loan defaults and credit losses, including on loans backed by real estate.
    • Limits on funding and liquidity, or higher costs to get it.
    • Falls in the assets it manages or administers, which would cut its fee income.
    • Cybersecurity incidents and other security breaches.
