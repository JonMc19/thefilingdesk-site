    What Pathward Financial does

    Pathward Financial is the holding company for Pathward, a national bank based in Sioux Falls, South Dakota. It says its purpose is to power financial inclusion: building payments and lending products for people and businesses that traditional banks underserve. It works with third parties, such as financial technology companies and other businesses, that offer products built on the bank.

    The company has two business lines. Partner Solutions provides payments and banking services behind partners' products, such as prepaid cards and consumer accounts. Commercial Finance makes loans to businesses, including term loans, asset-based loans and equipment finance. Its fiscal year ends September 30. As of September 30, 2025, 60% of its term lending exposure was in solar and other alternative energy projects, mostly construction.

    The bank earns interest on loans and leases and on investments, and fees from payments. A large part of its deposits, assets and income come from deposit accounts created through Partner Solutions customers. It says it competes in an extremely competitive market.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Borrowers failing to pay, and set-asides for loan losses proving too small.
    • Heavy reliance on deposits and income from a limited number of partner relationships.
    • Changes in how consumers use prepaid cards.
    • Depending on third parties whose performance it does not control.
    • Fraud losses from customer accounts.
    From Pathward Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
