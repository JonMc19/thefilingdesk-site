    What American Assets Trust does

    American Assets Trust is a real estate investment trust, or REIT: a company that owns property and pays out most of its rental income to shareholders. It owns, operates and develops office, retail and multifamily (apartment) properties, plus one mixed-use property that combines a hotel and a shopping center. Its properties are in Southern California, Northern California, Washington, Oregon, Texas and Hawaii.

    It earns money mainly from rent paid by office and retail tenants and by apartment residents, and from the hotel's room and related revenue. Its core markets include San Diego, the San Francisco Bay Area, Bellevue, Portland and Oahu. It also owns land at two properties that it holds for development.

    The company is led by its executive chairman and a management team that it describes as experienced in office, retail and apartment real estate. It carries out its business through an operating partnership, in which the company holds the controlling interest, and it uses debt and outside capital to buy, redevelop and build properties.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its properties are concentrated in a few regions, so local economic conditions and natural disasters weigh heavily.
    • Weak demand for office space could hurt its office properties.
    • Its large debt could lead to default, and refinancing may be hard or costly.
    • Dependence on a few large office tenants and on anchor stores at its shopping centers.
    • Trouble renewing leases or re-letting vacant space as leases end.
