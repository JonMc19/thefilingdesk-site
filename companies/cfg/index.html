    What Citizens Financial Group does

    Citizens Financial Group is a bank holding company based in Providence, Rhode Island. It offers everyday banking, lending and investment services to individuals, small businesses, mid-sized companies, large corporations and institutions. It has more than 1,000 branches in 14 states and the District of Columbia, plus online and mobile banking. At the end of 2025 it had total assets of $226.4 billion and deposits of $183.3 billion.

    It reports two segments. Consumer Banking serves individuals and small businesses with deposits, mortgages, home equity loans, credit cards, small business loans and wealth management. It also offers education and point-of-sale loans and some digital deposit products nationwide. Commercial Banking serves companies and institutions with loans and leases, deposit and treasury services, foreign exchange and risk management, and capital markets and advisory work. Like most banks, it earns money mainly from interest on loans and securities, less interest paid on deposits, and from fees.

    It has 17,398 full-time equivalent employees, mostly in New England and the Mid-Atlantic. Its main banking subsidiary is Citizens Bank, N.A.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates, which can affect its profit, its liquidity and the value of its securities.
    • Borrowers' credit quality getting worse, which can cause loan losses.
    • Reliance on customer deposits as its main low-cost source of funding.
    • Rules and limits from its bank regulators on what it can do.
    • Failing to carry out its business strategy, or to attract and keep skilled staff.
    From Citizens Financial Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
