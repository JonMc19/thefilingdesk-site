    What James Hardie Industries does

    James Hardie, domiciled in Ireland, makes exterior home and outdoor living products. They include fiber cement siding and trim, fiber gypsum interior walls and floors, and composite and PVC decking and railing. Its brands include Hardie, TimberTech, AZEK Exteriors, Versatex, fermacell and StruXure. It sells in North America, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, with the United States, Australia and Europe as its main markets.

    Its customers are professional contractors, builders and remodelers who buy mostly through specialty building products distributors, lumberyards and home improvement retailers. Its Siding & Trim segment makes fiber cement and PVC siding and trim in the United States for new homes and for repair and remodel work. Its products compete mainly with wood, vinyl, stucco, masonry and other building materials. Its strategy is to replace those materials with its own.

    On July 1, 2025 it bought The AZEK Company, which makes low-maintenance outdoor living products at plants in the United States. The 10-K says its business depends on residential and commercial construction, and its fiscal year ends March 31.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak residential and commercial construction, which drives demand for its products.
    • Competition from other building products makers and from products it hopes to replace.
    • Changes in the availability, quality and cost of raw materials and energy.
    • Problems running its plants, or failing to keep product quality and performance.
    • Trouble combining AZEK and other acquisitions with its business.
