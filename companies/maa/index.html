    Net margin is net income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Debt is Mid-America Apartment's notes payable, which cover all its secured and unsecured borrowings. Mid-America Apartment's cash flow statement splits its spending on its properties across several lines (capital improvements and development) with no total, so capital spending and free cash flow are left blank. Mid-America Apartment's filings for 2016 and 2017 tag most figures only for its operating partnership, so those years are mostly blank.

Net debt to EBITDA is debt minus cash, divided by operating income before depreciation and amortization: roughly how many years of those earnings the net debt equals.
