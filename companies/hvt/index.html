    What Havertys does

    Havertys is a specialty retailer of home furniture and accessories. J.J. Haverty began the business in 1885 in Atlanta, Georgia. It now has 129 stores in 17 states in the Southern and Midwest regions of the U.S. All of its stores use the Havertys name and none are franchised.

    It sells mainly to women in middle to upper-middle income households who own homes in the suburbs. Its stores serve over 90 cities and range from 15,000 to 60,000 selling square feet. It sells through a commissioned sales team and also offers a free design service, including in-home consultations. About 33.5% of its written sales in 2025 came from consultations with in-home designers, and the average sales ticket for customers who use the design service is generally twice its average.

    Customers research and buy both online and in stores. The company imports a substantial part of its merchandise from foreign sources and depends on third-party carriers to ship products from its vendors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A weak economy and lower consumer spending on discretionary items like furniture.
    • Competition from national, regional and local home furnishing retailers.
    • Political and economic risks of importing a substantial part of its merchandise.
    • Relying on third-party carriers, and on a small number of distribution centers that could be damaged.
    • Not matching changing consumer tastes and shopping preferences, or losing brand strength.
    From Havertys's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
