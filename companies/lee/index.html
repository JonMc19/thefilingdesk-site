    What Lee Enterprises does

    Lee Enterprises publishes local newspapers and news websites and sells advertising and marketing services to local businesses. It serves 72 mid-sized communities in 25 states, with flagship papers such as the St. Louis Post-Dispatch, the Omaha World-Herald and the Buffalo News. Readers can subscribe to digital or print editions.

    Its main revenue comes from subscriptions, advertising and digital marketing services. Advertising includes digital, print, video and social media campaigns. It also earns money from commercial printing, distribution, and a software service for managing website content called BLOX Digital. It competes with other media and digital companies for advertisers and with other news sources for subscribers.

    At the end of its 2025 fiscal year it had about 2,500 employees, about 250 of them union members. Newsprint, the paper it prints on, is its main raw material, and its price can swing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Print readers and advertisers moving to digital media, which may require large investments.
    • Falling advertising revenue, especially if retail weakens.
    • Competition from many local media and digital businesses.
    • Its debt, and the need for its lender's consent for certain actions.
    • Risks from generative AI, including intellectual property, legal and reputation problems.
    From Lee Enterprises's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 26, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
