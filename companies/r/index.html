    What Ryder does

    Ryder provides outsourced logistics and transportation services in North America. Companies hire it so they do not have to run their own trucks, warehouses and delivery networks. Its services cover moving goods from ports to the customer's door, including cross-border shipping, fleet and transportation management, warehousing, manufacturing support and final delivery.

    It reports three segments. Fleet Management Solutions leases and rents trucks, tractors and trailers to businesses, maintains them, supplies fuel and sells used vehicles. Supply Chain Solutions provides logistics services such as warehousing and distribution. Dedicated Transportation Solutions supplies vehicles and drivers for a customer's exclusive use, along with management and administrative support. Its revenue comes from leases, rentals and maintenance contracts and from logistics and transportation fees. It says it competes with companies that offer similar services, and with customers that choose to do the work themselves.

    Ryder says that companies doing their own logistics face labor shortages, government regulation and rising vehicle costs, and that these trends should lead more companies that own their fleets to outsource them.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker customer demand in a poor economy, or competition, could reduce its business.
    • It may not be able to resell its used vehicles for at least the value it has assumed.
    • Its profits depend on operating and pricing assumptions that may prove wrong.
    • It depends on a limited number of customers for much of its supply chain and dedicated transportation revenue.
    • Labor shortages or disputes could raise costs or disrupt operations.
    From Ryder's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
