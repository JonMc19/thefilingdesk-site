    What Werner Enterprises does

    Werner Enterprises is a trucking and logistics company based in Omaha, Nebraska. It mainly hauls truckload shipments of general goods across the United States, with authority to operate in parts of Canada and Mexico. It says it believes it is one of the largest truckload carriers in the country by revenue. At the end of 2025 its trucking segment had a fleet of 7,100 trucks, most of them company-operated.

    It has two reportable segments. Truckload Transportation Services runs dedicated fleets for a single customer, often serving a retail distribution center or factory, plus one-way fleets that include van, expedited, regional and temperature-controlled service. Werner Logistics does not own its trucks for most of its work. It arranges brokered shipments, intermodal rail moves and residential and commercial deliveries of large or heavy items. In 2025, trucking made up 69% of operating revenue, Werner Logistics 29% and other operations 2%.

    The freight it hauls is mainly retail merchandise, consumer products, food and beverages and manufactured goods. Its largest customer, Dollar General, accounted for 11% of total revenues in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic and geopolitical conditions that reduce freight demand.
    • Difficulty recruiting and keeping drivers and independent contractors.
    • Higher fuel prices or fuel shortages.
    • Relying on key customers, whose loss or financial failure would hurt results.
    • Relying on outside capacity providers for its logistics business.
    From Werner Enterprises's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
