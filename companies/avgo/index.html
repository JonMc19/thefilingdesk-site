    What Broadcom does

    Broadcom designs and sells computer chips and software that large businesses and governments use to run their computer systems. Its chips go into AI data centers, networking gear, servers and storage, smartphones, broadband modems, set-top boxes and factory equipment. Its software, including VMware products, helps companies run their own private clouds (computing systems they control rather than rent), manage mainframe computers and protect against cyberattacks.

    Broadcom reports two segments. Semiconductor solutions made 58% of net revenue in fiscal 2025, and infrastructure software made the rest. Its AI products include custom chips built to each customer's design and switches that move data between servers. A few customers bring in much of its money: one customer accounted for 32% of net revenue in fiscal 2025, and Broadcom believes its top five end customers made up about 40%. In chips it competes with other chipmakers and with large equipment makers that design their own; in software it competes with big enterprise software vendors and smaller specialists.

    Broadcom had about 33,000 employees at the end of fiscal 2025, about 57% of them in research and development. It has grown through acquisitions, including CA, Symantec's enterprise security business and VMware. Its main warehouse is in Malaysia.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing, or getting much less business from, the few large customers that account for much of its revenue.
    • Its reliance on outside manufacturers and a small number of suppliers for key parts of its chips.
    • Big spending on research and new business models may pay off slowly or not at all.
    • Its software growth depends on customers accepting its VMware products and business strategy.
    • Its large debt could limit its options and strain its finances.
    From Broadcom's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
