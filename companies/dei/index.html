    What Douglas Emmett does

    Douglas Emmett is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns and runs office buildings and apartment communities. Its properties are all in two places: coastal areas of Los Angeles County, California, and Honolulu, Hawaii. Its Los Angeles submarkets include Beverly Hills, Brentwood, Century City, Santa Monica, Westwood and Warner Center.

    At the end of 2025 its portfolio consisted of an office portfolio of 18.0 million square feet, 5,445 apartment units and rights to rent from two parcels of land leased out under ground leases. In all, it owned 70 office properties and 15 multifamily properties, some wholly owned and some through consolidated joint ventures. It earns its revenue from rent paid by tenants.

    The company says it picks submarkets with limited new supply, high barriers to entry and strong local economies, and that its office tenants tend to be small and affluent. It plans to raise its market share in its current submarkets and may enter similar ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Having every property in Los Angeles County and Honolulu, so local problems hit the whole business.
    • A large amount of debt that exposes it to interest rates and refinancing risk.
    • New leases that may bring in less rent than asking rents.
    • Tenants clustered in a few industries that could slow down together.
    • Intense competition from other properties for tenants.
