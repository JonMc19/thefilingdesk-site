    What Braze does

    Braze sells a customer engagement platform, which is software that helps companies send personalized messages to their customers across channels such as email, text, push notifications and in-app messages. Brands feed it data about how people use their apps and websites, and the platform uses that data, along with AI models and agents, to decide what to send and when.

    Braze was founded in 2011. It reports that as of January 2026 it had 2,609 customers around the world. Its fiscal year ends in January. Customers are brands that want direct, lasting relationships with the people who use their products.

    Braze earns its revenue by selling access to its software to these brand customers. It says it faces intense competition, including from well-established companies, and that it may have to cut prices or change its pricing model to stay competitive. It has a history of operating losses.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of operating losses, with no assurance it will reach or keep profitability.
    • Intense competition, including from well-established companies, that could hurt its ability to win customers.
    • Failing to attract new customers and renew existing ones.
    • Pressure to cut prices or change its pricing model.
    • Rapid revenue growth that may not continue, making its prospects hard to judge.
    From Braze's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
