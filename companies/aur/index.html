    What Aurora Innovation does

    Aurora Innovation develops self-driving technology. Its product, the Aurora Driver, is the hardware, software and data services that let a vehicle drive itself. The company was founded in 2017 and designs the Aurora Driver to work across several kinds of vehicles, from passenger cars to light commercial vehicles to Class 8 trucks, which are the heavy trucks used for long hauls.

    Trucking is its first driverless product. In April 2025 it launched driverless commercial operations with Hirschbach and Uber Freight and has since added more driverless customers. Through 2025 it also ran trucking pilots with FedEx, Schneider, Volvo Autonomous Solutions and Werner, in which the trucks hauled loads on their own under the watch of vehicle operators. Its partners include PACCAR and Volvo. In 2025 it also began a truck program to give customers more driverless capacity, using trucks it upfits for driverless operations.

    It sells Aurora Driver for Freight as a subscription service today. The 10-K says it expects in time to sell the Aurora Driver as a service, supplying the technology and earning a fee per mile or a similar charge. It also names ride hailing and local goods delivery as other markets it aims at.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Self-driving is an emerging technology, and it faces significant technical challenges in commercializing it.
    • It has had net losses since it began and may not become profitable.
    • Its business plans need a lot of capital, and it may not be able to raise more when needed.
    • It faces competition from companies with far more resources.
    • The market may not accept or adopt its services as fast as it expects, or at all.
    From Aurora Innovation's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
