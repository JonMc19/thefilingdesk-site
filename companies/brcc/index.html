    What Black Rifle Coffee does

    Black Rifle Coffee sells roasted coffee, single-serve coffee, ready-to-drink coffee and an energy drink, along with branded apparel, brewing equipment and outdoor gear. It was founded in 2014 by a U.S. Army veteran and says it aims to serve active-duty military, veterans, first responders and others who share their values. It is a public benefit corporation, which means it also commits to a stated public purpose.

    It reports one segment with three channels. In Wholesale it sells through grocery, club, convenience and specialty retailers such as Walmart and 7-Eleven; this channel had $258.0 million in sales in 2025. In its direct-to-consumer channel, sales on its website include a Coffee Club subscription, and had $117.6 million in sales in 2025. The third channel, Outposts, is its own and franchised coffee shops.

    It runs offices in Utah, Texas and Tennessee and a manufacturing facility in Manchester, Tennessee. As of December 31, 2025, it employed 468 employees in the United States. It relies on co-manufacturers and outside suppliers for its products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Harming its brand or its mission-driven image with customers.
    • Losing one or more of its main wholesale retail partners, a few of which account for much of its receivables.
    • Relying on co-manufacturers and suppliers, and swings in the cost of coffee beans and other commodities.
    • Competition from other coffee, ready-to-drink and energy drink makers.
    • Not succeeding in new products, new channels or the opening and running of Outposts.
    From Black Rifle Coffee's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
