    What Inseego does

    Inseego designs wireless networking equipment and cloud software. Its main products are 5G devices that give businesses a high-speed internet connection over mobile networks: mobile hotspots and fixed wireless access gear that connects a site to the internet over a cellular signal instead of a cable. They are sold to mobile carriers, enterprises and small and medium businesses. The devices work with cloud software for monitoring and managing the network and for automatic failover from a wired connection.

    It also sells a software-as-a-service product that carriers use to manage the subscriptions of their government and large enterprise customers. The company says its products are designed in the United States, and that it is the successor to Novatel Wireless.

    Inseego says it depends on two customers for a substantial portion of its revenue. It has outside companies make and warehouse many of its products, and it relies on sole-source suppliers for some components. In January 2026 it bought back all of its Series E preferred stock from an affiliate of Mubadala Capital for about $26 million in cash, common stock and additional notes, which was about a 38% discount to its liquidation value.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It depends on two customers for a substantial portion of its revenue.
    • The market for fixed wireless access may develop more slowly than it expects.
    • Its business suffers if wireless carriers do not deliver acceptable service.
    • It faces rapid change and strong competition in its markets.
    • It relies on outside manufacturers and on sole-source suppliers for some parts.
