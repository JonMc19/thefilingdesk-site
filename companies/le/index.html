    What Lands' End does

    Lands' End sells clothing, swimwear, outerwear, accessories, footwear, home products and uniforms. It sells mainly online at landsend.com and through third-party marketplaces, and also through its own stores and licensing deals. It began in 1963 as a catalog seller of sailboat hardware.

    It reports revenue in six channels. U.S. eCommerce sells through its own website. Europe eCommerce sells to consumers in Europe. Outfitters sells uniforms and logo apparel to businesses, and to student households through schools. Third Party sells through marketplace websites. Licensing earns royalties on the use of its trademark. Retail sells through its own U.S. stores. In fiscal 2025 its net revenue was approximately $1.34 billion, with operations in the United States, United Kingdom and Germany.

    Its sales are seasonal: it generated approximately 34.0% of its yearly net revenue in the fourth quarter. It employs approximately 3,900 people. It says it competes with direct-to-consumer companies and retailers, including department stores, specialty chains and outdoor stores.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak consumer spending in poor economic conditions.
    • Seasonal sales, with the fourth quarter carrying a large share of the year.
    • Rising costs and limited availability of catalog paper, printing, distribution and postage.
    • Markdowns and promotions that could reduce its gross margins.
    • Its debt and its debt terms, which limit how it can run the business.
    From Lands' End's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
