    What Duos Technologies Group does

    Duos Technologies Group, based in Jacksonville, Florida, is shifting from a maker of rail inspection and analytics technology to a provider of digital infrastructure. Its main focus is now modular edge data centers: small computing sites placed close to where people use them, sold to customers in education, healthcare, the public sector, enterprise and service providers. It says these deployments can bring both an initial deployment payment and recurring revenue tied to use.

    It works through three wholly owned subsidiaries. Duos Edge AI builds the edge data centers and includes a Technology Solutions unit that sources and integrates equipment for data center and AI projects. Duos Technologies keeps its older machine vision and AI analytics products, including for rail. Duos Energy provides power consulting and supply services for data-intensive operations.

    The company employs about 84 people. Its edge data centers are concentrated in Texas, on land it leases, and it has begun deployments in other states. It relies on local utilities for power and on carriers for network connections.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its shift to data centers puts capital and attention into a market where it has a shorter track record.
    • Its edge data centers are concentrated in Texas, so a local downturn could hurt it.
    • It leases the land its data centers sit on and may not renew leases or find new sites.
    • It depends on local utilities for power and on carriers for network connections.
    • Long bid cycles with fixed pricing leave it exposed to inflation and supply disruption.
