    What iQIYI does

    iQIYI runs an online video service in China. Users watch dramas, films, variety shows, children's content and animations on its platform, along with short stories told as micro dramas. As of December 31, 2025, its library held over 40,000 professionally produced long-form titles and approximately 20,000 micro dramas.

    It makes many of its own shows. It runs in-house studios that produce original drama series and variety shows, and it also buys content from other providers. It earns money from members who pay for the service and from advertisers who place ads on the platform. It also uses artificial intelligence in its work, for example a tool to look for promising scripts and an assistant for users.

    iQIYI says that in 2025 its original drama series were the main source of premium content and made up over 90% of the newly released drama series with popularity index scores of over 9,000.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of net losses, and the chance of losing money again.
    • Failing to predict what users like and to provide high-quality content, especially popular originals.
    • Failing to buy content from other providers on acceptable terms.
    • Failing to keep members and win new ones, or to keep and grow its advertising customers.
    • Needing a large amount of cash for content and technology, which it may not be able to raise.
    From iQIYI's annual report on Form 20-F, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
