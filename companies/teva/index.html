    What Teva Pharmaceuticals does

    Teva makes and sells medicines in two groups: generics, which are lower-cost copies of drugs whose patents have expired, along with biosimilars and over-the-counter products, and its own branded medicines. It is based in Israel, has about 34,000 employees across 57 markets, and has a large presence in the United States and Europe. Its generics are sold mainly to retail drug chains, mail-order distributors and wholesalers.

    It reports three segments, each selling its full range in its region: United States, Europe, and International Markets, which covers more than 35 other countries, including Canada, Israel, Russia and parts of Latin America. Its branded medicines focus on the central nervous system, meaning the brain and nerves. They include Austedo for movement disorders, Ajovy for migraine prevention, Uzedy for schizophrenia and Copaxone for multiple sclerosis. It also has branded respiratory and cancer drugs and biosimilars. Its Anda unit distributes medicines to pharmacies, hospitals and doctors' offices in the U.S. Other activities include selling the active ingredients used in drugs and making drugs for others.

    Teva calls its current plan "Pivot to Growth." It says the plan aims to grow its branded medicines, use capital where it expects the highest value, and cut costs. On March 31, 2025, it sold its Teva-Takeda business venture in Japan.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Generic medicines are a large part of its business, with pricing pressure, strong competition and a concentrated customer base.
    • Delays in launching new generic products and difficulty taking advantage of biosimilar opportunities.
    • Generic or biosimilar rivals that could cut sales of its own branded medicines.
    • Its need to develop and sell additional products and to protect its patents and other intellectual property.
    • Significant debt with large interest and principal payments and restrictive covenants.
    From Teva Pharmaceuticals's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
