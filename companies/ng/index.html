    What NovaGold Resources does

    NovaGold is a gold mining company that does not produce gold. It is focused on advancing the Donlin Gold project in Alaska, a development-stage gold project. The company says it does not currently generate operating earnings.

    NovaGold holds a 60% interest in Donlin Gold LLC, which owns the project. The other 40% is held by a subsidiary of Paulson Advantage Plus Master and Paulson Partners. Before June 2025 the project was owned equally with Barrick. NovaGold says its voting interest is contractually reduced, even though it holds 60% of the membership interests.

    Because it has no sales, the company has paid for its work mainly by selling shares and warrants to the public, through earlier convertible notes, and by selling assets. It says it expects to keep raising money this way. It had 12 full-time employees on November 30, 2025, and also uses outside consultants.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Never having produced gold commercially, so mining may never be established or profitable.
    • Reliance on its co-owner of Donlin Gold, with its own voting interest reduced by contract.
    • Net losses and a need for more financing to continue its work and fund construction.
    • Delays or failures in getting the permits it needs, and opposition from local groups.
    • Actual costs and results that may differ greatly from its estimates.
    From NovaGold Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed January 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
