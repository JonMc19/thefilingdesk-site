    What i3 Verticals does

    i3 Verticals sells cloud-based software to state and local governments and related agencies. Its software covers courts and public safety, public administration, utilities, transportation and schools. It says it has thousands of software installations across all 50 states and Canada.

    The company has reshaped itself through sales. In September 2024 it sold its merchant services business, which handled card payments for businesses, to Payroc for a cash price of approximately $439.5 million. In May 2025 it sold its healthcare revenue cycle management business. It still earns some revenue from volume and transaction-based fees for payment processing, but most of its revenue is from software and related services. Approximately 76% of its revenue from continuing operations is recurring.

    It sells through teams focused on specific markets, with about 30 employees in sales and 30 more in sales support. It names Tyler Technologies, Constellation Software and Roper Technologies among its competitors, and it has grown by buying other companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of operating losses, with a need for much more revenue to turn a profit.
    • Cybersecurity incidents or failures of its own or third-party software that could disrupt its services.
    • Falling behind rapid technology change and competition from larger software companies.
    • Depending on payment networks, a sponsor bank and a processing agreement with Payroc for its payments business.
    • Chargeback liabilities and rising interchange and sponsorship fees it may not be able to pass on.
    From i3 Verticals's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 21, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
