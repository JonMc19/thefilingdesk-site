    What Federated Hermes does

    Federated Hermes is an investment manager that has been in business since 1955. It runs mutual funds and other investment products, as well as separately managed accounts for institutions and others, in the U.S. and abroad. At the end of 2025 it managed $902.6 billion in assets. It also offers stewardship services, which are engagement with companies on behalf of investors, and develops real estate and renewable energy projects.

    It reports one business segment, investment management. Its main source of revenue is advisory fees, which are based mostly on the amount of assets it manages. It advises 176 funds, which it markets to banks, broker-dealers, other intermediaries and institutions, who use them for their own customers, including individual investors, corporations and retirement plans. Its funds are based in the U.S. and in countries such as Ireland, the U.K. and Luxembourg.

    Because fees follow assets, its revenue moves with the value of the assets it manages and with the money that flows in and out of its funds. It is regulated as an investment adviser in the U.S. and abroad.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling assets under management, which reduce its fees, and difficulty keeping its growth and size.
    • Interest rate changes and investment performance that affect fund assets and the fees it keeps.
    • Fee waivers it may have to give on money market funds when yields are very low.
    • Regulatory changes in the U.S., U.K. and EU that affect its funds and the markets they invest in.
    • A financial transaction tax that could hurt its offerings if it were broadly enacted.
    From Federated Hermes's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
