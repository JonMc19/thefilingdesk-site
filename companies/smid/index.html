    What Smith Midland does

    Smith-Midland makes precast concrete products. These are cast in its factories and then delivered to a site for installation, rather than poured on site. Its customers are mainly general contractors and federal, state and local transportation agencies in the Mid-Atlantic, Northeastern, Midwestern and Southeastern United States.

    It makes money in three ways: it manufactures and sells precast products, it rents out highway barriers, and it licenses its patented products to other precast makers in the United States and in other countries. Its proprietary products include SlenderWall, a lightweight concrete and steel exterior wall panel; J-J Hooks, a highway safety barrier; SoftSound, a finish that absorbs traffic noise; and Easi-Set and Easi-Span transportable concrete buildings. It also makes farm items such as cattleguards and feed troughs, and utility vaults, retaining walls and sound barriers.

    It runs its business through six wholly owned subsidiaries and is based in Midland, Virginia. On March 3, 2026 it had 285 employees, counting full-time, part-time and contract workers. Demand follows the construction cycle and public building budgets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand follows the construction cycle, weather, interest rates and public building budgets.
    • Profits are not guaranteed, and special barrier projects that boost results can be uneven.
    • Spending on more manufacturing capacity and barrier rentals may not lead to greater revenue.
    • Tariffs and government cost cutting may affect the business, which relies on public projects.
