    What Innovative Industrial Properties does

    IIP is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns specialized industrial and commercial buildings in the United States. Most of its properties are leased to state-licensed operators for their regulated cannabis facilities. It also has financial investments in the life science industry and plans to buy properties in that sector.

    It earns money mainly from rent. It buys properties through sale-leaseback deals, where it buys a building from the operator that uses it and leases it back, and through purchases from third parties. Leases are generally triple-net, meaning the tenant pays for repairs, maintenance, property taxes and insurance.

    At the end of 2025 it owned 111 properties covering 8.9 million rentable square feet in 19 states, and had invested $2.5 billion across them. It is managed internally and had 23 full-time employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Cannabis remains illegal under federal law, which could limit its and its tenants' ability to operate.
    • Tenants that default on rent, and the difficulty of re-leasing cannabis properties.
    • Tenants may be unable to keep the state and local licenses they need to operate.
    • Its tenants may owe higher taxes because of a federal tax rule that applies to cannabis businesses.
    • Risks in life science properties, a sector it is moving into.
    From Innovative Industrial Properties's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
