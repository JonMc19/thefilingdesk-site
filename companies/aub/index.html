    What Atlantic Union Bankshares does

    Atlantic Union Bankshares is a bank holding company based in Richmond, Virginia. Its subsidiary, Atlantic Union Bank, serves commercial and retail clients from branches and ATMs in Virginia, Maryland, Washington, D.C. and North Carolina. Non-bank affiliates provide equipment financing, investment services and capital markets services.

    It earns money the way banks do: it takes deposits and makes loans, including commercial real estate, construction, and commercial and industrial loans, and it also earns fees. It also earns revenue from mortgage lending. At December 31, 2025, it had $37.6 billion in assets.

    On April 1, 2025, it completed a merger with Sandy Spring Bancorp, gaining more than 50 branches in Virginia, Maryland and Washington, D.C., and it finished combining the branches and operations on October 14, 2025. It later sold a block of commercial real estate loans that it acquired in the merger.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Loan losses larger than the allowance it sets aside.
    • Heavy exposure to commercial real estate and construction loans.
    • Mortgage revenue that swings with interest rates and the housing market.
    • Difficulty keeping a stable base of low-cost deposits.
    • Lending to small and mid-sized local businesses, which can raise credit risk.
    From Atlantic Union Bankshares's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
