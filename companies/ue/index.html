    What Urban Edge Properties does

    Urban Edge Properties is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns, manages, buys, develops and redevelops retail properties, mainly along the Washington, D.C. to Boston corridor. Its portfolio is 69 shopping centers, two outlet centers and two malls with about 17.2 million square feet of leasable space, and the occupancy rate is 90.1%.

    It earns money by leasing space to retailers, including grocers, discounters, department stores, health clubs, do-it-yourself stores, restaurants and entertainment businesses. It tries to raise rents through built-in rent increases, lease renewals and new leases, and to add value by redeveloping existing sites.

    At the end of 2025 it had $165.5 million of active development, redevelopment and anchor repositioning projects. It also says it plans to sell properties that no longer meet its goals. It had 104 employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to renew leases or re-let space on similar terms, or losing rent from tenants that struggle.
    • Bankruptcy of tenants, and reliance on anchor tenants whose decisions or troubles could hurt its business.
    • Having many properties in the New York area, so that local economic cycles affect it strongly.
    • Competition and e-commerce that could hurt its retail tenants and its properties.
    • Failing to qualify as a REIT, which would mean paying income taxes at corporate rates.
    From Urban Edge Properties's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
