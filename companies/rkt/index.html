    What Rocket Companies does

    Rocket Companies is a US home finance company. Its main business, Rocket Mortgage, makes home loans and services them (collects payments and manages the loans) through an app and website. It also owns Redfin, a real estate brokerage and home search site, and offers other services such as personal loans.

    Rocket says it is the largest US mortgage originator by loan units and the largest servicer. It makes loans through two segments: Direct to Consumer, where clients deal with Rocket digitally or with its mortgage bankers, and Partner Network, which covers its wholesale, enterprise partner and correspondent channels. In the wholesale channel, independent mortgage brokers use Rocket's technology and loan products. In the correspondent channel, Rocket buys loans made by other lenders.

    On July 1, 2025 it bought Redfin, and on October 1, 2025 it bought Mr. Cooper, a mortgage servicer and originator. Its servicing portfolio had an unpaid principal balance of $2.1 trillion at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its ability to keep adapting its technology and products to stay relevant.
    • Cyberattacks, security breaches or technology failures that could harm its reputation.
    • Making servicing advances that may be slow to recover or not recoverable.
    • Counterparties that could end its servicing rights or subservicing contracts.
    • Its dependence on Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and Ginnie Mae, whose rules it must follow.
    From Rocket Companies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
