    What Methode Electronics does

    Methode Electronics designs and makes custom electronic and electro-mechanical parts. Its customers are car and truck makers, other vehicle and equipment makers, and the suppliers that sell to them. Its products include power distribution parts such as busbars and battery disconnect units, lighting, sensors, user interface components, and data networking and broadband equipment. They are used in cars, commercial vehicles, e-bikes, aerospace, rail, data centers and construction equipment.

    It reports three segments: Automotive, Industrial and Interface. Industrial makes lighting, industrial safety radio remote controls, cables and busbars. Interface makes copper-based data networking products and touch panels for appliances. In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 it sold its dataMate business, and its consumer appliance business is winding down as programs end. It had a Medical segment through fiscal 2024.

    Its supply arrangements typically use blanket purchase orders with production releases. In fiscal 2026 its five largest customers made up about 41% of net sales. It says its markets are highly competitive and that many competitors have greater resources. It had about 6,650 employees as of May 2, 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a few large customers for a big share of its sales.
    • Problems with the timing, quality and cost of launching new programs, its own or its customers'.
    • Customers give volume estimates but no firm commitments, and actual orders may fall short.
    • Tariffs and trade policy changes could hurt its global business.
    • Failing to anticipate market changes and what its customers will need next.
    From Methode Electronics's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
