    What Nutanix does

    Nutanix sells software that lets organizations run their applications, data and AI workloads in their own data centers, at the edge and in public clouds, from one platform. Its main product is the Nutanix Cloud Platform. The company first became known for hyperconverged infrastructure, which combines computing and storage in one software-managed system. It also offers a Kubernetes management product and security tools, and lets customers use outside storage with its software.

    Nutanix sells subscriptions to its software along with support and maintenance, which give customers software upgrades and technical support. Customers usually buy the hardware to run it from a channel partner or from a server maker that resells Nutanix software. The company relies mainly on indirect sales channels. Its top two distributors accounted for 39% of total revenue in fiscal 2026.

    Nutanix says Broadcom's changes to VMware's products and pricing may create an opening for it. It also says rivals are competing harder in the enterprise IT market.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from established companies and new entrants in the fast-changing enterprise IT market.
    • Not benefiting from the changes Broadcom has made to VMware's products, pricing and licensing.
    • Dependence on indirect sales channels, including two distributors that account for a large share of revenue.
    • Dependence on hardware makers and its server partners to deliver components on time and at a reasonable cost.
    • Customers not renewing or expanding their subscriptions, and risks from its use of AI.
    From Nutanix's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
