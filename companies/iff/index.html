    What International Flavors & Fragrances does

    IFF makes ingredients that go into food, drinks, household and personal care products, supplements and perfumes. It sells mainly to manufacturers of dairy, meat, beverages, snacks, baked goods, cleaning products, soaps, perfumes and similar goods. Its products include flavors, textures, scents, enzymes, cultures, probiotics and soy proteins. Sales in 2025 were $10.890 billion.

    In 2025 it had four continuing segments. Taste makes flavor compounds and natural taste solutions. Food Ingredients makes specialty ingredients such as plant-based proteins, emulsifiers, sweeteners and natural food preservatives. Health & Biosciences makes enzymes, food cultures and probiotics for foods, detergents, animal feed and ethanol production. Scent makes fragrances. A fifth segment, Pharma Solutions, was sold in May 2025 along with its Nitrocellulose business.

    The business is spread across many countries: sales in the U.S. were about 28% of the 2025 total, and no other country was more than 10%. No customer accounted for 10% or more of sales. IFF had about 21,500 employees at the end of 2025, including about 3,000 in research and development.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Shifts in consumer demand and preferences, and regulatory trends, that may reduce demand for its products.
    • Failing to carry out its strategic transformation, or to close deals and partnerships it plans.
    • Legal claims and investigations, including an ongoing antitrust matter.
    • Trade wars, tariffs, sanctions and supply chain disruptions.
    • Currency swings and other risks of operating in many countries.
