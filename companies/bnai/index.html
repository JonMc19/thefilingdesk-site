    What Brand Engagement Network does

    Brand Engagement Network sells conversational AI software to businesses. Its AI agents talk with people and connect what they ask for to a customer's data, systems and workflows. The technology runs on its own Engagement Language Model, which is meant to work in closed environments using data the customer approves, with built-in governance and compliance controls.

    The company configures its agents for each customer, including how they look, sound and interact, and links them to the customer's systems. They can run in the cloud, on a customer's own servers or in a mix, and inside apps, kiosks and software kits. Its first customers were in healthcare, for patient engagement, education and operational support. It has since moved into hospitality, insurance, and advertising and media, with partners in the United States, Mexico and South Africa.

    The company describes itself as pre-revenue, with minimal revenue from 2022 through 2024. It earns money from subscriptions and from setting up and customizing its agents. It acquired its base conversational AI technology in 2023 when it bought DM Lab Co., Ltd., and made its platform generally available in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a short operating history and a history of losses, and may not become profitable.
    • It expects to depend on a limited number of customers and end markets.
    • It may need more capital, and that may not be available on good terms.
    • Its sales cycles may be long, and customers may not renew or expand their subscriptions.
    • Its revenue growth depends partly on channel partners and other third parties.
