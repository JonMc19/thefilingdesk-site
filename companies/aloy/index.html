    What Realloys does

    The company in this report, then called Blackboxstocks, runs a web and mobile platform for people who trade stocks and options. It scans US stock and options markets all the time and sends members alerts about unusual activity, plus news, charts and data. A chat-style community inside the platform lets members talk to each other. It says its goal is to give everyday traders tools once available mainly to large institutions.

    It earns money from subscriptions. Members pay monthly or annual fees, and the plan they choose decides which tools they get. It also sells courses on trading, free classes are offered to members, and it says it may sell data access and custom tools to professional traders in the future. Members come from many countries.

    The report says the company expects more competition. It also describes a pending merger with REalloys Inc., in which REalloys would become a wholly owned subsidiary and its holders would receive shares of the company. The company's stock would be heavily diluted, and the report says it is not certain the deal will close.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not attracting enough new subscribers, or having existing ones cancel or renew on worse terms.
    • It must raise revenue to reach profitability, and its quarterly revenue can swing.
    • Growing competition for its trading platform and services.
    • Reliance on outside software and data providers whose terms could change.
    • The pending REalloys merger may not close, and would dilute current shareholders if it does.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
