    What Information Services Group does

    Information Services Group, known as ISG, is a technology research and advisory firm based in Stamford, Connecticut. It advises companies and public-sector bodies on sourcing technology and business services, cloud and data analytics, managing outside providers and risk, network and software choices, technology strategy, and change management. It was founded in 2006 and says it serves more than 900 clients.

    Its work is supported by ISG Research, which publishes market analyses and provider evaluations, and by its own digital platforms for managing suppliers, benchmarking operations and speeding up sourcing decisions. It says it tracks over 180,000 technology service contracts. Some of its engagements are fixed-fee or capped-fee, and some pay only on contingent results. Clients are mainly in manufacturing, banking and financial services, insurance and health sciences, plus government.

    ISG operates in over 20 countries. On December 31, 2025, it employed 1,290 people worldwide, of whom 1,254 were full-time.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to meet the covenants in its credit agreement, and interest costs on variable-rate debt.
    • Acquisitions that are hard to integrate, and the risks of selling businesses.
    • Clients can end, delay or shrink its engagements at any time.
    • Losing money on fixed-fee or capped-fee contracts, and swings from contingent-fee work.
    • Its AI efforts may not succeed and carry legal and operational risks.
    From Information Services Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
