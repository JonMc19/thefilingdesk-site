    What Wolverine World Wide does

    Wolverine World Wide designs, markets and licenses footwear and apparel. Its products include casual and athletic shoes, hiking and trail shoes, kids' shoes, work boots and uniform footwear. Its brands include Merrell, Saucony, Sweaty Betty, Chaco, Wolverine, Cat, Bates, Harley-Davidson and Hush Puppies. Cat and Harley-Davidson are used under licence from their owners. Third-party factories make its products.

    It reports two segments. The Active Group includes Merrell, Saucony, Sweaty Betty and Chaco. The Work Group includes Wolverine, Cat, Bates, Harley-Davidson and HYTEST. A third group, labeled Other, includes brands such as Sperry, Keds and Hush Puppies, and the Stride Rite licensed business. Revenue comes from selling to retailers and distributors, from its own online and store sales, and from licensees and joint ventures.

    Its products are sold in about 170 countries and territories. The company runs its own operations in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and parts of Europe and Asia Pacific. In other regions it uses distributors, licensees and joint ventures. At the start of 2026 it had about 3,050 employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep its brands appealing to consumers or to anticipate changing tastes.
    • Reliance on foreign factories, with production, quality and price problems that could disrupt supply.
    • A drop in purchases by wholesale customers, or customers demanding better terms.
    • Its direct-to-consumer business needs heavy investment and carries its own risks.
    • Problems with logistics and distribution, or with its online and information systems.
    From Wolverine World Wide's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
