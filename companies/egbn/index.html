    What Eagle Bancorp does

    Eagle Bancorp is the bank holding company for EagleBank, a Maryland-chartered commercial bank. The company is based in Bethesda, Maryland, and was formed in 1997 by local business people and professionals; the bank opened in 1998.

    The bank serves the Washington, D.C. area through twelve branches: six in suburban Maryland, three in the District of Columbia and three in Northern Virginia. It also has four lending centers and offers remote deposit and mobile banking. It makes money mainly from interest on loans funded by customer deposits, and a significant part of its loan book is secured by commercial properties.

    The bank owns a few small subsidiaries. One holds real estate acquired through foreclosure, one receives fees from an insurance referral program, and Landroval Municipal Finance focuses on lending to municipalities.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large part of its loans are secured by commercial properties, so weak property markets could hurt it.
    • Its reserve for credit losses rests on estimates and may not cover actual losses.
    • It faces competition for deposits and could see customers withdraw large deposits.
    • Deposits above the insured limit may be more likely to leave the bank.
    • Failing to meet capital and regulatory requirements would hurt its business.
    From Eagle Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
