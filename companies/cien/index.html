    What Ciena does

    Ciena is a network technology company. It sells the hardware, software and services that network operators use to carry data, voice and video traffic. Its main products are Networking Platforms, which include optical networking equipment and routing and switching equipment. These let operators add capacity, speed up transmission and direct traffic efficiently.

    It also sells Platform Software, which controls and runs networks across several layers, and Blue Planet automation software, which helps operators manage their services with less manual work. On top of that it offers services to help customers build, run and change their networks. It sells directly and through resellers and service partners.

    Its customers are cloud providers (companies running large data centers, such as for AI and web hosting), communications service providers (telephone, wireless and submarine cable network operators), and others such as cable operators, governments, research networks and enterprises. Ciena says growing network traffic, especially from artificial intelligence, is driving demand for more bandwidth. Its fiscal year ends in early November.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a small number of customers whose spending cuts could hurt its results.
    • Results that can swing widely from period to period.
    • Intense competition and technology bets that may not pay off.
    • Problems in developing and making its products, or in their performance and security.
    • Relying on third-party suppliers with limited sources of supply.
    From Ciena's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 12, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
