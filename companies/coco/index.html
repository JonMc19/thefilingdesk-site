    What Vita Coco does

    Vita Coco sells packaged coconut water under the Vita Coco brand, which it started in 2004. It also sells coconut oil, juice and milk, and a protein-infused fitness drink called PWR LIFT. Besides its own brands, it supplies private label (store-brand) coconut water and coconut oil to retailers. It stopped selling its Runa energy drink in December 2023 and stopped making its Ever & Ever water in 2024.

    The company buys coconut water from about 16 factories in six countries, backed by thousands of coconut farmers. It does not own any of the factories, which it says lets it react faster to changes in the market. It also uses co-packers, outside firms that package its products, in three countries. Its main markets are North America, the United Kingdom and Germany, and its products are sold in more than 35 countries.

    Vita Coco sells through clubs, food, drug, mass and convenience stores, online, and places such as offices, gyms, airports and schools. It competes with other beverage makers, including sports drinks, energy drinks, enhanced waters and other functional beverages. It is a public benefit corporation, a company that also commits to a stated public purpose.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Coconut water makes up a large share of its revenue, so lower demand for it would hurt.
    • Supply chain problems, or limited availability of coconuts, that raise costs or delay deliveries.
    • Its results are closely tied to its Americas operating segment.
    • Dependence on distributors and retail customers for most of its sales.
    • Strong competition in food and beverage retail, and the difficulty of predicting what consumers will want.
    From Vita Coco's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
