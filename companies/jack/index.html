    What Jack in the Box does

    Jack in the Box Inc., based in San Diego, operates and franchises two quick-service restaurant chains. Jack in the Box serves burgers such as the Jumbo Jack, breakfast sandwiches, tacos, curly fries, egg rolls and shakes, and lets customers order any item at any hour. Del Taco serves Mexican and American dishes such as burritos and fries.

    At the end of fiscal 2025 (September 28, 2025), Jack in the Box had 2,136 restaurants across 22 states, mainly in the western and southern U.S. Of those, 93% were franchised, which means independent owners run them and pay the company fees. Del Taco had 576 restaurants across 18 states.

    In April 2025 the company began exploring strategic alternatives for Del Taco. On October 15, 2025, it agreed to sell Del Taco Holdings Inc., which owns and operates the Del Taco restaurant business, to Yadav Enterprises, Inc. for an aggregate purchase price of $115 million in cash, subject to adjustments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher labor costs and changes in how many workers it can find.
    • Rising food and commodity costs, and failed deliveries from a limited number of suppliers.
    • A highly franchised business model, where franchisees' results affect the company.
    • Strong competition in the food service industry, with fewer marketing resources than some rivals.
    • The pending sale of Del Taco may not close on the expected terms or timeline.
    From Jack in the Box's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 19, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
