    What Visteon does

    Visteon is an automotive technology company based in Van Buren Township, Michigan. It designs and makes the electronics inside a vehicle's cockpit: digital instrument clusters, information displays, infotainment systems and cockpit domain controllers, which are computers that combine many separate vehicle functions. It also makes battery management systems and high voltage power electronics for electric vehicles, and sells engineering services.

    Its customers are carmakers, among them BMW, Ford, General Motors, Honda, Mercedes-Benz, Nissan, Stellantis, Toyota and Volkswagen. Visteon wins business by being awarded a customer's vehicle program, so its sales depend on how many vehicles those customers build. It says the auto industry is cyclical and sensitive to the economy.

    It makes and engineers its products in Brazil, Bulgaria, China, India, Japan, Mexico, Portugal, Slovakia, Thailand and Tunisia, and also works through joint ventures. It says car makers want more electronics and connectivity in vehicles and a wide range of engine types, from electric to gasoline.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Heavy dependence on Ford and General Motors, where lower vehicle output would hurt it.
    • Falls in production at its major customers in a cyclical auto industry.
    • Not realizing the sales from business it has been awarded, or losing a product program.
    • Price pressure from customers.
    • Supplier shortages, supplier distress and work stoppages that disrupt its operations.
    From Visteon's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
