    What Turtle Beach does

    Turtle Beach designs and sells gaming accessories, mainly headsets, but also controllers, keyboards, mice, microphones and flight and racing simulation hardware. Its products work with Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo consoles, personal computers, and mobile devices and tablets. It is based in San Diego, California.

    It sells at price levels from entry-level to ultra-premium, aimed at players from casual gamers to professional streamers and esports competitors. It says it has had the largest share of console gaming headset sales for 16 years running, citing Circana retail sales data. It makes money by selling these products, mostly through retailers, and its products are made by third-party manufacturers, many of them in China.

    It has grown by acquisition: ROCCAT in 2019, Neat Microphones in 2021 and Performance Designed Products in 2024. Sales depend on major game launches and on console cycles, and are seasonal.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Difficulty forecasting demand, especially as new game consoles replace older ones.
    • Reliance on third parties to make and ship its products, many of them in China, and the effect of tariffs.
    • Depending on the success of third-party gaming platforms and on releases of popular games.
    • Relying on a few large customers and on license agreements for some of its products.
    • Borrowing under a credit facility with liens on nearly all its working capital assets and covenants.
    From Turtle Beach's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
