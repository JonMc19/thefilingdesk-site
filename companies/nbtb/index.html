    What NBT Bancorp does

    NBT Bancorp is a bank holding company. Its main subsidiary, NBT Bank, was formed in 1856 and offers checking, savings and other deposit accounts, loans, and wealth management services to individuals, businesses and municipalities. It runs as a community bank with local decision-making. Its branches are in upstate New York, northeastern Pennsylvania, southern New Hampshire, western Massachusetts, Vermont, southern Maine and central and northwestern Connecticut.

    The company earns most of its money from net interest income, the difference between the interest it earns on loans and securities and the interest it pays on deposits and borrowings. It also earns fees, such as service charges on deposit accounts, card services, retirement plan administration, wealth management, trust and insurance services. It reports two segments: Banking and Retirement Plan Administration.

    On May 2, 2025, it completed the purchase of Evans Bancorp for $221.8 million in common stock. At the end of 2025 the company had total assets of $16.00 billion.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker economic conditions that could raise loan delinquencies and defaults.
    • Changes in interest rates that it cannot predict or control and that can squeeze its net interest income.
    • Customers leaving for competitors that offer better prices on deposits and loans.
    • Severe weather such as flooding or wildfires hurting its branches and its customers' properties.
    • Changes in the mix of its assets and liabilities that can change its net interest income.
    From NBT Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
