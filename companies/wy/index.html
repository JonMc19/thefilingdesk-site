    What Weyerhaeuser does

    Weyerhaeuser grows and harvests trees, sells land, and makes lumber and other building materials. It says it is one of the world's largest private owners of timberland. It owns or controls more than 10 million acres of timberland in the United States and manages long-term licenses on more than 14 million acres in Canada.

    It reports three segments. Timberlands grows and sells logs. Real Estate, Energy and Natural Resources sells land with a higher use than forestry, and earns income from minerals, wind and solar sites and carbon-related projects. Wood Products makes structural lumber, oriented strand board (panels used in building) and engineered wood products. From 2026 the land segment is renamed Strategic Land Solutions.

    The company is a real estate investment trust (REIT), which lets it pass income from its timberlands to shareholders without paying corporate tax on it. Its wood products business is taxed as a normal company. The 10-K says the U.S. housing market strongly affects all three segments. It competes on product quality, service levels and price, and against substitutes for wood.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Demand for its products swings with the economy in cycles.
    • Weak demand for new homes and for home repair and remodeling, which it depends on heavily.
    • Higher interest rates that make mortgages harder to get and cut housing demand.
    • Competition from many companies and from substitutes for wood products.
    • Seasonal weather that slows building activity in winter and cuts demand for its logs and wood.
    From Weyerhaeuser's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
