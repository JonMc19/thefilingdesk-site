    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Years to 2019 come from Texas Pacific Land Trust, which became Texas Pacific Land Corporation in January 2021. Texas Pacific Land reports no borrowings, so debt is counted as zero. From 2023 Texas Pacific Land's filings label its capital spending in their own way, so capital spending and free cash flow are blank from 2023. Share counts and per-share figures for earlier years are restated for stock splits (3-for-1 and 3-for-1), so every year is on today's share basis.
