    What Artisan Partners Asset Management does

    Artisan Partners is an investment management firm that runs money for institutions and individuals around the world. It offers active strategies in several asset classes, such as equities, fixed income and alternatives, and it lets each of its investment teams work on its own philosophy and process.

    It earns nearly all its revenue from investment management fees, which are mostly a set percentage of the assets under management (AUM) of its clients. Its revenue therefore moves with the value of those assets and the fee rates it charges. It also earns performance fees from some strategies.

    Artisan grows by launching new strategies from existing teams and by adding new teams. It says it may close a strategy to new investors to protect performance, even if that hurts short-term results. Its teams compete with many other investment managers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing key investment professionals or senior leaders could hurt the business.
    • Poor investment results would lead clients to take money out, cutting its revenue.
    • Difficult markets reduce its assets under management and lead clients to withdraw funds.
    • Many strategies invest in non-U.S. companies, which brings currency and political risks.
    • It may be unable to keep its current fee rates.
