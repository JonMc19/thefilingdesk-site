    What Kyndryl does

    Kyndryl provides IT infrastructure services: it designs, builds, manages and modernizes the computer systems that large organizations run on. It serves thousands of customers in more than 60 countries. Services include cloud, core enterprise systems, applications, data and AI, digital workplace, security and resiliency, and network services. It works in private, public and multi-cloud setups, and partners with major cloud providers, system integrators and software vendors. Kyndryl says it is the world's largest IT infrastructure services provider.

    Kyndryl was separated from IBM, taking over the infrastructure services unit of IBM's Global Technology Services segment. It reports four segments by geography: United States, Japan, Principal Markets and Strategic Markets. By industry, 17 percent of its revenue is from retail, travel and logistics companies, 14 percent from the industrial sector, and 13 percent from healthcare and the public sector. Financial services is also a key industry.

    As of March 31, 2026, it had approximately 72,000 employees in 60 countries.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to win new customers, keep current ones and sell them more services.
    • Competition that could hurt its results.
    • Misjudging the cost and time of service contracts, which can reduce their profit.
    • Trouble hiring and equipping employees with the skills customers need.
    • An ongoing SEC investigation relating to its cash management practices and disclosures.
    From Kyndryl's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 29, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
