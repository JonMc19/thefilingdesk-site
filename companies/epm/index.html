    What Evolution Petroleum does

    Evolution Petroleum is an independent energy company that buys and develops oil and natural gas properties in the United States. Its holdings are mostly non-operated working interests, where it pays a share of the costs of wells that other companies run, and mineral and royalty interests, where it receives a share of production without paying drilling or lifting costs.

    It earns money from the oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids that come from these properties. It says the mix of regions, commodities, operators and ownership types reduces its reliance on any single property or basin. It says its non-operated approach lets it invest alongside experienced operators and choose when to spend.

    After the fiscal year ended June 30, 2026, the company bought mineral and royalty interests in the Midland Basin of the Permian Basin in Texas for a total price of $16.0 million. It paid with proceeds from a new public offering of its stock and some borrowing under its credit facility. The company also pays a quarterly dividend, which its board declared in September 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its existing oil and gas production will decline, and it may not find or buy enough new reserves to sustain the business.
    • Limited control over properties that other companies operate.
    • Not completing acquisitions at its usual pace or at good prices, or struggling to integrate those it buys.
    • Losses from its derivative contracts and the need for large amounts of capital to grow.
    • Government regulation, environmental liability and climate change risks that could raise its costs.
    From Evolution Petroleum's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
