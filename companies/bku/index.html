    What BankUnited does

    BankUnited is a bank holding company with total assets of $35.0 billion at the end of 2025. Its one subsidiary, BankUnited, is a national bank based in Miami Lakes, Florida, with operations in Florida, the New York tri-state area, Dallas, Atlanta and Charlotte. It serves individuals, small businesses, middle-market companies, large corporations and institutions, and offers some commercial lending and deposit products nationally and some consumer deposits online.

    Its main focus is lending to small, middle-market and larger businesses. Commercial loans include lines of credit, equipment loans, owner-occupied commercial real estate loans, mortgage warehouse lines and subscription finance facilities. Two lending subsidiaries do equipment financing: Pinnacle, which finances state and local governments, and Bridge, which finances business and franchise equipment and is being de-emphasized. It also makes commercial real estate loans, mostly on apartments, industrial buildings, warehouses and shopping centers, and has pulled back on office lending.

    BankUnited does not originate residential mortgages. It buys them, mainly closed-end first-lien jumbo loans, through correspondent channels and community partners. It says its strategy is to build a regional commercial and small business bank, and that it has grown mostly on its own rather than by acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to realize the expected benefits of its business strategy.
    • Credit losses as a lender, and an allowance for credit losses that may not be adequate.
    • Strong competition from other financial institutions.
    • Concerns about the stability of other regional and mid-size banks.
    • A credit rating downgrade, or being unable to raise capital when needed.
    From BankUnited's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
