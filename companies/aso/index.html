    What Academy Sports + Outdoors does

    Academy Sports + Outdoors is a sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer. Founded in Texas in 1938, it ran 322 stores in 21 states at the end of its fiscal year ended January 31, 2026, mostly in the southern United States. It also sells online, with options to pick up an order in a store or have it shipped.

    It makes money selling products to shoppers, many of them active families. By category, outdoor made up 31% of 2025 net sales, sports and recreation 22%, apparel 27% and footwear 20%. About 78% of merchandise sales came from national brands such as Nike, Under Armour and Columbia Sportswear. The rest came from its own private label brands, which are priced below national brands.

    It bought from about 1,500 vendors in 2025. It says it competes with other sporting goods and outdoor retailers, and it plans to keep opening stores in existing and new markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition from other sporting goods and outdoor retailers.
    • Managing inventory badly, or misreading what customers want to buy.
    • Reliance on goods made in other countries, with the trade and shipping risks that brings.
    • Concentration of its stores in the southern United States, which exposes it to regional conditions.
    • Disruptions at its distribution centers or in how inventory reaches stores.
    From Academy Sports + Outdoors's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
