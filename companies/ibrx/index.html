    What ImmunityBio does

    ImmunityBio is a biotechnology company that develops treatments to help the immune system fight cancer and some infectious diseases. Its first and only approved product, ANKTIVA, is a protein that activates certain immune cells. It is used together with the standard BCG treatment for adults with non-muscle invasive bladder cancer that no longer responds to BCG.

    ANKTIVA is approved in the United States, the United Kingdom and Saudi Arabia, and received conditional approval in the European Union in February 2026. In Saudi Arabia it also has conditional approval for use with other drugs in a form of metastatic lung cancer. The company is trying to win approval for more uses, including bladder tumors with papillary growths and lung cancer.

    It has many other candidates in development, grouped under a platform it calls Cancer BioShield. The company has a financing agreement with Oberland, called the RIPA, under which it must make payments, and it also owes money on a promissory note to a related party.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has a single approved product and limited experience selling medicines.
    • Payment obligations under its RIPA financing agreement could hurt its finances and its ability to raise money.
    • The RIPA includes operating limits and default events that could keep it from pursuing opportunities.
    • It may not win approval of ANKTIVA for more uses or sell it successfully in new markets.
    • Its reliance on third parties and the cost of raising money through shares, debt or licenses.
