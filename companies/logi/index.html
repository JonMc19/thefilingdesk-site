    What Logitech does

    Logitech designs computer and video accessories that pair hardware with software. Its categories are gaming gear, keyboards and combos, mice and other pointing devices, video collaboration systems, webcams, tablet accessories and headsets. It sells most of them under the Logitech and Logitech G names. Its products work with services such as Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Spotify and Twitch.

    Logitech reports one operating segment, Peripherals. It sells in the Americas; Europe, the Middle East and Africa; and Asia Pacific, through retailers, online sellers, distributors, resellers and its own web store. Amazon and its affiliates accounted for 18% of gross sales in fiscal 2026, the latest year in this filing. Logitech names competitors by product, including Razer, HP, Dell, Lenovo, Apple, Cisco, Poly, Jabra, Bose and Sony.

    It runs its own factories as well as using outside makers. Its Suzhou operation in China handles approximately 35% of its total production by value. Its manufacturing is spread across six countries. Logitech is a Swiss company, based in Lausanne, and its shares trade on the SIX Swiss Exchange and Nasdaq.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to design and launch new products on time and at a reasonable cost.
    • Reliance on a small number of suppliers for key parts and on its factories, especially in China.
    • Heavy dependence on retailers, e-tailers and distributors to sell its products.
    • Competition, including price pressure from rival brands and store brands.
    • Whether its efforts to grow into new product categories pay off.
    From Logitech's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 21, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
