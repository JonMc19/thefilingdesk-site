    What Axos Financial does

    Axos Financial is a bank holding company that describes itself as a technology-driven financial services company, with about $30.0 billion in assets. Its bank takes deposits and makes loans to customers across the country through online and mobile banking, with no need for a branch visit. Deposits include checking, savings and time accounts.

    It runs two segments. The Banking segment makes single family and multifamily mortgages, commercial real estate loans, loans to businesses such as lender finance and asset-based loans, equipment loans and leases, and auto and other consumer loans. It earns interest on these, plus fees from loans it originates for sale, operating lease income and deposit account fees.

    The Securities segment serves independent investment advisors and brokerage firms with custody, clearing, stock lending and margin lending through Axos Clearing and Axos Advisor Services. It also offers self-directed trading and digital investment management to individuals through Axos Invest. The company says it had about $47.8 billion of assets under custody or administration at Axos Clearing.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates that could hurt its earnings.
    • Loan losses if the economy weakens, especially in its large real estate and multifamily loan portfolios.
    • Falling real estate values, particularly in California and New York.
    • Strict bank and securities regulation, and the cost of failing to comply.
    • An allowance for credit losses that may prove too small to cover actual loan losses.
    From Axos Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 20, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
