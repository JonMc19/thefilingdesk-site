    What Garrett Motion does

    Garrett Motion designs, makes and sells turbochargers, air and fluid compressors, and high-speed electric motors. Turbochargers force more air into an engine so it uses less fuel for the power it makes. Its customers are vehicle and equipment makers, called OEMs, and independent aftermarket distributors that sell replacement parts. They use its products in cars, commercial vehicles, ships and industrial equipment.

    Most of its sales still come from turbochargers for gasoline, diesel and natural gas engines. It also sells electric boosting and hydrogen fuel cell air compressors. It is developing electric products for traction (E-Powertrain) and cooling (E-Cooling compressor), and says it put about 50% of its 2025 research and development spending into electric compression and E-Powertrain work. A new line of larger turbochargers is aimed at marine and industrial uses, including backup power for data centers.

    It supplies more than 60 OEMs worldwide. Its top ten customers made up about 62% of net sales in 2025 and its largest customer about 12%.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling behind if it does not deliver new products as the auto industry shifts away from combustion engines.
    • Depending on a few large customers, and losing any of them.
    • Strong competition, and pressure from customers on pricing and terms.
    • Rising costs and shortages of raw materials and components from suppliers.
    • Risks in its international operations and the unique risks of its aftermarket business.
    From Garrett Motion's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
