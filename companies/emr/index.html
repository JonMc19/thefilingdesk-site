    What Emerson Electric does

    Emerson is a technology and software company that sells automation equipment and software to manufacturers. Its customers include makers in process industries such as chemical, power and energy, hybrid industries such as life sciences, metals and mining, and food and beverage, and discrete industries. Its portfolio includes intelligent devices, control systems, and software for design and optimization.

    It reports two business groups. Intelligent Devices includes Final Control, Measurement & Analytical, Discrete Automation and Safety & Productivity. Software and Control includes Control Systems & Software and Test & Measurement. By destination, the Americas were 51 percent of 2025 sales, Asia, Middle East and Africa 30 percent and Europe 19 percent.

    Emerson has reshaped its mix of businesses in recent years. In March 2025 it bought the rest of AspenTech for about $7.2 billion. In 2023 it bought National Instruments and sold a majority stake in its Climate Technologies business. It says its businesses operate in highly competitive markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Needing to research, develop and market new or improved products successfully.
    • Failing to defend its intellectual property.
    • Acquisitions and divestitures that face regulatory requirements and may run into problems.
    • Shortages or price increases for raw materials and components.
    • Recessions or downturns in the end markets it serves.
    From Emerson Electric's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 10, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
