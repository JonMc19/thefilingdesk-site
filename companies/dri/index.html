    What Darden Restaurants does

    Darden Restaurants owns and runs full-service restaurants in the United States. On May 31, 2026, it operated 2,202 restaurants, including 949 Olive Garden and 618 LongHorn Steakhouse restaurants. Its other brands include Yard House, Ruth's Chris Steak House, Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen, The Capital Grille, Chuy's, Seasons 52 and Eddie V's. Another 167 restaurants are run by independent operators under franchise and development agreements, in the U.S. and in other countries.

    Sales come mainly from food and drink. The company reports four segments: Olive Garden, LongHorn Steakhouse, Fine Dining (Ruth's Chris, The Capital Grille and Eddie V's), and Other Business, which covers its remaining brands plus royalties and fees from franchised and managed restaurants. It says it does not rely on any major customers. At Olive Garden the average check was about $25.00 per person in fiscal 2026. At Yard House, alcoholic drinks made up 28.9 percent of sales.

    Darden bought Chuy's in October 2024 and Ruth's Chris in June 2023. In July 2025 it sold eight Olive Garden restaurants in Canada to Recipe Unlimited, which now runs them under a franchise agreement. It decided to close about half of its Bahama Breeze restaurants in 2026 and to convert the rest to other Darden brands over 12 to 18 months. Darden became a separate public company in 1995, when General Mills distributed its stock to General Mills shareholders.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Rising costs for food, labor, health care and utilities that menu price increases may not cover.
    • Trouble hiring, training and keeping restaurant team members.
    • Food safety problems or food-borne illness concerns, and health scares such as outbreaks of flu or other diseases.
    • Intense competition, and weaker guest spending when the economy or consumer confidence slips.
    • Failing to meet its growth plans by opening or acquiring restaurants, or from problems with its franchisees.
    From Darden Restaurants's annual report on Form 10-K, filed July 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
