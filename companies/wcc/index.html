    What WESCO International does

    Wesco is a business-to-business distributor. It buys electrical, communications, security, utility and industrial products from manufacturers and sells them, with supply chain services, to contractors, manufacturers, data center builders, telecom providers and utilities. It is based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, employs approximately 21,000 people and operates more than 700 sites in about 50 countries.

    It has three segments. Electrical & Electronic Solutions supplies electrical equipment, wire and cable, lighting, safety and maintenance products to construction, industrial and equipment-maker customers, for projects like data centers, hospitals and solar plants. Communications & Security Solutions serves data center, network infrastructure and security customers. Utility & Broadband Solutions serves utilities and broadband builders.

    Wesco also sells services such as logistics, procurement, warehousing, kitting and labeling, and limited assembly of products. It says its markets are highly fragmented, with thousands of small regional competitors and several large multinational ones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions or financial market disruptions hurting it and its customers.
    • Tariffs and trade policy changes that raise its costs or disrupt supply.
    • Acquisitions and divestitures that may not deliver the expected benefits.
    • Information system failures, or problems with its use of AI and cloud services run by others.
    • Currency swings and legal risks from operating in many countries.
