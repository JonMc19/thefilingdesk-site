    What Automatic Data Processing does

    ADP sells payroll and human resources software and services to employers, from single-employee small businesses to large companies with tens of thousands of employees. Its cloud-based products handle payroll, benefits, time tracking and tax filing. It also sells HR outsourcing, which ADP says serves over three million client employees. In fiscal 2026, in the United States, it moved more than $3.5 trillion in client funds to employees, tax authorities and other payees.

    ADP reports two segments. Employer Services sells the software and outsourcing. The Professional Employer Organization (PEO) segment, called ADP TotalSource, shares the role of employer with each client. In a PEO, ADP and the client are both employers of the client's staff, called worksite employees, and ADP provides benefits, risk management and workers' compensation programs. ADP says the PEO serves more than 19,000 clients and more than 770,000 worksite employees in all 50 U.S. states.

    ADP says it competes with other business outsourcing companies, companies that provide ERP (business management software) services, providers of cloud-based HR software and financial institutions. It also says no one client or industry is material to its overall revenues. It is adding AI agents to its products. In fiscal 2026 it invested approximately $1.405 billion in research and development.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Rapid technology change, including AI, and the need to keep upgrading its products.
    • Failing to comply with, or changes in, laws on payroll, privacy, data protection and AI.
    • Disruptions to its systems, data centers or cloud services that clients rely on to run payroll.
    • Market, interest rate, credit and liquidity risks on the funds it holds for clients.
    • Currency swings, because it earns a significant share of its revenue outside the United States.
    From Automatic Data Processing's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
