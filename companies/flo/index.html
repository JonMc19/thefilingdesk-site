    What Flowers Foods does

    Flowers Foods, based in Thomasville, Georgia, says it is the second-largest maker and seller of packaged bakery foods in the United States. It makes breads, buns, rolls, snack cakes, cookies, crackers, bagels, English muffins, tortillas and baking mixes. Its brands include Nature's Own, Dave's Killer Bread, Canyon Bakehouse, Simple Mills, Wonder and Tastykake.

    It sells to grocery and other stores, restaurants, institutions and wholesalers. It reports sales in two groups: Branded Retail, which was 65.9% of sales in fiscal 2025, and Other, which includes store-brand products, foodservice, thrift stores and contract manufacturing for other companies. Most fresh products go out through a direct-store-delivery system: bakeries deliver to independent distributors, who own the rights to sell its brands in a territory and who stock shelves in stores. It also has company-run territories and a warehouse delivery system for some snacks.

    In February 2025 Flowers bought Simple Mills, which makes crackers, cookies, snack bars and baking mixes using simple ingredients, and Simple Mills' products are made by outside manufacturers. The company had about 10,300 employees at the start of 2026, plus about 5,500 full-time leased and temporary workers. It reports one segment.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Sales of fresh packaged bread are weak, and consumers may shift to cheaper store brands.
    • Changes to its independent distributor model, including court rulings on whether distributors are contractors.
    • Higher costs for ingredients, packaging, fuel and labor, and tariffs, which it may not be able to pass on in prices.
    • The loss of, or financial trouble at, a significant customer.
    • Buying Simple Mills may not deliver the expected benefits, and the goodwill on Simple Mills could be written down.
    From Flowers Foods's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
