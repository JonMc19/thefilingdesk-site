    What Powerfleet does

    Powerfleet sells tracking and monitoring systems for vehicles and equipment, combining small connected devices, video cameras and cloud software. Customers are fleet operators, trucking and shipping companies, rental car firms, car makers, and warehouses and factories that run forklifts and similar machines. Its central software platform, called Unity, collects data from many sources and turns it into reports and alerts.

    Its products fall into two groups. Warehouse, Yard and Site products track equipment inside facilities and support safety and compliance, including video that detects people near moving trucks. On-Road products track heavy trucks, trailers, containers and their cargo, and can extend to rental cars and private fleets. It sells under the brands Powerfleet, Pointer, Cellocator, MiX by Powerfleet and Fleet Complete. In its fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, services made up 81.1% of total revenues.

    Powerfleet is based in Woodcliff Lake, New Jersey, and had 2,658 employees worldwide on March 31, 2026. It has grown through acquisitions and is still working to combine them.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not fully realizing the benefits of its acquisitions and business changes.
    • Past losses and a large accumulated deficit, with profit not assured.
    • Supply chain problems and reliance on a few suppliers for key parts.
    • Falling behind in fast-changing technology, including AI.
    • Possibly needing more capital to fund its operations.
