    What LPL Financial does

    LPL Financial provides the platform that independent financial advisors use to run their practices. It describes itself as the largest independent broker-dealer in the country, as well as an investment advisory firm and a custodian. It supports more than 32,000 financial advisors and the wealth management practices of approximately 1,200 financial institutions, servicing and custodying approximately $2.4 trillion in brokerage and advisory assets.

    Advisors choose among affiliation models, investment products, technology tools and practice management services. LPL's revenue comes from several sources: advisory fees and commissions generated by its advisors, other asset-based fees from product sponsors, recordkeeping and networking services, and fees from client cash programs. Its client cash programs include two FDIC-insured bank sweep vehicles, a client cash account and a money market account.

    LPL says its advisor-mediated model avoids conflicts from product manufacturing, underwriting and market-making. The parent company, LPL Financial Holdings Inc., was incorporated in Delaware in 2005.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to attract and keep experienced, productive advisors in a competitive market.
    • Interest rate changes that affect fees from banks in its client cash programs and its profit.
    • Damage to its reputation that could cost it revenue.
    • Litigation, arbitration claims and regulatory actions.
    • Risks that come with the independent broker-dealer business model.
    From LPL Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
