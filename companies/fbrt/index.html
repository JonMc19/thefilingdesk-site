    What Franklin BSP Realty Trust does

    Franklin BSP Realty Trust is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that lends against commercial real estate. It is externally managed by Benefit Street Partners, a Franklin Templeton company, which runs its day-to-day affairs and is paid fees for doing so. The company has two business units.

    Commercial Real Estate Financing originates, buys and manages commercial property debt, including first mortgage loans, subordinated and mezzanine loans, and bridge loans. It also holds real estate securities such as commercial mortgage-backed securities, makes conduit loans that it plans to sell into securitizations, and owns some property it took over after loans defaulted.

    The Agency Business came from the July 1, 2025 purchase of NewPoint. It originates, sells and services multifamily loans under programs of Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, Ginnie Mae and the Federal Housing Administration. The company is an approved lender for several of these programs. The company earns mainly interest on its loans, and fees from the Agency Business. As of December 31, 2025, it had 223 employees, all at NewPoint.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large amount of debt, and the chance it needs to borrow more.
    • Not earning more on its loans than it pays on its borrowings.
    • Lenders asking for more collateral when asset values fall, which could strain its cash.
    • Changes in short-term interest rates, which affect its net income.
    • The usual risks of commercial real estate behind its loans, including defaults and delays in resolving them.
