    What Avient does

    Avient makes specialty polymer materials and sells them to companies that make plastic products. Its products include specialty engineered materials, performance fibers, advanced composites, color and additive solutions, liquid colorants and silicone colorants. It sits between large chemical producers, its raw material suppliers, and the designers and manufacturers of polymer products, its customers.

    It has two reportable segments: Color, Additives and Inks, and Specialty Engineered Materials. Its end markets include consumer, packaging, defense, healthcare, industrial, transportation, building and construction, telecommunications and energy. In 2025 the company had sales of $3.3 billion, with about 61% to customers outside the United States.

    Avient is headquartered in Avon Lake, Ohio, and has 98 manufacturing sites in North America, South America, Asia, Europe, the Middle East and Africa. It was formed as PolyOne in 2000 and changed its name to Avient in 2020.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Risks of operating worldwide, including trade policy changes and new tariffs.
    • Hazards at its plants from specialty formulation and from storing and moving raw materials.
    • Swings in the cost and supply of raw materials, energy and logistics.
    • Competition that could cut sales and earnings.
    • Debt agreements with covenants that limit what it can do.
    From Avient's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
