    What Enova International does

    Enova International is an online lender. It makes loans and lines of credit to consumers and small businesses that have bank accounts but limited access to credit from traditional banks and card issuers. It offers consumer installment loans in 37 states and in Brazil, and small business loans and lines of credit in 49 states and Washington, D.C. It also runs a money transfer service, Pangea, for sending money from the United States to Mexico, other Latin American countries and Asia.

    It earns money mainly from interest and fees on its loans. It markets under names including CashNetUSA, NetCredit, OnDeck and Headway Capital. It has one reportable segment. It decides whom to lend to with its own scoring models, many of which use machine learning, and usually funds approved loans the next business day. Some loans are made through partner banks, for which it earns marketing and servicing fees. The United States provides nearly all of its revenue.

    It has agreed to acquire Grasshopper, a deal that still needs regulatory approval.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It is heavily regulated, and new laws could restrict its products or raise its costs.
    • Negative public views of its lending, or an unfavorable CFPB supervision outcome, could hurt demand.
    • It needs access to payment systems such as the Automated Clearing House to send and collect money.
    • Customers who give false or incorrect information could lead it to misjudge who can repay.
    • The pending Grasshopper acquisition may not close, or may not work out as planned.
