    What Aptiv does

    Aptiv supplies technology and parts to automakers. It describes itself as an industrial technology company, and its products run from sensors to cloud software, including the wiring and electronics that carry power and data through a vehicle. Its customers are the companies that build cars and trucks.

    Since the first quarter of 2025 it has reported three segments: Advanced Safety and User Experience, Engineered Components Group and Electrical Distribution Systems. From 2026 the first two are renamed Intelligent Systems and Engineered Components, with no change to what they include. Its sales depend on how many vehicles its customers make and which models and options they sell.

    In January 2025 Aptiv announced that it would spin off its Electrical Distribution Systems business into a separate publicly traded company, to be named Versigent, and planned to finish by April 1, 2026. It reported separation costs of about $178 million in 2025. It is a Jersey company, tax resident in Switzerland, whose shares trade on the NYSE.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Disruptions in the supply of raw materials and other supplies that it and its customers use.
    • The cyclical nature of auto sales and production, and a prolonged recession or downturn in car sales.
    • A drop in its customers' market share or changes in the mix of vehicles they sell.
    • Aggressive competition in China and in the auto technology and component industry.
    • Late deliveries that force it to absorb extra costs under its contracts.
    From Aptiv's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
