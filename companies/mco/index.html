    What Moody's does

    Moody's is a risk-information company with two segments: Moody's Investors Service (MIS) and Moody's Analytics (MA). It has about 16,000 employees in more than 40 countries and is based in New York.

    MIS publishes credit ratings, research and risk analysis on corporate, financial institution and government debt and on structured finance securities. Issuers use its ratings to bring debt to market, and investors use them to understand debt markets. MIS also earns smaller amounts from pricing services in the Asia-Pacific region, second party opinions on sustainability and net zero assessments, and from the non-ratings work of ICRA.

    MA sells data, research and analytical tools to financial services, corporate and public sector customers. It has three parts: Research & Insights, a research business on fixed income and the economy; Data & Information, built on a large database of companies and credit; and Decision Solutions, three cloud-based subscription businesses for banking, insurance and know-your-customer checks.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Laws and regulation affecting credit rating agencies and its customers.
    • Litigation and government investigations tied to its rating opinions and business practices.
    • Dependence on the volume of debt securities issued in the capital markets.
    • New competing products and technologies, and pricing pressure.
    • Damage to its reputation or credibility.
    From Moody's's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
