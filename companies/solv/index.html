    What Solventum does

    Solventum develops, makes and sells healthcare products and software. Its customers range from large hospitals to local clinics and dental practices. Its products are used across a patient's care, from prevention and diagnosis to treatment and recovery. It was spun off from 3M, and still has significant business dealings with 3M.

    It reports three segments. MedSurg, at 57.9% of 2025 sales, sells products such as negative pressure wound therapy, advanced wound dressings, I.V. site management, sterilization assurance and surgical supplies. Dental Solutions, at 16.2%, sells braces, clear aligners, cements and bonding agents. Health Information Systems, at 16.3%, sells software that helps hospitals with clinical documentation, coding and billing.

    In 2025 Solventum bought Acera Surgical, a privately held bioscience company, and sold its Purification and Filtration business to Thermo Fisher Scientific. It says it competes in highly competitive markets and that customers in the healthcare industry are consolidating.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not getting the expected benefits of the spin-off from 3M, and its continuing commercial ties to 3M.
    • Debt taken on in the spin-off, and difficulty raising money on good terms.
    • Competition and consolidation among healthcare customers that could push it to cut prices.
    • New products that may not be accepted by the market or arrive on time.
    • Possible liabilities related to PFAS chemicals.
    From Solventum's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
