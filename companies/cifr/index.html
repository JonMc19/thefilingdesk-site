    What Cipher Digital does

    Cipher Digital develops and runs large data centers built for high-performance computing, which means the heavy computing behind artificial intelligence. The company was formerly a bitcoin miner and changed its name to Cipher Digital on February 20, 2026. It says it has become a vertically integrated data center developer and operator.

    Its business covers securing land and electric power, connecting to the grid, developing sites, designing and building data centers, and operating them. The company plans to lease its data centers to large cloud companies, which it calls hyperscalers, and other computing customers under long-term contracts. It still runs bitcoin mining at times, as an interim or complementary use of the power at its sites.

    Its income depends on tenants. It says its business has significant tenant concentration. Building the data centers takes large amounts of money and it relies on financing and debt to pay for them.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not finishing its data centers on time or within its cost estimates.
    • Needing large amounts of capital and financing to build them.
    • Depending on a small number of tenants for its data center leases.
    • Higher power costs, outages, or not having enough access to power.
    • Demand for data centers, and the AI industry that drives it, not growing as expected.
    From Cipher Digital's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
