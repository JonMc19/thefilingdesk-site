    What G-III Apparel Group does

    G-III Apparel Group designs, sources and sells clothing and fashion accessories. Its products include outerwear, dresses, sportswear, suits, swimwear, handbags, footwear and luggage. It sells to department stores and other retailers at wholesale, and to shoppers through its own stores and websites.

    It has more than 30 brands. Its owned brands include DKNY, Donna Karan, Karl Lagerfeld and Vilebrequin. It also makes products under licenses from other brand owners, including Calvin Klein, Tommy Hilfiger, Levi's, Nautica and sports leagues such as the NFL, NBA and Major League Baseball. Owned brands were approximately 57% of net sales in fiscal 2026.

    The company, a Delaware corporation formed in 1989, has done business since 1974. It also licenses some of its own brands to partners for other product categories and regions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing or not renewing its major license agreements, which carry minimum sales and royalty payments.
    • Depending on the strategies and reputation of the brand owners that license to it.
    • Relying on a small number of large customers.
    • Losses at its retail stores if its turnaround plan does not work.
    • Leasing large amounts of real estate.
    From G-III Apparel Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
