    What Verizon does

    Verizon sells wireless phone service, home and business internet, and related services in the United States under the Verizon family of brands. Customers buy service postpaid (billed monthly, a month in advance) or prepaid. It also sells phones, tablets and other devices, and lets consumers pay for them in installments. Through fixed wireless access (FWA), it delivers home and business internet over its 5G and 4G LTE wireless networks instead of cables.

    Verizon reports two segments. The Consumer segment had revenue of $106.8 billion in 2025, about 77% of the total, and about 116 million wireless retail connections, including FWA. The Business segment, which serves companies, governments, schools and other carriers, had revenue of $29.1 billion, about 21%. Verizon names AT&T and T-Mobile US as national wireless competitors, and says cable companies such as Comcast and Charter also resell wireless service. In broadband it competes with cable companies, satellite providers and others.

    At the end of 2025 Verizon had about 89,900 full-time-equivalent employees, 89% of them in the U.S. In the fourth quarter of 2025 it announced plans to cut more than 13,000 positions. In January 2026 it completed its purchase of Frontier Communications, a broadband provider, which extended its fiber network to 31 states and Washington D.C. It also bought Starry, a fixed wireless provider serving multi-unit buildings in five U.S. markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition, including aggressive pricing and promotions, some of it aimed directly at Verizon's customers.
    • Failing to keep up with new technology and changing demand, or to phase out old, unprofitable services at a reasonable cost.
    • Network failures and disruptions that keep it from giving customers reliable service.
    • Claims about lead-sheathed copper cables in its older network, which could bring lawsuits, penalties and removal costs.
    • Its large debt, which could grow, and borrowing costs that could rise.
    From Verizon's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
