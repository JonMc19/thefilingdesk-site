    What AST SpaceMobile does

    AST SpaceMobile is building a network of satellites in low Earth orbit meant to give ordinary smartphones cellular broadband service where land-based coverage does not reach. The company calls the planned product the SpaceMobile Service. It is still in development, and the company has not yet started to earn revenue from it. It also pursues government uses for its technology and patents.

    The plan is to work through mobile network operators (MNOs), the phone companies that already serve users. AST says it has partnerships with over 50 of them, with nearly 3 billion subscribers worldwide. Users would not need new equipment or have to sign up with AST. AST intends to use a revenue-sharing model in its agreements with the operators, though it describes this as an intention.

    The network uses spectrum, the radio frequencies that carry signals, that belongs to the operators, and AST has signed usage agreements for more. These include an agreement with Ligado Networks for mid-band spectrum, which still depends on conditions including regulatory approval. AST says it owns the technology and controls the manufacturing, itself or through contract makers, for approximately 95% of the sub-systems in its Block 2 satellites. It reported a net loss attributable to common stockholders of $341.9 million for 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The service is still in development and may not be finished on time or at all, and may cost more than planned.
    • It must raise a lot more money, and it has a history of losses and may never be profitable.
    • Dependence on mobile network operators and on regulatory approvals to use the spectrum it needs.
    • Dependence on its founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer.
    • Reliance on outside suppliers for equipment, satellite parts and launches, and strong competition.
    From AST SpaceMobile's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
