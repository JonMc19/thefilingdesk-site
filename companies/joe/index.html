    What St. Joe does

    St. Joe is a real estate development, asset management and operating company in Northwest Florida. At the end of 2025 it owned 165,000 acres of land there, and approximately 87% of its real estate is in Bay, Gulf and Walton counties. It was incorporated in Florida in 1936.

    It reports three segments. In residential, it develops and sells homesites, mainly to homebuilders and on a limited basis to retail customers, and a joint venture is building homes in the Latitude Margaritaville Watersound community. Hospitality runs the Watersound Club, which offers members golf courses and a beach club, along with other hospitality operations. In commercial, it owns, develops and leases property such as multi-family, senior living, self-storage, medical, office and retail space.

    Part of its land lies in the Bay-Walton Sector Plan, which gives it the legal right to develop over 170,000 homes and over 22 million square feet of retail, commercial and industrial space. At the start of 2026 it had 906 full-time and 225 part-time and seasonal employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Homesite sales can swing sharply from one period to the next.
    • Failing to lease new commercial properties on the terms it expects.
    • Geographic concentration in Northwest Florida.
    • Hospitality costs rising or its ability to charge prices being limited.
    • Insurance that is renewed yearly and may become costlier or harder to get.
