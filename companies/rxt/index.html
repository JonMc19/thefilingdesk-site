    What Rackspace Technology does

    Rackspace Technology sells cloud and AI services to businesses. It designs, builds and runs technology systems for customers, from private cloud infrastructure it runs for them to workloads on the big public clouds. It focuses on regulated and mission-critical settings where uptime, data control and security matter most.

    It works with the large public cloud providers, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure, and with VMware by Broadcom for private clouds. Customers pay for these managed services, which can include running their applications, data and security. It also sells through referral and reseller partners.

    It also runs its own data centers. Its report says it has a history of losses, and it says that its strategies need significant investments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to win and keep customers and to sell more services at comparable margins.
    • A history of losses, and no assurance it will become profitable.
    • Reliance on third-party cloud providers and on referral and reseller partners.
    • Service outages, security breaches and cyberattacks that could cost it customers.
    • Data centers that need reliable electric power and network connections.
    From Rackspace Technology's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
