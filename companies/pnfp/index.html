    Pinnacle Financial Partners' revenue rose 18.3% to $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $642 million, up 35.1%. Its return on equity was 9.5%, up from 7.6% a year earlier.     Return on equity is net income as a share of shareholders' equity; return on tangible equity leaves out goodwill and other intangible assets. The efficiency ratio is operating costs as a share of revenue, so lower means the bank spends less to earn each dollar. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.
