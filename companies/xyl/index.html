    What Xylem does

    Xylem is a water technology company with 2025 revenues of $9.0 billion and approximately 22,000 employees worldwide. It designs, makes and services equipment and software used across the water cycle: delivering and treating drinking water, collecting and treating wastewater, measuring water and energy use, and returning water to the environment. It sells through direct sales forces and independent channel partners in approximately 150 countries.

    It reports four segments: Water Infrastructure, with pumps and treatment equipment for utilities and industry; Applied Water, with pumps, valves, heat exchangers and controls for buildings and industry; Measurement and Control Solutions, with smart meters, communication devices, analytics and test instruments; and Water Solutions and Services, with maintenance, rapid response, outsourced solutions and rental equipment.

    The company says its installed base of products leads to steady parts, replacement and service revenue. Its 10-K describes the water industry as highly fragmented, with many competitors in each market.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Industry and economic conditions could hurt its markets and its customers' demand.
    • Inflation, tariffs and other cost increases have hurt and could hurt its results.
    • Its global sales and supply chain expose it to geopolitical, regulatory and currency risks.
    • It may be unable to compete successfully or to commercialize new products.
    • It may be unable to execute large projects on time, on budget or to the required performance.
