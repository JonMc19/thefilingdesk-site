    What Ingersoll Rand does

    Ingersoll Rand makes industrial equipment that moves and controls air, gas, powder and liquid. Its products include air and gas compressors, vacuum pumps, blowers, fluid transfer equipment, power tools, lifting equipment, and pumps and liquid handling systems. It sells them under more than 90 brands, including Ingersoll Rand and Gardner Denver. Customers include manufacturers and companies in life sciences, food and beverage, clean energy, infrastructure and water treatment.

    It has two segments. Industrial Technologies and Services sells compression, vacuum, blower and related equipment. Precision and Science Technologies sells specialized equipment for precision dosing, liquid handling and powder handling, for both industrial and life science customers. Equipment wears out and needs parts and service, so the company also sells replacement parts, consumables and repair services. This aftermarket business was 36.5% of its revenue in 2025.

    The company has sales in all major regions. It names Atlas Copco, Flowserve, IDEX, Dover, Graco and Thermo Fisher Scientific among its competitors. It had over 60 key factories and over 50 service and repair centers, and over 21,000 employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weakness in the global economy and in the industries that buy its equipment.
    • More than half of its sales and operations are outside the United States, with political and currency risks.
    • Tariff changes and higher raw material costs, or reliance on certain suppliers.
    • Product liability and warranty claims, or recalls, because its equipment is used in critical processes.
    • Acquisitions that may be hard to integrate or may not work out as planned.
    From Ingersoll Rand's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
