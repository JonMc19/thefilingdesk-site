    What Aviat Networks does

    Aviat Networks designs, makes and sells wireless equipment that carries network traffic between points, using microwave and millimeter wave radio links over short, medium and long distances. It also sells routers, software tools for running and managing networks, and services such as network design, installation and support. It sources and integrates other makers' gear, such as antennas and power supplies, so customers can build a complete network.

    It sells to two types of customers. The first is communications service providers: mobile and fixed telephone networks, broadband and internet providers. The second is private network operators, which build their own networks for cost, independence or security reasons. These include government agencies, transportation and energy companies, utilities, public safety agencies and broadcasters. Aviat sells mostly directly, and to a lesser extent through agents and resellers.

    Aviat is based in Austin, Texas, and had 900 employees on July 3, 2026. It says its sales are split about evenly between North America and the rest of the world, and it competes with other network equipment makers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Long sales cycles that delay revenue and make it harder to forecast.
    • Depending on timely new products and customer acceptance of them.
    • Relying on third-party service partners and contract manufacturers.
    • Falling average selling prices and strong competition.
    • Customers' financial trouble, which could raise credit and payment risks.
    From Aviat Networks's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
