    What Nature's Sunshine Products does

    Nature's Sunshine Products makes and sells herbal and other nutritional supplements and personal care products. Its line of over 800 products covers general health, immune, heart, digestive and weight management products, and items such as aloe vera gel, shampoo and toothpaste. It buys herbs and other raw materials in bulk, then tests, formulates, packages and ships them. Most products are made at its plant in Spanish Fork, Utah, and contract manufacturers make some.

    It sells through direct selling: independent consultants who use the products themselves or resell them to consumers, under the Nature's Sunshine Products and Synergy WorldWide brands. It pays these consultants commissions, which it calls volume incentives, based on their own sales and those of their sales organization. It reports four segments by region: Asia, Europe, North America, and Latin America and Other.

    The 10-K says that in some markets a significant amount of revenue depends on only a few consultants and their large sales networks. Orders usually ship within 24 hours of payment, so it keeps a considerable inventory of raw materials and finished goods.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Direct selling laws that could restrict or force changes to how it sells in some countries.
    • Failing to attract and keep independent consultants, or losing key ones with large sales networks.
    • Strict government rules on its products, and difficulty registering them for sale abroad.
    • Product liability claims, and obligations arising from what its consultants do.
    • Currency swings, and supply chain or manufacturing interruptions.
    From Nature's Sunshine Products's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
