    What Idaho Strategic Resources does

    Idaho Strategic Resources is a small mining company in Idaho. It mines gold at its Golden Chest Mine in the Murray Gold Belt in northern Idaho, and runs the New Jersey Mill, which it manages and mostly owns and which processes ore from that mine. It also holds exploration land in the same area.

    Its revenue comes from selling the gold it produces, so its results depend on how much gold it recovers and what it costs to do so. The company also holds three rare earth element properties in central Idaho, Mineral Hill, Lemhi Pass and Diamond Creek. Rare earths are a group of minerals used in many high-tech products. These properties are exploration projects.

    The company says it competes with other small mining companies for the money needed for exploration and development, and for skilled mining workers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not earning enough from its mines to fund its work, with a stated risk that it may not continue as a going concern.
    • Falling metal prices or rising costs, such as fuel and power, that could make production unprofitable.
    • Mining and milling problems, such as lower ore grades, equipment failures and ground failures.
    • Exploration properties, including its rare earth projects, that may never prove to hold a worthwhile deposit.
    From Idaho Strategic Resources's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
