    What Yeti does

    YETI, based in Austin, Texas, designs and sells outdoor and recreation products, from coolers and drinkware to bags and apparel. It was founded in 2006 by two brothers who wanted sturdier gear for hunting and fishing, and its first product was a hard cooler. Its buyers range from serious outdoor enthusiasts to people who like the design and quality.

    In 2025, Coolers & Equipment made up 40% of net sales, Drinkware 58% and Other 2%. YETI sells directly to consumers through its own website and stores and through wholesale partners. Direct sales were 60% of net sales in 2025, and wholesale was the rest. The company operates as one reportable segment.

    YETI relies on contract manufacturers to make its products. It says it competes with numerous other brands and retailers that offer a wide variety of products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weakening its brand, which its demand depends on.
    • Failing to design, develop and market new products that customers want.
    • Wrongly forecasting demand for its products or failing to manage its growth.
    • Reliance on third-party contract manufacturers and on getting raw materials.
    • Competition from many other brands and retailers.
    From Yeti's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
