    What Dianthus Therapeutics does

    Dianthus Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company, which means it is testing its medicines in people and has none approved for sale. It is working on treatments for severe autoimmune diseases, in which the immune system attacks the body's own tissue.

    Its lead candidate, claseprubart, is an antibody that blocks one active part of the complement system, a branch of the immune system that can cause damage in these diseases. The company designed it to be given less often, as an injection patients can give themselves with a pre-filled pen. Dianthus believes it could be used for many complement-driven diseases.

    Its second candidate, DNTH212, is a fusion protein that targets two immune pathways at once, one involving interferon production and one involving B cells. It is also designed for self-injection.

    Dianthus has a limited operating history and has not finished any late-stage clinical trial. It says it will need substantial new capital to fund its work.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has no approved products, a limited operating history and has not finished a late-stage clinical trial.
    • It will need substantial additional capital, and may not be able to raise it on acceptable terms.
    • It has lost money since it began and expects more losses for the foreseeable future.
    • It depends heavily on the success of one candidate, claseprubart, and its planned trials.
    • Its approach to finding and developing medicines is unproven, and competitors are working on the same diseases.
    From Dianthus Therapeutics's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
