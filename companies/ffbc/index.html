    What First Financial Bancorp does

    First Financial Bancorp is a financial holding company based in Cincinnati, Ohio. Its subsidiary, First Financial Bank, was founded in 1863 and is chartered in Ohio. The bank serves individuals and businesses with commercial lending, real estate lending and consumer financing, along with deposit accounts, cash management services, and trust and wealth management.

    Its real estate loans are secured by homes or by commercial property such as apartments, shopping centers and offices. Commercial and industrial loans cover business needs such as inventory, receivables and equipment. The bank also does lease and equipment financing through its Summit Funding Group subsidiary, and lends across the U.S. to restaurant franchisees through First Franchise Capital and to the insurance industry, investment advisors, accountants and indirect auto finance companies through Oak Street Funding.

    As with other banks, it earns money from the interest on its loans, and it also earns fees. In August 2025 it agreed to merge with BankFinancial Corporation.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Policy changes by the Federal Reserve that affect banks, the money supply or credit conditions.
    • Loan defaults and foreclosures, which no bank can avoid.
    • Failures of its systems, employees or counterparties.
    • Fraud and cybersecurity incidents that could bring liability and harm its reputation.
    • Needing to borrow more if deposits fall.
    From First Financial Bancorp's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
