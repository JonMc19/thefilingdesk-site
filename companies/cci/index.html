    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Crown Castle's filings tag its operating costs differently from year to year (in some years only its services costs), so gross profit and gross margin are left blank. Crown Castle's shareholders' equity turned negative in 2024 after write-downs, so return on equity and debt to equity are left blank from then. Figures from 2023 leave out the fiber and small-cell businesses Crown Castle agreed to sell in 2025, as restated in its later filings; earlier years include them, so revenue falls in 2023. Crown Castle's capital spending to 2022 includes the fiber businesses and from 2023 doesn't, and its 2023 quarters can't be separated on the same basis, so capital spending and free cash flow are left blank.

Net debt to EBITDA is debt minus cash, divided by operating income before depreciation and amortization: roughly how many years of those earnings the net debt equals.
