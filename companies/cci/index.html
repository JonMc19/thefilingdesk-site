    Crown Castle

    CCI · NYSE · Real Estate · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Crown Castle's revenue fell 4.4% to $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 48.7%, up from 47.5% a year earlier. It paid out $2.1 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count rose 0.7%.

    About these figures. Crown Castle's filings tag its operating costs differently from year to year (in some years only its services costs), so gross profit and gross margin are left blank.     How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation.
