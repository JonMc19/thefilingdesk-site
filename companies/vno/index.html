    What Vornado Realty Trust does

    Vornado is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that owns and runs mostly New York City property. Nearly all of its properties are held through an operating partnership, in which Vornado is the sole general partner and owned about 91.3% of the common limited partnership interest at the end of 2025.

    Its holdings in Manhattan include 51 operating properties: office space in 26 of them, street retail space in 45 and two residential buildings. It is also redeveloping and developing sites, including 350 Park Avenue, PENN 2 and other buildings near Penn Station, and a studio campus at Pier 94 with partners. Elsewhere it owns THE MART in Chicago and a controlling interest in an office complex at 555 California Street in San Francisco. It also holds a stake in Alexander's, Inc., which owns five properties, and owns a building cleaning and security services business.

    Vornado earns money mainly from rent paid by tenants. In 2025 it bought the 623 Fifth Avenue office condominium and sold part of the 666 Fifth Avenue flagship retail property to UNIQLO, among other deals. It says it plans to pay for growth with internal funds, asset sales and the public and private capital markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Trends in office real estate that hurt demand for its buildings.
    • A large share of its properties is in the New York area, so local economic cycles matter.
    • Difficulty renewing leases or re-leasing vacant space on good terms.
    • Tenants going bankrupt, which cuts revenue and cash.
    • Losses not fully covered by insurance, including from terrorism and climate change.
    From Vornado Realty Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
