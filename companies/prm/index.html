    What Perimeter Solutions does

    Perimeter Solutions makes industrial products and services for niche uses. Its operations cover firefighting products, lubricant additives and electronic components. In January 2026 it bought Medical Manufacturing Technologies, which makes machinery for the medical device industry.

    It reports two segments. Fire Safety makes and sells fire retardants and suppressants, with related equipment and services, for wildfire, industrial and structural firefighting. Its retardant customers are mostly government agencies. It says it supplies retardants listed on the USDA Forest Service Qualified Products List. Specialty Products makes the other items, such as lubricant additives and electronic components.

    About 76% of its annual revenue comes from the United States, about 10% from Europe and about 7% from Canada, with another 7% from other countries. It runs through general managers with wide freedom to run their own units, and says it puts its free cash flow first into its businesses, then share buybacks, then acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a few customers, including the USDA Forest Service, the Bureau of Land Management and California, for much of its Fire Safety revenue.
    • Heightened risks of selling to the U.S. government, which can audit it and end contracts.
    • Production interruptions or shutdowns at its plants.
    • Raw materials with few sources of supply that can rise in cost or run short.
    • Substantial debt that limits its cash flow and flexibility.
    From Perimeter Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
