    What Genie Energy does

    Genie Energy provides energy services and reports two segments. Genie Retail Energy sells electricity and natural gas to homes and small businesses in deregulated markets in the United States through retail energy providers it owns, such as IDT Energy and Residents Energy. Most of their customers are in the Eastern and Midwestern United States and Texas. A subsidiary, Mirabito, supplies natural gas to commercial customers in Florida.

    Genie Renewables is a group of smaller businesses. Genie Solar is an integrated solar energy company. CityCom Solar markets community solar and related products. Diversegy advises industrial, commercial and municipal customers on buying energy. Roded Recycling makes plastic pallets from recycled materials.

    Genie owns all of Genie Retail Energy and most, but not all, of the renewables businesses. In 2022 it stopped operating its former international retail segment, which sold energy in the United Kingdom, Finland and Sweden.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The retail energy business is highly competitive, and Genie may have to cut prices or take on more costs.
    • Its strategy rests on current regulatory conditions, which could change.
    • Weather and swings in commodity prices can hurt its results and raise customer churn.
    • Fixed-rate products can lose money if it misjudges future commodity prices or customer usage.
    • Its growth depends partly on entering new markets.
