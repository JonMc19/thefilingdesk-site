    What Valero Energy does

    Valero makes and sells fuels. It is a manufacturer and marketer of petroleum-based and low-carbon liquid transportation fuels and petrochemical products. It sells mainly in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland and Latin America. It is based in San Antonio, Texas.

    It runs three segments. Refining turns crude oil into gasoline, diesel and other products at 15 refineries in the U.S., Canada and the U.K. Renewable Diesel is a joint venture with Darling Ingredients, called DGD, that makes low-carbon fuels at two plants on the Gulf Coast. Ethanol runs 12 plants in the Mid-Continent region of the U.S. Valero says its refineries have a combined capacity of about 3.2 million barrels per day.

    Valero earns money from the margin between what it pays for crude oil and other feedstocks and what it gets for its products. Its customers are mostly refined product wholesalers and retailers. It says competitors with different businesses or more resources can have an advantage at times.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Volatile margins, set by feedstock costs and product prices it does not control.
    • An interruption at any of its refineries or plants.
    • Changes in laws and demand that could cut demand for its products, including low-carbon fuel rules.
    • Its natural gas, electricity and water supply and prices.
    • Losses and extra costs from its hedging transactions.
    From Valero Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
