    What Hewlett Packard Enterprise does

    Hewlett Packard Enterprise sells computing, networking and cloud technology to businesses and governments, from small companies to large global enterprises. Its products include servers, networking equipment, and software and services for running data and artificial intelligence workloads across company data centers, the edge and the cloud.

    It reports five segments: Server, Hybrid Cloud, Networking, Financial Services, and Corporate Investments and Other. The Server segment sells general-purpose servers and systems for high-performance computing and AI. Financial Services offers customers financing for technology purchases. In fiscal 2025 its server and networking products were the product categories that each brought in more than a tenth of net revenue.

    HPE says one distributor accounted for about a tenth of its revenue in fiscal 2025, mainly in Server and Networking. It describes a shift toward a platform model with more software and services, and says it competes in an intensely competitive industry. Its roots go back to a partnership founded in 1939 by William Hewlett and David Packard.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in the technology industry that could hurt its sales and margins.
    • Long sales cycles and large orders that make quarterly revenue swing widely.
    • Supply chain problems that make planning and inventory harder to manage.
    • Not achieving the expected benefits of its cost reduction actions.
    • Its debt, and uncertainty about whether it will keep paying dividends.
    From Hewlett Packard Enterprise's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
