    What AGCO does

    AGCO makes farm machinery and precision agriculture technology under the Fendt, Massey Ferguson, PTx and Valtra brands. Its main products are tractors, from compact models under 40 horsepower to high horsepower tractors for large row-crop farms. It also makes combines, hay and forage equipment, planters, tillage implements and other application equipment, and parts. Its precision agriculture tools include retrofit kits that work across mixed fleets of machines.

    It sells most of its products through about 2,800 independent dealers and distributors in about 140 countries. It also offers retail and wholesale financing to buyers through finance joint ventures with Rabobank. Tractors made up the largest share of its net sales in 2025, and its parts and service business is another line. AGCO says dealers' retail sales to farmers are highly seasonal and follow planting and harvesting seasons.

    In 2024 AGCO changed its mix of businesses through the PTx Trimble joint venture and the sale of most of its Grain & Protein business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on the agricultural industry, so that anything that hurts farmers hurts its results.
    • Seasonal swings in sales that significantly affect its performance.
    • Most sales depending on financing being available to retail customers.
    • Strong competition from other makers of agricultural equipment.
    • Failing to deliver precision agriculture and other new technology that customers want.
    From AGCO's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
