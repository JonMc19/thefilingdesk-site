    What KBR does

    KBR provides science, technology, engineering and logistics support to the U.S. federal government, allied nations and commercial clients. Its work includes defense and national security systems engineering, space operations and satellite support, aviation support, cyber and data analytics, scientific research, and engineering and project management for energy projects.

    It has two core segments. Mission Technology Solutions supports defense, intelligence, space, aviation and other government programs, and it includes LinQuest, an engineering and data company acquired in fiscal 2024. Sustainable Technology Solutions licenses more than 85 proprietary process technologies that aim to cut emissions or improve efficiency in areas such as ammonia, chemicals and refining. A Corporate segment sits outside the core.

    Government agencies are its main customers, so its results depend on winning and keeping contracts. It also does part of its work through joint ventures and partnerships, and it uses subcontractors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a few large customers and on U.S. and foreign government agencies.
    • Winning new contracts on time, and facing cost overruns on fixed-price contracts.
    • Backlog of unfilled orders that can be cut or canceled, so it may not be a reliable guide to future revenue.
    • Dependence on subcontractors, suppliers and equipment makers.
    • Liability claims and contract disputes that may exceed its insurance.
    From KBR's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
