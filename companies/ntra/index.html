    What Natera does

    Natera is a diagnostics company, based in Austin, Texas, that sells genetic tests run on blood samples. Its tests read cell-free DNA, fragments of DNA that float in the blood. It focuses on three areas. In oncology, its blood-based tests help guide cancer treatment decisions. In women's health, it offers prenatal tests and hereditary cancer screening. In organ health, it tests for kidney, heart and lung transplant rejection and for chronic kidney disease.

    It sells through its own sales force in the United States and through a global network of over 100 laboratory and distribution partners. Its labs are in Austin, San Carlos (California) and Boulder (Colorado). It processed about 3.5 million tests in 2025.

    Its revenue was $2,306.1 million in 2025, almost all from products. It has never been profitable: its net loss was $208.2 million in 2025. It says it faces intense competition in its field.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of net losses that it expects to continue for the near future.
    • Not growing sales of its tests and services as fast as planned.
    • Intense competition from other diagnostics companies.
    • Its reliance on third-party laboratories and on data centers for its software.
    • Its tests not working as expected, or its own labs being unable to run.
    From Natera's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
