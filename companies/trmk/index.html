    What Trustmark does

    Trustmark Corporation is a bank holding company based in Jackson, Mississippi. Its main subsidiary, Trustmark Bank, was first chartered in 1889 and had total assets of $18.923 billion at the end of 2025. The bank has offices in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Mississippi, Tennessee and Texas, and it had 2,543 full-time equivalent associates.

    Trustmark reports two segments, General Banking and Wealth Management. The bank makes commercial loans to businesses, including loans for commercial real estate and construction, and takes deposits and offers treasury management services. It offers consumers checking and savings accounts, installment and real estate loans, and mortgage lending. Wealth Management provides trust, investment, brokerage, retirement plan and custody services. A subsidiary also provides funding for projects in low-income communities.

    The company says its largest source of revenue is net interest income, the difference between what it earns on loans and securities and what it pays on deposits and borrowings. It converted from a national bank to a Mississippi-chartered bank in August 2025 and changed its name to Trustmark Bank.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its main revenue, net interest income, depends on interest rate changes it cannot predict.
    • Lending risk: borrowers may not repay, and its allowance for credit losses may prove too low.
    • Strong competition in financial services and the soundness of other financial institutions.
    • Heavy regulation, including capital requirements and limits on paying dividends.
    • Hurricanes and other natural disasters in the Gulf region where it operates, and cyber breaches.
    From Trustmark's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
