    What MP Materials does

    MP Materials mines and processes rare earth elements, a group of metals used in the strong permanent magnets inside electric vehicle motors, wind turbines, robots, electronics and defense equipment. It owns the Mountain Pass mine and processing site in California and a magnet-making plant in Fort Worth, Texas, which began making finished magnets in December 2025.

    The company reports two segments. Materials covers mining and refining at Mountain Pass and sells mainly neodymium-praseodymium oxide and metal, mostly to customers in Japan, South Korea and the rest of Asia. In the past it earned most of its revenue selling rare earth concentrate to a distributor that resold it to refiners in China. Magnetics covers the Fort Worth plant, which began selling magnetic precursor products to General Motors in the first quarter of 2025.

    In July 2025 MP Materials signed agreements with the U.S. Department of War. Under them it will expand the Fort Worth plant, build a second magnet plant, and add heavy rare earth refining at Mountain Pass. The department also agreed to a minimum price for the company's neodymium-praseodymium products and has the right to buy all magnets from the second plant.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in demand for, and prices of, rare earth materials and magnet materials.
    • Its agreements with the Department of War could be changed or challenged, and they limit some company actions.
    • Mountain Pass is its only rare earth mining and processing site, and it must reach its planned refining output.
    • Competition, including from rival producers and from substitutes for its materials.
    • Trade and political developments, especially involving China, that affect its customers and supply chains.
    From MP Materials's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
