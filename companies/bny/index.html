    BNY Mellon financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Bank of New York Mellon Corp · NYSE: BNY Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 25, 2026 · 0001390777-26-000033 Updated October 7, 2026

    BNY Mellon's revenue rose 7.8% to $20.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $5.5 billion, up 22.5%. Its return on equity was 13.0%, up from 11.0% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 65.0%, down from 68.2% a year earlier. It paid out $5.0 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 4.2%.
