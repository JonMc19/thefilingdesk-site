    What Brookdale Senior Living does

    Brookdale operates senior living communities in the United States. At the end of 2025 it ran 584 communities in 41 states, with room for about 51,000 residents. They include independent living, assisted living, memory care and continuing care retirement communities, which offer several levels of care in one place so residents can stay as their needs change.

    It earns most of its revenue from fees paid by residents. Its 10-K says its revenue depends on private pay sources, with some payments from government programs. Brookdale owned 370 of its communities, leased 178 and managed 36 for others. It competes with other senior living providers, and says new construction by competitors can reduce its occupancy.

    Brookdale describes itself as an operating company. It says it focuses on running its communities well, and it is working on its technology systems, including a new enterprise resource planning system for accounting and operations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Residents may be unable to afford its fees, because its revenue depends on private payment.
    • New senior housing construction and lower industry occupancy could hurt its occupancy and revenue.
    • Its communities are concentrated in certain regions, leaving it open to local downturns.
    • Resident agreements ending and empty units in leased communities could cut revenue.
    • Debt, lease and interest payments could lead to defaults if its cash flow falls short.
