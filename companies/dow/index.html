    What Dow does

    Dow is a chemicals and materials company. It makes plastics, chemicals and coatings that go into packaging, buildings and infrastructure, cars and consumer products. It runs manufacturing sites in 29 countries, employs about 34,600 people, and had sales of approximately $40 billion in 2025. Dow Inc. is a holding company that runs all its businesses through a subsidiary, The Dow Chemical Company.

    Dow has three operating segments. Packaging & Specialty Plastics makes ethylene, a basic chemical building block, and polyethylene and related plastics used in food packaging, pipe, health and hygiene products and wire and cable. Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure makes chemicals from ethylene oxide and propylene oxide, used in appliances, coatings, furniture, construction and cleaning products. Performance Materials & Coatings uses acrylic, cellulose and silicone technology for paints and coatings, home and personal care, and electronics.

    Dow shares ownership of several plants with partners, including joint ventures in Kuwait, Thailand and Saudi Arabia. Its key raw materials include natural gas, ethane, naphtha and other hydrocarbons. It lists competitors including ExxonMobil, INEOS, LyondellBasell, SABIC, Shell and Sinopec. Its subsidiary Union Carbide faces asbestos-related lawsuits, and the company put its asbestos-related liability at $708 million at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the balance of supply and demand for its products, which change its earnings.
    • The cost and availability of raw materials and energy.
    • Climate change rules and the cost of meeting its climate commitments.
    • Tighter rules on the safe use of chemicals and plastics and their effect on the environment.
    • Asbestos-related lawsuits against its Union Carbide subsidiary, and other legal claims.
    From Dow's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2017 to 2024, earnings per share grew 14.7% a year and net income 13.3% a year, as buybacks cut the share count by 5%. Dow then lost money in fiscal 2025.
