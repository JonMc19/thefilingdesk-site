    What Pattern Group does

    Pattern is an ecommerce company that helps brands sell their products on online marketplaces such as Amazon. Its technology and experts work across more than 70 marketplaces to sell to consumers in more than 100 countries, and it uses machine learning and AI to manage advertising, content, pricing, forecasting and customer service.

    Most of its revenue comes from buying products from its brand partners and selling them to consumers on those marketplaces. Pattern says its platform sits as a thin layer between brands, marketplaces and consumers, and that selling directly to shoppers lets it control content, pricing, logistics and customer service and collect marketplace data to improve its models.

    Pattern says brands find marketplace selling complicated, with advertising, pricing, inventory, fulfillment and marketplace rules to manage, and many lack the scale, technology or skills to do it well alone. At the end of 2025 it employed about 2,300 full-time employees and engaged about 420 contractors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on its relationship with Amazon and other marketplaces, and on any limits they place on selling brand partners' products.
    • Losing access to the marketplace data its technology uses, or relying on data that is wrong.
    • Losing key brand partners or failing to sign new ones.
    • Misjudging consumer demand and holding too much or too little inventory.
    • Relying on its two co-founders, who are married, and on its senior team.
    From Pattern Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
