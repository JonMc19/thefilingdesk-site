    What Pilgrim's Pride does

    Pilgrim's Pride produces, processes, markets and distributes fresh, frozen and value-added chicken and pork products to retailers, distributors and foodservice operators. JBS S.A., through its subsidiaries, owns 82.28% of its common stock.

    It reports three segments: the U.S., Europe and Mexico. It sells chicken in all three and also pork and lamb in the U.K. Fresh products include whole and cut-up chicken and case-ready packs for grocery meat counters. Prepared products include breaded and marinated chicken, nuggets, sausages, bacon and deli meats. It also exports products. Customers include chain restaurants and grocery chains, such as Chick-fil-A, Kroger, Costco, McDonald's, Tesco and Wal-Mart in Mexico, across the U.S., the U.K., Europe, Mexico and over 120 other countries.

    The company is vertically integrated, which it says lets it control nearly every step of production. Its network includes about 4,500 growers, 36 feed mills, 50 hatcheries and 39 processing plants.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the prices of feed ingredients, chicken and pork, which make earnings cyclical.
    • Outbreaks of animal diseases such as avian influenza and African swine fever.
    • Product contamination that could lead to liability claims and recalls.
    • Special risks of operating in foreign markets.
    • Competition from other vertically integrated chicken and pork companies.
    From Pilgrim's Pride's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
