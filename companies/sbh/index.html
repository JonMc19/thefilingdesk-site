    What Sally Beauty does

    Sally Beauty Holdings is a specialty retailer and distributor of professional beauty supplies. It sells hair care, hair color, styling tools and nail products. It operates two segments.

    Sally Beauty sells to retail shoppers and salon professionals in North America, South America and Europe, mainly through its own stores under the Sally Beauty name and through its websites, a mobile app and third-party online marketplaces. Beauty Systems Group (BSG) is a distributor that sells only to salons and licensed beauty professionals in the United States, including Puerto Rico, and Canada. It uses company-operated Cosmo Prof stores, franchised stores, salon business consultants and digital platforms.

    It does not make the products it sells. It buys them from recognized brand makers and private label fillers, and says it depends on a limited number of manufacturers for a large part of what it sells. It says its core strength is professional hair color.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition in an industry that is consolidating.
    • Failing to keep up with changing consumer tastes and buying habits.
    • Reliance on a limited number of manufacturers, and the loss of exclusive distribution rights from key vendors.
    • Price and supply changes for inventory that it may not be able to pass on to customers.
    • Diversion of professional products sold by BSG, and e-commerce that could pull sales from its stores.
    From Sally Beauty's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
