    What Madison Square Garden Entertainment does

    MSG Entertainment runs live entertainment venues and shows. Its venues are Madison Square Garden, the Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden, Radio City Music Hall, the Beacon Theatre and The Chicago Theatre. It also produces the Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes.

    It reports one segment. Its business has two parts: its own production, the Christmas Spectacular, and a bookings business that brings concerts, family shows, special events and sporting events to its venues each year, drawing millions of guests. It says it is helped by its presence in New York and its ties to performers and event organizers.

    The company was set up in 2022 as a unit of Sphere Entertainment, then separated in April 2023 when Sphere Entertainment gave most of its shares to its own stockholders. Sphere Entertainment has since sold the rest of its stake. In June 2025 the company changed its legal home from Delaware to Nevada. Its fiscal year ends on June 30.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition for audiences, performers and events.
    • Relying on the continued popularity of the Christmas Spectacular and on the events booked at its venues.
    • Needing licenses from third parties to perform music at its venues, and losing them or renewing on worse terms.
    • Losing or changing a New York City real estate tax exemption for the Madison Square Garden complex.
    • Leasing some of its venues, and being hit by a downturn in the economy.
    From Madison Square Garden Entertainment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
