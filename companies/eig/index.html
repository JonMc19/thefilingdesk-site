    What Employers does

    Employers Holdings is an insurance holding company based in Reno, Nevada. Its subsidiaries specialize in workers' compensation insurance, which pays for medical care and lost wages when employees are hurt at work. They focus on small and mid-sized businesses in lower-hazard industries. In February 2026 it launched an excess workers' compensation product for self-insured enterprises.

    It makes money by selling policies and collecting premiums, and by investing the premiums until claims are paid. It sells through traditional insurance agents, specialty agents, brokers and other distribution partners. It sells only one line of insurance. It operates in all states except North Dakota, Ohio, Washington and Wyoming, which are served only by state funds. Its business is concentrated in California.

    It sells through five insurance companies, each of which has a financial strength rating of A (Excellent) from the rating firm AM Best.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Pricing its policies too low could badly hurt its results.
    • Selling a single line of insurance in a competitive market limits its ability to charge adequate rates.
    • Its business is concentrated in California, tying results to that state's economy and rules.
    • Its estimate of future claims could prove too low.
    • A downgrade in its financial strength rating could cut the business it can write.
