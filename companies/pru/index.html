    What Prudential Financial does

    Prudential Financial is a financial services company based in Newark, New Jersey, with operations in the United States, Asia, Europe and Latin America. It sells life insurance, annuities, retirement-related products and services, mutual funds and investment management to individuals and institutions, through its own and third-party distribution networks. It reports about $1.609 trillion in assets under management.

    Its main operations are PGIM, its global investment management business; its U.S. businesses, made up of Retirement Strategies, Group Insurance and Individual Life; its International Businesses; a Closed Block division; and Corporate and Other. The Closed Block holds certain older participating insurance and annuity products, set aside when the main insurer, The Prudential Insurance Company of America, converted from a policyholder-owned company to a stock company in 2001.

    The 10-K says its results depend on the value of its investment portfolios, on insurance risks such as how long customers live, and on how policyholders behave.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losses if issuers default or the value of its investment portfolios falls.
    • Insurance risks, such as customers dying or becoming ill sooner or more often than it priced for.
    • Profit on products and fees that depend on interest rates, markets and exchange rates.
    • Not having enough liquid cash to meet its obligations.
    • Events that change its basic business model, such as shifts in how customers buy or in the rules it works under.
    From Prudential Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
