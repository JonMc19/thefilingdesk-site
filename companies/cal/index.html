    What Caleres does

    Caleres is a global footwear company, founded in 1878 as Brown Shoe Company. It runs shoe stores and e-commerce sites, and it designs, sources, makes and distributes footwear for all ages. Women's footwear makes up the largest share of its net sales, followed by men's, children's, and clothing and accessories.

    It reports two segments. Famous Footwear is a family retailer with 821 stores at the end of 2025 plus two websites, selling mainly brand-name footwear from makers such as Nike, Skechers, adidas, Crocs and Converse, along with some company-owned and licensed brands.

    Brand Portfolio designs, sources and sells branded, licensed and private-label footwear, wholesale to online retailers, national chains, department stores and others, and through its own stores and websites. Its brands include Sam Edelman, Naturalizer, Allen Edmonds and Stuart Weitzman. At the end of 2025 it had 81 stores in the United States, four in Canada and 103 in East and Southeast Asia, and franchise partners ran 148 international branded stores.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • U.S. and international trade policy, including tariffs and trade restrictions.
    • Weaker consumer demand, inflation and supply chain disruptions.
    • Failing to anticipate fashion trends and consumer preferences.
    • Loss of major branded suppliers, or sales lost through customer concentration.
    • Heavy competition and pricing pressure, and swings in currency rates that raise costs.
