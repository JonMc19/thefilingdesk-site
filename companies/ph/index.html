    What Parker Hannifin does

    Parker-Hannifin designs and makes motion and control products: parts and systems that move, control and seal equipment, such as hydraulic, pneumatic, filtration and engineered materials products. It also provides aftermarket support. Customers are mainly in aerospace and defense, industrial equipment, transportation, off-highway vehicles, energy, and heating, cooling and refrigeration. It was incorporated in Ohio in 1938.

    It has two reportable segments. Of its $21.5 billion in net sales for fiscal 2026, the Diversified Industrial segment made up 67% and the Aerospace Systems segment made up 33%. Diversified Industrial sells to equipment makers and to distributors that serve the replacement market. Aerospace Systems sells to commercial transport, regional, defense, helicopter and business jet customers.

    Parker runs a decentralized operating structure and a management system it calls the Win Strategy. It offers hundreds of thousands of individual part numbers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions around the world that cut demand for its products.
    • Swings in the price and supply of raw materials and components.
    • Needing heavy spending on new products to stay competitive.
    • Problems integrating acquired companies and managing joint ventures.
    • Debt and credit facility covenants that could limit its flexibility.
    From Parker Hannifin's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 21, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
