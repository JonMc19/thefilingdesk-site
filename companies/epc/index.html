    What Edgewell Personal Care does

    Edgewell makes and sells personal care products, mainly razors and shaving gels and creams, and sun and skin care products. Its brands include Schick, Wilkinson Sword, Edge, Skintimate and Billie for shaving, and Banana Boat, Hawaiian Tropic, Cremo and Wet Ones for sun and skin care. It operates in over 20 countries and sells in more than 50.

    It reports two segments: Wet Shave and Sun and Skin Care. It sells mostly through retailers. Walmart is its only customer named as a large share of sales, at 18.8% of net sales in fiscal 2026. Edgewell says it holds the number two global market share position in wet shave, and that Banana Boat and Hawaiian Tropic together lead the US sun care category. Competition for shelf space and shoppers' loyalty is strong.

    On February 2, 2026, Edgewell sold its Feminine Care business to Essity. That business is now shown as discontinued operations, so the company is more focused on shaving and sun and skin care.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher costs for raw materials and tariffs could cut its profit margins.
    • Changes in US and international trade policies could hurt its business.
    • Losing a principal customer, such as Walmart, or changes in what its retail customers do.
    • Competition in shaving and sun care that could cost it customers and shelf space.
    • Product recalls or liability claims that could harm its brands and results.
    From Edgewell Personal Care's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
