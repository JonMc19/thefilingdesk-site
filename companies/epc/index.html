    What Edgewell Personal Care does

    Edgewell makes and sells personal care products. It has three segments: Wet Shave, Sun and Skin Care, and Feminine Care. Its shaving brands include Schick, Wilkinson Sword, Hydro, Quattro, Intuition, Skintimate and Edge. In sun care it sells Banana Boat and Hawaiian Tropic. It also owns Wet Ones wipes and feminine care brands such as Playtex, Stayfree, Carefree and o.b.

    Edgewell operates in about 20 countries and its products are sold in more than 50. Its biggest customer is Walmart, which accounted for approximately 17.4% of its net sales in fiscal 2025. In wet shave it names The Procter & Gamble Company (Gillette), The Bic Group and Dorco as its main competitors.

    The company has grown mostly by buying brands. It was spun off from Ralston Purina in 2000 and in 2015 separated its battery and lighting business into a different company. On November 6, 2025 its board approved an agreement to sell its Feminine Care segment.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher production costs, including raw materials and tariffs, that could cut its margins.
    • Changes in U.S. and international trade policy.
    • Strong competition that could hurt its sales and its relationships with retailers.
    • Failure to attract and keep key people.
    • Increasing product and environmental regulation around the world.
    From Edgewell Personal Care's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
