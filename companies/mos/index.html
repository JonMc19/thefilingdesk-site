    Mosaic

    MOS · NYSE · Materials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Mosaic's revenue rose 8.4% to $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 6.8%, up from 5.6% a year earlier. Free cash flow was −$535 million, against $47 million a year earlier. It paid out $280 million in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 0.6%.

    How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation.
