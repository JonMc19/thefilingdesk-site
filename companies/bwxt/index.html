    What BWX Technologies does

    BWX Technologies makes nuclear components and has operated for more than 100 years. Its core work is designing and building precision parts for naval nuclear ships, along with nuclear reactors and fuel, for the U.S. Government. It also processes special nuclear materials, restores environmental sites, serves the nuclear power industry and supplies medical radioisotopes and radiopharmaceuticals.

    It has two reportable segments. Government Operations makes naval reactors, components and fuel for the Department of Energy's Naval Nuclear Propulsion Program, and supplies valves, manifolds and fittings to naval and commercial shipping customers. It is also the largest domestic supplier of research reactor fuel elements for colleges, universities and national laboratories. Commercial Operations covers its work for commercial customers, such as the nuclear power industry and medical isotopes.

    The company relies on large contracts, so a small number of projects can be a big part of its work at any time. Much of its backlog depends on U.S. Government demand for nuclear components, including nuclear-powered submarines and aircraft carriers for the Navy.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in the federal budget or government spending that fund many of its contracts.
    • Large U.S. Government contracts that come up for renewal and competitive rebidding.
    • Fixed-price contracts where actual costs may run above its estimates.
    • Reliance on a small number of big projects for a large part of its operations.
    • Losing skilled workers, or labor disputes and work stoppages.
    From BWX Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
