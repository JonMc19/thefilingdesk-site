    What Okta does

    Okta sells cloud software that controls who can log in to which apps and data. Its two platforms, Okta and Auth0, handle "identity": checking that employees, contractors, partners and customers are who they say they are, and giving them the right access. Companies use the Okta Platform to manage workforce access, and both platforms to secure the logins of their own customers. Developers use Auth0 to build login features into the software they make.

    Okta earns most of its revenue by selling multi-year subscriptions to these cloud services. It sells through its own field and inside sales teams and through channel partners such as cloud marketplaces, resellers and system integrators. As of January 31, 2026, more than 20,000 customers used its products, from large enterprises to small businesses, universities, nonprofits and government agencies. It also lists over 7,000 integrations with other apps and security vendors.

    About 20% of its revenue came from outside the United States in fiscal 2026. Okta says it faces competition, especially from larger, well-established companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep customers renewing and buying more subscriptions.
    • Intense competition, especially from larger, better-established companies.
    • Falling behind in fast-changing technology, including the use of AI.
    • Its reliance on channel partners to grow sales.
    • Sales to public sector entities, which carry their own challenges and risks.
    From Okta's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
