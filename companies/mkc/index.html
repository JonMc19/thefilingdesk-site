    What McCormick does

    McCormick makes and sells herbs, spices, seasoning mixes, condiments and other flavor products. Its customers include retailers, food manufacturers and foodservice businesses. Its brands include McCormick, French's, Frank's RedHot, Lawry's, Cholula, Club House, Gourmet Garden, OLD BAY and Zatarain's. It reaches consumers in about 150 countries and territories.

    It has two segments. The Consumer segment sells branded products to shoppers in stores and online. The Flavor Solutions segment sells flavors and ingredients to food and beverage makers and restaurants. In 2025 the Consumer segment brought in about 58% of net sales and 67% of operating income, and Flavor Solutions about 42% and 33%.

    McCormick also has joint ventures, the largest being McCormick de Mexico. In January 2026 it bought an additional share in it and now holds a controlling interest. Its major sales, distribution and production sites are in North America, Europe and China.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Pressure from large retail customers, including customer consolidation, and losing a large customer.
    • Trouble getting raw materials, and supply chain disruptions.
    • Changes in global trade policies, such as tariffs.
    • Competition in its markets.
    • Labor shortages, turnover and rising labor costs.
    From McCormick's annual report on Form 10-K, filed January 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
