    What Aurinia Pharmaceuticals does

    Aurinia sells LUPKYNIS (voclosporin), a pill for adults with active lupus nephritis, a serious kidney disease caused by lupus. The company says it was the first FDA-approved oral therapy for this condition, in January 2021. It is also developing aritinercept for other autoimmune diseases.

    In the United States Aurinia sells LUPKYNIS to two specialty pharmacies and a specialty distributor. For Europe and Japan it sells inventory to its partner Otsuka under a licensing deal, which also pays milestones and royalties. In 2025 its net product sales were $271.3 million. Lonza makes voclosporin for it under contract.

    Aurinia is therefore closely tied to one product. It also repurchased some of its own shares in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending heavily on the commercial success of LUPKYNIS, its one product.
    • Its sales in Europe and Japan depend on its partner Otsuka meeting its contract duties.
    • Pricing rules and insurance coverage may limit what it earns from LUPKYNIS.
    • Patents covering LUPKYNIS may not hold up, and other companies may claim it infringes theirs.
    • Drug development is long and uncertain, and early aritinercept results may not carry over.
    From Aurinia Pharmaceuticals's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
