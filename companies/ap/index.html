    What Ampco Pittsburgh does

    Ampco-Pittsburgh makes specialty metal products and customized equipment for industry around the world. It was incorporated in Pennsylvania in 1929 and reports two segments: Forged and Cast Engineered Products and Air and Liquid Processing.

    The first segment makes forged hardened steel rolls and cast rolls, which steel, aluminum and other metal producers use in their rolling mills. It also makes forged engineered products for the steel distribution, oil and gas, and aluminum and plastic extrusion industries. It operates in the United States, Sweden and Slovenia and has stakes in two joint ventures in China. It competes with companies in Europe, Asia and the Americas.

    The Air and Liquid Processing segment includes Aerofin (heat exchange coils), Buffalo Air Handling and Buffalo Pumps, with operations in Virginia and New York. It sells through independent sales offices in the United States and Canada and says it has several major competitors. It says demand is rising from power generation and U.S. military markets.

    In October 2025 its British roll subsidiary, Union Electric Steel UK, entered administration, a formal insolvency process, and is being wound down.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It needs enough cash to cover operations, debt payments and asbestos claim payments.
    • Demand for its products is cyclical and can fall in economic downturns.
    • Excess steel-making capacity worldwide could push down prices for its products.
    • Its subsidiaries could face large liability for asbestos injury claims tied to older products.
    • Restructuring may not deliver the expected benefits, and it may struggle to match its capacity to demand.
