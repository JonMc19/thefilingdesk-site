    What USA Rare Earth does

    USA Rare Earth wants to build a supply chain for rare earth materials, from a mine to finished magnets, in the United States. Rare earth elements are used in magnets and other parts for semiconductors, radar and other advanced equipment. It plans to make rare earth oxides, metals, alloys and permanent magnets.

    It says it holds mining rights to one of North America's largest recoverable deposits of heavy rare earth elements, including dysprosium, terbium and yttrium, at its Round Top Project. It is developing a magnet factory in Stillwater, Oklahoma and a processing and separation plant in Wheat Ridge, Colorado, and has not yet started selling neo magnets.

    In January 2026 it signed a letter of intent for a collaboration with the U.S. government, which is expected to provide a total of $1.6 billion in direct funding awards and loans, subject to definitive agreements and milestones.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its Stillwater magnet facility is not finished, and it has no history of commercial operations.
    • Delays, extra costs and complications in developing its projects, and research that may not reach its goals.
    • Relying on outside supplies of rare earth materials until its Round Top Project can provide them.
    • Needing large amounts of capital, having negative operating cash flow, and not yet being profitable.
    • Changes in demand for and prices of magnets, magnet materials and feedstock.
