    What Dominion Energy does

    Dominion Energy is a utility company based in Richmond, Virginia. It mainly provides regulated electricity service in Virginia, North Carolina and South Carolina through two subsidiaries, Virginia Power and Dominion Energy South Carolina (DESC). It also develops and runs regulated offshore wind and solar power, and has some nonregulated operations that include long-term contracted electric generation.

    It runs its daily operations through three main segments: Dominion Energy Virginia, Dominion Energy South Carolina and Contracted Energy. A fourth group, Corporate and Other, holds corporate functions and unallocated debt. The Virginia segment serves about 2.8 million residential, commercial, industrial and government customers, including data centers. The South Carolina segment sells electricity to about 0.8 million customers and natural gas to about 0.5 million customers.

    In 2024 Dominion sold most of its regulated gas distribution businesses to Enbridge. It says data centers, many of them in Loudoun County, Virginia, drive much of the growth in demand for power and require large investments in power lines and generation.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Delays, cost increases or failure to finish its large Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project.
    • Not being able to recover some project costs through the rates regulators allow.
    • Heavy investment needed to meet fast-growing power demand from data centers.
    • Regulatory penalties if it is found not to comply with laws, or from new laws and tariffs.
