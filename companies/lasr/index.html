    What nLight does

    nLIGHT designs, makes and sells high-power lasers. Its customers are mostly companies that build laser systems or manufacturing tools and put nLIGHT's lasers inside them. It sells into three end markets: aerospace and defense, industrial, and microfabrication, meaning the making of very small parts.

    It reports two segments. Laser Products sells semiconductor lasers, which are built on chips made from gallium arsenide, and fiber lasers, which use a special fiber to amplify the light from those chips into a stronger beam. For defense customers it also makes high-energy pulsed and continuous-wave fiber lasers, fiber amplifiers and beam control systems for directed energy weapons and laser sensing systems. Advanced Development handles development work, often under government contracts.

    The company says its vertical integration, meaning it makes key parts itself, lets it build different products on the same core technology. Some of its U.S. government contracts are classified, which limits what it can tell investors about that part of the business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition in its markets, and falling average selling prices for lasers.
    • Changes in the markets it serves, and the need to keep up with rapid technological change.
    • Depending on winning new U.S. government awards and design wins, some of them classified.
    • High fixed costs and inventory levels that can hurt its profits when sales fall.
    • Reliance on third parties to make some of its products and parts.
