    What Eastern Bankshares does

    Eastern Bankshares is a bank holding company based in Boston, Massachusetts. Its only direct subsidiary is Eastern Bank, a Massachusetts-chartered bank founded in 1818. Through the bank it offers lending, deposit, and trust and wealth management services.

    It makes money mainly by lending the deposits it gathers through its branch network. Its loans include commercial and industrial loans (among them asset-based lending), commercial real estate, commercial construction, small business, residential real estate and home equity loans. Deposit products include checking, money market and savings accounts and certificates of deposit.

    Through Cambridge Trust Wealth Management, a division of the bank, it provides wealth management and trust services. It also offers lock box collection, cash management and account reconciliation services to corporate and institutional customers, and cash management to municipal clients. Federal and state banking regulators supervise it, and it has recently acquired HarborOne.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in interest rates could hurt its profits and financial condition.
    • If its set-aside for loan losses is too small, its earnings and capital could fall.
    • Its loans are concentrated in one region, so a local downturn could hurt it.
    • It may not gain the expected benefits from the HarborOne deal, or may struggle to integrate it.
    • Harm to its reputation could cost it customers, investors and employees.
