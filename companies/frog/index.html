    What JFrog does

    JFrog sells a software platform that companies use to store, secure, manage and deliver the software they build. It calls this a system of record for the software supply chain. The JFrog Platform serves developers, security teams, machine learning engineers and AI agents, with tools for governance, security and delivery of applications. JFrog Artifactory, which stores and manages software packages, is at the center of the platform.

    JFrog earns most of its revenue from subscriptions, and it recognizes a significant portion over the length of each subscription. It says the majority of the Fortune 500 use its platform. Its customers are software-driven businesses that release code often and need to keep it secure and compliant.

    JFrog says it competes with current and future competitors, some of which have greater financial, technical and other resources than it does.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Slower growth, and a history of losses, so it may not be consistently profitable.
    • Heavy reliance on JFrog Artifactory: weaker demand for it would affect the whole business.
    • Customers not renewing subscriptions or buying more.
    • Competition from companies with greater resources.
    • Markets for its products evolving more slowly or differently than it expects.
    From JFrog's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
