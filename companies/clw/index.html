    What Clearwater Paper does

    Clearwater Paper makes paperboard, the stiff paper used for packaging. Its main product is solid bleached sulfate (SBS) paperboard, which independent converters print and shape into folding cartons and food service items such as cups and plates. It also sells small amounts of pulp and does custom sheeting and slitting of paperboard.

    The company sells to converters across North America and has all of its plants in the continental United States. It calls itself an independent, non-integrated supplier, meaning it does not turn the board into finished packages itself. It says it is one of the five largest paperboard producers in North America, with about 11% of available production capacity in 2025. It says it competes with other SBS makers and also with other kinds of paperboard.

    The company changed shape in 2024. In May it bought a paperboard mill in Augusta, Georgia from Graphic Packaging. In October it sold its private label tissue business to Sofidel America, which left paperboard as its only line of business. It still shares some facilities in Lewiston, Idaho with Sofidel.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak demand for paperboard, and added industry supply that can hurt its results and cash flow.
    • Making mainly one kind of product, so it is exposed to customers switching to other paperboard grades.
    • Losing, or getting lower prices or orders from, its large customers.
    • Competitors with more money than it has, and consolidation among paperboard makers and converters.
    • Costs of keeping its mills running, and harm from any interruption at its facilities.
    From Clearwater Paper's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
