    What FirstEnergy does

    FirstEnergy is an electric utility holding company. Its subsidiaries move and deliver electricity and, in West Virginia, also generate some of it. Its electric companies serve over six million customers in Ohio, Pennsylvania, West Virginia, Maryland, New Jersey and New York, across about 65,000 square miles. Its transmission operations include more than 24,000 miles of high-voltage lines.

    Most of its business is regulated: state and federal regulators set the rates it can charge customers, and it earns money on the value of the assets, called the rate base, that regulators let it recover through rates. The 10-K gives the rate base of its electric companies as approximately $21.3 billion at December 31, 2025. Its main subsidiaries are local distribution utilities in Ohio, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland and West Virginia, plus a transmission company, FirstEnergy Transmission, of which FirstEnergy owns a slim majority and Brookfield owns the rest.

    One subsidiary, Monongahela Power, owns or contractually controls generation capacity that it supplies to its customers in West Virginia, including solar and a share of a pumped-storage hydroelectric plant in Virginia. FirstEnergy is also a partner in Valley Link, a joint transmission venture formed in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Securities class-action lawsuits against it could harm its reputation, finances and its access to capital.
    • Federal and state regulators can delay or deny recovery of its costs, and rising customer rates may limit the returns it can earn.
    • Its plans to grow its distribution and transmission businesses depend on many things outside its control.
    • Cost-saving initiatives may not produce the benefits it expects.
    • Construction projects and operating its equipment may run over budget or schedule, or fail.
    From FirstEnergy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
