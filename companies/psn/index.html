    What Parsons does

    Parsons provides technology, engineering and project management services to governments and infrastructure owners. It works in six end markets: cyber and electronic warfare, space and missile defense, critical infrastructure protection, transportation, water and environment, and urban development. It says it has been in business for more than 81 years.

    It has two reportable segments. Federal Solutions supplies technology and engineering to the US government, including cyber, air and missile defense, intelligence, electronic warfare, space ground systems and border security work. Critical Infrastructure provides program management, planning, design and engineering for transportation, bridges, roads, rail and transit, water and urban projects for federal, state and local customers and private infrastructure owners. In fiscal 2025 Federal Solutions made up 51% of revenue and Critical Infrastructure 49%.

    Parsons earns money by winning contracts for systems integration, program management, design engineering and product development, where it builds software and hardware for defense, security and infrastructure customers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in government budgets, spending and priorities that could reduce future revenue.
    • Depending on the US federal government as its largest single customer.
    • Penalties, or being barred from government contracts, if it breaks complex procurement rules.
    • Government audits and cost reviews that could lower profit or cash flow.
    • Profit that varies with its mix of contracts and how well it estimates costs.
    From Parsons's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
