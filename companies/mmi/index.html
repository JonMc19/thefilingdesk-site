    What Marcus & Millichap does

    Marcus & Millichap is a brokerage for commercial real estate. Its agents help owners sell apartment buildings, offices, shops and other investment properties, represent buyers, and arrange loans. At the end of 2025 it had 1,808 brokerage and financing professionals, mostly commission-based independent contractors, in over 80 offices in the United States and Canada.

    It earns its money from commissions when a property sells and from fees when a property is financed. In 2025, about 84% of revenue came from brokerage commissions, 14% from financing fees and 2% from other work such as consulting and advisory services.

    The company focuses on the private client market, meaning properties priced from $1 million to under $10 million. About 64% of its 2025 brokerage commissions came from that market. It says these buyers and sellers are driven partly by personal circumstances, so they follow the economy less closely than large investors do. It competes with national firms focused on larger deals and with local and regional firms.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic and commercial property conditions, which cut sales volume.
    • Lower office demand as more people work from home or in a hybrid way.
    • Limited or costly debt and equity capital for property buyers.
    • A loss sharing duty to its lending partner if loans it sells go bad.
    • Competitors with more resources, and seasonal swings in results.
    From Marcus & Millichap's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2022, earnings per share grew 7.7% a year and net income 8.3% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 3% and diluted each share's earnings. Marcus & Millichap then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
