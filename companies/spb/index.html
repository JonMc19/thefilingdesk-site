    What Spectrum Brands does

    Spectrum Brands makes and sells branded household products. It is run in three segments: Global Pet Care, Home and Garden, and Home and Personal Care. Pet Care sells chews and treats, small animal food, dog and cat food, and aquarium products. Home and Garden sells household and outdoor insect and weed controls, repellents and cleaning products. Home and Personal Care sells small home appliances and personal care appliances.

    The company makes, markets and distributes its products in North America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, Latin America and Asia-Pacific. It sells through retailers, wholesalers and distributors, and a small number of key customers account for a large share of its sales. Some of its products are seasonal.

    Spectrum plans to separate its Home and Personal Care business into an independent, publicly traded company. It says the plan may not be completed on the expected timeline or at all.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The planned split of its Home and Personal Care business may be delayed, or may not bring the intended benefits.
    • Depending on a small number of large retail customers, and retailers consolidating.
    • Disruption in its global supply chain, including reliance on suppliers in China.
    • Competition in markets where it may lose share and sales.
    • Reliance on outside partners and outsourcing arrangements it does not control.
    From Spectrum Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 18, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
