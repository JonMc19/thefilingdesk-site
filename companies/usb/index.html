    U.S. Bancorp's revenue rose 4.4% to $28.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $7.6 billion, up 20.2%. Its return on equity was 12.2%, up from 11.1% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 58.8%, down from 62.6% a year earlier. It paid out $3.7 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 0.2%.
