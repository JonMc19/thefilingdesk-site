    What Advance Auto Parts does

    Advance Auto Parts sells replacement car parts, accessories, batteries and maintenance items in North America. Customers are professional installers, such as repair shops, and do-it-yourself drivers. Its stores carry brand-name, original equipment manufacturer and its own brands of parts for cars, vans, SUVs and light and heavy trucks.

    It sells through stores and e-commerce sites under two names. Advance Auto Parts stores serve both professional and do-it-yourself customers. Carquest stores, 239 of them including 157 in Canada, focus mainly on professional customers. A further 809 independently owned stores operate under the Carquest name. At the start of 2026 it had 4,305 company stores in total, after opening 39 and closing 522 in 2025.

    In November 2024 Advance sold its Worldpac business for net proceeds of about $1.44 billion to focus on its blended-box store model. It also has a restructuring plan from late 2024, which closed stores and cut jobs. It employed about 28,274 full-time and 25,733 part-time team members, mostly in stores.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not carrying out its business strategy successfully.
    • Problems and disruption from restructuring its operations.
    • Not realizing the expected benefits of past and possible sales of businesses.
    • Relying on suppliers, freight carriers and other outside parties in its supply chain.
    • Failing to attract, train and keep enough qualified store staff and leaders.
    From Advance Auto Parts's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
