    What Bread Financial does

    Bread Financial is a consumer lender. It issues credit cards and offers pay-over-time loans to people in the United States, and it also takes savings deposits. Most of its cards carry the name of a retailer or other brand partner, either as a private label card (usable only at that brand) or as a co-brand card (usable anywhere). It also sells its own Bread-branded general purpose cards and Bread Pay installment and split-pay loans.

    Its partners include travel, entertainment, jewelry, specialty apparel, sporting goods, health and beauty, and electronics and furniture companies, as well as small and medium-sized businesses. Bread Financial reports one segment. Its main source of revenue is interest and fees on the loans it makes, and to a lesser extent payments under its contracts with brand partners.

    At the end of 2025 the company had $18.8 billion in credit card and other loans, spread across about 34 million open accounts.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Customers who default on unsecured loans it cannot collect.
    • Depending on a limited number of brand partners for a large share of revenue.
    • A business concentrated in U.S. consumer credit.
    • An allowance for credit losses that may prove too small.
    • Fraud, and risk models that may not be accurate.
    From Bread Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
