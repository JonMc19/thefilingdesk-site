    What CarGurus does

    CarGurus runs online car marketplaces in the U.S., the U.K. and Canada. Shoppers use the sites to research, compare and find new and used cars. Dealers pay to list their inventory and to use CarGurus software for pricing, marketing and managing the cars they have for sale. CarGurus describes its platform as connecting a large audience of car shoppers with a network of dealers.

    Shoppers see a rating on each used car listing, from Great Deal to Overpriced. The rating comes from CarGurus' own estimate of the car's market value in the local market. The company also offers an AI-powered shopping assistant called CG Discover.

    CarGurus gets its revenue from three sources: dealer subscription fees, advertising from car makers and other brands, and partnerships with financing companies. In August 2025 its board decided to wind down CarOffer, its business that let dealers trade cars with one another and make instant cash offers. The wind-down was complete at the end of 2025, and CarGurus now reports one segment.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on dealers: losing subscribers, or dealers closing or merging, would cut its revenue.
    • Failing to keep or raise the number of paying dealers or the fees they pay.
    • Relying on internet search engines to send shoppers to its websites.
    • Problems with new products, such as failing to build ones consumers and dealers adopt.
    • Disputes over the accuracy of its pricing and valuation ratings.
    From CarGurus's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
