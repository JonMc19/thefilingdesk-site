    What W. W. Grainger does

    Grainger distributes maintenance, repair and operating (MRO) products and services: safety and security items, material handling and storage, pumps and plumbing equipment, cleaning and maintenance supplies, metalworking and hand tools. It serves more than 4.6 million customers, from small businesses to large corporations, government entities and institutions in industries such as commercial, healthcare and manufacturing. It says no single end customer was above 10% of sales in 2025.

    It has two reportable segments. High-Touch Solutions North America serves mid-size and large businesses with complex buying needs through its Grainger-branded businesses in the United States, Canada, Mexico and Puerto Rico, using sales and service representatives and an on-site inventory service called KeepStock. Endless Assortment is an online platform for smaller and mid-size businesses and includes Zoro in the U.S. and MonotaRO in Japan.

    Grainger buys its products from suppliers around the world. In the fourth quarter of 2025 it left the U.K. market by selling its Cromwell business and closing Zoro U.K.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Inflation that makes its costs grow faster than its sales.
    • Supply chain disruptions that hurt results.
    • Weakness in the economy and in the finances of its customers.
    • Commodity price swings that affect its profit margins.
    • Competition in a crowded industry, and the added risks of growing its online sales.
    From W. W. Grainger's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
