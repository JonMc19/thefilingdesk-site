    What Progressive does

    Progressive is an insurance holding company. Its insurance subsidiaries sell personal and commercial auto insurance, home and renters insurance, and coverage for motorcycles, boats and other recreational vehicles. They also sell business insurance, mostly to small businesses, including general liability and commercial property, workers' compensation insurance for the transportation industry, and other specialty coverage. It operates throughout the United States.

    Insurers earn money from premiums customers pay, less the claims and costs of running the business, and from investing the premiums until claims are paid. Progressive's Personal Lines segment, which sells personal auto, special vehicle and residential property insurance, brought in 87% of its net premiums written in 2025. Personal auto is its largest product. It says it ranked second in U.S. private passenger auto insurance by premiums written, in a market with about 230 competitors.

    Its insurance and claims operations are managed state by state, because insurance is regulated by each state, and supported by national operations. In California it runs a separate agency auto organization.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Pricing risks and setting aside loss reserves accurately, since errors could make its results worse.
    • Severe weather and other catastrophes, including effects of climate change, on its results.
    • Predicting its reinsurance needs and getting enough reinsurance coverage.
    • Highly competitive property and casualty insurance markets and rivals' new initiatives.
    • Adjusting claims accurately, and following a complex set of laws and regulations.
    From Progressive's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
