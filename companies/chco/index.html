    What City does

    City Holding is a financial holding company based in Charleston, West Virginia. It runs one bank, City National Bank of West Virginia, with 96 branches in West Virginia, Kentucky, Virginia and southeastern Ohio. The company reports one segment, community banking.

    The bank sells four kinds of services. Commercial banking covers business loans, commercial real estate loans, construction loans and cash management. Consumer banking covers checking, savings and money market accounts, certificates of deposit, retirement accounts, and installment and real estate loans. Mortgage banking covers fixed and adjustable-rate home loans and construction financing. Wealth and investment management covers trusts, estates, investment accounts and retirement plan services.

    Like most banks, it earns money mainly from the interest on its loans and securities, less what it pays on deposits, and from fees for its services. It offers credit cards and merchant card services through agreements with outside vendors. It says its customer base is diverse. The bank has 934 full-time equivalent employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak local economies and falling real estate values that leave loans under-collateralized.
    • Borrowers failing to repay loans, and an allowance for credit losses that may prove too small.
    • Changes in interest rates that affect its earnings.
    • Competition from other banks and from nonbank alternatives for customers.
    • Heavy regulation, which also limits the dividends it can pay.
    From City's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
