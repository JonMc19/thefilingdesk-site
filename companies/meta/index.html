    What Meta Platforms does

    Meta owns Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger and Threads, apps where people post photos, videos and updates, follow others and send messages. It also offers Meta AI, an assistant built into its apps and available on its own. Meta says an average of 3.58 billion people used at least one of its apps each day in December 2025. It also sells hardware: Meta Quest virtual reality headsets and AI glasses made with Ray-Ban and Oakley.

    Meta reports two segments. Family of Apps brings in substantially all of its revenue, by selling ads that businesses place across its apps and on other apps and websites. Most advertisers buy and manage these ads themselves through its online tools. Reality Labs sells headsets, glasses, software and content, and works on virtual and augmented reality and other long-term technology. It loses money: Meta says the segment cut its 2025 operating profit by about $19.19 billion. Meta says it competes with other online services for users' attention and with other ad sellers for marketers' budgets, as well as with other companies building advanced AI models.

    Meta had 78,865 employees at the end of 2025, with offices in more than 90 cities. It builds its own data centers and is spending heavily on computing power to develop AI. Its founder and CEO controls a majority of the shareholder votes.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing users, or users spending less time on its apps, which would weaken its ad business.
    • It earns nearly all its revenue from ads, so marketers cutting back their spending could seriously hurt it.
    • Its reliance on mobile operating systems and technologies it does not control, such as those made by Apple and Google.
    • Its heavy spending on AI and Reality Labs may not pay off and reduces its profit margins.
    • Government investigations, settlements and new rules on privacy, content and competition that could force changes to its products.
    From Meta Platforms's annual report on Form 10-K, filed January 29, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
