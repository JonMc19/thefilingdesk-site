    What Biglari Holdings does

    Biglari Holdings is a holding company whose subsidiaries run restaurants, property and casualty insurance and reinsurance, licensing and media, and oil and gas. Its largest businesses franchise and operate restaurants. Sardar Biglari founded the company, is its Chairman and CEO, and makes all major investment and capital allocation decisions, while managers run each unit's daily operations.

    Restaurants run through Steak n Shake and Western Sizzlin, with a combined 435 units at the end of 2025. Steak n Shake, founded in 1934, serves Steakburgers, fries and milkshakes. It had 131 company-run restaurants, 179 franchise partner units and 94 traditional franchise units. Western Sizzlin, a steak and buffet chain, had 3 company-run restaurants and 28 franchise units. Franchisees pay fees to the company.

    Its insurers include First Guard, which sells commercial truck insurance, and Southern Pioneer, which sells garage liability, commercial property and homeowners coverage. Its oil and gas units are Southern Oil, offshore in Louisiana state waters, and Abraxas Petroleum, in the Permian Basin.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Dependence on its Chairman and CEO, Sardar Biglari.
    • Mr. Biglari owns over half of its shares, so he can control votes by stockholders, and it uses exemptions as a controlled company.
    • Limits on its access to capital that could hurt its ability to meet cash needs.
    • Weather and other events that change how often or how severely insurance claims occur.
    • Oil and natural gas price swings, and competition across its businesses.
    From Biglari Holdings's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
