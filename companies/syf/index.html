    What Synchrony Financial does

    Synchrony is a consumer finance company that offers credit through retailers, manufacturers, healthcare providers and other businesses it calls partners. Its products include private label, dual, co-brand and general purpose credit cards, short- and long-term installment loans, and consumer banking products. It reaches shoppers through mobile apps, websites, online marketplaces and point-of-sale platforms as well as in stores. Its partners include Lowe's, Sam's Club, Amazon and PayPal.

    It makes money mainly from interest and fees on the loans it makes to consumers. During 2025 it financed $182.3 billion of purchase volume and ended the year with $103.8 billion of loan receivables. Partners promote its credit products because they can raise sales and loyalty. It reports one business segment, with five sales platforms organized by partner type: Home & Auto, Digital, Diversified & Value, Health & Wellness and Lifestyle. Nearly all of its revenue-generating activity is in the United States.

    It offers its credit products mostly through its wholly owned subsidiary, Synchrony Bank, which also takes FDIC-insured deposits such as savings accounts, money market accounts and certificates of deposit. Deposits were $81.1 billion at the end of 2025 and are its main funding source.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing partners, since a significant percentage of its interest and fees comes from a small number of large retail partners.
    • Heavy concentration in U.S. consumer credit, so results are tied to that market and its regulation.
    • An allowance for credit losses that may prove too low to cover loan losses.
    • Needing enough funding and deposits to meet its obligations when financial markets are strained.
    • Cyber-attacks, fraud and failures by third parties that provide services it depends on.
    From Synchrony Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
