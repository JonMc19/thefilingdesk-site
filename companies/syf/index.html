    Synchrony Financial

    SYF · NYSE · Financials · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Synchrony Financial's revenue fell 2.8% to $19.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Net income was $3.6 billion, up 1.5%. Its return on equity was 21.3%, down from 23.0% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 27.1%, up from 24.8% a year earlier. It paid out $3.4 billion in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 6.7%.
