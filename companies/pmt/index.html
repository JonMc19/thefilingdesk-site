    What PennyMac Mortgage Investment Trust does

    PennyMac Mortgage Investment Trust (PMT) is a specialty finance company that invests mainly in mortgage-related assets. It is structured as a real estate investment trust (REIT) and works through its operating partnership. It has no staff of its own for this: it is managed by PCM, a subsidiary of PennyMac Financial Services, and PCM's sister company PLS originates and services its loans for it.

    It has three segments. Correspondent production buys newly made prime-quality home loans from lenders, pools them and sells them, mostly to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac, either as loans or as mortgage-backed securities. Credit sensitive strategies holds credit risk transfer arrangements, which take on credit losses on loans it has sold, and subordinate mortgage-backed securities. Interest rate sensitive strategies holds mortgage servicing rights, Agency mortgage-backed securities and related structured products, with hedging against interest rate moves.

    It says its aim is to give investors returns, mainly through dividends and secondarily through growth in value. It finances its investments with borrowings and depends on Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and other government-sponsored entities for a significant part of its business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Interest rate changes that could cut its results, cash flows and the value of its investments.
    • Dependence on PCM and PLS, its manager and servicer, and on Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac.
    • Heavy borrowing, and the need to raise more debt or equity capital to finance assets.
    • Failing to keep Agency approvals, state licenses and net worth and liquidity requirements.
    • Rising delinquencies, defaults and foreclosures that cause losses on loans and servicing rights.
    From PennyMac Mortgage Investment Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
