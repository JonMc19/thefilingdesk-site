    What Nexstar Media Group does

    Nexstar owns and operates local television stations and national networks, and produces news, sports and entertainment programming. It says it is the largest local television broadcasting company in the United States, with over 200 owned or partner stations in 116 local markets. Its stations are affiliates of CBS, FOX, NBC and ABC, and also of The CW and MyNetworkTV. It owns 80.8% of The CW and runs NewsNation, a national news network, plus the Antenna TV and REWIND TV channels. It also has a stake in TV Food Network.

    Nexstar earns money mainly from distribution revenue and advertising revenue. Distribution revenue is the fees that cable, satellite and online TV distributors pay, generally per subscriber, to carry its stations' signals and its networks. Advertising revenue comes from selling airtime and digital ads to local and national businesses and, in election years, to political candidates and groups. It generated $4.9 billion of revenue in 2025. About 68% of its advertising revenue came from local programming, excluding its owned network assets.

    In August 2025 Nexstar agreed to buy TEGNA, which owns 64 television stations and two radio stations. It expects the deal to close by the second half of 2026. The company employs about 6,000 journalists and 1,600 salespeople.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The planned TEGNA merger may not close on time, or its benefits may take longer to arrive than expected.
    • Fewer cable and satellite subscribers, or failing to renew deals with distributors on good terms.
    • Failing to renew its network affiliation agreements with the major networks.
    • Advertising pressure from big technology companies and economic downturns, and loss of its largest advertisers.
    • A large amount of debt and mostly fixed costs, so a small revenue drop can hurt results.
    From Nexstar Media Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
