    What AeroVironment does

    AeroVironment designs and builds uncrewed aircraft, strike and defense systems, and space, cyber and directed energy products, mainly for military customers. Its products include small and medium uncrewed aircraft such as the Puma and JUMP 20, command and control software, loitering munitions such as the Switchblade, counter-drone systems such as the Titan, and electronic warfare tools.

    It reports two segments, which it set up on May 1, 2025, after buying BlueHalo. Autonomous Systems covers uncrewed aircraft, precision strike and defensive systems, and command and control software. Space, Cyber and Directed Energy covers space-based platforms, laser communications, directed energy and cyber and electronic warfare work.

    AeroVironment has an international manufacturing footprint. It says new products in its research pipeline may become growth platforms in the future.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Relying heavily on the U.S. government, especially the Department of Defense, for sales.
    • Cuts or delays in government budgets, spending priorities and contract awards.
    • Competition from firms with much greater resources.
    • Cost overruns and weaker margins on its contracts.
    • Trouble combining acquired businesses, such as BlueHalo, into its own.
    From AeroVironment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 29, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
