    What Apple Hospitality REIT does

    Apple Hospitality REIT is a real estate investment trust, a company that owns property and pays out much of its income, that owns hotels in the United States. At the end of 2025 it owned 217 hotels with 29,583 guest rooms in 37 states and the District of Columbia. Almost all operate under Marriott or Hilton brands.

    It makes money from the rooms and other hotel revenue. The company does not run the hotels itself: they are operated under management agreements by one of 16 hotel management companies that are not affiliated with it. It reports one segment and has no foreign operations.

    It focuses on upscale hotels that are built around rooms, spread across many markets. It buys and sells hotels over time, and in 2025 it bought two and sold seven. It says it keeps debt low compared with other real estate companies.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on Marriott and Hilton brands for nearly all of its hotels.
    • Dependence on third-party managers to run its hotels well.
    • Hotel industry conditions it cannot control, and competition in its markets.
    • Not finding or closing acquisitions, or sales of hotels, as planned.
    • Failing to qualify as a REIT, which would bring tax costs.
    From Apple Hospitality REIT's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
