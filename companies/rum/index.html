    What Rumble does

    Rumble runs a video sharing and livestreaming platform, Rumble.com, along with mobile and connected TV apps. Anyone can watch for free, and creators upload videos and stream live. The company says it was founded in 2013 to give small creators tools and distribution. It also runs Locals.com, where users subscribe to creators, and Rumble Studio, a livestreaming and monetization tool. It reports a user base of 52 million monthly active users in the fourth quarter of 2025.

    Rumble has two business units. Rumble Services covers the video platform, Rumble Studio, a crypto wallet that lets viewers tip creators, and Rumble Advertising Center, its own ad marketplace and network. Rumble Cloud sells computing, storage and networking services over the internet. The majority of its revenue comes from advertising: display ads, video ads and creator sponsorships. The rest comes from subscriptions such as Rumble Premium, pay-per-view and tipping. It shares revenue with creators. Average revenue per user was $0.46 in the fourth quarter of 2025.

    In February 2025 Tether Investments bought Class A shares for $775 million. In November 2025 Rumble agreed to a business combination with Northern Data, a company in AI and high-performance computing, which Rumble expects to close in the second quarter of 2026. Chris Pavlovski keeps super-majority voting control of Rumble.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not growing or keeping its active user base, and not reaching or keeping profitability.
    • Its dependence on advertising for the majority of revenue, and on advertisers keeping or raising their budgets.
    • Significant competition for viewers and advertising spending.
    • Dependence on mobile operating systems, internet providers, advertising networks and data centers it does not control.
    • Fixed payments to attract content creators that do not depend on the revenue those creators bring in.
    From Rumble's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
