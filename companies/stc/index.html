    What Stewart Information Services does

    Stewart Information Services is a title insurance and real estate services company. Title insurance protects a buyer or a lender against claims on who owns a property, and lenders in the United States generally require it for a loan on real estate. Stewart sells it through its own offices and through a network of independent agents.

    It reports three segments. The title segment searches property records, handles closings and issues title policies, and also covers home and personal insurance services, tax-deferred property exchanges and digital customer tools. The real estate solutions segment serves the mortgage industry with credit and property information, property preservation and field services, valuation management, online notarization and search services. A third segment holds corporate costs.

    Revenue in both main segments is closely tied to real estate activity, so it moves with mortgage interest rates and home sales. The company was founded in 1893. It employed about 7,800 people at the end of 2025, with about 6,000 of them in the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue falls when real estate and mortgage activity slows, as can happen when interest rates rise.
    • Claims could force it to raise its reserves for title losses.
    • Title insurance waivers and alternatives introduced by lenders and others could cut demand for its products.
    • Independent title agents who issue its policies operate with substantial independence from it.
    • Acquisitions and strategic investments may turn out to be unsuccessful.
