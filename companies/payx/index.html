    What Paychex does

    Paychex provides human resources, payroll, employee benefits and insurance services, mainly to small and mid-sized businesses. As of May 31, 2026 it served approximately 840,000 customers in the United States and parts of Europe, of which approximately 800,000 are payroll clients. It sells cloud-based software and advisory services through direct and virtual sales teams, referrals from partners and customers, and online marketing.

    It reports two segments. Management Solutions offers technology for HR and payroll, along with HR advice. PEO and Insurance Solutions offers professional employer organization services, in which Paychex shares certain employer duties for its clients' staff, plus insurance help with benefits and business coverage. Over 60% of its revenue comes from services other than payroll processing.

    On April 14, 2025 Paychex bought Paycor, a provider of HCM (human capital management), payroll and talent software. Its fiscal year ends May 31, and at the end of fiscal 2026 it employed approximately 17,600 people. It includes AI-powered tools in its products.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep pace with changes in technology or deliver timely product updates.
    • Risks and challenges from using AI in its operations and products.
    • Software defects or development delays that could harm customer relationships.
    • Cyberattacks or security breaches that could cost it revenue or lead to liability.
    • Extra risks from its co-employment relationship in its PEO business, and from changes in health insurance and workers' compensation costs.
