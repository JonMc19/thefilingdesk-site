    What Black Rock Coffee Bar does

    Black Rock Coffee Bar runs drive-thru coffee shops that serve coffee, cold brew, lattes and its own energy drinks, called Fuel. It was founded in Beaverton, Oregon in 2008. At December 31, 2025 it had 181 locations in seven states, from the Pacific Northwest to Texas. The company says it is the largest fully company-owned coffee retailer in the country.

    It makes money by selling drinks and food to guests in its own stores. Every location has a drive-thru, and approximately 73% also have a seating area it calls a lobby. It expects most new stores to have both. It roasts its coffee beans in small batches at two roasting facilities and delivers them to its stores weekly. The company says it competes with other coffee stores, quick service restaurants and convenience stores.

    Its growth plan depends on opening new stores in existing and new markets, and the company reports a history of losses.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of losses; it may not reach or keep profitability, especially if it keeps growing quickly.
    • Food safety and quality concerns that could hurt its brand.
    • Opening new stores that may not be successful or profitable, and managing fast growth.
    • Changing tastes, including public or medical views on caffeine and sugar.
    • Competition from other coffee stores, fast-food restaurants and convenience stores.
    From Black Rock Coffee Bar's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 4, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
