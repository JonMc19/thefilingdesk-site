    What Natural Gas Services Group does

    Natural Gas Services Group rents out natural gas compressors, machines that raise the pressure of gas so it can be moved or used in oil and gas production. It designs, installs, services and maintains units driven by gas engines or electric motors. It also offers call-out repair services on equipment its customers own. It is based in Southlake, Texas.

    Renting compressors is its main source of revenue and profit. Its customers are oil and gas producers, which mostly use the larger units for gas lift, a method that uses compressed gas to help oil flow up from wells. The Permian Basin in West Texas and New Mexico is its largest rental area, at 78 percent of rental revenue in 2025. It had about 60 customers for its rental fleet at the end of 2025.

    Two customers, Occidental and Devon, together made up 59 percent of its revenue in 2025. In 2025 it added about 70 large new units to its fleet and began paying a quarterly dividend. The company says the compression business is highly competitive and that some rivals have more money. It competes on unit availability, service, flexibility, reliability and price.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Lower oil and gas prices or lower industry spending would reduce demand for its compressors.
    • Two customers account for most of its revenue, and losing either would hurt.
    • About half of its rental agreements run month to month and could end on short notice.
    • Its reliance on a few suppliers could mean shortages and higher prices for parts.
    • Strong competition, and rules limiting fracturing or tightening environmental duties, could cut profits.
    From Natural Gas Services Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
