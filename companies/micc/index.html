    What Magnum Ice Cream does

    The Magnum Ice Cream Company, based in Amsterdam, makes and sells ice cream. It says it is the world's largest ice cream company. Its shares trade on Euronext Amsterdam, the London Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange.

    Its ice cream brands include Magnum, Ben & Jerry's, Wall's, Cornetto, Twister, Breyers, Talenti and Yasso. It sells them in 80 markets around the world through retailers, shops and other outlets that keep the products in freezer cabinets.

    Until the Demerger, which was set out in an agreement dated 1 October 2025, the business operated as a Business Group inside Unilever. Since the split it is a separate listed company. Under transitional services agreements, Unilever still handles some sales to customers for it, while the company sets prices, fills customer orders and bears the inventory risk. Its ingredients include cocoa, vanilla and dairy, and its costs depend on commodity prices.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Setting up as a standalone company after the split from Unilever, including its reliance on Unilever services for a transition period.
    • Rising prices and limited supply of key ingredients such as cocoa, vanilla and dairy.
    • Disruption to its freezer-chain deliveries, factories and logistics.
    • Inflation, currency swings, trade restrictions and political tension in the many markets where it sells.
    • Cyber security incidents, which it handled alongside Unilever during 2025.
