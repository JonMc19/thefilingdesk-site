    What Devon Energy does

    Devon Energy is an independent energy company. It explores for, develops and produces oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids (NGLs), which are hydrocarbons separated from gas. Its work is concentrated in onshore areas of the United States. It was founded in 1971, has been publicly held since 1988 and is based in Oklahoma City.

    It owns assets in four areas: the Delaware Basin, the Rockies, the Eagle Ford and the Anadarko Basin. Its income comes from selling the oil, gas and NGLs it produces, so its results depend heavily on commodity prices, which are volatile. Its current drilling is mostly in unconventional oil and gas assets, which lose output faster than conventional wells, so it must keep drilling or buying reserves to hold production up.

    Devon says its strategy is to deliver competitive returns to shareholders, keep a strong balance sheet and pay and grow a fixed dividend. It has also signed a merger agreement with Coterra, a transaction that was still pending when the 10-K was filed.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Volatile oil, gas and NGL prices that strongly affect its results and the value of its properties.
    • Needing to find or buy new reserves to avoid a decline in output, which takes a lot of capital.
    • Drilling that can be costly and unprofitable, with accidents, weather and equipment failures.
    • Its pending merger with Coterra may be delayed, may not be completed, or may disrupt its plans.
    • Its debt and hedging activities, which can limit its flexibility.
