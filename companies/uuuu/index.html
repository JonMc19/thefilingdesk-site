    What Energy Fuels does

    Energy Fuels is a company that mines and processes uranium, and also works with vanadium, rare earth elements (REEs) and heavy mineral sands. It is incorporated in Ontario, Canada and reports as a U.S. company. Its White Mesa Mill in Utah is the only conventional uranium mill, and the only uranium, vanadium and REE recovery facility, operating in the United States.

    It mines uranium at three conventional mines in Arizona and Utah: Pinyon Plain, La Sal and Pandora. It has six long-term uranium contracts with major U.S. utilities, including two new ones completed in the fourth quarter of 2025. It is also expanding commercial separation of rare earth elements at the mill and looking at recovering radioisotopes for emerging cancer treatments.

    Its projects include the Vara Mada Project in Madagascar, the Donald Project in Australia, in which it holds a small minority interest, and the Bahia Project in Brazil. It is also responsible for closing and reclaiming its Kwale Project in Kenya. It had approximately 1,069 full-time employees at December 31, 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its results depend on market prices for uranium, vanadium, rare earths and heavy mineral sands, which are cyclical and swing a lot.
    • Most of its properties have no established mineral reserves, so mining them may never be economic.
    • Government administrations and public opinion that may not support mining or nuclear energy.
    • Heavy regulation, and the risks and costs of mining, extraction, processing and building projects.
    • Competition in the uranium and rare earth industries, and technology change that could make its methods obsolete.
    From Energy Fuels's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
