    What Winnebago Industries does

    Winnebago Industries makes recreational vehicles, boats and batteries. Its brands include Winnebago and Grand Design for RVs, Newmar for motorhomes, and Chris-Craft and Barletta for boats. It also makes battery systems that supply "house power" for RVs, boats and other low-speed vehicles, and some parts for other manufacturers and commercial vehicles.

    It reports three segments: Towable RV, Motorhome RV and Marine. Towable RVs, such as travel trailers and fifth wheels, are pulled by cars, pickups or vans and used as living space on trips. The company builds towables in Indiana, motorhomes in Iowa and Indiana, boats in Indiana and Florida, and batteries in Florida. It sells RVs and boats mostly to independent dealers in the United States and Canada, who then sell to the buyer. It also sells boats internationally through dealers. It earns money from these sales and from parts and service.

    At the end of fiscal 2026 on August 29, 2026, it employed about 5,000 people.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker consumer spending, since RVs and boats are discretionary purchases.
    • Tight credit that could stop dealers and shoppers from financing purchases.
    • Its business depends on independent dealers, and a major dealer could be lost.
    • It may have to buy back unsold products if a dealer defaults.
    • Dependence on a small group of suppliers for some parts, and tariffs and higher material costs.
    From Winnebago Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed October 21, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
