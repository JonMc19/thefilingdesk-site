    What Bilibili does

    Bilibili runs a video community in China, aimed mainly at younger people. Users watch videos made by other users, with topics such as lifestyle, games, anime, entertainment and knowledge. Creators post the videos, and the company also offers live broadcasts, professional video series, comics, audio drama and mobile games.

    It earns revenue mainly from value-added services such as live broadcasting and paid content, from advertising, from mobile games, and from IP derivatives (products based on its content). Its costs include revenue-sharing with creators, content costs and server and bandwidth costs. It competes mostly with other video companies in mainland China for users, content and advertisers.

    In 2025 Bilibili had an average of 112 million daily active users and 368 million monthly active users, each up 8% from 2024.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Keeping users and creators engaged, as its business depends on the videos creators post.
    • Content on its platform that breaks Chinese rules, which could bring penalties.
    • Licenses and approvals it needs in mainland China for its online businesses.
    • Rising content costs and the cost of computing and bandwidth.
    • Limits Chinese authorities set on online games, including playing time for minors.
