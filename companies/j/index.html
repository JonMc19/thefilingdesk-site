    What Jacobs Solutions does

    Jacobs sells engineering, consulting and technical services to governments and companies. Its team of approximately 43,000 people works across advanced manufacturing, cities and places, energy, environmental, life sciences, transportation and water projects. The work runs from advice and planning through design, program delivery and lifecycle management.

    It reports two operating segments: Infrastructure & Advanced Facilities, and its majority investment in PA Consulting, a consulting firm in which it holds a 65% interest, bought in 2021. It has also bought BlackLynx and StreetLight to add technology-enabled services. Most of its contracts are cost-reimbursable, meaning the client pays its costs plus a fee, and the rest are mostly fixed-price.

    The U.S. federal government accounted for approximately 8% of its total revenue in fiscal 2025. Jacobs says it works in a highly competitive business.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Winning new contracts on time, since its results depend on awards and their timing.
    • Fixed-price contracts that can lead to cost overruns and reduced profit or losses.
    • Backlog that clients can adjust, cancel or suspend, so it may not predict future revenue.
    • Dangerous project sites and the safety risks of its work.
    • Added risks of work for the U.S. federal government and other governments.
    From Jacobs Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 20, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
