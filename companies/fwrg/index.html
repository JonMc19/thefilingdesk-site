    What First Watch Restaurant Group does

    First Watch runs restaurants that serve made-to-order breakfast, brunch and lunch, and it calls this Daytime Dining. Its restaurants do not serve dinner. It uses no microwave ovens, heat lamps or deep fryers, and its menu changes four to five times a year with the seasons. It also sells fresh juices.

    It makes money from sales at its company-owned restaurants and from franchisees, who own and run others under its name. At the end of 2025 it had 633 restaurants in 32 states, 560 of them company-owned and 73 franchised. Its average restaurant took in $2.3 million in 2025, while open only 7.5 hours a day.

    Each restaurant has a staff of about 30 team members led by general managers. The company says its focus on one daytime shift helps it hire employees who want to avoid night work. Most of its restaurants are in the southeastern part of the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in the economy and in what diners prefer to eat.
    • Not opening new restaurants on schedule or profitably, which its growth plan depends on.
    • Higher food costs and reliance on a limited number of suppliers and distributors.
    • Franchisees whose results and conduct it has limited control over.
    • Food safety problems and bad publicity that could hurt its reputation.
    From First Watch Restaurant Group's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
