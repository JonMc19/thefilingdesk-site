    What Piper Sandler Companies does

    Piper Sandler is an investment bank and institutional securities firm founded in 1895. Its clients include corporations, private equity groups, public entities, non-profit groups and institutional investors in the United States and abroad. It is based in Minneapolis, with offices across the US and in London, Aberdeen, Munich, Paris, Zurich, Abu Dhabi Global Markets and Hong Kong.

    It reports one segment. In investment banking, it advises corporate clients and financial sponsors on mergers and acquisitions, stock and debt financings, private placements, restructurings and private capital. It focuses mainly on middle-market clients in healthcare, financial services, services and industrials, energy, power and infrastructure, consumer, technology and chemicals. For government and non-profit clients, it underwrites municipal bond issues and gives municipal financial advice.

    The firm also does institutional sales and trading in stocks and fixed income products, offers research on equities and macro topics, and runs alternative asset management strategies. The 10-K says its financing and advisory work is transactional, meaning it is paid per deal and not on a continuing basis.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Market and economic conditions directly affect its results, and declines in specific sectors can hit it hard.
    • Its financing and advisory work is deal by deal, with no guarantee of follow-on business.
    • The investment banking deals it expects may differ from the ones it actually completes.
    • Competition from financial firms with greater resources, and pricing pressure in brokerage.
    • Conflicts of interest or damage to its reputation could hurt its business.
    From Piper Sandler Companies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
