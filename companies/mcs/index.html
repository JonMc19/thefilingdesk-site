    What Marcus does

    Marcus runs movie theatres and hotels and resorts, mainly in the Midwest. Its theatres operate under the Marcus Theatres, Movie Tavern by Marcus and BistroPlex brands, and many offer recliner seating, bars and food delivered to the seat. It also runs a family entertainment center next to one theatre in Appleton, Wisconsin.

    The company reports two segments. In theatres, it earns money from tickets, food and drinks, and from pre-show advertising sold through outside providers. At the end of 2025 it owned or operated 78 theatres with 985 screens in 17 states. About 71% of the screens are in theatres with 12 or more screens. It says it is the fourth largest theatre circuit in the United States. In hotels and resorts, it earns money from room stays, food and beverage, and from fees for managing properties for partners and third parties.

    Marcus owns and operates seven hotels and resorts in Wisconsin, Illinois and Nebraska, and manages nine more. It says the theatre industry is very fragmented, so deals to buy theatres or circuits are hard to predict. It competes with other theatre chains and with other lodging operators.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling attendance if too few films, or too few films that appeal to audiences, are released.
    • Shorter windows before films reach home video, piracy and other ways to watch at home.
    • Weaker ties with film distributors, or fewer of them, which could raise film costs.
    • Heavy capital needs and debt that takes a lot of cash to service.
    • Harsh Midwest winter weather and seasonal swings in results.
    From Marcus's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
