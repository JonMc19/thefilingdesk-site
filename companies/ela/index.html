    What Envela does

    Envela buys and sells used goods and recycles electronics. It has two segments. The consumer segment runs retail stores that buy and sell pre-owned jewelry, diamonds, gemstones, luxury watches and bullion (bars and coins of precious metal). Its earliest predecessor was founded in 1972, and it has traded silver since 1972 and gold since 1974.

    The commercial segment handles end-of-life technology for businesses. It breaks down old electronics and recovers base and precious metals and other materials, and it provides IT asset disposition (ITAD) services such as data destruction, refurbishing and remarketing of company IT equipment. Its recycling business was founded in 2009.

    In 2025 the company added one store, completing a plan to open 7 stores. The company says it faces intense competition across all of its markets.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Precious metals markets are unpredictable, and it may not be able to raise retail prices when its commodity costs rise.
    • Weak economic conditions in its key markets could lower consumer confidence and spending.
    • Intense competition in all of its markets.
    • Demand for its products and services may fall, and it may not adapt.
    • Difficulty budgeting or forecasting its results and cash needs, which makes comparing periods hard.
