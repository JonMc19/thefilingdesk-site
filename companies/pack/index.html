    What Ranpak does

    Ranpak makes paper-based protective packaging and automated packing equipment for e-commerce and industrial companies. Its packaging fills empty space in shipping boxes and wraps items. It also sells automation and machine vision systems that speed up end-of-line packing. The company says its packaging is recyclable, renewable and biodegradable.

    Ranpak places its packing machines with customers and says its revenue from this installed base recurs. It had net revenue of $395.0 million in 2025. About 47% came from North America and about 45% from Europe, with the rest from Asia and other locations. About 40% of net revenue came from sales to e-commerce end-users.

    The company is expanding into cold chain packaging, which keeps food and drinks cold in transit, and into paper alternatives to plastic mailers. It has a paper conversion facility in Malaysia. JS Capital is a large stockholder.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not getting enough paper from the few suppliers whose paper suits its products.
    • Rising prices of kraft paper and other inputs that could cut its profit margins.
    • Losing e-commerce customers or a drop in their volume of shipments.
    • Risks from operating in many countries, including currency swings and disruptions at its assembly sites.
    • Investments in research and development that may not pay off.
    From Ranpak's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
