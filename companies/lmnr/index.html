    What Limoneira does

    Limoneira is an agribusiness company based in Santa Paula, California. It grows lemons, avocados, oranges and wine grapes on land in Ventura and San Luis Obispo Counties in California, Yuma County in Arizona, and in Chile and Argentina. It says it is one of California's oldest citrus growers and, according to the California Avocado Commission, one of the largest avocado growers in the United States.

    Its operations are fruit production, sales and marketing, rental operations, and real estate and capital investment, and it reports them in four segments. It earns money by selling its fruit and by renting out homes, commercial space and leased land. It runs its own packinghouses in Santa Paula and Yuma, where it packs lemons it grows and lemons grown by others. In Chile it has a 47% interest in a citrus packing and marketing business, Rosales, and owns orchards.

    Beginning November 1, 2025, under a license agreement with Sunkist Growers, a marketing cooperative, it is merging its citrus sales and marketing into Sunkist.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its decision to merge its citrus sales and marketing into Sunkist may not work as planned.
    • Bad weather, frost, wildfires and earthquakes that can damage crops.
    • Crop disease and pests that harm fresh produce.
    • Swings in market supply and prices for its fruit.
    • Not having enough water, and changes in immigration laws that affect harvesting.
    From Limoneira's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 23, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
