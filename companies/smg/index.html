    What Scotts Miracle-Gro does

    Scotts Miracle-Gro sells lawn and garden products. Its brands include Scotts lawn fertilizer and grass seed, Miracle-Gro soil and plant food, Ortho weed and pest control, and Tomcat rodent control. It is also the exclusive agent of Monsanto, a unit of Bayer, for marketing and distributing certain consumer Roundup products in the United States and some other countries.

    It reports three segments. U.S. Consumer is its lawn and garden business in the United States. Hawthorne sells nutrients, lighting and other supplies for indoor and hydroponic (soil-free) growing. Other is mostly its Canadian consumer business. Its sales are concentrated: The Home Depot and Lowe's are its two largest customers, and the only ones that each account for more than 10% of net sales.

    Its North America consumer business is highly seasonal, with more than 75% of annual net sales in its second and third fiscal quarters combined. At the end of fiscal 2025, on September 30, 2025, it employed about 5,200 associates and used more at peak times, including seasonal and temporary workers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Misjudging demand and holding too much or too little inventory.
    • Depending on a small number of retail customers, mainly The Home Depot and Lowe's.
    • Higher costs or shortages of raw materials, fuel and transportation.
    • Tariffs and trade disputes affecting goods made or sourced outside the U.S.
    • Weather and climate that can reduce lawn and garden sales.
    From Scotts Miracle-Gro's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
