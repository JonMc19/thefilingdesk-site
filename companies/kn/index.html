    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. Knowles' long-term debt already includes the part due within a year, and the separate short-term borrowings its notes show are that same part at face value, so debt is its long-term debt total alone. The SEC's structured data has no full-year figure for Knowles' cash from operations, so cash from operations, free cash flow and the ratios built on them are blank for fiscal years; the quarterly figures are shown. Knowles' 2025 and 2026 filings restate 2023 revenue to US$456.8 million (first reported as US$707.6 million), while the first two quarters of 2023 stay as first reported, so the four quarters of fiscal 2023 add up to more than the year.
