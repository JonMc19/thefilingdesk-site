    What Penn Entertainment does

    PENN Entertainment runs casinos, racetracks and online sports betting and online casino (iCasino) businesses in 28 jurisdictions across North America. At the end of 2025 it owned, managed or had interests in 42 gaming and racing properties in 19 states, and offered live sports betting at its properties in 14 states. Its PENN Play loyalty program has over 33 million members.

    It reports five segments. The Northeast, South, West and Midwest segments are its retail casinos, which earn money from slot machines and table games, food and beverage, and hotels. The Interactive segment covers online sports betting, iCasino, social gaming and media, including its theScore media app. Most of the land and buildings it uses are leased under long-term master leases, the largest with Gaming and Leisure Properties, a real estate investment trust.

    In November 2025 PENN ended its U.S. sportsbook agreement with ESPN, effective December 1, 2025. It then renamed its U.S. online sports betting product theScore Bet and shifted its digital focus toward U.S. iCasino and its Canadian operations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition from other casinos, online gaming and other leisure activities keeps growing.
    • Its business is sensitive to cuts in discretionary consumer spending when the economy is weak.
    • A large share of its cash flow goes to interest and rent payments under its debt and leases, and most of its facilities are leased.
    • It may not compete well or earn adequate returns from its online sports betting and iCasino operations.
    • The end of its ESPN partnership could hurt its business and reputation.
    From Penn Entertainment's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    From fiscal 2016 to 2022, earnings per share grew 1.4% a year and net income 12.5% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 93% and diluted each share's earnings. Penn Entertainment then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
