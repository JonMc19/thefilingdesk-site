    What Becton Dickinson does

    BD (Becton, Dickinson and Company) develops, makes and sells medical supplies, devices, laboratory equipment and diagnostic products. Its customers include healthcare institutions, physicians, life science researchers, clinical laboratories, the pharmaceutical industry and the general public. Its products focus on medication management and patient safety, infection prevention, surgical and interventional procedures, drug delivery, anesthesiology care, diagnosing infectious diseases and cancers, and cellular research.

    At September 30, 2025, BD had three segments: BD Medical, BD Life Sciences and BD Interventional. Since October 1, 2025 it has reorganized into five segments: Medical Essentials, Connected Care, BioPharma Systems, Interventional, and Life Sciences. The company was incorporated in New Jersey in 1906, as successor to a business started in 1897.

    BD has agreed to separate its Biosciences and Diagnostic Solutions business and combine it with Waters Corporation. BD's shareholders are expected to own approximately 39.2% of the combined company. Its Alaris infusion pump business in the United States is operating under an amended consent decree.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition in the medical technology industry, which is very competitive.
    • Changes in reimbursement practices, coverage policies and payer cost controls.
    • An amended consent decree that affects its Alaris infusion pump business in the United States.
    • Interruptions to its manufacturing or sterilization operations, or to the supply of raw materials.
    • Cuts to customers' research budgets or government funding.
    From Becton Dickinson's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 25, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
