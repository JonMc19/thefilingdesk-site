    What Dick's Sporting Goods does

    Dick's Sporting Goods is a sports retailer. It sells sports equipment, apparel, footwear and accessories through stores and online. Its banners include Dick's Sporting Goods, Golf Galaxy, Public Lands and Going Going Gone!, plus larger "experience" stores such as House of Sport. It also owns GameChanger, a subscription app for live streaming, scheduling and scorekeeping in youth sports.

    In fiscal 2025 it bought Foot Locker, which sells sneakers and athletic apparel under the Foot Locker, Kids Foot Locker, Champs Sports, WSS and atmos names in North America, Europe, Asia and Australia. The company now reports two segments, Dick's and Foot Locker. Foot Locker also earns royalties from licensed stores run by other operators. Dick's says its own vertical brands, which it owns or licenses and sells only in its stores and online, were its second largest vendor, at $1.8 billion, or about 13% of net sales in the Dick's business.

    It buys from about 1,500 vendors. Nike is the largest, at about 31% of purchases across both businesses, and the company has no long-term purchase contracts with vendors. Its ScoreCard loyalty program has about 30 million members and accounts for over 75% of Dick's total sales. The company says it competes with other sporting goods and retail businesses, including online-only sellers. The Foot Locker deal closed on September 8, 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak consumer spending in a downturn, and intense competition from other sporting goods and retail businesses.
    • Not gaining the expected benefits from the Foot Locker deal, or struggling to combine the two businesses.
    • Its reliance on a few key suppliers and on products made outside the United States.
    • Strategic plans, including new store formats and real estate moves, that may not deliver the results it expects.
    • Debt and the terms of its debt agreements that could limit its flexibility.
    From Dick's Sporting Goods's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
