    What Leonardo DRS does

    Leonardo DRS designs and builds defense technology for the U.S. military and allied forces. Its products include sensors, computers used on ships and in the field, systems that protect vehicles and troops, and electric power and propulsion for naval vessels. The U.S. defense department is its main customer, and it also sells to the defense agencies of allied countries.

    It reports two segments. Advanced Sensing and Computing makes sensors such as infrared cameras, radar, electronic warfare and signals intelligence equipment, along with rugged computing that processes data close to where it is collected. Integrated Mission Systems makes power conversion, control and distribution systems, ship propulsion systems, motors, force protection systems, and transportation and logistics systems. It supplies propulsion and power systems for U.S. Navy shipbuilding programs such as the Columbia Class submarine.

    The company says it is a mid-sized defense technology firm and that demand is concentrated in counter-drone systems, advanced infrared sensing, network computing and electric power for navy ships. Its products are used on land, in the air, at sea, in space and in cyber operations. It says it competes with many other defense contractors and also depends on relationships with partners to win work.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on U.S. defense spending for the vast majority of its revenue.
    • Government contracts that are often only partly funded and can be ended at any time.
    • Delays or cuts in appropriations, including from government shutdowns.
    • Results that swing with its mix of fixed-price and cost-plus contracts and with its cost estimates.
    • Frequent government audits and strict security and procurement rules.
