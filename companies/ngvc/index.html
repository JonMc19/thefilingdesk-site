    What Natural Grocers does

    Natural Grocers is a specialty grocery chain that sells natural and organic groceries, body care products, pet care products, books and dietary supplements. It does not sell grocery products it knows contain artificial flavors, preservatives or sweeteners, synthetic colors, or partially hydrogenated oils. It also offers free nutrition education to customers. Its stores use a smaller format than a typical supermarket.

    The company makes money by selling these products to shoppers in its own stores. A typical store carries an average of about 20,000 products, including about 6,500 dietary supplements. It also sells its own Natural Grocers brand products. It competes with conventional supermarkets, natural and specialty food markets, mass and discount retailers, warehouse clubs, supplement retailers, online retailers and others.

    It was started in 1958 and had 169 stores in 21 states as of September 30, 2025. It plans to open six to eight new stores in fiscal 2026. It runs its own bulk food repackaging facility and distribution center.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to grow profitably, including through opening new stores.
    • Heavy competition from supermarkets, discounters and online sellers, and pressure on its margins.
    • Less supply of certified organic products and products that meet its own standards, or trouble with key suppliers.
    • Product recalls, and suppliers selling their specialty products through other channels.
    • Large lease obligations, higher wage costs and union activity.
    From Natural Grocers's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 11, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
