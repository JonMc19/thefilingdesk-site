    What Nicolet Bankshares does

    Nicolet Bankshares is a bank holding company based in Wisconsin. Its main subsidiary, Nicolet National Bank, opened in Green Bay on November 1, 2000, and offers commercial and consumer banking to businesses, farms and households. At the end of 2025 the company had total assets of $9.2 billion, loans of $6.8 billion and deposits of $7.7 billion.

    A bank earns most of its money from the gap between the interest it charges on loans and the interest it pays on deposits. Nicolet also owns an investment advisory firm that provides brokerage and investment advice, and an insurance firm that delivers a crop insurance product linked to its farm lending. It says its aim is to be the lead community bank in the communities it serves.

    On February 13, 2026, after the year covered by the report, it completed a merger with MidWest One Financial Group, whose bank served customers in central and eastern Iowa, the Minneapolis and St. Paul area, southwestern Wisconsin and Denver. MidWest One had total assets of approximately $6 billion at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense and fast-changing competition for customers, including from technology-driven rivals.
    • Trouble carrying out its growth plans and integrating the banks it acquires, including MidWest One.
    • Fraud, which it calls a major and increasing risk for all banks.
    • Cybersecurity breaches and its reliance on technology systems and outside service providers.
    • Credit losses that rise and demand that falls when the economy weakens, including from inflation.
    From Nicolet Bankshares's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
