    What Rimini Street does

    Rimini Street provides support for software that companies use to run their businesses, such as enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, instead of the software makers themselves. Clients pay it for help with fixes, security and regulatory updates, usually as a subscription. It also offers managed services and tools that use artificial intelligence (AI) with ERP software.

    Its core product is Rimini Support, which it sells as an alternative to the vendor's own support. It says clients can use it to control their technology plans and lower their costs. It has widened its offering over time, with its Rimini Custom program, managed services for Workday, Dayforce and ServiceNow, and solutions for security, interoperability, observability and consulting.

    The company says it is the largest independent software support provider by number of active clients. It recognizes subscription revenue over the length of each contract, so a drop or rise in sales shows up in its results only gradually. It was formed in Nevada in 2005 and became a public company through a 2017 merger.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue growth may not continue, and clients may not renew at the rates it expects.
    • Software vendors' efforts to push clients to cloud versions of their software could cut demand for its support.
    • It may need to change its pricing to compete.
    • Its new AI-based ERP products may not succeed and bring legal and operational challenges.
    • It may not be able to scale fast enough to meet clients' changing needs.
