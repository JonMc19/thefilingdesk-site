    What Gen Digital does

    Gen Digital sells software that helps consumers protect their devices, identity, privacy and money online, and personal finance products. Its brands include Norton, Avast, LifeLock and MoneyLion. It says its products are used by nearly 500 million users in more than 150 countries.

    Gen makes money mainly from subscriptions, most of them on annual terms and some monthly. It sells them directly through its websites and mobile apps and through partners such as retailers, phone companies and employee benefit providers. It also gives away free versions, such as Avast, to reach more people, and hopes some users will upgrade to paid plans. Of its total users, about 79 million are paid customers.

    The acquisition of MoneyLion added a marketplace branded Engine by Gen, where consumers see offers for financial and other products and services. The company's stated plan is to move from protecting people online toward helping them manage their financial lives too.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to build new and better products as rivals and online threats change.
    • Keeping existing customers and getting free users to become paying ones.
    • Heavy competition that may force it to change its prices.
    • Reliance on partners and sales channels that can end or renegotiate their agreements at any time.
    • Risks from artificial intelligence, including AI-driven cyber threats.
    From Gen Digital's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 21, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
