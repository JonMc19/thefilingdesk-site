    What OneWater Marine does

    OneWater Marine is a boat retailer. At the end of September 2025 it had 95 dealerships in 17 states, mainly in the Southeast, Gulf Coast, Mid-Atlantic and Northeast. It sells new and pre-owned boats, arranges financing and insurance, repairs and maintains boats, sells marine parts and accessories, and rents slips and storage at some locations. In fiscal 2025 it sold over 9,500 boats, buying new ones from over 35 manufacturers.

    It reports two segments. The Dealerships segment represents about 92% of revenue. The Distribution segment, about 8%, makes, assembles and distributes marine products such as jack plates, bilge pumps, lighting and cleaning products through three owned businesses, selling to distributors, big box retailers, online retailers and directly to consumers.

    OneWater grows largely by buying other businesses: since 2014 it has made 35 acquisitions. It says it is one of the largest marine retailers in the United States, and it is a holding company that runs its business through One Water Marine Holdings, LLC.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on boat manufacturers, who have control over its business and whose consent it needs to buy dealers.
    • Weak consumer spending, higher interest rates or inflation reducing boat purchases.
    • Tariffs and other trade measures that raise its costs or disrupt its supply chain.
    • A highly seasonal business, and the loss of manufacturer rebates and incentives.
    • Acquisitions that cost more or are harder to combine than expected.
    From OneWater Marine's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 15, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
