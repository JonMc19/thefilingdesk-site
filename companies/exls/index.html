    What EXL does

    EXL provides data, analytics and AI-based services to other companies, and runs parts of their business operations for them. Its clients are in insurance, healthcare and life sciences, banking and capital markets, retail, communications and media, and energy, among others. It helps them handle tasks such as claims, customer service and data work, and build AI into how they operate.

    EXL reports four segments, set up in 2025 around the industries it serves: Insurance; Healthcare and Life Sciences; Banking, Capital Markets and Diversified Industries; and International Growth Markets. Its former Analytics segment is now a capability inside each of them. The 10-K also names "Strategic Growth Units" that build its data, digital engineering and AI skills.

    EXL is headquartered in New York and has over 65,000 employees on six continents. Its delivery network has operations centers in India, the United States, the Philippines, South Africa, Colombia, Bulgaria, Romania, the United Kingdom, the Czech Republic, Mexico and Ireland. Its Asian operations are primarily in India and the Philippines.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A drop in client demand, or failing to keep up with new technology in its services.
    • Its use of AI may not succeed and brings competitive, reputational and legal risks.
    • Trouble hiring, training and keeping enough skilled employees.
    • Breaches of the sensitive client and employee data it handles.
    • Wage increases that could cut into its profit margin.
    From EXL's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
