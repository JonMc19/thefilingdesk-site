    What FTI Consulting does

    FTI Consulting is a consulting firm that advises companies, law firms and governments facing a crisis or a major change, such as a bankruptcy, a lawsuit, an investigation or a merger. It sells the time and expertise of its professionals, and it reports five segments.

    Corporate Finance, its largest, advises on deals, restructurings and turnarounds and made 41% of 2025 revenue. Forensic and Litigation Consulting handles investigations and disputes, and Economic Consulting gives expert analysis for antitrust and arbitration cases. Technology provides e-discovery software and data services for legal teams. Strategic Communications advises on corporate reputation, financial communications and public affairs.

    The firm says it derived approximately 63% of its 2025 revenue from the work of professionals based in the United States. It had 6,421 billable professionals at the end of 2025 across the five segments, working from offices in many countries. It says seasonal factors such as employee and client vacations can affect the timing of its revenue.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue and profit swing with M&A, capital markets, legal and regulatory activity and the economy.
    • Poor use of its professionals' time or lower billing rates could hurt its results.
    • Clients may not pay their fees or may ask to renegotiate them.
    • Failing to recruit and keep qualified professionals, whose hiring costs are high.
    • Protecting client data matters most for its Technology segment, and a breach could harm it.
    From FTI Consulting's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
