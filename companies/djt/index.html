    What Trump Media & Technology Group does

    Trump Media & Technology Group (TMTG) runs Truth Social, a social media platform it describes as a place for free expression, and Truth+, a TV streaming service with live channels and on-demand content. It has also started Truth.Fi, a financial services brand that includes investment products and a digital asset strategy with a bitcoin treasury. Truth Social went on general release in the first quarter of 2022, and the company says its apps run on iOS, Android and the web.

    The company is early in its life and expects to keep losing money from operations for the foreseeable future. It is working on ways to earn revenue from advertising on both Truth Social and Truth+, and in 2025 it began testing a paid plan with premium features, called the Patriot Package. It ended 2025 with approximately $2,473.2 million of cash, investments, digital assets and similar items, and approximately $947.1 million of debt, not counting lease liabilities.

    In December 2025 TMTG agreed to merge with TAE Technologies. If it closes, TMTG expects each side's shareholders to own about half of the combined company. As of the date of the filing, a trust of which Donald J. Trump is the sole beneficiary owned approximately 41.1% of the voting power of TMTG's common stock.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its short history, which makes the business hard to judge, and its expected operating losses.
    • Failing to build an audience for Truth Social and Truth+ or to earn money from them.
    • Needing more money, with no assurance that financing will be available.
    • Competition with larger and more established social media and streaming services.
    • Its bitcoin holdings, which could change its financial results significantly.
