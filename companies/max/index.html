    What MediaAlpha does

    MediaAlpha runs an online marketplace where insurance companies and agents buy leads: consumers who are shopping for insurance. Buyers, which it calls Demand Partners, are generally insurance carriers, agents or distributors. Sellers, called Supply Partners, are typically carriers or insurance-focused websites that want to earn money from their visitors.

    Its platform works in real time and lets buyers set prices for different groups of consumers. Its main lines are property and casualty insurance, mainly auto, plus health and life insurance. It earns fees on the money partners spend through the platform, which it calls Transaction Value. That totaled $2.2 billion in 2025. The same insurer can be both a buyer and a seller, which the company says deepens the relationship.

    It says that in 2025 consumers shopping through its partners' websites and its own sites generated an average of 11.8 million referrals a month. It competes with technology companies that run digital customer acquisition for insurers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reliance on a small group of buyers and sellers for much of its business.
    • Swings in the property and casualty insurance cycle that change how much insurers spend on advertising.
    • The FTC matter, which has hurt its under-65 health business and could continue to.
    • Partners pushing for lower fees or moving deals to private marketplaces.
    • Competition from other digital customer acquisition companies.
    From MediaAlpha's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
