    What American Tower does

    American Tower is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns and leases communications sites. Its main business is renting space on towers and similar sites to wireless service providers, radio and television broadcasters, wireless data providers, government agencies and others. It also holds a portfolio of data center facilities in the United States. It calls this site leasing and data center business its property operations, which accounted for 97% of total revenues in 2025.

    The rest comes from services operations in the United States, which help customers add tenants and equipment to its sites. These include site applications, zoning and permitting, structural analysis and construction management. Its portfolio is mostly towers that it owns or operates under long-term leases, plus in-building and outdoor distributed antenna systems, or DAS, which provide wireless coverage in specific areas, and fiber and other telecom property.

    The company reports six segments, and it has grown its portfolio through acquisitions, long-term leases and building new sites. In 2025, approximately 82% of its property segment revenue came from communications sites, excluding data centers, DAS and fiber.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A substantial portion of its revenue comes from a small number of customers.
    • Disputes with customers could hurt its business and results.
    • Customers that merge, leave their businesses or share sites could cut its revenue.
    • Competition could hurt its revenue and its ability to earn a return on new assets.
    • Its debt and debt-service obligations, and restrictive loan terms, could limit its flexibility.
