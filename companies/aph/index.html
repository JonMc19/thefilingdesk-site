    What Amphenol does

    Amphenol designs, makes and sells electrical, electronic and fiber optic connectors and interconnect systems, antennas, sensors and sensor-based products, and several kinds of cable. Connectors are the parts that link electronic components and carry power and signals between them. Its customers work in automotive, commercial aerospace, communications networks, defense, industrial, information technology and data communications, and mobile devices. Parts of the company date to 1932, and it has been incorporated in Delaware since 1986.

    It reports three segments. Communications Solutions makes connectors, cable, antennas and other products, including high-speed and fiber optic products, and is its largest segment. Harsh Environment Solutions makes ruggedized interconnect products, specialty cable and printed circuits. Interconnect and Sensor Systems makes sensors, sensor-based systems, connectors and value-add interconnect systems.

    Amphenol works with customers at the design stage. It says it grows by serving many different markets and customers, and by buying other companies. On January 31, 2025 it completed the acquisition of the Outdoor Wireless Networks segment and Distributed Antenna Systems business, together called Andrew, from Vistance Networks.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Difficulty obtaining certain raw materials and components for itself and its suppliers and customers.
    • Competition in all areas of its business.
    • Swings in its end markets, some of which are cyclical and can drop quickly.
    • Costs and trouble in buying and integrating other companies, and possible goodwill write-downs.
    • Complex export and import controls, sanctions and government contracting rules.
    From Amphenol's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
