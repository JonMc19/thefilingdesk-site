    Goldman Sachs financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    The Goldman Sachs Group, Inc. · NYSE: GS Latest fiscal year ended December 31, 2025 10-K filed February 25, 2026 · 0000886982-26-000091 Updated October 5, 2026

    Goldman Sachs's revenue rose 8.9% to $58.3B in fiscal 2025. Net income was $17.2B, up 20.3%. Its return on equity was 13.9%, up from 11.9% a year earlier. Its efficiency ratio was 64.4%, up from 63.1% a year earlier. It paid out $17.6B in dividends and buybacks. The diluted share count fell 4.8%.
