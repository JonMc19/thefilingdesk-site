    What NIO does

    NIO is a Chinese company that designs, develops, makes and sells smart electric vehicles. Its Chinese name, Weilai, means Blue Sky Coming. It sells vehicles under three brands: NIO, which it describes as its smart electric vehicle brand; ONVO, a family-oriented brand; and FIREFLY, a small high-end electric car brand.

    In 2025 it delivered 326,028 vehicles, including 178,806 from NIO, 107,808 from ONVO and 39,414 from FIREFLY. It builds technology in-house in 12 areas, including assisted and intelligent driving, battery swapping, and the electric powertrain and battery. Battery swapping lets drivers exchange a drained battery for a charged one at a station, and NIO relies on a Battery Asset Company to provide Battery as a Service to its users.

    Its global research and development center for production models is in Shanghai, its advanced vehicle manufacturing center is in Anhui, and its global research and development center for software is in Beijing. It makes its vehicles in three manufacturing bases.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition in the automotive market and swings in the China vehicle market.
    • A record of significant losses and negative cash flow, which may continue in the near term.
    • Limited experience making vehicles at scale, with possible delays in launching products and ramping up production.
    • Reliance on its Battery Asset Company to run Battery as a Service smoothly.
    • Rising international political tension and trade policies, and the loss of government incentives for electric vehicles.
