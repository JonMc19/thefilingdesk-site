    What Korn Ferry does

    Korn Ferry is a consulting firm that works on the people side of business. It helps clients choose and develop leaders, hire staff, design pay and benefits, and plan the roles and skills they need. Its clients include large companies, which it says range from emerging businesses to global enterprises.

    It gets fees from several services. Executive search finds senior leaders, and professional search and interim staffing fill other roles. Recruitment process outsourcing runs a client's hiring for it. Consulting and Digital sell advice, assessments and software, including subscription products such as its Korn Ferry Talent Suite. It reports segments for executive search in North America, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, Asia Pacific and Latin America, and others for its other services. Its fee revenue was $924.1 million in fiscal 2026.

    The firm says it has studied how people and organizations perform for over 50 years. It had 8,965 full-time employees on April 30, 2026. Its fiscal year ends on April 30.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Competition that could cost it market share or force it to lower its fees.
    • Limits on recruiting from certain clients because of off-limit agreements.
    • Failing to recruit and keep experienced consultants, which could cost it clients.
    • Damage to its professional reputation and brand.
    • Fixed-fee projects that cost more than it estimated, and keeping its consultants busy and billing.
    From Korn Ferry's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
