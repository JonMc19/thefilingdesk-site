    What Ambev does

    Ambev is a Brazilian drinks company. It says it is the largest brewer in Latin America by sales volume, and that it is one of the largest beer producers in the world, by its own estimates. It makes, distributes and sells beer, soft drinks, other alcoholic drinks and non-alcoholic, non-carbonated products directly in 11 countries in the Americas. Its beer brands include Skol, Brahma, Antarctica and Quilmes, and it also sells international brands such as Budweiser, Corona and Stella Artois. In soft drinks it sells Pepsi under exclusive production and bottling agreements with PepsiCo, and says it leads the non-cola market with Guaraná Antarctica.

    Ambev runs four business segments: Brazil, which has a beer division and a division for non-alcoholic drinks; Central America and the Caribbean; Latin America South, which covers Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay, Uruguay and Chile; and Canada, where it runs Labatt. Brazil is the biggest, with more than half of net sales. Its reporting currency is the Brazilian real.

    Ambev's indirect controlling shareholder is Anheuser-Busch InBev (ABI), which can decide the outcome of its most significant corporate actions. As a foreign company, Ambev files an annual report on Form 20-F instead of a 10-K.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Operational problems that could partly or temporarily shut down its plants.
    • Falls in the value of the Brazilian real and other currencies it operates in.
    • Volatile commodity prices for its ingredients and packaging.
    • Tax increases on beverages, and tax evasion by competitors.
    • Economic and political uncertainty in Brazil, and a controlling shareholder that can decide major actions.
