    What Travere Therapeutics does

    Travere Therapeutics is a drug company based in San Diego that develops and sells medicines for rare kidney and metabolic diseases. Its main product is FILSPARI (sparsentan), which the U.S. Food and Drug Administration fully approved in September 2024 to slow the decline of kidney function in adults with primary IgA nephropathy, a rare, progressive kidney disease, who are at risk of getting worse. It had first won accelerated approval in February 2023. It also sells Thiola and Thiola EC, which treat cystinuria, a rare genetic disorder that causes recurring kidney stones.

    It earns money by selling its approved medicines. As of the annual report, it was also seeking approval of FILSPARI for focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS), another rare kidney disease. It had asked the FDA for traditional approval, and the FDA's target date for a decision was extended to April 13, 2026.

    It is also running a pivotal Phase 3 trial of pegtibatinase, an investigational enzyme replacement therapy for homocystinuria (HCU), a genetic disorder. It paused enrollment in September 2024 to improve the manufacturing process, and restarted it in the first quarter of 2026.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its future depends heavily on selling FILSPARI well and winning acceptance from doctors, patients and payers.
    • Its late-stage programs may not win approval, including the FSGS application and pegtibatinase.
    • It faces generic and other competition, and its medicines must prove advantages over other therapies.
    • It depends on third parties to make and distribute its products.
    • Healthcare reform, price controls and changes in insurance reimbursement could cut pricing and demand.
