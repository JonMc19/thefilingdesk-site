    What 10x Genomics does

    This company sells instruments, chemical consumables and software that let scientists study biology one cell at a time and see where molecules sit inside tissue. Its customers are researchers at universities, medical research centers and drug companies. The company says its products have been used in more than 10,000 peer-reviewed articles.

    It has two main product families. Its single cell portfolio, built on the Chromium platform, splits samples into tiny partitions, tags each with a barcode, and then reads them together on a sequencer, so researchers can measure many cells in parallel. Its spatial portfolio, built on the Visium and Xenium platforms, maps where biological molecules are in a tissue sample. It earns money from selling instruments and from the chips, reagents and other consumables customers must buy to run them.

    Revenue was $642.8 million in 2025, up 5% from the year before. It reported a net loss in 2025 and in 2024.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its quarterly and yearly results can swing widely and are hard to predict.
    • It faces intense competition, including rivals developing new technologies that could replace its products.
    • Price cuts or discounts could hurt its results if it does not gain offsetting benefits.
    • It may fail to develop new products or improved versions of existing ones.
    • Its demand depends on research spending by institutions, and a reduction would limit sales.
