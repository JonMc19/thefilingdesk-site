    What Stoneridge does

    Stoneridge, founded in 1965, makes electronic systems for commercial trucks, cars, off-highway and agricultural vehicles. Its products include sensors, actuators, driver information displays, camera vision systems such as its MirrorEye camera monitor system, control modules, connectivity and compliance products, and vehicle tracking and monitoring services.

    It sells mostly to vehicle makers, mainly on a sole-source basis, and to some suppliers that sell to those makers. It says it has shifted its mix toward smart products, meaning products with embedded electronics or logic. It has plants and offices in many countries.

    It reported three segments at the end of 2025: Control Devices, Electronics and Stoneridge Brazil. In January 2026 it announced the sale of the Control Devices segment after a strategic review, and it now operates in two segments.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A downturn in commercial, automotive, off-highway and agricultural vehicle markets.
    • Loss or insolvency of a principal customer, or loss of a vehicle model it supplies.
    • Future sales from awarded programs not turning out as estimated.
    • Debt that could limit its flexibility, and the covenants on it.
    • Raw material price swings and prices fixed in advance, so cost overruns fall on it.
    From Stoneridge's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
