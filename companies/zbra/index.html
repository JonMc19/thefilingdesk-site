    What Zebra Technologies does

    Zebra Technologies designs, makes and sells tools that let businesses track and manage items, assets and people, known as automatic identification and data capture (AIDC). Its products include mobile computers, barcode scanners and imagers, RFID readers, specialty printers for barcode labels and ID cards, and real-time location systems. It also sells labels and other supplies, machine vision and self-serve touchscreen products, software, and services such as maintenance, repair and technical support.

    Customers are in retail and e-commerce, manufacturing, transportation and logistics, healthcare, hospitality and the public sector. Zebra sells through its own sales force and through a network of channel partners (resellers and distributors) in 179 countries. It earns money from hardware and supplies sales, services, and cloud software subscriptions.

    In the fourth quarter of 2025 Zebra changed its two reportable segments to Connected Frontline, which covers mobile computing products and related services and software, and Asset Visibility and Automation, which covers barcode and card printing, RFID, location systems, data capture and machine vision. It operates in 129 facilities and has about 10,700 employees worldwide.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Having to adapt its business to a fast-changing technology environment, possibly at high cost.
    • Competition from companies building their own artificial intelligence technologies that may match or beat its own.
    • An order backlog that may not predict future results and may be slow to turn into revenue.
    • Debt that could grow and add to its risks if it borrows more.
    From Zebra Technologies's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
