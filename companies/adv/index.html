    What Advantage Solutions does

    Advantage Solutions provides outsourced sales, marketing, merchandising and sampling services to makers of consumer packaged goods (everyday products sold in stores) and to retailers, mainly in North America. Its services cover grocery, mass-market, club, pharmacy and convenience stores, in more than 100,000 retail locations, for more than 4,000 clients.

    It reports three segments. Branded Services represents manufacturers to retailers, stocks and maintains shelves and displays in stores, and runs shopper marketing, including online. Experiential Services runs in-store and digital sampling, demonstrations and events. Retailer Services supports retailers with merchandising, private brands and retail media. It earns revenue as commissions, fees for service, or cost-plus pricing, depending on the contract.

    Its own employees, which it calls teammates, are a major cost. It says it has simplified operations by selling non-core businesses and updating its enterprise systems.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Wage increases and trouble hiring, training and keeping the many workers who deliver its services.
    • Its largest clients account for a significant part of its revenue.
    • Consolidation among retailers and manufacturers, and clients changing their programs, which could squeeze prices.
    • Policies of retailers that it does not control, and changes in how retail works, including online.
    • Supply chain disruptions and tariffs that could hurt its clients and its own profits.
    From Advantage Solutions's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
