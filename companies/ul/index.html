    What Unilever does

    Unilever makes and sells everyday consumer products around the world. In its 2025 annual report it describes four Business Groups: Beauty & Wellbeing, Personal Care, Home Care and Foods. Its products are sold to shoppers through retailers and other customers.

    Unilever earns its money by selling these branded goods. In 2025 it reported turnover (its word for sales) of €50.5 billion and a net profit of €6.2 billion. Its biggest brands, which it calls Power Brands, made up 78% of turnover.

    In 2025 Unilever separated its Ice Cream business through a demerger. The report presents its figures for the continuing business only, so earlier years are restated to leave Ice Cream out. The company says its aim is to build a more focused and simpler business with stronger brands.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in what consumers want and buy, and the need to keep launching products that sell.
    • Its choices about which businesses and brands to invest in, sell or keep.
    • The cost and rules around plastic packaging.
    • Climate change and nature loss, which could raise ingredient costs and disrupt supply.
    • Making its new operating model work after the Ice Cream demerger.
    From Unilever's annual report on Form 20-F, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
