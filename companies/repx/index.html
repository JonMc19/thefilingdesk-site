    What Riley Exploration Permian does

    Riley Exploration Permian is an independent oil and natural gas company that drills horizontal wells in the Permian Basin. Most of its acreage is in Yoakum County, Texas and Eddy County, New Mexico. It targets oil-rich formations that it says produce cash flow over the long term.

    It makes money by selling the oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids from its wells. Sales go to a small number of purchasers, as is customary in the business. In 2025 one purchaser accounted for 60% of the revenue it bought, and the company says the loss of either of its two main purchasers could materially hurt it. Following a deal in which Targa bought its gas gatherer, it expects to sell nearly all its natural gas and liquids to a single purchaser.

    The company says its goals include a higher return on invested capital, free cash flow, a flexible balance sheet and returns to shareholders. It says competition in the industry is intense, which makes it harder to buy properties and sell its oil and gas.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling oil and gas prices could force write-downs of its properties and cut cash flow.
    • Limits on disposal of produced water in parts of the Permian Basin could raise its costs.
    • It depends on a small number of purchasers for its sales.
    • Wells may not produce as expected, and reserve estimates rest on assumptions that can prove wrong.
    • Its assets are concentrated in one region, so local rules or problems could affect all its wells.
