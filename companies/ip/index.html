    What International Paper does

    International Paper makes packaging. It makes containerboard (linerboard, medium and other paper that is the base for boxes) and turns it into corrugated boxes, bulk bins, shipping containers and specialty packaging. Customers use these to protect, ship and display goods.

    The company reports two segments: Packaging Solutions North America and Packaging Solutions EMEA (Europe, the Middle East and Africa). In 2025 it completed the acquisition of DS Smith, a packaging company, and says net sales were $23.63 billion for the year. It sold its Global Cellulose Fibers business for $1.5 billion, completed in January 2026, and has exited other non-core businesses.

    In January 2026 International Paper announced plans to split into two independent, publicly traded companies, one in North America and one in EMEA, which it aims to finish near the end of 2026 or early 2027. It also announced plans in 2025 for two new packaging plants. It had about 62,602 employees at the end of 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The planned split into two companies may not be completed as expected or bring the benefits sought.
    • Material disruptions at one of its manufacturing facilities.
    • Labor and employee relations problems.
    • Keeping current customers and winning new ones.
    • Variable-rate debt and the effect of financing costs.
    From International Paper's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
