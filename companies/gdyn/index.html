    What Grid Dynamics Holdings does

    Grid Dynamics is a technology services firm that helps large companies, mainly Fortune 1000 businesses, plan, build and run artificial intelligence systems. It describes itself as an enterprise AI transformation partner. Its work draws on distributed systems, real-time data, machine learning and natural language processing, and includes data and machine learning platform engineering, cloud and product engineering, Internet of Things and edge computing, and digital engagement services.

    It earns money by providing these engineering services to clients, which the filing describes as outsourced services. The company was founded in 2006, is headquartered in Silicon Valley and has offices in the Americas, Europe and India. It also has personnel in Ukraine.

    Grid Dynamics says its revenues have historically depended on a limited number of clients and industries, and that its business is seasonal, tied to holidays in the countries where it works and to the U.S. retail cycle, which affects several of its retail clients. It also pursues acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a limited number of clients and industries for its revenues.
    • Net losses in recent years, which may continue.
    • Personnel in Ukraine and the effects of the military action there.
    • Revenues that depend heavily on clients in the U.S., so a U.S. downturn would hurt.
    • Trouble managing growth, including into new geographies and industries.
    From Grid Dynamics Holdings's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 5, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
