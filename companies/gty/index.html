    What Getty Realty does

    Getty Realty is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns and leases single-tenant retail properties. These include convenience stores, express tunnel car washes, automotive service centers, drive-thru quick service restaurants and other freestanding retail properties. Its predecessor was founded in 1955. It has 1,174 properties in 44 states and Washington, D.C.

    It earns money from rent paid under net leases, in which tenants operate under national and regional retail brands. It acquires properties, finances them and funds the development of new ones. It says its approach rests on the view that cars will remain the main way people travel in the United States. As a REIT it must pay out most of its taxable income to shareholders.

    Getty is run by its own management team, based in New York, and it has few employees. In 2025 it paid for more properties by selling stock, borrowing and issuing new senior notes.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on tenants to pay rent, renew leases and re-lease or buy back properties.
    • Having tenants that depend on the same industry for their revenue.
    • Tenants whose credit quality is hard to judge because they are not rated.
    • Costs and liabilities under environmental laws.
    • Having a material portion of properties concentrated in certain states.
    From Getty Realty's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
