    What ProPetro does

    ProPetro, based in Midland, Texas, provides oilfield services to oil and gas producers, mostly in the Permian Basin of West Texas and New Mexico. Its main service is hydraulic fracturing, which pumps pressurized fluid into a well to crack rock and release oil and gas. It also offers wireline services (lowering tools and instruments into wells) and other related work.

    Its customers are mainly oil and natural gas producers in North America, and a small group of them accounts for most of its revenue. It was previously also in cementing, but sold its cementing business in Utah in 2024. In 2024 it bought a company that provides wet sand for fracturing at well sites.

    In December 2024 it formed a subsidiary, PROPWR, which provides turnkey power generation using mobile equipment installed at customers' sites, for oil and gas producers, industrial projects and data centers. This business began producing revenue in the third quarter of 2025. ProPetro says it competes against different companies in each service line and that its markets are highly competitive.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Oil and gas cycles make its operating results rise and fall.
    • Most of its work is in the Permian Basin, so problems in that one region could hurt it.
    • The new power generation business is a new line of work and may not meet its expectations.
    • A handful of customers provide much of its revenue, and the loss of work from one would hurt.
    • Debt and a need for large amounts of capital could restrict its operations and growth.
    From ProPetro's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
