    What Malibu Boats does

    Malibu Boats designs, builds and sells recreational powerboats under nine brands: Malibu, Axis, Pursuit, Maverick, Cobia, Pathfinder, Hewes, Cobalt and Saxdor. Its range covers performance sport boats for water sports such as wakeboarding and wake surfing, sterndrive and outboard boats for general boating, fiberglass fishing boats, and premium adventure dayboats.

    It sells through independent dealers, and its segments are Malibu, Saltwater Fishing, Cobalt and Saxdor. Malibu boats are its premium sport boats, and Axis boats are a lower-priced alternative. Pursuit and the Maverick Boat Group make saltwater fishing boats, Cobalt makes sterndrive boats, and Saxdor, a recent acquisition, makes premium adventure dayboats. The company says it ranks among the market leaders in several of these categories in the United States.

    It holds proprietary technology, such as the Surf Gate wake system, which it says introduced new buyers to water sports. Its fiscal year ends June 30.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large fixed-cost base that cuts profit when demand falls.
    • Difficulty forecasting demand and managing inventory.
    • Higher costs from suppliers, including tariffs, and its dependence on a small group of component suppliers.
    • Problems carrying out its manufacturing strategy, and finding and keeping factory workers.
    • Trouble completing and integrating acquisitions such as Saxdor.
    From Malibu Boats's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
