    What CPI Card Group does

    CPI Card Group is a payments technology company. It makes debit and credit cards and prepaid debit cards for US banks and credit unions, card processors, financial technology companies and prepaid program managers. It also personalizes the cards with each cardholder's data, mails them out, and runs a software-based service that lets a bank issue a personalized card on the spot in a branch. It produces mostly contactless cards, and it sells cards made partly from recycled materials under the Second Wave and Earthwise names.

    Its revenue comes mainly from producing secure debit and credit cards, including prepaid debit cards, and from related services. It has two reporting segments: Debit and Credit, and Prepaid Debit, which sells secure packaging, prepaid debit cards and related services to prepaid program managers. It says it serves thousands of customers through direct and indirect sales, and that the market is highly competitive, relatively saturated and increasingly consolidated.

    In May 2025 it bought Arroweye, which lets it offer an on-demand card service that cuts the need for customers to hold inventories of customized cards. It had about 1,700 employees at the end of 2025 and is starting to sell to other industries, such as healthcare.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losing existing customers or failing to win new ones.
    • Competition in a highly competitive, saturated and consolidating market that could cut its market share and profits.
    • Supply chain disruptions, and tariffs and other changes in trade policy.
    • Production disruptions, quality problems and product defect claims.
    • Covenants and restrictions in its debt agreements that may limit its business and growth.
