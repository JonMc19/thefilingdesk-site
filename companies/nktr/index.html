    What Nektar Therapeutics does

    Nektar Therapeutics is a research-based drug company that develops medicines that act on the immune system. It has no drug of its own on the market. Its pipeline targets autoimmune diseases, in which the immune system attacks the body's healthy cells, and cancer.

    Its lead drug candidate, rezpegaldesleukin, is designed to increase the function of regulatory T cells, immune cells that hold other immune cells in check. It has been tested in atopic dermatitis, a skin disease, and Nektar is studying it in other conditions, including alopecia areata and type 1 diabetes. Nektar says it owns full rights to it after Eli Lilly ended a 2017 license agreement in 2023. Other candidates include NKTR-255 for cancer and NKTR-0165 and NKTR-0166, which are earlier-stage candidates for autoimmune disease.

    Nektar has earned revenue from collaboration agreements, such as up-front payments, milestones and royalties. It says that after the Lilly agreement ended it has no collaboration agreement for rezpegaldesleukin, so it expects no partner payments for its lead drug candidates.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It depends heavily on rezpegaldesleukin advancing in clinical studies.
    • It needs a lot of capital and expects continued losses.
    • Its revenue has come from partners, and it may not strike new partnerships on good terms.
    • It relies on contract manufacturers and, for key reagents, a single supplier.
    • Competing immunotherapy drugs could make its candidates obsolete.
