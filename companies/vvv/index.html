    What Valvoline does

    Valvoline runs and franchises quick-service car maintenance centers in the United States and Canada. A visit takes about 15 minutes and customers stay in the car. It covers oil changes, battery, bulb and wiper replacements, tire rotations and other manufacturer-recommended maintenance.

    The company and its franchise partners operate about 2,200 service centers under the Valvoline Instant Oil Change and Valvoline Great Canadian Oil Change names. It also supports more than 240 locations through its Express Care platform. Part of its results therefore depends on the financial results of its franchisees.

    Valvoline was founded in 1866 and in 1873 became the first trademarked motor oil brand in the U.S. It was part of Ashland from 1950, began running and franchising service centers in the late 1980s, and was spun off as a separate company in 2016. It says it faces significant competition that puts downward pressure on prices and margins.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition that pushes down prices and margins.
    • Rising and volatile supply costs, and supply chain problems.
    • Weaker demand from customer spending trends, a weak economy or industry changes.
    • Dependence on the financial results of its franchisees.
    • Problems with its new enterprise resource planning (ERP) system, and growth goals it may not meet.
    From Valvoline's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 21, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
