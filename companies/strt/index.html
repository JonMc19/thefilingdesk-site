    What Strattec does

    Strattec makes access and security parts for vehicles. Its products include locks and keys, electronic key fobs and digital keys, door and trunk latches, and power systems that open sliding doors, tailgates, lift gates and deck lids. It also sells door handles through a joint venture. It says its products are safety critical and work in gas, hybrid and electric vehicles.

    Its customers are mainly automakers and the large suppliers that sell to them: such sales are approximately 90% of its total sales. The rest goes to service channels, the aftermarket and non-automotive customers. A significant portion of its sales is to General Motors, Ford and Stellantis, and its products are model specific, so it depends on how many of those vehicles they build and sell.

    Strattec works with automakers on a vehicle's design years before it goes into production. Once it wins a vehicle platform it typically supplies it for the platform's life, normally five to seven years, though that is not guaranteed. It has no firm backlog of orders, because sales follow purchase orders. It does product engineering in the United States and Mexico, and had 2,654 associates on June 28, 2026, most of them in Mexico.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A large part of its sales comes from a few customers and vehicle programs, so losing one would hurt.
    • The auto industry is cyclical, and weak vehicle production would reduce demand for its products.
    • Changes in tariffs, trade policy and U.S.-Mexico border rules could raise its costs or delay shipments.
    • It is smaller than many auto suppliers and faces competition, including from Chinese suppliers.
    • Shortages of semiconductor chips and other parts, or labor cost inflation in Mexico, could hurt production.
    From Strattec's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 28, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
