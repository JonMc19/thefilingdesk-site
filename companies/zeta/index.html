    What Zeta Global does

    Zeta Global sells cloud software that helps large companies find, reach and keep customers through marketing. Its main product, the Zeta Marketing Platform, combines a large database of consumer data with artificial intelligence to plan and send personalized marketing by email, text, social media, websites, connected TV and chat.

    Customers use the platform to decide whom to target, send messages across many channels and measure the results. The company relies on a few large accounts. It calls customers that have generated at least a set amount of revenue over 12 months "super-scaled," and says 87% of its 2025 revenue came from them. It had 184 such customers at the end of 2025, out of 2,651 in all.

    Zeta says its customers often work with several marketing providers at once and can switch between them without much cost. It runs its platform on a mix of public cloud services and its own private computing resources. It recently bought the enterprise business of Marigold, another marketing software company.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its growth depends on adding, growing and keeping its largest customers.
    • Long sales cycles make it hard to predict when new customers will bring in revenue.
    • Its results swing with election years, when it usually earns more.
    • Intense competition, and customers that can use rival providers at little cost.
    • Trouble combining acquisitions such as Marigold's enterprise business.
    From Zeta Global's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
