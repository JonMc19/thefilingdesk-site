    What First Hawaiian does

    First Hawaiian is a bank holding company based in Honolulu. Its subsidiary, First Hawaiian Bank, was founded in 1858 and says it is the largest bank headquartered in Hawaii by loans and leases and by net income. It runs 49 branches in Hawaii, Guam and Saipan.

    The bank offers deposit products such as checking and savings accounts, and lends to consumers and businesses. For middle-market and large Hawaii businesses it makes commercial and industrial loans, including auto dealer flooring (financing dealers' inventory), commercial real estate and construction loans. For individuals and small businesses it offers residential mortgages, indirect auto financing and other consumer loans. It also provides trust, private banking and investment management services, credit cards and merchant processing.

    It reports two segments, Retail Banking and Commercial Banking, and puts other activities, including Treasury, in Corporate/Other. It earns money from interest on its loans and securities and from fees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak financial markets or economic conditions in Hawaii, Guam and Saipan.
    • A large share of its loans is secured by real estate, so a real estate downturn would hurt.
    • Concentrated exposure to certain asset classes and large borrowers.
    • Interest rate changes that could hurt its earnings.
    • Losing deposits, which would raise its funding costs.
    From First Hawaiian's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
