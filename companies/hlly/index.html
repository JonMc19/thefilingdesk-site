    What Holley does

    Holley designs, makes and sells performance parts for cars and trucks, aimed at automotive enthusiasts mainly in the United States, Canada and Europe. Its products include carburetors, fuel pumps, fuel injection systems, superchargers, exhaust headers and mufflers, ignition components, engine tuners, shifters, transmissions and related software. It has locations in the United States, Canada, Italy and China.

    It owns over 65 brands in over 35 product categories, including Holley, Holley EFI, MSD, Simpson, Flowmaster, EDGE, Cataclean and Accel. Its top five brands generated 49% of 2025 sales. Holley EFI, its largest brand, was 17% of sales.

    It sells through e-tailers, warehouse distributors, traditional retailers and jobber/installers. Its top ten distribution partners made up 42% of 2025 sales, and the largest alone 22%. It says it can expand into new areas such as exterior accessories and mobile electronics.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Higher tariffs and trade limits on the products and materials it imports.
    • Weaker consumer spending on hobby purchases in a slow economy, and fuel shortages or high fuel prices.
    • A shift toward electric vehicles, or away from vehicle ownership, that reduces demand for its parts.
    • Relying on a few distribution partners and on foreign suppliers.
    • Supply disruptions and rising raw material costs.
    From Holley's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
