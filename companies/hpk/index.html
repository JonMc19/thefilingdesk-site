    What HighPeak Energy does

    HighPeak Energy explores for, develops and produces crude oil, natural gas liquids (NGLs) and natural gas. All of its work is in the Permian Basin of West Texas, mainly in the Midland Basin in Howard and Borden Counties, and to a lesser extent Scurry and Mitchell Counties. Its acreage is in two core areas, Flat Top and Signal Peak.

    It earns revenue by selling the oil and gas it produces. Its business needs a lot of capital to develop new wells, and it says its cash flows can vary a lot from year to year because the industry rises and falls. It uses debt, and the report notes that its debt could limit its flexibility.

    HighPeak is a Delaware corporation formed in October 2019 and its office is in Midland, Texas. It describes itself as a "controlled company" under Nasdaq rules, which means it is exempt from some governance requirements and stockholders do not get all the usual protections.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Reserve estimates that turn out to be wrong.
    • Needing a lot of capital for development, and difficulty reaching the capital markets.
    • Debt that could limit its cash flows and how it runs its business.
    • Swings in the oil and gas cycle that make results change a lot from year to year.
    • Depending on transportation and storage facilities it does not control.
    From HighPeak Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 11, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
