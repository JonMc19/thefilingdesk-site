    What UniFirst does

    UniFirst designs, makes, rents, cleans, delivers and sells uniforms and protective clothing, and rents and sells floor mats, wiping products and facility service items. It also sells first aid cabinet services, safety supplies and fire protection services. Customers are businesses of all sizes in many industries in North America. Usually each week it picks up soiled items and delivers clean ones.

    It reports three segments. Uniform & Facility Service Solutions covers uniforms and non-garment items in the United States and Canada, including cleanroom garments. First Aid & Safety Solutions sells first aid cabinet services, safety supplies, fire safety and safety training. Other is its nuclear garment decontamination business.

    In fiscal 2026 it manufactured approximately 65% of the garments it placed in service, which it says lets it offer custom garment programs and better control quality, price and speed. On March 10, 2026 it agreed to a merger with Cintas Corporation.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in the uniform and facility services industry.
    • Higher labor and health care costs, and labor unrest or union campaigns that could disrupt its business.
    • Inflation that raises its costs when it cannot pass them on in its prices.
    • Failing to carry out its acquisition strategy and to grow its business.
    • Weak general economic conditions that cut demand from its customers.
    From UniFirst's annual report on Form 10-K, filed October 28, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
