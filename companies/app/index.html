    What AppLovin does

    AppLovin sells advertising software that helps companies find users for their mobile apps and helps app makers earn money from the ads shown in them. Its customers are advertisers, mostly app developers who want more users, and publishers, mainly makers of mobile games who sell ad space inside their apps.

    Its main product is Axon Ads Manager, which uses an AI recommendation engine to match advertiser demand with publisher ad space in auctions that run in fractions of a second. Advertisers set a target return on their ad spend and are charged based on those goals, not a fixed price per view or click. Axon Ads Manager brings in substantially all of the company's revenue. Other products are MAX, which runs auctions for publishers' ad space, Adjust, a service that measures and analyzes app marketing, and Wurl, a platform for streaming video and ads on connected TV.

    On June 30, 2025, AppLovin completed the sale of its Apps business, which made mobile games. It says it generally does not have long-term agreements with its clients, and that its revenue has been concentrated in the mobile app ecosystem.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Results that may swing from one period to the next.
    • Changes in the policies of the third-party platforms it depends on, which could hurt its advertising products.
    • Heavy dependence on its co-founder and chief executive and a lean management team.
    • Revenue concentrated in mobile apps, with no guarantee of expanding beyond them.
    • Clients that can leave at any time because it generally has no long-term agreements with them.
    From AppLovin's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
