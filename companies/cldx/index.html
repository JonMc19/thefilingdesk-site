    What Celldex Therapeutics does

    Celldex Therapeutics is a biotechnology company developing antibody drugs. It has no product on the market. Its work centers on mast cells, immune cells involved in allergic and inflammatory reactions, and it targets diseases where current treatments fall short.

    Its lead candidate, barzolvolimab, is an antibody that blocks a receptor called KIT on mast cells. It is being tested in chronic spontaneous urticaria, a long-lasting itchy skin condition with hives, where Celldex says enrollment is complete in its Phase 3 studies and it expects topline data in the fourth quarter of 2026. It also began a Phase 3 study in cold urticaria and symptomatic dermographism in December 2025.

    Celldex has no product revenue and says it will need to raise capital to run its business, including to develop, make and sell its drug candidates.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It has no product revenue and will need to raise capital.
    • It expects losses and may never be profitable.
    • Its drug candidates need regulatory approval, which can be slow, limited or denied.
    • Success in early trials does not ensure success in later ones.
    • Slow patient enrollment or serious side effects could delay or stop testing.
