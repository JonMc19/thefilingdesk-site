    What Simmons First National does

    Simmons First National is a financial holding company based in Pine Bluff, Arkansas. Its lead subsidiary, Simmons Bank, has operated since 1903 and serves individuals and businesses through about 222 financial centers in Arkansas, Kansas, Missouri, Oklahoma, Tennessee and Texas. At the end of 2025 the company had total assets of $24.54 billion, loans of $17.49 billion, deposits of $20.18 billion and equity capital of $3.42 billion.

    For businesses, the bank makes loans for commercial real estate, construction, equipment and general business needs, along with small business administration and agricultural loans, corporate credit cards, deposit products and treasury management. For consumers, it offers deposit accounts, mobile banking, credit cards and real estate, home equity and other loans. It also has a trust department and insurance agencies, and a networking arrangement with a third-party broker-dealer for brokerage services.

    The bank works in three groups: community banking, commercial banking and wealth strategy. The company says it is not dependent on any single customer or industry, and that it grows both on its own and by acquiring other financial institutions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Interest rate and monetary policy changes that could reduce its profits and the value of its investment securities.
    • A shortage of liquidity or of low-cost deposits, or a rise in its funding costs.
    • Credit risks in lending, including a reserve for credit losses that may be too small.
    • Heightened risk in its commercial real estate, construction and commercial loans.
    • Developments in the wider financial services industry and the economy that could hurt its results.
