    What United Community Banks does

    United Community Banks is a bank holding company based in Greenville, South Carolina. Its main subsidiary, United Community Bank, offers deposit accounts, secured and unsecured loans, mortgages, payment solutions, equipment finance, wealth management, trust services, private banking, investment advice and insurance. Customers include businesses, individuals, governments, schools, and energy, health care and real estate firms. It serves them through branches, other offices, the internet and mobile apps.

    It makes money in the usual way for a bank, mainly from interest on loans and securities and from fees. At the end of 2025 it had consolidated total assets of $28.0 billion. It operates in Georgia, South Carolina, North Carolina, Tennessee, Florida and Alabama, and nationally through its SBA and USDA lending and equipment finance businesses.

    It has grown through acquisitions as well as organic growth. On May 1, 2025, it completed the purchase of ANB, a bank with one location in the Fort Lauderdale area. In 2025 it also redeemed all of its Series I preferred stock and all of its outstanding senior debt.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • It may not carry out its strategy to grow its banking businesses.
    • Acquisitions may not succeed if the businesses are not integrated well.
    • It faces intense competition, and technology-driven disruption may displace traditional banks.
    • Customers' changing habits may make its in-person service approach less effective.
    • Fraud and cyberattacks are growing risks for banks like it.
