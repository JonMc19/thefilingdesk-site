    What Ibotta does

    Ibotta runs the Ibotta Performance Network, which delivers digital offers from consumer packaged goods brands to shoppers. Clients, mostly consumer packaged goods brands, load promotions into the network. Ibotta then distributes them to consumers through its own cash back app, website and browser extension, and through third-party publishers.

    Publishers use Ibotta's content to run their own digital offer programs under their own names. They include Walmart, Dollar General, Family Dollar, Instacart and DoorDash. Ibotta gets paid when a client's promotion leads to a purchase by a consumer. It also works with partners such as Circana and ABCS Insights so that clients can have the effect of their campaigns checked by a third party.

    At the end of 2025 it worked with over 900 clients, representing over 3,100 brands. Most of its offers cover everyday categories such as grocery, but it also sources offers for toys, clothing, beauty, electronics, pet and home goods. In 2025 it introduced LiveLift, a set of tools meant to help brands measure and drive added sales. It had about 800 full-time employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of net losses, with expenses expected to rise.
    • Failing to keep or grow the supply of offers and redemptions on its network.
    • Not renewing or expanding relationships with its clients, retailers and publishers.
    • Depending on publishers, and on technology partners that pass its content to them.
    • Competition from other promotional tools, including paper coupons and other digital coupons.
    From Ibotta's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
