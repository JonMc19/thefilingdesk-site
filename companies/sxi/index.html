    What Standex International does

    Standex, based in Salem, New Hampshire, is an industrial manufacturer that makes engineered components and custom products for commercial and industrial customers. It runs four segments: Electronics, Aerospace and Defense, Scientific, and Engraving & Hydraulics.

    Electronics makes sensing and switching components, transformers and magnetic power parts. Aerospace and Defense serves military and space customers. Scientific makes temperature-controlled equipment, such as refrigerators and freezers, for medical, laboratory, pharmacy and biotech buyers. Engraving & Hydraulics applies custom textures and finishes to the tooling used to make consumer goods and car parts, and makes hydraulic cylinders for dump trucks, refuse trucks and other mobile equipment.

    Its fiscal year ends June 30. At that date in 2026 it had about 4,100 employees, about 1,400 of them in the United States. It says it aims to grow in markets such as the power grid, defense, commercial space, life sciences, renewable energy and electric vehicles, partly through acquisitions, and it sells off businesses that do not fit.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tariffs on parts and finished goods from China, India and other countries.
    • Higher prices or shortages of the commodities it uses.
    • Failing to launch new products or to find and complete acquisitions that fuel growth.
    • Depending on its credit facility for the capital to run its businesses and fund acquisitions.
    • Risks of operating in many countries, including hostilities that disrupt shipping.
    From Standex International's annual report on Form 10-K, filed August 14, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
