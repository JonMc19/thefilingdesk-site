    What Cheesecake Factory does

    Cheesecake Factory owns and operates restaurants in the United States and Canada. As of February 23, 2026, it ran 368 restaurants: 216 The Cheesecake Factory, 48 North Italia, 43 Flower Child and 55 other restaurants from its Fox Restaurant Concepts group. Another 35 The Cheesecake Factory restaurants operate abroad under licensing agreements. It also runs two bakeries that make cheesecakes and other baked goods for its restaurants, international licensees and outside customers.

    Most of its revenue comes from restaurant sales. The Cheesecake Factory restaurants offer moderate prices, a menu of approximately 225 items and a full-service bar. In fiscal 2025, alcoholic drinks were 11% of sales at those restaurants, and food and drinks taken off the premises were approximately 21%. It uses a third party for delivery and offers online ordering for to-go sales.

    The company warns that wage and commodity inflation, tariffs and supply chain problems could raise its costs or delay new restaurant openings. Its business started in 1972 as a small bakery, and the first restaurant opened in Beverly Hills, California, in 1978.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak economic conditions that reduce spending by diners and raise its costs.
    • Not being able to grow sales at its existing restaurants.
    • Damage to its brands and reputation, which it calls its greatest asset.
    • Labor cost inflation and attempts to organize its workers.
    • Food safety problems or health emergencies that cut customer traffic or disrupt its supply chain.
    From Cheesecake Factory's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
