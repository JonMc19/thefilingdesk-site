    What Ametek does

    Ametek makes electronic instruments and electromechanical devices, with operations in North America, Europe, Asia and South America. It sells worldwide to makers of aircraft, medical equipment, industrial machinery and power systems, and to process industries.

    It reports two operating groups. Electronic Instruments builds instruments for process, power, industrial and aerospace markets. Process and analytical instruments were 70% of that group's 2025 net sales and the rest came from aerospace and power instruments. Electromechanical makes precision motion control products, medical components and devices, thermal management systems, specialty metals and electrical interconnects for aerospace and defense, medical and automation markets.

    Ametek grows partly by buying other companies, and it says it holds strong positions in narrow niche markets. Its customer base is spread out: no single customer makes up more than 2% of net sales. It has factories in China, Czechia, Malaysia, Mexico and Serbia, close to customers.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A downturn in the markets it sells into, which are cyclical, including aerospace and defense.
    • Risks of selling and operating in many countries, including export law compliance.
    • Failing to find, complete or successfully absorb the acquisitions its growth plan relies on.
    • Shortages or higher prices for components and raw materials.
    • Competition in each of its industries, and failing to develop new products on time.
    From Ametek's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 17, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
