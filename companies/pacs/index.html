    What PACS Group does

    PACS Group runs skilled nursing facilities in the United States. These provide round-the-clock care to people who have left the hospital but still need help recovering, and some also offer long-term care. It also provides assisted living and independent living in some of its communities. It operates 321 post-acute care facilities in 17 states, serving over 31,700 patients a day.

    PACS was founded in 2013. It says it has grown mainly by buying skilled nursing facilities that focus on long-term custodial care and turning them into facilities for short-term transitional care. It says it gives each facility local leaders who run it independently, with technology, support and back-office services from the center. It reports a single segment and earns revenue mainly from Medicare, Medicaid and other payors.

    The 10-K says a short-seller report was published about the company in November 2024. An audit committee investigation found material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting and led to a restatement of two quarters of financial statements. The company and some executives face a securities class action, and the company has received subpoenas from the Department of Justice.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its revenue depends on payments from Medicare, Medicaid and other payors, and changes in those could hurt it.
    • A short-seller report led to restated results, weaknesses in controls, lawsuits and government investigations.
    • A shortage of nurses and other staff could raise its labor costs.
    • Providing poor quality of care could hurt its patients and its business.
    • It may not be able to buy more facilities at good prices.
