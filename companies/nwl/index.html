    What Newell Brands does

    Newell Brands makes and sells household and consumer products under brands that include Rubbermaid, Sharpie, Graco, Coleman, Yankee Candle, Paper Mate and FoodSaver. It sells in over 150 countries.

    It runs three operating segments. Home and Commercial Solutions covers kitchen, home storage and commercial products, including Yankee Candle, which also has its own stores. Learning and Development covers writing instruments, art products, labeling and baby gear. Outdoor and Recreation covers outdoor products such as technical apparel and on-the-go beverageware. It sells mostly to mass merchants, warehouse clubs, department stores, e-commerce retailers and distributors, and also directly to consumers online.

    A few customers matter a great deal. Amazon, its largest customer in 2025, accounted for approximately 17% of net sales, and Walmart, the second largest, for approximately 13%. Newell says it competes with many large, well-established makers of consumer products. It describes a multi-year turnaround plan and a plan to cut its global workforce by over 900 employees, mostly professional and clerical staff.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Intense competition in a market dominated by large retailers, including e-commerce companies.
    • Depending on a few large customers, who may consolidate or press it to cut prices.
    • Whether its turnaround plan and restructuring work as hoped.
    • Failing to keep launching new products and to build its brands.
    • Reliance on outside suppliers and vendors, and the burden of its debt.
    From Newell Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2022, earnings per share fell 15.0% a year and net income 15.1% a year, while buybacks cut the share count by 1%. Newell Brands then lost money in fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2025.
