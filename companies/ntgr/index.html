    What Netgear does

    Netgear makes networking equipment, software and services for businesses, homes and service providers. Its products include WiFi routers and mesh systems, mobile hotspots, switches, wireless access points, firewalls and tools for managing networks from the cloud. It also sells subscription services for performance, security, privacy and support.

    The company reports two segments. Enterprise serves small and medium businesses with products such as audio and video over Ethernet, cloud-managed networking, and security. Consumer sells WiFi routers and mesh systems, subscription services and 4G/5G mobile products to households and individuals. It sells through retailers, distributors and broadband service providers, such as telephone and cable companies, in the Americas, Europe, the Middle East and Africa, and Asia Pacific. Net revenue was $699.6 million in 2025.

    Netgear says it is in the middle of a multi-phase transformation. The first phase, which began in 2024, is finished. The second phase aims to strengthen its core businesses through better product development, selling and cost control. A later phase may add growth initiatives, including acquisitions.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on a few retail and distribution channels and large customers whose purchases can drop or be delayed.
    • Struggles in selling through broadband service providers, a hard channel to manage.
    • Falling selling prices for its products over their life, which squeezes revenue and margins.
    • Reliance on third-party manufacturers and the supply chain risks that follow.
    • Trouble using AI, software and subscriptions to drive growth as technology changes.
    From Netgear's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
