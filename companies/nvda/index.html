    Nvidia financials, fiscal 2017 to 2026

    NVIDIA CORP · Nasdaq: NVDA Latest fiscal year ended January 25, 2026 10-K filed February 25, 2026 · 0001045810-26-000021 Updated October 7, 2026
    Read our report: Nvidia revenue rises 65.5% to $215.9 billion as gross margin slips to 71.1%

    Nvidia's revenue rose 65.5% to $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Its operating margin was 60.4%, down from 62.4% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $96.7 billion, up 58.9%. It paid out $41.1 billion in dividends and buybacks, 42% of free cash flow. The diluted share count fell 1.2%.

    How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation, and this site does not show share prices.
