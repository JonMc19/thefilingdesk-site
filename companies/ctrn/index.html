    What Citi Trends does

    Citi Trends is an off-price retailer (it sells brand-name and fashion goods at lower prices than traditional stores) of apparel for women, men and children, family footwear, accessories and home products. It says it focuses on Black customers and locates its stores in Black communities, mainly in outdoor community shopping centers in urban, suburban and rural markets.

    As of January 31, 2026, it operated 590 stores in 33 states. A store averages about 11,000 square feet of selling space. It sells in three tiers: basics at the lowest prices for budget shoppers, a core tier it calls better, and a top tier it calls best, where it is adding trend items priced below specialty retail and well-known brands at steep discounts to the manufacturer's suggested retail price.

    The company says its stores' community locations and the size of its store network are hard for others to copy. It generally uses previously occupied store sites.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to read changes in fashion tastes quickly enough.
    • Competition from other apparel retailers, and its dependence on the outdoor community shopping centers where its stores sit.
    • Mismanaging inventory or not being able to fill its merchandising needs.
    • Seasonal swings that make the first and fourth quarters more exposed to bad events.
    • Reliance on third parties to produce and deliver its products, and the need to hire and keep staff while controlling labor costs.
    From Citi Trends's annual report on Form 10-K, filed April 15, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
