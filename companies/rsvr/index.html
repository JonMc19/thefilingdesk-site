    What Reservoir Media does

    Reservoir Media is an independent music company. It owns and manages music copyrights and recordings, some dating back to the early 1900s, and also runs a management business for artists and writers. It grows partly by buying song catalogs and by signing new songwriters and artists.

    It reports two segments. Music Publishing represents songwriters and collects money when their songs are used. It brought in about $117 million in revenue for the year ended March 31, 2026, roughly 66% of the total. Recorded Music includes labels such as Chrysalis Records and Tommy Boy Music, and markets and distributes recordings from its catalog. It contributed about $52 million, roughly 29% of revenue.

    Its publishing catalog includes songs by writers such as Joni Mitchell and Miles Davis, and it works with current writer-performers such as Snoop Dogg and Sheryl Crow. Its recorded music catalog covers artists such as De La Soul and Queen Latifah.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Strong competition in the music business, which could reduce its profits.
    • Trouble carrying out its strategy, including buying and integrating catalogs.
    • Currency swings and events in foreign countries where it operates.
    • Rates for much of its revenue are set or limited by governments or collecting societies.
    • Past material weaknesses in its financial reporting controls.
    From Reservoir Media's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 28, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
