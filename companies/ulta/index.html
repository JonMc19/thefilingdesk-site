    What Ulta Beauty does

    Ulta Beauty is a specialty beauty retailer. It sells cosmetics, fragrance, skin care, hair care and wellness products, and it offers salon services. It was founded in Illinois in 1990 and says it is the largest specialty beauty retailer in the U.S. It sells through its stores and digital platforms.

    Its range spans prestige, mass-market and salon brands at many price points. Its target shopper is what it calls a "beauty enthusiast," someone who is passionate about beauty and expects a good shopping experience. Outside the U.S., it owns Space NK, a retailer in the U.K. and Ireland, runs a joint venture in Mexico, and has a franchise in the Middle East.

    Ulta Beauty competes in a crowded market. As of January 31, 2026 it employed about 65,000 associates in the U.S.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak consumer spending on beauty products and salon services.
    • Strong competition from other beauty sellers.
    • Managing inventory badly, which could cut sales and results.
    • Not meeting shoppers' merchandise needs as they change.
    • Disruption at its distribution, fast fulfillment and market fulfillment centers.
    From Ulta Beauty's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
