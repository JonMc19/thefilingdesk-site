    What NRG Energy does

    NRG sells electricity, natural gas and smart-home services to about 8 million residential customers, and also to large business, data center and wholesale customers. Its brands include NRG, Reliant, Direct Energy, Green Mountain Energy and Vivint. It sells power in 25 U.S. states, the District of Columbia and eight Canadian provinces, with its strongest position in Texas.

    NRG makes money by selling power and gas under fixed, indexed or month-to-month prices, and by selling smart-home equipment, installation and monitoring through Vivint. It also owns power plants, mostly natural gas and coal, mainly in Texas, which supply part of its own customers' demand. It reports five segments: Texas, East, West/Other, Vivint Smart Home and corporate. NRG says it competes with utilities, other retail energy sellers, independent power producers and, in smart homes, technology companies and security providers.

    In January 2026 NRG completed the purchase of a group of 18 natural gas and dual-fuel power plants in nine states, along with a demand response business, which pays customers to use less power at busy times. It is also building new gas plants in Texas.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Combining the newly bought power plants and demand response business with NRG may disrupt its business.
    • Swings in power and natural gas prices, and in what it costs to supply its retail customers.
    • Expected growth in power demand, including from data centers, may not happen.
    • Running power plants carries hazards that insurance may not fully cover.
    • Struggling to keep customers and sell them more products, such as bundling energy with smart-home services.
    From NRG Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
