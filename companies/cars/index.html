    What Cars.com does

    Cars Commerce, which runs the Cars.com marketplace, operates an online platform for buying and selling cars. Shoppers use it to search listings, read reviews and expert content, and use financing and trade-in value tools. It says its marketplace had an average of 26 million monthly users in 2025, plus more than 4.6 million monthly unique vehicle identification numbers (VINs) and more than 16 million consumer reviews at the end of 2025.

    Its customers are dealers and automakers (OEMs). It says about 19,500 franchise and independent dealers in the U.S. and Canada use its platform, and substantially all OEMs selling vehicles in the U.S. and Canada do business with it. Dealers subscribe to the marketplace, to more tools such as dealer websites and trade and appraisal tools, or to website products alone.

    Substantially all of its revenue comes from subscription products sold to dealers and other customers in or near the auto industry. It also offers media advertising solutions and AI-powered shopping features.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Exposure to the wider auto industry, including consumer demand and direct-to-consumer sales models.
    • Revenue that comes almost entirely from subscriptions sold to auto dealers and related customers.
    • Dealer or automaker closures and consolidation, and influence held by a few large OEMs and dealer groups.
    • Keeping its brand strong and drawing traffic to its sites with its own content.
    • The growing use and regulation of generative and agentic AI, and risks in its expanding Canadian operations.
    From Cars.com's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
