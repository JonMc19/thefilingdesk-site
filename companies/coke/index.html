    Coca-Cola Consolidated

    COKE · Nasdaq · Consumer Staples · Financials, fiscal 2016 to 2025

    Coca-Cola Consolidated's revenue rose 4.8% to $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 13.2%, down from 13.3% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $620 million, up 22.6%. It paid out $2.7 billion in dividends and buybacks, 435% of free cash flow.

    Valuation

    Two ways to value Coca-Cola Consolidated yourself, both starting from its filings: a discounted cash flow model and the price-to-earnings method.

    1Discounted cash flow2P/E method

    1Discounted cash flow (DCF)

    A discounted cash flow (DCF) model values a business as the cash it may generate in future, discounted back to today. The starting figures come from Coca-Cola Consolidated's 10-K (or, for net cash and shares, its latest 10-Q) and the assumptions are yours: start from one of three example scenarios, then change any figure to see what it implies for a share.

    The example scenarios need Coca-Cola Consolidated's diluted shares, which its filings' structured data doesn't give for its latest year. You can enter your own figure in the calculator.

    How the model works. Free cash flow grows at the first rate for five years and the second rate for five more, and each year is discounted at the discount rate. Everything after year 10 is valued as year-10 cash flow × (1 + growth after year 10) ÷ (discount rate − growth after year 10), discounted back ten years. Net cash is added and the total is divided by diluted shares. The result depends entirely on the assumptions. It is not a forecast, a price target or a recommendation.

    2Price-to-earnings (P/E) method

    Another common way to value a company is to multiply its earnings per share by a price-to-earnings (P/E) multiple: the number of years of today's earnings an investor pays for a share. A higher multiple usually means investors expect earnings to grow. Choose a multiple, for example from similar companies, to see the value it gives.

    The P/E method needs positive earnings, and Coca-Cola Consolidated's latest diluted earnings per share were not available.
