    What VAALCO Energy does

    VAALCO Energy finds, develops and produces crude oil, natural gas and natural gas liquids (NGLs). It is based in Houston, Texas, and most of its assets are in Africa: Gabon, Egypt, Cote d'Ivoire, Equatorial Guinea and Nigeria. It also had producing properties in Canada, which it sold in February 2026, leaving the Canadian oil and gas business entirely.

    Its revenue comes from selling the oil and gas it produces. Much of its work is offshore, which the company says carries special risks. It does not operate some of its assets, and on others it shares decisions with partners under joint operating agreements. It says it tries to lower costs, fund drilling from cash on hand and cash from operations, and buy properties like the ones it has.

    It uses derivative contracts to hedge some price exposure. The company also notes that the floating production ship at its Cote d'Ivoire field stopped producing in early 2025 for scheduled maintenance.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Needing a lot of capital for its projects and not being able to raise it.
    • Failing to replace the oil reserves it produces, and reserve estimates that prove wrong.
    • Limited control over assets and joint operations run by partners.
    • Special risks of offshore operations, including the production ship stopping at its Cote d'Ivoire field.
    • Losing its interest in Block P in Equatorial Guinea if it does not meet its commitments.
    From VAALCO Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 16, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
