    What AtriCure does

    AtriCure makes devices that surgeons use to treat atrial fibrillation (Afib), an irregular heartbeat, to manage the left atrial appendage (a small pouch in the heart where blood clots can form), and to ease pain after surgery.

    Its ablation and left atrial appendage products are used during open-heart surgery for another condition, such as a valve repair or bypass, and in stand-alone minimally invasive procedures, sometimes combined with catheter ablation by a heart-rhythm specialist. Its pain products, including cryoICE cryoSPHERE and cryoXT probes, freeze nerves during heart, chest or amputation surgery to give temporary pain relief while the nerves regrow.

    AtriCure sells to medical centers through its own sales force in the United States, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, the Benelux region, Australia and Canada, and through distributors in other countries. Its business is mainly transacted in U.S. dollars. Its manufacturing is highly concentrated, and it relies on single or limited suppliers for some parts.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its products may not win wide acceptance in the United States.
    • Competing products and procedures may take market share.
    • Clinical data may not be positive or meet FDA requirements.
    • Its reliance on independent distributors outside the U.S. and on single or limited-source suppliers.
    • Its manufacturing is concentrated, so a disruption at its facilities could hurt sales.
