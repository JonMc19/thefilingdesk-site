    What Aemetis does

    Aemetis makes fuels from farm products and waste. It is based in Cupertino, California, and says it focuses on renewable natural gas and renewable fuels with a lower carbon footprint. It has three main operations.

    In California it runs an ethanol plant in Keyes that can make 65 million gallons a year. The plant also sells its leftover grain products as animal feed to local dairies and feedlots, and sells carbon dioxide to the food and beverage industries. Also in California, it collects biogas from dairy waste in twelve digesters and turns it into renewable natural gas that goes into the utility pipeline for use as vehicle fuel. In India it runs a plant in Kakinada that can make about 80 million gallons a year of biodiesel from vegetable oils and animal waste. It also sells the glycerin that the process leaves over.

    Aemetis says it competes with makers of petroleum-based fuels and with other companies offering alternatives to them. Many of them have more resources and experience than it has. It is also building more dairy digesters, a fuel station for its natural gas, and other projects.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Losses so far, and a debt load that could limit its cash flow and force it to scale back.
    • Its reliance on outside financing, and the chance it cannot repay or refinance its Third Eye Capital debt when due.
    • Its profit depends on the gap between feedstock and energy costs and the prices of fuel and animal feed.
    • Its reliance on tax credits, carbon credits and grants that could end or shrink.
    • Its dependence on working capital agreements with J.D. Heiskell and other key vendors.
