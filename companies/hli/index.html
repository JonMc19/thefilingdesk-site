    What Houlihan Lokey does

    Houlihan Lokey is an investment bank that gives advice to companies, financial sponsors (such as private equity firms) and government agencies. It earns fees for its advice, in three segments: Corporate Finance, which covers mergers and acquisitions and capital raising; Financial Restructuring, which advises on restructurings in and out of bankruptcy; and Financial and Valuation Advisory, which covers financial opinions, valuations and related consulting.

    As of March 31, 2026, the firm had more than 1,900 financial professionals in more than thirty offices worldwide, and it served more than 2,000 clients a year, from closely held companies to Fortune Global 500 corporations. It says most Corporate Finance engagements involve mid-sized deals. It competes with boutique firms, other independent investment banks and the largest global banks.

    Houlihan Lokey says it does not lend, sell or trade securities, or write investment research, so as to avoid conflicts with its clients' interests. It also says its employees own a large share of the company.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weak markets that reduce the number or size of the deals it advises on.
    • Relying on advisory engagements in Corporate Finance and Financial Restructuring for much of its revenue.
    • Losing its Managing Directors and other senior staff, or failing to hire new ones.
    • Goodwill and other acquired intangible assets that could be written down.
    • Risks of international operations and of currency changes.
    From Houlihan Lokey's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
