    What Lifetime Brands does

    Lifetime Brands designs, sources and sells kitchenware, tableware and other home products. Kitchenware covers tools, cutlery, cookware and bakeware. Tableware covers dinnerware, stemware, flatware and giftware. Home Solutions covers items such as thermal beverageware, bath scales, food storage and home decor. Brands include Farberware and KitchenAid (both licensed) and Mikasa and Taylor (owned).

    It sells mainly to retailers, which may resell online, and to a lesser extent to distributors, plus a limited selection directly to consumers on its own websites. Its products are aimed mainly at shoppers buying moderately priced items.

    It sells abroad through subsidiaries in the U.K. and Europe, China, Hong Kong, Australia and New Zealand, and through an alliance with a Canadian company. It says it keeps looking to invest in companies that own or license complementary brands.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Economic conditions and other factors outside its control that affect its business.
    • Inflation or deflation in supply chain costs, including raw materials and sourcing.
    • Duties, tariffs and other trade barriers, and changes in China's economic policies.
    • Substantial debt and heavy seasonality that raises its borrowing needs.
    • Higher costs and difficulty getting insurance on acceptable terms.
    From Lifetime Brands's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 12, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2021, earnings per share fell 2.7% a year and net income grew 5.8% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 52% and diluted each share's earnings. Lifetime Brands then lost money in fiscal 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
