    What Zscaler does

    Zscaler sells cloud-based cybersecurity. Its main product, the Zero Trust Exchange, connects users, devices, workloads and AI agents to apps and data based on who they are and company policy, rather than where they are on a network. The idea behind "zero trust" is that no connection is trusted by default. Zscaler says this replaces older security built around firewalls and VPNs (virtual private networks), which were designed to guard a fixed perimeter.

    The service runs on over 200 public data centers and thousands of private sites around the world, in over 185 countries. Zscaler was founded in 2007. Its offerings include zero trust networking and security services for users, branches, cloud workloads, and connected devices. It sells to organizations and works with channel partners.

    Its customers include over 40% of the Forbes Global 2000 and over 50% of the Fortune 500. The 10-K says Zscaler faces intense and increasing competition.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A history of annual net losses, with no assurance of reaching or keeping profitability.
    • Customers not renewing subscriptions or adding more users and services.
    • Its reliance on channel partners for part of its sales and growth.
    • Intense and growing competition that could cost it market share.
    • Interruptions or damage to its network of data centers, or a breach of its own platform.
    From Zscaler's annual report on Form 10-K, filed September 3, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
