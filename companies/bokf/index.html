    What BOK Financial does

    BOK Financial is a bank holding company based in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Its main bank, BOKF, NA, takes deposits and makes loans for middle-market businesses, financial institutions and consumers. It operates under several local bank names, including Bank of Oklahoma, Bank of Texas and Bank of Albuquerque, in Oklahoma, Texas, New Mexico, Colorado, Arizona, Arkansas, Kansas and Missouri. It reports total consolidated assets of $52 billion.

    It has three segments: Commercial Banking, Consumer Banking and Wealth Management. Beyond loans and deposits, it offers cash management, trust and fiduciary services, mortgage banking, and brokerage and trading services. It lends to energy companies and sells commodity derivatives so customers can manage price risk. A broker-dealer subsidiary sells securities and underwrites municipal bonds, and a private wealth subsidiary advises wealthy clients.

    BOK Financial says it lets each local market make its own decisions under common company standards, and that it looks to grow by expanding in neighboring states, hiring talent and buying other banks.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Regional economic trouble, including long periods of low oil and gas prices that hurt energy customers.
    • Weakness in industries it lends to, such as commercial real estate and healthcare.
    • Changes in mortgage interest rates that hurt its mortgage banking and its holdings of mortgage servicing rights.
    • Losing deposits or seeing funding costs rise as markets or credit ratings shift.
    • Its principal shareholder controls a majority of the common stock, and the stock trades thinly.
    From BOK Financial's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 18, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
