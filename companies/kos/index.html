    What Kosmos Energy does

    Kosmos Energy finds and produces oil and natural gas from deepwater fields, which are offshore and far from shore. Its production comes from assets offshore Ghana, Equatorial Guinea, Mauritania, Senegal and the Gulf of America. It is listed on the NYSE and the London Stock Exchange.

    It earns revenue by selling the oil and gas it produces. It was founded in 2003 to find oil and gas in under-explored parts of West Africa, and it found the Jubilee Field offshore Ghana in 2007 and the Greater Tortue Ahmeyim gas field offshore Mauritania and Senegal in 2015. Its strategy now centers on raising production through infill drilling and work on existing wells, exploring near existing infrastructure, and buying assets. It bought assets in Equatorial Guinea in 2017, Deep Gulf Energy in the Gulf of America in 2018, and more interests in Ghana and the Kodiak field in 2021 and 2022.

    Kosmos often shares its fields with partners. For example, the Jubilee and Greater Tortue Ahmeyim fields are run as units whose partners hold set interests, and the report notes that these interests are subject to redetermination, meaning they can be adjusted.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Having limited proved reserves, and drilling wells that may not find oil and gas in commercial amounts.
    • Estimating its discoveries and reserves wrongly, and drilling that costs more than planned.
    • Third parties and partners that do not meet their obligations to it.
    • Being unable to sell some of the natural gas from its license areas.
    • Not getting the equipment and infrastructure it needs on time.
    From Kosmos Energy's annual report on Form 10-K, filed March 2, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
