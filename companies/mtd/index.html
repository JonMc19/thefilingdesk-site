    What Mettler Toledo does

    Mettler-Toledo makes precision instruments and the software and services that go with them. Laboratories, factories and food businesses use them for research, quality control and manufacturing. Its products include laboratory balances, pipettes, titrators, pH meters, analytical instruments, industrial weighing systems and weighing and labeling systems for supermarkets. Its products are sold in more than 140 countries.

    Laboratory instruments and related service made up about 56% of net sales in 2025. Industrial instruments and related service were about 39%, and the retail business about 5%. Service, meaning service contracts, on-demand work and replacement parts, brings in part of this revenue. By region, 42% of 2025 net sales were in North and South America.

    The company reports five segments: U.S. Operations, Swiss Operations, Western European Operations, Chinese Operations and Other Operations. It sells through its own sales and service network, which has a direct presence in about 40 countries, and through distributors.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • An economic downturn in the developed countries where it sells most of its products.
    • Its large amount of business in China and other risks of operating internationally.
    • Disruptions to its ability to make and deliver products, or to get the materials it needs.
    • Cybersecurity attacks and system failures that could harm its business or customer data.
    • Problems with distributors and other third parties it does not control, and the departure of key employees.
    From Mettler Toledo's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 6, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
