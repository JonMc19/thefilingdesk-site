    What Ecovyst does

    Ecovyst makes and sells sulfuric acid, an industrial chemical, and also regenerates used sulfuric acid. Its customers include oil refiners. Refiners use sulfuric acid as a catalyst, a helper chemical, in a process called alkylation. The spent acid left over has to be regenerated or recycled, which the company says most refiners no longer want to do themselves. Ecovyst takes that work on.

    The company earns money from selling virgin (new) acid and from regeneration services, and says it serves customers in many end uses. It says it holds a number one or number two supply share position for products and services that produced more than 95% of its 2025 sales. It describes its products as a small part of its customers' costs but important to their processes.

    On December 31, 2025 it sold its Advanced Materials and Catalysts segment, including its Zeolyst joint venture, to Technip Energies, and used part of the cash to repay a term loan. That business is shown as discontinued operations. It now runs nine manufacturing plants and had 617 employees, all in the United States.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Not being able to pass higher raw material prices, such as natural gas, on to customers, or to keep key suppliers.
    • Reliance on a limited number of customers for a meaningful part of its business.
    • New technology or changes in customers' products that could cut the need for its products or services.
    • Substantial competition in the industries where it operates.
    • Customers that do not pay or do not perform their contracts.
    From Ecovyst's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
