    What Alibaba Group does

    Alibaba is a Chinese technology company that runs online shopping platforms, a cloud computing business and related services. It says its strategic priorities are artificial intelligence (AI) with cloud, and consumption. It provides the technology and marketing reach that merchants, brands and retailers use to reach customers, and it sells AI and cloud services to businesses.

    In its fiscal year 2026 (ended March 31, 2026) its businesses were the Alibaba China E-commerce Group, the Alibaba International Digital Commerce Group, the AI + Cloud businesses and others. The China e-commerce group includes Taobao and Tmall, and the Taobao Instant Commerce platform for on-demand delivery of meals, groceries and other goods. The cloud unit, the Cloud Intelligence Group, rents out computing power and offers its own Qwen family of AI models.

    Alibaba says the China e-commerce, international commerce and cloud groups together accounted for a substantial majority of its revenue in fiscal year 2026. It relies on Alipay, run by Ant Group, to handle payments on its marketplaces.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to keep the trust of consumers and merchants in its marketplaces.
    • Strong competition, and the need to keep up with fast changes in technology such as AI.
    • Chinese laws and rules on competition, data and platforms, which could limit how it operates.
    • Its reliance on Alipay, run by Ant Group, to process payments on its marketplaces.
    • Trade tensions and geopolitical conflict, including the chance of its securities being delisted from U.S. exchanges.
    From Alibaba Group's annual report on Form 20-F, filed May 20, 2026, in our words; the 20-F has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
