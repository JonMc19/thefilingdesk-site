    What J.M. Smucker does

    J.M. Smucker, founded in 1897, makes and sells branded food and beverages, mostly in the United States. Operations outside the U.S. are mainly in Canada, and net sales outside the U.S. were 4 percent of the total in fiscal 2026. Its main products are coffee, frozen handheld foods, sweet baked goods, pet snacks, peanut butter, cat food, fruit spreads, toppings and syrups, and baking mixes.

    It has five reportable segments. Three are U.S. Retail segments: Coffee, Frozen Handheld and Spreads, and Pet Foods. These sell mainly through direct sales and brokers to food retailers, club stores, discount stores, online sellers, pet stores, drug stores and mass merchandisers. The other two are Sweet Baked Snacks, sold in many retail channels, and Away From Home, which sells through foodservice distributors and operators such as restaurants, schools and healthcare facilities.

    It has reshaped its mix in recent years. It bought Hostess Brands in November 2023, and it has sold several smaller businesses, including Voortman in December 2024 and some sweet baked snacks value brands in March 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The loss of or a change with a significant customer, including its largest customer.
    • Problems with single-source suppliers, or with products made at only one site.
    • Higher costs of raw materials and transportation that it may not pass on to customers.
    • Consumer perceptions of its brands, and competition from private label and cheaper brands.
    • Risks from acquisitions, sales of businesses and restructuring, and its substantial debt.
    From J.M. Smucker's annual report on Form 10-K, filed June 9, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
