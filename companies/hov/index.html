    What Hovnanian Enterprises does

    Hovnanian Enterprises builds and sells homes through its subsidiaries: single-family detached homes, attached townhomes and condominiums, urban infill homes and active lifestyle homes in planned communities. It builds for first-time buyers, move-up buyers, luxury buyers, active lifestyle buyers and empty nesters. It was founded in 1959 as a New Jersey homebuilder.

    It has two operations. Homebuilding has three segments: Northeast, Southeast and West. Financial services provides mortgage loans and title services to its homebuilding customers. It was offering homes for sale in 140 communities in 27 markets in 13 states. Base prices ranged from $182,000 to $1,191,000, and the average sales price, including options, was $519,000 nationwide in fiscal 2025.

    It has delivered more than 382,000 homes since its predecessor was incorporated, including 6,431 homes in fiscal 2025 together with its unconsolidated joint ventures. At the end of October 2025 it had 1,891 full-time associates, with 1,194 in homebuilding.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A downturn in the homebuilding industry, or rising mortgage rates and costs for homebuyers.
    • Finding suitable land and lots at acceptable prices, and having the cash to buy them.
    • Relying on subcontractors to build its homes properly.
    • Raw material and labor shortages and price swings that raise building costs.
    • Competition from homebuilders with more sales and financial resources.
    From Hovnanian Enterprises's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 22, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
