    What Evolv Technologies does

    Evolv Technologies sells security screening equipment that uses artificial intelligence to detect weapons. Its customers include schools, hospitals, sports and entertainment venues, tourist attractions, houses of worship and industrial workplaces. Evolv Express screens people for concealed threats, and Evolv eXpedite screens bags automatically without a trained X-ray operator. Both run on Evolv's own software and cloud services.

    Evolv sells through a model it calls Security-as-a-Service. It offers two ways to buy: a pure subscription model and a purchase subscription model. Both need hardware and an active, connected software subscription. A subscription also includes Evolv Insights, a cloud analytics tool that shows customers how their screening systems perform.

    It sells through its own sales force and through reseller partners. It recognizes much of its revenue gradually over the term of each agreement. It was formed in 2021 as a Delaware corporation.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Material weaknesses in its financial reporting controls, which led to a restatement of earlier financial statements.
    • A history of losses, with no assurance of reaching or keeping profitability.
    • Depending on reseller partners to market, sell and support its products.
    • Delays or the loss of a limited or sole source supplier.
    • Its products failing, or being seen to fail, to detect a weapon.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
