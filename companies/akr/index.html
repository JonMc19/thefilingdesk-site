    What Acadia Realty Trust does

    Acadia Realty Trust is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns and runs shopping properties. Its own portfolio is mostly street retail and select urban properties, plus suburban centers in areas where little new space can be built. Its offices are in Rye, New York.

    It makes money from rent paid by retail tenants. It also runs an investment management business that buys suburban shopping centers and urban retail properties through funds and joint ventures with institutional partners, so it earns fees and a share of profits from them as well as owning part of the assets. A smaller structured finance program invests in first mortgage loans and other notes backed by real estate.

    Acadia also builds and redevelops properties. It raises money for purchases by selling shares, and it has authority to buy back its own shares. Its tenants are retailers, so its results follow how well they do.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Tenants, especially its largest ones, could leave, fail to renew or go bankrupt.
    • Online shopping could hurt its retail tenants and weaken demand for store space.
    • It depends on retail property, so a drop in demand for store space hits it harder than a mixed portfolio.
    • Its development and acquisition projects may cost more or earn less than planned.
    • Debt, interest rates and its ability to raise capital could limit its growth.
    From Acadia Realty Trust's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 13, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
