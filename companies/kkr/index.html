    What KKR does

    KKR is a global investment firm. It manages money for institutional and individual investors in areas such as private equity, credit (lending), infrastructure, real estate and energy. It also offers capital markets services and, through Global Atlantic, which it bought in 2021, retirement and life insurance. Founded in 1976, it says it pioneered the leveraged buyout, a purchase of a company funded largely with borrowed money. It has 36 offices around the world.

    At the end of 2025 it managed $744 billion of assets, of which $219 billion came from Global Atlantic. It reports three segments: Asset Management, Insurance and Strategic Holdings. In Asset Management it earns recurring management fees for running client money. It also earns transaction and monitoring fees, carried interest (a set share of the investment gains it makes for outside investors) and investment income from its own capital invested alongside clients.

    Asset Management has five business lines: Private Equity, Real Assets, Credit and Liquid Strategies, Capital Markets and Principal Activities. The 10-K says traditional private equity was less than 25% of total assets under management at the end of 2025. KKR has about 4,200 employees, plus about 800 more in subsidiary organizations.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Difficult market and economic conditions hurting the firm's investments and earnings.
    • Parts of its earnings and cash flow swing widely, and it may have to return carried interest to its funds.
    • Being unable to raise money from institutional or individual investors for its funds and insurance business.
    • Many investments are hard to sell, hard to value, and made with a lot of borrowed money.
    • Interest rates and changing assumptions affecting its insurance business, and a downgrade of its insurers' ratings.
    From KKR's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
