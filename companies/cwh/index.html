    What Camping World does

    Camping World describes itself as America's largest retailer of RVs and related products and services. It sells new and used RVs, arranges financing, services and repairs RVs, installs parts and accessories, and sells RV and outdoor products. At the end of 2025 it operated 196 store locations, all selling or servicing RVs, plus call centers in four states. It sells under the Camping World and Good Sam brands.

    It reports two segments. RV and Outdoor Retail covers its dealerships and stores. Good Sam Services and Plans sells extended vehicle service contracts, roadside assistance, insurance, travel protection, and travel planning and directories. It says its stores and centers serve about 4.2 million active customers.

    The company has one location in Elkhart, Indiana that sells new RVs at discounted prices under a factory-direct model. It also sells exclusive-brand RVs and offers a digital retail experience online. It competes with other sellers of RV services, protection plans, products and resources.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in consumer tastes, or misjudging what customers will want.
    • The availability and cost of financing for the company and its customers.
    • High fuel prices or fuel shortages that discourage RV travel.
    • Depending on the well-being and reputation of the RV makers it buys from.
    • Managing its inventory well in a volatile market.
    From Camping World's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 27, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
