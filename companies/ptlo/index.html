    What Portillo's does

    Portillo's runs restaurants that serve Chicago-style street food. The menu includes Chicago-style hot dogs and sausages, Italian beef sandwiches, char-broiled burgers, salads, crinkle-cut fries and chocolate cake and shakes. Dick Portillo started the business in 1963 in a small trailer called "The Dog House."

    At the end of fiscal 2025 (December 28, 2025) the company owned and operated 102 restaurants in 11 states, including one held through a venture with C&O Chicago. Nearly all were built with double-lane drive-thrus and also serve dine-in, carryout, delivery and catering guests. It earns its money from restaurant sales. It says it mixes parts of fast-casual and quick-service restaurants and builds large restaurants that handle high volumes.

    Portillo's Inc. is a holding company whose main operating business is Portillo's OpCo. At year end it owned most of OpCo, and its earlier owners held the rest. It also has a venture to open a restaurant at Dallas-Fort Worth airport in 2026. It had 7,890 team members.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Changes in the economy, food and commodity costs and what customers want.
    • Opening new restaurants that may miss expectations, be unprofitable or close.
    • Funding its growth and capital spending when money may not be easy to get.
    • Food safety problems and food-borne illness at its restaurants.
    • Having most restaurants in the Midwest, which ties it to conditions in that region.
    From Portillo's's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
