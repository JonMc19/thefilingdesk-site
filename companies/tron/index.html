    What Tron does

    Tron Inc., formerly SRM Entertainment, runs two unrelated businesses. The first is a digital asset treasury: it holds the TRX token, the native token of the TRON blockchain, and says it holds more of it than any other public company. As of March 18, 2026, it held approximately 9,769,626 TRX tokens and approximately 549,676,892 staked TRX (sTRX) tokens. It stakes its tokens through a decentralized finance protocol called JustLend and earns network rewards in return.

    The second business is a toy and souvenir maker, run through a subsidiary. It designs, develops and makes figures, plush toys, accessories, apparel and homewares, many based on well-known movies and books. It sells them to theme parks and other venues, including Walt Disney Parks and Resorts, Universal Parks and Destinations, SeaWorld and Six Flags.

    The company is a Nevada corporation incorporated in 2022. In 2025 a company called Bravemorning, whose sole shareholder is a Tron director, bought a controlling stake in it. The company says Bravemorning owned approximately 86.6% of its common stock after that purchase. The company changed its name and ticker to Tron in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • The TRX token is highly volatile, and swings in its price are likely to affect the company's financial results.
    • Its TRX strategy brings stricter regulatory oversight, and rules on digital assets could change.
    • Its toy and souvenir results can swing from quarter to quarter and depend on theme park demand.
    • A controlling shareholder holds most of its voting power, so other shareholders have little say.
    • Its operations in Hong Kong and China expose it to changes in Chinese government policy.
