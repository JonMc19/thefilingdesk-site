    What A. O. Smith does

    A. O. Smith makes water heaters, boilers, heat pumps, tanks and water treatment products for homes and businesses. Its water heaters run on electricity, natural gas or propane, and include tankless and heat pump models. Its boilers heat large commercial buildings as well as homes, and its water treatment line includes water softeners and whole-home filters.

    It has two segments, North America and Rest of World. North America made up approximately 78 percent of its 2025 sales, and Rest of World, mostly China, made up approximately 22 percent. A large share of North American sales comes from replacing existing products. It sells through about 800 independent wholesale plumbing distributors and through home center chains, with an exclusive relationship with Lowe's for A. O. Smith branded products.

    The company names Rheem, Bradford White, Rinnai, Aerco and Navien as its main North American water heater and boiler competitors. In China it names Haier/Casarte, Midea/COLMO and Rinnai.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Weaker demand if the economy slows, especially in North America and China.
    • Tariffs and trade rules that raise the cost of the raw materials it needs.
    • Losing, or lower orders from, one or more of its largest customers.
    • Competition from other makers that could force it to cut prices.
    • Price swings and limited suppliers for the materials and components it buys.
    From A. O. Smith's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 10, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
