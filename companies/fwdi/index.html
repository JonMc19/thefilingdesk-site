    What Forward Industries does

    Forward Industries has two lines of activity. The first is a design business that provides hardware and software product design and engineering services, mainly to medical and technology customers in the United States. Its work covers medical products, smart displays, beverage vending machines, software apps, lighting, security systems, cameras, wearables and vehicle controls, from first concept through engineering, prototypes and files for manufacturing. It also offers targeted help to clients that have their own development teams.

    The second is a digital asset treasury. In September 2025 the company announced a plan to buy Solana (SOL), a cryptocurrency, and other digital assets, and to make SOL its main treasury reserve asset. It announced initial SOL purchases of approximately $1.58 billion in total. It plans to stake its SOL, meaning lock it up to help run the Solana network, and to earn extra revenue through partnerships in that ecosystem. It changed its Nasdaq ticker symbol from FORD to FWDI in November 2025.

    Forward says few competitors offer the full range of design skills it has, though many design and engineering firms compete in specific industries. As of November 30, 2025, it had approximately 60 employees.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • A new strategy built around SOL that may not be carried out successfully.
    • Regulators may treat some digital assets it holds as securities, which could hurt their value.
    • Assets concentrated in SOL, so problems specific to Solana would hit it hard.
    • Uncertainty about how digital assets are classified, which could bring regulatory scrutiny and added rules.
    • Competition from many design and engineering firms in specific industries.
    From Forward Industries's annual report on Form 10-K, filed December 11, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2016 to 2021, earnings per share fell 6.5% a year and net income 3.6% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 20% and diluted each share's earnings. Forward Industries then lost money in fiscal 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
