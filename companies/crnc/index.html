    What Cerence does

    Cerence sells voice and AI software that carmakers build into their vehicles, so drivers can talk to the car. Its products include a voice assistant based on generative AI, which handles voice activation and natural speech, and audio software that reduces noise and improves calls and voice use inside and outside the car. It sells on a white-label basis, so the assistant carries the carmaker's own brand and personality. Most of its business is automotive, though it says the software could also work in trucks, two-wheelers, televisions, watches and kiosks.

    Its customers include nearly all major automakers, such as BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volkswagen Group, Stellantis, Renault, Toyota, Ford and General Motors. It also works with tier 1 suppliers, the companies that sell components to carmakers, such as Bosch, Continental and Aptiv. In fiscal 2025, automakers represented approximately 50% of revenue, and tier 1 suppliers the other half.

    Its revenue is spread across regions: Europe and Asia each made up about 42% of fiscal 2025 revenue. It runs software on the car and in the cloud, and is shifting toward cloud-connected services.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Highly competitive, fast-changing market in which it may not compete successfully.
    • Weakness in the auto industry or the wider economy lowering its sales.
    • Losing any of its largest customers, which could seriously hurt the business.
    • Having to win carmakers' approval and renew service contracts, with no guarantee it will.
    • Its shift to cloud-connected services may slow its near-term revenue growth.
    From Cerence's annual report on Form 10-K, filed November 20, 2025, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2018 to 2021, earnings per share grew 94.1% a year and net income 98.1% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 8% and diluted each share's earnings. Cerence then lost money in fiscal 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
