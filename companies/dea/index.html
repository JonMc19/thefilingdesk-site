    What Easterly Government Properties does

    Easterly Government Properties is a real estate investment trust, or REIT, that owns and manages office and other commercial buildings leased to US government agencies. It focuses on buildings that agencies use for essential work. It earns rent from them, either directly or through the US General Services Administration (GSA), the federal agency that leases space for the government. It says it gets about 90% of its revenue this way.

    At the end of 2025 it wholly owned 93 operating properties and held ten more through an unconsolidated joint venture. Its leases with federal agencies typically run for initial terms of ten to 20 years. It also builds and renovates properties to suit the needs of tenant agencies, and it earns a construction management fee for this work.

    It aims to add properties by buying and developing them, and it is looking at buildings leased to state and local governments as well. It runs its business through an operating partnership, in which it is the sole general partner.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Depending on the US government and its agencies for almost all of its revenue.
    • Being unable to renew leases or re-lease space on good terms as leases expire.
    • Leases that let a tenant agency leave before the lease ends and stop paying rent.
    • Government shutdowns and budget cuts or impasses.
    • Risks in developing and redeveloping properties for tenant agencies.
    From Easterly Government Properties's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 23, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
