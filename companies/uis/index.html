    What Unisys does

    Unisys is an information technology services company that helps large businesses and governments run, modernize and secure their technology. Its origins go back to 1873, and it formed in 1986. Clients rely on it for complex, regulated work such as moving systems to the cloud, supporting employees' devices and running large computer systems.

    It reports three segments. Digital Workplace Solutions provides IT support for workplace devices and software, from the data center to the edge. Cloud, Applications and Infrastructure Solutions covers cloud, application development and modernization, and business processing. Enterprise Computing Solutions delivers proprietary and hybrid computing in the cloud and on premises for mission-critical workloads at clients in financial services, travel, health care and the public sector.

    It faces aggressive competition, including rivals with lower prices or different contract terms, and it works with many government and public sector clients.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Failing to grow revenue, expand margins and generate enough cash across its businesses.
    • Selling its ClearPath Forward products as a subscription service faster than expected, which would hurt its cash position in the short and medium term.
    • Not keeping up with fast technology change such as artificial intelligence.
    • The added risks of government contracts, and aggressive competition on price and terms.
    • Trouble attracting and keeping skilled employees, and cyber incidents that disrupt its systems.
    From Unisys's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.

    Income statement

    From fiscal 2018 to 2020, earnings per share grew 184.9% a year and net income 215.3% a year, as new shares raised the share count by 22% and diluted each share's earnings. Unisys then lost money in fiscal 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.
