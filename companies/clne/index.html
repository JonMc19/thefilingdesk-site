    What Clean Energy Fuels does

    Clean Energy Fuels sells natural gas as a vehicle fuel in the United States and Canada, mostly as renewable natural gas (RNG), which is made from methane captured from sources such as dairy waste and landfills. It sells compressed and liquefied natural gas to fleets such as heavy-duty trucks, airports, refuse haulers and public transit. It also sells bulk natural gas to industrial users.

    Besides selling fuel, it designs, builds, operates and maintains fueling stations, transports gas by "virtual" pipelines, and sells government credits it earns by selling RNG as a vehicle fuel. It develops and runs dairy RNG projects, and buys the RNG it sells from third-party sources. Its RNG sales grew to 237.4 million gasoline gallon equivalents in 2025, and RNG was 88% of its vehicle fuel sales that year.

    The company says it has been in the alternative vehicle fuels business for over 28 years. It also says it provided about half of the RNG used as transportation fuel in California in 2025.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Fleets and other customers may not adopt its vehicle fuels as fast as it expects.
    • Dependence on vehicle and engine makers, which it does not control, to produce natural gas vehicles.
    • Its RNG business may not succeed: supply, demand and the credit markets are uncertain.
    • Delays or cost overruns in building or buying RNG projects, and reliance on third-party suppliers.
    • Debt and changes in government rules that support renewable fuels.
    From Clean Energy Fuels's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 24, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
