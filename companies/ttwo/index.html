    What Take-Two Interactive does

    Take-Two makes and publishes video games for game consoles, personal computers and phones. It works through three labels: Rockstar Games, maker of Grand Theft Auto and Red Dead Redemption; 2K, publisher of NBA 2K, WWE 2K, Borderlands, BioShock and Civilization; and Zynga, which makes free-to-play phone games such as Words With Friends, Toon Blast and Zynga Poker. Take-Two says the Grand Theft Auto series has sold-in, meaning shipped to stores and digital platforms, over 465 million units.

    Nearly all of its revenue comes from its games: the games themselves, extra content, in-game purchases and virtual currency, plus advertising inside its phone games. It reports one business segment. Most sales go through digital stores run by platform owners, and its five largest customers made up 80.6% of net revenue in the year to March 2026, with Apple, Sony, Google and Microsoft each above 10.0%. About 40.8% of revenue came from outside the United States. It names Electronic Arts, Epic Games, Roblox, Tencent and Ubisoft among its competitors, along with Sony, Microsoft and Nintendo, which also make their own games.

    Take-Two had 12,909 full-time employees in March 2026, 9,998 of them working on game development in studios across North America, Europe and Asia. Rockstar Games planned to release Grand Theft Auto VI in November 2026, during the company's 2027 fiscal year.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Its dependence on Grand Theft Auto and a few other hit games, so a weak or delayed release can hurt its results.
    • Delays and rising costs in making new games, which take years and large budgets to develop.
    • Its reliance on a few platform owners, such as Apple, Sony, Google and Microsoft, that approve its games, set store fees and also compete with it.
    • The shift to free-to-play phone games and in-game spending, a business with heavy competition and changing player habits.
    • Laws on content, data privacy and in-game purchases that are still changing and could limit how it sells its games.
    From Take-Two Interactive's annual report on Form 10-K, filed May 22, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
