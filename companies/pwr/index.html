    What Quanta Services does

    Quanta Services builds and maintains infrastructure for utilities and other industries. It provides design, engineering, procurement, construction, upgrade, repair and maintenance services for electric power transmission and distribution networks, substations, wind, solar and gas power plants, battery storage, communications networks, gas utility systems, pipelines and industrial facilities. It also does electrical and mechanical work for large power users such as data centers, advanced factories, hospitals and drug plants.

    Quanta reports two segments: Electric Infrastructure Solutions, and Underground Utility and Infrastructure Solutions. It earns money under master service agreements, repair and maintenance contracts, and fixed price and other construction and engineering contracts. Its customers include utilities, renewable energy companies, large technology companies, communications providers and industrial and energy delivery businesses.

    The company's work is spread across subsidiaries it calls operating companies, and it depends on skilled craft labor. It owns and leases trucks, trailers and specialty construction equipment.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Project problems that delay work or cut profit, and cost more than planned.
    • Operating hazards such as wildfires and explosions, which could lead to large liabilities.
    • A labor-intensive business that may struggle to hire and keep qualified workers.
    • Losing business from certain significant customers.
    • Changes in its estimates of contract revenue and costs, which could reduce or erase profit.
    From Quanta Services's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 19, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
