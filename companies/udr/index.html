    Operating margin is operating income as a share of revenue. Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital spending. Return on capital is after-tax operating income as a share of the money invested in the business: debt plus equity, minus cash. Market cap is the last closing share price times the latest diluted share count, so it is approximate. P/E is the last close divided by diluted earnings per share over the last four quarters, or the latest fiscal year where the quarters aren't available; there is none when the company made a loss.

    About these figures. UDR's redeemable minority stakes (operating partnership units) are counted between liabilities and equity in the balance-sheet check. UDR's cash flow statement splits its spending on its properties across several lines (capital improvements and development) with no total, so capital spending and free cash flow are left blank.

    US$ millions unless stated, at each fiscal year end. Net debt to EBITDA is debt minus cash, divided by operating income before depreciation and amortization: roughly how many years of those earnings the net debt equals. Debt to equity is debt as a multiple of shareholders' equity. Interest coverage is operating income divided by interest expense. The current ratio is current assets over current liabilities; the quick ratio leaves out inventory.

    Cash flow

    Free cash flow is cash from operations minus capital expenditure. The SEC's structured data for UDR's filings doesn't give both for any year, so free cash flow is left out.
