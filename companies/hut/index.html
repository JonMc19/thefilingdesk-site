    What Hut 8 does

    Hut 8 describes itself as an energy infrastructure company. It builds its business in three layers: power, data centers and computing. It acquires and manages power assets such as powered land and electrical connections, builds and runs data centers on that power, and runs computing businesses that use specialized chips. Its main focus now is developing and running large data centers for artificial intelligence and other energy-heavy computing.

    At the end of 2025 its power layer covered 1,020 megawatts of capacity across 15 sites in the United States and Canada, including about 310 megawatts tied to four power plants it sold in the first quarter of 2026. Its data centers include five used for Bitcoin-type computing, five traditional cloud and colocation sites, and one idle site. It is developing an AI data center in Louisiana.

    Its computing layer has three brands. American Bitcoin, a majority-owned company listed on Nasdaq, accumulates Bitcoin and runs mining computers. Hut 8 Canada provides cloud and data center services in Canada. Highrise AI rents graphics-chip computing power for AI work. It also holds a joint venture, King Mountain.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Cost, delays and other problems in building or expanding data centers.
    • Needing more capital and being unable to raise it on good terms.
    • Risks from its joint venture and its majority-owned American Bitcoin company.
    • Dependence on large amounts of electricity whose price and availability can change.
    • Not attracting or keeping customers across its platform.

    Piotroski F-score: how many of nine simple tests of profitability, funding and efficiency the company passed in the year (Piotroski, 2000); 8 or 9 is strong, 0 to 2 weak.
