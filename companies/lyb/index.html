    What LyondellBasell does

    LyondellBasell is a chemical and plastics maker. It produces olefins such as ethylene (the building block for many plastics and chemicals) and the plastics made from them, polyethylene and polypropylene. These go into packaging, housewares, cars, construction materials and many other goods. It also makes propylene oxide and its derivatives, fuel additives, styrene and acetyls, plastic compounds, and it licenses chemical process technology and sells catalysts.

    It reports five segments: Olefins and Polyolefins-Americas, Olefins and Polyolefins-Europe, Asia, International, Intermediates and Derivatives, Advanced Polymer Solutions, and Technology. In 2025, sales of olefins and co-products were about 14% of consolidated revenue, polyethylene about 24% and polypropylene about 19%. No single customer accounted for a tenth or more of its revenue. It says it is the world's second largest producer of polypropylene.

    The company had about 18,970 employees at the end of 2025. It ceased operations at its Houston refinery in February 2025 and now reports that business as discontinued. It also holds joint ventures, including a half interest in a Louisiana polyethylene venture.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Swings in the industries it serves, which can cause large changes in its results.
    • Changes in energy prices, including a sustained drop in crude oil prices.
    • Higher costs or limited supply of raw materials and energy.
    • Risks of chemical operations, including liabilities that may not be fully insured.
    • A capital-intensive business that relies on cash from operations and outside financing.
