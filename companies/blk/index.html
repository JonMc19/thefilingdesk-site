    What BlackRock does

    BlackRock manages money for other people. Its clients range from pension plans, charities, central banks and insurers to individual savers, who mostly reach it through brokers, banks and financial advisers. It invests their money in stocks, bonds, cash, private assets such as infrastructure and private loans, and commodities, through mutual funds, its iShares exchange-traded funds (funds that trade on a stock exchange) and accounts run for single clients. At the end of 2025 it managed $14.0 trillion of client assets.

    Most of its revenue comes from fees charged as a share of the assets it manages, plus fees for beating agreed benchmarks on some funds and income from lending out securities. Exchange-traded funds made up 42% of its long-term assets. It also sells software and data, chiefly Aladdin, a system that investment firms use to manage portfolios and risk; this brought in $2.0 billion in 2025. BlackRock says it competes with other fund managers, insurers, banks, brokers and financial technology firms.

    BlackRock has about 24,900 employees in more than 30 countries, and says about 60% of them work outside the United States. In July 2025 it bought HPS Investment Partners, a private credit manager, adding to its earlier purchases of the infrastructure investor GIP and the data provider Preqin.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Falling markets or shifts in interest and exchange rates could shrink the assets it manages, and with them its fees and earnings.
    • Clients and fund boards can end or decline to renew its advisory contracts, and weak investment results could drive clients away.
    • Losing ground to rival technology firms could cost Aladdin clients, and outages at the third parties Aladdin relies on could hurt it.
    • Its recent acquisitions, including HPS, may not deliver the benefits it expects.
    • Problems at the third parties its exchange-traded funds depend on could disturb the prices at which those funds trade.
    From BlackRock's annual report on Form 10-K, filed February 25, 2026, in our words; the 10-K has the full description and every risk factor. Updated with each new annual report.
