    What Aeva Technologies does

    Aeva Technologies develops LiDAR sensors, which use laser light to measure the distance to objects and map surroundings. Its sensors use a method called FMCW, which also measures the speed of every point it sees, and it builds the key optical parts on a silicon chip module. It targets two markets: automotive makers and their suppliers, for driver assistance and autonomous driving, and industrial automation, where it sells precision laser sensors for factories.

    Aeva is an early stage company with a history of losses. It has mostly sold prototypes and engineering services to customers for their own development and testing programs. It says commercial deliveries of its automotive products are not expected in 2026 and may not happen at all. It depends on a few customers: in 2025 its top three customers made up 64% of revenue.

    At the end of 2025 Aeva had 239 full-time employees, mostly in Mountain View, California, and most work in research and development.

    Its biggest risks, as it describes them

    • Being an early stage company with a history of losses, which may never become profitable.
    • Customer programs it supports may be delayed or never reach commercial production.
    • Depending on a small number of customers for most of its revenue.
    • Large customers and suppliers have strong bargaining power and may have competing in-house products.
    • Its products may not win commercial acceptance or may take longer to reach the market than expected.
