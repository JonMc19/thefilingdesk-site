    Anheuser-Busch InBev's revenue fell 0.8% to $59.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Its operating margin was 26.0%, up from 25.9% a year earlier. Free cash flow was $11.2 billion, up 0.3%. It paid out $6.8 billion in dividends and buybacks, 61% of free cash flow.

    About these figures. Its shares trade in the US as American depositary shares (ADSs), and one ADS can stand for several ordinary shares, so per-share figures are per ordinary share and the page has no market value, P/E or valuation model.
