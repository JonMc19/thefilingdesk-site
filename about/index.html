    About The Filing Desk

    The Filing Desk publishes short reports on what listed companies' results show. Each one starts from the company's own filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), sets out what changed in the business, and links to every source.

    Where the numbers come from

    • Annual and quarterly reports (Forms 10-K and 10-Q) filed with the SEC, taken from the structured XBRL data on EDGAR.
    • Company earnings releases, for items the filings don't break out.

    When a later filing restates an earlier year, the reports use the latest figure the company filed.

    How figures are calculated

    • Free cash flow is net cash from operating activities minus additions to property and equipment.
    • Margins are the item divided by revenue for the same period.
    • Years are the company's fiscal years, which may not match calendar years.

    Each report lists any other definitions it uses.

    What the reports don't do

    They don't cover share prices, valuation or analyst forecasts, and they don't rate stocks or recommend buying, selling or holding anything. They describe what companies reported.

    The week in filings

    The Sunday story is different from the reports. It takes the week's biggest business and markets topic and explains it using news coverage, public posts, market data and the filings, with analysis of what it could mean next. Every fact is attributed and every figure is checked against its source before publishing, and passages that look ahead are marked as analysis. The stories are researched and drafted with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Like everything here, they never recommend buying or selling anything.

    Photos

    Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, under licences that allow anyone to reuse them. Each one is credited under the photo with its author and licence. They show the companies, places and people in a story; they aren't taken at the events described unless the caption says so.

    Corrections

    If a figure doesn't match the filing, open an issue on the site's GitHub page. Confirmed errors are corrected in the report, with a note saying what changed.

    Disclaimer

    Everything on this site is for information only. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Figures may contain errors, so check the original filings before relying on them. The Filing Desk is not affiliated with the SEC or with any company it covers.
