    Hewlett Packard Enterprise vs Seagate Technology

    Hewlett Packard Enterprise HPE · Seagate Technology STX · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Hewlett Packard Enterprise reported revenue of $34.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.8 times Seagate Technology's $12.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Hewlett Packard Enterprise's revenue grew 13.8% in its latest year, and Seagate Technology's grew 34.1%. Hewlett Packard Enterprise had an operating margin of -1.3%, against 33.6% at Seagate Technology. Free cash flow was $627 million at Hewlett Packard Enterprise and $3.1 billion at Seagate Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and July 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
