    Ibotta vs National CineMedia

    Ibotta IBTA · National CineMedia NCMI · Advertising

    Ibotta reported revenue of $342 million in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times National CineMedia's $243 million in fiscal 2025. Ibotta's revenue fell 6.8% in its latest year, and National CineMedia's grew 1.0%. Ibotta had an operating margin of -0.2%, against -5.7% at National CineMedia. Free cash flow was $75 million at Ibotta and $3 million at National CineMedia. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
