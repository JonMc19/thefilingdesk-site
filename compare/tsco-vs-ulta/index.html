    Tractor Supply vs Ulta Beauty

    Tractor Supply TSCO · Ulta Beauty ULTA · Other Specialty Retail

    Tractor Supply reported revenue of $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Ulta Beauty's $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Tractor Supply's revenue grew 4.3% in its latest year, and Ulta Beauty's grew 9.7%. Tractor Supply had an operating margin of 9.4%, against 12.4% at Ulta Beauty. Free cash flow was $740 million at Tractor Supply and $1.1 billion at Ulta Beauty. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
