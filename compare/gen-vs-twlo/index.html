    Gen Digital vs Twilio

    Gen Digital GEN · Twilio TWLO · Systems Software

    Twilio reported revenue of $5.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Gen Digital's $5.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Gen Digital's revenue grew 27.1% in its latest year, and Twilio's grew 13.7%. Gen Digital had an operating margin of 42.4%, against 3.1% at Twilio. Free cash flow was $1.5 billion at Gen Digital and $997 million at Twilio. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
