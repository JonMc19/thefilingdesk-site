    Columbia Sportswear vs Tapestry

    Columbia Sportswear COLM · Tapestry TPR · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

Free cash flow was $217 million at Columbia Sportswear and $1.8 billion at Tapestry. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
