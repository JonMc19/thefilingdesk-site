    Peloton Interactive vs Yeti

    Peloton Interactive PTON · Yeti YETI · Leisure Products

    Peloton Interactive reported revenue of $2.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Yeti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Peloton Interactive's revenue fell 1.8% in its latest year, and Yeti's grew 2.1%. Peloton Interactive had an operating margin of 6.6%, against 11.4% at Yeti. Free cash flow was $378 million at Peloton Interactive and $212 million at Yeti. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
