    Penguin Solutions vs Tower Semiconductor

    Penguin Solutions PENG · Tower Semiconductor TSEM · Semiconductors

    Penguin Solutions reported revenue of $1.7 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Tower Semiconductor's $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Penguin Solutions' revenue grew 26.5% in its latest year, and Tower Semiconductor's grew 9.1%. Penguin Solutions had an operating margin of 9.6%, against 12.4% at Tower Semiconductor. Free cash flow was −$163 million at Penguin Solutions and −$49 million at Tower Semiconductor. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
