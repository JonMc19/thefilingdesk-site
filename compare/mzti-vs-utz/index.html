    Marzetti vs Utz Brands

    Marzetti MZTI · Utz Brands UTZ · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Marzetti reported revenue of $1.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Utz Brands' $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Marzetti's revenue grew 1.1% in its latest year, and Utz Brands' grew 2.1%. Marzetti had an operating margin of 12.4%, against 1.4% at Utz Brands. Free cash flow was $206 million at Marzetti and $9 million at Utz Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
