    Dell Technologies vs Hewlett Packard Enterprise

    Dell Technologies DELL · Hewlett Packard Enterprise HPE · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Dell Technologies reported revenue of $113.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.3 times Hewlett Packard Enterprise's $34.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Dell Technologies' revenue grew 18.8% in its latest year, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise's grew 13.8%. Dell Technologies had an operating margin of 7.2%, against -1.3% at Hewlett Packard Enterprise. Free cash flow was $8.6 billion at Dell Technologies and $627 million at Hewlett Packard Enterprise. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 30 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
