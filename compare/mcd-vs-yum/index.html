    McDonald's vs Yum! Brands

    McDonald's MCD · Yum! Brands YUM · Restaurants

    McDonald's reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.3 times Yum! Brands' $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025. McDonald's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Yum! Brands' grew 8.8%. McDonald's had an operating margin of 46.1%, against 31.3% at Yum! Brands. Free cash flow was $7.2 billion at McDonald's and $1.6 billion at Yum! Brands.
