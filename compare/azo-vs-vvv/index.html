    AutoZone vs Valvoline

    AutoZone AZO · Valvoline VVV · Automotive Retail

    AutoZone reported revenue of $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 11.1 times Valvoline's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. AutoZone's revenue grew 2.4% in its latest year, and Valvoline's grew 5.6%. AutoZone had an operating margin of 19.1%, against 22.8% at Valvoline. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at AutoZone and $38 million at Valvoline. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
