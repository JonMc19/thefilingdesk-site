    Darden Restaurants vs McDonald's

    Darden Restaurants DRI · McDonald's MCD · Restaurants

    McDonald's reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Darden Restaurants' $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Darden Restaurants' revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and McDonald's grew 3.7%. Darden Restaurants had an operating margin of 12.0%, against 46.1% at McDonald's. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants and $7.2 billion at McDonald's. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
