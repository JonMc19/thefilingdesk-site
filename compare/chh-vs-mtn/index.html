    Choice Hotels vs Vail Resorts

    Choice Hotels CHH · Vail Resorts MTN · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Vail Resorts reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.8 times Choice Hotels' $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Choice Hotels' revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Vail Resorts' fell 4.3%. Choice Hotels had an operating margin of 28.1%, against 14.8% at Vail Resorts. Free cash flow was $164 million at Choice Hotels and $248 million at Vail Resorts. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
