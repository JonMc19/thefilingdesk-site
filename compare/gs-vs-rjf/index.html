    Goldman Sachs vs Raymond James Financial

    Goldman Sachs GS · Raymond James Financial RJF · Investment Banking & Brokerage

    Goldman Sachs reported revenue of $58.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.7 times Raymond James Financial's $15.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Goldman Sachs' revenue grew 8.9% in its latest year, and Raymond James Financial's grew 6.6%. Goldman Sachs had a return on equity of 13.9%, against 17.7% at Raymond James Financial. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
