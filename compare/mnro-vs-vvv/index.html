    Monro vs Valvoline

    Monro MNRO · Valvoline VVV · Automotive Retail

    Valvoline reported revenue of $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Monro's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Monro's revenue fell 3.2% in its latest year, and Valvoline's grew 5.6%. Monro had an operating margin of 1.7%, against 22.8% at Valvoline. Free cash flow was $39 million at Monro and $38 million at Valvoline. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
