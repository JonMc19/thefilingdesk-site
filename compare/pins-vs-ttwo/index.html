    Pinterest vs Take-Two Interactive

    Pinterest PINS · Take-Two Interactive TTWO · Interactive Home Entertainment

    Take-Two Interactive reported revenue of $6.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times Pinterest's $4.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Pinterest's revenue grew 15.8% in its latest year, and Take-Two Interactive's grew 18.2%. Pinterest had an operating margin of 7.6%, against -1.6% at Take-Two Interactive. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Pinterest and $462 million at Take-Two Interactive. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
