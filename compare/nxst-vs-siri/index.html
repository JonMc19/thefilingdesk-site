    Nexstar Media Group vs SiriusXM

    Nexstar Media Group NXST · SiriusXM SIRI · Broadcasting

    SiriusXM reported revenue of $8.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Nexstar Media Group's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Nexstar Media Group's revenue fell 8.5% in its latest year, and SiriusXM's fell 1.6%. Nexstar Media Group had an operating margin of 17.1%, against 17.2% at SiriusXM. Free cash flow was $743 million at Nexstar Media Group and $1.2 billion at SiriusXM.
