    Kontoor Brands vs Ralph Lauren

    Kontoor Brands KTB · Ralph Lauren RL · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Ralph Lauren reported revenue of $8.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.6 times Kontoor Brands' $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Kontoor Brands' revenue grew 20.9% in its latest year, and Ralph Lauren's grew 14.6%. Kontoor Brands had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 14.5% at Ralph Lauren. Free cash flow was $435 million at Kontoor Brands and $746 million at Ralph Lauren. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
