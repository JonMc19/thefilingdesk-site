    Exelixis vs Neurocrine Biosciences

    Exelixis EXEL · Neurocrine Biosciences NBIX · Biotechnology

    Neurocrine Biosciences reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Exelixis' $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Exelixis' revenue grew 7.0% in its latest year, and Neurocrine Biosciences' grew 21.4%. Exelixis had an operating margin of 37.6%, against 21.6% at Neurocrine Biosciences. Free cash flow was $876 million at Exelixis and $749 million at Neurocrine Biosciences. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
