    nVent Electric vs Nextpower

    nVent Electric NVT · Nextpower NXT · Electrical Components & Equipment

    nVent Electric reported revenue of $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Nextpower's $3.6 billion in fiscal 2026. nVent Electric's revenue grew 29.5% in its latest year, and Nextpower's grew 20.3%. nVent Electric had an operating margin of 15.8%, against 19.6% at Nextpower. Free cash flow was $372 million at nVent Electric and $514 million at Nextpower. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
