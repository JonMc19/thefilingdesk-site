    General Mills vs Hormel Foods

    General Mills GIS · Hormel Foods HRL · Packaged Foods & Meats

    General Mills reported revenue of $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Hormel Foods' $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and Hormel Foods' grew 1.6%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 5.9% at Hormel Foods. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $534 million at Hormel Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and October 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
