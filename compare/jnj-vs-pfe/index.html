    Johnson & Johnson vs Pfizer

    Johnson & Johnson JNJ · Pfizer PFE · Pharmaceuticals

    Johnson & Johnson reported revenue of $94.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Pfizer's $62.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Johnson & Johnson's revenue grew 6.0% in its latest year, and Pfizer's fell 1.6%. Free cash flow was $19.7 billion at Johnson & Johnson and $9.1 billion at Pfizer. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
