    CarGurus vs Yelp

    CarGurus CARG · Yelp YELP · Interactive Media & Services

    Yelp reported revenue of $1.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times CarGurus' $907 million in fiscal 2025. CarGurus' revenue grew 13.7% in its latest year, and Yelp's grew 3.8%. CarGurus had an operating margin of 26.9%, against 12.6% at Yelp. Free cash flow was $289 million at CarGurus and $324 million at Yelp.
