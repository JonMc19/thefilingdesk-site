    Accenture vs Cognizant

    Accenture ACN · Cognizant CTSH · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Accenture reported revenue of $69.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.3 times Cognizant's $21.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Accenture's revenue grew 7.4% in its latest year, and Cognizant's grew 7.0%. Accenture had an operating margin of 14.7%, against 16.1% at Cognizant. Free cash flow was $10.9 billion at Accenture and $2.6 billion at Cognizant. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
