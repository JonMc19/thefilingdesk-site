    Roblox vs Take-Two Interactive

    Roblox RBLX · Take-Two Interactive TTWO · Interactive Home Entertainment

    Take-Two Interactive reported revenue of $6.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times Roblox's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Roblox's revenue grew 35.8% in its latest year, and Take-Two Interactive's grew 18.2%. Roblox had an operating margin of -25.2%, against -1.6% at Take-Two Interactive. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Roblox and $462 million at Take-Two Interactive. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
