    Salesforce vs Intuit

    Salesforce CRM · Intuit INTU · Application Software

    Salesforce reported revenue of $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.9 times Intuit's $21.4 billion in fiscal 2026. Salesforce's revenue grew 9.6% in its latest year, and Intuit's grew 13.9%. Salesforce had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 27.4% at Intuit. Free cash flow was $14.4 billion at Salesforce and $8.7 billion at Intuit. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
