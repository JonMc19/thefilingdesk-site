    Jabil vs TE Connectivity

    Jabil JBL · TE Connectivity TEL · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    Jabil reported revenue of $29.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times TE Connectivity's $17.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Jabil's revenue grew 3.2% in its latest year, and TE Connectivity's grew 8.9%. Jabil had an operating margin of 4.0%, against 18.6% at TE Connectivity. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Jabil and $3.2 billion at TE Connectivity. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and September 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
