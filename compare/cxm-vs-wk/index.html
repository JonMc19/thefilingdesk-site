    Sprinklr vs Workiva

    Sprinklr CXM · Workiva WK · Application Software

    Workiva reported revenue of $885 million in fiscal 2025, about the same as Sprinklr's $857 million in fiscal 2026. Sprinklr's revenue grew 7.6% in its latest year, and Workiva's grew 19.8%. Sprinklr had an operating margin of 4.7%, against -4.8% at Workiva. Free cash flow was $158 million at Sprinklr and $138 million at Workiva. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
