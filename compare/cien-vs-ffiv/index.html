    Ciena vs F5

    Ciena CIEN · F5 FFIV · Communications Equipment

    Ciena reported revenue of $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times F5's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Ciena's revenue grew 18.8% in its latest year, and F5's grew 9.7%. Ciena had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 24.8% at F5. Free cash flow was $665 million at Ciena and $906 million at F5. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
