    Booking vs Royal Caribbean Group

    Booking BKNG · Royal Caribbean Group RCL · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Booking reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Royal Caribbean Group's $17.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Booking's revenue grew 13.4% in its latest year, and Royal Caribbean Group's grew 8.8%. Booking had an operating margin of 32.8%, against 27.4% at Royal Caribbean Group. Free cash flow was $9.1 billion at Booking and $1.2 billion at Royal Caribbean Group.
