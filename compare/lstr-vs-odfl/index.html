    Landstar System vs Old Dominion

    Landstar System LSTR · Old Dominion ODFL · Cargo Ground Transportation

    Old Dominion reported revenue of $5.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Landstar System's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Landstar System's revenue fell 1.6% in its latest year, and Old Dominion's fell 5.5%. Landstar System had an operating margin of 3.2%, against 24.8% at Old Dominion. Free cash flow was $215 million at Landstar System and $955 million at Old Dominion. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
