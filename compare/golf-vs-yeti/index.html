    Acushnet vs Yeti

    Acushnet GOLF · Yeti YETI · Leisure Products

    Acushnet reported revenue of $2.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Yeti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Acushnet's revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Yeti's grew 2.1%. Acushnet had an operating margin of 11.7%, against 11.4% at Yeti. Free cash flow was $120 million at Acushnet and $212 million at Yeti. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
