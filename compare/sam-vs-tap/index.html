    Boston Beer vs Molson Coors

    Boston Beer SAM · Molson Coors TAP · Distillers & Vintners

    Molson Coors reported revenue of $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.7 times Boston Beer's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Boston Beer's revenue fell 2.4% in its latest year, and Molson Coors' fell 4.2%. Boston Beer had an operating margin of 7.4%, against -21.0% at Molson Coors. Free cash flow was $216 million at Boston Beer and $1.1 billion at Molson Coors. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
