    Salesforce vs Atlassian

    Salesforce CRM · Atlassian TEAM · Application Software

    Salesforce reported revenue of $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 6.3 times Atlassian's $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Salesforce's revenue grew 9.6% in its latest year, and Atlassian's grew 26.0%. Salesforce had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 0.2% at Atlassian. Free cash flow was $14.4 billion at Salesforce and $1.3 billion at Atlassian. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
