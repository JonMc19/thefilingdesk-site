    Mueller Water Products vs EnPro Industries

    Mueller Water Products MWA · EnPro Industries NPO · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Mueller Water Products reported revenue of $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times EnPro Industries' $1.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Mueller Water Products' revenue grew 8.8% in its latest year, and EnPro Industries' grew 9.0%. Mueller Water Products had an operating margin of 18.2%, against 14.1% at EnPro Industries. Free cash flow was $172 million at Mueller Water Products and $159 million at EnPro Industries. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
