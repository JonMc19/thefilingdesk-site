    General Mills vs Hershey

    General Mills GIS · Hershey HSY · Packaged Foods & Meats

    General Mills reported revenue of $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times Hershey's $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and Hershey's grew 4.4%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 12.3% at Hershey. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $1.8 billion at Hershey. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
