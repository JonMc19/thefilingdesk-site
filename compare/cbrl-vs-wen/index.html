    Cracker Barrel vs Wendy's

    Cracker Barrel CBRL · Wendy's WEN · Restaurants

    Cracker Barrel reported revenue of $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Wendy's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Cracker Barrel's revenue fell 4.7% in its latest year, and Wendy's fell 3.1%. Cracker Barrel had an operating margin of -0.4%, against 15.8% at Wendy's. Free cash flow was $88 million at Cracker Barrel and $243 million at Wendy's. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
