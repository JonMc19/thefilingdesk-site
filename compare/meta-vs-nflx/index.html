    Meta Platforms vs Netflix

    Meta Platforms META · Netflix NFLX · Interactive Media & Services

    Meta Platforms reported revenue of $201.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.4 times Netflix's $45.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Meta Platforms' revenue grew 22.2% in its latest year, and Netflix's grew 15.8%. Meta Platforms had an operating margin of 41.4%, against 29.5% at Netflix. Free cash flow was $46.1 billion at Meta Platforms and $9.5 billion at Netflix.
