    MongoDB vs Tyler Technologies

    MongoDB MDB · Tyler Technologies TYL · Application Software

    MongoDB reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Tyler Technologies' $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. MongoDB's revenue grew 22.8% in its latest year, and Tyler Technologies' grew 9.1%. MongoDB had an operating margin of -5.6%, against 15.3% at Tyler Technologies. Free cash flow was $500 million at MongoDB and $638 million at Tyler Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
