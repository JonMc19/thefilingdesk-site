    AdaptHealth vs Revvity

    AdaptHealth AHCO · Revvity RVTY · Health Care Equipment

    AdaptHealth reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Revvity's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. AdaptHealth's revenue fell 0.5% in its latest year, and Revvity's grew 3.7%. AdaptHealth had an operating margin of 2.8%, against 12.5% at Revvity. Free cash flow was $219 million at AdaptHealth and $509 million at Revvity. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
