    Comfort Systems USA vs Tetra Tech

    Comfort Systems USA FIX · Tetra Tech TTEK · Construction & Engineering

    Comfort Systems USA reported revenue of $9.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Tetra Tech's $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Comfort Systems USA and $439 million at Tetra Tech. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
