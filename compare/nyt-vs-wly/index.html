    New York Times vs John Wiley & Sons

    New York Times NYT · John Wiley & Sons WLY · Publishing

    New York Times reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times John Wiley & Sons' $1.7 billion in fiscal 2026. New York Times' revenue grew 9.2% in its latest year, and John Wiley & Sons' fell 0.1%. New York Times had an operating margin of 15.3%, against 16.5% at John Wiley & Sons. Free cash flow was $550 million at New York Times and $209 million at John Wiley & Sons. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
