    Lululemon Athletica vs Tapestry

    Lululemon Athletica LULU · Tapestry TPR · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Lululemon Athletica reported revenue of $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Tapestry's $8.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Lululemon Athletica's revenue grew 4.9% in its latest year, and Tapestry's grew 14.2%. Lululemon Athletica had an operating margin of 19.9%, against 23.9% at Tapestry. Free cash flow was $922 million at Lululemon Athletica and $1.8 billion at Tapestry. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
