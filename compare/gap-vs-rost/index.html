    Gap vs Ross Stores

    Gap GAP · Ross Stores ROST · Apparel Retail

    Ross Stores reported revenue of $22.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Gap's $15.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Gap's revenue grew 1.9% in its latest year, and Ross Stores' grew 7.7%. Gap had an operating margin of 7.3%, against 11.9% at Ross Stores. Free cash flow was $823 million at Gap and $2.2 billion at Ross Stores.
