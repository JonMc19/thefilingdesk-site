    Digi International vs InterDigital

    Digi International DGII · InterDigital IDCC · Communications Equipment

    InterDigital reported revenue of $834 million in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times Digi International's $430 million in fiscal 2025. Digi International's revenue grew 1.5% in its latest year, and InterDigital's fell 4.0%. Digi International had an operating margin of 13.1%, against 55.3% at InterDigital. Free cash flow was $105 million at Digi International and $529 million at InterDigital. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
