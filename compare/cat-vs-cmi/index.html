    Caterpillar vs Cummins

    Caterpillar CAT · Cummins CMI · Construction Machinery & Heavy Transportation Equipment

    Caterpillar reported revenue of $67.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Cummins' $33.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Caterpillar's revenue grew 4.3% in its latest year, and Cummins' fell 1.3%. Caterpillar had an operating margin of 16.5%, against 11.9% at Cummins. Free cash flow was $8.9 billion at Caterpillar and $2.4 billion at Cummins.
