    Rockwell Automation vs Vertiv

    Rockwell Automation ROK · Vertiv VRT · Electrical Components & Equipment

    Vertiv reported revenue of $10.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Rockwell Automation's $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Rockwell Automation's revenue grew 0.9% in its latest year, and Vertiv's grew 27.7%. Rockwell Automation had an operating margin of 20.4%, against 17.9% at Vertiv. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Rockwell Automation and $1.9 billion at Vertiv. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
