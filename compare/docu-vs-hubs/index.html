    Docusign vs HubSpot

    Docusign DOCU · HubSpot HUBS · Application Software

    Docusign reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as HubSpot's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Docusign's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and HubSpot's grew 19.2%. Docusign had an operating margin of 9.3%, against 0.2% at HubSpot. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Docusign and $708 million at HubSpot. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
