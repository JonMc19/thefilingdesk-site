    Applied Materials vs Teradyne

    Applied Materials AMAT · Teradyne TER · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Applied Materials reported revenue of $28.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 8.9 times Teradyne's $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Applied Materials' revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Teradyne's grew 13.1%. Applied Materials had an operating margin of 29.2%, against 20.4% at Teradyne. Free cash flow was $5.7 billion at Applied Materials and $450 million at Teradyne. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
