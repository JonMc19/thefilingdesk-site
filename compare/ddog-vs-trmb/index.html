    Datadog vs Trimble

    Datadog DDOG · Trimble TRMB · Application Software

    Trimble reported revenue of $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Datadog's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Datadog's revenue grew 27.7% in its latest year, and Trimble's fell 2.6%. Datadog had an operating margin of -1.3%, against 16.5% at Trimble. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Datadog and $361 million at Trimble. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
