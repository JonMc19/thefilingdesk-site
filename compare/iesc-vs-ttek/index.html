    IES vs Tetra Tech

    IES IESC · Tetra Tech TTEK · Construction & Engineering

    Tetra Tech reported revenue of $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times IES's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. IES's revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and Tetra Tech's grew 4.7%. IES had an operating margin of 11.4%, against 7.5% at Tetra Tech. Free cash flow was $219 million at IES and $439 million at Tetra Tech. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
