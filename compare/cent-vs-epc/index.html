    Central Garden & Pet vs Edgewell Personal Care

    Central Garden & Pet CENT · Edgewell Personal Care EPC · Household Products

    Central Garden & Pet reported revenue of $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Edgewell Personal Care's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Central Garden & Pet's revenue fell 2.2% in its latest year, and Edgewell Personal Care's fell 1.3%. Central Garden & Pet had an operating margin of 8.0%, against 4.3% at Edgewell Personal Care. Free cash flow was $291 million at Central Garden & Pet and $41 million at Edgewell Personal Care. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
