    Brunswick vs Yeti

    Brunswick BC · Yeti YETI · Leisure Products

    Brunswick reported revenue of $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.9 times Yeti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Brunswick's revenue grew 2.4% in its latest year, and Yeti's grew 2.1%. Brunswick had an operating margin of -0.8%, against 11.4% at Yeti. Free cash flow was $396 million at Brunswick and $212 million at Yeti. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
