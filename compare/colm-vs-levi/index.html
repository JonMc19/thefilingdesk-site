    Columbia Sportswear vs Levi Strauss

    Columbia Sportswear COLM · Levi Strauss LEVI · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Levi Strauss reported revenue of $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Columbia Sportswear's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Columbia Sportswear's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Levi Strauss' grew 4.1%. Columbia Sportswear had an operating margin of 6.1%, against 10.8% at Levi Strauss. Free cash flow was $217 million at Columbia Sportswear and $308 million at Levi Strauss. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
