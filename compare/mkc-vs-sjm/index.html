    McCormick vs J.M. Smucker

    McCormick MKC · J.M. Smucker SJM · Packaged Foods & Meats

McCormick had an operating margin of 15.7%, against 4.0% at J.M. Smucker. Free cash flow was $740 million at McCormick and $1.2 billion at J.M. Smucker. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and April 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
