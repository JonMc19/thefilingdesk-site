    Hershey vs Kraft Heinz

    Hershey HSY · Kraft Heinz KHC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Kraft Heinz reported revenue of $24.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Hershey's $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Hershey's revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Kraft Heinz's fell 3.5%. Hershey had an operating margin of 12.3%, against -18.7% at Kraft Heinz. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at Hershey and $3.7 billion at Kraft Heinz. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
