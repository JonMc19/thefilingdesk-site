    GameStop vs Upbound Group

    GameStop GME · Upbound Group UPBD · Computer & Electronics Retail

    Upbound Group reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times GameStop's $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025. GameStop's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and Upbound Group's grew 8.7%. GameStop had an operating margin of 6.4%, against 4.8% at Upbound Group. Free cash flow was $597 million at GameStop and $239 million at Upbound Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
