    Fortinet vs Zscaler

    Fortinet FTNT · Zscaler ZS · Systems Software

    Fortinet reported revenue of $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Zscaler's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2026. Fortinet's revenue grew 14.2% in its latest year, and Zscaler's grew 25.4%. Fortinet had an operating margin of 30.7%, against -4.0% at Zscaler. Free cash flow was $2.2 billion at Fortinet and $852 million at Zscaler. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
