    Graco vs RBC Bearings

    Graco GGG · RBC Bearings RBC · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Graco reported revenue of $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times RBC Bearings' $1.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Graco's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and RBC Bearings' grew 14.3%. Graco had an operating margin of 27.9%, against 22.5% at RBC Bearings. Free cash flow was $638 million at Graco and $343 million at RBC Bearings. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 26 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
