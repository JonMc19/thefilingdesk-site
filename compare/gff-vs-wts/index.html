    Griffon vs Watts Water Technologies

    Griffon GFF · Watts Water Technologies WTS · Building Products

    Griffon reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Watts Water Technologies' $2.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Griffon's revenue fell 4.0% in its latest year, and Watts Water Technologies' grew 8.3%. Griffon had an operating margin of 8.2%, against 18.4% at Watts Water Technologies. Free cash flow was $305 million at Griffon and $356 million at Watts Water Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
