    Kohl's vs Newegg Commerce

    Kohl's KSS · Newegg Commerce NEGG · Broadline Retail

    Kohl's reported revenue of $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 10.7 times Newegg Commerce's $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Kohl's revenue fell 4.3% in its latest year, and Newegg Commerce's grew 16.9%. Kohl's had an operating margin of 4.0%, against -0.7% at Newegg Commerce. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Kohl's and −$30 million at Newegg Commerce. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
