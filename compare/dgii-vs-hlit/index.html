    Digi International vs Harmonic

    Digi International DGII · Harmonic HLIT · Communications Equipment

    Digi International reported revenue of $430 million in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Harmonic's $360 million in fiscal 2025. Digi International's revenue grew 1.5% in its latest year, and Harmonic's fell 26.1%. Digi International had an operating margin of 13.1%, against 3.9% at Harmonic. Free cash flow was $105 million at Digi International and $97 million at Harmonic. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
