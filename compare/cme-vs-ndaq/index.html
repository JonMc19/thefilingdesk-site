    CME Group vs Nasdaq

    CME Group CME · Nasdaq NDAQ · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Nasdaq reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times CME Group's $6.5 billion in fiscal 2025. CME Group's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Nasdaq's grew 11.7%. CME Group had an operating margin of 64.9%, against 28.2% at Nasdaq. Free cash flow was $4.2 billion at CME Group and $2.0 billion at Nasdaq.
