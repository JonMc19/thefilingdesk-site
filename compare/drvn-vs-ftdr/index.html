    Driven Brands vs Frontdoor

    Driven Brands DRVN · Frontdoor FTDR · Specialized Consumer Services

    Frontdoor reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Driven Brands' $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Driven Brands' revenue grew 6.3% in its latest year, and Frontdoor's grew 13.6%. Free cash flow was $108 million at Driven Brands and $390 million at Frontdoor. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
