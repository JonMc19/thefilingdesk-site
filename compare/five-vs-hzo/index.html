    Five Below vs MarineMax

    Five Below FIVE · MarineMax HZO · Specialty Stores

    Five Below reported revenue of $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times MarineMax's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Five Below's revenue grew 22.9% in its latest year, and MarineMax's fell 5.0%. Five Below had an operating margin of 9.6%, against 1.5% at MarineMax. Free cash flow was $412 million at Five Below and $12 million at MarineMax. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
