    ABM Industries vs Clean Harbors

    ABM Industries ABM · Clean Harbors CLH · Environmental & Facilities Services

    ABM Industries reported revenue of $8.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Clean Harbors' $6.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $155 million at ABM Industries and $442 million at Clean Harbors. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
