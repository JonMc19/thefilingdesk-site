    Fair Isaac vs Unity Software

    Fair Isaac FICO · Unity Software U · Application Software

    Fair Isaac reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Unity Software's $1.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Fair Isaac's revenue grew 15.9% in its latest year, and Unity Software's grew 2.0%. Fair Isaac had an operating margin of 46.5%, against -25.9% at Unity Software. Free cash flow was $770 million at Fair Isaac and $404 million at Unity Software. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
