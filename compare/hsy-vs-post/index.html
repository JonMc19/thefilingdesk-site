    Hershey vs Post

    Hershey HSY · Post POST · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hershey reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Post's $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Hershey's revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Post's grew 3.0%. Hershey had an operating margin of 12.3%, against 9.8% at Post. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at Hershey and $488 million at Post. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
