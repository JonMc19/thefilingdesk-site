    Callaway Golf vs Yeti

    Callaway Golf CALY · Yeti YETI · Leisure Products

    Callaway Golf reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Yeti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Callaway Golf's revenue fell 0.8% in its latest year, and Yeti's grew 2.1%. Callaway Golf had an operating margin of 6.2%, against 11.4% at Yeti. Free cash flow was $302 million at Callaway Golf and $212 million at Yeti. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
