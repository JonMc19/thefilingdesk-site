    Check Point vs UiPath

    Check Point CHKP · UiPath PATH · Systems Software

    Check Point reported revenue of $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times UiPath's $1.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Check Point's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and UiPath's grew 12.7%. Check Point had an operating margin of 30.5%, against 3.5% at UiPath. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Check Point and $352 million at UiPath. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
