    Qorvo vs Skyworks Solutions

    Qorvo QRVO · Skyworks Solutions SWKS · Semiconductors

    Skyworks Solutions reported revenue of $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Qorvo's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2026. Qorvo's revenue fell 1.1% in its latest year, and Skyworks Solutions' fell 2.2%. Qorvo had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 12.2% at Skyworks Solutions. Free cash flow was $680 million at Qorvo and $1.1 billion at Skyworks Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and October 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
