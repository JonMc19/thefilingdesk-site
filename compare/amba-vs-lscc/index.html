    Ambarella vs Lattice Semiconductor

    Ambarella AMBA · Lattice Semiconductor LSCC · Semiconductors

Free cash flow was $58 million at Ambarella and $133 million at Lattice Semiconductor. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
