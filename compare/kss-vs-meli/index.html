    Kohl's vs MercadoLibre

    Kohl's KSS · MercadoLibre MELI · Broadline Retail

    MercadoLibre reported revenue of $28.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times Kohl's $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Kohl's revenue fell 4.3% in its latest year, and MercadoLibre's grew 39.1%. Kohl's had an operating margin of 4.0%, against 11.1% at MercadoLibre. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Kohl's and $10.8 billion at MercadoLibre. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
