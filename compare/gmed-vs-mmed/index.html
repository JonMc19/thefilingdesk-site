    Globus Medical vs MiniMed Group

    Globus Medical GMED · MiniMed Group MMED · Health Care Equipment

    MiniMed Group reported revenue of $3.1 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Globus Medical's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $589 million at Globus Medical and −$420 million at MiniMed Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 24), so the periods don't line up exactly.
