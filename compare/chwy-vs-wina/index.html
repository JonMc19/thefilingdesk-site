    Chewy vs Winmark

    Chewy CHWY · Winmark WINA · Other Specialty Retail

    Chewy reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 146.4 times Winmark's $86 million in fiscal 2025. Chewy's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and Winmark's grew 5.9%. Chewy had an operating margin of 2.0%, against 63.4% at Winmark. Free cash flow was $562 million at Chewy and $45 million at Winmark. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
