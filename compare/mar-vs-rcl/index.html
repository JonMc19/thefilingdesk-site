    Marriott International vs Royal Caribbean Group

    Marriott International MAR · Royal Caribbean Group RCL · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Marriott International reported revenue of $26.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Royal Caribbean Group's $17.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Marriott International's revenue grew 4.3% in its latest year, and Royal Caribbean Group's grew 8.8%. Marriott International had an operating margin of 15.8%, against 27.4% at Royal Caribbean Group. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Marriott International and $1.2 billion at Royal Caribbean Group.
