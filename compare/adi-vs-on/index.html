    Analog Devices vs ON Semiconductor

    Analog Devices ADI · ON Semiconductor ON · Semiconductors

    Analog Devices reported revenue of $11.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times ON Semiconductor's $6.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Analog Devices' revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and ON Semiconductor's fell 15.3%. Analog Devices had an operating margin of 26.6%, against 1.4% at ON Semiconductor. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Analog Devices and $1.4 billion at ON Semiconductor. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
