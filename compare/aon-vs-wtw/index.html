    Aon vs Willis Towers Watson

    Aon AON · Willis Towers Watson WTW · Insurance Brokers

    Aon reported revenue of $17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Willis Towers Watson's $9.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Aon's revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and Willis Towers Watson's fell 2.2%. Aon had an operating margin of 25.3%, against 23.0% at Willis Towers Watson. Free cash flow was $3.2 billion at Aon and $1.5 billion at Willis Towers Watson.
