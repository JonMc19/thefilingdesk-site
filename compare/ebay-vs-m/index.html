    eBay vs Macy's

    eBay EBAY · Macy's M · Broadline Retail

    Macy's reported revenue of $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times eBay's $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025. eBay's revenue grew 8.0% in its latest year, and Macy's fell 1.7%. eBay had an operating margin of 20.5%, against 4.5% at Macy's. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at eBay and $1.1 billion at Macy's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
