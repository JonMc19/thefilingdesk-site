    Meta Platforms vs Microsoft

    Meta Platforms META · Microsoft MSFT

    Microsoft reported revenue of $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.7 times Meta Platforms' $201.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Meta Platforms' revenue grew 22.2% in its latest year, and Microsoft's grew 17.8%. Meta Platforms had an operating margin of 41.4%, against 46.8% at Microsoft. Free cash flow was $46.1 billion at Meta Platforms and $67.0 billion at Microsoft. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
