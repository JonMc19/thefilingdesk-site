    Fair Isaac vs Pegasystems

    Fair Isaac FICO · Pegasystems PEGA · Application Software

    Fair Isaac reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Pegasystems' $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Fair Isaac's revenue grew 15.9% in its latest year, and Pegasystems' grew 16.6%. Fair Isaac had an operating margin of 46.5%, against 15.1% at Pegasystems. Free cash flow was $770 million at Fair Isaac and $491 million at Pegasystems. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
