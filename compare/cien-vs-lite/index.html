    Ciena vs Lumentum

    Ciena CIEN · Lumentum LITE · Communications Equipment

    Ciena reported revenue of $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Lumentum's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Ciena's revenue grew 18.8% in its latest year, and Lumentum's grew 83.2%. Ciena had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 17.4% at Lumentum. Free cash flow was $665 million at Ciena and $300 million at Lumentum. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
