    Sonic Automotive vs Valvoline

    Sonic Automotive SAH · Valvoline VVV · Automotive Retail

    Sonic Automotive reported revenue of $15.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 8.9 times Valvoline's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Sonic Automotive's revenue grew 6.5% in its latest year, and Valvoline's grew 5.6%. Sonic Automotive had an operating margin of 2.4%, against 22.8% at Valvoline. Free cash flow was $418 million at Sonic Automotive and $38 million at Valvoline. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
