    McCormick vs Post

    McCormick MKC · Post POST · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Post reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times McCormick's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. McCormick's revenue grew 1.7% in its latest year, and Post's grew 3.0%. McCormick had an operating margin of 15.7%, against 9.8% at Post. Free cash flow was $740 million at McCormick and $488 million at Post. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
