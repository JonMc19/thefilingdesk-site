    Becton Dickinson vs Philips

    Becton Dickinson BDX · Philips PHG · Health Care Equipment

    Becton Dickinson reported revenue of $21.8 billion in fiscal 2025, and Philips €17.8 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Becton Dickinson's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and Philips' fell 1.0%. Becton Dickinson had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 8.0% at Philips. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
