    Celestica vs Fabrinet

    Celestica CLS · Fabrinet FN · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    Celestica reported revenue of $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.7 times Fabrinet's $4.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Celestica's revenue grew 28.5% in its latest year, and Fabrinet's grew 35.7%. Celestica had an operating margin of 8.4%, against 10.0% at Fabrinet. Free cash flow was $458 million at Celestica and $4 million at Fabrinet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
