    Autoliv vs Mobileye

    Autoliv ALV · Mobileye MBLY · Automotive Parts & Equipment

    Autoliv reported revenue of $10.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.7 times Mobileye's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Autoliv's revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Mobileye's grew 14.5%. Autoliv had an operating margin of 10.1%, against -23.2% at Mobileye. Free cash flow was $716 million at Autoliv and $523 million at Mobileye. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
