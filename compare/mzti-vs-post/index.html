    Marzetti vs Post

    Marzetti MZTI · Post POST · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Post reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.2 times Marzetti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Marzetti's revenue grew 1.1% in its latest year, and Post's grew 3.0%. Marzetti had an operating margin of 12.4%, against 9.8% at Post. Free cash flow was $206 million at Marzetti and $488 million at Post. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
