    Hershey vs J.M. Smucker

    Hershey HSY · J.M. Smucker SJM · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hershey reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times J.M. Smucker's $9.1 billion in fiscal 2026. Hershey's revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and J.M. Smucker's grew 3.7%. Hershey had an operating margin of 12.3%, against 4.0% at J.M. Smucker. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at Hershey and $1.2 billion at J.M. Smucker. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
