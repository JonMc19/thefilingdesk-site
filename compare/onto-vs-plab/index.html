    Onto Innovation vs Photronics

    Onto Innovation ONTO · Photronics PLAB · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Onto Innovation reported revenue of $1.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Photronics' $849 million in fiscal 2025. Onto Innovation's revenue grew 1.8% in its latest year, and Photronics' fell 2.0%. Onto Innovation had an operating margin of 13.2%, against 24.5% at Photronics. Free cash flow was $300 million at Onto Innovation and $60 million at Photronics. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
