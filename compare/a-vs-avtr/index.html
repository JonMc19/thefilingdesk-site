    Agilent Technologies vs Avantor

    Agilent Technologies A · Avantor AVTR · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    Agilent Technologies reported revenue of $6.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Avantor's $6.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Agilent Technologies' revenue grew 6.7% in its latest year, and Avantor's fell 3.4%. Agilent Technologies had an operating margin of 21.3%, against -3.8% at Avantor. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Agilent Technologies and $495 million at Avantor. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
