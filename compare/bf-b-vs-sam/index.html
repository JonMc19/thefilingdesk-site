    Brown–Forman vs Boston Beer

    Brown–Forman BF-B · Boston Beer SAM · Distillers & Vintners

    Brown–Forman reported revenue of $3.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.0 times Boston Beer's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Brown–Forman's revenue fell 1.2% in its latest year, and Boston Beer's fell 2.4%. Brown–Forman had an operating margin of 25.5%, against 7.4% at Boston Beer. Free cash flow was $893 million at Brown–Forman and $216 million at Boston Beer. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 30 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
