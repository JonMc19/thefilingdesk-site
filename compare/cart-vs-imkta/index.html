    Maplebear vs Ingles Markets

    Maplebear CART · Ingles Markets IMKTA · Food Retail

    Ingles Markets reported revenue of $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Maplebear's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Maplebear's revenue grew 10.8% in its latest year, and Ingles Markets' fell 5.4%. Maplebear had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 2.2% at Ingles Markets. Free cash flow was $910 million at Maplebear and $40 million at Ingles Markets. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
