    Albertsons vs Sprouts Farmers Market

    Albertsons ACI · Sprouts Farmers Market SFM · Food Retail

    Albertsons reported revenue of $83.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 9.4 times Sprouts Farmers Market's $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Albertsons' revenue grew 3.5% in its latest year, and Sprouts Farmers Market's grew 14.1%. Albertsons had an operating margin of 0.9%, against 7.8% at Sprouts Farmers Market. Free cash flow was $527 million at Albertsons and $468 million at Sprouts Farmers Market. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 28 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
