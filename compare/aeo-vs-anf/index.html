    American Eagle Outfitters vs Abercrombie & Fitch

    American Eagle Outfitters AEO · Abercrombie & Fitch ANF · Apparel Retail

    American Eagle Outfitters reported revenue of $5.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Abercrombie & Fitch's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. American Eagle Outfitters' revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Abercrombie & Fitch's grew 6.4%. American Eagle Outfitters had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 13.3% at Abercrombie & Fitch. Free cash flow was $195 million at American Eagle Outfitters and $378 million at Abercrombie & Fitch.
