    Palantir vs Synopsys

    Palantir PLTR · Synopsys SNPS · Application Software

    Synopsys reported revenue of $7.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Palantir's revenue grew 56.2% in its latest year, and Synopsys' grew 15.1%. Palantir had an operating margin of 31.6%, against 13.0% at Synopsys. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and $1.3 billion at Synopsys. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
