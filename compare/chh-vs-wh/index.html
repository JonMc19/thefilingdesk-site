    Choice Hotels vs Wyndham Hotels & Resorts

    Choice Hotels CHH · Wyndham Hotels & Resorts WH · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Choice Hotels reported revenue of $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Wyndham Hotels & Resorts' $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Choice Hotels' revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts' grew 1.5%. Choice Hotels had an operating margin of 28.1%, against 28.1% at Wyndham Hotels & Resorts. Free cash flow was $164 million at Choice Hotels and $321 million at Wyndham Hotels & Resorts.
