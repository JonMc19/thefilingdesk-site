    Amcor vs Packaging Corporation of America

    Amcor AMCR · Packaging Corporation of America PKG · Paper & Plastic Packaging Products & Materials

    Amcor reported revenue of $23.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.6 times Packaging Corporation of America's $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025.
