    Hormel Foods vs Kraft Heinz

    Hormel Foods HRL · Kraft Heinz KHC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Kraft Heinz reported revenue of $24.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Hormel Foods' $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Hormel Foods' revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Kraft Heinz's fell 3.5%. Hormel Foods had an operating margin of 5.9%, against -18.7% at Kraft Heinz. Free cash flow was $534 million at Hormel Foods and $3.7 billion at Kraft Heinz. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
