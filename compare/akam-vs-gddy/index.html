    Akamai Technologies vs GoDaddy

    Akamai Technologies AKAM · GoDaddy GDDY · Internet Services & Infrastructure

    GoDaddy reported revenue of $5.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Akamai Technologies' $4.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Akamai Technologies' revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and GoDaddy's grew 8.3%. Akamai Technologies had an operating margin of 13.5%, against 22.8% at GoDaddy. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Akamai Technologies and $1.6 billion at GoDaddy.
