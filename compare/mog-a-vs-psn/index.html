    Moog vs Parsons

    Moog MOG-A · Parsons PSN · Aerospace & Defense

    Parsons reported revenue of $6.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Moog's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Moog's revenue grew 7.0% in its latest year, and Parsons' fell 5.7%. Moog had an operating margin of 11.7%, against 6.6% at Parsons. Free cash flow was $128 million at Moog and $410 million at Parsons. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
