    Griffon vs Simpson Manufacturing

    Griffon GFF · Simpson Manufacturing SSD · Building Products

    Griffon reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Simpson Manufacturing's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Griffon's revenue fell 4.0% in its latest year, and Simpson Manufacturing's grew 4.5%. Griffon had an operating margin of 8.2%, against 19.6% at Simpson Manufacturing. Free cash flow was $305 million at Griffon and $298 million at Simpson Manufacturing. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
