    Elbit Systems vs TransDigm Group

    Elbit Systems ESLT · TransDigm Group TDG · Aerospace & Defense

    TransDigm Group reported revenue of $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Elbit Systems' $7.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Elbit Systems' revenue grew 16.3% in its latest year, and TransDigm Group's grew 11.2%. Elbit Systems had an operating margin of 8.5%, against 47.2% at TransDigm Group. Free cash flow was $553 million at Elbit Systems and $1.8 billion at TransDigm Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
