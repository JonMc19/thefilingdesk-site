    GoDaddy vs Shopify

    GoDaddy GDDY · Shopify SHOP · Internet Services & Infrastructure

    Shopify reported revenue of $11.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.3 times GoDaddy's $5.0 billion in fiscal 2025. GoDaddy's revenue grew 8.3% in its latest year, and Shopify's grew 30.1%. GoDaddy had an operating margin of 22.8%, against 12.7% at Shopify. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at GoDaddy and $2.0 billion at Shopify.
