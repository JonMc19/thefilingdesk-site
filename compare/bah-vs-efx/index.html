    Booz Allen Hamilton vs Equifax

    Booz Allen Hamilton BAH · Equifax EFX · Research & Consulting Services

    Booz Allen Hamilton reported revenue of $11.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.8 times Equifax's $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Booz Allen Hamilton's revenue fell 6.4% in its latest year, and Equifax's grew 6.9%. Booz Allen Hamilton had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 18.0% at Equifax. Free cash flow was $951 million at Booz Allen Hamilton and $1.1 billion at Equifax. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
