    Broadcom vs Nvidia

    Broadcom AVGO · Nvidia NVDA · Semiconductors

    Nvidia reported revenue of $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.4 times Broadcom's $63.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Broadcom's revenue grew 23.9% in its latest year, and Nvidia's grew 65.5%. Broadcom had an operating margin of 39.9%, against 60.4% at Nvidia. Free cash flow was $26.9 billion at Broadcom and $96.7 billion at Nvidia. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 2 and January 25), so the periods don't line up exactly.
