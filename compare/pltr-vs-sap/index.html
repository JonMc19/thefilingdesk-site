    Palantir vs SAP SE

    Palantir PLTR · SAP SE SAP · Application Software

    Palantir reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, and SAP SE €36.8 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and €8.4 billion at SAP SE.
