    Gildan Activewear vs Kontoor Brands

    Gildan Activewear GIL · Kontoor Brands KTB · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Gildan Activewear reported revenue of $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Kontoor Brands' $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Gildan Activewear's revenue grew 10.7% in its latest year, and Kontoor Brands' grew 20.9%. Gildan Activewear had an operating margin of 17.1%, against 10.7% at Kontoor Brands. Free cash flow was $500 million at Gildan Activewear and $435 million at Kontoor Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
