    Corning vs Littelfuse

    Corning GLW · Littelfuse LFUS · Electronic Components

    Corning reported revenue of $15.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 6.5 times Littelfuse's $2.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Corning's revenue grew 19.1% in its latest year, and Littelfuse's grew 8.9%. Corning had an operating margin of 14.6%, against 1.6% at Littelfuse. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Corning and $366 million at Littelfuse. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
