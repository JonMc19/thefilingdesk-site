    Penguin Solutions vs Synaptics

    Penguin Solutions PENG · Synaptics SYNA · Semiconductors

    Penguin Solutions reported revenue of $1.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times Synaptics' $1.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Penguin Solutions' revenue grew 26.5% in its latest year, and Synaptics' grew 11.4%. Penguin Solutions had an operating margin of 9.6%, against -5.6% at Synaptics. Free cash flow was −$163 million at Penguin Solutions and $101 million at Synaptics. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 28 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
