    Sanmina vs TE Connectivity

    Sanmina SANM · TE Connectivity TEL · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    TE Connectivity reported revenue of $17.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Sanmina's $8.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Sanmina's revenue grew 7.4% in its latest year, and TE Connectivity's grew 8.9%. Sanmina had an operating margin of 4.4%, against 18.6% at TE Connectivity. Free cash flow was $473 million at Sanmina and $3.2 billion at TE Connectivity. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and September 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
