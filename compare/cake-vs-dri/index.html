    Cheesecake Factory vs Darden Restaurants

    Cheesecake Factory CAKE · Darden Restaurants DRI · Restaurants

    Darden Restaurants reported revenue of $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.5 times Cheesecake Factory's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Cheesecake Factory's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Darden Restaurants' grew 9.4%. Cheesecake Factory had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 12.0% at Darden Restaurants. Free cash flow was $155 million at Cheesecake Factory and $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 30 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
