    Adobe vs Oracle

    Adobe ADBE · Oracle ORCL · Application Software

    Oracle reported revenue of $67.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.8 times Adobe's $23.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Adobe's revenue grew 10.5% in its latest year, and Oracle's grew 17.4%. Adobe had an operating margin of 36.6%, against 30.6% at Oracle. Free cash flow was $9.9 billion at Adobe and −$23.7 billion at Oracle. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 28 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
