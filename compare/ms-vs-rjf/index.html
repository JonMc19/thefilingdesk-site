    Morgan Stanley vs Raymond James Financial

    Morgan Stanley MS · Raymond James Financial RJF · Investment Banking & Brokerage

    Morgan Stanley reported revenue of $70.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.4 times Raymond James Financial's $15.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Morgan Stanley's revenue grew 14.4% in its latest year, and Raymond James Financial's grew 6.6%. Morgan Stanley had a return on equity of 15.6%, against 17.7% at Raymond James Financial. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
