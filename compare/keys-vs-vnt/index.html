    Keysight Technologies vs Vontier

    Keysight Technologies KEYS · Vontier VNT · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Keysight Technologies reported revenue of $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Vontier's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Keysight Technologies' revenue grew 8.0% in its latest year, and Vontier's grew 3.2%. Keysight Technologies had an operating margin of 16.3%, against 18.3% at Vontier. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Keysight Technologies and $441 million at Vontier. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
