    Globant vs Teradata

    Globant GLOB · Teradata TDC · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Globant reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Teradata's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Globant's revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Teradata's fell 5.0%. Globant had an operating margin of 7.0%, against 12.3% at Teradata. Free cash flow was $282 million at Globant and $286 million at Teradata.
