    Palantir vs Trimble

    Palantir PLTR · Trimble TRMB · Application Software

    Palantir reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Trimble's $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Palantir's revenue grew 56.2% in its latest year, and Trimble's fell 2.6%. Palantir had an operating margin of 31.6%, against 16.5% at Trimble. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and $361 million at Trimble. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
