    Dutch Bros vs Domino's

    Dutch Bros BROS · Domino's DPZ · Restaurants

    Domino's reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.0 times Dutch Bros' $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Dutch Bros' revenue grew 27.9% in its latest year, and Domino's grew 5.0%. Dutch Bros had an operating margin of 9.8%, against 19.3% at Domino's. Free cash flow was $54 million at Dutch Bros and $672 million at Domino's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
