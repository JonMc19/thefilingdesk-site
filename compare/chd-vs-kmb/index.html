    Church & Dwight vs Kimberly-Clark

    Church & Dwight CHD · Kimberly-Clark KMB · Household Products

    Kimberly-Clark reported revenue of $16.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.7 times Church & Dwight's $6.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Church & Dwight's revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Kimberly-Clark's fell 2.1%. Church & Dwight had an operating margin of 17.4%, against 14.3% at Kimberly-Clark. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Church & Dwight and $1.6 billion at Kimberly-Clark.
