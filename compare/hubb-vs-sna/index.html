    Hubbell vs Snap-on

    Hubbell HUBB · Snap-on SNA · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Hubbell reported revenue of $5.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Snap-on's $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Hubbell's revenue grew 3.8% in its latest year, and Snap-on's grew 0.9%. Hubbell had an operating margin of 20.7%, against 25.8% at Snap-on. Free cash flow was $875 million at Hubbell and $1.0 billion at Snap-on. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
