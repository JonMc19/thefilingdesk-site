    Brown–Forman vs Anheuser-Busch InBev

    Brown–Forman BF-B · Anheuser-Busch InBev BUD · Distillers & Vintners

    Anheuser-Busch InBev reported revenue of $59.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 15.1 times Brown–Forman's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Brown–Forman's revenue fell 1.2% in its latest year, and Anheuser-Busch InBev's fell 0.8%. Brown–Forman had an operating margin of 25.5%, against 26.0% at Anheuser-Busch InBev. Free cash flow was $893 million at Brown–Forman and $11.2 billion at Anheuser-Busch InBev. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
