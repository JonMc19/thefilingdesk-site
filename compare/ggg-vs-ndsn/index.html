    Graco vs Nordson

    Graco GGG · Nordson NDSN · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Nordson reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Graco's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Graco's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and Nordson's grew 3.8%. Graco had an operating margin of 27.9%, against 25.5% at Nordson. Free cash flow was $638 million at Graco and $661 million at Nordson. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 26 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
