    Hilton Worldwide vs Norwegian Cruise Line

    Hilton Worldwide HLT · Norwegian Cruise Line NCLH · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

Hilton Worldwide's revenue grew 7.7% in its latest year, and Norwegian Cruise Line's grew 3.7%. Hilton Worldwide had an operating margin of 22.4%, against 15.9% at Norwegian Cruise Line. Free cash flow was $2.0 billion at Hilton Worldwide and −$1.2 billion at Norwegian Cruise Line.
