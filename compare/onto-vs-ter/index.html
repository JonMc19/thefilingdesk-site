    Onto Innovation vs Teradyne

    Onto Innovation ONTO · Teradyne TER · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

Onto Innovation's revenue grew 1.8% in its latest year, and Teradyne's grew 13.1%. Onto Innovation had an operating margin of 13.2%, against 20.4% at Teradyne. Free cash flow was $300 million at Onto Innovation and $450 million at Teradyne. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
