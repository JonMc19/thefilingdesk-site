    Alphabet vs Microsoft

    Alphabet GOOGL · Microsoft MSFT

    Alphabet reported revenue of $402.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Microsoft's $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Alphabet's revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and Microsoft's grew 17.8%. Alphabet had an operating margin of 32.0%, against 46.8% at Microsoft. Free cash flow was $73.3 billion at Alphabet and $67.0 billion at Microsoft. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
