    Flowers Foods vs Lamb Weston

    Flowers Foods FLO · Lamb Weston LW · Packaged Foods & Meats

Flowers Foods' revenue grew 3.0% in its latest year, and Lamb Weston's grew 2.5%. Flowers Foods had an operating margin of 3.3%, against 8.9% at Lamb Weston. Free cash flow was $319 million at Flowers Foods and $540 million at Lamb Weston. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
