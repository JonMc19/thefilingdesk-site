    Becton Dickinson vs Boston Scientific

    Becton Dickinson BDX · Boston Scientific BSX · Health Care Equipment

    Becton Dickinson reported revenue of $21.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Boston Scientific's $20.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Becton Dickinson's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and Boston Scientific's grew 19.9%. Becton Dickinson had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 18.0% at Boston Scientific. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at Becton Dickinson and $3.7 billion at Boston Scientific. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
