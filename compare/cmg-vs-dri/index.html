    Chipotle Mexican Grill vs Darden Restaurants

    Chipotle Mexican Grill CMG · Darden Restaurants DRI · Restaurants

    Darden Restaurants reported revenue of $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Chipotle Mexican Grill's $11.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Chipotle Mexican Grill's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Darden Restaurants' grew 9.4%. Chipotle Mexican Grill had an operating margin of 16.2%, against 12.0% at Darden Restaurants. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Chipotle Mexican Grill and $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
