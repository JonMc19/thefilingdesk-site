    Netflix vs Spotify Technology

    Netflix NFLX · Spotify Technology SPOT · Movies & Entertainment

    Netflix reported revenue of $45.2 billion in fiscal 2025, and Spotify Technology €17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Netflix's revenue grew 15.8% in its latest year, and Spotify Technology's grew 9.7%. Netflix had an operating margin of 29.5%, against 12.8% at Spotify Technology. Free cash flow was $9.5 billion at Netflix and €2.9 billion at Spotify Technology.
