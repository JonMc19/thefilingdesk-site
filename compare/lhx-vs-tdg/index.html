    L3Harris vs TransDigm Group

    L3Harris LHX · TransDigm Group TDG · Aerospace & Defense

    L3Harris reported revenue of $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times TransDigm Group's $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025. L3Harris' revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and TransDigm Group's grew 11.2%. L3Harris had an operating margin of 9.7%, against 47.2% at TransDigm Group. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at L3Harris and $1.8 billion at TransDigm Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
