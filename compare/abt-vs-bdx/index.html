    Abbott Laboratories vs Becton Dickinson

    Abbott Laboratories ABT · Becton Dickinson BDX · Health Care Equipment

    Abbott Laboratories reported revenue of $44.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Becton Dickinson's $21.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Abbott Laboratories' revenue grew 5.7% in its latest year, and Becton Dickinson's grew 8.2%. Abbott Laboratories had an operating margin of 18.2%, against 11.8% at Becton Dickinson. Free cash flow was $7.4 billion at Abbott Laboratories and $2.7 billion at Becton Dickinson. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
