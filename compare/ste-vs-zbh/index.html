    Steris vs Zimmer Biomet

    Steris STE · Zimmer Biomet ZBH · Health Care Equipment

    Zimmer Biomet reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Steris' $5.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Steris' revenue grew 8.7% in its latest year, and Zimmer Biomet's grew 7.2%. Steris had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 13.3% at Zimmer Biomet. Free cash flow was $972 million at Steris and $1.5 billion at Zimmer Biomet. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
