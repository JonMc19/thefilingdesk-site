    Sally Beauty vs Signet Jewelers

    Sally Beauty SBH · Signet Jewelers SIG · Specialty Stores

    Signet Jewelers reported revenue of $6.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.8 times Sally Beauty's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $173 million at Sally Beauty and $525 million at Signet Jewelers. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
