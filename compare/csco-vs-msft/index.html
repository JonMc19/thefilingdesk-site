    Cisco vs Microsoft

    Cisco CSCO · Microsoft MSFT · Communications Equipment

    Microsoft reported revenue of $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 5.2 times Cisco's $63.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Cisco's revenue grew 11.8% in its latest year, and Microsoft's grew 17.8%. Cisco had an operating margin of 24.3%, against 46.8% at Microsoft. Free cash flow was $12.8 billion at Cisco and $67.0 billion at Microsoft. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 25 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
