    Adobe vs Microsoft

    Adobe ADBE · Microsoft MSFT · Application Software

    Microsoft reported revenue of $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 14.0 times Adobe's $23.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Adobe's revenue grew 10.5% in its latest year, and Microsoft's grew 17.8%. Adobe had an operating margin of 36.6%, against 46.8% at Microsoft. Free cash flow was $9.9 billion at Adobe and $67.0 billion at Microsoft. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 28 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
