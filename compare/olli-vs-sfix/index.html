    Ollie's Bargain Outlet vs Stitch Fix

    Ollie's Bargain Outlet OLLI · Stitch Fix SFIX · Broadline Retail

    Ollie's Bargain Outlet reported revenue of $2.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Stitch Fix's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026.
