    Hasbro vs Mattel

    Hasbro HAS · Mattel MAT · Leisure Products

    Hasbro reported revenue of $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Mattel's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Hasbro's revenue grew 13.1% in its latest year, and Mattel's fell 0.6%. Hasbro had an operating margin of 0.2%, against 10.2% at Mattel. Free cash flow was $830 million at Hasbro and $483 million at Mattel. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
