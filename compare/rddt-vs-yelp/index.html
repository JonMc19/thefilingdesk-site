    Reddit vs Yelp

    Reddit RDDT · Yelp YELP · Interactive Media & Services

    Reddit reported revenue of $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Yelp's $1.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Reddit's revenue grew 69.4% in its latest year, and Yelp's grew 3.8%. Reddit had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 12.6% at Yelp. Free cash flow was $684 million at Reddit and $324 million at Yelp.
