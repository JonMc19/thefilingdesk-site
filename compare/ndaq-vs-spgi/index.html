    Nasdaq vs S&P Global

    Nasdaq NDAQ · S&P Global SPGI · Financial Exchanges & Data

    S&P Global reported revenue of $15.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times Nasdaq's $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Nasdaq's revenue grew 11.7% in its latest year, and S&P Global's grew 7.9%. Nasdaq had an operating margin of 28.2%, against 42.2% at S&P Global. Free cash flow was $2.0 billion at Nasdaq and $5.5 billion at S&P Global.
