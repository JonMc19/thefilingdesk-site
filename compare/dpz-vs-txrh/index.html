    Domino's vs Texas Roadhouse

    Domino's DPZ · Texas Roadhouse TXRH · Restaurants

    Texas Roadhouse reported revenue of $5.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Domino's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Domino's revenue grew 5.0% in its latest year, and Texas Roadhouse's grew 9.4%. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
