    AT&T vs Verizon

    AT&T T · Verizon VZ · Integrated Telecommunication Services

    Verizon reported revenue of $138.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as AT&T's $125.6 billion in fiscal 2025. AT&T's revenue grew 2.7% in its latest year, and Verizon's grew 2.5%. AT&T had an operating margin of 19.2%, against 21.2% at Verizon. Free cash flow was $19.4 billion at AT&T and $20.1 billion at Verizon.
