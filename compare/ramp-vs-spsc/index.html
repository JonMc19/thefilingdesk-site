    LiveRamp vs SPS Commerce

    LiveRamp RAMP · SPS Commerce SPSC · Application Software

    LiveRamp reported revenue of $813 million in fiscal 2026, about the same as SPS Commerce's $752 million in fiscal 2025. LiveRamp's revenue grew 9.0% in its latest year, and SPS Commerce's grew 17.8%. LiveRamp had an operating margin of 10.3%, against 15.7% at SPS Commerce. Free cash flow was $166 million at LiveRamp and $152 million at SPS Commerce. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
