    FedEx vs United Airlines

    FedEx FDX · United Airlines UAL · Industrials

    FedEx reported revenue of $94.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times United Airlines' $59.1 billion in fiscal 2025. FedEx's revenue grew 7.7% in its latest year, and United Airlines' grew 3.5%. FedEx had an operating margin of 5.8%, against 8.0% at United Airlines. Free cash flow was $5.1 billion at FedEx and $2.6 billion at United Airlines. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
