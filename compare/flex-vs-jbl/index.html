    Flex vs Jabil

    Flex FLEX · Jabil JBL · Electronic Manufacturing Services

Flex had an operating margin of 4.9%, against 4.0% at Jabil. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Flex and $1.2 billion at Jabil. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
