    Chefs' Warehouse vs Performance Food Group

    Chefs' Warehouse CHEF · Performance Food Group PFGC · Food Distributors

    Performance Food Group reported revenue of $67.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 16.3 times Chefs' Warehouse's $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Chefs' Warehouse's revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and Performance Food Group's grew 7.2%. Chefs' Warehouse had an operating margin of 3.5%, against 1.3% at Performance Food Group. Free cash flow was $88 million at Chefs' Warehouse and $1.0 billion at Performance Food Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 26 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
