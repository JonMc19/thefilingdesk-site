    Cisco vs Intel

    Cisco CSCO · Intel INTC · Information Technology

    Cisco reported revenue of $63.3 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Intel's $52.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Cisco's revenue grew 11.8% in its latest year, and Intel's fell 0.5%. Cisco had an operating margin of 24.3%, against -4.2% at Intel. Free cash flow was $12.8 billion at Cisco and −$4.9 billion at Intel. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 25 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
