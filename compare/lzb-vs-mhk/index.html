    La-Z-Boy vs Mohawk Industries

    La-Z-Boy LZB · Mohawk Industries MHK · Home Furnishings

    Mohawk Industries reported revenue of $10.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.1 times La-Z-Boy's $2.1 billion in fiscal 2026. La-Z-Boy's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Mohawk Industries' fell 0.5%. La-Z-Boy had an operating margin of 6.1%, against 4.5% at Mohawk Industries. Free cash flow was $128 million at La-Z-Boy and $616 million at Mohawk Industries. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 25 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
