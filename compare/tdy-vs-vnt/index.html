    Teledyne Technologies vs Vontier

    Teledyne Technologies TDY · Vontier VNT · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Teledyne Technologies reported revenue of $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Vontier's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Teledyne Technologies' revenue grew 7.9% in its latest year, and Vontier's grew 3.2%. Teledyne Technologies had an operating margin of 18.8%, against 18.3% at Vontier. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Teledyne Technologies and $441 million at Vontier. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
