    Fortinet vs Gen Digital

    Fortinet FTNT · Gen Digital GEN · Systems Software

    Fortinet reported revenue of $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Gen Digital's $5.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Fortinet's revenue grew 14.2% in its latest year, and Gen Digital's grew 27.1%. Fortinet had an operating margin of 30.7%, against 42.4% at Gen Digital. Free cash flow was $2.2 billion at Fortinet and $1.5 billion at Gen Digital. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
