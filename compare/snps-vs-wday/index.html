    Synopsys vs Workday

    Synopsys SNPS · Workday WDAY · Application Software

    Workday reported revenue of $9.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times Synopsys' $7.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Synopsys' revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and Workday's grew 13.1%. Synopsys had an operating margin of 13.0%, against 7.5% at Workday. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Synopsys and $2.8 billion at Workday. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
