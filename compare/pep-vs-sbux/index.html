    PepsiCo vs Starbucks

    PepsiCo PEP · Starbucks SBUX · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    PepsiCo reported revenue of $93.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times Starbucks' $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025. PepsiCo's revenue grew 2.2% in its latest year, and Starbucks' grew 2.8%. PepsiCo had an operating margin of 12.2%, against 7.9% at Starbucks. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at PepsiCo and $2.4 billion at Starbucks. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
