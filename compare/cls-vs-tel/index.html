    Celestica vs TE Connectivity

    Celestica CLS · TE Connectivity TEL · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    TE Connectivity reported revenue of $17.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Celestica's $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Celestica's revenue grew 28.5% in its latest year, and TE Connectivity's grew 8.9%. Celestica had an operating margin of 8.4%, against 18.6% at TE Connectivity. Free cash flow was $458 million at Celestica and $3.2 billion at TE Connectivity. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
