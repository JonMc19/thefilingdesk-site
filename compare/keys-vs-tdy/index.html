    Keysight Technologies vs Teledyne Technologies

    Keysight Technologies KEYS · Teledyne Technologies TDY · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Teledyne Technologies reported revenue of $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Keysight Technologies' $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Keysight Technologies' revenue grew 8.0% in its latest year, and Teledyne Technologies' grew 7.9%. Keysight Technologies had an operating margin of 16.3%, against 18.8% at Teledyne Technologies. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Keysight Technologies and $1.1 billion at Teledyne Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
