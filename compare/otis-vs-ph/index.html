    Otis Worldwide vs Parker Hannifin

    Otis Worldwide OTIS · Parker Hannifin PH · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Parker Hannifin reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Otis Worldwide's $14.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Otis Worldwide's revenue grew 1.2% in its latest year, and Parker Hannifin's grew 8.3%. Otis Worldwide had an operating margin of 14.8%, against 23.6% at Parker Hannifin. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Otis Worldwide and $3.9 billion at Parker Hannifin. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
