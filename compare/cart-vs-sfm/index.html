    Maplebear vs Sprouts Farmers Market

    Maplebear CART · Sprouts Farmers Market SFM · Food Retail

    Sprouts Farmers Market reported revenue of $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.4 times Maplebear's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Maplebear's revenue grew 10.8% in its latest year, and Sprouts Farmers Market's grew 14.1%. Maplebear had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 7.8% at Sprouts Farmers Market. Free cash flow was $910 million at Maplebear and $468 million at Sprouts Farmers Market. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
