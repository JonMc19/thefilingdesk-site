    General Mills vs Mondelez International

    General Mills GIS · Mondelez International MDLZ · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Mondelez International reported revenue of $38.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times General Mills' $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and Mondelez International's grew 5.8%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 9.2% at Mondelez International. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $3.2 billion at Mondelez International. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
