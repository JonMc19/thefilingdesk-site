    Five Below vs Sally Beauty

    Five Below FIVE · Sally Beauty SBH · Specialty Stores

    Five Below reported revenue of $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Sally Beauty's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Five Below's revenue grew 22.9% in its latest year, and Sally Beauty's fell 0.4%. Five Below had an operating margin of 9.6%, against 8.9% at Sally Beauty. Free cash flow was $412 million at Five Below and $173 million at Sally Beauty. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
