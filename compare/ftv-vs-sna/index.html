    Fortive vs Snap-on

    Fortive FTV · Snap-on SNA · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Snap-on reported revenue of $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Fortive's $4.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Fortive's revenue grew 1.9% in its latest year, and Snap-on's grew 0.9%. Fortive had an operating margin of 17.3%, against 25.8% at Snap-on. Free cash flow was $978 million at Fortive and $1.0 billion at Snap-on. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
