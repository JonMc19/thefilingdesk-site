    Coupang vs Macy's

    Coupang CPNG · Macy's M · Broadline Retail

    Coupang reported revenue of $34.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Macy's $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Coupang's revenue grew 14.1% in its latest year, and Macy's fell 1.7%. Coupang had an operating margin of 1.4%, against 4.5% at Macy's. Free cash flow was $522 million at Coupang and $1.1 billion at Macy's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
