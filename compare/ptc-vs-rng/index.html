    PTC vs RingCentral

    PTC PTC · RingCentral RNG · Application Software

    PTC reported revenue of $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as RingCentral's $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025. PTC's revenue grew 19.2% in its latest year, and RingCentral's grew 4.8%. PTC had an operating margin of 35.9%, against 4.8% at RingCentral. Free cash flow was $857 million at PTC and $587 million at RingCentral. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
