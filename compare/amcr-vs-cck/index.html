    Amcor vs Crown

    Amcor AMCR · Crown CCK · Paper & Plastic Packaging Products & Materials

    Amcor reported revenue of $23.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.9 times Crown's $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Amcor's revenue grew 56.6% in its latest year, and Crown's grew 4.8%. Amcor had an operating margin of 8.1%, against 12.6% at Crown. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Amcor and $1.1 billion at Crown. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
