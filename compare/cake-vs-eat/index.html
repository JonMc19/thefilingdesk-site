    Cheesecake Factory vs Brinker International

    Cheesecake Factory CAKE · Brinker International EAT · Restaurants

    Brinker International reported revenue of $5.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Cheesecake Factory's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Cheesecake Factory's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Brinker International's grew 7.9%. Cheesecake Factory had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 10.7% at Brinker International. Free cash flow was $155 million at Cheesecake Factory and $558 million at Brinker International. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 30 and June 24), so the periods don't line up exactly.
