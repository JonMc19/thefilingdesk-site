    Bright Horizons Family Solutions vs Driven Brands

    Bright Horizons Family Solutions BFAM · Driven Brands DRVN · Specialized Consumer Services

    Bright Horizons Family Solutions reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Driven Brands' $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Bright Horizons Family Solutions' revenue grew 9.2% in its latest year, and Driven Brands' grew 6.3%. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
