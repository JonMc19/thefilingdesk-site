    Raymond James Financial vs Charles Schwab

    Raymond James Financial RJF · Charles Schwab SCHW · Investment Banking & Brokerage

    Charles Schwab reported revenue of $23.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Raymond James Financial's $15.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Raymond James Financial's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Charles Schwab's grew 22.0%. Raymond James Financial had a return on equity of 17.7%, against 18.1% at Charles Schwab. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
