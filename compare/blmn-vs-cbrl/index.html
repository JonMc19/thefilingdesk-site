    Bloomin' Brands vs Cracker Barrel

    Bloomin' Brands BLMN · Cracker Barrel CBRL · Restaurants

    Bloomin' Brands reported revenue of $4.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Cracker Barrel's $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Bloomin' Brands' revenue grew 0.1% in its latest year, and Cracker Barrel's fell 4.7%. Bloomin' Brands had an operating margin of 0.9%, against -0.4% at Cracker Barrel. Free cash flow was $97 million at Bloomin' Brands and $88 million at Cracker Barrel. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
