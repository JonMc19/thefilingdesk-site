    Boot Barn vs Designer Brands

    Boot Barn BOOT · Designer Brands DBI · Apparel Retail

    Designer Brands reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Boot Barn's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Boot Barn's revenue grew 17.9% in its latest year, and Designer Brands' fell 3.9%. Boot Barn had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 1.6% at Designer Brands. Free cash flow was $126 million at Boot Barn and $78 million at Designer Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
