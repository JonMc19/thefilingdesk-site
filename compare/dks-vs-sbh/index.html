    Dick's Sporting Goods vs Sally Beauty

    Dick's Sporting Goods DKS · Sally Beauty SBH · Specialty Stores

    Dick's Sporting Goods reported revenue of $17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.7 times Sally Beauty's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Dick's Sporting Goods' revenue grew 28.1% in its latest year, and Sally Beauty's fell 0.4%. Dick's Sporting Goods had an operating margin of 6.4%, against 8.9% at Sally Beauty. Free cash flow was $400 million at Dick's Sporting Goods and $173 million at Sally Beauty. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
