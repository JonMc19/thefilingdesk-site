    Visa vs Block

    Visa V · Block XYZ · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    Visa reported revenue of $40.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Block's $24.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Visa's revenue grew 11.3% in its latest year, and Block's grew 0.3%. Visa had an operating margin of 60.0%, against 7.1% at Block. Free cash flow was $21.6 billion at Visa and $2.4 billion at Block. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
