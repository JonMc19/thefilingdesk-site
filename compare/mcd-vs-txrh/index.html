    McDonald's vs Texas Roadhouse

    McDonald's MCD · Texas Roadhouse TXRH · Restaurants

    McDonald's reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.6 times Texas Roadhouse's $5.9 billion in fiscal 2025. McDonald's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Texas Roadhouse's grew 9.4%. McDonald's had an operating margin of 46.1%, against 8.1% at Texas Roadhouse. Free cash flow was $7.2 billion at McDonald's and $342 million at Texas Roadhouse. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
