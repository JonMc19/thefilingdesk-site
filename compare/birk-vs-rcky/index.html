    Birkenstock vs Rocky Brands

    Birkenstock BIRK · Rocky Brands RCKY · Footwear

    Birkenstock reported revenue of €2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, and Rocky Brands $482 million in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Birkenstock's revenue grew 16.2% in its latest year, and Rocky Brands' grew 6.2%. Birkenstock had an operating margin of 26.2%, against 7.7% at Rocky Brands. Free cash flow was €308 million at Birkenstock and $10 million at Rocky Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
