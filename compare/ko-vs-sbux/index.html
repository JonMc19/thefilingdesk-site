    Coca-Cola vs Starbucks

    Coca-Cola KO · Starbucks SBUX · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    Coca-Cola reported revenue of $47.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Starbucks' $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Coca-Cola's revenue grew 1.9% in its latest year, and Starbucks' grew 2.8%. Coca-Cola had an operating margin of 28.7%, against 7.9% at Starbucks. Free cash flow was $5.3 billion at Coca-Cola and $2.4 billion at Starbucks. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
