    Aramark vs Genuine Parts

    Aramark ARMK · Genuine Parts GPC · Distributors

    Genuine Parts reported revenue of $24.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Aramark's $18.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Aramark's revenue grew 6.3% in its latest year, and Genuine Parts' grew 3.5%. Free cash flow was $432 million at Aramark and $421 million at Genuine Parts. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
