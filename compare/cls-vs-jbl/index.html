    Celestica vs Jabil

    Celestica CLS · Jabil JBL · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    Jabil reported revenue of $36.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.9 times Celestica's $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Celestica's revenue grew 28.5% in its latest year, and Jabil's grew 20.6%. Celestica had an operating margin of 8.4%, against 4.7% at Jabil. Free cash flow was $458 million at Celestica and $1.4 billion at Jabil. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
