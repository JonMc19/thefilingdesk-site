    RH vs Williams-Sonoma

    RH RH · Williams-Sonoma WSM · Homefurnishing Retail

    Williams-Sonoma reported revenue of $7.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.3 times RH's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. RH's revenue grew 8.1% in its latest year, and Williams-Sonoma's grew 1.2%. RH had an operating margin of 11.3%, against 18.1% at Williams-Sonoma. Free cash flow was $252 million at RH and $1.1 billion at Williams-Sonoma. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and February 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
