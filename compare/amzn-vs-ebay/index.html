    Amazon vs eBay

    Amazon AMZN · eBay EBAY · Broadline Retail

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 64.6 times eBay's $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and eBay's grew 8.0%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 20.5% at eBay. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $1.4 billion at eBay.
