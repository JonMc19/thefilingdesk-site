    Hormel Foods vs McCormick

    Hormel Foods HRL · McCormick MKC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hormel Foods reported revenue of $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times McCormick's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Hormel Foods' revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and McCormick's grew 1.7%. Hormel Foods had an operating margin of 5.9%, against 15.7% at McCormick. Free cash flow was $534 million at Hormel Foods and $740 million at McCormick. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
