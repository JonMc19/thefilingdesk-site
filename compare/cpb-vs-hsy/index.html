    Campbell's vs Hershey

    Campbell's CPB · Hershey HSY · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hershey reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Campbell's $9.7 billion in fiscal 2026. Campbell's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and Hershey's grew 4.4%. Campbell's had an operating margin of 8.7%, against 12.3% at Hershey. Free cash flow was $678 million at Campbell's and $1.8 billion at Hershey. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
