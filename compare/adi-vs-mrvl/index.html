    Analog Devices vs Marvell Technology

    Analog Devices ADI · Marvell Technology MRVL · Semiconductors

    Analog Devices reported revenue of $11.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Marvell Technology's $8.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Analog Devices' revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and Marvell Technology's grew 42.1%. Analog Devices had an operating margin of 26.6%, against 16.1% at Marvell Technology. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Analog Devices and $1.4 billion at Marvell Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
