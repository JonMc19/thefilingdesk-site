    Inter Parfums vs Spectrum Brands

    Inter Parfums IPAR · Spectrum Brands SPB · Household Products

    Spectrum Brands reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times Inter Parfums' $1.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Inter Parfums' revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and Spectrum Brands' fell 5.2%. Inter Parfums had an operating margin of 18.2%, against 4.5% at Spectrum Brands. Free cash flow was $190 million at Inter Parfums and $165 million at Spectrum Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
