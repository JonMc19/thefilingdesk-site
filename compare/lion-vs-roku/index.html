    Lionsgate Studios vs Roku

    Lionsgate Studios LION · Roku ROKU · Movies & Entertainment

    Roku reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Lionsgate Studios' $2.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Lionsgate Studios' revenue grew 1.8% in its latest year, and Roku's grew 15.2%. Lionsgate Studios had an operating margin of 3.7%, against -0.1% at Roku. Free cash flow was $11 million at Lionsgate Studios and $478 million at Roku. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
