    Axon Enterprise vs TransDigm Group

    Axon Enterprise AXON · TransDigm Group TDG · Aerospace & Defense

    TransDigm Group reported revenue of $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.2 times Axon Enterprise's $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Axon Enterprise's revenue grew 33.5% in its latest year, and TransDigm Group's grew 11.2%. Axon Enterprise had an operating margin of -2.2%, against 47.2% at TransDigm Group. Free cash flow was $75 million at Axon Enterprise and $1.8 billion at TransDigm Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
