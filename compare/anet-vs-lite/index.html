    Arista Networks vs Lumentum

    Arista Networks ANET · Lumentum LITE · Communications Equipment

    Arista Networks reported revenue of $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.0 times Lumentum's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Arista Networks' revenue grew 28.6% in its latest year, and Lumentum's grew 83.2%. Arista Networks had an operating margin of 42.8%, against 17.4% at Lumentum. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Arista Networks and $300 million at Lumentum. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
