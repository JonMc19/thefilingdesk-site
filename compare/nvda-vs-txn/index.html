    Nvidia vs Texas Instruments

    Nvidia NVDA · Texas Instruments TXN · Semiconductors

    Nvidia reported revenue of $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 12.2 times Texas Instruments' $17.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Nvidia's revenue grew 65.5% in its latest year, and Texas Instruments' grew 13.1%. Nvidia had an operating margin of 60.4%, against 34.1% at Texas Instruments. Free cash flow was $96.7 billion at Nvidia and $2.6 billion at Texas Instruments. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 25 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
