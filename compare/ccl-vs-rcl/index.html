    Carnival vs Royal Caribbean Group

    Carnival CCL · Royal Caribbean Group RCL · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Carnival reported revenue of $26.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Royal Caribbean Group's $17.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Carnival's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Royal Caribbean Group's grew 8.8%. Carnival had an operating margin of 16.8%, against 27.4% at Royal Caribbean Group. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Carnival and $1.2 billion at Royal Caribbean Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
