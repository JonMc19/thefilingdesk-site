    Phibro Animal Health vs PTC Therapeutics

    Phibro Animal Health PAHC · PTC Therapeutics PTCT · Pharmaceuticals

Free cash flow was $10 million at Phibro Animal Health and $702 million at PTC Therapeutics. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
