    Carnival vs MGM Resorts

    Carnival CCL · MGM Resorts MGM · Consumer Discretionary

    Carnival reported revenue of $26.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times MGM Resorts' $17.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Carnival's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and MGM Resorts' grew 1.7%. Carnival had an operating margin of 16.8%, against 5.7% at MGM Resorts. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Carnival and $1.5 billion at MGM Resorts. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
