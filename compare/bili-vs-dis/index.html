    Bilibili vs Disney

    Bilibili BILI · Disney DIS · Movies & Entertainment

    Bilibili reported revenue of CN¥30.3 billion in fiscal 2025, and Disney $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Bilibili's revenue grew 13.1% in its latest year, and Disney's grew 3.4%. Bilibili had an operating margin of 3.7%, against 18.6% at Disney. Free cash flow was CN¥6.6 billion at Bilibili and $10.1 billion at Disney. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
