    Papa John's vs Shake Shack

    Papa John's PZZA · Shake Shack SHAK · Restaurants

    Papa John's reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Shake Shack's $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Papa John's revenue fell 0.3% in its latest year, and Shake Shack's grew 15.4%. Papa John's had an operating margin of 4.3%, against 4.3% at Shake Shack. Free cash flow was $61 million at Papa John's and $57 million at Shake Shack. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
