    Okta vs Trimble

    Okta OKTA · Trimble TRMB · Application Software

    Trimble reported revenue of $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Okta's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Okta's revenue grew 11.8% in its latest year, and Trimble's fell 2.6%. Okta had an operating margin of 5.1%, against 16.5% at Trimble. Free cash flow was $875 million at Okta and $361 million at Trimble. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
