    Johnson Controls vs Lennox International

    Johnson Controls JCI · Lennox International LII · Building Products

    Johnson Controls reported revenue of $23.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.5 times Lennox International's $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Johnson Controls' revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Lennox International's fell 2.7%. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Johnson Controls and $639 million at Lennox International. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
