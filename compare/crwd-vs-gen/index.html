    CrowdStrike vs Gen Digital

    CrowdStrike CRWD · Gen Digital GEN · Systems Software

    Gen Digital reported revenue of $5.0 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as CrowdStrike's $4.8 billion in fiscal 2026. CrowdStrike's revenue grew 21.7% in its latest year, and Gen Digital's grew 27.1%. CrowdStrike had an operating margin of -6.1%, against 42.4% at Gen Digital. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at CrowdStrike and $1.5 billion at Gen Digital. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and April 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
