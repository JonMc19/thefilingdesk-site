    Check Point vs Nutanix

    Check Point CHKP · Nutanix NTNX · Systems Software

    Nutanix reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Check Point's $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Check Point's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and Nutanix's grew 12.4%. Check Point had an operating margin of 30.5%, against 9.6% at Nutanix. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Check Point and $841 million at Nutanix. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
