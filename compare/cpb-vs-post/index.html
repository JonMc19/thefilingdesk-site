    Campbell's vs Post

    Campbell's CPB · Post POST · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Campbell's reported revenue of $9.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Post's $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Campbell's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and Post's grew 3.0%. Campbell's had an operating margin of 8.7%, against 9.8% at Post. Free cash flow was $678 million at Campbell's and $488 million at Post. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 2 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
