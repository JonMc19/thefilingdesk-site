    Graco vs Kennametal

    Graco GGG · Kennametal KMT · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Kennametal reported revenue of $2.4 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Graco's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Graco's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and Kennametal's grew 19.8%. Graco had an operating margin of 27.9%, against 20.1% at Kennametal. Free cash flow was $638 million at Graco and −$81 million at Kennametal. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 26 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
