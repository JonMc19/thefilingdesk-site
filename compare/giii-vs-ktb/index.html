    G-III Apparel Group vs Kontoor Brands

    G-III Apparel Group GIII · Kontoor Brands KTB · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Kontoor Brands reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as G-III Apparel Group's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2025. G-III Apparel Group's revenue fell 7.0% in its latest year, and Kontoor Brands' grew 20.9%. G-III Apparel Group had an operating margin of 3.6%, against 10.7% at Kontoor Brands. Free cash flow was $264 million at G-III Apparel Group and $435 million at Kontoor Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
