    Synopsys vs Atlassian

    Synopsys SNPS · Atlassian TEAM · Application Software

    Synopsys reported revenue of $7.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Atlassian's $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Synopsys' revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and Atlassian's grew 26.0%. Synopsys had an operating margin of 13.0%, against 0.2% at Atlassian. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Synopsys and $1.3 billion at Atlassian. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
