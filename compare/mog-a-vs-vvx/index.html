    Moog vs V2X

    Moog MOG-A · V2X VVX · Aerospace & Defense

    V2X reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Moog's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Moog's revenue grew 7.0% in its latest year, and V2X's grew 3.6%. Moog had an operating margin of 11.7%, against 4.3% at V2X. Free cash flow was $128 million at Moog and $170 million at V2X. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
