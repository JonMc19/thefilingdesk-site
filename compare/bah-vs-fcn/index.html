    Booz Allen Hamilton vs FTI Consulting

    Booz Allen Hamilton BAH · FTI Consulting FCN · Research & Consulting Services

    Booz Allen Hamilton reported revenue of $11.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.0 times FTI Consulting's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Booz Allen Hamilton's revenue fell 6.4% in its latest year, and FTI Consulting's grew 2.4%. Booz Allen Hamilton had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 10.3% at FTI Consulting. Free cash flow was $951 million at Booz Allen Hamilton and $94 million at FTI Consulting. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
