    EnerSys vs Sensata Technologies

    EnerSys ENS · Sensata Technologies ST · Electrical Components & Equipment

    EnerSys reported revenue of $3.8 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Sensata Technologies' $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. EnerSys' revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Sensata Technologies' fell 5.8%. EnerSys had an operating margin of 11.4%, against 6.4% at Sensata Technologies. Free cash flow was $468 million at EnerSys and $490 million at Sensata Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
