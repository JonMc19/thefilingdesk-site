    Monster Beverage vs PepsiCo

    Monster Beverage MNST · PepsiCo PEP · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    PepsiCo reported revenue of $93.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 11.3 times Monster Beverage's $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Monster Beverage's revenue grew 10.7% in its latest year, and PepsiCo's grew 2.2%. Monster Beverage had an operating margin of 29.2%, against 12.2% at PepsiCo. Free cash flow was $2.0 billion at Monster Beverage and $7.7 billion at PepsiCo. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
