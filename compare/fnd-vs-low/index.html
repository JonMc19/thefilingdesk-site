    Floor & Decor vs Lowe's

    Floor & Decor FND · Lowe's LOW · Home Improvement Retail

    Lowe's reported revenue of $86.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 18.4 times Floor & Decor's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Floor & Decor's revenue grew 5.1% in its latest year, and Lowe's grew 3.1%. Floor & Decor had an operating margin of 5.8%, against 11.8% at Lowe's. Free cash flow was $64 million at Floor & Decor and $7.7 billion at Lowe's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 25 and January 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
