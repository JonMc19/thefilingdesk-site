    Howmet Aerospace vs Moog

    Howmet Aerospace HWM · Moog MOG-A · Aerospace & Defense

    Howmet Aerospace reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Moog's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Howmet Aerospace's revenue grew 11.1% in its latest year, and Moog's grew 7.0%. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
