    Boeing vs L3Harris

    Boeing BA · L3Harris LHX · Aerospace & Defense

    Boeing reported revenue of $89.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.1 times L3Harris' $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Boeing's revenue grew 34.5% in its latest year, and L3Harris' grew 2.5%. Boeing had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 9.7% at L3Harris. Free cash flow was −$1.9 billion at Boeing and $2.7 billion at L3Harris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
