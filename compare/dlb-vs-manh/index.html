    Dolby vs Manhattan Associates

    Dolby DLB · Manhattan Associates MANH · Application Software

Free cash flow was $436 million at Dolby and $374 million at Manhattan Associates. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 26 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
