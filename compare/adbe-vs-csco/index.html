    Adobe vs Cisco

    Adobe ADBE · Cisco CSCO · Application Software

    Cisco reported revenue of $63.3 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.7 times Adobe's $23.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Adobe's revenue grew 10.5% in its latest year, and Cisco's grew 11.8%. Adobe had an operating margin of 36.6%, against 24.3% at Cisco. Free cash flow was $9.9 billion at Adobe and $12.8 billion at Cisco. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 28 and July 25), so the periods don't line up exactly.
