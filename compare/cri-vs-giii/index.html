    Carter's vs G-III Apparel Group

    Carter's CRI · G-III Apparel Group GIII · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    G-III Apparel Group reported revenue of $3.0 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Carter's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Carter's revenue grew 1.9% in its latest year, and G-III Apparel Group's fell 7.0%. Carter's had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 3.6% at G-III Apparel Group. Free cash flow was $69 million at Carter's and $264 million at G-III Apparel Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
