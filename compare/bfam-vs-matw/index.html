    Bright Horizons Family Solutions vs Matthews International

    Bright Horizons Family Solutions BFAM · Matthews International MATW · Specialized Consumer Services

    Bright Horizons Family Solutions reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Matthews International's $1.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Bright Horizons Family Solutions had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 5.0% at Matthews International. Free cash flow was $259 million at Bright Horizons Family Solutions and −$59 million at Matthews International. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
