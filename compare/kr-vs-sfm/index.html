    Kroger vs Sprouts Farmers Market

    Kroger KR · Sprouts Farmers Market SFM · Food Retail

    Kroger reported revenue of $147.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 16.8 times Sprouts Farmers Market's $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Kroger's revenue grew 0.3% in its latest year, and Sprouts Farmers Market's grew 14.1%. Kroger had an operating margin of 1.3%, against 7.8% at Sprouts Farmers Market. Free cash flow was $3.5 billion at Kroger and $468 million at Sprouts Farmers Market. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
