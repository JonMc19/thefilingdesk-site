    Franklin Electric vs Graco

    Franklin Electric FELE · Graco GGG · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Graco reported revenue of $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Franklin Electric's $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Franklin Electric's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Graco's grew 5.8%. Franklin Electric had an operating margin of 12.6%, against 27.9% at Graco. Free cash flow was $194 million at Franklin Electric and $638 million at Graco. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
