    Darling Ingredients vs Ingredion

    Darling Ingredients DAR · Ingredion INGR · Agricultural Products & Services

    Ingredion reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Darling Ingredients' $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Darling Ingredients' revenue grew 7.4% in its latest year, and Ingredion's fell 2.8%. Darling Ingredients had an operating margin of 4.5%, against 14.1% at Ingredion. Free cash flow was $679 million at Darling Ingredients and $511 million at Ingredion. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
