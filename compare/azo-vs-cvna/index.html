    AutoZone vs Carvana

    AutoZone AZO · Carvana CVNA · Automotive Retail

    Carvana reported revenue of $20.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as AutoZone's $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025. AutoZone's revenue grew 2.4% in its latest year, and Carvana's grew 48.6%. AutoZone had an operating margin of 19.1%, against 9.3% at Carvana. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at AutoZone and $889 million at Carvana. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
