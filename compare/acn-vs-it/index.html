    Accenture vs Gartner

    Accenture ACN · Gartner IT · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Accenture reported revenue of $69.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 10.7 times Gartner's $6.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Accenture's revenue grew 7.4% in its latest year, and Gartner's grew 3.7%. Accenture had an operating margin of 14.7%, against 15.8% at Gartner. Free cash flow was $10.9 billion at Accenture and $1.2 billion at Gartner. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
