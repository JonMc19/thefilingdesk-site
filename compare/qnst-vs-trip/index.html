    QuinStreet vs Tripadvisor

    QuinStreet QNST · Tripadvisor TRIP · Interactive Media & Services

    Tripadvisor reported revenue of $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times QuinStreet's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026. QuinStreet's revenue grew 18.3% in its latest year, and Tripadvisor's grew 3.0%. QuinStreet had an operating margin of 2.7%, against 4.2% at Tripadvisor. Free cash flow was $128 million at QuinStreet and $163 million at Tripadvisor. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
