    AMD vs Arm Holdings

    AMD AMD · Arm Holdings ARM · Semiconductors

AMD had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 18.3% at Arm Holdings. Free cash flow was $6.7 billion at AMD and $979 million at Arm Holdings. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
