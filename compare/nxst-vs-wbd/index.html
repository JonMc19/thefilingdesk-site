    Nexstar Media Group vs Warner Bros. Discovery

    Nexstar Media Group NXST · Warner Bros. Discovery WBD · Broadcasting

    Warner Bros. Discovery reported revenue of $37.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 7.5 times Nexstar Media Group's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Nexstar Media Group's revenue fell 8.5% in its latest year, and Warner Bros. Discovery's fell 5.2%. Nexstar Media Group had an operating margin of 17.1%, against 2.0% at Warner Bros. Discovery. Free cash flow was $743 million at Nexstar Media Group and $3.1 billion at Warner Bros. Discovery.
