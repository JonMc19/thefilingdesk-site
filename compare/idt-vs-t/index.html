    IDT vs AT&T

    IDT IDT · AT&T T · Integrated Telecommunication Services

    AT&T reported revenue of $125.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 96.8 times IDT's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026. IDT's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and AT&T's grew 2.7%. IDT had an operating margin of 9.3%, against 19.2% at AT&T. Free cash flow was $68 million at IDT and $19.4 billion at AT&T. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
