    Cummins vs Wabtec

    Cummins CMI · Wabtec WAB · Construction Machinery & Heavy Transportation Equipment

    Cummins reported revenue of $33.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.0 times Wabtec's $11.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Cummins' revenue fell 1.3% in its latest year, and Wabtec's grew 7.5%. Cummins had an operating margin of 11.9%, against 16.1% at Wabtec. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Cummins and $1.5 billion at Wabtec.
