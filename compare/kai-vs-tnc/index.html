    Kadant vs Tennant

    Kadant KAI · Tennant TNC · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Tennant reported revenue of $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Kadant's $1.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Kadant's revenue fell 0.1% in its latest year, and Tennant's fell 6.5%. Kadant had an operating margin of 14.9%, against 5.7% at Tennant. Free cash flow was $154 million at Kadant and $43 million at Tennant. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
