    Salesforce vs Oracle

    Salesforce CRM · Oracle ORCL · Application Software

    Oracle reported revenue of $67.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times Salesforce's $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026. Salesforce's revenue grew 9.6% in its latest year, and Oracle's grew 17.4%. Salesforce had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 30.6% at Oracle. Free cash flow was $14.4 billion at Salesforce and −$23.7 billion at Oracle. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
