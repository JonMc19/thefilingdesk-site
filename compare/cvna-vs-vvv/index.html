    Carvana vs Valvoline

    Carvana CVNA · Valvoline VVV · Automotive Retail

    Carvana reported revenue of $20.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 11.9 times Valvoline's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Carvana's revenue grew 48.6% in its latest year, and Valvoline's grew 5.6%. Carvana had an operating margin of 9.3%, against 22.8% at Valvoline. Free cash flow was $889 million at Carvana and $38 million at Valvoline. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
