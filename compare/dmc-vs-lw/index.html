    Del Monte vs Lamb Weston

    Del Monte DMC · Lamb Weston LW · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Lamb Weston reported revenue of $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Del Monte's $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Del Monte's revenue grew 1.0% in its latest year, and Lamb Weston's grew 2.5%. Del Monte had an operating margin of 3.2%, against 8.9% at Lamb Weston. Free cash flow was $181 million at Del Monte and $540 million at Lamb Weston. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 26 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
