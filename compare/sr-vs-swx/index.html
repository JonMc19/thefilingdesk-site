    Spire vs Southwest Gas

    Spire SR · Southwest Gas SWX · Gas Utilities

    Spire reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Southwest Gas' $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Spire's revenue fell 4.5% in its latest year, and Southwest Gas' fell 21.6%. Spire had an operating margin of 21.2%, against 24.4% at Southwest Gas. Free cash flow was −$344 million at Spire and −$252 million at Southwest Gas. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
