    Microsoft vs Palantir

    Microsoft MSFT · Palantir PLTR · Information Technology

    Microsoft reported revenue of $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 74.1 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Microsoft's revenue grew 17.8% in its latest year, and Palantir's grew 56.2%. Microsoft had an operating margin of 46.8%, against 31.6% at Palantir. Free cash flow was $67.0 billion at Microsoft and $2.1 billion at Palantir. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
