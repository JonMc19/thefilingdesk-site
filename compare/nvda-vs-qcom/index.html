    Nvidia vs Qualcomm

    Nvidia NVDA · Qualcomm QCOM · Semiconductors

    Nvidia reported revenue of $215.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 5.0 times Qualcomm's $43.1 billion in fiscal 2026. Nvidia's revenue grew 65.5% in its latest year, and Qualcomm's fell 2.6%. Nvidia had an operating margin of 60.4%, against 20.1% at Qualcomm. Free cash flow was $96.7 billion at Nvidia and $8.7 billion at Qualcomm. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 25 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
