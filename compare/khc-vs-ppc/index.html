    Kraft Heinz vs Pilgrim's Pride

    Kraft Heinz KHC · Pilgrim's Pride PPC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Kraft Heinz reported revenue of $24.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Pilgrim's Pride's $18.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Kraft Heinz's revenue fell 3.5% in its latest year, and Pilgrim's Pride's grew 3.5%. Kraft Heinz had an operating margin of -18.7%, against 8.7% at Pilgrim's Pride. Free cash flow was $3.7 billion at Kraft Heinz and $653 million at Pilgrim's Pride. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
