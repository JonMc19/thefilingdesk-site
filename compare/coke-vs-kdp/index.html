    Coca-Cola Consolidated vs Keurig Dr Pepper

    Coca-Cola Consolidated COKE · Keurig Dr Pepper KDP · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    Keurig Dr Pepper reported revenue of $16.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.3 times Coca-Cola Consolidated's $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Coca-Cola Consolidated's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Keurig Dr Pepper's grew 8.2%. Coca-Cola Consolidated had an operating margin of 13.2%, against 21.5% at Keurig Dr Pepper. Free cash flow was $620 million at Coca-Cola Consolidated and $1.5 billion at Keurig Dr Pepper.
