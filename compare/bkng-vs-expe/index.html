    Booking vs Expedia Group

    Booking BKNG · Expedia Group EXPE · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Booking reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Expedia Group's $14.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Booking's revenue grew 13.4% in its latest year, and Expedia Group's grew 7.6%. Booking had an operating margin of 32.8%, against 12.7% at Expedia Group. Free cash flow was $9.1 billion at Booking and $3.1 billion at Expedia Group.
