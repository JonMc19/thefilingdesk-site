    Leggett & Platt vs La-Z-Boy

    Leggett & Platt LEG · La-Z-Boy LZB · Home Furnishings

    Leggett & Platt reported revenue of $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times La-Z-Boy's $2.1 billion in fiscal 2026. Leggett & Platt's revenue fell 7.5% in its latest year, and La-Z-Boy's grew 0.8%. Free cash flow was $281 million at Leggett & Platt and $128 million at La-Z-Boy. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 25), so the periods don't line up exactly.
