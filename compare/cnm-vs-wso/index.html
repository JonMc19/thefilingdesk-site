    Core & Main vs Watsco

    Core & Main CNM · Watsco WSO · Trading Companies & Distributors

    Core & Main reported revenue of $7.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Watsco's $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Core & Main's revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Watsco's fell 5.0%. Core & Main had an operating margin of 9.4%, against 9.9% at Watsco. Free cash flow was $604 million at Core & Main and $535 million at Watsco. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
