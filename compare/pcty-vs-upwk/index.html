    Paylocity vs Upwork

    Paylocity PCTY · Upwork UPWK · Human Resource & Employment Services

    Paylocity reported revenue of $1.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.2 times Upwork's $788 million in fiscal 2025. Paylocity's revenue grew 11.0% in its latest year, and Upwork's grew 2.4%. Paylocity had an operating margin of 21.8%, against 16.4% at Upwork. Free cash flow was $497 million at Paylocity and $242 million at Upwork. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
