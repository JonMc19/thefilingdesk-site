    Allegion vs Fortune Brands Innovations

    Allegion ALLE · Fortune Brands Innovations FBIN · Building Products

    Fortune Brands Innovations reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Allegion's $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Allegion's revenue grew 7.8% in its latest year, and Fortune Brands Innovations' fell 3.2%. Allegion had an operating margin of 21.1%, against 11.6% at Fortune Brands Innovations. Free cash flow was $686 million at Allegion and $367 million at Fortune Brands Innovations. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
