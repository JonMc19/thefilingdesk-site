    Cirrus Logic vs Tower Semiconductor

    Cirrus Logic CRUS · Tower Semiconductor TSEM · Semiconductors

    Cirrus Logic reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Tower Semiconductor's $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Cirrus Logic's revenue grew 5.3% in its latest year, and Tower Semiconductor's grew 9.1%. Cirrus Logic had an operating margin of 23.1%, against 12.4% at Tower Semiconductor. Free cash flow was $637 million at Cirrus Logic and −$49 million at Tower Semiconductor. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
