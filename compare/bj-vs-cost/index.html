    BJ's Wholesale Club vs Costco

    BJ's Wholesale Club BJ · Costco COST · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Costco reported revenue of $303.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 14.1 times BJ's Wholesale Club's $21.5 billion in fiscal 2025. BJ's Wholesale Club's revenue grew 4.7% in its latest year, and Costco's grew 10.1%. BJ's Wholesale Club had an operating margin of 3.8%, against 3.9% at Costco. Free cash flow was $328 million at BJ's Wholesale Club and $9.4 billion at Costco. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and August 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
