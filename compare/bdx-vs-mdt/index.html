    Becton Dickinson vs Medtronic

    Becton Dickinson BDX · Medtronic MDT · Health Care Equipment

    Medtronic reported revenue of $36.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.7 times Becton Dickinson's $21.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Becton Dickinson's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and Medtronic's grew 8.4%. Becton Dickinson had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 17.8% at Medtronic. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at Becton Dickinson and $5.4 billion at Medtronic. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and April 24), so the periods don't line up exactly.
