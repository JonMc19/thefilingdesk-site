    Middleby vs Nordson

    Middleby MIDD · Nordson NDSN · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Middleby reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Nordson's $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Middleby's revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Nordson's grew 3.8%. Middleby had an operating margin of 18.0%, against 25.5% at Nordson. Free cash flow was $559 million at Middleby and $661 million at Nordson. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
