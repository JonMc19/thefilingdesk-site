    Cboe Global Markets vs FactSet

    Cboe Global Markets CBOE · FactSet FDS · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Cboe Global Markets reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times FactSet's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Cboe Global Markets' revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and FactSet's grew 5.4%. Cboe Global Markets had an operating margin of 31.1%, against 32.2% at FactSet. Free cash flow was $1.7 billion at Cboe Global Markets and $617 million at FactSet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
