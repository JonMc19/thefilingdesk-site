    Maplebear vs Kroger

    Maplebear CART · Kroger KR · Food Retail

    Kroger reported revenue of $147.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 39.5 times Maplebear's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Maplebear's revenue grew 10.8% in its latest year, and Kroger's grew 0.3%. Maplebear had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 1.3% at Kroger. Free cash flow was $910 million at Maplebear and $3.5 billion at Kroger. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
