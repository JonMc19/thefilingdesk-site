    Intuitive Surgical vs Steris

    Intuitive Surgical ISRG · Steris STE · Health Care Equipment

    Intuitive Surgical reported revenue of $10.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Steris' $5.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Intuitive Surgical's revenue grew 20.5% in its latest year, and Steris' grew 8.7%. Intuitive Surgical had an operating margin of 29.3%, against 18.6% at Steris. Free cash flow was $2.5 billion at Intuitive Surgical and $972 million at Steris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
