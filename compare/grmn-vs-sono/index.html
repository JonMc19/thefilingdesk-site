    Garmin vs Sonos

    Garmin GRMN · Sonos SONO · Consumer Electronics

    Garmin reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.0 times Sonos' $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Garmin's revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and Sonos' fell 4.9%. Garmin had an operating margin of 25.9%, against -3.5% at Sonos. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Garmin and $108 million at Sonos. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
