    Birkenstock vs Caleres

    Birkenstock BIRK · Caleres CAL · Footwear

    Birkenstock reported revenue of €2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, and Caleres $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Birkenstock's revenue grew 16.2% in its latest year, and Caleres' grew 1.3%. Birkenstock had an operating margin of 26.2%, against 0.2% at Caleres. Free cash flow was €308 million at Birkenstock and $39 million at Caleres. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
