    Apple vs Amazon

    Apple AAPL · Amazon AMZN · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Apple's $416.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Apple's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Amazon's grew 12.4%. Apple had an operating margin of 32.0%, against 11.2% at Amazon. Free cash flow was $98.8 billion at Apple and $7.7 billion at Amazon. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
