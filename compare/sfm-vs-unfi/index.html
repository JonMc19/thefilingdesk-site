    Sprouts Farmers Market vs United Natural Foods

    Sprouts Farmers Market SFM · United Natural Foods UNFI · Food Retail

    United Natural Foods reported revenue of $31.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.5 times Sprouts Farmers Market's $8.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Sprouts Farmers Market's revenue grew 14.1% in its latest year, and United Natural Foods' fell 2.0%. Sprouts Farmers Market had an operating margin of 7.8%, against 0.7% at United Natural Foods. Free cash flow was $468 million at Sprouts Farmers Market and $323 million at United Natural Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and August 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
