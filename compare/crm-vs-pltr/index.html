    Salesforce vs Palantir

    Salesforce CRM · Palantir PLTR · Application Software

    Salesforce reported revenue of $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 9.3 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Salesforce's revenue grew 9.6% in its latest year, and Palantir's grew 56.2%. Salesforce had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 31.6% at Palantir. Free cash flow was $14.4 billion at Salesforce and $2.1 billion at Palantir. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
