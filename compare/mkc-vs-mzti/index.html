    McCormick vs Marzetti

    McCormick MKC · Marzetti MZTI · Packaged Foods & Meats

    McCormick reported revenue of $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.5 times Marzetti's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2026. McCormick's revenue grew 1.7% in its latest year, and Marzetti's grew 1.1%. McCormick had an operating margin of 15.7%, against 12.4% at Marzetti. Free cash flow was $740 million at McCormick and $206 million at Marzetti. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
