    Coca-Cola Consolidated vs Monster Beverage

    Coca-Cola Consolidated COKE · Monster Beverage MNST · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    Monster Beverage reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Coca-Cola Consolidated's $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Coca-Cola Consolidated's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Monster Beverage's grew 10.7%. Coca-Cola Consolidated had an operating margin of 13.2%, against 29.2% at Monster Beverage. Free cash flow was $620 million at Coca-Cola Consolidated and $2.0 billion at Monster Beverage.
