    Federal Signal vs Greenbrier Companies

    Federal Signal FSS · Greenbrier Companies GBX · Construction Machinery & Heavy Transportation Equipment

    Greenbrier Companies reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Federal Signal's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Federal Signal's revenue grew 17.1% in its latest year, and Greenbrier Companies' fell 24.0%. Federal Signal had an operating margin of 15.6%, against 5.8% at Greenbrier Companies. Free cash flow was $227 million at Federal Signal and −$240 million at Greenbrier Companies. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
