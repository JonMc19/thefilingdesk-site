    Hilton Worldwide vs Marriott International

    Hilton Worldwide HLT · Marriott International MAR · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Marriott International reported revenue of $26.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Hilton Worldwide's $12.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Hilton Worldwide's revenue grew 7.7% in its latest year, and Marriott International's grew 4.3%. Hilton Worldwide had an operating margin of 22.4%, against 15.8% at Marriott International. Free cash flow was $2.0 billion at Hilton Worldwide and $2.6 billion at Marriott International.
