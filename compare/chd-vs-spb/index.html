    Church & Dwight vs Spectrum Brands

    Church & Dwight CHD · Spectrum Brands SPB · Household Products

    Church & Dwight reported revenue of $6.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Spectrum Brands' $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Church & Dwight's revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Spectrum Brands' fell 5.2%. Church & Dwight had an operating margin of 17.4%, against 4.5% at Spectrum Brands. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Church & Dwight and $165 million at Spectrum Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
