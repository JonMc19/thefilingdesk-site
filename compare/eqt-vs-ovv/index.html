    EQT vs Ovintiv

    EQT EQT · Ovintiv OVV · Oil & Gas Exploration & Production

    Ovintiv reported revenue of $8.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as EQT's $8.6 billion in fiscal 2025. EQT's revenue grew 63.9% in its latest year, and Ovintiv's fell 2.7%. EQT had an operating margin of 37.6%, against 12.7% at Ovintiv. Free cash flow was $2.8 billion at EQT and $1.5 billion at Ovintiv.
