    AMD vs Broadcom

    AMD AMD · Broadcom AVGO · Semiconductors

    Broadcom reported revenue of $63.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times AMD's $34.6 billion in fiscal 2025. AMD's revenue grew 34.3% in its latest year, and Broadcom's grew 23.9%. AMD had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 39.9% at Broadcom. Free cash flow was $6.7 billion at AMD and $26.9 billion at Broadcom. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and November 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
