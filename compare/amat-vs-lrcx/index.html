    Applied Materials vs Lam Research

    Applied Materials AMAT · Lam Research LRCX · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Applied Materials reported revenue of $28.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Lam Research's $23.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Applied Materials' revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Lam Research's grew 26.0%. Applied Materials had an operating margin of 29.2%, against 35.3% at Lam Research. Free cash flow was $5.7 billion at Applied Materials and $4.9 billion at Lam Research. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and June 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
