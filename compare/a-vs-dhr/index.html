    Agilent Technologies vs Danaher

    Agilent Technologies A · Danaher DHR · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    Danaher reported revenue of $24.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.5 times Agilent Technologies' $6.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Agilent Technologies' revenue grew 6.7% in its latest year, and Danaher's grew 2.9%. Agilent Technologies had an operating margin of 21.3%, against 19.1% at Danaher. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Agilent Technologies and $5.3 billion at Danaher. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
