    F5 vs Motorola Solutions

    F5 FFIV · Motorola Solutions MSI · Communications Equipment

    Motorola Solutions reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.8 times F5's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. F5's revenue grew 9.7% in its latest year, and Motorola Solutions' grew 8.0%. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
