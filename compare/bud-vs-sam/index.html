    Anheuser-Busch InBev vs Boston Beer

    Anheuser-Busch InBev BUD · Boston Beer SAM · Distillers & Vintners

    Anheuser-Busch InBev reported revenue of $59.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 30.2 times Boston Beer's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Anheuser-Busch InBev's revenue fell 0.8% in its latest year, and Boston Beer's fell 2.4%. Anheuser-Busch InBev had an operating margin of 26.0%, against 7.4% at Boston Beer. Free cash flow was $11.2 billion at Anheuser-Busch InBev and $216 million at Boston Beer. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
