    A10 Networks vs BlackBerry

    A10 Networks ATEN · BlackBerry BB · Systems Software

    BlackBerry reported revenue of $549 million in fiscal 2026, 1.9 times A10 Networks' $291 million in fiscal 2025. A10 Networks' revenue grew 11.0% in its latest year, and BlackBerry's grew 2.6%. A10 Networks had an operating margin of 16.2%, against 8.8% at BlackBerry. Free cash flow was $65 million at A10 Networks and $46 million at BlackBerry. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and February 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
