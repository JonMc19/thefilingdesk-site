    Palantir vs Snowflake

    Palantir PLTR · Snowflake SNOW · Application Software

    Snowflake reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Palantir's revenue grew 56.2% in its latest year, and Snowflake's grew 29.2%. Palantir had an operating margin of 31.6%, against -30.6% at Snowflake. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and $1.1 billion at Snowflake. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
