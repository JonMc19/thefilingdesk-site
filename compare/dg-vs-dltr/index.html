    Dollar General vs Dollar Tree

    Dollar General DG · Dollar Tree DLTR · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Dollar General reported revenue of $42.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Dollar Tree's $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Dollar General's revenue grew 5.2% in its latest year, and Dollar Tree's grew 10.4%. Dollar General had an operating margin of 5.2%, against 8.5% at Dollar Tree. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Dollar General and $1.1 billion at Dollar Tree. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
