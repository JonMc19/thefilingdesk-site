    L3Harris vs Lockheed Martin

    L3Harris LHX · Lockheed Martin LMT · Aerospace & Defense

    Lockheed Martin reported revenue of $75.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.4 times L3Harris' $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025. L3Harris' revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and Lockheed Martin's grew 5.6%. L3Harris had an operating margin of 9.7%, against 10.3% at Lockheed Martin. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at L3Harris and $6.9 billion at Lockheed Martin. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
