    Apple vs Alphabet

    Apple AAPL · Alphabet GOOGL · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Apple reported revenue of $416.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Alphabet's $402.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Apple's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Alphabet's grew 15.1%. Apple had an operating margin of 32.0%, against 32.0% at Alphabet. Free cash flow was $98.8 billion at Apple and $73.3 billion at Alphabet. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
