    LeMaitre Vascular vs STAAR Surgical

    LeMaitre Vascular LMAT · STAAR Surgical STAA · Health Care Equipment

Free cash flow was $74 million at LeMaitre Vascular and −$40 million at STAAR Surgical. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
