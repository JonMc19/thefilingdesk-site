    Amazon vs Walmart

    Amazon AMZN · Walmart WMT · Broadline Retail

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Walmart's $713.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Walmart's grew 4.7%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 4.2% at Walmart. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $14.9 billion at Walmart. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
