    Paycom vs Paylocity

    Paycom PAYC · Paylocity PCTY · Human Resource & Employment Services

    Paycom reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Paylocity's $1.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Paycom's revenue grew 8.9% in its latest year, and Paylocity's grew 11.0%. Paycom had an operating margin of 27.6%, against 21.8% at Paylocity. Free cash flow was $408 million at Paycom and $497 million at Paylocity. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
