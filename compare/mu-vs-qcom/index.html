    Micron Technology vs Qualcomm

    Micron Technology MU · Qualcomm QCOM · Semiconductors

    Micron Technology reported revenue of $133.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.0 times Qualcomm's $44.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Micron Technology's revenue grew 256.3% in its latest year, and Qualcomm's grew 13.7%. Micron Technology had an operating margin of 74.6%, against 27.9% at Qualcomm. Free cash flow was $59.0 billion at Micron Technology and $12.8 billion at Qualcomm. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 3 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
