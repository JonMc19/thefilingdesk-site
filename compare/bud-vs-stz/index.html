    Anheuser-Busch InBev vs Constellation Brands

    Anheuser-Busch InBev BUD · Constellation Brands STZ · Distillers & Vintners

    Anheuser-Busch InBev reported revenue of $59.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 6.5 times Constellation Brands' $9.1 billion in fiscal 2026. Anheuser-Busch InBev's revenue fell 0.8% in its latest year, and Constellation Brands' fell 10.5%. Anheuser-Busch InBev had an operating margin of 26.0%, against 29.8% at Constellation Brands. Free cash flow was $11.2 billion at Anheuser-Busch InBev and $1.8 billion at Constellation Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and February 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
