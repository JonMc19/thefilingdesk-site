    Mastercard vs PayPal

    Mastercard MA · PayPal PYPL · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    PayPal reported revenue of $33.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Mastercard's $32.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Mastercard's revenue grew 16.4% in its latest year, and PayPal's grew 4.3%. Mastercard had an operating margin of 57.6%, against 18.3% at PayPal. Free cash flow was $17.2 billion at Mastercard and $5.6 billion at PayPal.
