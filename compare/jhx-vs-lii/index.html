    James Hardie Industries vs Lennox International

    James Hardie Industries JHX · Lennox International LII · Building Products

    Lennox International reported revenue of $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as James Hardie Industries' $4.8 billion in fiscal 2026. James Hardie Industries' revenue grew 24.7% in its latest year, and Lennox International's fell 2.7%. James Hardie Industries had an operating margin of 9.3%, against 20.1% at Lennox International. Free cash flow was $206 million at James Hardie Industries and $639 million at Lennox International. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
