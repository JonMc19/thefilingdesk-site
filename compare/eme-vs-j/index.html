    Emcor vs Jacobs Solutions

    Emcor EME · Jacobs Solutions J · Construction & Engineering

    Emcor reported revenue of $17.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Jacobs Solutions' $12.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Emcor's revenue grew 16.6% in its latest year, and Jacobs Solutions' grew 4.6%. Emcor had an operating margin of 10.1%, against 7.2% at Jacobs Solutions. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Emcor and $607 million at Jacobs Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
