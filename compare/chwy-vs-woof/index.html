    Chewy vs Petco

    Chewy CHWY · Petco WOOF · Other Specialty Retail

    Chewy reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Petco's $6.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Chewy's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and Petco's fell 2.5%. Chewy had an operating margin of 2.0%, against 2.0% at Petco. Free cash flow was $562 million at Chewy and $187 million at Petco. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
