    McDonald's vs Shake Shack

    McDonald's MCD · Shake Shack SHAK · Restaurants

    McDonald's reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 18.6 times Shake Shack's $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. McDonald's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Shake Shack's grew 15.4%. McDonald's had an operating margin of 46.1%, against 4.3% at Shake Shack. Free cash flow was $7.2 billion at McDonald's and $57 million at Shake Shack.
