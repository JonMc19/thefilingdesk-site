    Haemonetics vs Envista

    Haemonetics HAE · Envista NVST · Health Care Supplies

    Envista reported revenue of $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Haemonetics' $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Haemonetics' revenue fell 2.0% in its latest year, and Envista's grew 8.3%. Haemonetics had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 8.0% at Envista. Free cash flow was $260 million at Haemonetics and $230 million at Envista. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
