    Ciena vs Motorola Solutions

    Ciena CIEN · Motorola Solutions MSI · Communications Equipment

    Motorola Solutions reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.4 times Ciena's $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Ciena's revenue grew 18.8% in its latest year, and Motorola Solutions' grew 8.0%. Ciena had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 25.6% at Motorola Solutions. Free cash flow was $665 million at Ciena and $2.6 billion at Motorola Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
