    PVH vs Ralph Lauren

    PVH PVH · Ralph Lauren RL · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    PVH reported revenue of $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Ralph Lauren's $8.1 billion in fiscal 2026. PVH's revenue grew 3.4% in its latest year, and Ralph Lauren's grew 14.6%. PVH had an operating margin of 2.6%, against 14.5% at Ralph Lauren. Free cash flow was $538 million at PVH and $746 million at Ralph Lauren. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
