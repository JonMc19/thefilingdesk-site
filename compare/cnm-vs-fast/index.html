    Core & Main vs Fastenal

    Core & Main CNM · Fastenal FAST · Trading Companies & Distributors

    Fastenal reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Core & Main's $7.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Core & Main's revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Fastenal's grew 8.7%. Core & Main had an operating margin of 9.4%, against 20.2% at Fastenal. Free cash flow was $604 million at Core & Main and $1.1 billion at Fastenal. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
