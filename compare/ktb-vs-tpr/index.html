    Kontoor Brands vs Tapestry

    Kontoor Brands KTB · Tapestry TPR · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Tapestry reported revenue of $8.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.5 times Kontoor Brands' $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Kontoor Brands' revenue grew 20.9% in its latest year, and Tapestry's grew 14.2%. Kontoor Brands had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 23.9% at Tapestry. Free cash flow was $435 million at Kontoor Brands and $1.8 billion at Tapestry. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
