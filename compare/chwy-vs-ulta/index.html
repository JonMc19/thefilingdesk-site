    Chewy vs Ulta Beauty

    Chewy CHWY · Ulta Beauty ULTA · Other Specialty Retail

    Chewy reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Ulta Beauty's $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Chewy's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and Ulta Beauty's grew 9.7%. Chewy had an operating margin of 2.0%, against 12.4% at Ulta Beauty. Free cash flow was $562 million at Chewy and $1.1 billion at Ulta Beauty. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
