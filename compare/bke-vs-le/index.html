    Buckle vs Lands' End

    Buckle BKE · Lands' End LE · Apparel Retail

    Buckle reported revenue of $1.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Lands' End's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Buckle's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Lands' End's grew 0.7%. Buckle had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 3.8% at Lands' End. Free cash flow was $206 million at Buckle and $20 million at Lands' End. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
