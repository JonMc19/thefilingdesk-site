    Chewy vs Tractor Supply

    Chewy CHWY · Tractor Supply TSCO · Other Specialty Retail

    Tractor Supply reported revenue of $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Chewy's $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Chewy's revenue grew 6.2% in its latest year, and Tractor Supply's grew 4.3%. Chewy had an operating margin of 2.0%, against 9.4% at Tractor Supply. Free cash flow was $562 million at Chewy and $740 million at Tractor Supply. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
