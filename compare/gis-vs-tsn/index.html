    General Mills vs Tyson Foods

    General Mills GIS · Tyson Foods TSN · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Tyson Foods reported revenue of $54.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.0 times General Mills' $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and Tyson Foods' grew 2.1%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 2.0% at Tyson Foods. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $1.2 billion at Tyson Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
