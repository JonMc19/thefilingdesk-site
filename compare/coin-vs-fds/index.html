    Coinbase vs FactSet

    Coinbase COIN · FactSet FDS · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Coinbase reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.1 times FactSet's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Coinbase's revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and FactSet's grew 5.4%. Coinbase had an operating margin of 20.0%, against 32.2% at FactSet. Free cash flow was $2.3 billion at Coinbase and $617 million at FactSet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
