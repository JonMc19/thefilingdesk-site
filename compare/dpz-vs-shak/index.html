    Domino's vs Shake Shack

    Domino's DPZ · Shake Shack SHAK · Restaurants

    Domino's reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.4 times Shake Shack's $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Domino's revenue grew 5.0% in its latest year, and Shake Shack's grew 15.4%. Domino's had an operating margin of 19.3%, against 4.3% at Shake Shack. Free cash flow was $672 million at Domino's and $57 million at Shake Shack. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
