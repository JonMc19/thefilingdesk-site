    Parker Hannifin vs Stanley Black & Decker

    Parker Hannifin PH · Stanley Black & Decker SWK · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Parker Hannifin reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times Stanley Black & Decker's $15.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
