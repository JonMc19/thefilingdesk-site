    Albertsons vs Kroger

    Albertsons ACI · Kroger KR · Food Retail

    Kroger reported revenue of $147.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Albertsons' $83.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Albertsons' revenue grew 3.5% in its latest year, and Kroger's grew 0.3%. Albertsons had an operating margin of 0.9%, against 1.3% at Kroger. Free cash flow was $527 million at Albertsons and $3.5 billion at Kroger. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 28 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
