    Abbott Laboratories vs Medtronic

    Abbott Laboratories ABT · Medtronic MDT · Health Care Equipment

    Abbott Laboratories reported revenue of $44.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Medtronic's $36.4 billion in fiscal 2026. Abbott Laboratories' revenue grew 5.7% in its latest year, and Medtronic's grew 8.4%. Abbott Laboratories had an operating margin of 18.2%, against 17.8% at Medtronic. Free cash flow was $7.4 billion at Abbott Laboratories and $5.4 billion at Medtronic. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and April 24), so the periods don't line up exactly.
