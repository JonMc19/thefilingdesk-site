    Chipotle Mexican Grill vs McDonald's

    Chipotle Mexican Grill CMG · McDonald's MCD · Restaurants

    McDonald's reported revenue of $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.3 times Chipotle Mexican Grill's $11.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Chipotle Mexican Grill's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and McDonald's grew 3.7%. Chipotle Mexican Grill had an operating margin of 16.2%, against 46.1% at McDonald's. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Chipotle Mexican Grill and $7.2 billion at McDonald's.
