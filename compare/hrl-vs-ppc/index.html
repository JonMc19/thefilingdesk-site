    Hormel Foods vs Pilgrim's Pride

    Hormel Foods HRL · Pilgrim's Pride PPC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Pilgrim's Pride reported revenue of $18.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Hormel Foods' $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Hormel Foods' revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Pilgrim's Pride's grew 3.5%. Hormel Foods had an operating margin of 5.9%, against 8.7% at Pilgrim's Pride. Free cash flow was $534 million at Hormel Foods and $653 million at Pilgrim's Pride. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
