    Illumina vs Bio-Techne

    Illumina ILMN · Bio-Techne TECH · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    Illumina reported revenue of $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.6 times Bio-Techne's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Illumina's revenue fell 0.7% in its latest year, and Bio-Techne's fell 0.4%. Illumina had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 20.7% at Bio-Techne. Free cash flow was $931 million at Illumina and $263 million at Bio-Techne. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
