    SiriusXM vs Warner Bros. Discovery

    SiriusXM SIRI · Warner Bros. Discovery WBD · Broadcasting

    Warner Bros. Discovery reported revenue of $37.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.4 times SiriusXM's $8.6 billion in fiscal 2025. SiriusXM's revenue fell 1.6% in its latest year, and Warner Bros. Discovery's fell 5.2%. SiriusXM had an operating margin of 17.2%, against 2.0% at Warner Bros. Discovery. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at SiriusXM and $3.1 billion at Warner Bros. Discovery.
