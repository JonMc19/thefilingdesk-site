    Cheesecake Factory vs Yum! Brands

    Cheesecake Factory CAKE · Yum! Brands YUM · Restaurants

    Yum! Brands reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Cheesecake Factory's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Cheesecake Factory's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Yum! Brands' grew 8.8%. Cheesecake Factory had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 31.3% at Yum! Brands. Free cash flow was $155 million at Cheesecake Factory and $1.6 billion at Yum! Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
