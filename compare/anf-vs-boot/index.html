    Abercrombie & Fitch vs Boot Barn

    Abercrombie & Fitch ANF · Boot Barn BOOT · Apparel Retail

    Abercrombie & Fitch reported revenue of $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.3 times Boot Barn's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Abercrombie & Fitch's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Boot Barn's grew 17.9%. Abercrombie & Fitch had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 13.3% at Boot Barn. Free cash flow was $378 million at Abercrombie & Fitch and $126 million at Boot Barn. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
