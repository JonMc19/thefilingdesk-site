    Arista Networks vs F5

    Arista Networks ANET · F5 FFIV · Communications Equipment

    Arista Networks reported revenue of $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.9 times F5's $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Arista Networks' revenue grew 28.6% in its latest year, and F5's grew 9.7%. Arista Networks had an operating margin of 42.8%, against 24.8% at F5. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Arista Networks and $906 million at F5. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
