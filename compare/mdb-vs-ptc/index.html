    MongoDB vs PTC

    MongoDB MDB · PTC PTC · Application Software

    PTC reported revenue of $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as MongoDB's $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026. MongoDB's revenue grew 22.8% in its latest year, and PTC's grew 19.2%. MongoDB had an operating margin of -5.6%, against 35.9% at PTC. Free cash flow was $500 million at MongoDB and $857 million at PTC. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
