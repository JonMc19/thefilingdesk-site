    Costco vs Dollar Tree

    Costco COST · Dollar Tree DLTR · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Costco reported revenue of $303.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 15.6 times Dollar Tree's $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Costco's revenue grew 10.1% in its latest year, and Dollar Tree's grew 10.4%. Costco had an operating margin of 3.9%, against 8.5% at Dollar Tree. Free cash flow was $9.4 billion at Costco and $1.1 billion at Dollar Tree. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
