    JBT Marel vs Valmont Industries

    JBT Marel JBTM · Valmont Industries VMI · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Valmont Industries reported revenue of $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as JBT Marel's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. JBT Marel's revenue grew 121.3% in its latest year, and Valmont Industries' grew 0.7%. JBT Marel had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 10.1% at Valmont Industries. Free cash flow was $238 million at JBT Marel and $311 million at Valmont Industries. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
