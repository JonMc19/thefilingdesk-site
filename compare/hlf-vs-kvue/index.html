    Herbalife vs Kenvue

    Herbalife HLF · Kenvue KVUE · Personal Care Products

    Kenvue reported revenue of $15.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.0 times Herbalife's $5.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Herbalife's revenue grew 0.9% in its latest year, and Kenvue's fell 2.1%. Herbalife had an operating margin of 9.6%, against 16.0% at Kenvue. Free cash flow was $253 million at Herbalife and $1.7 billion at Kenvue. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
