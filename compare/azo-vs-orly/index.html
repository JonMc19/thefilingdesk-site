    AutoZone vs O'Reilly Automotive

    AutoZone AZO · O'Reilly Automotive ORLY · Automotive Retail

    AutoZone reported revenue of $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as O'Reilly Automotive's $17.8 billion in fiscal 2025. AutoZone's revenue grew 2.4% in its latest year, and O'Reilly Automotive's grew 6.4%. AutoZone had an operating margin of 19.1%, against 19.5% at O'Reilly Automotive. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at AutoZone and $1.6 billion at O'Reilly Automotive. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
