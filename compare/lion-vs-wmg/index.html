    Lionsgate Studios vs Warner Music Group

    Lionsgate Studios LION · Warner Music Group WMG · Movies & Entertainment

    Warner Music Group reported revenue of $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times Lionsgate Studios' $2.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Lionsgate Studios' revenue grew 1.8% in its latest year, and Warner Music Group's grew 4.4%. Lionsgate Studios had an operating margin of 3.7%, against 10.3% at Warner Music Group. Free cash flow was $11 million at Lionsgate Studios and $539 million at Warner Music Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
