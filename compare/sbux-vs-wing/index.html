    Starbucks vs Wingstop

    Starbucks SBUX · Wingstop WING · Restaurants

    Starbucks reported revenue of $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 53.4 times Wingstop's $697 million in fiscal 2025. Starbucks' revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Wingstop's grew 11.3%. Starbucks had an operating margin of 7.9%, against 25.7% at Wingstop. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Starbucks and $106 million at Wingstop. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 28 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
