    Arm Holdings vs ON Semiconductor

    Arm Holdings ARM · ON Semiconductor ON · Semiconductors

    ON Semiconductor reported revenue of $6.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Arm Holdings' $4.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Arm Holdings' revenue grew 22.8% in its latest year, and ON Semiconductor's fell 15.3%. Arm Holdings had an operating margin of 18.3%, against 1.4% at ON Semiconductor. Free cash flow was $979 million at Arm Holdings and $1.4 billion at ON Semiconductor. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
