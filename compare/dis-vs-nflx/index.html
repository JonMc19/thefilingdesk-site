    Disney vs Netflix

    Disney DIS · Netflix NFLX · Movies & Entertainment

    Disney reported revenue of $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Netflix's $45.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Disney's revenue grew 3.4% in its latest year, and Netflix's grew 15.8%. Disney had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 29.5% at Netflix. Free cash flow was $10.1 billion at Disney and $9.5 billion at Netflix. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
