    Teledyne Technologies vs Zebra Technologies

    Teledyne Technologies TDY · Zebra Technologies ZBRA · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Teledyne Technologies reported revenue of $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Zebra Technologies' $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Teledyne Technologies' revenue grew 7.9% in its latest year, and Zebra Technologies' grew 8.3%. Teledyne Technologies had an operating margin of 18.8%, against 13.0% at Zebra Technologies. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Teledyne Technologies and $831 million at Zebra Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
