    Apogee Enterprises vs Armstrong World Industries

    Apogee Enterprises APOG · Armstrong World Industries AWI · Building Products

Free cash flow was $95 million at Apogee Enterprises and $246 million at Armstrong World Industries. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
