    Nordson vs SPX Technologies

    Nordson NDSN · SPX Technologies SPXC · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Nordson reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times SPX Technologies' $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Nordson's revenue grew 3.8% in its latest year, and SPX Technologies' grew 14.2%. Nordson had an operating margin of 25.5%, against 15.5% at SPX Technologies. Free cash flow was $661 million at Nordson and $241 million at SPX Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
