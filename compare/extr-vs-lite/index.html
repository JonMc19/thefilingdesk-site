    Extreme Networks vs Lumentum

    Extreme Networks EXTR · Lumentum LITE · Communications Equipment

    Lumentum reported revenue of $3.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.3 times Extreme Networks' $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Extreme Networks' revenue grew 12.6% in its latest year, and Lumentum's grew 83.2%. Extreme Networks had an operating margin of 4.9%, against 17.4% at Lumentum. Free cash flow was $95 million at Extreme Networks and $300 million at Lumentum. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
