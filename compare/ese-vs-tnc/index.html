    ESCO Technologies vs Tennant

    ESCO Technologies ESE · Tennant TNC · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Tennant reported revenue of $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as ESCO Technologies' $1.1 billion in fiscal 2025. ESCO Technologies' revenue grew 19.2% in its latest year, and Tennant's fell 6.5%. ESCO Technologies had an operating margin of 15.6%, against 5.7% at Tennant. Free cash flow was $206 million at ESCO Technologies and $43 million at Tennant. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
