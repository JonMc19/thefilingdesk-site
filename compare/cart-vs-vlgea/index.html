    Maplebear vs Village Super Market

    Maplebear CART · Village Super Market VLGEA · Food Retail

    Maplebear reported revenue of $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Village Super Market's $2.4 billion in fiscal 2026. Maplebear's revenue grew 10.8% in its latest year, and Village Super Market's grew 3.7%. Maplebear had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 2.7% at Village Super Market. Free cash flow was $910 million at Maplebear and $55 million at Village Super Market. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and July 25), so the periods don't line up exactly.
