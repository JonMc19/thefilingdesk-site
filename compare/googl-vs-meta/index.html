    Alphabet vs Meta Platforms

    Alphabet GOOGL · Meta Platforms META · Interactive Media & Services

    Alphabet reported revenue of $402.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Meta Platforms' $201.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Alphabet's revenue grew 15.1% in its latest year, and Meta Platforms' grew 22.2%. Alphabet had an operating margin of 32.0%, against 41.4% at Meta Platforms. Free cash flow was $73.3 billion at Alphabet and $46.1 billion at Meta Platforms.
