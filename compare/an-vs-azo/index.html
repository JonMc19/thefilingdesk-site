    AutoNation vs AutoZone

    AutoNation AN · AutoZone AZO · Automotive Retail

    AutoNation reported revenue of $27.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times AutoZone's $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025. AutoNation's revenue grew 3.2% in its latest year, and AutoZone's grew 2.4%. AutoNation had an operating margin of 4.5%, against 19.1% at AutoZone. Free cash flow was −$198 million at AutoNation and $1.8 billion at AutoZone. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
