    Colgate-Palmolive vs Energizer

    Colgate-Palmolive CL · Energizer ENR · Household Products

    Colgate-Palmolive reported revenue of $20.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 6.9 times Energizer's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Colgate-Palmolive's revenue grew 1.4% in its latest year, and Energizer's grew 2.3%. Free cash flow was $3.6 billion at Colgate-Palmolive and $63 million at Energizer. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
