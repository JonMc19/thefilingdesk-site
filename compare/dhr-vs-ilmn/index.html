    Danaher vs Illumina

    Danaher DHR · Illumina ILMN · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    Danaher reported revenue of $24.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.7 times Illumina's $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Danaher's revenue grew 2.9% in its latest year, and Illumina's fell 0.7%. Danaher had an operating margin of 19.1%, against 18.6% at Illumina. Free cash flow was $5.3 billion at Danaher and $931 million at Illumina. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
