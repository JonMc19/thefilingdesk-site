    Marsh McLennan vs Willis Towers Watson

    Marsh McLennan MRSH · Willis Towers Watson WTW · Insurance Brokers

    Marsh McLennan reported revenue of $27.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.8 times Willis Towers Watson's $9.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Marsh McLennan's revenue grew 10.3% in its latest year, and Willis Towers Watson's fell 2.2%. Marsh McLennan had an operating margin of 23.1%, against 23.0% at Willis Towers Watson. Free cash flow was $5.0 billion at Marsh McLennan and $1.5 billion at Willis Towers Watson.
