    Amer Sports vs Levi Strauss

    Amer Sports AS · Levi Strauss LEVI · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Amer Sports reported revenue of $6.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Levi Strauss' $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Amer Sports' revenue grew 26.7% in its latest year, and Levi Strauss' grew 4.1%. Amer Sports had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 10.8% at Levi Strauss. Free cash flow was $513 million at Amer Sports and $308 million at Levi Strauss. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
