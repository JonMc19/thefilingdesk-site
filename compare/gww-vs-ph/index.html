    W. W. Grainger vs Parker Hannifin

    W. W. Grainger GWW · Parker Hannifin PH · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Parker Hannifin reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times W. W. Grainger's $17.9 billion in fiscal 2025. W. W. Grainger's revenue grew 4.5% in its latest year, and Parker Hannifin's grew 8.3%. W. W. Grainger had an operating margin of 13.9%, against 23.6% at Parker Hannifin.
