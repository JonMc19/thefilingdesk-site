    Dexcom vs Edwards Lifesciences

    Dexcom DXCM · Edwards Lifesciences EW · Health Care Equipment

    Edwards Lifesciences reported revenue of $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Dexcom's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Dexcom's revenue grew 15.6% in its latest year, and Edwards Lifesciences' grew 11.6%. Dexcom had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 20.8% at Edwards Lifesciences. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Dexcom and $1.3 billion at Edwards Lifesciences.
