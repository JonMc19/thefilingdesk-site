    FormFactor vs Photronics

    FormFactor FORM · Photronics PLAB · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Photronics reported revenue of $849 million in fiscal 2025, about the same as FormFactor's $785 million in fiscal 2025. FormFactor's revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Photronics' fell 2.0%. FormFactor had an operating margin of 7.3%, against 24.5% at Photronics. Free cash flow was $12 million at FormFactor and $60 million at Photronics. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
