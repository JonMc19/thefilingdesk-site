    Graham vs Stride

    Graham GHC · Stride LRN · Education Services

    Graham reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Stride's $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026. Graham's revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and Stride's grew 4.7%. Graham had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 17.9% at Stride. Free cash flow was $275 million at Graham and $433 million at Stride. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
