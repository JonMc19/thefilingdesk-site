    McDonald's vs Starbucks

    McDonald's MCD · Starbucks SBUX · Restaurants

    Starbucks reported revenue of $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times McDonald's $26.9 billion in fiscal 2025. McDonald's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Starbucks' grew 2.8%. McDonald's had an operating margin of 46.1%, against 7.9% at Starbucks. Free cash flow was $7.2 billion at McDonald's and $2.4 billion at Starbucks. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
