    Cencora vs McKesson

    Cencora COR · McKesson MCK · Health Care Distributors

    McKesson reported revenue of $403.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Cencora's $321.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Cencora's revenue grew 9.3% in its latest year, and McKesson's grew 12.4%. Cencora had an operating margin of 0.8%, against 1.5% at McKesson. Free cash flow was $3.2 billion at Cencora and $5.7 billion at McKesson. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
