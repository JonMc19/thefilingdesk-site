    ACI Worldwide vs Samsara

    ACI Worldwide ACIW · Samsara IOT · Application Software

    ACI Worldwide reported revenue of $1.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Samsara's $1.6 billion in fiscal 2026. ACI Worldwide's revenue grew 10.4% in its latest year, and Samsara's grew 29.6%. ACI Worldwide had an operating margin of 18.8%, against -3.2% at Samsara. Free cash flow was $310 million at ACI Worldwide and $207 million at Samsara. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
