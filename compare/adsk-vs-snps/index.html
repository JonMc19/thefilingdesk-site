    Autodesk vs Synopsys

    Autodesk ADSK · Synopsys SNPS · Application Software

    Autodesk reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Synopsys' $7.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Autodesk's revenue grew 17.5% in its latest year, and Synopsys' grew 15.1%. Autodesk had an operating margin of 21.9%, against 13.0% at Synopsys. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Autodesk and $1.3 billion at Synopsys. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
