    Intercontinental Exchange vs Moody's

    Intercontinental Exchange ICE · Moody's MCO · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Intercontinental Exchange reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Moody's $7.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Intercontinental Exchange's revenue grew 7.5% in its latest year, and Moody's grew 8.9%. Intercontinental Exchange had an operating margin of 39.0%, against 43.4% at Moody's. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Intercontinental Exchange and $2.6 billion at Moody's.
