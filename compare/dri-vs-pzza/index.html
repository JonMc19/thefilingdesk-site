    Darden Restaurants vs Papa John's

    Darden Restaurants DRI · Papa John's PZZA · Restaurants

    Darden Restaurants reported revenue of $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 6.4 times Papa John's $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Darden Restaurants' revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and Papa John's fell 0.3%. Darden Restaurants had an operating margin of 12.0%, against 4.3% at Papa John's. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants and $61 million at Papa John's. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
