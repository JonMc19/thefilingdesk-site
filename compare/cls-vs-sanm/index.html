    Celestica vs Sanmina

    Celestica CLS · Sanmina SANM · Electronic Manufacturing Services

    Celestica reported revenue of $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Sanmina's $8.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Celestica's revenue grew 28.5% in its latest year, and Sanmina's grew 7.4%. Celestica had an operating margin of 8.4%, against 4.4% at Sanmina. Free cash flow was $458 million at Celestica and $473 million at Sanmina. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
