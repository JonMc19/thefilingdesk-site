    American Eagle Outfitters vs Urban Outfitters

    American Eagle Outfitters AEO · Urban Outfitters URBN · Apparel Retail

    Urban Outfitters reported revenue of $6.2 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as American Eagle Outfitters' $5.5 billion in fiscal 2025. American Eagle Outfitters' revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Urban Outfitters' grew 11.1%. American Eagle Outfitters had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 9.8% at Urban Outfitters. Free cash flow was $195 million at American Eagle Outfitters and $315 million at Urban Outfitters.
