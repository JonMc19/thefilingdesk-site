    Belden vs Littelfuse

    Belden BDC · Littelfuse LFUS · Electronic Components

    Belden reported revenue of $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Littelfuse's $2.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Belden's revenue grew 10.3% in its latest year, and Littelfuse's grew 8.9%. Belden had an operating margin of 11.6%, against 1.6% at Littelfuse. Free cash flow was $219 million at Belden and $366 million at Littelfuse. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
