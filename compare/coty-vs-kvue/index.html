    Coty vs Kenvue

    Coty COTY · Kenvue KVUE · Personal Care Products

    Kenvue reported revenue of $15.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.6 times Coty's $5.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Coty's revenue fell 1.5% in its latest year, and Kenvue's fell 2.1%. Coty had an operating margin of -1.4%, against 16.0% at Kenvue. Free cash flow was $348 million at Coty and $1.7 billion at Kenvue. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
