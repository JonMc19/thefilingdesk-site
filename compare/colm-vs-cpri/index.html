    Columbia Sportswear vs Capri Holdings

    Columbia Sportswear COLM · Capri Holdings CPRI · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Capri Holdings reported revenue of $3.5 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Columbia Sportswear's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Columbia Sportswear's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Capri Holdings' fell 4.1%. Columbia Sportswear had an operating margin of 6.1%, against 0.7% at Capri Holdings. Free cash flow was $217 million at Columbia Sportswear and $14 million at Capri Holdings. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
