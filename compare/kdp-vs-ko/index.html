    Keurig Dr Pepper vs Coca-Cola

    Keurig Dr Pepper KDP · Coca-Cola KO · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

Free cash flow was $1.5 billion at Keurig Dr Pepper and $5.3 billion at Coca-Cola.
