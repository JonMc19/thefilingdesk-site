    Levi Strauss vs Under Armour

    Levi Strauss LEVI · Under Armour UA · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Levi Strauss reported revenue of $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Under Armour's $5.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Levi Strauss' revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Under Armour's fell 3.8%. Levi Strauss had an operating margin of 10.8%, against -3.3% at Under Armour. Free cash flow was $308 million at Levi Strauss and −$162 million at Under Armour. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
