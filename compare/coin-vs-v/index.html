    Coinbase vs Visa

    Coinbase COIN · Visa V · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Visa reported revenue of $40.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.6 times Coinbase's $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Coinbase's revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and Visa's grew 11.3%. Coinbase had an operating margin of 20.0%, against 60.0% at Visa. Free cash flow was $2.3 billion at Coinbase and $21.6 billion at Visa. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
