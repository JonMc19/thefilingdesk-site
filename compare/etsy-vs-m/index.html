    Etsy vs Macy's

    Etsy ETSY · Macy's M · Broadline Retail

    Macy's reported revenue of $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 7.8 times Etsy's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Etsy's revenue grew 2.7% in its latest year, and Macy's fell 1.7%. Etsy had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 4.5% at Macy's. Free cash flow was $678 million at Etsy and $1.1 billion at Macy's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
