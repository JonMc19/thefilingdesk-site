    Trimble vs Tyler Technologies

    Trimble TRMB · Tyler Technologies TYL · Application Software

    Trimble reported revenue of $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Tyler Technologies' $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Trimble's revenue fell 2.6% in its latest year, and Tyler Technologies' grew 9.1%. Trimble had an operating margin of 16.5%, against 15.3% at Tyler Technologies. Free cash flow was $361 million at Trimble and $638 million at Tyler Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
