    Domino's vs Darden Restaurants

    Domino's DPZ · Darden Restaurants DRI · Restaurants

    Darden Restaurants reported revenue of $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.7 times Domino's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Domino's revenue grew 5.0% in its latest year, and Darden Restaurants' grew 9.4%. Domino's had an operating margin of 19.3%, against 12.0% at Darden Restaurants. Free cash flow was $672 million at Domino's and $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
