    Domino's vs Wingstop

    Domino's DPZ · Wingstop WING · Restaurants

    Domino's reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 7.1 times Wingstop's $697 million in fiscal 2025. Domino's revenue grew 5.0% in its latest year, and Wingstop's grew 11.3%. Domino's had an operating margin of 19.3%, against 25.7% at Wingstop. Free cash flow was $672 million at Domino's and $106 million at Wingstop. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
