    HP vs Sandisk

    HP HPQ · Sandisk SNDK · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    HP reported revenue of $55.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.7 times Sandisk's $20.2 billion in fiscal 2026. HP's revenue grew 3.2% in its latest year, and Sandisk's grew 175.3%. HP had an operating margin of 5.7%, against 61.2% at Sandisk. Free cash flow was $2.8 billion at HP and $11.5 billion at Sandisk. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and July 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
