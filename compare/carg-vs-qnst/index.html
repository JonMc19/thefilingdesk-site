    CarGurus vs QuinStreet

    CarGurus CARG · QuinStreet QNST · Interactive Media & Services

    QuinStreet reported revenue of $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times CarGurus' $907 million in fiscal 2025. CarGurus' revenue grew 13.7% in its latest year, and QuinStreet's grew 18.3%. CarGurus had an operating margin of 26.9%, against 2.7% at QuinStreet. Free cash flow was $289 million at CarGurus and $128 million at QuinStreet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
