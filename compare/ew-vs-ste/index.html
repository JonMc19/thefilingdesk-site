    Edwards Lifesciences vs Steris

    Edwards Lifesciences EW · Steris STE · Health Care Equipment

    Edwards Lifesciences reported revenue of $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Steris' $5.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Edwards Lifesciences' revenue grew 11.6% in its latest year, and Steris' grew 8.7%. Edwards Lifesciences had an operating margin of 20.8%, against 18.6% at Steris. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Edwards Lifesciences and $972 million at Steris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
