    Knowles vs Littelfuse

    Knowles KN · Littelfuse LFUS · Electronic Components

Knowles had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 1.6% at Littelfuse. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
