    Mondelez International vs McCormick

    Mondelez International MDLZ · McCormick MKC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Mondelez International reported revenue of $38.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.6 times McCormick's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Mondelez International's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and McCormick's grew 1.7%. Mondelez International had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 15.7% at McCormick. Free cash flow was $3.2 billion at Mondelez International and $740 million at McCormick. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
