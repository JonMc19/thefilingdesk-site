    Moody's vs Nasdaq

    Moody's MCO · Nasdaq NDAQ · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Nasdaq reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Moody's $7.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Moody's revenue grew 8.9% in its latest year, and Nasdaq's grew 11.7%. Moody's had an operating margin of 43.4%, against 28.2% at Nasdaq. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Moody's and $2.0 billion at Nasdaq.
