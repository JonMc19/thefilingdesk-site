    Cadence Design Systems vs OpenText

    Cadence Design Systems CDNS · OpenText OTEX · Application Software

    Cadence Design Systems reported revenue of $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as OpenText's $5.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Cadence Design Systems' revenue grew 14.1% in its latest year, and OpenText's grew 1.5%. Cadence Design Systems had an operating margin of 28.2%, against 20.6% at OpenText. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at Cadence Design Systems and $808 million at OpenText. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
