    Illumina vs Waters

    Illumina ILMN · Waters WAT · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    Illumina reported revenue of $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Waters' $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Illumina's revenue fell 0.7% in its latest year, and Waters' grew 7.0%. Illumina had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 25.4% at Waters. Free cash flow was $931 million at Illumina and $540 million at Waters. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
