    Levi Strauss vs Lululemon Athletica

    Levi Strauss LEVI · Lululemon Athletica LULU · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Lululemon Athletica reported revenue of $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Levi Strauss' $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Levi Strauss' revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Lululemon Athletica's grew 4.9%. Levi Strauss had an operating margin of 10.8%, against 19.9% at Lululemon Athletica. Free cash flow was $308 million at Levi Strauss and $922 million at Lululemon Athletica. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and February 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
