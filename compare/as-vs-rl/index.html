    Amer Sports vs Ralph Lauren

    Amer Sports AS · Ralph Lauren RL · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Ralph Lauren reported revenue of $8.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Amer Sports' $6.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Amer Sports' revenue grew 26.7% in its latest year, and Ralph Lauren's grew 14.6%. Amer Sports had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 14.5% at Ralph Lauren. Free cash flow was $513 million at Amer Sports and $746 million at Ralph Lauren. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
