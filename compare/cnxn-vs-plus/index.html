    PC Connection vs ePlus

    PC Connection CNXN · ePlus PLUS · Technology Distributors

    PC Connection reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times ePlus' $2.4 billion in fiscal 2026. PC Connection's revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and ePlus' grew 22.1%. PC Connection had an operating margin of 3.5%, against 6.8% at ePlus. Free cash flow was $58 million at PC Connection and −$121 million at ePlus. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
