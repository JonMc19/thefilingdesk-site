    NetApp vs Seagate Technology

    NetApp NTAP · Seagate Technology STX · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Seagate Technology reported revenue of $12.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.8 times NetApp's $6.9 billion in fiscal 2026. NetApp's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Seagate Technology's grew 34.1%. NetApp had an operating margin of 24.2%, against 33.6% at Seagate Technology. Free cash flow was $1.9 billion at NetApp and $3.1 billion at Seagate Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 24 and July 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
