    Accenture vs DXC Technology

    Accenture ACN · DXC Technology DXC · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Accenture reported revenue of $69.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.5 times DXC Technology's $12.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Accenture's revenue grew 7.4% in its latest year, and DXC Technology's fell 1.8%. Accenture had an operating margin of 14.7%, against 7.7% at DXC Technology. Free cash flow was $10.9 billion at Accenture and $1.0 billion at DXC Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
