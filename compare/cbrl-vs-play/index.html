    Cracker Barrel vs Dave & Buster's

    Cracker Barrel CBRL · Dave & Buster's PLAY · Restaurants

    Cracker Barrel reported revenue of $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times Dave & Buster's $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Cracker Barrel's revenue fell 4.7% in its latest year, and Dave & Buster's fell 1.4%. Cracker Barrel had an operating margin of -0.4%, against 4.1% at Dave & Buster's. Free cash flow was $88 million at Cracker Barrel and −$101 million at Dave & Buster's. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and February 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
