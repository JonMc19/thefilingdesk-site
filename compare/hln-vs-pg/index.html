    Haleon vs Procter & Gamble

    Haleon HLN · Procter & Gamble PG · Personal Care Products

    Haleon reported revenue of £11.0 billion in fiscal 2025, and Procter & Gamble $87.0 billion in fiscal 2026, in different currencies. Haleon's revenue fell 1.8% in its latest year, and Procter & Gamble's grew 3.3%. Haleon had an operating margin of 21.9%, against 22.7% at Procter & Gamble. Free cash flow was £2.3 billion at Haleon and $15.1 billion at Procter & Gamble. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
