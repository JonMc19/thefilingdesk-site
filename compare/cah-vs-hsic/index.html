    Cardinal Health vs Henry Schein

    Cardinal Health CAH · Henry Schein HSIC · Health Care Distributors

Cardinal Health's revenue grew 14.2% in its latest year, and Henry Schein's grew 4.0%. Cardinal Health had an operating margin of 1.0%, against 5.0% at Henry Schein. Free cash flow was $4.5 billion at Cardinal Health and $573 million at Henry Schein. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
