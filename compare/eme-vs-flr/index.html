    Emcor vs Fluor

    Emcor EME · Fluor FLR · Construction & Engineering

    Emcor reported revenue of $17.0 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Fluor's $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Emcor's revenue grew 16.6% in its latest year, and Fluor's fell 5.0%. Emcor had an operating margin of 10.1%, against -2.4% at Fluor. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Emcor and −$437 million at Fluor.
