    Allegion vs UFP Industries

    Allegion ALLE · UFP Industries UFPI · Building Products

    UFP Industries reported revenue of $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Allegion's $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Allegion's revenue grew 7.8% in its latest year, and UFP Industries' fell 5.0%. Allegion had an operating margin of 21.1%, against 5.8% at UFP Industries. Free cash flow was $686 million at Allegion and $276 million at UFP Industries. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
