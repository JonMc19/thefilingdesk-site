    Dolby vs Guidewire Software

    Dolby DLB · Guidewire Software GWRE · Application Software

    Guidewire Software reported revenue of $1.5 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Dolby's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Dolby's revenue grew 5.9% in its latest year, and Guidewire Software's grew 22.7%. Dolby had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 10.2% at Guidewire Software. Free cash flow was $436 million at Dolby and $378 million at Guidewire Software. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 26 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
