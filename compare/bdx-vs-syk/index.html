    Becton Dickinson vs Stryker

    Becton Dickinson BDX · Stryker SYK · Health Care Equipment

    Stryker reported revenue of $25.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.1 times Becton Dickinson's $21.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Becton Dickinson's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and Stryker's grew 11.2%. Becton Dickinson had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 19.5% at Stryker. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at Becton Dickinson and $4.3 billion at Stryker. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
