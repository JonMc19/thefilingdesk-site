    Campbell's vs McCormick

    Campbell's CPB · McCormick MKC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Campbell's reported revenue of $9.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times McCormick's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Campbell's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and McCormick's grew 1.7%. Campbell's had an operating margin of 8.7%, against 15.7% at McCormick. Free cash flow was $678 million at Campbell's and $740 million at McCormick. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 2 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
