    Sysco vs US Foods

    Sysco SYY · US Foods USFD · Food Distributors

    Sysco reported revenue of $84.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.1 times US Foods' $39.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Sysco's revenue grew 3.9% in its latest year, and US Foods' grew 4.1%. Sysco had an operating margin of 3.7%, against 3.0% at US Foods. Free cash flow was $1.9 billion at Sysco and $959 million at US Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 27 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
