    Dollar General vs PriceSmart

    Dollar General DG · PriceSmart PSMT · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Dollar General and $103 million at PriceSmart. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 30 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
