    General Mills vs McCormick

    General Mills GIS · McCormick MKC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    General Mills reported revenue of $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.7 times McCormick's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and McCormick's grew 1.7%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 15.7% at McCormick. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $740 million at McCormick. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and November 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
