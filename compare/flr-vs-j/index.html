    Fluor vs Jacobs Solutions

    Fluor FLR · Jacobs Solutions J · Construction & Engineering

Fluor had an operating margin of -2.4%, against 7.2% at Jacobs Solutions. Free cash flow was −$437 million at Fluor and $607 million at Jacobs Solutions.
