    Cadence Design Systems vs Zoom Communications

    Cadence Design Systems CDNS · Zoom Communications ZM · Application Software

    Cadence Design Systems reported revenue of $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Zoom Communications' $4.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Cadence Design Systems' revenue grew 14.1% in its latest year, and Zoom Communications' grew 4.4%. Cadence Design Systems had an operating margin of 28.2%, against 23.1% at Zoom Communications. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at Cadence Design Systems and $1.9 billion at Zoom Communications. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
