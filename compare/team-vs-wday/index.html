    Atlassian vs Workday

    Atlassian TEAM · Workday WDAY · Application Software

    Workday reported revenue of $9.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Atlassian's $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Atlassian's revenue grew 26.0% in its latest year, and Workday's grew 13.1%. Atlassian had an operating margin of 0.2%, against 7.5% at Workday. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Atlassian and $2.8 billion at Workday. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
