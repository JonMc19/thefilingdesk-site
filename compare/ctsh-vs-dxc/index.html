    Cognizant vs DXC Technology

    Cognizant CTSH · DXC Technology DXC · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Cognizant reported revenue of $21.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times DXC Technology's $12.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Cognizant's revenue grew 7.0% in its latest year, and DXC Technology's fell 1.8%. Cognizant had an operating margin of 16.1%, against 7.7% at DXC Technology. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Cognizant and $1.0 billion at DXC Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
