    Brown–Forman vs Constellation Brands

    Brown–Forman BF-B · Constellation Brands STZ · Distillers & Vintners

    Constellation Brands reported revenue of $9.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.3 times Brown–Forman's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Free cash flow was $893 million at Brown–Forman and $1.8 billion at Constellation Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 30 and February 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
