    First Solar vs Marvell Technology

    First Solar FSLR · Marvell Technology MRVL · Semiconductors

    Marvell Technology reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times First Solar's $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025. First Solar's revenue grew 24.1% in its latest year, and Marvell Technology's grew 42.1%. First Solar had an operating margin of 30.6%, against 16.1% at Marvell Technology. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at First Solar and $1.4 billion at Marvell Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
