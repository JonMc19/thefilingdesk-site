    Amazon vs Macy's

    Amazon AMZN · Macy's M · Broadline Retail

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 31.7 times Macy's $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Macy's fell 1.7%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 4.5% at Macy's. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $1.1 billion at Macy's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
