    Intercontinental Exchange vs Nasdaq

    Intercontinental Exchange ICE · Nasdaq NDAQ · Financial Exchanges & Data

    Intercontinental Exchange reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Nasdaq's $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Intercontinental Exchange's revenue grew 7.5% in its latest year, and Nasdaq's grew 11.7%. Intercontinental Exchange had an operating margin of 39.0%, against 28.2% at Nasdaq. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Intercontinental Exchange and $2.0 billion at Nasdaq.
