    Albertsons vs Maplebear

    Albertsons ACI · Maplebear CART · Food Retail

    Albertsons reported revenue of $83.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 22.2 times Maplebear's $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Albertsons' revenue grew 3.5% in its latest year, and Maplebear's grew 10.8%. Albertsons had an operating margin of 0.9%, against 13.3% at Maplebear. Free cash flow was $527 million at Albertsons and $910 million at Maplebear. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
