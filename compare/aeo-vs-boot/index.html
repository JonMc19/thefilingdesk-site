    American Eagle Outfitters vs Boot Barn

    American Eagle Outfitters AEO · Boot Barn BOOT · Apparel Retail

    American Eagle Outfitters reported revenue of $5.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times Boot Barn's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2026. American Eagle Outfitters' revenue grew 4.1% in its latest year, and Boot Barn's grew 17.9%. American Eagle Outfitters had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 13.3% at Boot Barn. Free cash flow was $195 million at American Eagle Outfitters and $126 million at Boot Barn. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
