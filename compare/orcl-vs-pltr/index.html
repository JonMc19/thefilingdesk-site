    Oracle vs Palantir

    Oracle ORCL · Palantir PLTR · Application Software

    Oracle reported revenue of $67.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 15.1 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Oracle's revenue grew 17.4% in its latest year, and Palantir's grew 56.2%. Oracle had an operating margin of 30.6%, against 31.6% at Palantir. Free cash flow was −$23.7 billion at Oracle and $2.1 billion at Palantir. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
