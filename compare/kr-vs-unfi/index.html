    Kroger vs United Natural Foods

    Kroger KR · United Natural Foods UNFI · Food Retail

    Kroger reported revenue of $147.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.7 times United Natural Foods' $31.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Free cash flow was $3.5 billion at Kroger and $323 million at United Natural Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and August 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
