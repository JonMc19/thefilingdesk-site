    Abercrombie & Fitch vs Ross Stores

    Abercrombie & Fitch ANF · Ross Stores ROST · Apparel Retail

    Ross Stores reported revenue of $22.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.3 times Abercrombie & Fitch's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Abercrombie & Fitch's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Ross Stores' grew 7.7%. Abercrombie & Fitch had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 11.9% at Ross Stores. Free cash flow was $378 million at Abercrombie & Fitch and $2.2 billion at Ross Stores.
