    Dick's Sporting Goods vs Signet Jewelers

    Dick's Sporting Goods DKS · Signet Jewelers SIG · Specialty Stores

    Dick's Sporting Goods reported revenue of $17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times Signet Jewelers' $6.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Dick's Sporting Goods' revenue grew 28.1% in its latest year, and Signet Jewelers' grew 1.6%. Dick's Sporting Goods had an operating margin of 6.4%, against 5.8% at Signet Jewelers. Free cash flow was $400 million at Dick's Sporting Goods and $525 million at Signet Jewelers.
