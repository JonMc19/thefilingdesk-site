    ACM Research vs Photronics

    ACM Research ACMR · Photronics PLAB · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    ACM Research reported revenue of $901 million in fiscal 2025, about the same as Photronics' $849 million in fiscal 2025. ACM Research's revenue grew 15.2% in its latest year, and Photronics' fell 2.0%. ACM Research had an operating margin of 12.1%, against 24.5% at Photronics. Free cash flow was −$67 million at ACM Research and $60 million at Photronics. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
