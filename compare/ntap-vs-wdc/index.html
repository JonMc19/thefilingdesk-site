    NetApp vs Western Digital

    NetApp NTAP · Western Digital WDC · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Western Digital reported revenue of $12.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.9 times NetApp's $6.9 billion in fiscal 2026. NetApp's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Western Digital's grew 35.7%. NetApp had an operating margin of 24.2%, against 34.5% at Western Digital. Free cash flow was $1.9 billion at NetApp and $3.5 billion at Western Digital. Their fiscal years end in different months (April 24 and July 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
