    Amazon vs Alphabet

    Amazon AMZN · Alphabet GOOGL · Broadline Retail

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Alphabet's $402.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Alphabet's grew 15.1%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 32.0% at Alphabet. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $73.3 billion at Alphabet.
