    Franklin Electric vs RBC Bearings

    Franklin Electric FELE · RBC Bearings RBC · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Franklin Electric reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as RBC Bearings' $1.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Franklin Electric's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and RBC Bearings' grew 14.3%. Franklin Electric had an operating margin of 12.6%, against 22.5% at RBC Bearings. Free cash flow was $194 million at Franklin Electric and $343 million at RBC Bearings. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
