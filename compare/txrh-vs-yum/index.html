    Texas Roadhouse vs Yum! Brands

    Texas Roadhouse TXRH · Yum! Brands YUM · Restaurants

Brands' grew 8.8%. Texas Roadhouse had an operating margin of 8.1%, against 31.3% at Yum! Brands. Free cash flow was $342 million at Texas Roadhouse and $1.6 billion at Yum! Brands.
