    Snap-on vs Timken

    Snap-on SNA · Timken TKR · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Snap-on reported revenue of $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Timken's $4.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Snap-on's revenue grew 0.9% in its latest year, and Timken's grew 0.2%. Snap-on had an operating margin of 25.8%, against 11.8% at Timken. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Snap-on and $406 million at Timken. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
