    Broadcom vs Micron Technology

    Broadcom AVGO · Micron Technology MU · Semiconductors

    Micron Technology reported revenue of $133.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.1 times Broadcom's $63.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Broadcom's revenue grew 23.9% in its latest year, and Micron Technology's grew 256.3%. Broadcom had an operating margin of 39.9%, against 74.6% at Micron Technology. Free cash flow was $26.9 billion at Broadcom and $59.0 billion at Micron Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 2 and September 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
