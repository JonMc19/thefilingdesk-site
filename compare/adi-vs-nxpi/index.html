    Analog Devices vs NXP Semiconductors

    Analog Devices ADI · NXP Semiconductors NXPI · Semiconductors

    NXP Semiconductors reported revenue of $12.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Analog Devices' $11.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Analog Devices' revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and NXP Semiconductors' fell 2.7%. Analog Devices had an operating margin of 26.6%, against 24.8% at NXP Semiconductors. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Analog Devices and $2.4 billion at NXP Semiconductors. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
