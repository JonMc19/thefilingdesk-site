    OpenText vs Palantir

    OpenText OTEX · Palantir PLTR · Application Software

    OpenText reported revenue of $5.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. OpenText's revenue grew 1.5% in its latest year, and Palantir's grew 56.2%. OpenText had an operating margin of 20.6%, against 31.6% at Palantir. Free cash flow was $808 million at OpenText and $2.1 billion at Palantir. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
