    Coca-Cola Consolidated vs National Beverage

    Coca-Cola Consolidated COKE · National Beverage FIZZ · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    Coca-Cola Consolidated reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 6.1 times National Beverage's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Coca-Cola Consolidated's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and National Beverage's fell 1.7%. Coca-Cola Consolidated had an operating margin of 13.2%, against 19.5% at National Beverage. Free cash flow was $620 million at Coca-Cola Consolidated and $156 million at National Beverage. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and May 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
