    Fox vs SiriusXM

    Fox FOXA · SiriusXM SIRI · Broadcasting

    Fox reported revenue of $17.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.0 times SiriusXM's $8.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Fox's revenue grew 5.1% in its latest year, and SiriusXM's fell 1.6%. Free cash flow was $1.5 billion at Fox and $1.2 billion at SiriusXM. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
