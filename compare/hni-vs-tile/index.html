    HNI vs Interface

    HNI HNI · Interface TILE · Office Services & Supplies

    HNI reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Interface's $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. HNI's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Interface's grew 5.4%. HNI had an operating margin of 4.4%, against 11.8% at Interface. Free cash flow was $208 million at HNI and $122 million at Interface. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
