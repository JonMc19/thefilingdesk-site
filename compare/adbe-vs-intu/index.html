    Adobe vs Intuit

    Adobe ADBE · Intuit INTU · Application Software

    Adobe reported revenue of $23.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Intuit's $21.4 billion in fiscal 2026. Adobe's revenue grew 10.5% in its latest year, and Intuit's grew 13.9%. Adobe had an operating margin of 36.6%, against 27.4% at Intuit. Free cash flow was $9.9 billion at Adobe and $8.7 billion at Intuit. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 28 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
