    Darden Restaurants vs Starbucks

    Darden Restaurants DRI · Starbucks SBUX · Restaurants

    Starbucks reported revenue of $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.8 times Darden Restaurants' $13.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Darden Restaurants' revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and Starbucks' grew 2.8%. Darden Restaurants had an operating margin of 12.0%, against 7.9% at Starbucks. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Darden Restaurants and $2.4 billion at Starbucks. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
