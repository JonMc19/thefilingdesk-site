    Haleon vs Unilever

    Haleon HLN · Unilever UL · Personal Care Products

    Haleon reported revenue of £11.0 billion in fiscal 2025, and Unilever €50.5 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Haleon's revenue fell 1.8% in its latest year, and Unilever's fell 3.8%. Haleon had an operating margin of 21.9%, against 17.9% at Unilever. Free cash flow was £2.3 billion at Haleon and €6.9 billion at Unilever.
