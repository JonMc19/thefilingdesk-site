    Crocs vs Deckers Brands

    Crocs CROX · Deckers Brands DECK · Footwear

    Deckers Brands reported revenue of $5.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.4 times Crocs' $4.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Crocs' revenue fell 1.5% in its latest year, and Deckers Brands' grew 9.8%. Crocs had an operating margin of 3.7%, against 23.1% at Deckers Brands. Free cash flow was $659 million at Crocs and $1.1 billion at Deckers Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
