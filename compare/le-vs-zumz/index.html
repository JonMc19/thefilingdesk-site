    Lands' End vs Zumiez

    Lands' End LE · Zumiez ZUMZ · Apparel Retail

    Lands' End reported revenue of $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Zumiez's $929 million in fiscal 2025. Lands' End's revenue grew 0.7% in its latest year, and Zumiez's grew 4.5%. Lands' End had an operating margin of 3.8%, against 1.8% at Zumiez. Free cash flow was $20 million at Lands' End and $42 million at Zumiez. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
