    Apple vs Hewlett Packard Enterprise

    Apple AAPL · Hewlett Packard Enterprise HPE · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Apple reported revenue of $416.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 12.1 times Hewlett Packard Enterprise's $34.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Apple's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise's grew 13.8%. Apple had an operating margin of 32.0%, against -1.3% at Hewlett Packard Enterprise. Free cash flow was $98.8 billion at Apple and $627 million at Hewlett Packard Enterprise. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and October 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
