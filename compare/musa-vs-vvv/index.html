    Murphy USA vs Valvoline

    Murphy USA MUSA · Valvoline VVV · Automotive Retail

    Murphy USA reported revenue of $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 11.3 times Valvoline's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Murphy USA's revenue fell 4.2% in its latest year, and Valvoline's grew 5.6%. Murphy USA had an operating margin of 3.7%, against 22.8% at Valvoline. Free cash flow was $374 million at Murphy USA and $38 million at Valvoline. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
