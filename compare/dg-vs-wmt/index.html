    Dollar General vs Walmart

    Dollar General DG · Walmart WMT · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Walmart reported revenue of $713.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 16.7 times Dollar General's $42.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Dollar General's revenue grew 5.2% in its latest year, and Walmart's grew 4.7%. Dollar General had an operating margin of 5.2%, against 4.2% at Walmart. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Dollar General and $14.9 billion at Walmart. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
