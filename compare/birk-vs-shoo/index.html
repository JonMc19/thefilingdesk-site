    Birkenstock vs Steven Madden

    Birkenstock BIRK · Steven Madden SHOO · Footwear

    Birkenstock reported revenue of €2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, and Steven Madden $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Birkenstock's revenue grew 16.2% in its latest year, and Steven Madden's grew 11.0%. Birkenstock had an operating margin of 26.2%, against 3.2% at Steven Madden. Free cash flow was €308 million at Birkenstock and $120 million at Steven Madden. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
