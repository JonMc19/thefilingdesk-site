    AMD vs Texas Instruments

    AMD AMD · Texas Instruments TXN · Semiconductors

    AMD reported revenue of $34.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Texas Instruments' $17.7 billion in fiscal 2025. AMD's revenue grew 34.3% in its latest year, and Texas Instruments' grew 13.1%. AMD had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 34.1% at Texas Instruments. Free cash flow was $6.7 billion at AMD and $2.6 billion at Texas Instruments. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
