    BJ's Wholesale Club vs Dollar Tree

    BJ's Wholesale Club BJ · Dollar Tree DLTR · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    BJ's Wholesale Club reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Dollar Tree's $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025. BJ's Wholesale Club's revenue grew 4.7% in its latest year, and Dollar Tree's grew 10.4%. BJ's Wholesale Club had an operating margin of 3.8%, against 8.5% at Dollar Tree. Free cash flow was $328 million at BJ's Wholesale Club and $1.1 billion at Dollar Tree.
