    Henry Schein vs McKesson

    Henry Schein HSIC · McKesson MCK · Health Care Distributors

    McKesson reported revenue of $403.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 30.6 times Henry Schein's $13.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Henry Schein's revenue grew 4.0% in its latest year, and McKesson's grew 12.4%. Henry Schein had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 1.5% at McKesson. Free cash flow was $573 million at Henry Schein and $5.7 billion at McKesson. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
