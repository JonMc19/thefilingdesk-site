    Affirm vs Global Payments

    Affirm AFRM · Global Payments GPN · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    Global Payments reported revenue of $7.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Affirm's $4.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Affirm's revenue grew 32.1% in its latest year, and Global Payments' fell 0.4%. Affirm had an operating margin of 9.8%, against 22.8% at Global Payments. Free cash flow was $993 million at Affirm and $2.0 billion at Global Payments. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
