    Curtiss-Wright vs Moog

    Curtiss-Wright CW · Moog MOG-A · Aerospace & Defense

    Moog reported revenue of $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Curtiss-Wright's $3.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Curtiss-Wright's revenue grew 12.1% in its latest year, and Moog's grew 7.0%. Curtiss-Wright had an operating margin of 18.1%, against 11.7% at Moog. Free cash flow was $554 million at Curtiss-Wright and $128 million at Moog. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
