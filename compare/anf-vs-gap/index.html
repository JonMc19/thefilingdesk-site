    Abercrombie & Fitch vs Gap

    Abercrombie & Fitch ANF · Gap GAP · Apparel Retail

    Gap reported revenue of $15.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.9 times Abercrombie & Fitch's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Abercrombie & Fitch's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Gap's grew 1.9%. Abercrombie & Fitch had an operating margin of 13.3%, against 7.3% at Gap. Free cash flow was $378 million at Abercrombie & Fitch and $823 million at Gap.
