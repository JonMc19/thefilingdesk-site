    Fair Isaac vs RingCentral

    Fair Isaac FICO · RingCentral RNG · Application Software

    Fair Isaac reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as RingCentral's $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Fair Isaac's revenue grew 27.3% in its latest year, and RingCentral's grew 4.8%. Fair Isaac had an operating margin of 49.9%, against 4.8% at RingCentral. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Fair Isaac and $587 million at RingCentral. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
