    Costco vs Dollar General

    Costco COST · Dollar General DG · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Costco reported revenue of $303.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 7.1 times Dollar General's $42.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Costco's revenue grew 10.1% in its latest year, and Dollar General's grew 5.2%. Costco had an operating margin of 3.9%, against 5.2% at Dollar General. Free cash flow was $9.4 billion at Costco and $2.4 billion at Dollar General. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and January 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
