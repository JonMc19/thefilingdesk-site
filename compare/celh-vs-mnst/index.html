    Celsius vs Monster Beverage

    Celsius CELH · Monster Beverage MNST · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    Monster Beverage reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.3 times Celsius' $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Celsius' revenue grew 85.5% in its latest year, and Monster Beverage's grew 10.7%. Celsius had an operating margin of 5.6%, against 29.2% at Monster Beverage. Free cash flow was $323 million at Celsius and $2.0 billion at Monster Beverage.
