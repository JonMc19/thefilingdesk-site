    General Mills vs Kraft Heinz

    General Mills GIS · Kraft Heinz KHC · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Kraft Heinz reported revenue of $24.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times General Mills' $18.4 billion in fiscal 2026. General Mills' revenue fell 5.5% in its latest year, and Kraft Heinz's fell 3.5%. General Mills had an operating margin of 4.8%, against -18.7% at Kraft Heinz. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at General Mills and $3.7 billion at Kraft Heinz. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
