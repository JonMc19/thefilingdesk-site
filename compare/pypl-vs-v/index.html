    PayPal vs Visa

    PayPal PYPL · Visa V · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    Visa reported revenue of $40.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times PayPal's $33.2 billion in fiscal 2025. PayPal's revenue grew 4.3% in its latest year, and Visa's grew 11.3%. PayPal had an operating margin of 18.3%, against 60.0% at Visa. Free cash flow was $5.6 billion at PayPal and $21.6 billion at Visa. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
