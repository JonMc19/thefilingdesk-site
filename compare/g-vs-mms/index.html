    Genpact vs Maximus

    Genpact G · Maximus MMS · Data Processing & Outsourced Services

    Maximus reported revenue of $5.4 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Genpact's $5.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Genpact's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Maximus' grew 2.4%. Genpact had an operating margin of 14.8%, against 9.7% at Maximus. Free cash flow was $735 million at Genpact and $366 million at Maximus. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
