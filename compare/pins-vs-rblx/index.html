    Pinterest vs Roblox

    Pinterest PINS · Roblox RBLX · Interactive Home Entertainment

Pinterest had an operating margin of 7.6%, against -25.2% at Roblox. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Pinterest and $1.4 billion at Roblox.
