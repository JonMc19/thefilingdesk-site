    Lumentum vs Ubiquiti

    Lumentum LITE · Ubiquiti UI · Communications Equipment

    Ubiquiti reported revenue of $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Lumentum's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Lumentum's revenue grew 83.2% in its latest year, and Ubiquiti's grew 27.2%. Lumentum had an operating margin of 17.4%, against 36.2% at Ubiquiti. Free cash flow was $300 million at Lumentum and $909 million at Ubiquiti. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 27 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
