    Cadence Design Systems vs Workday

    Cadence Design Systems CDNS · Workday WDAY · Application Software

    Workday reported revenue of $9.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.8 times Cadence Design Systems' $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Cadence Design Systems' revenue grew 14.1% in its latest year, and Workday's grew 13.1%. Cadence Design Systems had an operating margin of 28.2%, against 7.5% at Workday. Free cash flow was $1.6 billion at Cadence Design Systems and $2.8 billion at Workday. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
