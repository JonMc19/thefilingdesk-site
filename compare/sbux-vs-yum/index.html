    Starbucks vs Yum! Brands

    Starbucks SBUX · Yum! Brands YUM · Restaurants

    Starbucks reported revenue of $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.5 times Yum! Brands' $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Starbucks' revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Yum! Brands' grew 8.8%. Starbucks had an operating margin of 7.9%, against 31.3% at Yum! Brands. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Starbucks and $1.6 billion at Yum! Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
