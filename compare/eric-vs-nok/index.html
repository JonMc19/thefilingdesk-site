    Ericsson vs Nokia

    Ericsson ERIC · Nokia NOK · Communications Equipment

    Ericsson reported revenue of SEK 236.7 billion in fiscal 2025, and Nokia €19.9 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Ericsson's revenue fell 4.5% in its latest year, and Nokia's grew 3.5%. Ericsson had an operating margin of 16.3%, against 4.5% at Nokia. Free cash flow was SEK 30.3 billion at Ericsson and €1.5 billion at Nokia.
