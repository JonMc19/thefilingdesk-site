    Benchmark Electronics vs Plexus

    Benchmark Electronics BHE · Plexus PLXS · Electronic Manufacturing Services

Free cash flow was $88 million at Benchmark Electronics and $154 million at Plexus. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
