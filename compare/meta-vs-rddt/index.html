    Meta Platforms vs Reddit

    Meta Platforms META · Reddit RDDT · Interactive Media & Services

    Meta Platforms reported revenue of $201.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 91.2 times Reddit's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Meta Platforms' revenue grew 22.2% in its latest year, and Reddit's grew 69.4%. Meta Platforms had an operating margin of 41.4%, against 20.1% at Reddit. Free cash flow was $46.1 billion at Meta Platforms and $684 million at Reddit.
