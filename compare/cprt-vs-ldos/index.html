    Copart vs Leidos

    Copart CPRT · Leidos LDOS · Diversified Support Services

    Leidos reported revenue of $17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.7 times Copart's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2026. Copart's revenue grew 0.4% in its latest year, and Leidos' grew 3.1%. Copart had an operating margin of 35.4%, against 12.3% at Leidos. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Copart and $1.6 billion at Leidos. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
