    Amphenol vs Littelfuse

    Amphenol APH · Littelfuse LFUS · Electronic Components

    Amphenol reported revenue of $23.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 9.7 times Littelfuse's $2.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Amphenol's revenue grew 51.7% in its latest year, and Littelfuse's grew 8.9%. Amphenol had an operating margin of 25.4%, against 1.6% at Littelfuse. Free cash flow was $4.4 billion at Amphenol and $366 million at Littelfuse. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
