    AbbVie vs Gilead Sciences

    AbbVie ABBV · Gilead Sciences GILD · Biotechnology

    AbbVie reported revenue of $61.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Gilead Sciences' $29.4 billion in fiscal 2025. AbbVie's revenue grew 8.6% in its latest year, and Gilead Sciences' grew 2.4%. AbbVie had an operating margin of 24.6%, against 34.0% at Gilead Sciences. Free cash flow was $17.8 billion at AbbVie and $9.5 billion at Gilead Sciences.
