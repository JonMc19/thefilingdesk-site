    Datadog vs Fair Isaac

    Datadog DDOG · Fair Isaac FICO · Application Software

    Datadog reported revenue of $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Fair Isaac's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Datadog's revenue grew 27.7% in its latest year, and Fair Isaac's grew 15.9%. Datadog had an operating margin of -1.3%, against 46.5% at Fair Isaac. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Datadog and $770 million at Fair Isaac. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
