    Central Garden & Pet vs Spectrum Brands

    Central Garden & Pet CENT · Spectrum Brands SPB · Household Products

    Central Garden & Pet reported revenue of $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Spectrum Brands' $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Central Garden & Pet's revenue fell 2.2% in its latest year, and Spectrum Brands' fell 5.2%. Central Garden & Pet had an operating margin of 8.0%, against 4.5% at Spectrum Brands. Free cash flow was $291 million at Central Garden & Pet and $165 million at Spectrum Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
