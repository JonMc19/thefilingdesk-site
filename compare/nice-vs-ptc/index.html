    NiCE vs PTC

    NiCE NICE · PTC PTC · Application Software

    NiCE reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as PTC's $2.7 billion in fiscal 2025. NiCE's revenue grew 7.7% in its latest year, and PTC's grew 19.2%. NiCE had an operating margin of 21.9%, against 35.9% at PTC. Free cash flow was $698 million at NiCE and $857 million at PTC. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
