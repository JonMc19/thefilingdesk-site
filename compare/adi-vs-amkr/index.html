    Analog Devices vs Amkor Technology

    Analog Devices ADI · Amkor Technology AMKR · Semiconductors

    Analog Devices reported revenue of $11.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Amkor Technology's $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Analog Devices' revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and Amkor Technology's grew 6.2%. Analog Devices had an operating margin of 26.6%, against 7.0% at Amkor Technology. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Analog Devices and $191 million at Amkor Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
