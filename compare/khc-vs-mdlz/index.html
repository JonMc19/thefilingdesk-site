    Kraft Heinz vs Mondelez International

    Kraft Heinz KHC · Mondelez International MDLZ · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Mondelez International reported revenue of $38.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Kraft Heinz's $24.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Kraft Heinz's revenue fell 3.5% in its latest year, and Mondelez International's grew 5.8%. Kraft Heinz had an operating margin of -18.7%, against 9.2% at Mondelez International. Free cash flow was $3.7 billion at Kraft Heinz and $3.2 billion at Mondelez International. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
