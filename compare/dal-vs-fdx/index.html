    Delta Air Lines vs FedEx

    Delta Air Lines DAL · FedEx FDX · Passenger Airlines

    FedEx reported revenue of $94.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Delta Air Lines' $63.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Delta Air Lines' revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and FedEx's grew 7.7%. Delta Air Lines had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 5.8% at FedEx. Free cash flow was $3.8 billion at Delta Air Lines and $5.1 billion at FedEx. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and May 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
