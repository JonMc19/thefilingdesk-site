    AECOM vs Jacobs Solutions

    AECOM ACM · Jacobs Solutions J · Construction & Engineering

    AECOM reported revenue of $16.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Jacobs Solutions' $12.0 billion in fiscal 2025. AECOM's revenue grew 0.2% in its latest year, and Jacobs Solutions' grew 4.6%. AECOM had an operating margin of 6.4%, against 7.2% at Jacobs Solutions. Free cash flow was $685 million at AECOM and $607 million at Jacobs Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and September 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
