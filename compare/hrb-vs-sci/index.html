    H&R Block vs Service Corp Intl

    H&R Block HRB · Service Corp Intl SCI · Specialized Consumer Services

    Service Corp Intl reported revenue of $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as H&R Block's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2026. H&R Block's revenue grew 4.9% in its latest year, and Service Corp Intl's grew 2.9%. Free cash flow was $756 million at H&R Block and $554 million at Service Corp Intl. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
