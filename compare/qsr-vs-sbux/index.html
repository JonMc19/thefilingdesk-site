    Restaurant Brands International vs Starbucks

    Restaurant Brands International QSR · Starbucks SBUX · Restaurants

    Starbucks reported revenue of $37.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.9 times Restaurant Brands International's $9.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Restaurant Brands International's revenue grew 12.2% in its latest year, and Starbucks' grew 2.8%. Restaurant Brands International had an operating margin of 23.3%, against 7.9% at Starbucks. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Restaurant Brands International and $2.4 billion at Starbucks. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
