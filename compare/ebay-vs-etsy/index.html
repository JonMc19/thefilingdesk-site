    eBay vs Etsy

    eBay EBAY · Etsy ETSY · Broadline Retail

    eBay reported revenue of $11.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.8 times Etsy's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. eBay's revenue grew 8.0% in its latest year, and Etsy's grew 2.7%. eBay had an operating margin of 20.5%, against 9.2% at Etsy. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at eBay and $678 million at Etsy.
