    Roku vs Warner Music Group

    Roku ROKU · Warner Music Group WMG · Movies & Entertainment

    Warner Music Group reported revenue of $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Roku's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Roku's revenue grew 15.2% in its latest year, and Warner Music Group's grew 4.4%. Roku had an operating margin of -0.1%, against 10.3% at Warner Music Group. Free cash flow was $478 million at Roku and $539 million at Warner Music Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
