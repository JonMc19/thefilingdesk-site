    Kontoor Brands vs Warby Parker

    Kontoor Brands KTB · Warby Parker WRBY · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Kontoor Brands reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.6 times Warby Parker's $872 million in fiscal 2025. Kontoor Brands' revenue grew 20.9% in its latest year, and Warby Parker's grew 13.0%. Kontoor Brands had an operating margin of 10.7%, against -0.6% at Warby Parker. Free cash flow was $435 million at Kontoor Brands and $44 million at Warby Parker. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
