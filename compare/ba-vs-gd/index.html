    Boeing vs General Dynamics

    Boeing BA · General Dynamics GD · Aerospace & Defense

    Boeing reported revenue of $89.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times General Dynamics' $52.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Boeing's revenue grew 34.5% in its latest year, and General Dynamics' grew 10.1%. Boeing had an operating margin of 4.8%, against 10.2% at General Dynamics. Free cash flow was −$1.9 billion at Boeing and $4.0 billion at General Dynamics.
