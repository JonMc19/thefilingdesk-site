    Steven Madden vs Wolverine World Wide

    Steven Madden SHOO · Wolverine World Wide WWW · Footwear

    Steven Madden reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Wolverine World Wide's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $120 million at Steven Madden and $126 million at Wolverine World Wide. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
