    Hershey vs Mondelez International

    Hershey HSY · Mondelez International MDLZ · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Mondelez International reported revenue of $38.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.3 times Hershey's $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Hershey's revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Mondelez International's grew 5.8%. Hershey had an operating margin of 12.3%, against 9.2% at Mondelez International. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at Hershey and $3.2 billion at Mondelez International.
