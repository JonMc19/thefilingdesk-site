    OSI Systems vs Ralliant

    OSI Systems OSIS · Ralliant RAL · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Ralliant reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times OSI Systems' $1.8 billion in fiscal 2026. OSI Systems' revenue grew 4.2% in its latest year, and Ralliant's fell 4.0%. OSI Systems had an operating margin of 12.3%, against -57.2% at Ralliant. Free cash flow was $245 million at OSI Systems and $358 million at Ralliant. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
