    FactSet vs MSCI

    FactSet FDS · MSCI MSCI · Financial Exchanges & Data

    MSCI reported revenue of $3.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times FactSet's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. FactSet's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and MSCI's grew 9.8%. FactSet had an operating margin of 32.2%, against 54.7% at MSCI. Free cash flow was $617 million at FactSet and $1.5 billion at MSCI. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
