    ACI Worldwide vs Dynatrace

    ACI Worldwide ACIW · Dynatrace DT · Application Software

    Dynatrace reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as ACI Worldwide's $1.8 billion in fiscal 2025. ACI Worldwide's revenue grew 10.4% in its latest year, and Dynatrace's grew 18.8%. ACI Worldwide had an operating margin of 18.8%, against 12.2% at Dynatrace. Free cash flow was $310 million at ACI Worldwide and $530 million at Dynatrace. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
