    Aramark vs Pool

    Aramark ARMK · Pool POOL · Distributors

Aramark had an operating margin of 4.3%, against 11.0% at Pool. Free cash flow was $432 million at Aramark and $310 million at Pool. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
