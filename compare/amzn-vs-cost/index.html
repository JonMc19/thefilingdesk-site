    Amazon vs Costco

    Amazon AMZN · Costco COST

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.4 times Costco's $303.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Costco's grew 10.1%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 3.9% at Costco. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $9.4 billion at Costco. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
