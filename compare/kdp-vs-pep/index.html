    Keurig Dr Pepper vs PepsiCo

    Keurig Dr Pepper KDP · PepsiCo PEP · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    PepsiCo reported revenue of $93.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.7 times Keurig Dr Pepper's $16.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Keurig Dr Pepper's revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and PepsiCo's grew 2.2%. Keurig Dr Pepper had an operating margin of 21.5%, against 12.2% at PepsiCo. Free cash flow was $1.5 billion at Keurig Dr Pepper and $7.7 billion at PepsiCo. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
