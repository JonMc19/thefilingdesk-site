    Powerfleet vs Digi International

    Powerfleet AIOT · Digi International DGII · Communications Equipment

    Powerfleet reported revenue of $444 million in fiscal 2026, about the same as Digi International's $430 million in fiscal 2025. Powerfleet's revenue grew 22.4% in its latest year, and Digi International's grew 1.5%. Powerfleet had an operating margin of 4.4%, against 13.1% at Digi International. Free cash flow was $9 million at Powerfleet and $105 million at Digi International. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
