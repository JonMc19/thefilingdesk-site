    Dexcom vs Steris

    Dexcom DXCM · Steris STE · Health Care Equipment

    Steris reported revenue of $5.9 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Dexcom's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Dexcom's revenue grew 15.6% in its latest year, and Steris' grew 8.7%. Dexcom had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 18.6% at Steris. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Dexcom and $972 million at Steris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
