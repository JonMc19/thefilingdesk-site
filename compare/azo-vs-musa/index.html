    AutoZone vs Murphy USA

    AutoZone AZO · Murphy USA MUSA · Automotive Retail

    Murphy USA reported revenue of $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as AutoZone's $18.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at AutoZone and $374 million at Murphy USA. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
