    Floor & Decor vs Home Depot

    Floor & Decor FND · Home Depot HD · Home Improvement Retail

    Home Depot reported revenue of $164.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 35.2 times Floor & Decor's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Floor & Decor's revenue grew 5.1% in its latest year, and Home Depot's grew 3.2%. Floor & Decor had an operating margin of 5.8%, against 12.7% at Home Depot. Free cash flow was $64 million at Floor & Decor and $12.6 billion at Home Depot. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 25 and February 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
