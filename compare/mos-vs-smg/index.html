    Mosaic vs Scotts Miracle-Gro

    Mosaic MOS · Scotts Miracle-Gro SMG · Fertilizers & Agricultural Chemicals

    Mosaic reported revenue of $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.5 times Scotts Miracle-Gro's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Mosaic's revenue grew 8.4% in its latest year, and Scotts Miracle-Gro's fell 3.9%. Mosaic had an operating margin of 6.8%, against 10.5% at Scotts Miracle-Gro. Free cash flow was −$535 million at Mosaic and $274 million at Scotts Miracle-Gro. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
