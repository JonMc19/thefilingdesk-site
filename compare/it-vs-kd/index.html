    Gartner vs Kyndryl

    Gartner IT · Kyndryl KD · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Kyndryl reported revenue of $15.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.3 times Gartner's $6.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Gartner's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Kyndryl's grew 0.2%. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Gartner and $340 million at Kyndryl. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
