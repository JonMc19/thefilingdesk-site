    Covista vs Strategic Education

    Covista CVSA · Strategic Education STRA · Education Services

    Covista reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Strategic Education's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Covista's revenue grew 9.3% in its latest year, and Strategic Education's grew 4.0%. Covista had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 13.7% at Strategic Education. Free cash flow was $393 million at Covista and $154 million at Strategic Education. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
