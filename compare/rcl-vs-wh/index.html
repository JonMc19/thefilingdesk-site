    Royal Caribbean Group vs Wyndham Hotels & Resorts

    Royal Caribbean Group RCL · Wyndham Hotels & Resorts WH · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Royal Caribbean Group reported revenue of $17.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 12.6 times Wyndham Hotels & Resorts' $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Royal Caribbean Group's revenue grew 8.8% in its latest year, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts' grew 1.5%. Royal Caribbean Group had an operating margin of 27.4%, against 28.1% at Wyndham Hotels & Resorts. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Royal Caribbean Group and $321 million at Wyndham Hotels & Resorts.
