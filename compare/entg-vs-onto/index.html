    Entegris vs Onto Innovation

    Entegris ENTG · Onto Innovation ONTO · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Entegris reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.2 times Onto Innovation's $1.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Entegris' revenue fell 1.4% in its latest year, and Onto Innovation's grew 1.8%. Entegris had an operating margin of 14.3%, against 13.2% at Onto Innovation. Free cash flow was $396 million at Entegris and $300 million at Onto Innovation. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
