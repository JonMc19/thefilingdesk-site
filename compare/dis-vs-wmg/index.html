    Disney vs Warner Music Group

    Disney DIS · Warner Music Group WMG · Movies & Entertainment

    Disney reported revenue of $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 14.1 times Warner Music Group's $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Disney's revenue grew 3.4% in its latest year, and Warner Music Group's grew 4.4%. Disney had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 10.3% at Warner Music Group. Free cash flow was $10.1 billion at Disney and $539 million at Warner Music Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
