    Cooper Companies vs Envista

    Cooper Companies COO · Envista NVST · Health Care Supplies

Cooper Companies had an operating margin of 16.7%, against 8.0% at Envista. Free cash flow was $434 million at Cooper Companies and $230 million at Envista. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
