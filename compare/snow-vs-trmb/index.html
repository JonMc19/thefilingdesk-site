    Snowflake vs Trimble

    Snowflake SNOW · Trimble TRMB · Application Software

    Snowflake reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Trimble's $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Snowflake's revenue grew 29.2% in its latest year, and Trimble's fell 2.6%. Snowflake had an operating margin of -30.6%, against 16.5% at Trimble. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Snowflake and $361 million at Trimble. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
