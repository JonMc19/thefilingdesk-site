    Enphase Energy vs Ultra Clean

    Enphase Energy ENPH · Ultra Clean UCTT · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

Free cash flow was $96 million at Enphase Energy and $15 million at Ultra Clean. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
