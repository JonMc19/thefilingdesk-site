    Axon Enterprise vs L3Harris

    Axon Enterprise AXON · L3Harris LHX · Aerospace & Defense

    L3Harris reported revenue of $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 7.9 times Axon Enterprise's $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Axon Enterprise's revenue grew 33.5% in its latest year, and L3Harris' grew 2.5%. Axon Enterprise had an operating margin of -2.2%, against 9.7% at L3Harris. Free cash flow was $75 million at Axon Enterprise and $2.7 billion at L3Harris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
