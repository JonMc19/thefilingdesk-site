    ESAB vs Graco

    ESAB ESAB · Graco GGG · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    ESAB reported revenue of $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Graco's $2.2 billion in fiscal 2025. ESAB's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Graco's grew 5.8%. ESAB had an operating margin of 14.5%, against 27.9% at Graco. Free cash flow was $213 million at ESAB and $638 million at Graco. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
