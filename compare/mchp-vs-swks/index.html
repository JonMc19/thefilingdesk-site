    Microchip Technology vs Skyworks Solutions

    Microchip Technology MCHP · Skyworks Solutions SWKS · Semiconductors

    Microchip Technology reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Skyworks Solutions' $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Microchip Technology's revenue grew 7.1% in its latest year, and Skyworks Solutions' fell 2.2%. Microchip Technology had an operating margin of 10.4%, against 12.2% at Skyworks Solutions. Free cash flow was $871 million at Microchip Technology and $1.1 billion at Skyworks Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and October 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
