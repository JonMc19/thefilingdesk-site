    Crocs vs Wolverine World Wide

    Crocs CROX · Wolverine World Wide WWW · Footwear

    Crocs reported revenue of $4.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Wolverine World Wide's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Free cash flow was $659 million at Crocs and $126 million at Wolverine World Wide. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
