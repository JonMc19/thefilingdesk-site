    Urban Outfitters vs Victoria's Secret

    Urban Outfitters URBN · Victoria's Secret VSXY · Apparel Retail

    Victoria's Secret reported revenue of $6.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Urban Outfitters' $6.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Urban Outfitters' revenue grew 11.1% in its latest year, and Victoria's Secret's grew 5.2%. Urban Outfitters had an operating margin of 9.8%, against 4.1% at Victoria's Secret. Free cash flow was $315 million at Urban Outfitters and $312 million at Victoria's Secret.
