    Daktronics vs Novanta

    Daktronics DAKT · Novanta NOVT · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Novanta reported revenue of $981 million in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Daktronics' $839 million in fiscal 2026. Daktronics' revenue grew 10.9% in its latest year, and Novanta's grew 3.3%. Daktronics had an operating margin of 7.2%, against 9.6% at Novanta. Free cash flow was $34 million at Daktronics and $48 million at Novanta. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
