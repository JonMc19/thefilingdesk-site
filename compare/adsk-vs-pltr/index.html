    Autodesk vs Palantir

    Autodesk ADSK · Palantir PLTR · Application Software

    Autodesk reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.6 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Autodesk's revenue grew 17.5% in its latest year, and Palantir's grew 56.2%. Autodesk had an operating margin of 21.9%, against 31.6% at Palantir. Free cash flow was $2.4 billion at Autodesk and $2.1 billion at Palantir. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
