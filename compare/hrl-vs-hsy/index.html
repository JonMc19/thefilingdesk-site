    Hormel Foods vs Hershey

    Hormel Foods HRL · Hershey HSY · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hormel Foods reported revenue of $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Hershey's $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Hormel Foods' revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Hershey's grew 4.4%. Hormel Foods had an operating margin of 5.9%, against 12.3% at Hershey. Free cash flow was $534 million at Hormel Foods and $1.8 billion at Hershey. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 26 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
