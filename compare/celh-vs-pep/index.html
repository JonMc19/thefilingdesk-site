    Celsius vs PepsiCo

    Celsius CELH · PepsiCo PEP · Soft Drinks & Non-alcoholic Beverages

    PepsiCo reported revenue of $93.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 37.3 times Celsius' $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Celsius' revenue grew 85.5% in its latest year, and PepsiCo's grew 2.2%. Celsius had an operating margin of 5.6%, against 12.2% at PepsiCo. Free cash flow was $323 million at Celsius and $7.7 billion at PepsiCo. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
