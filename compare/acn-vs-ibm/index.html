    Accenture vs IBM

    Accenture ACN · IBM IBM · IT Consulting & Other Services

Free cash flow was $10.9 billion at Accenture and $12.1 billion at IBM. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
