    Amer Sports vs Tapestry

    Amer Sports AS · Tapestry TPR · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Tapestry reported revenue of $8.0 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Amer Sports' $6.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Amer Sports' revenue grew 26.7% in its latest year, and Tapestry's grew 14.2%. Amer Sports had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 23.9% at Tapestry. Free cash flow was $513 million at Amer Sports and $1.8 billion at Tapestry. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
