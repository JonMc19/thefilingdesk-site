    Cardinal Health vs McKesson

    Cardinal Health CAH · McKesson MCK · Health Care Distributors

Free cash flow was $4.5 billion at Cardinal Health and $5.7 billion at McKesson. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and March 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
