    ESAB vs Middleby

    ESAB ESAB · Middleby MIDD · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Middleby reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as ESAB's $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. ESAB's revenue grew 3.7% in its latest year, and Middleby's grew 1.6%. ESAB had an operating margin of 14.5%, against 18.0% at Middleby. Free cash flow was $213 million at ESAB and $559 million at Middleby. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
