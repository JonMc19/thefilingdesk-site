    Marvell Technology vs Texas Instruments

    Marvell Technology MRVL · Texas Instruments TXN · Semiconductors

    Texas Instruments reported revenue of $17.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Marvell Technology's $8.2 billion in fiscal 2026. Marvell Technology's revenue grew 42.1% in its latest year, and Texas Instruments' grew 13.1%. Marvell Technology had an operating margin of 16.1%, against 34.1% at Texas Instruments. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Marvell Technology and $2.6 billion at Texas Instruments. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
