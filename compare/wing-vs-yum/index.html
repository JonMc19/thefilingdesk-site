    Wingstop vs Yum! Brands

    Wingstop WING · Yum! Brands YUM · Restaurants

    Yum! Brands reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 11.8 times Wingstop's $697 million in fiscal 2025. Wingstop's revenue grew 11.3% in its latest year, and Yum! Brands' grew 8.8%. Wingstop had an operating margin of 25.7%, against 31.3% at Yum! Brands. Free cash flow was $106 million at Wingstop and $1.6 billion at Yum! Brands. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
