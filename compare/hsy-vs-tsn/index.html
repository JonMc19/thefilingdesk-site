    Hershey vs Tyson Foods

    Hershey HSY · Tyson Foods TSN · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Tyson Foods reported revenue of $54.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.7 times Hershey's $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Hershey's revenue grew 4.4% in its latest year, and Tyson Foods' grew 2.1%. Hershey had an operating margin of 12.3%, against 2.0% at Tyson Foods. Free cash flow was $1.8 billion at Hershey and $1.2 billion at Tyson Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
