    Disney vs MGM Resorts

    Disney DIS · MGM Resorts MGM

    Disney reported revenue of $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.4 times MGM Resorts' $17.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Disney's revenue grew 3.4% in its latest year, and MGM Resorts' grew 1.7%. Disney had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 5.7% at MGM Resorts. Free cash flow was $10.1 billion at Disney and $1.5 billion at MGM Resorts. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
