    Kohl's vs Macy's

    Kohl's KSS · Macy's M · Broadline Retail

    Macy's reported revenue of $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Kohl's $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Kohl's revenue fell 4.3% in its latest year, and Macy's fell 1.7%. Kohl's had an operating margin of 4.0%, against 4.5% at Macy's. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Kohl's and $1.1 billion at Macy's.
