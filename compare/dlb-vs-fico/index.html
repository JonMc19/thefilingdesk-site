    Dolby vs Fair Isaac

    Dolby DLB · Fair Isaac FICO · Application Software

    Fair Isaac reported revenue of $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Dolby's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Dolby's revenue grew 5.9% in its latest year, and Fair Isaac's grew 15.9%. Dolby had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 46.5% at Fair Isaac. Free cash flow was $436 million at Dolby and $770 million at Fair Isaac. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 26 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
