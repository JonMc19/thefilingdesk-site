    Bath & Body Works vs Chewy

    Bath & Body Works BBWI · Chewy CHWY · Other Specialty Retail

    Chewy reported revenue of $12.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Bath & Body Works' $7.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Bath & Body Works' revenue fell 0.2% in its latest year, and Chewy's grew 6.2%. Bath & Body Works had an operating margin of 15.4%, against 2.0% at Chewy. Free cash flow was $865 million at Bath & Body Works and $562 million at Chewy. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and February 1), so the periods don't line up exactly.
