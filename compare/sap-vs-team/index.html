    SAP SE vs Atlassian

    SAP SE SAP · Atlassian TEAM · Application Software

    SAP SE reported revenue of €36.8 billion in fiscal 2025, and Atlassian $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026, in different currencies. SAP SE's revenue grew 7.7% in its latest year, and Atlassian's grew 26.0%. SAP SE had an operating margin of 26.1%, against 0.2% at Atlassian. Free cash flow was €8.4 billion at SAP SE and $1.3 billion at Atlassian. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
