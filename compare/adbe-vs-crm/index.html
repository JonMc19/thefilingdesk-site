    Adobe vs Salesforce

    Adobe ADBE · Salesforce CRM · Application Software

    Salesforce reported revenue of $41.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.7 times Adobe's $23.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Adobe's revenue grew 10.5% in its latest year, and Salesforce's grew 9.6%. Adobe had an operating margin of 36.6%, against 20.1% at Salesforce. Free cash flow was $9.9 billion at Adobe and $14.4 billion at Salesforce. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 28 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
