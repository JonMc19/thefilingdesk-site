    L3Harris vs RTX

    L3Harris LHX · RTX RTX · Aerospace & Defense

    RTX reported revenue of $88.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.1 times L3Harris' $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025. L3Harris' revenue grew 2.5% in its latest year, and RTX's grew 9.7%. L3Harris had an operating margin of 9.7%, against 10.5% at RTX. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at L3Harris and $7.9 billion at RTX. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
