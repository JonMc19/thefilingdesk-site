    Arista Networks vs Nokia

    Arista Networks ANET · Nokia NOK · Communications Equipment

    Arista Networks reported revenue of $9.0 billion in fiscal 2025, and Nokia €19.9 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Arista Networks' revenue grew 28.6% in its latest year, and Nokia's grew 3.5%. Arista Networks had an operating margin of 42.8%, against 4.5% at Nokia. Free cash flow was $4.3 billion at Arista Networks and €1.5 billion at Nokia.
