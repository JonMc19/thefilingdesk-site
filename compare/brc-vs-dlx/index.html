    Brady vs Deluxe

    Brady BRC · Deluxe DLX · Commercial Printing

    Deluxe reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Brady's $1.7 billion in fiscal 2026. Brady's revenue grew 9.8% in its latest year, and Deluxe's grew 0.5%. Brady had an operating margin of 15.9%, against 10.9% at Deluxe. Free cash flow was $193 million at Brady and $175 million at Deluxe. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
