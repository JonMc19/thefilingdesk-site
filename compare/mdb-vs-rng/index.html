    MongoDB vs RingCentral

    MongoDB MDB · RingCentral RNG · Application Software

    RingCentral reported revenue of $2.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as MongoDB's $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026. MongoDB's revenue grew 22.8% in its latest year, and RingCentral's grew 4.8%. MongoDB had an operating margin of -5.6%, against 4.8% at RingCentral. Free cash flow was $500 million at MongoDB and $587 million at RingCentral. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
