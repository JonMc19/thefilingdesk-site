    Raymond James Financial vs Stifel

    Raymond James Financial RJF · Stifel SF · Investment Banking & Brokerage

    Raymond James Financial reported revenue of $15.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.5 times Stifel's $6.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Raymond James Financial's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Stifel's grew 6.7%. Raymond James Financial had a return on equity of 17.7%, against 11.7% at Stifel. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
