    Cirrus Logic vs Skyworks Solutions

    Cirrus Logic CRUS · Skyworks Solutions SWKS · Semiconductors

    Skyworks Solutions reported revenue of $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times Cirrus Logic's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Cirrus Logic's revenue grew 5.3% in its latest year, and Skyworks Solutions' fell 2.2%. Cirrus Logic had an operating margin of 23.1%, against 12.2% at Skyworks Solutions. Free cash flow was $637 million at Cirrus Logic and $1.1 billion at Skyworks Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and October 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
