    Home Depot vs Lowe's

    Home Depot HD · Lowe's LOW · Home Improvement Retail

    Home Depot reported revenue of $164.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.9 times Lowe's $86.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Home Depot's revenue grew 3.2% in its latest year, and Lowe's grew 3.1%. Home Depot had an operating margin of 12.7%, against 11.8% at Lowe's. Free cash flow was $12.6 billion at Home Depot and $7.7 billion at Lowe's. Their fiscal years end in different months (February 1 and January 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
