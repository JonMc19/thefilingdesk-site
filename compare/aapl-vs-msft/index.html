    Apple vs Microsoft

    Apple AAPL · Microsoft MSFT · Information Technology

    Apple reported revenue of $416.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Microsoft's $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Apple's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Microsoft's grew 17.8%. Apple had an operating margin of 32.0%, against 46.8% at Microsoft. Free cash flow was $98.8 billion at Apple and $67.0 billion at Microsoft. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
