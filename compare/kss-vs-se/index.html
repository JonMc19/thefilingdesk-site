    Kohl's vs Sea Limited

    Kohl's KSS · Sea Limited SE · Broadline Retail

    Sea Limited reported revenue of $22.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Kohl's $15.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Kohl's revenue fell 4.3% in its latest year, and Sea Limited's grew 36.4%. Kohl's had an operating margin of 4.0%, against 8.7% at Sea Limited. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at Kohl's and $4.5 billion at Sea Limited. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
