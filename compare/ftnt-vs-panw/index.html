    Fortinet vs Palo Alto Networks

    Fortinet FTNT · Palo Alto Networks PANW · Systems Software

    Palo Alto Networks reported revenue of $11.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.7 times Fortinet's $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Fortinet's revenue grew 14.2% in its latest year, and Palo Alto Networks' grew 24.5%. Fortinet had an operating margin of 30.7%, against 6.0% at Palo Alto Networks. Free cash flow was $2.2 billion at Fortinet and $4.1 billion at Palo Alto Networks. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and July 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
