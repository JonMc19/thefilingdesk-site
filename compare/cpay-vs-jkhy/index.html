    Corpay vs Jack Henry & Associates

    Corpay CPAY · Jack Henry & Associates JKHY · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    Corpay reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Jack Henry & Associates' $2.5 billion in fiscal 2026. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Corpay and $695 million at Jack Henry & Associates. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
