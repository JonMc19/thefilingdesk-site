    Hewlett Packard Enterprise vs Western Digital

    Hewlett Packard Enterprise HPE · Western Digital WDC · Technology Hardware, Storage & Peripherals

    Hewlett Packard Enterprise reported revenue of $34.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.7 times Western Digital's $12.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Hewlett Packard Enterprise's revenue grew 13.8% in its latest year, and Western Digital's grew 35.7%. Hewlett Packard Enterprise had an operating margin of -1.3%, against 34.5% at Western Digital. Free cash flow was $627 million at Hewlett Packard Enterprise and $3.5 billion at Western Digital. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and July 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
