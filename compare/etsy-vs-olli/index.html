    Etsy vs Ollie's Bargain Outlet

    Etsy ETSY · Ollie's Bargain Outlet OLLI · Broadline Retail

    Etsy reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Ollie's Bargain Outlet's $2.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Etsy's revenue grew 2.7% in its latest year, and Ollie's Bargain Outlet's grew 16.6%. Etsy had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 11.2% at Ollie's Bargain Outlet. Free cash flow was $678 million at Etsy and $195 million at Ollie's Bargain Outlet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
