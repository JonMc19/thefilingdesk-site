    Columbia Sportswear vs Ralph Lauren

    Columbia Sportswear COLM · Ralph Lauren RL · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Ralph Lauren reported revenue of $8.1 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.4 times Columbia Sportswear's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Columbia Sportswear's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Ralph Lauren's grew 14.6%. Columbia Sportswear had an operating margin of 6.1%, against 14.5% at Ralph Lauren. Free cash flow was $217 million at Columbia Sportswear and $746 million at Ralph Lauren. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and March 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
