    Illinois Tool Works vs Parker Hannifin

    Illinois Tool Works ITW · Parker Hannifin PH · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Parker Hannifin reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.3 times Illinois Tool Works' $16.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Illinois Tool Works' revenue grew 0.9% in its latest year, and Parker Hannifin's grew 8.3%. Illinois Tool Works had an operating margin of 26.3%, against 23.6% at Parker Hannifin. Free cash flow was $2.7 billion at Illinois Tool Works and $3.9 billion at Parker Hannifin. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
