    GlobalFoundries vs Marvell Technology

    GlobalFoundries GFS · Marvell Technology MRVL · Semiconductors

    Marvell Technology reported revenue of $8.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times GlobalFoundries' $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025. GlobalFoundries' revenue grew 0.6% in its latest year, and Marvell Technology's grew 42.1%. GlobalFoundries had an operating margin of 11.7%, against 16.1% at Marvell Technology. Free cash flow was $1.0 billion at GlobalFoundries and $1.4 billion at Marvell Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
