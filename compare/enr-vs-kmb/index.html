    Energizer vs Kimberly-Clark

    Energizer ENR · Kimberly-Clark KMB · Household Products

    Kimberly-Clark reported revenue of $16.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 5.6 times Energizer's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Energizer's revenue grew 2.3% in its latest year, and Kimberly-Clark's fell 2.1%. Free cash flow was $63 million at Energizer and $1.6 billion at Kimberly-Clark. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
