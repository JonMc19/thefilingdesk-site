    Magnum Ice Cream vs Tyson Foods

    Magnum Ice Cream MICC · Tyson Foods TSN · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Magnum Ice Cream reported revenue of €7.9 billion in fiscal 2025, and Tyson Foods $54.4 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Magnum Ice Cream's revenue fell 0.5% in its latest year, and Tyson Foods' grew 2.1%. Magnum Ice Cream had an operating margin of 7.6%, against 2.0% at Tyson Foods. Free cash flow was €126 million at Magnum Ice Cream and $1.2 billion at Tyson Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
