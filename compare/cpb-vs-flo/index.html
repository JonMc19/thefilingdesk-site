    Campbell's vs Flowers Foods

    Campbell's CPB · Flowers Foods FLO · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Campbell's reported revenue of $9.7 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.9 times Flowers Foods' $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Campbell's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and Flowers Foods' grew 3.0%. Campbell's had an operating margin of 8.7%, against 3.3% at Flowers Foods. Free cash flow was $678 million at Campbell's and $319 million at Flowers Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 2 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
