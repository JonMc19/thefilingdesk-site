    Costco vs Target

    Costco COST · Target TGT · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Costco reported revenue of $303.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.9 times Target's $104.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Costco's revenue grew 10.1% in its latest year, and Target's fell 1.7%. Costco had an operating margin of 3.9%, against 4.9% at Target. Free cash flow was $9.4 billion at Costco and $2.8 billion at Target. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 30 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
