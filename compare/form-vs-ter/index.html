    FormFactor vs Teradyne

    FormFactor FORM · Teradyne TER · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Teradyne reported revenue of $3.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.1 times FormFactor's $785 million in fiscal 2025. FormFactor's revenue grew 2.8% in its latest year, and Teradyne's grew 13.1%. FormFactor had an operating margin of 7.3%, against 20.4% at Teradyne. Free cash flow was $12 million at FormFactor and $450 million at Teradyne. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
