    Albany International vs ESCO Technologies

    Albany International AIN · ESCO Technologies ESE · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Albany International reported revenue of $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as ESCO Technologies' $1.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Albany International's revenue fell 3.9% in its latest year, and ESCO Technologies' grew 19.2%. Albany International had an operating margin of -3.0%, against 15.6% at ESCO Technologies. Free cash flow was $83 million at Albany International and $206 million at ESCO Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
