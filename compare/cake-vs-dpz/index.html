    Cheesecake Factory vs Domino's

    Cheesecake Factory CAKE · Domino's DPZ · Restaurants

    Domino's reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Cheesecake Factory's $3.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Cheesecake Factory's revenue grew 4.8% in its latest year, and Domino's grew 5.0%. Cheesecake Factory had an operating margin of 5.0%, against 19.3% at Domino's. Free cash flow was $155 million at Cheesecake Factory and $672 million at Domino's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 30 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
