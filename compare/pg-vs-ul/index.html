    Procter & Gamble vs Unilever

    Procter & Gamble PG · Unilever UL · Personal Care Products

    Procter & Gamble reported revenue of $87.0 billion in fiscal 2026, and Unilever €50.5 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Procter & Gamble's revenue grew 3.3% in its latest year, and Unilever's fell 3.8%. Procter & Gamble had an operating margin of 22.7%, against 17.9% at Unilever. Free cash flow was $15.1 billion at Procter & Gamble and €6.9 billion at Unilever. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
