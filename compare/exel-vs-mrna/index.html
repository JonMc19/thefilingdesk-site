    Exelixis vs Moderna

    Exelixis EXEL · Moderna MRNA · Biotechnology

    Exelixis reported revenue of $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Moderna's $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Exelixis' revenue grew 7.0% in its latest year, and Moderna's fell 39.9%. Exelixis had an operating margin of 37.6%, against -158.1% at Moderna. Free cash flow was $876 million at Exelixis and −$2.1 billion at Moderna. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 2 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
