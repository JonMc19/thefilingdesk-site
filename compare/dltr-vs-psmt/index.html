    Dollar Tree vs PriceSmart

    Dollar Tree DLTR · PriceSmart PSMT · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Dollar Tree reported revenue of $19.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.7 times PriceSmart's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Dollar Tree's revenue grew 10.4% in its latest year, and PriceSmart's grew 7.2%. Dollar Tree had an operating margin of 8.5%, against 4.4% at PriceSmart. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Dollar Tree and $103 million at PriceSmart. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
