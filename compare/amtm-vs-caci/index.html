    Amentum vs CACI International

    Amentum AMTM · CACI International CACI · Diversified Support Services

    Amentum reported revenue of $14.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times CACI International's $9.6 billion in fiscal 2026. Amentum's revenue grew 71.6% in its latest year, and CACI International's grew 10.9%. Amentum had an operating margin of 3.3%, against 9.6% at CACI International. Free cash flow was $516 million at Amentum and $780 million at CACI International. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 3 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
