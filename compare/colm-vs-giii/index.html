    Columbia Sportswear vs G-III Apparel Group

    Columbia Sportswear COLM · G-III Apparel Group GIII · Apparel, Accessories & Luxury Goods

    Columbia Sportswear reported revenue of $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as G-III Apparel Group's $3.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Columbia Sportswear's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and G-III Apparel Group's fell 7.0%. Columbia Sportswear had an operating margin of 6.1%, against 3.6% at G-III Apparel Group. Free cash flow was $217 million at Columbia Sportswear and $264 million at G-III Apparel Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
