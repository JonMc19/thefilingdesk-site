    Buckle vs Urban Outfitters

    Buckle BKE · Urban Outfitters URBN · Apparel Retail

    Urban Outfitters reported revenue of $6.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 4.8 times Buckle's $1.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Buckle's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Urban Outfitters' grew 11.1%. Buckle had an operating margin of 20.1%, against 9.8% at Urban Outfitters. Free cash flow was $206 million at Buckle and $315 million at Urban Outfitters.
