    FTI Consulting vs Thomson Reuters

    FTI Consulting FCN · Thomson Reuters TRI · Research & Consulting Services

FTI Consulting's revenue grew 2.4% in its latest year, and Thomson Reuters' grew 3.0%. FTI Consulting had an operating margin of 10.3%, against 28.5% at Thomson Reuters. Free cash flow was $94 million at FTI Consulting and $2.0 billion at Thomson Reuters.
