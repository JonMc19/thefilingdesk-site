    Grid Dynamics Holdings vs Hackett Group

    Grid Dynamics Holdings GDYN · Hackett Group HCKT · IT Consulting & Other Services

    Grid Dynamics Holdings reported revenue of $412 million in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Hackett Group's $306 million in fiscal 2025. Grid Dynamics Holdings' revenue grew 17.5% in its latest year, and Hackett Group's fell 2.6%. Grid Dynamics Holdings had an operating margin of -0.5%, against 7.7% at Hackett Group. Free cash flow was $25 million at Grid Dynamics Holdings and $32 million at Hackett Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
