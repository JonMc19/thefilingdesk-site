    Digital Turbine vs Varonis Systems

    Digital Turbine APPS · Varonis Systems VRNS · Application Software

    Varonis Systems reported revenue of $624 million in fiscal 2025, about the same as Digital Turbine's $565 million in fiscal 2026. Digital Turbine's revenue grew 15.2% in its latest year, and Varonis Systems' grew 13.2%. Digital Turbine had an operating margin of 6.0%, against -23.5% at Varonis Systems. Free cash flow was $11 million at Digital Turbine and $135 million at Varonis Systems. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
