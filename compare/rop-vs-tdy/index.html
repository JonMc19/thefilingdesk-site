    Roper Technologies vs Teledyne Technologies

    Roper Technologies ROP · Teledyne Technologies TDY · Electronic Equipment & Instruments

    Roper Technologies reported revenue of $7.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Teledyne Technologies' $6.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Roper Technologies' revenue grew 12.3% in its latest year, and Teledyne Technologies' grew 7.9%. Roper Technologies had an operating margin of 28.3%, against 18.8% at Teledyne Technologies. Free cash flow was $2.5 billion at Roper Technologies and $1.1 billion at Teledyne Technologies. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
