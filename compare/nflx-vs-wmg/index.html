    Netflix vs Warner Music Group

    Netflix NFLX · Warner Music Group WMG · Movies & Entertainment

    Netflix reported revenue of $45.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 6.7 times Warner Music Group's $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Netflix's revenue grew 15.8% in its latest year, and Warner Music Group's grew 4.4%. Netflix had an operating margin of 29.5%, against 10.3% at Warner Music Group. Free cash flow was $9.5 billion at Netflix and $539 million at Warner Music Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
