    Knight-Swift vs Landstar System

    Knight-Swift KNX · Landstar System LSTR · Cargo Ground Transportation

    Knight-Swift reported revenue of $7.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Landstar System's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Knight-Swift's revenue grew 0.8% in its latest year, and Landstar System's fell 1.6%. Knight-Swift had an operating margin of 2.9%, against 3.2% at Landstar System. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at Knight-Swift and $215 million at Landstar System. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
