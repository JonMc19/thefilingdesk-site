    Monolithic Power Systems vs Skyworks Solutions

    Monolithic Power Systems MPWR · Skyworks Solutions SWKS · Semiconductors

    Skyworks Solutions reported revenue of $4.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Monolithic Power Systems' $2.8 billion in fiscal 2025. Monolithic Power Systems' revenue grew 26.4% in its latest year, and Skyworks Solutions' fell 2.2%. Monolithic Power Systems had an operating margin of 26.1%, against 12.2% at Skyworks Solutions. Free cash flow was $666 million at Monolithic Power Systems and $1.1 billion at Skyworks Solutions. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and October 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
