    Ciena vs Ubiquiti

    Ciena CIEN · Ubiquiti UI · Communications Equipment

    Ciena reported revenue of $4.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Ubiquiti's $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Ciena's revenue grew 18.8% in its latest year, and Ubiquiti's grew 27.2%. Ciena had an operating margin of 4.1%, against 36.2% at Ubiquiti. Free cash flow was $665 million at Ciena and $909 million at Ubiquiti. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
