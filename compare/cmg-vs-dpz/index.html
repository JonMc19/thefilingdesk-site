    Chipotle Mexican Grill vs Domino's

    Chipotle Mexican Grill CMG · Domino's DPZ · Restaurants

    Chipotle Mexican Grill reported revenue of $11.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.4 times Domino's $4.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Chipotle Mexican Grill's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Domino's grew 5.0%. Chipotle Mexican Grill had an operating margin of 16.2%, against 19.3% at Domino's. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Chipotle Mexican Grill and $672 million at Domino's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
