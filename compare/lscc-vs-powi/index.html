    Lattice Semiconductor vs Power Integrations

    Lattice Semiconductor LSCC · Power Integrations POWI · Semiconductors

    Lattice Semiconductor reported revenue of $523 million in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Power Integrations' $444 million in fiscal 2025. Lattice Semiconductor's revenue grew 2.7% in its latest year, and Power Integrations' grew 5.9%. Lattice Semiconductor had an operating margin of 2.1%, against 2.3% at Power Integrations. Free cash flow was $133 million at Lattice Semiconductor and $87 million at Power Integrations. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 3 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
