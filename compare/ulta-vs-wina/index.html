    Ulta Beauty vs Winmark

    Ulta Beauty ULTA · Winmark WINA · Other Specialty Retail

    Ulta Beauty reported revenue of $12.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 143.9 times Winmark's $86 million in fiscal 2025. Ulta Beauty's revenue grew 9.7% in its latest year, and Winmark's grew 5.9%. Ulta Beauty had an operating margin of 12.4%, against 63.4% at Winmark. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Ulta Beauty and $45 million at Winmark. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
