    Macy's vs MercadoLibre

    Macy's M · MercadoLibre MELI · Broadline Retail

    MercadoLibre reported revenue of $28.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Macy's $22.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Macy's revenue fell 1.7% in its latest year, and MercadoLibre's grew 39.1%. Macy's had an operating margin of 4.5%, against 11.1% at MercadoLibre. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Macy's and $10.8 billion at MercadoLibre. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
