    IES vs MYR Group

    IES IESC · MYR Group MYRG · Construction & Engineering

    MYR Group reported revenue of $3.7 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as IES's $3.4 billion in fiscal 2025. IES's revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and MYR Group's grew 8.8%. IES had an operating margin of 11.4%, against 4.6% at MYR Group. Free cash flow was $219 million at IES and $232 million at MYR Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
