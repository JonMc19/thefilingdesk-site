    Analog Devices vs STMicroelectronics

    Analog Devices ADI · STMicroelectronics STM · Semiconductors

    STMicroelectronics reported revenue of $11.8 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Analog Devices' $11.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Analog Devices' revenue grew 16.9% in its latest year, and STMicroelectronics' fell 11.1%. Analog Devices had an operating margin of 26.6%, against 1.5% at STMicroelectronics. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 1 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
