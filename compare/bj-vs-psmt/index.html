    BJ's Wholesale Club vs PriceSmart

    BJ's Wholesale Club BJ · PriceSmart PSMT · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    BJ's Wholesale Club reported revenue of $21.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.1 times PriceSmart's $5.3 billion in fiscal 2025. BJ's Wholesale Club's revenue grew 4.7% in its latest year, and PriceSmart's grew 7.2%. BJ's Wholesale Club had an operating margin of 3.8%, against 4.4% at PriceSmart. Free cash flow was $328 million at BJ's Wholesale Club and $103 million at PriceSmart. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
