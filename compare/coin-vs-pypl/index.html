    Coinbase vs PayPal

    Coinbase COIN · PayPal PYPL · Financials

    PayPal reported revenue of $33.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.6 times Coinbase's $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Coinbase's revenue grew 9.4% in its latest year, and PayPal's grew 4.3%. Coinbase had an operating margin of 20.0%, against 18.3% at PayPal. Free cash flow was $2.3 billion at Coinbase and $5.6 billion at PayPal.
