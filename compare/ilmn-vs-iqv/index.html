    Illumina vs IQVIA

    Illumina ILMN · IQVIA IQV · Life Sciences Tools & Services

    IQVIA reported revenue of $16.3 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.8 times Illumina's $4.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Illumina's revenue fell 0.7% in its latest year, and IQVIA's grew 5.9%. Illumina had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 13.4% at IQVIA. Free cash flow was $931 million at Illumina and $2.1 billion at IQVIA. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
