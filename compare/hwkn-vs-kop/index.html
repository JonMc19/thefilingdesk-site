    Hawkins vs Koppers

    Hawkins HWKN · Koppers KOP · Commodity Chemicals

    Koppers reported revenue of $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.7 times Hawkins' $1.1 billion in fiscal 2026. Hawkins' revenue grew 11.2% in its latest year, and Koppers' fell 10.2%. Hawkins had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 8.9% at Koppers. Free cash flow was $86 million at Hawkins and $68 million at Koppers. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 29 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
