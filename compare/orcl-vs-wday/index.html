    Oracle vs Workday

    Oracle ORCL · Workday WDAY · Application Software

Oracle had an operating margin of 30.6%, against 7.5% at Workday. Free cash flow was −$23.7 billion at Oracle and $2.8 billion at Workday. Their fiscal years end in different months (May 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
