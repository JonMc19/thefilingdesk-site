    Freshpet vs Simply Good Foods

    Freshpet FRPT · Simply Good Foods SMPL · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Simply Good Foods reported revenue of $1.4 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.2 times Freshpet's $1.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Freshpet's revenue grew 13.0% in its latest year, and Simply Good Foods' fell 6.5%. Freshpet had an operating margin of 6.9%, against -13.2% at Simply Good Foods. Free cash flow was $12 million at Freshpet and $157 million at Simply Good Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 29), so the periods don't line up exactly.
