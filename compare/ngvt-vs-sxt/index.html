    Ingevity vs Sensient Technologies

    Ingevity NGVT · Sensient Technologies SXT · Specialty Chemicals

    Sensient Technologies reported revenue of $1.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Ingevity's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Ingevity's revenue fell 2.7% in its latest year, and Sensient Technologies' grew 3.5%. Ingevity had an operating margin of 35.9%, against 12.8% at Sensient Technologies. Free cash flow was $274 million at Ingevity and $38 million at Sensient Technologies.
