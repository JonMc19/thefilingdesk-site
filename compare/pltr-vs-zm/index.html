    Palantir vs Zoom Communications

    Palantir PLTR · Zoom Communications ZM · Application Software

    Zoom Communications reported revenue of $4.9 billion in fiscal 2026, about the same as Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Palantir's revenue grew 56.2% in its latest year, and Zoom Communications' grew 4.4%. Palantir had an operating margin of 31.6%, against 23.1% at Zoom Communications. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and $1.9 billion at Zoom Communications. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
