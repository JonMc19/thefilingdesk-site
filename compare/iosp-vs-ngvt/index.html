    Innospec vs Ingevity

    Innospec IOSP · Ingevity NGVT · Specialty Chemicals

    Innospec reported revenue of $1.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Ingevity's $1.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Innospec's revenue fell 3.6% in its latest year, and Ingevity's fell 2.7%. Innospec had an operating margin of 7.3%, against 35.9% at Ingevity. Free cash flow was $88 million at Innospec and $274 million at Ingevity.
