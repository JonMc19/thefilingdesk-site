    ArcelorMittal vs Reliance

    ArcelorMittal MT · Reliance RS · Steel

    ArcelorMittal reported revenue of $61.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 4.3 times Reliance's $14.3 billion in fiscal 2025. ArcelorMittal's revenue fell 1.7% in its latest year, and Reliance's grew 3.3%. ArcelorMittal had an operating margin of 5.9%, against 7.1% at Reliance. Free cash flow was $471 million at ArcelorMittal and $502 million at Reliance.
