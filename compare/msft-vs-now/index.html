    Microsoft vs ServiceNow

    Microsoft MSFT · ServiceNow NOW · Systems Software

    Microsoft reported revenue of $331.8 billion in fiscal 2026, 25.0 times ServiceNow's $13.3 billion in fiscal 2025. Microsoft's revenue grew 17.8% in its latest year, and ServiceNow's grew 20.9%. Microsoft had an operating margin of 46.8%, against 13.7% at ServiceNow. Free cash flow was $67.0 billion at Microsoft and $4.6 billion at ServiceNow. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
