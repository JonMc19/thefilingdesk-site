    CrowdStrike vs Fortinet

    CrowdStrike CRWD · Fortinet FTNT · Systems Software

    Fortinet reported revenue of $6.8 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times CrowdStrike's $4.8 billion in fiscal 2026. CrowdStrike's revenue grew 21.7% in its latest year, and Fortinet's grew 14.2%. CrowdStrike had an operating margin of -6.1%, against 30.7% at Fortinet. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at CrowdStrike and $2.2 billion at Fortinet. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
