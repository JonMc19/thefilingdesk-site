    Church & Dwight vs Colgate-Palmolive

    Church & Dwight CHD · Colgate-Palmolive CL · Household Products

    Colgate-Palmolive reported revenue of $20.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.3 times Church & Dwight's $6.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Church & Dwight's revenue grew 1.6% in its latest year, and Colgate-Palmolive's grew 1.4%. Church & Dwight had an operating margin of 17.4%, against 16.2% at Colgate-Palmolive. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Church & Dwight and $3.6 billion at Colgate-Palmolive.
