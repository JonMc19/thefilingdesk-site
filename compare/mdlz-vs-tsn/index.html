    Mondelez International vs Tyson Foods

    Mondelez International MDLZ · Tyson Foods TSN · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Tyson Foods reported revenue of $54.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.4 times Mondelez International's $38.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Mondelez International's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and Tyson Foods' grew 2.1%. Mondelez International had an operating margin of 9.2%, against 2.0% at Tyson Foods. Free cash flow was $3.2 billion at Mondelez International and $1.2 billion at Tyson Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 27), so the periods don't line up exactly.
