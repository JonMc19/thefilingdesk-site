    Huntington Ingalls Industries vs L3Harris

    Huntington Ingalls Industries HII · L3Harris LHX · Aerospace & Defense

    L3Harris reported revenue of $21.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Huntington Ingalls Industries' $12.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Huntington Ingalls Industries' revenue grew 8.2% in its latest year, and L3Harris' grew 2.5%. Huntington Ingalls Industries had an operating margin of 5.3%, against 9.7% at L3Harris. Free cash flow was $794 million at Huntington Ingalls Industries and $2.7 billion at L3Harris. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
