    CME Group vs FactSet

    CME Group CME · FactSet FDS · Financial Exchanges & Data

    CME Group reported revenue of $6.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.8 times FactSet's $2.3 billion in fiscal 2025. CME Group's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and FactSet's grew 5.4%. CME Group had an operating margin of 64.9%, against 32.2% at FactSet. Free cash flow was $4.2 billion at CME Group and $617 million at FactSet. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and August 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
