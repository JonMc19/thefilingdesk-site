    Applied Industrial Technologies vs Watsco

    Applied Industrial Technologies AIT · Watsco WSO · Trading Companies & Distributors

    Watsco reported revenue of $7.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Applied Industrial Technologies' $5.0 billion in fiscal 2026. Applied Industrial Technologies' revenue grew 8.8% in its latest year, and Watsco's fell 5.0%. Applied Industrial Technologies had an operating margin of 11.1%, against 9.9% at Watsco. Free cash flow was $461 million at Applied Industrial Technologies and $535 million at Watsco. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
