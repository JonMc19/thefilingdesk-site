    Carnival vs United Airlines

    Carnival CCL · United Airlines UAL

    United Airlines reported revenue of $59.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.2 times Carnival's $26.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Carnival's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and United Airlines' grew 3.5%. Carnival had an operating margin of 16.8%, against 8.0% at United Airlines. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Carnival and $2.6 billion at United Airlines. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
