    AMD vs Micron Technology

    AMD AMD · Micron Technology MU · Semiconductors

    Micron Technology reported revenue of $133.2 billion in fiscal 2026, 3.8 times AMD's $34.6 billion in fiscal 2025. AMD's revenue grew 34.3% in its latest year, and Micron Technology's grew 256.3%. AMD had an operating margin of 10.7%, against 74.6% at Micron Technology. Free cash flow was $6.7 billion at AMD and $59.0 billion at Micron Technology. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 27 and September 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
