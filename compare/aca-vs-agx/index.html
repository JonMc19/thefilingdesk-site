    Arcosa vs Argan

    Arcosa ACA · Argan AGX · Construction & Engineering

    Arcosa reported revenue of $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.1 times Argan's $945 million in fiscal 2026. Arcosa's revenue grew 12.2% in its latest year, and Argan's grew 8.1%. Arcosa had an operating margin of 11.9%, against 14.3% at Argan. Free cash flow was $176 million at Arcosa and $411 million at Argan. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
