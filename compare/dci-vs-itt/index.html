    Donaldson vs ITT

    Donaldson DCI · ITT ITT · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    ITT reported revenue of $3.9 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Donaldson's $3.9 billion in fiscal 2026. Donaldson's revenue grew 5.3% in its latest year, and ITT's grew 8.5%. Donaldson had an operating margin of 15.4%, against 17.4% at ITT. Free cash flow was $426 million at Donaldson and $548 million at ITT. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
