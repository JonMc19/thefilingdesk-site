    Dexcom vs Revvity

    Dexcom DXCM · Revvity RVTY · Health Care Equipment

    Dexcom reported revenue of $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.6 times Revvity's $2.9 billion in fiscal 2025. Dexcom's revenue grew 15.6% in its latest year, and Revvity's grew 3.7%. Dexcom had an operating margin of 19.6%, against 12.5% at Revvity. Free cash flow was $1.1 billion at Dexcom and $509 million at Revvity. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and December 28), so the periods don't line up exactly.
