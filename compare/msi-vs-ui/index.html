    Motorola Solutions vs Ubiquiti

    Motorola Solutions MSI · Ubiquiti UI · Communications Equipment

    Motorola Solutions reported revenue of $11.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.6 times Ubiquiti's $3.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Motorola Solutions' revenue grew 8.0% in its latest year, and Ubiquiti's grew 27.2%. Motorola Solutions had an operating margin of 25.6%, against 36.2% at Ubiquiti. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Motorola Solutions and $909 million at Ubiquiti. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
