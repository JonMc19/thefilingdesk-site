    Spotify Technology vs Warner Music Group

    Spotify Technology SPOT · Warner Music Group WMG · Movies & Entertainment

    Spotify Technology reported revenue of €17.2 billion in fiscal 2025, and Warner Music Group $6.7 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Spotify Technology's revenue grew 9.7% in its latest year, and Warner Music Group's grew 4.4%. Spotify Technology had an operating margin of 12.8%, against 10.3% at Warner Music Group. Free cash flow was €2.9 billion at Spotify Technology and $539 million at Warner Music Group. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
