    Birkenstock vs Wolverine World Wide

    Birkenstock BIRK · Wolverine World Wide WWW · Footwear

    Birkenstock reported revenue of €2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, and Wolverine World Wide $1.9 billion in fiscal 2025, in different currencies. Birkenstock's revenue grew 16.2% in its latest year, and Wolverine World Wide's grew 6.8%. Birkenstock had an operating margin of 26.2%, against 8.0% at Wolverine World Wide. Free cash flow was €308 million at Birkenstock and $126 million at Wolverine World Wide. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
