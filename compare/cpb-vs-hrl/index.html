    Campbell's vs Hormel Foods

    Campbell's CPB · Hormel Foods HRL · Packaged Foods & Meats

    Hormel Foods reported revenue of $12.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Campbell's $9.7 billion in fiscal 2026. Campbell's revenue fell 5.0% in its latest year, and Hormel Foods' grew 1.6%. Campbell's had an operating margin of 8.7%, against 5.9% at Hormel Foods. Free cash flow was $678 million at Campbell's and $534 million at Hormel Foods. Their fiscal years end in different months (August 2 and October 26), so the periods don't line up exactly.
