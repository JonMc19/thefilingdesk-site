    Palantir vs Atlassian

    Palantir PLTR · Atlassian TEAM · Application Software

    Atlassian reported revenue of $6.6 billion in fiscal 2026, 1.5 times Palantir's $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Palantir's revenue grew 56.2% in its latest year, and Atlassian's grew 26.0%. Palantir had an operating margin of 31.6%, against 0.2% at Atlassian. Free cash flow was $2.1 billion at Palantir and $1.3 billion at Atlassian. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
