    Fair Isaac vs Trimble

    Fair Isaac FICO · Trimble TRMB · Application Software

    Trimble reported revenue of $3.6 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.8 times Fair Isaac's $2.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Fair Isaac's revenue grew 15.9% in its latest year, and Trimble's fell 2.6%. Fair Isaac had an operating margin of 46.5%, against 16.5% at Trimble. Free cash flow was $770 million at Fair Isaac and $361 million at Trimble. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 30 and January 2), so the periods don't line up exactly.
