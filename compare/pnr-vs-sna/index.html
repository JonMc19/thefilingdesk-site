    Pentair vs Snap-on

    Pentair PNR · Snap-on SNA · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Snap-on reported revenue of $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Pentair's $4.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Pentair's revenue grew 2.3% in its latest year, and Snap-on's grew 0.9%. Pentair had an operating margin of 20.5%, against 25.8% at Snap-on. Free cash flow was $746 million at Pentair and $1.0 billion at Snap-on. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
