    Affirm vs Shift4

    Affirm AFRM · Shift4 FOUR · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

Affirm had an operating margin of 9.8%, against 8.4% at Shift4. Free cash flow was $993 million at Affirm and $624 million at Shift4.
