    Ingersoll Rand vs Snap-on

    Ingersoll Rand IR · Snap-on SNA · Industrial Machinery & Supplies & Components

    Ingersoll Rand reported revenue of $7.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times Snap-on's $5.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Ingersoll Rand's revenue grew 5.8% in its latest year, and Snap-on's grew 0.9%. Ingersoll Rand had an operating margin of 15.0%, against 25.8% at Snap-on. Free cash flow was $1.2 billion at Ingersoll Rand and $1.0 billion at Snap-on. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and January 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
