    Ametek vs Rockwell Automation

    Ametek AME · Rockwell Automation ROK · Electrical Components & Equipment

    Rockwell Automation reported revenue of $8.3 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Ametek's $7.4 billion in fiscal 2025. Ametek's revenue grew 6.6% in its latest year, and Rockwell Automation's grew 0.9%. Ametek had an operating margin of 25.8%, against 20.4% at Rockwell Automation. Free cash flow was $1.7 billion at Ametek and $1.4 billion at Rockwell Automation. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and September 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
