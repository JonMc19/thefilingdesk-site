    Photronics vs Veeco Instruments

    Photronics PLAB · Veeco Instruments VECO · Semiconductor Materials & Equipment

    Photronics reported revenue of $849 million in fiscal 2025, 1.3 times Veeco Instruments' $664 million in fiscal 2025. Photronics' revenue fell 2.0% in its latest year, and Veeco Instruments' fell 7.4%. Photronics had an operating margin of 24.5%, against 5.4% at Veeco Instruments. Free cash flow was $60 million at Photronics and $53 million at Veeco Instruments. Their fiscal years end in different months (October 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
