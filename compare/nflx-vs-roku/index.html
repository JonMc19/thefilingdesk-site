    Netflix vs Roku

    Netflix NFLX · Roku ROKU · Movies & Entertainment

    Netflix reported revenue of $45.2 billion in fiscal 2025, 9.5 times Roku's $4.7 billion in fiscal 2025. Netflix's revenue grew 15.8% in its latest year, and Roku's grew 15.2%. Netflix had an operating margin of 29.5%, against -0.1% at Roku. Free cash flow was $9.5 billion at Netflix and $478 million at Roku.
