    CrowdStrike vs Twilio

    CrowdStrike CRWD · Twilio TWLO · Systems Software

    Twilio reported revenue of $5.1 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as CrowdStrike's $4.8 billion in fiscal 2026. CrowdStrike's revenue grew 21.7% in its latest year, and Twilio's grew 13.7%. CrowdStrike had an operating margin of -6.1%, against 3.1% at Twilio. Free cash flow was $1.3 billion at CrowdStrike and $997 million at Twilio. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
