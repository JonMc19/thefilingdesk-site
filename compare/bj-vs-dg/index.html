    BJ's Wholesale Club vs Dollar General

    BJ's Wholesale Club BJ · Dollar General DG · Consumer Staples Merchandise Retail

    Dollar General reported revenue of $42.7 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.0 times BJ's Wholesale Club's $21.5 billion in fiscal 2025. BJ's Wholesale Club's revenue grew 4.7% in its latest year, and Dollar General's grew 5.2%. BJ's Wholesale Club had an operating margin of 3.8%, against 5.2% at Dollar General. Free cash flow was $328 million at BJ's Wholesale Club and $2.4 billion at Dollar General. Their fiscal years end in different months (January 31 and January 30), so the periods don't line up exactly.
