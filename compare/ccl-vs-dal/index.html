    Carnival vs Delta Air Lines

    Carnival CCL · Delta Air Lines DAL

    Delta Air Lines reported revenue of $63.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.4 times Carnival's $26.6 billion in fiscal 2025. Carnival's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Delta Air Lines' grew 2.8%. Carnival had an operating margin of 16.8%, against 9.2% at Delta Air Lines. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Carnival and $3.8 billion at Delta Air Lines. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
