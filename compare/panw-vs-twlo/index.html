    Palo Alto Networks vs Twilio

    Palo Alto Networks PANW · Twilio TWLO · Systems Software

    Palo Alto Networks reported revenue of $11.5 billion in fiscal 2026, 2.3 times Twilio's $5.1 billion in fiscal 2025. Palo Alto Networks' revenue grew 24.5% in its latest year, and Twilio's grew 13.7%. Palo Alto Networks had an operating margin of 6.0%, against 3.1% at Twilio. Free cash flow was $4.1 billion at Palo Alto Networks and $997 million at Twilio. Their fiscal years end in different months (July 31 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
