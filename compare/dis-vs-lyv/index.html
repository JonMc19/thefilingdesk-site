    Disney vs Live Nation Entertainment

    Disney DIS · Live Nation Entertainment LYV · Movies & Entertainment

    Disney reported revenue of $94.4 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.7 times Live Nation Entertainment's $25.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Disney's revenue grew 3.4% in its latest year, and Live Nation Entertainment's grew 8.8%. Disney had an operating margin of 18.6%, against 5.0% at Live Nation Entertainment. Free cash flow was $10.1 billion at Disney and $334 million at Live Nation Entertainment. Their fiscal years end in different months (September 27 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
