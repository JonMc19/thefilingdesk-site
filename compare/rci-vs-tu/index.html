    Rogers Communications vs Telus

    Rogers Communications RCI · Telus TU · Wireless Telecommunication Services

    Rogers Communications reported revenue of C$21.7 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Telus' C$20.5 billion in fiscal 2025. Rogers Communications' revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Telus' grew 0.6%. Rogers Communications had an operating margin of 45.2%, against 11.5% at Telus. Free cash flow was C$2.2 billion at Rogers Communications and C$2.1 billion at Telus.
