    Haemonetics vs Lantheus

    Haemonetics HAE · Lantheus LNTH · Health Care Supplies

    Lantheus reported revenue of $1.5 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.2 times Haemonetics' $1.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Haemonetics' revenue fell 2.0% in its latest year, and Lantheus' grew 0.5%. Haemonetics had an operating margin of 11.8%, against 20.2% at Lantheus. Free cash flow was $260 million at Haemonetics and $354 million at Lantheus. Their fiscal years end in different months (March 28 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
