    Chipotle Mexican Grill vs Brinker International

    Chipotle Mexican Grill CMG · Brinker International EAT · Restaurants

    Chipotle Mexican Grill reported revenue of $11.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 2.1 times Brinker International's $5.8 billion in fiscal 2026. Chipotle Mexican Grill's revenue grew 5.4% in its latest year, and Brinker International's grew 7.9%. Chipotle Mexican Grill had an operating margin of 16.2%, against 10.7% at Brinker International. Free cash flow was $1.4 billion at Chipotle Mexican Grill and $558 million at Brinker International. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 31 and June 24), so the periods don't line up exactly.
