    Amazon vs Meta Platforms

    Amazon AMZN · Meta Platforms META · Broadline Retail

    Amazon reported revenue of $716.9 billion in fiscal 2025, 3.6 times Meta Platforms' $201.0 billion in fiscal 2025. Amazon's revenue grew 12.4% in its latest year, and Meta Platforms' grew 22.2%. Amazon had an operating margin of 11.2%, against 41.4% at Meta Platforms. Free cash flow was $7.7 billion at Amazon and $46.1 billion at Meta Platforms.
