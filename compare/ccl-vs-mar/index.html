    Carnival vs Marriott International

    Carnival CCL · Marriott International MAR · Hotels, Resorts & Cruise Lines

    Carnival reported revenue of $26.6 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Marriott International's $26.2 billion in fiscal 2025. Carnival's revenue grew 6.4% in its latest year, and Marriott International's grew 4.3%. Carnival had an operating margin of 16.8%, against 15.8% at Marriott International. Free cash flow was $2.6 billion at Carnival and $2.6 billion at Marriott International. Their fiscal years end in different months (November 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
