    BJ's Restaurants vs Dave & Buster's

    BJ's Restaurants BJRI · Dave & Buster's PLAY · Restaurants

    Dave & Buster's reported revenue of $2.1 billion in fiscal 2025, 1.5 times BJ's Restaurants' $1.4 billion in fiscal 2025. BJ's Restaurants' revenue grew 3.1% in its latest year, and Dave & Buster's fell 1.4%. BJ's Restaurants had an operating margin of 3.3%, against 4.1% at Dave & Buster's. Free cash flow was $41 million at BJ's Restaurants and −$101 million at Dave & Buster's. Their fiscal years end in different months (December 30 and February 3), so the periods don't line up exactly.
