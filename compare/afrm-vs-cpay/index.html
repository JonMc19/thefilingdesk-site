    Affirm vs Corpay

    Affirm AFRM · Corpay CPAY · Transaction & Payment Processing Services

    Corpay reported revenue of $4.5 billion in fiscal 2025, about the same as Affirm's $4.3 billion in fiscal 2026. Affirm's revenue grew 32.1% in its latest year, and Corpay's grew 13.9%. Affirm had an operating margin of 9.8%, against 44.0% at Corpay. Free cash flow was $993 million at Affirm and $1.3 billion at Corpay. Their fiscal years end in different months (June 30 and December 31), so the periods don't line up exactly.
