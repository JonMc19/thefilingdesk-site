    01Insider buying and selling

    Baker Bros. Advisors, a large shareholder that is also listed as a director, reported buying $157.0 million of Kodiak Sciences stock on September 28. It was the largest open-market purchase on Form 4 this week. Next came Conifer Management's $97.8 million purchase of Group 1 Automotive.

    In all, 180 buyers reported $508.6 million of purchases, and 362 sellers reported $1.2 billion of sales. Sales under pre-arranged 10b5-1 plans made up 68.3% of that. The largest sale was GitLab director Sytse Sijbrandij's $101.1 million, made under such a plan.

    02Results filed this week

    Two covered companies filed quarterly reports for the three months to August 31. Carnival reported revenue of $8.4 billion, up 3.5% from a year earlier. Its net income rose 3.7% to $1.9 billion, or $1.40 per share against $1.33.

    Nike reported revenue of $11.2 billion, down 4.3%. Net income fell 2.1% to $712.0 million, or $0.48 per share against $0.49. Tesla filed its third-quarter production and deliveries report on Form 8-K, not its results. The release says: "Tesla will post its financial results for the third quarter of 2026 after market close on Wednesday, October 21, 2026."

    03What companies talked about

    Companies filed 1,075 8-K filings this week. Of these, 101 filings mentioned artificial intelligence, close to the usual share. Mentions of tariffs appeared in 89 filings, a share 19.7% below the usual week of the last nine.

    The phrase "workforce reduction" showed up in only 7 filings. That is a small count, but its share was 186.8% above the usual week. "Recession" appeared in 8 filings, a share 50.5% below the usual week. The week brought 33 Form 10-Q filings and 43 Form 10-K filings, so it was not a heavy reporting week.

    04Who got attention

    AbbVie's English Wikipedia page drew 6,180 page views this week, 84.9% more than its average over the previous four weeks. AMD rose 35% and Palantir 26.0%. Page views show interest, not the reason for it.

    At the other end, Apple's page drew 41,818 page views, 43.2% fewer than its four-week average of 73,597 page views.

    How this issue was made

    • Insider trades come from every Form 4 filed with the SEC from September 28, 2026 to October 2, 2026: open-market purchases (code P) and sales (code S), valued at the reported price. Option exercises, gifts and grants are left out, as are trades in funds and REITs that aren't listed. A trade reported identically by several related entities (a fund and its manager, say) is counted once. 3 filings with an impossible price or amount, almost always a total typed in the price box, were left out.
    • Results come from 10-Q and 10-K filings by the 52 companies covered on this site, read from the SEC's structured (XBRL) data, compared with the same period a year earlier.
    • Themes count 8-K filings that contain each phrase, using EDGAR full-text search, per 100 8-Ks filed that week.
    • Attention uses English Wikipedia page views from the Wikimedia Foundation, for the seven days to Saturday.
    • This issue describes filings and public data. It doesn't cover share prices, valuation or analyst forecasts.
