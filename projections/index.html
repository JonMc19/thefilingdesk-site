    Projections

    Pick a company and project its revenue, net income and earnings per share five years ahead in a bull, a base and a bear case. A range of price-to-earnings (P/E) multiples turns each year's earnings into a share price, and the yearly change from today's price it would mean. Or value the company with a discounted cash flow model. The starting assumptions come from each company's own filings, and every one is yours to change.
