    Open-market purchases and sales by officers, directors and 10% owners, from their Form 4 filings, added up for each person and company over the period; "days" counts the days they traded. Grants, option exercises and shares withheld for tax aren't counted, and the same trade reported by related owners (a fund and its manager) is counted once. "Plan" marks a sale under a 10b5-1 trading plan, which an insider sets up in advance. Insiders buy and sell for many reasons, such as taxes, spreading their savings or a plan set up months earlier, so a trade on its own says little about where a company is heading.
