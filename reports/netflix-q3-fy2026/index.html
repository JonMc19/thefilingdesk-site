    Netflix revenue rose 11.9% to $12.9 billion in Q3 as operating margin widened

    By Jon Mc Gowan, The Filing Desk

    Netflix, Inc. · Nasdaq: NFLX Quarter ended September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q filed October 21, 2026 · 0001065280-26-000291 Published October 21, 2026

    Netflix's revenue grew 11.9% to $12.9 billion in the third quarter of 2026. Operating margin was 34.6%, up 6.4 points on the same quarter a year earlier. Net income rose 43.9% to $3.7 billion.

    Revenue growth slowed, but stayed in double digits

    Revenue of $12.9 billion was 11.9% above the third quarter of 2025. Growth was 17.2% a year ago and 13.4% in the second quarter of 2026. The company said the increase came primarily from $1,057 million of growth in subscription revenue, which it said reflects increases in memberships and pricing, and $256 million of growth in advertising revenue.

    Operating margin widened as cost of revenues fell

    Operating income was $4.5 billion, up 37.3%. Cost of revenues, which is mainly the amortization of content, fell 1.4% to $6.1 billion while revenue grew. Gross margin was 52.8%, against 46.4% a year earlier.

    The company said the prior-year quarter included $619 million of expense for non-income tax assessments in Brazil. Content amortization was up $233 million in the quarter. Technology and development spending was $1.0 billion, up 21%, faster than revenue.

    Cash flow and buybacks

    Cash from operations was $3.2 billion and capital spending was $279 million, up from $165 million. Free cash flow, which is the first minus the second, was $2.9 billion, a free cash flow margin of 22.4%. Netflix repurchased $4.8 billion of its stock in the quarter, against $1.9 billion a year earlier. Diluted shares were 4,195 million, down 3.3% from a year earlier.

    The first quarter of 2026 stands out in the charts. The company said its cash from operations for the nine months includes the receipt of a $2.8 billion termination fee after the WBD transaction ended in the first quarter of 2026.

    What the company said about the rest of the year

    In its earnings letter, Netflix said it expects, for 2026, revenue of $51.1B and an operating margin of 31.5%. This is the company's own statement, not our forecast.
