    Take-Two revenue fell 15.6% to $1.50 billion in the September quarter

    By Jon Mc Gowan, The Filing Desk

    Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc. · Nasdaq: TTWO Quarter ended September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q filed November 9, 2026 · 0001628280-26-072254 Published November 10, 2026

    Take-Two's revenue fell 15.6% to $1.50 billion in its fiscal second quarter, compared with the same quarter a year earlier. The net loss narrowed to $54 million from $134 million, and gross margin rose to 57.4%. The company spent $138 million on fixed assets, more than four times the year-earlier figure.

    Revenue fell on every platform

    Revenue was $1.50 billion, down from $1.77 billion a year earlier. In the first half of fiscal 2027, revenue was $3.03 billion, which is 7.5% lower than the first half of fiscal 2026. The quarter before, Q1 fiscal 2027, had grown 2.0% on the year.

    Take-Two's 10-Q breaks revenue down by platform. It says net revenue decreased on mobile, on console games and on PC and other. The filing names lower revenue from Color Block Jam, Match Factory!, NBA 2K, Toy Blast and Merge Dragons! on mobile, and from the Borderlands and Mafia franchises on console.

    Gross margin rose but the operating loss widened

    Gross profit was $860 million on revenue of $1.50 billion, a gross margin of 57.4%, up from 55.2% in Q2 fiscal 2026. Gross profit itself was 12.2% lower.

    Take-Two reported an operating loss of $118 million, against a loss of $98 million a year earlier. The operating margin was -7.9%. The net loss was $54 million, or $0.29 per diluted share, compared with $134 million, or $0.73 per share. The smaller net loss came from the income tax line: Take-Two booked a tax benefit of $58 million in the quarter, against a charge of $18 million a year earlier.

    Cash flow turned negative as capital spending rose

    Cash used by operations was $5 million in the quarter, against $128 million provided a year earlier. Capital spending was $138 million, up from $32 million. Free cash flow, cash from operations minus capital spending, was negative $143 million, against positive $96 million. The 10-Q says Take-Two bought the London building where it previously leased office space on August 10, 2026,.

    Cash and short-term investments were $1.67 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $2.12 billion a year earlier. Total debt was $2.52 billion, against $3.07 billion.
