    Lucid's third-quarter revenue rose 4% to $350 million as its net loss narrowed

    By Jon Mc Gowan, The Filing Desk

    Lucid Group, Inc. · Nasdaq: LCID Quarter ended September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q filed November 9, 2026 · 0001628280-26-072216 Published November 11, 2026

    Lucid's revenue rose 3.9% to $350 million in the third quarter of 2026, and its net loss narrowed 15.0% to $831 million. The company still spent $1.04 billion more cash than it brought in, 29.5% less than in the second quarter. The shares rose 4.7% on November 10, the first trading day after the earnings release.

    Revenue grew slightly and losses on each sale shrank

    Revenue of $350 million grew 3.9% from a year earlier but fell 13.6% from the second quarter. In its 10-Q, Lucid said the increase came mainly from the ramp-up of the Lucid Gravity, which raised its average selling price, and from more pre-owned vehicle sales. It said fewer Lucid vehicle deliveries and lower regulatory credit sales partly offset those gains. For the nine months, revenue was $1,037 million.

    Lucid's cost of revenue was higher than its revenue in every quarter shown. Gross loss was $258 million, against $334 million a year earlier, so gross margin improved to -73.7% from -99.1%. The 10-Q gives lower inventory write-downs and a favorable product mix as the main reasons, partly offset by lower regulatory credits and higher unabsorbed fixed overhead from lower production volumes.

    Operating expenses were little changed

    Research and development spending fell 4.0% to $312 million. The operating loss was $858 million, 8.9% smaller than a year earlier, and the net loss was $831 million, or $2.74 per diluted share. Diluted shares were 395 million, 26.6% more than a year earlier.

    Cash outflow fell from the second quarter

    Cash used in operations was $753 million, and spending on property and equipment was $287 million. That left free cash flow at -$1.04 billion, compared with -$1.48 billion in the second quarter and -$955 million a year earlier. Lucid said in its earnings release that it expects its liquidity "to fund operations and key investments into Q4 2027."

    Cash and equivalents ended the quarter at $948 million, and debt at $4.4 billion, up from $2.4 billion a year earlier. Shareholders' equity was negative, at $2.1 billion below zero. The company's earnings release says Lucid ended the quarter with $2.0 billion in total liquidity, and that its Public Investment Fund announced a $1.75 billion increase to its delayed-draw term loan facility on the day of the release.
