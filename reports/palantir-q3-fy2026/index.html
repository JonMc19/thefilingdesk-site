    Palantir revenue rose 95.9% to $2.3 billion in Q3 as operating margin widened

    By Jon Mc Gowan, The Filing Desk

    Palantir Technologies Inc. · Nasdaq: PLTR Quarter ended September 30, 2026 Form 10-Q filed November 3, 2026 · 0001321655-26-000048 Published November 3, 2026

    Palantir's revenue grew 95.9% to $2.3 billion in the third quarter of 2026, the fastest growth in the twelve quarters covered here. Operating margin was 46.9%, up 13.6 points on the same quarter a year earlier. Net income rose 137.0% to $1.1 billion.

    Revenue nearly doubled

    Revenue of $2.3 billion was 95.9% above the third quarter of 2025. Growth was 62.7% a year ago and 92.8% in the second quarter of 2026. Compared with the second quarter, revenue was up 19.5%. For the first nine months of 2026, revenue was $5.9 billion, against $3.1 billion a year earlier.

    The 10-Q splits the increase between the two customer groups. It says revenue from government customers increased by $513 million, or 81%, and revenue from commercial customers increased by $619 million, or 113%, over the same quarter a year earlier.

    Operating margin rose as costs grew more slowly than revenue

    Operating income was $1.1 billion, up 176.1% from $393 million. Gross margin was 83.4%, against 82.5% a year earlier. It was 84.7% in the second quarter and 86.8% in the first.

    Research and development spending was $220 million, up 52.8%, which is slower than revenue growth. Stock-based compensation was $319 million, up 85.5%, or 13.8% of revenue against 14.6% a year earlier. The company said the increase came from new grants awarded since September 30, 2025.

    Net income and cash flow more than doubled

    Net income attributable to common stockholders was $1.1 billion, a net margin of 48.8%. Income taxes were $14 million, or 1.2% of pretax income. Diluted earnings per share were $0.44, up from $0.18.

    Cash from operations rose 137.2% to $1.2 billion. Capital spending was $8 million, so free cash flow was $1.2 billion, a margin of 51.8%. Free cash flow for the first nine months was $3.3 billion. Cash and short-term investments stood at $10.6 billion at quarter end, with no debt.

    In its earnings release, the company said it was raising its full-year 2026 revenue guidance. The company's outlook is its own and is not a forecast by The Filing Desk.
